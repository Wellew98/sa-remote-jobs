What this page does not tell you

Worth saying plainly, because most pages on this subject imply otherwise.

We do not publish what these agencies charge. One of the 12 states a client-side figure anywhere we can quote it, and eleven do not, so any fee table on this page would be one fact and eleven guesses. Ask each agency directly.

We also do not rank them, take a commission on placements, or run an enquiry form. The role counts above are what we can verify: postings each agency had open when the site last rebuilt.

What each agency page does carry is the evidence we hold on it — who it places, how people are paid, and the quote from its own site that we checked. Browse all companies.
