Where South Africans Find Remote Work

Every platform here has been checked against its own site: whether South Africans can join, what it pays, and how the money reaches you. Pick a category to start.

Agencies & Talent Platforms

They find the work for you. You apply once, they match you to a role at an overseas company, and they are paid by the employer — never by you.

12 platforms · 742 roles

AI Training Platforms

You join the platform, not a company. They match you to AI training and evaluation work — paid by the task or by the hour, project to project rather than as a job.

7 platforms · 15 roles

Testing & Research Platforms

You are paid to try things and answer questions — usability tests, surveys and research studies, priced per study rather than per hour.

3 platforms

Microtask Platforms

Short, self-serve tasks you pick up as they appear: search rating, image and audio labelling, small verification jobs. Paid per task.

3 platforms

Transcription & Writing Platforms

You are paid per audio minute or per word to transcribe, caption or write. Work is claimed from a queue rather than assigned.

2 platforms

Tutoring Platforms

You teach or tutor online, usually setting your own rate and hours. The platform brings the students and takes a cut.

6 platforms

Freelance Marketplaces

You list your own services and bid for work. The platform handles the contract and the payment, and takes a fee.

3 platforms

Developer Platforms

Vetted talent networks for engineers. You pass a technical screen once, then they match you to longer-term contract roles.
