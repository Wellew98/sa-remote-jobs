Join Hire Hangar and work with fast-growing global companies while building a long-term career. About the Role Join a cutting-edge tech company revolutionizing the content automation landscape. We're on the hunt for a powerhouse copywriter with an impeccable record in English-language publications. In this individual contributor role, you'll own the final review process, ensuring every piece of content shines with clarity, consistency, and a unique brand voice. Your sharp editorial eye and tech-savvy mindset will be critical in pushing our content quality to new heights in a fast-paced, innovative environment. Key Responsibilities - Elite Final Review: Lead meticulous final reviews of content across multiple channels, guaranteeing flawless accuracy and style that consistently aligns with our brand's standards. - Editorial Mastery: Elevate content quality and readability by applying your expert editorial skills, while collaborating with internal teams to fine-tune messaging and tone. - Brand Voice Guardian: Draw on your experience with major brands to preserve and enhance the distinctive voice that sets our communications apart. - Tech-Forward Adaptability: Seamlessly blend your editorial expertise with a robust understanding of modern technology trends, ensuring our content remains ahead of the curve. - Process Innovation: Continuously refine and improve our review processes to boost efficiency and maintain the highest editorial standards. Required Qualifications - Extensive experience as a copywriter specializing in English-language publications. - A minimum of 10+ years of copywriting experience if you hold a college degree, or 8+ years without a degree. - Proven direct editorial experience, ideally with a well-known major brand. - A portfolio that demonstrates exceptional attention to detail and superior editorial standards. - Ability to work autonomously while also thriving in a collaborative, tech-centric team environment. - Proficiency with digital platforms and content management systems, highlighting your adaptability in a tech-driven setting. Preferred Attributes - A track record of successfully balancing a brand’s voice with innovative, forward-thinking content strategies. - Experience in high-energy, fast-paced environments where precision and adaptability are paramount. - A proactive, quality-focused mindset committed to consistently elevating content standards and processes.
