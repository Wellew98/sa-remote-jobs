🌎 Remote | 🎙️ English Voice Recording | ⏱️ Under 60 Minutes | 💰 $15–$27+ per Completed Task

We are in the final days of this global research project and are actively looking for additional participants to complete recordings by Tuesday, September 15.

Volga Partners is completing the

final recruitment phase of a global language and voice research project, and we are expanding several participant groups that are currently underrepresented in our research sample.

All eligible adults are welcome to apply. To help us build a more representative research dataset, we are particularly interested in hearing from:

• Adults age 50+, including adults 55–70 and 71+

  • Mandarin, Cantonese & other Chinese language backgrounds
  • Vietnamese speakers
  • Portuguese speakers
  • Albanian speakers
  • European / Metropolitan French speakers
  • Canadian French speakers
  • African, North African / Maghrebi, Arabic-French, Caribbean and Haitian French speakers
  • Participants who can complete the activity using an iPad, MacBook or iMac

All recordings are completed in English. Participants should have strong English reading and comprehension skills while also having a connection to one of the language backgrounds listed above.

What You'll Do

Complete a short, guided English voice-recording activity remotely using your own device. Most participants finish in approximately 20–35 minutes, and the entire activity is designed to take less than 60 minutes.

No professional voice, acting, or research experience is required.

Compensation

💰 $25 — European / Metropolitan French & Canadian French

💰 $20 — Albanian

💰 $15 — Chinese, Vietnamese, Portuguese and other listed French language groups

Additional $2 incentive: Qualifying participants may receive an additional $2 based on an identified research sampling need, including participants in certain age groups or participants completing the activity using an iPad, MacBook or iMac.

Interested?

Complete the brief Global Language Research Participant Interest Form:

https://wkf.ms/4gochub

Our team will review your information and contact you if your profile matches one of the remaining research groups.

Referrals are encouraged. If you know someone who may fit one of the participant groups above, please share this opportunity with your family, friends, colleagues, or community.
