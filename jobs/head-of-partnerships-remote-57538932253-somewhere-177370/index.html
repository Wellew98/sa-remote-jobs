The Opportunity: Scale a Category Leader We are a specialized Cost Segregation firm experiencing rapid growth. For US-based property owners and investors, our service is one of the most powerful tax-saving tools available—but many still don’t know it exists. We are looking for a high-impact Partnerships Manager to lead our expansion. This isn’t a "networking" role; it is a performance-driven sales and alliances position. Your mission is to identify, pitch, and secure revenue-share and affiliate programs with Tax Advisors, CPAs, Wealth Managers, Property Management, and Investment companies. AI-Driven: Build and iterate on AI workflows to handle company research, lead scraping of property firms, personalized outreach, nurturing, opportunity grading, and more at scale. AI-Forward: You don’t just use Claude; you are interested in building AI functions that automate the research required to find the right property management and accounting firms to target.
