Join Hire Hangar and work with fast-growing global companies while building a long-term career.

Job TitleGrowth Marketing Manager

LocationRemote

Time ZoneUS Time Zones (EST–PST)

Key Responsibilities

  • Design and execute growth marketing strategies aligned to revenue and pipeline targets
  • Own full-funnel experimentation across paid media, lifecycle email, landing pages, and conversion flows
  • Optimize acquisition and conversion performance across channels using data and testing frameworks
  • Partner with Product Marketing to position AI-driven value propositions clearly and effectively
  • Collaborate with Sales and RevOps to improve funnel velocity and conversion rates
  • Track and report on growth metrics including CAC, CPL, MQL, SQL, pipeline, and revenue impact

Required Qualifications

  • Proven experience in B2B growth or demand marketing within a SaaS environment
  • Strong understanding of AI-powered products and their impact on buyer behavior and messaging
  • Demonstrated success driving measurable pipeline and revenue through experimentation-led programs
  • Hands-on experience with marketing automation and CRM platforms (e.g., HubSpot or Marketo with Salesforce)
  • Strong analytical skills with the ability to translate data into actionable insights
  • Prior experience working fully remote, fluency with remote collaboration tools (Slack, Zoom, Google Workspace, Asana, or similar), and experience working with US- or UK-based companies. Candidates without this background will not be considered.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in high-growth or Series B+ SaaS companies
  • Familiarity with product-led growth or freemium motions
  • Experience with A/B testing frameworks and experimentation tools
  • Exposure to AI-first or highly technical SaaS products

Tools & Technology

  • Marketing Automation: HubSpot, Marketo (or similar)
  • CRM: Salesforce
  • Analytics & Experimentation: Google Analytics, Looker, Optimizely (or similar)
  • Paid Media: Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads
  • Collaboration: Slack, Zoom, Google Workspace, Asana

Please NOTE

It is crucial that you complete the application form in full. As part of the application process, you will be required to record a video. If your application is successful, you will receive an email confirming next steps—the video is the first step of the interview process. If you do not record a video, we will not be able to consider you for ANY open roles.
