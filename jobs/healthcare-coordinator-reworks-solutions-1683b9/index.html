About The Role

We are looking for a highly organised, detail-oriented and proactive Healthcare Coordinator to oversee administrative tracking and coordination across hospital and healthcare facility operations.

The successful candidate will be responsible for maintaining accurate records of hospital referrals, patient stays, discharge dates and associated invoices. They will also monitor billing requirements linked to individual hospital agreements and coordinate Medicaid application timelines with facility business office managers and finance coordinators.

This role is heavily focused on accuracy, financial recordkeeping, deadline management and communication across multiple teams. The Healthcare Coordinator will ensure that important information is properly tracked, outstanding requirements are followed up on, and potential discrepancies or delays are brought to the attention of the relevant stakeholders.

The successful candidate must be confident working with dates, figures, invoices and administrative records, with the ability to manage their workload independently and communicate professionally with US-based healthcare teams.

Please note

This role is responsible for tracking Medicaid application timelines, monitoring outstanding requirements and following up with facility teams. The actual preparation and submission of Medicaid applications remain the responsibility of the individual facility teams.

Key Responsibilities

1. Hospital Records & Patient Tracking

Maintain accurate and up-to-date records of hospital referrals and patient admissions.

Track patient stays, admission dates, discharge dates and other relevant hospital information.

Monitor changes to patient records and ensure all information is updated accordingly.

Maintain organised records of hospital activity and related administrative documentation.

Identify missing, inconsistent or inaccurate information and follow up with the relevant teams.

Ensure patient records are properly maintained and accessible for reporting and review.

2. Billing & Invoice Coordination

Maintain records of invoices associated with hospital stays and individual hospital agreements.

Track billing requirements and ensure relevant information is accurately documented.

Monitor invoice details, dates, figures and supporting records for inconsistencies.

Compare invoice information against recorded hospital stays and applicable agreement requirements.

Identify potential billing discrepancies and flag them to the appropriate team for review.

Follow up on outstanding invoice information or documentation.

Maintain accurate financial tracking records and ensure information is kept up to date.

3. Medicaid Application Tracking & Coordination

Monitor Medicaid application timelines across multiple healthcare facilities.

Coordinate with facility Business Office Managers to track application progress and outstanding requirements.

Maintain visibility of application deadlines and important milestones.

Follow up with facility teams regarding missing information, documentation or outstanding application items.

Identify applications at risk of delays and proactively communicate these concerns.

Keep accurate records of application statuses, outstanding requirements and follow-up activity.

Ensure facility teams are aware of upcoming deadlines and outstanding actions.

4. Facility & Finance Team Communication

Maintain regular communication with facility finance coordinators and Business Office Managers.

Keep relevant stakeholders informed of submission deadlines and outstanding requirements.

Send professional email reminders and follow-ups to ensure tasks remain on track.

Escalate delays, missing information and unresolved issues to the appropriate stakeholders.

Communicate discrepancies or concerns clearly and tactfully.

Build effective working relationships with multiple facility teams to support consistent administrative coordination.

5. Reporting, Organisation & Administrative Oversight

Maintain centralised trackers and spreadsheets covering hospital activity, invoices, billing requirements and Medicaid application timelines.

Ensure records are accurate, current and properly organised.

Monitor upcoming deadlines and identify items requiring immediate attention.

Provide regular updates on outstanding tasks, application progress and billing-related concerns.

Identify recurring administrative issues and highlight opportunities to improve tracking and follow-up processes.

Manage multiple deadlines independently and ensure important actions are not overlooked.

Essential Requirements

Strong written and spoken English communication skills.

Ability to write clear, professional and tactful emails to healthcare professionals and facility management teams.

Excellent attention to detail, particularly when working with dates, figures, invoices and records.

Strong organisational and administrative skills.

Ability to manage multiple deadlines and competing priorities independently.

Confidence following up with multiple teams to obtain outstanding information.

Strong written communication and professional stakeholder management skills.

Ability to identify discrepancies, missing information and inconsistencies in records.

Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to take initiative.

Comfortable working with spreadsheets, digital records and administrative systems.

Reliable, proactive and capable of working independently with minimal supervision.

Advantageous Experience

Previous bookkeeping, invoicing or accounts administration experience.

Experience working with financial records, billing information or invoice reconciliation.

Previous healthcare administration or medical administration experience.

Exposure to medical aid, health insurance or healthcare funding processes.

Familiarity with Medicaid applications, eligibility processes or US healthcare administration.

Experience working with hospitals, healthcare facilities or medical billing departments.

Previous experience coordinating with finance teams, business office managers or healthcare administrators.

Experience maintaining trackers, financial spreadsheets and deadline management systems.

Software & Systems

The successful candidate should be comfortable working with

Google Sheets / Microsoft Excel

Maintaining trackers, recording financial information and monitoring deadlines.

Email & Communication Platforms

Gmail, Outlook, Microsoft Teams or similar.

Financial Administration Systems

Exposure to invoicing, bookkeeping or billing platforms would be advantageous.

Healthcare Administration Systems

Experience with patient records, hospital administration or medical billing platforms would be beneficial.

The ability to learn new systems and manage information accurately is essential.

Ideal Candidate Profile

The ideal candidate is someone who is exceptionally organised, detail-oriented and comfortable working with administrative and financial information.

They should be confident managing multiple records, tracking important dates and deadlines, identifying discrepancies and following up with different teams to ensure outstanding items are addressed.

This is a coordination-heavy role that requires someone who does not wait for others to provide updates. They must take ownership of their trackers, proactively monitor deadlines and communicate with stakeholders before issues become delays.

We are looking for someone who

Is naturally detail-oriented and enjoys working with accurate records and figures.

Has strong written communication skills and can compose professional emails confidently.

Is comfortable communicating with multiple teams and following up when information is outstanding.

Can manage deadlines and responsibilities without constant supervision.

Is proactive in identifying missing information, discrepancies and potential delays.

Can handle confidential healthcare and financial information responsibly.

Is dependable, organised and consistent with administrative follow-through.

Can work independently while maintaining strong communication with the wider team.

Ultimately, we are looking for someone who can take ownership of healthcare administration tracking, ensure billing and Medicaid timelines remain visible, and make sure important information, deadlines and follow-ups never fall through the cracks.
