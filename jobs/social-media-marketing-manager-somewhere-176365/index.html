About the Company

Our company is a global, distributed AI technology company focused on building a thriving community around open-source AI. Their platform empowers users to create content using cutting-edge generative art and media models. While the team has a strong engineering foundation, our client is now expanding its marketing capabilities to support rapid growth and deepen community engagement. With 250+ remote staff worldwide, the company fosters a collaborative, flexible culture that encourages innovation and experimentation.

About the Role

Our client is seeking a Social Media Manager to lead content creation, distribution, and community engagement across multiple social platforms. This is a high-priority, urgent hire due to recent changes in marketing leadership. The Social Media Manager will own the social content calendar, experiment with emerging platforms, amplify user-generated content, and ensure consistent engagement with our clients community. The ideal candidate is creative, adaptable, and execution-focused—capable of turning ideas into compelling content that resonates with users in the AI and generative media space.

Key Responsibilities

Content Creation & Management

  • Build and manage a comprehensive content calendar across key channels.
  • Create, publish, and manage content across Instagram, YouTube, Reddit, and additional emerging platforms.
  • Curate and amplify user-generated content to strengthen community visibility and engagement.
  • Collaborate with internal creatives and engineers to translate technical concepts into engaging social storytelling.

Community Engagement

  • Actively interact with the community on all platforms to foster conversation and excitement.
  • Respond to comments, questions, and content submissions in an authentic, timely manner.
  • Maintain consistent posting routines to keep platforms active and growing.
  • Channel Growth & Experimentation
  • Identify and explore new social channels and content trends to expand brand reach.
  • Test creative strategies, formats, and storytelling approaches.
  • Analyze post and channel performance to refine content direction and improve engagement metrics.

Skills & Requirements

Non-Negotiable Technical Skills

  • Stream Diffusion: Understanding and hands-on familiarity with this open-source AI model.
  • TouchDesigner: Ability to work with this visual programming tool used for real-time media creation, often used by digital artists and DJs.

Helpful Knowledge

  • Experience with generative media tools such as Hugging Face, ComfyUI, or similar platforms.
  • Understanding of generative art, open-source AI workflows, and modern creative tech ecosystems.
  • Hard Skills
  • Strong proficiency using social media management platforms and native analytics tools.
  • Demonstrated experience creating and distributing content across Instagram, Reddit, YouTube, and related platforms.
  • Basic content production skills: copywriting, short-form editing, simple graphic creation, and asset organisation.
  • Comfortable working with AI tools, creative software, or open-source technologies.

Soft Skills

  • Excellent critical thinking and problem-solving skills.
  • Highly creative with strong storytelling instincts.
  • Fast mover—execution-oriented and comfortable iterating quickly.
  • Collaborative communicator who thrives in distributed teams.
  • Enthusiastic about community building, user engagement, and the open-source AI movement.

Ideal Traits

You’re the kind of person who

  • Loves experimenting with emerging platforms and new content formats.
  • Thrives in an environment where ambiguity is normal and creativity is encouraged.
  • Is execution-driven and moves fast.
  • Is energized by engaging with online communities and amplifying user voices.
  • Learns quickly and adapts based on performance metrics and feedback.

Bonus Points

Experience working in AI, tech, creative tools, or open-source communities.

Fluency in Portuguese or experience engaging Brazilian digital communities.

Prior experience scaling social channels from early stages.
