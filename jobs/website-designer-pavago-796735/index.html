About the Role

We’re hiring a Website Designer to design, build, and optimize modern, responsive, and conversion-focused websites.

This is not purely a graphic design role. You’ll combine UI/UX design with hands-on website implementation , working across platforms such as WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, and Squarespace .

You’ll own projects from visual concepts and layouts through CMS implementation, responsive testing, SEO optimization, and ongoing improvements.

If you have a strong eye for design, understand how websites drive conversions, and are comfortable with Figma, WordPress/Webflow, HTML, and CSS , this role could be a strong fit.
