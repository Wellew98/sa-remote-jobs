Technical Interior Architect

Utopia South Africa (remote) Full-time Design & Creative Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

We are seeking a highly skilled Technical Interior FF&E Specialist with advanced Revit expertise to support the technical delivery of luxury hospitality interior projects. This role focuses on developing, coordinating, and delivering detailed interior documentation from Stage 2 through Stage 5, ensuring design intent is accurately translated into coordinated, buildable, and procurement-ready information. You will work across high-end hospitality and residential projects, contributing to the production of technically robust interior packages within a BIM-led environment. Location & Collaboration Format This is a fully remote collaboration. We collaborate in UK timezone. Utopia does not provide visa sponsorship and/or relocation package for this position. Responsabilities Produce and coordinate detailed Revit-based interior documentation across all project stages (2–5) Develop technical drawing packages including GA plans, layouts, RCPs, elevations, sections, and bespoke joinery details Prepare and manage FF&E and OS&E schedules, specifications, and asset registers Ensure documentation is suitable for tender, procurement, and construction use Coordinate closely with architectural, structural, MEP, and interior design teams within shared BIM environments Support technical resolution of materials, finishes, furniture systems, and bespoke interior elements Support FF&E/OS&E procurement documentation, sample tracking, and product approvals Ensure all solutions meet durability, performance, maintenance, and luxury hospitality standards Maintain consistency, accuracy, and quality across all deliverables
