About Huzzle

At Huzzle, we connect exceptional talent with top opportunities at leading companies across the UK, US, Canada, Europe, and Australia. Our clients include high-growth businesses across industries such as SaaS, Home Services, Construction, and HVAC.

Unlike traditional outsourcing agencies, we place you directly with a client where you’re hired in-house as a valued member of their team.

Role TypeFull-time

EngagementIndependent Contractor

Job Summary

We are hiring a Sales Development Representative with experience in HVAC, home services, or construction-related sales. This is a remote SDR role focused on outbound prospecting, lead qualification, and booking appointments for the sales team.

You will play a key role in generating pipeline by reaching out to homeowners, property managers, and business clients, positioning HVAC solutions, and setting high-quality meetings.

Key Responsibilities

Conduct outbound calls, SMS, and email outreach to generate new business opportunities

Identify and qualify leads for HVAC installations, maintenance contracts, and service agreements

Book appointments and consultations for field sales reps or technicians

Build and manage a consistent pipeline of prospects in CRM systems

Follow up with inbound inquiries and nurture leads through the sales funnel

Maintain accurate records of all interactions and activities in CRM tools

Collaborate with marketing and operations teams to optimize lead generation campaigns

Meet and exceed weekly and monthly KPIs (calls, appointments set, conversions)

2+ years of experience in Sales Development, Enterprise SDR, or B2B outbound sales

Experience in HVAC, home services, construction, or property services is highly preferred

Proven track record in outbound calling and appointment setting

Strong communication and objection-handling skills

Comfortable working US business hours

Experience using CRM tools (e.g., HubSpot, Salesforce, ServiceTitan is a plus)

Highly organized, self-motivated, and target-driven

Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, high-volume outreach environment

💻 Fully Remote: Work from anywhere with international teams

🚀 Career Growth: Join companies in SaaS, MarTech, and B2B services

🤝 Peer Community: Connect with high-performing sales professionals in our network

🧭 Ongoing Support: Receive guidance from Huzzle before and after placement
