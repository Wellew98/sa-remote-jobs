Senior Denial & Appeals Specialist (Dermatology Billing | Remote) 💰 Starting at $10/hour | Full-Time | 100% Remote We are seeking an experienced and analytically sharp Senior Denial & Appeals Specialist to take ownership of complex claim denials, manage appeals end-to-end, and drive recovery on high-dollar and high-priority accounts across a multi-location dermatology practice. This is a senior-level individual contributor role for a billing professional who understands denial workflows deeply, navigates payer-specific processes with precision, and produces measurable recovery outcomes.
