Our client in the UK delivers endurance events and races for athletes of all abilities. They are looking for a Communications Lead to be their first point of contact for their participants, owning the contact inbox for all races and responding to enquiries received through their Instagram channels. They are looking for someone who can work independently, represent the company in a friendly, professional and consistent voice, and liaise closely with the internal team to make sure every athlete gets accurate, timely answers.

Key responsibilities

Manage the race contact inbox

Monitor and respond to all emails received through the central contact address for every race.

Triage enquiries, prioritising time-sensitive messages (e.g. race week queries, urgent logistics issues).

Maintain agreed response times and keep a clear record of enquiries and outcomes.

Escalate complex, sensitive or unusual cases to the team promptly, with a clear summary so the athlete does not have to repeat themselves.

Manage Instagram enquiries

Monitor and respond to direct messages and comments relating to events and enquiries.

Answer questions in a way that reflects the brand voice: approachable, encouraging and professional.

Direct people to the right resources (website, FAQs, athlete guides) and move more detailed or personal queries to email where appropriate.

Flag any negative feedback, complaints or reputational concerns to the team.

Handle withdrawals and transfers

Respond to requests to withdraw from, or transfer, an event entry, applying terms and conditions accurately and consistently.

Clearly explain the options, deadlines, fees and any eligibility criteria to the athlete.

Log requests and outcomes, and pass any approved changes to the relevant internal team member for processing.

Refer exceptions, disputes and special circumstances to the team for a decision rather than making commitments outside policy.

Answer event logistics questions

Respond to questions about event logistics, including (as applicable) registration and race collection, race-day schedule, start times, course information, travel and parking, accommodation, spectators and supporters, and race-day arrangements.

Keep up to date with event briefings and any changes, so information given to athletes is current and consistent.

Answer kit and equipment questions

Respond to questions about mandatory and recommended kit, equipment requirements, and event kit such as race packs, merchandise and finisher items.

Provide clear, accurate guidance in line with the event rules and the official kit list, and escalate technical or rules-related queries you are unsure of.

Communication quality and continuous improvement

Maintain a library of approved responses and templates for common queries, in agreement with the team.

Spot recurring questions and recommend updates to FAQs, athlete guides or website content to reduce repeat enquiries.

Provide a regular summary (e.g. weekly or post-event) of enquiry volumes, common themes, issues and athlete feedback.

Essential

Excellent written communication skills, with a clear, warm and professional tone.

Proven experience in customer service, communications or community management, ideally in events, sport or a similar consumer-facing environment.

Confident handling of email inboxes and social media direct messages (Instagram in particular).

Ability to interpret and apply terms and conditions and policies accurately and fairly.

Strong organisation and attention to detail, with the ability to manage a high volume of enquiries and shifting priorities, particularly around event dates.

Good judgement on when to resolve an enquiry independently and when to escalate.

Reliable, self-directed and comfortable working remotely and to deadlines.

Desirable

Experience in endurance sport, triathlon, running or race event operations.

Familiarity with event registration or ticketing platforms and shared inbox or helpdesk tools.

Experience handling sensitive or emotional customer situations with empathy.

Understanding of data protection requirements when handling personal information.

Measures of success

Enquiries answered within agreed response times.

Accurate, consistent application of withdrawal and transfer terms.

Positive athlete feedback and a strong, consistent brand voice across email and Instagram.

Timely and well-documented escalations.

Fewer repeat queries over time through improved FAQs and templates.
