Position: SDR Role Summary This position is responsible for driving outbound sales pipeline creation through phone-based outreach for a three-year-old SaaS platform specializing in contractor collections. The business is transitioning from inbound referrals to an aggressive outbound sales model to solve demo generation bottlenecks and double revenue by year-end. The successful candidate will join a targeted outbound sales team focused on high-volume cold calling to reach an audience that relies on direct phone communication. Key Details - Employment Type: Full-time (Permanent) - Seniority Level: Mid-level (3+ years of experience) - Location: Remote (South Africa candidates only) - Working Hours: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM EST - Salary / Rate: Base Salary of $1,000 – $1,500 USD per month, plus $25 per qualified demo show-up (Bonus structure disclosed later in the interview process) Role Responsibilities - Execute high-volume cold calling as the primary outbound sales channel to engage contractors (Ideal Customer Profile) who are unresponsive to email and LinkedIn outreach. - Generate qualified demo bookings to support company revenue growth goals. - Work in a hands-on, direct-outreach SDR capacity daily. - Participate in group onboarding and structured training alongside peers. - Contribute to an outbound team structure  - Achieve target demo conversion metrics to help the organization double its revenue by year-end. Required Skills & Experience (Hard Skills) - Minimum of 3+ years of experience as a Sales Development Representative (SDR). - Must be currently employed in a hands-on SDR position (no unemployed candidates accepted for any reason). - Demonstrated long-term sales track record with no history of job-hopping. - Extensive experience with phone-based sales and cold calling outreach. - High-level English proficiency. - Low-level/neutral accent (suitable for call reception in the US market). - Ability to work align with US business hours (9:00 AM – 5:00 PM EST). Key Attributes (Soft Skills) - Strong alignment with a competitive sales culture. - A collaborative mindset suited for South Africa-based team cohesion and bonding. - Resilience and persistence in cold outreach environments. Why This Role This opportunity places you at the ground floor of a major strategic shift within an established SaaS business, transitioning from inbound referrals to a high-impact outbound sales model. Joining alongside a synchronized cohort of peers, you will directly drive demo generation and revenue expansion.
