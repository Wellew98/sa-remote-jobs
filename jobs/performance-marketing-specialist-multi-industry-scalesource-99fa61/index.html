Performance Marketing Specialist (Multi-Industry)

Remote · Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm (Pacific, Central, or Eastern) · $1,250/month for the first 90 days, then $1,500/month

About the role

We're looking for a Performance Marketing Specialist who knows that launching a campaign is only the beginning.

You'll be responsible for helping campaigns perform — monitoring the numbers, spotting what's working (and what isn't), and making the changes that improve results.

You'll work across

paid advertising, email and SMS marketing , collaborating with writers, designers, and project managers to get campaigns launched and then continuously optimized.

The businesses and industries you support may vary. What doesn't change is the expectation: don't just launch campaigns. Pay attention to what happens next and use the data to make them better.

What you'll be doing

Plan, launch, monitor, and optimize paid advertising campaigns across Meta, Google, and LinkedIn

Adjust

budgets, targeting, and campaign settings based on performance

Work with writers and designers to prepare ad copy and creative, then evaluate how those assets perform

Build and send

email and SMS campaigns using Klaviyo or Mailchimp

Test subject lines, timing, messaging, and other campaign variables to improve engagement

Review campaign performance regularly and turn the numbers into clear actions and recommendations

Use

ChatGPT or similar AI tools to create ad variations and speed up execution, while applying your own judgment to determine what should actually run

Coordinate with content, design, and project management teams to keep campaigns moving and launches on schedule

Flag performance issues early and make recommendations before a campaign problem becomes a bigger one

What we're looking for

2–5 years of hands-on digital marketing experience , ideally managing campaigns from setup through optimization

Demonstrated experience running

paid advertising campaigns in:

Meta Ads Manager

Google Ads

LinkedIn Campaign Manager

Experience using Klaviyo or Mailchimp for email and/or SMS campaigns

Proficiency in

Adobe Photoshop and Premiere for basic creative edits and asset preparation

Strong written English, since you'll be working with marketing copy and customer-facing communications

Comfortable working with campaign performance data and using it to determine what to test or change

Experience with

ChatGPT or similar AI tools is expected

Knowledge of SEO, Google Analytics, SEMrush, basic HTML/CSS for email templates, or additional video editing skills is a plus, but not required

The kind of person who will do well here

You're someone who doesn't need to be told that a campaign is underperforming before you notice it.

You pay attention to the numbers, ask questions when something doesn't look right, and are comfortable making adjustments based on what the data is telling you.

You can work across different channels and aren't afraid to get hands-on — whether that's adjusting an ad campaign, testing an email subject line, reviewing creative, or pulling together a quick performance summary.

You also take ownership of your work. If something is going to miss a deadline or a campaign isn't performing as expected, you communicate early rather than waiting for someone else to discover it.

A few practical things matter too

Reliable internet — you need to be online and available during campaign launches and client calls

Consistent and reliable attendance

Strong ownership of deadlines and deliverables

Clear communication when priorities or timelines change

The ability to work independently without constant follow-up

How the process works

Six steps. Same steps whether or not there's a client waiting.

We read your CV. Not a keyword scan, an actual read. If it's clearly AI-generated with nothing specific in it, that's usually obvious and it doesn't help you.

A short intro video. Two minutes of you talking is worth more than a page of text. We're checking if you're clear and comfortable on camera, your English skills, and your professional setup (camera, lighting, sound...)

A call with a recruiter, about 30 minutes. We'll ask you to walk us through something real you've done, so have a specific example ready instead of a general answer.

A couple of short take-home exercises. Nothing sneaky, nothing that takes your whole evening.

A reference check. We'll ask for someone who actually managed or worked closely with you, not a character reference.

If a client's ready: one interview with them. They make the final call on who they hire, but you don't repeat anything you've already done here.

Here's the honest part: sometimes when you finish this process, there's no job waiting yet. That's not a runaround, it's how we work. We'd rather spend the time getting to know good people properly now, so that when a client does come looking for someone like you, we already know you're ready, instead of starting the search from scratch. If that's your situation, you won't be left guessing. We'll tell you where things stand, including when the honest answer is "nothing new yet." And if you take another job while you're waiting, that's completely fine, just tell us, and we'll pick things back up whenever the timing works for you.

What to actually expect from us

If you make it to an interview, you'll get real, specific feedback about how it went either way, not a copy-paste line. If your CV doesn't move forward past the application stage, you'll still hear back, but it'll be a quick automated note rather than a personal one. With how many applications a posting like this gets, that's the only honest way we can get back to everyone fast.
