The Role

This is a senior client-facing role for someone who wants to operate at the intersection of strategy, growth, sales, and execution.

You will work directly with founders and business owners, understand their business in depth, identify what is holding growth back, and help them decide what to do next.

You are not here to simply manage a relationship.

You are here to become a trusted partner in the growth of the business.

That means understanding the client's goals, offer, market, acquisition strategy, sales process, delivery model, current constraints, and commercial priorities - then helping them turn that into a clear plan of action.

In some conversations, you will be helping a founder sharpen their positioning.

In others, you will be diagnosing why pipeline is weak, why sales are not converting, why execution is stalling, or where the next growth opportunity sits.

Your job is to create clarity. To tell clients what matters now. To show them what needs to happen next. And to make sure the right actions actually happen.

This is not a traditional Customer Success role.

It is a role for someone who wants to become an exceptional commercial operator by working closely with ambitious founders and helping them solve real business problems.

The Opportunity

You will have exposure to a large number of businesses, markets, offers, sales models, and growth challenges.

That creates an unusually steep learning curve.

Instead of spending years inside one company solving one set of problems, you will see patterns across dozens of businesses and develop the judgment to quickly understand:

What is actually holding growth back

What is noise and what matters

Where the highest-leverage opportunity is

What the founder should focus on now

What should be stopped

What should be doubled down on

How strategy should translate into execution

Over time, the expectation is that clients see you as someone whose judgment they trust.

Someone who can walk into a complicated situation, understand it quickly, simplify it, and give them a clear direction forward.

If you want to build serious capability in growth strategy, founder communication, sales, operations, and commercial decision-making, this role gives you the environment to do it.

What You Will Own

Become a True Growth Partner to Clients

You will work closely with founders to understand the business beyond surface-level account updates.

You will build a clear picture of

Their business model

Target market

ICP

Offer

Pricing

Positioning

Acquisition channels

Sales process

Current pipeline

Delivery model

Team capacity

Growth goals

Immediate constraints

From there, you will help determine the highest-priority actions required to move the business forward.

Advise, Don't Just Support

We are not looking for someone who simply responds to questions.

We want someone capable of leading the thinking.

You should be comfortable saying

"This is the problem I think we need to solve first."

"This is where I think you're focusing on the wrong thing."

"This is the strategy I would recommend."

"This is what I would do over the next 30 days."

"This is what needs to happen before we scale."

You will challenge clients when needed, simplify decisions, and help them avoid wasting time on low-leverage work.

Your value comes from your judgment, not just your responsiveness.

Turn Strategy Into Action

Good advice without execution is useless.

Every strategic conversation should translate into clear priorities.

You will help clients move from broad goals to focused execution:

Define the objective

Identify the constraint

Decide the strategy

Break it into clear actions

Assign ownership

Set timelines

Review what happened

Adjust based on results

You should naturally reduce complexity rather than add to it.

If a client has fifteen things they could be doing, your job is often to help them identify the two or three that actually matter.

Help Clients Build Revenue

You will think commercially.

That means understanding the complete journey from market to revenue:

Positioning → Acquisition → Conversations → Pipeline → Sales → Revenue → Delivery → Expansion

You will help clients understand where that journey is breaking down.

For example

If acquisition is weak, you investigate the offer, market, message, and channel.

If leads are coming in but deals are not closing, you look at qualification, sales process, follow-up, positioning, and pricing.

If sales are happening but delivery is becoming difficult, you help identify what needs to change operationally.

You should be comfortable moving across the business rather than treating growth as a single function.

Work Across Growth Channels

Depending on the client, you may be advising across areas such as:

Cold email

Outbound

Paid acquisition

Offer development

ICP and positioning

Lead generation

CRM and pipeline

Sales process

Follow-up

Proposal strategy

Pricing

Go-to-market

Operational priorities

AI implementation and services

You do not need to personally be the world's best practitioner in every function.

You do need enough commercial understanding to identify what is working, what is not, ask the right questions, and make good strategic recommendations.

Lead High-Level Client Conversations

You will spend significant time speaking directly with founders and senior operators.

These are not scripted check-ins.

The conversations should be valuable enough that a founder would pay for the thinking itself.

You will

Review business performance

Analyse what has changed

Identify opportunities

Diagnose problems

Challenge assumptions

Set priorities

Recommend strategy

Help unblock decisions

Create accountability

Keep momentum high

The standard is not whether the meeting happened.

The standard is whether the meeting changed what happens next.

Own the Bigger Picture

Clients may have multiple people supporting different parts of their business.

Your role is to maintain the full commercial picture.

You should understand what everyone is working on, how it connects to the overall strategy, and whether the combined effort is actually moving the client toward their objective.

If teams become disconnected, priorities conflict, or something important is falling through the cracks, you take ownership of resolving it.

The client should feel like they have one clear strategic partner who understands the whole business.

Identify Risk Before It Becomes a Problem

Strong operators notice problems early.

You should recognise when

A client is losing momentum

Execution has stalled

The strategy is not producing results

Expectations have become misaligned

Lead flow is weak

Sales follow-up is poor

Internal delivery is becoming a blocker

The client is focusing on the wrong priorities

Trust is beginning to decline

Then act.

Do not wait for a formal escalation.

Part of being a trusted partner is being willing to address uncomfortable realities before they become larger problems.

Skills & Competencies

Commercial Judgment

You understand how businesses grow and make money.

You can connect strategy to revenue and distinguish between activity and actual commercial progress.

Strong Diagnostic Ability

You can listen to a complicated situation and quickly work out what the underlying issue is.

You separate symptoms from root causes.

Strategic Thinking

You can move between the big picture and immediate execution.

You know how to turn an ambitious goal into the next few actions that actually matter.

Founder-Level Communication

You can communicate confidently with intelligent, ambitious, demanding people.

You are comfortable challenging ideas without becoming confrontational.

You can give clear advice even when it is not what the client hoped to hear.

High Ownership

You do not wait to be told what to do when something is obviously not working.

You investigate, decide what needs to happen, and move it forward.

Structured Execution

You are excellent at turning conversations into decisions, priorities, actions, owners, and timelines.

Curiosity

You genuinely enjoy understanding businesses.

You want to know why an offer works.

Why one market responds differently from another.

Why one sales process converts and another does not.

Why certain founders execute faster than others.

That curiosity compounds into judgment.

AI Fluency

You actively use AI to improve your thinking and output.

You should use AI to analyse businesses, prepare for conversations, research markets, structure plans, review performance, document decisions, and increase the quality and speed of your work.

The Person We Want

This role is best suited to someone who wants to accelerate their career by taking on real responsibility.

You want to become the kind of person founders trust when making important decisions.

You want to develop judgment, not just knowledge.

You want to understand how businesses grow from the inside.

You are excited by the idea of working across many different companies, seeing patterns quickly, and becoming better at solving commercial problems every week.

You do not want to spend your career running scripted calls.

You want to sit across from a founder, understand what they are trying to build, and help them build it.

You Will Probably Not Fit If

You want a traditional Customer Success role

You prefer being given the answer rather than finding it

You are uncomfortable advising people more senior than you

You avoid giving direct recommendations

You prefer following scripts to thinking independently

You enjoy relationship management but are less interested in business strategy

You are uncomfortable discussing revenue, sales, pricing, or performance

You need very clear boundaries around your responsibilities

You focus heavily on activity rather than outcomes

You are looking for a low-pressure role with a predictable learning curve

Experience

7+ years in consulting, growth, client strategy, account management, sales, operations, or a senior client-facing role

Experience working directly with founders, executives, or business owners

Strong commercial understanding and the ability to think across acquisition, sales, and delivery

Experience helping businesses solve growth or execution problems

Strong understanding of B2B go-to-market

Direct exposure to areas such as outbound, sales, offer creation, paid acquisition, pipeline management, consulting, or agency delivery

Experience operating in fast-moving environments where judgment matters more than rigid process

The opportunity to build deep skill across strategy, acquisition, sales, operations, and AI
