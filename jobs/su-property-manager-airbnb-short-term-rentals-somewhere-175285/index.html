Property Manager – Airbnb & Short-Term Rentals Location: South Africa Salary Range: 900 - 2500 USD Role Description: The Property Manager for Airbnb and Short-Term Rentals is responsible for overseeing the day-to-day operations, guest experience, and maintenance of vacation rental properties. This role ensures that each property is consistently clean, well-maintained, and meets the highest standards for guest satisfaction. The ideal candidate is detail-oriented, highly organized, and has experience in hospitality, customer service, and property management within the short-term rental industry. Key Responsibilities: - Manage a portfolio of Airbnb and short-term rental properties from listing to check-out. - Ensure timely and professional communication with guests before, during, and after their stay. - Coordinate cleaning crews, maintenance staff, and third-party vendors to ensure properties are clean, functional, and guest-ready. - Monitor guest reviews and respond promptly to issues or feedback to maintain high ratings and Superhost status. - Oversee check-in/check-out processes, including smart lock systems or key exchange procedures. - Conduct regular inspections of properties to ensure they meet quality and safety standards. - Maintain inventory of property supplies (linens, toiletries, etc.) and replenish as needed. - Create and optimize listings on Airbnb and other booking platforms (VRBO, Booking.com, etc.) with high-quality photos, descriptions, and pricing strategies. - Implement dynamic pricing strategies to maximize occupancy and revenue. - Ensure compliance with local regulations, licenses, and tax requirements. - Manage calendars, avoid double-bookings, and coordinate with booking platforms. Qualifications: - Proven experience (2+ years) managing short-term rental properties or working in hospitality/property management. - Strong knowledge of Airbnb and other short-term rental platforms. - Excellent communication and customer service skills. - Highly organized and capable of multitasking in a fast-paced environment. - Ability to handle urgent issues calmly and professionally. - Tech-savvy; comfortable using Airbnb, property management software, messaging tools, and pricing automation tools. - Availability to respond to guest inquiries and issues during evenings/weekends when necessary. - Valid driver’s license and reliable transportation (if managing properties on-site). Preferred Skills: - Experience with dynamic pricing tools (e.g., Beyond Pricing, PriceLabs). - Familiarity with smart home technologies (e.g., smart locks, thermostats, cameras). - Background in hospitality, real estate, or tourism is a plus. - Multilingual skills are a plus, especially in tourist-heavy markets. - Knowledge of local housing laws, occupancy permits, and tax regulations. Details:  - Independent contractor agreement paid 2x monthly. - Client has a desire for long term commitment.
