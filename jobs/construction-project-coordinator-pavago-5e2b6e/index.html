Construction Project Coordinator (Commercial Construction) – Remote

Construction Administration | AIA Billing | Bonding | Buildertrend | Project Coordination

Position Type

Full-Time, Remote

Working Hours

U.S. Business Hours

About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a Construction Project Coordinator to support the successful execution of commercial construction projects from pre-construction through project completion.

This is a detail-driven operations role responsible for managing construction contracts, bonding processes, AIA billing, project documentation, and administrative coordination across multiple active projects. You’ll work closely with project managers, contractors, municipalities, vendors, and clients to ensure projects remain organized, compliant, and on schedule.

If you thrive in structured environments, enjoy coordinating complex construction workflows, and have experience with commercial construction administration, this role offers the opportunity to become an integral part of a growing construction organization.

What You’ll Own

Contracts & Bonding Management

Review and manage commercial construction contracts throughout the project lifecycle.

Coordinate bid bonds, performance bonds, and maintenance bonds.

Prepare and submit bonding documentation accurately and on time.

Communicate with bonding companies, municipalities, and general contractors.

Track contract compliance and maintain organized project documentation.

AIA Billing & Project Administration

Prepare and submit AIA billing packages.

Maintain and update Schedule of Values (SOV).

Track retainage and project payment milestones.

Ensure billing submissions are accurate and completed on schedule.

Monitor deadlines and proactively identify potential payment or billing issues.

Buildertrend & Project Systems Management

Maintain project documentation within Buildertrend.

Track project schedules, milestones, receipts, and progress.

Keep project records accurate, organized, and up to date.

Maintain CRM and project management system integrity.

Support leadership with project reporting and visibility.

Administrative & Project Support

Coordinate communication with clients, contractors, vendors, and project stakeholders.

Support pre-construction and pre-sales administrative activities.

Organize contracts, permits, and project documentation.

Assist project managers with operational coordination.

Participate in weekly project meetings and provide project updates.

Requirements

Must-Have Qualifications

Experience

Experience working with commercial construction contracts.

Hands-on experience with AIA billing and payment applications.

Experience managing Schedule of Values (SOV).

Knowledge of bid bonds, performance bonds, and maintenance bonds.

Experience tracking retainage and construction billing documentation.

Core Skills

Strong Microsoft Excel and Microsoft Office proficiency.

Excellent written and verbal English communication skills.

Outstanding organizational and time-management abilities.

Ability to manage multiple construction projects simultaneously.

Exceptional attention to detail and documentation accuracy.

Nice to Have

Experience using Buildertrend or similar construction management software.

Background in commercial construction, landscaping, or project management.

Experience with CRM and project tracking systems.

Basic bookkeeping or accounting experience within construction.

Familiarity with construction financial reporting and payment tracking.

Tools & Technologies

What Makes You a Strong Fit

Highly organized and exceptionally detail-oriented.

Thrive in structured, deadline-driven environments.

Strong ownership mindset with excellent follow-through.

Comfortable coordinating multiple moving parts simultaneously.

Professional communicator with contractors, municipalities, vendors, and internal teams.

Proactively identify and resolve issues before they become problems.

What a Typical Day Looks Like

Review commercial construction contracts and project documentation.

Prepare bonding packages and submit required documentation.

Process AIA billing applications and update Schedule of Values.

Track retainage, payment milestones, and billing schedules.

Update Buildertrend and project management systems.

Coordinate with contractors, municipalities, and vendors.

Participate in project meetings and communicate status updates.

Monitor project deadlines and ensure documentation remains accurate and complete.

In short

You serve as the operational backbone of commercial construction projects, ensuring contracts, billing, documentation, and project coordination remain accurate, compliant, and on schedule.

Key Metrics for Success (KPIs)

Accurate and timely AIA billing submissions.

Successful management of bonding documentation and deadlines.

Accuracy of project records and CRM data.

Timely completion of contract administration tasks.

Reduced billing delays and payment issues.

Effective coordination across multiple active projects.

Consistent adherence to project deadlines and documentation requirements.
