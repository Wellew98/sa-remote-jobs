Read the posting in full · 254 words

Our client in the UK is looking for a versatile designer to join the team permanently. This is a broad role that touches almost every part of the business - customer-facing creative work, production graphics, UI designs for our data visualisation products, and working on developing the design systems that underpin them. It's a role that rewards genuine range: the ability to shift between a brand campaign brief and a stat pack template, between a motion graphics package and a Figma component, without losing quality in the transition. Everyone on the team has real ownership of their work and real impact on the product and the brand - this role is no different.

Responsibilities

Customer graphics — campaign assets, social graphics, broadcast-ready visuals, and branded content for football clubs, media companies, and athletes.

T3 Studios and video production

— graphics packages, motion-ready assets, templates, and visual support for our studios and production team.

Stat pack design

— our stat packs are used by some of the world's leading broadcasters and rights holders as on-air production tools. Data-rich, visually precise, and built to perform in a broadcast environment.

Toolbox template design

— our Toolbox platform lets clients generate their own graphics. You'll design templates that sit inside the product, balancing creative quality with the constraints of a data-driven system.

Design systems

— we have two design systems: one for our stat packs and one for the Toolbox UI. You'll contribute to both by building out components, maintaining consistency, and helping us scale without losing quality.
