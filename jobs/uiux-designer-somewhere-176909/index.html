UI / UX Designer

LocationRemote

Time Zone RequirementGMT ±3–4 hours (Eastern Europe, South Africa preferred)

TypeFull-Time

LevelMid–Senior (flexible based on experience)

About the Role

We are hiring its first in-house UI / UX Designer to take full ownership of product design across the platform.

Today, design work is handled externally, which has created friction, misalignment, and slower execution. This role is a critical hire that will bring design fully in-house — enabling tighter collaboration, faster iteration, and a more cohesive product experience.

You will work closely with the Chief Product Officer and engineering team to design products from 0-to-1 , helping define how it looks, feels, and functions as it continues to scale.

This is a high-impact role with real ownership — not a pixel-pushing position, but a chance to shape the foundation of a growing product.

Design Vision

We’re looking for a designer who can execute a clean, modern, YC-style aesthetic :

-

Simple

- Minimal

-

Functional

- Polished

The goal is to move away from generic or “template-driven” design and build a product experience that feels modern, intentional, and intuitive from day one.

What You’ll Do

- Own the end-to-end UI/UX design function as our first internal designer

- Design core product experiences for 0-to-1 builds

- Translate product requirements into intuitive user flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity designs

- Collaborate closely with the CPO and engineers in real time

- Improve usability, clarity, and consistency across the platform

- Create and maintain design systems and reusable components

- Proactively communicate design decisions, tradeoffs, and timelines

- Iterate quickly based on feedback and product priorities

What We’re Looking For

Must-Haves

- 3–7+ years of experience in UI/UX or product design

- Experience designing SaaS or web-based products

- Strong portfolio showcasing clean, modern product work

- Proven ability to design products from scratch (0-to-1)

- Excellent communication skills — this is non-negotiable

- Ability to work collaboratively in real time with product and engineering

- Availability within GMT ±3–4 hours for overlap

Nice-to-Haves

- Startup or early-stage product experience

- Experience working closely with product leaders and engineers

- Familiarity with modern design systems and component-based workflows

- Experience shaping brand or visual identity at an early stage

Tools & Workflow

You’ll likely work with tools such as

- Figma (primary)

- Product collaboration tools (Notion, Linear, Jira, etc.)

- Direct collaboration with engineering and product leadership

Exact tooling may evolve as the team grows.

Why This Role Is Exciting

- Be the first in-house designer — real ownership from day one

- Work directly with the CPO and influence product direction

- Design real products, not marketing pages

- Help define the visual identity and UX foundation of the company

- Opportunity to grow into a lead design role as the team scales
