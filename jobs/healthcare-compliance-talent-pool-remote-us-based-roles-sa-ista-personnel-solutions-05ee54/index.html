HEALTHCARE COMPLIANCE TALENT POOL – This is not a live vacancy, but a candidate talent pool

This is not an active vacancy. It is an ongoing talent pool for South African healthcare compliance and documentation professionals.

We are building a pipeline of qualified compliance and documentation professionals to be considered for remote, US-based opportunities as they become available through our clients. When a role opens, we will reach out directly to candidates whose profiles are the closest match.

ABOUT ISTA PERSONNEL SOLUTIONS

ISTA Personnel Solutions is a fast-growing, global BPO company. We are not a recruitment agency. We operate as a dedicated extension of our U.S.-based clients’ teams, delivering high-quality operational support with precision, efficiency, and professionalism. We work across healthcare, ABA therapy, and broader professional services, connecting skilled South Africans with international operations that value quality and reliability.

WHO WE ARE LOOKING FOR

We welcome applications from professionals across healthcare administration and compliance disciplines, including:

Compliance coordinators

Healthcare documentation specialists

Verification and quality assurance administrators

Provider credentialing administrators

Insurance billing and verification administrators

Medical or therapy administrative support professionals

ABA therapy/Clinical support administrators

Case tracking and enrolment coordinators

Healthcare operations support professionals

WHAT WE TYPICALLY LOOK FOR

Minimum 1–2 years of healthcare administration, compliance, or documentation verification experience

Strong written and verbal English communication skills

High attention to detail and accuracy

Comfortable working in structured, process-driven environments

Confident with consistent follow-ups and accountability conversations

Ability to manage multiple cases and meet strict turnaround times

Experience working on portals, tracking sheets, or electronic systems

Exposure to US healthcare, clinical or ABA environments is advantageous but not required

BPO experience is a bonus

WORKING HOURS

Most roles require alignment to US business hours (EST, CST or PST) - Working hours subject to daylight savings

This may include afternoon or evening shifts in South Africa

Working on US and SA public holidays - Candidates will be reimbursed for SA Public holidays

REMOTE WORK REQUIREMENTS

All roles are fully remote. Candidates must have:

Fibre internet (minimum 25 Mbps) - Wi-Fi only will not be sufficient

Reliable backup power to manage loadshedding (UPS, Invertor, Generator, etc.)

A dedicated, professional workspace

HOW TO APPLY

Submit your resume and complete the short screening questionnaire. There is no closing date, this pool remains open. Shortlisted candidates will be contacted as suitable roles becomes available.

We look forward to connecting with South Africa's healthcare compliance talent!
