This is a high-autonomy, systems-first role. You will not be handed tasks — you will own the platform, proactively identify gaps, build the automations that move leads through our pipelines, and ensure the GHL environment serves the origination team, the business development team, and leadership without friction. This role reports to the Operations Manager and works in close day-to-day coordination with the Growth & Digital Manager (paid media, SEO strategy, and overall digital performance) and the Graphic Designer (brand assets and creative templates). You are the technical layer that connects the marketing team’s strategy to execution — the Growth & Digital Manager drives traffic; the GHL Specialist ensures the system captures, qualifies, nurtures, and reports on every lead that traffic generates. While your core function is GHL architecture and automation, you will also provide supporting capability in two adjacent areas when needed: paid media campaign execution (assisting during peak periods or campaigns) and quick-turn design execution (simple asset updates, template implementation, and minor visual fixes alongside the Graphic Designer to avoid bottlenecks on time-sensitive requests). These are not primary responsibilities — they are supporting skills that make you a more valuable member of a lean, high-output team. You also serve as the internal GHL subject matter expert — training the broader team, building the reference documentation, and driving adoption across origination, underwriting, and business development. Role Overview We are seeking a highly technical, systems-driven GHL & Marketing Automation Specialist to own the architecture, build, and ongoing operation of our GoHighLevel ecosystem across multiple brands and business lines. This is a high-autonomy, systems-first role. You will not be handed tasks; you will own the platform, proactively identify gaps, and build the automations that move leads through our pipelines. You will serve as the technical backbone of our lead generation and marketing operations, ensuring every lead is captured, qualified, nurtured, routed, and reported accurately. You will act as our internal subject matter expert, training the broader team and maintaining critical documentation.Key Responsibilities - GHL Architecture, CRM & Pipeline Management (25%): Design, build, and maintain pipelines for our divisions (Residential Private Lending, Commercial Brokerage, Reverse Mortgages). Ensure mobile responsiveness, accessibility, and SEO optimization. - Marketing Automation & Nurture Campaigns (20%): Develop automation workflows for all lead sources.
