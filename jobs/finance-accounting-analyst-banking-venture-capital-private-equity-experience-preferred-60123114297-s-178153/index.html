Job TitleFinance & Accounting Analyst

LocationFully Remote

ScheduleFull-Time 9AM - 5PM EST (Aligned with U.S. Business Hours)

Compensation:  $2000 - $3200

Experience LevelSenior (5–8 Years)

About the Company

Our client is a Smart Building Technology company pioneering advanced smart glass and window systems for homes and buildings. Their innovative products enable solar-powered smart-glass functionality without the need for external power or electrical installation.

As the company scales, they are investing in building a more robust, efficient, and future-proof financial infrastructure. This role will play a critical part in modernizing back-office operations while supporting strategic financial decision-making.

Role Overview

The Finance & Accounting Analyst will serve as a key contributor across both financial strategy and core accounting operations. This role is responsible for owning financial modeling, forecasting, and analysis, while also supporting day-to-day accounting functions.

This is a high-impact, senior-level role designed to strengthen financial visibility, improve processes, and support leadership with data-driven insights—especially as the company undergoes an ERP transition and broader operational restructuring.

Key Responsibilities

Financial Modeling & Analysis

  • Build, maintain, and optimize financial models for forecasting and strategic planning
  • Conduct variance analysis and provide actionable insights into financial performance
  • Deliver data-driven recommendations to support executive decision-making
  • Develop forecasting frameworks to improve financial visibility and planning accuracy

Strategic Finance Support

  • Assist in restructuring and strengthening the back-office finance function
  • Support cost optimization initiatives and process improvements
  • Help distribute financial responsibilities to de-risk operations and improve scalability
  • Contribute to building a more resilient financial structure to support company growth

ERP Systems & Migration

  • Operate within Microsoft Dynamics for accounting and financial workflows
  • Support the migration from QuickBooks to Microsoft Dynamics
  • Ensure data integrity, accuracy, and continuity during system transition
  • Identify and implement process improvements within the ERP environment

Core Accounting Operations

  • Manage and support full-cycle accounting, including:
  • Accounts Payable (AP)
  • Accounts Receivable (AR)
  • Account Reconciliations
  • Vendor & Customer Setup
  • Journal Entries
  • Month-End Close
  • Financial Statement Preparation
  • Ensure all financial records are accurate, organized, and audit-ready
  • Support payroll processing and cash flow tracking as needed
  • Maintain consistency and accuracy across all financial reporting

Required Qualifications

  • 5–8 years of experience in finance, accounting, or financial analysis roles
  • Proven experience building and maintaining financial models in Microsoft Excel
  • Strong background in full-cycle accounting operations
  • Solid understanding of U.S. business models and accounting practices
  • Excellent written and verbal English communication skills
  • High attention to detail with strong analytical and problem-solving abilities

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with Microsoft Dynamics or similar ERP systems
  • Background in manufacturing, hardware, or technology companies
  • CPA or equivalent certification (nice to have)
  • MBA or equivalent certification (nice to have)
  • Experience with payroll processing, budgeting, and cash flow management

What This Role Offers

  • Opportunity to play a key role in building and modernizing a company’s financial infrastructure
  • Direct impact on financial strategy, forecasting, and operational efficiency
  • Exposure to ERP transformation and system optimization
  • High-ownership role with visibility into leadership decision-making
  • Ability to help shape a scalable and resilient finance function

Ideal Candidate Profile

  • A finance professional who can balance hands-on accounting with strategic analysis
  • Strong financial modeling expertise with the ability to build systems from the ground up
  • Comfortable operating in evolving environments with changing priorities
  • A self-starter who thrives in high-ownership roles
  • Process-oriented with a focus on efficiency, accuracy, and continuous improvement

Additional Notes

  • This is a fully remote position
  • The role is designed to reduce reliance on external consultants by bringing financial modeling and strategic capabilities in-house
  • The position plays a critical role in supporting ERP migration and long-term operational scalability
