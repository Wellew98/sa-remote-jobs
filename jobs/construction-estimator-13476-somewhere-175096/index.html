Construction Estimator - Xactimate Specialist Location: South Africa | LATAM Commitment: Full-time, Long-term We are seeking a highly motivated and experienced Construction Estimator to join our dynamic and growing team! If you're an expert in Xactimate , have extensive real-world construction experience , and are looking for a challenging long-term opportunity, we want to hear from you. This senior-level remote role offers the exciting balance of office-based work (meetings, negotiations, reporting) with hands-on field experience. Our diverse projects span across the majority of the United States and extend to intriguing international locations ensuring no two days are alike! You'll be part of a team dedicated to continuous learning, specialized training, and utilizing the latest technology to be the best in the field. What You'll Be Doing: As a key member of our estimating team, you will be responsible for: - Generating accurate and highly detailed scopes of work and cost estimates for a wide range of property damages, primarily focusing on fire and water damage losses typically exceeding $100,000 (averaging $200,000 - $400,000) . - Creating precise sketches and diagrams using Xactimate . - Traveling to subject properties , sometimes on an emergency basis, to photograph damages and confirm scope and finishes (Matterport proficiency is a plus; training provided). - Collaborating closely with insurance carrier building consultants and independent adjusters to obtain agreed scopes and build strong relationships. - Documenting, scoping, and estimating unforeseen damages and supplemental repairs , including upgrades for current building standards and codes. - Working directly with general contractors or insured's project managers to ensure all property damages are correctly estimated. - Providing technical explanations for repair methods to clients, adjusters, and consultants, justifying increased construction costs or additional labor. - Preparing comprehensive scopes of work and unit cost differences to effectively negotiate and maximize insurance settlements. - Creating budgets and conceptual estimates , conducting cost analysis, and coordinating estimates. - Managing project timelines and schedules , and resolving complex technical issues. - Mentoring team members and ensuring adherence to department standards and documentation. What We're Looking For: - 3+ years of construction estimating experience , with a strong background in disaster restoration and the insurance industries. - High-level proficiency in Xactimate is a must. - Strong understanding of construction methodology and building codes . - Educational background in engineering, architecture, or construction project management. - Proficient in MS Office (especially Excel) , Bluebeam Revu , and construction scheduling programs (e.g., Primavera, MS Projects). - Excellent verbal and written communication skills with a professional demeanor. - Self-motivated, detail-oriented, and enthusiastic, with a strong work ethic. - Ability to identify potential site hazards (e.g., asbestos, lead paint, mold). Why Join Us? - Work with a team of highly motivated, top-tier professionals in a collaborative environment. - Engage in challenging and diverse projects across the globe. - Continuous opportunities for specialized training, certifications, and professional development. - A company that values expertise, continuous improvement, and mutual respect.
