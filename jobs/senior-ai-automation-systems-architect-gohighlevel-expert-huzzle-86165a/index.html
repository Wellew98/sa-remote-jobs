About Huzzle

At Huzzle, we connect high-performing B2B sales professionals with global companies across the UK, US, Canada, Europe, and Australia. Our clients include startups, digital agencies, and tech platforms in industries like SaaS, MarTech, FinTech, and EdTech. We match top sales talent to full-time remote roles where they’re hired directly into client teams and provided ongoing support by Huzzle.

As part of our talent pool, you’ll have access to exclusive SDR opportunities matched to your background and preferences.

Job Summary

We’re hiring a

Senior AI Automation & Systems Architect to lead the design, implementation, and optimization of scalable automation systems and product infrastructure. This role sits at the intersection of automation engineering, product development, and client communication , making it ideal for someone who can both build complex systems and clearly explain them to non-technical stakeholders .

You will own the technical backbone of operations developing workflows, integrating platforms, and ensuring all systems are scalable, reliable, and well-documented.

Key Responsibilities

Product & Documentation

Audit, optimize, and scale existing client-facing automation systems

Build advanced automation workflows using tools like GoHighLevel, n8n, Make, or Zapier

Develop and maintain clear technical documentation (SOPs, guides, internal knowledge base)

Integrate multiple tools and platforms via APIs and webhooks

Client Solutions

Design end-to-end AI and automation systems tailored to client business needs

Build workflows for lead generation, nurturing, content creation, and sales pipelines

Architect integrations across CRM, email, calendars, payment systems, and project management tools

Maintain and troubleshoot live systems to ensure performance and scalability

Communication & Support

Translate technical concepts into clear, actionable insights for non-technical stakeholders

Advise clients and internal teams on best implementation strategies

Create structured outlines for technical content (videos, documentation, articles)

Provide ongoing technical support across systems and workflows

Internal Infrastructure

Own and scale internal automation systems for onboarding, operations, and data management

Build and maintain CRM systems (primarily GoHighLevel)

Ensure all systems are reliable, efficient, and scalable

Experience & Technical Expertise

3+ years of hands-on experience building automation systems in real business environments

Expert-level proficiency in

GoHighLevel (HighLevel)

Strong experience with automation tools ( n8n, Make, Zapier, or similar )

Solid understanding of

APIs, webhooks, and system integrations

Experience connecting CRMs with email platforms, calendars, payments, and project tools

Data structuring experience using Airtable, Google Sheets, or similar tools

Experience implementing AI into workflows and business processes

Technical Communication

Strong documentation skills (SOPs, user guides, technical specs)

Fluent English (written and verbal) with the ability to explain complex systems clearly

Product & Business Understanding

Experience working directly with clients or external stakeholders

Strong product thinking (scalability, UX, maintainability, build vs. buy decisions)

Soft Skills

Self-starter with the ability to manage projects independently

Comfortable in fast-paced, ambiguous environments

Strong problem-solving and systems-thinking mindset
