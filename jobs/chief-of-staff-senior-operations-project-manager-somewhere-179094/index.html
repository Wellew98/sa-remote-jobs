About the Company: It is a subscription-based creative service platform that offers unlimited graphic design, custom illustrations, motion graphics, and presentation design for a flat monthly fee.

About the Role: We are looking for a strategic and execution-driven Chief of Staff / Senior Project Manager to serve as a right-hand operational partner ("Number 2") to our executive leadership.   This role goes far beyond traditional virtual or executive assistance. We need a business-savvy professional with prior executive team experience, ideally within the software and technology sectors. Rather than doing all the hands-on tasks, your core responsibility is to ensure operational alignment, seamless communication across teams, and the effective execution of key business priorities. .

Key Responsibilities

  • Operations & Execution Leadership: Serve as the primary point of contact to coordinate, organize, and track critical initiatives, ensuring projects stay on schedule.
  • Senior Project Oversight: Monitor organizational workflows, identify bottlenecks, and drive cross-functional accountability.
  • Executive Alignment: Work closely with the CEO/executive level to translate strategic vision into clear, actionable plans.
  • Cross-Functional Communication: Facilitate clear, transparent, and timely communication between business units and leadership.
  • Business & Financial Acumen: Apply strong business and financial judgment to evaluate key decisions and keep operations running smoothly.

Qualifications

  • Relevant Experience: Proven track record in senior project management, operations, or Chief of Staff roles within Software / Tech companies.
  • Business & Financial Literacy: Strong understanding of business dynamics and finance (far beyond basic administrative experience).
  • Executive Team Background: Experience working directly alongside C-suite leaders and executive management.
  • High Ownership & Organization: Highly proactive self-starter who can organize complex workflows and solve problems independently.
  • Communication Skills: Excellent written and spoken English skills for clear executive-level interaction.
