Accounting & Bookkeeping Specialist (Multi-Industry)

Remote · Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm (Pacific, Central, or Eastern) · $1,250/month for the first 90 days, then $1,500/month

Some of the roles we hire for have a client ready to go right away. Others don't yet. Either way, you go through the same process. If there's no client yet, we're building a group of people we've already gotten to know, so we can move fast the moment one shows up. Your recruiter will tell you plainly which situation applies to you.

What this job actually is

Somebody has to make sure the numbers are actually right, every month, without anyone having to ask. Transactions get coded. Accounts get reconciled. Bills get paid, invoices get chased, and the close happens on a schedule instead of whenever there's time. That somebody is you.

This shows up differently depending on the business. The chart of accounts looks different, the transaction volume looks different, sometimes there's a CPA in the loop and sometimes you're closer to the finance lead yourself. What doesn't change is the standard: the books are clean, the close is on time, and if something doesn't tie out, you say so instead of forcing it.

What you'll actually do

Code transactions consistently so the same expense hits the same account every time

Reconcile accounts on a set monthly cadence, and actually investigate a variance instead of plugging it

Handle AP and ARpay bills on time, invoice promptly, and follow up on anything unpaid without being asked

Run month-end close against a checklist, and produce a short summary of what happened

Keep cash and finance reports going out on schedule, with anything unusual called out plainly

Flag a problem the moment you see it, even when it's not technically your mistake to fix

What we're actually looking for

3+ years of hands-on bookkeeping or accounting work where you personally owned the ledger, not just supported someone who did

Real time inside QuickBooks Online and/or Xero, doing the actual reconciliation and close work

Comfortable enough in Excel or Google Sheets to build a formula without looking it up every time

Experience running or contributing to a month-end close on a real, recurring cadence

Your written and spoken English is genuinely fluent, not "good enough"

Experience with US GAAP basics, US payroll, 1099s, or a CPA relationship is a plus, not a requirement

A few things that matter more than they sound like they should

Internet that doesn't drop calls

You close on schedule because that's who you are, not because someone's watching

If a number doesn't tie out, you say so before the deadline, not after

You document the judgment calls instead of keeping them in your head

How the process actually works

Six steps. Same steps whether or not there's a client waiting.

We read your CV. An actual read, not a keyword scan.

A short intro video. Two minutes of you talking is worth more than a page of text. We're checking if you're clear and comfortable on camera, your English skills, and your professional setup (camera, lighting, sound...)

A call with a recruiter, about 30 minutes. Half of it is conversation. We'll ask you to walk you through something real you've done, so have a specific number or example ready. This is also when your recruiter tells you clearly whether there's a client waiting for this role or not.

A couple of short take-home exercises. Nothing sneaky, nothing that eats your evening.

A reference check, from someone who actually managed or worked closely with you, not a character reference.

If a client's ready: one interview with them. They make the final call, but you don't repeat anything you've already done here.

Here's the honest part, in plain terms: sometimes when you finish this process, there's no job waiting yet. That's not a runaround, it's how we work. We'd rather spend the time getting to know good people properly now, so that when a client comes looking for someone like you, we already know you're ready. If that's your situation, you won't be left guessing. We'll tell you honestly where things stand, including when the honest answer is "nothing new yet." And if you take another job while you're waiting, that's fine, just tell us.

What to actually expect from us

If you make it to an interview, you'll get real, specific feedback about how it went either way. If your CV doesn't move forward, you'll still hear back, but it'll be a quick automated note rather than a personal one. That's the only honest way we can get back to everyone given how many applications a posting like this gets.
