eCommerce Associate Role Description We are seeking a highly skilled data-driven eCommerce Associate based in South Africa to manage and optimize our online sales channels for U.S. clients. This role involves product listing, website maintenance, digital marketing, and performance analysis to drive revenue growth.   Qualifications - 2+ years of experience in eCommerce, digital marketing, or related fields. - Proven experience as an eCommerce Associate or similar role. - Strong understanding of eCommerce platforms (e.g., Shopify, Magento, Amazon, WooCommerce) and digital marketing tools. - Proficiency in analytics tools such as Google Analytics and heatmap tools. - Experience managing online campaigns and A/B testing. - Excellent project management and organizational skills. - Strong analytical mindset with a focus on data-driven decision-making. - Excellent communication and team collaboration skills. - Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Business, or a related field (preferred). Responsibilities - Manage product listings, pricing, and promotions across e-commerce platforms. - Optimize product pages for SEO and conversions. - Oversee inventory management and coordinate with suppliers. - Execute and monitor digital marketing campaigns (Google Ads, Meta Ads, email marketing). - Analyze sales data and customer behavior to improve performance. - Manage customer service inquiries and resolve issues efficiently. - Collaborate with internal teams to enhance the user experience. ​​​​ Compensation $1,000 – $1,800 a month, paid in USD depending on experience. Details - Independent contractor agreement paid 2x monthly. - Client has a desire for long term commitment.
