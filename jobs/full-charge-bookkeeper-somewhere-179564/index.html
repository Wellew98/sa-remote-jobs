Company Overview

Our client is a growing roofing and exterior construction contractor serving homeowners and commercial property owners throughout Metro Atlanta and North Georgia. Its services range from repairs to complete roofing and exterior renovation projects.

They are hiring a Full-Charge Bookkeeper to take ownership of its day-to-day accounting, job-cost accuracy and financial reporting.

Role Overview

The company is looking for a Full-Charge Bookkeeper who can manage everything from daily transaction entry through month-end close and management reporting.

This is more than a basic data-entry position. The successful candidate will act as a trusted steward of the company’s financial records and provide leadership with accurate information about cash flow, receivables, payables and project profitability.

The ideal candidate is organized, proactive, responsive and dependable. They must be comfortable working independently, resolving discrepancies and meeting recurring deadlines without constant oversight.

Responsibilities

  • Manage day-to-day bookkeeping, transaction entry and general-ledger maintenance to keep records accurate, current and tax-ready.
  • Complete the month-end close, including bank, credit-card, merchant-account and applicable liability reconciliations in QuickBooks Online.
  • Prepare and review financial statements, including the profit and loss statement, balance sheet and statement of cash flows.
  • Manage accounts payable and accounts receivable, including vendor bills, customer invoicing, payment application and collection follow-up.
  • Coordinate and process payroll accurately and on time while maintaining appropriate supporting records.
  • Maintain accurate project-level job costing by assigning materials, subcontractor labor, commissions and other direct expenses to the correct project and service category.
  • Prepare profitability reports by individual job and by service or trade, including revenue, estimated versus actual costs, gross profit and gross margin.
  • Reconcile customer contracts, change orders, invoices, payments and job costs between the company’s project-management system and QuickBooks Online.
  • Maintain subcontractor records, including W-9s, Certificates of Insurance, workers’ compensation documentation and expiration dates.
  • Assist with tracking insurance-related project financials, including contract values, deductibles, depreciation, approved additions, payments received and outstanding customer balances.
  • Monitor financial trends, perform variance analysis and identify missing costs, overdue balances, margin concerns, documentation gaps and accounting discrepancies.
  • Prepare cash-flow forecasts and organized daily, weekly and monthly reports for leadership.
  • Work with company leadership and outside accounting professionals to support tax preparation, insurance audits and other financial reviews.
  • Develop and maintain clear accounting procedures and organized supporting documentation.

Qualifications

  • Language Requirement: Fluent English, with the ability to explain accounting information clearly to U.S.-based executives and team members.
  • Experience: Three to five years of combined bookkeeping and accounting experience, preferably supporting small-to-medium businesses.
  • Strong command of QuickBooks Online, including reconciliations, general-ledger maintenance, journal entries and advanced reporting.
  • Strong accounting fundamentals, including double-entry accounting, accruals, adjusting entries and financial-statement preparation.
  • Experience managing accounts payable, accounts receivable, payroll and month-end close.
  • Experience with job costing, project-based accounting or profitability reporting.
  • Strong Excel or Google Sheets skills.
  • Excellent attention to detail and the ability to identify and resolve discrepancies.
  • Ability to work independently and manage recurring responsibilities without constant oversight.
  • Strong written and verbal communication.
  • Reliable availability during the required working hours.
  • Stable internet connection and dependable computer equipment.
  • Available to onboard and begin work promptly.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Degree in Accounting or Finance, QuickBooks ProAdvisor certification or Certified Bookkeeper designation.
  • Experience with a construction, restoration, home-services or other project-based company.
  • Experience with work-in-progress reporting and profitability reporting by job or service category.
  • Experience reconciling QuickBooks Online with a CRM or project-management platform.
  • Familiarity with JobNimbus, Buildertrend or similar software.
  • Familiarity with subcontractor W-9s, 1099s, Certificates of Insurance and workers’ compensation documentation.
  • Familiarity with insurance-related project accounting.
  • Prior construction experience and JobNimbus experience are helpful but not required. We will train the right candidate on its industry, systems and internal processes.
