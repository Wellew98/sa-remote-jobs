EOS Integrator / Head of Growth & Operations

This job is open to candidates working remotely from anywhere

Monday – Friday | 8 hours per day, most of them overlapping with US Central Time

Please tell us why

.

Most operators keep the machine running. This role is for someone who builds the machine — and then runs it at full speed.

Calvin Smith is an entrepreneur and EOS Implementer building a personal brand and coaching business designed to help entrepreneurs access systems, coaching, and opportunities they'd never find on their own.

The business has a podcast, a speaking programme, a Skool community, an EOS consulting pipeline, and a content operation across multiple platforms. Right now those pieces exist. What's missing is the person who connects them into a system that compounds.

That's this role.

What this actually is

This is a player-coach seat. You will build the operating system, lead the team, and still get your hands dirty when something needs doing. You are not here to sit in meetings and send status updates. You are here to make things move.

Think of it as running a small media and consulting business inside a larger one — with clear goals, a real scoreboard, and the autonomy to figure out how to hit the numbers.

What you'll own

Build the operating system — scorecards, dashboards, meeting rhythm, accountability structure, and reporting so the whole team knows what's happening and what's next

Hire and lead the team responsible for social growth, LinkedIn, podcast, speaking outreach, EOS sales pipeline, Skool community, content, and AI automation

Use AI and automation daily to make the team faster, cut manual work, and build systems that don't break when you're not watching

Turn 12-month goals into weekly execution — social follower targets, podcast bookings, speaking engagements, EOS sessions sold, Skool revenue

Protect Calvin's time — he should be focused on content, sales, EOS sessions, speaking, and relationships, not chasing updates or re-explaining priorities

Your 12-month goals

Non-LinkedIn social followers1,000,000+ within 12 months

LinkedIn followers250,000+ within 12 months

EOS sessions50+ per year at $5,000/session

Guest podcast appearances2+ per month

Owned podcast episodes2+ per month

Speaking engagements: 6+ per year, with 3+ outside the U.S.

What you'll need

Leadership

5+ years leading teams, including direct accountability conversations when people miss targets

Managed 7+ people at the same time

Run weekly meetings where people reported numbers, not just updates

Can lead and execute simultaneously — not one or the other

Operations

3+ years in ops, chief of staff, agency ops, creator ops, founder support, or growth ops

Built and managed a weekly scorecard, KPI dashboard, or reporting system personally

Managed 5+ active projects or workstreams simultaneously

2+ years working directly with a founder, creator, coach, or consultant

AI and automation

Use AI tools daily — Claude, ChatGPT, Zapier, n8n, or similar

Built at least 3 real workflows, automations, SOPs, or reporting systems using these tools

Can explain what you built, why it mattered, and what improved

Not learning AI here — already using it and getting better every week

Growth and content awareness — you don't need to be the expert, but you need to understand enough to lead the people doing the work:

Real familiarity with at least 3 of: social media growth, LinkedIn growth, podcast operations, sales pipeline/CRM, online courses/Skool/community, speaking or event outreach

This probably isn't right for you if

You need clear instructions before you start

You prefer managing people over doing work yourself

Big targets with no playbook make you slow down

You avoid hard conversations about performance

You want a calm, structured, corporate environment

How we work

Calvin's business runs on EOS — clear goals, weekly scorecards, honest feedback, and real accountability. No hiding behind process. No pretending to be busy. If a number isn't improving, we say so and fix it.

Core values: Have Fun. Try New Shit Fast. Best in the World. Put in the Damn Work. Hold Accountability.

Internal operating principle: FITFO — Figure It The F* Out. Be resourceful, be direct, be fast, own your results.

How to apply

We know your CV might not be up to date. Send what you have. Applications take a few minutes.

You'll be asked a few short questions. Specific, numbers-based answers are expected. This helps us filter the best candidates at early stages of the process. If you have the right experience and you put the effort, it will be noticed. Vague answers won't move forward.

A short video submission might be required at the right stage of the process.

Everyone gets a response within 24 business hours.
