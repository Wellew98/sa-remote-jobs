Please tell us why . Most operators keep the machine running. This role is for someone who builds the machine — and then runs it at full speed. Calvin Smith is an entrepreneur and EOS Implementer building a personal brand and coaching business designed to help entrepreneurs access systems, coaching, and opportunities they'd never find on their own. The business has a podcast, a speaking programme, a Skool community, an EOS consulting pipeline, and a content operation across multiple platforms. Right now those pieces exist. What's missing is the person who connects them into a system that compounds. That's this role. What this actually is This is a player-coach seat. You will build the operating system, lead the team, and still get your hands dirty when something needs doing. You are not here to sit in meetings and send status updates. You are here to make things move. Think of it as running a small media and consulting business inside a larger one — with clear goals, a real scoreboard, and the autonomy to figure out how to hit the numbers. What you'll own Build the operating system — scorecards, dashboards, meeting rhythm, accountability structure, and reporting so the whole team knows what's happening and what's next Hire and lead the team responsible for social growth, LinkedIn, podcast, speaking outreach, EOS sales pipeline, Skool community, content, and AI automation Use AI and automation daily to make the team faster, cut manual work, and build systems that don't break when you're not watching Turn 12-month goals into weekly execution — social follower targets, podcast bookings, speaking engagements, EOS sessions sold, Skool revenue Protect Calvin's time — he should be focused on content, sales, EOS sessions, speaking, and relationships, not chasing updates or re-explaining priorities Your 12-month goals Non-LinkedIn social followers: 1,000,000+ within 12 months LinkedIn followers: 250,000+ within 12 months EOS sessions: 50+ per year at $5,000/session Guest podcast appearances: 2+ per month Owned podcast episodes: 2+ per month Speaking engagements: 6+ per year, with 3+ outside the U.S. What you'll need Leadership: 5+ years leading teams, including direct accountability conversations when people miss targets Managed 7+ people at the same time Run weekly meetings where people reported numbers, not just updates Can lead and execute simultaneously — not one or the other Operations: 3+ years in ops, chief of staff, agency ops, creator ops, founder support, or growth ops Built and managed a weekly scorecard, KPI dashboard, or reporting system personally Managed 5+ active projects or workstreams simultaneously 2+ years working directly with a founder, creator, coach, or consultant AI and automation: Use AI tools daily — Claude, ChatGPT, Zapier, n8n, or similar Built at least 3 real workflows, automations, SOPs, or reporting systems using these tools Can explain what you built, why it mattered, and what improved Not learning AI here — already using it and getting better every week Growth and content awareness — you don't need to be the expert, but you need to understand enough to lead the people doing the work: Real familiarity with at least 3 of: social media
