ABOUT US

Anavah Talent is a U.S.-based recruiting and staffing firm helping growing companies build high-performing remote teams across the Philippines, Latin America, and South Africa.

We're looking for a

Client Operations Lead to own the client experience and keep hiring engagements moving from kickoff through successful placement.

This is a high-ownership role combining

client success, project management, recruitment delivery, and operations . You'll work closely with leadership and our recruiting team while helping build the systems and processes that allow us to scale.

ABOUT YOU

You're a strong relationship builder and organized operator who enjoys keeping complex projects on track.

You

Build client trust through proactive communication and follow-through

Manage multiple projects and deadlines without losing attention to detail

Solve problems before they become escalations

Coordinate effectively across teams without direct authority

Enjoy improving processes and building systems

Communicate exceptionally well in written and spoken English

Thrive in fast-moving, remote startup environments

TYPICAL DAY

You'll lead client kickoffs, monitor hiring pipelines, coordinate with recruiters, facilitate interview scheduling and feedback, follow up on outstanding actions, and provide proactive client updates.

You'll also improve delivery workflows, build SOPs, maintain dashboards, and identify opportunities to reduce manual work through better processes and automation.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Client Success & Recruitment Delivery

Own day-to-day client relationships throughout active searches

Lead client kickoffs and establish communication cadence and expectations

Coordinate with recruiters to keep searches moving

Monitor pipelines, interviews, feedback, offers, and onboarding

Identify bottlenecks and escalate risks proactively

Keep clients informed of progress, challenges, and next steps

Operations & Process Improvement

Improve client onboarding and recruitment delivery workflows

Create and maintain SOPs and documentation

Track delivery KPIs and maintain reporting dashboards

Identify opportunities for automation and efficiency

Maintain accurate information across systems such as Monday.com and Workable

WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

Within your first 90 days, you'll be expected to

Independently manage client communication

Keep active hiring projects organized and moving

Build strong relationships with clients and internal teams

Proactively identify and resolve delivery bottlenecks

Become proficient in Anavah's systems and recruitment process

Implement meaningful improvements to our client delivery workflows

If you enjoy building relationships, solving operational problems, and creating structure in a fast-growing company, we'd love to meet you.
