LocationRemote

Time ZoneUS Time Zones (EST–PST)

ROLE OVERVIEW

We are seeking an experienced and detail-oriented Sales Administrator to support a high-performing sales organization. This role is ideal for a proactive professional with a strong operational mindset who thrives behind the scenes — keeping systems clean, processes running smoothly, and the sales team focused on closing. Experience in the education sector is a strong advantage.

Key Responsibilities

  • Manage and maintain the CRM (HubSpot), ensuring data accuracy, pipeline hygiene, and up-to-date records
  • Support the sales team with administrative tasks including contract preparation, proposals, and sales documentation
  • Track and report on key sales metrics, pipeline activity, and performance dashboards
  • Coordinate scheduling, follow-ups, and communications between the sales team and prospects or clients
  • Assist in onboarding new clients and ensuring a smooth handoff from sales to delivery or account management
  • Manage inbound inquiries and route them appropriately within the sales team
  • Maintain and organize sales collateral, templates, and shared resources
  • Identify and flag process inefficiencies and support continuous improvement initiatives
  • Liaise with marketing, operations, and finance teams to support the full sales cycle

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

  • 3+ years of experience in a Sales Administrator or Sales Manager role
  • Hands-on experience with HubSpot CRM — managing pipelines, workflows, reporting, and data integrity
  • Prior remote work experience with strong fluency in remote collaboration tools (Slack, Zoom, Google Workspace, or similar); candidates without prior remote experience will not be considered
  • Experience working with US or UK-based companies or customers
  • Excellent organizational skills with strong attention to detail and the ability to manage multiple priorities
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills
  • Comfortable working independently in a fast-paced, distributed environment

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

  • Background in or exposure to the education sector (EdTech, training, e-learning, or academic services)
  • Experience supporting or managing a sales team in a senior or lead capacity
  • Familiarity with sales enablement tools, proposal software, or document management platforms
  • Exposure to CRM administration, workflow automation, or sales operations functions

TOOLS & TECHNOLOGY

  • HubSpot CRM (required)
  • Google Workspace or Microsoft 365
  • Slack, Zoom, and task management tools (Asana, Monday.com http://Monday.com, or similar)
  • VoIP and communication platforms
  • Document and proposal tools
