About Huzzle

At Huzzle, we connect exceptional talent with top opportunities at leading companies across the UK, US, Canada, Europe, and Australia. Our clients include fast-growing startups, digital agencies, SaaS companies, and tech-enabled businesses across industries such as MarTech, FinTech, EdTech, and eCommerce. Unlike traditional outsourcing agencies, we place you directly with innovative companies where you become a valued member of the team.

About the Company

Our client is an elite performance marketing partner specializing in the Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) health and wellness space. Operating as a senior-led growth studio, the team functions as an embedded "in-house" partner for high-growth startups and mid-market brands. They focus on scaling customer acquisition through strategic media buying on Google, Meta, and YouTube, with a proven track record of helping brands achieve 7- to 9-figure scale. The studio is defined by an ownership mentality, prioritizing rapid execution, data-driven attribution, and full-funnel optimization over traditional agency overhead.

The team consists of seasoned brand-side operators who have successfully navigated complex compliance challenges and multi-million dollar acquisitions. They foster a high-impact, collaborative environment where results are measured by lower acquisition costs and scalable growth. Joining this fast-moving company offers the opportunity to work alongside elite marketers who value speed, technical excellence, and measurable impact in the competitive eCommerce landscape.

Job Type

Full-time

Job Summary

This team is a performance-driven growth marketing studio helping high-growth DTC brands scale profitably. As the first AI Implementation Specialist, you will be the architect of the company’s internal efficiency. Moving beyond founder-led execution, you will design, ship, and manage the automated systems that allow the team to scale. This is a role for a builder who understands that the future of agency work isn't just about human effort—it’s about leveraging AI and sophisticated workflows to outperform the competition.

Key Responsibilities

Build and ship custom automations using Make.com and AI models (Claude/Claude Code) to streamline high-impact workflows.

Audit existing manual processes across paid media, reporting, and creative to identify automation opportunities.

Develop internal tools and lightweight apps that improve team speed and data accuracy.

Integrate data across platforms including Shopify, Google Ads, Meta, and Looker Studio via APIs and webhooks.

Document all new systems and lead internal training to ensure 100% adoption and usage.

Proactively research and recommend emerging AI tools to keep the team at the cutting edge of productivity.

Proven track record of building and shipping real-world automations and functional tools.

Advanced hands-on experience with Make.com and LLMs (Claude, Claude Code, or similar).

Proficiency in Google Sheets (advanced formulas/logic), APIs, and webhooks.

Basic coding ability in Python or JavaScript to bridge gaps between platforms.

Outcome-driven mindset: You measure success by hours saved and measurable efficiency gains.

Ability to operate autonomously, moving from a high-level idea to a working system with minimal direction.

Strong communication skills to teach and document workflows for non-technical team members.

Nice to have

Familiarity with Paid Media workflows (Google Ads, Meta Ads, and attribution models).

Experience with Shopify data integrations or plugin development.

Proficiency with Cursor or similar AI-integrated development environments.

Experience utilizing GitHub for version control.

🌎 Fully remote work environment (EST hours)

🚀 Career growth path into AI leadership

📈 Opportunity to shape the foundational systems of a scaling studio

🎯 Future potential for performance-based commissions/bonuses

🧠 High-autonomy environment for proactive builders
