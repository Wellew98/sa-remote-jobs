Read the posting in full · 121 words

Our client in the UK is looking for a Stock Controller, they are looking for someone who has dealt with product/stock in the past. The role also requires a lot of reconciliation between systems and investigation of discrepancies and experience from across finance – sales invoices and purchase invoices.

Key Responsibilities

Reconciliation of stock across all locations between warehouse system and ERP (SAP ByDesign) to help drive availability.

Investigation and resolution of stock discrepancies, and implementing process improvements to avoid repeat issues.

Management of all stock deliveries into the ERP.

Monitoring stock valuation and investigating discrepancies.

Managing stock counts and preparing year‑end files for auditors.

Developing and improving stock management processes.

Ownership of specific general ledger accounts in relation to stock.
