Through industry-leading coaching programs, proven business development systems, and long-term advisory partnerships, the company empowers commercial real estate professionals to increase revenue, strengthen pipelines, and achieve sustainable growth. As demand for its coaching programs continues to grow, the company is seeking a high-performing sales professional who can both convert qualified opportunities and proactively generate new business. Why This Role Exists The business continues to attract strong inbound interest from commercial real estate professionals seeking coaching and business growth solutions. To capitalize on this demand and accelerate growth, the company is looking for a Closer / Account Executive who can confidently guide prospects through the sales process while also creating additional pipeline opportunities. This role exists to drive revenue growth by combining consultative high-ticket closing with proactive business development activities. About the Role We are looking for a commercially driven Closer / Account Executive who thrives in both closing and prospecting environments. This role combines high-ticket consultative sales with business development responsibilities. You will conduct discovery and sales consultations with qualified prospects, present coaching programs, overcome objections, and guide prospects through the enrollment process.
