We're looking for a Senior

Cold Caller to own outbound phone-based outreach for a newly founded private equity firm actively sourcing acquisition targets across IT managed services, marketing agencies, BPO, accounting and finance, logistics, SaaS, software development, and recruiting. You will make a high volume of outbound calls to business owners and company leaders to identify potential acquisition interest, qualify conversations, and book meetings for the deal team.

This is a pure cold calling role. You will be dialing business owners and operators all day, opening conversations from cold, and moving qualified interest forward to the next step. The work is repetitive, the rejection rate is high, and results come from consistent volume and a professional, confident phone manner. If you are resilient, disciplined, and motivated by a clear and measurable output, this role is a strong fit.

What You'll Work On

High-Volume Outbound Cold Calling

Make high-volume outbound calls to business owners and company leaders across target verticals daily

Open cold conversations professionally and pivot quickly to qualify acquisition interest

Handle objections confidently and keep conversations moving toward a booked meeting or callback

Maintain a consistent, high call volume throughout the full working day without losing energy or quality

Lead Qualification and Pipeline Management

Qualify prospects based on defined criteria including business type, revenue range, ownership structure, and interest level

Log all call activity, outcomes, and notes accurately in the CRM after every interaction

Book qualified meetings for the deal team with full context and qualification notes

Follow up on warm contacts and callbacks consistently

Reporting and Communication

Meet daily and weekly call volume targets consistently

Report on call activity, connect rates, and meetings booked regularly

Flag patterns in objections or prospect feedback that could improve targeting or messaging

What You Bring

Senior-level cold calling experience with a track record of booking meetings or qualified conversations from cold outreach

Confident, clear, and professional phone presence with the ability to engage a business owner or executive from a cold call

Comfortable with high rejection rates and able to reset quickly between calls without losing momentum

Fluent, professional spoken English; this is a phone-first role and your voice is the product

Organized with clean CRM habits and accurate call logging

Reliable, self-directed, and disciplined with the ability to hit daily activity targets independently

Quiet, professional calling environment and reliable high-speed internet

Nice to Have

Experience cold calling in a B2B context including business owners, executives, or operators

Background in deal sourcing, business development, or financial services outreach

Familiarity with the verticals being targeted including IT services, marketing agencies, BPO, accounting, logistics, SaaS, or recruiting

How to Apply

Please include

Your

updated resume

A short Loom video (1 to 2 minutes) introducing yourself, describing your cold calling experience, and giving an example of how you handle a business owner who immediately says they are not interested in selling

Only candidates who submit a Loom video will be moved to the next step of the hiring process.

If you are resilient, disciplined, and comfortable making a high volume of cold calls to business owners every day, this role gives you the volume, the mission, and the direct impact to do your best work.
