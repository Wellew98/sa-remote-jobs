Our client is a US-based ABA therapy provider that is looking for a reliable, detail-focused Bookkeeper to keep the financial records of a group of related companies accurate and up to date. You’ll begin with one company and take on more as the role grows.

Day-to-day, you’ll process payments and deposits, reconcile bank accounts and credit cards, record payroll-related entries (this role does not process payroll), handle expense claims and collections, keep intercompany balances accurate, and support the month-end close. You’ll work in QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise and Excel, and you’ll need a sound understanding of how transactions should be recorded, not just entered.

This is a fully remote role for someone who enjoys accuracy, takes ownership of their numbers, and is comfortable working independently with a US-based team.

PLEASE NOTE

Working hours

Monday to Friday, 16:00–01:00 SAST (subject to US daylight saving changes)

Public Holidays

You will be required to work on both South African and U.S. public holidays (compensation for SA public holidays in accordance with the BCEA).

Internet Requirements

A fixed fibre line with a minimum speed of 25 Mbps (upload & download) and the ability to support a wired Ethernet connection is mandatory. Applicants without a fixed fibre line cannot be considered.

Power Backup

A reliable power backup solution is required to manage load shedding and power outages. Applicants without a power backup cannot be considered.

Work Environment

This is a

fully remote working role and you must be based in South Africa.

Responsibilities

Maintain the books and records for a group of related companies, starting with one entity and taking on more as the role grows.

Process 100 payments and 200 deposits accurately in QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise.

Reconcile 5 bank accounts and 3 credit cards and investigate and resolve any discrepancies.

Enter payroll-related bookkeeping entries. This role does not process payroll.

Process and record expense claims.

Follow up on outstanding payments owed to the business (collections).

Support the month-end close by making sure the month’s records are complete and accurate.

Record insurance-related transactions correctly, including deciding whether items are expenses or prepaid expenses.

Keep intercompany balances accurate where the companies owe each other.

Use Excel to track, check and report on your work.

Import bank data into the accounting system while automatic bank feeds are being set up.

What we’re looking for

A solid understanding of bookkeeping, including preparing bank reconciliations and investigating discrepancies.

Hands-on experience with QuickBooks Desktop, ideally the Enterprise edition.

Proficiency in Excel and Word.

Accuracy and good organisation when working with high volumes of transactions.

Clear written English for working with a US-based team.

The discipline to work independently in a remote environment.

If you are not contacted within 14 working days, please consider your application unsuccessful.
