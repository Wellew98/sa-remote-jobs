ABOUT THE ROLE

Anavah Talent is a fast-growing U.S.-based recruiting and staffing firm helping companies build exceptional remote teams across the Philippines, Latin America, and South Africa.

We're looking for an Operations Specialist to run the operational infrastructure behind client delivery, finance, contractor operations, and internal business processes. This is a highly execution-oriented role for someone organized, detail-oriented, comfortable across multiple systems, and able to keep recurring processes moving without constant supervision. You'll work closely with both the Client Operations Manager and Finance Specialist, supporting them with execution, coordination, reporting, documentation, system maintenance, and follow-through. You also own the contractor experience from onboarding through offboarding and ensuring every contractor feels supported, informed, and valued.

ABOUT YOU

Are you the person who loves crossing items off a checklist and keeping everything in perfect order? Do you take pride in accurate data entry and well-organized folders? You make sure things don't fall through the cracks. You're organized without needing excessive structure from others, and you can manage recurring deadlines while handling unexpected requests.

You also genuinely enjoy supporting people. You're the kind of person who remembers a contractor's anniversary, follows up on a PTO request, and checks in when someone's been quiet. You don't blindly follow a process when something looks wrong, you investigate, ask questions, and escalate appropriately. You naturally look for ways to make repetitive work simpler, clearer, or more automated. You enjoy supporting multiple people and aren't easily flustered by shifting priorities.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Contractor Engagement & Lifecycle Ownership

Serve as the primary non-payroll point of contact for contractors

Run scheduled 7/30/60/90-day and annual contractor check-ins

Coordinate contractor engagement initiatives — anniversaries, birthdays, recognition, and team moments

Track PTO, contractor changes, and lifecycle events in accurate records

Support contractor onboarding and offboarding (paperwork, accounts, access, first-week experience)

Maintain Independent Contractor Agreements, tax forms, background checks, and compliance docs

Flag contractor concerns, morale risks, or retention signals to the CEO before they escalate

Finance & Contractor Operations

Collect and organize contractor timesheets, invoices, reimbursements, bonuses, and adjustments

Prepare payroll and billing data for review; assist with reconciliation and discrepancy tracking

Maintain contractor masterfiles, billing trackers, and payment trackers

Track missing agreements, tax forms, background checks, PTO, and lifecycle records

Client & Delivery Operations Support

Maintain client and hiring project trackers in Monday.com — statuses, owners, deadlines, next actions

Track hiring milestones, interviews, feedback, offers, and start dates

Monitor outstanding client and internal action items; flag stalled or overdue work

Coordinate scheduling, documentation, and follow-ups

Support client onboarding, post-placement workflows, and recurring client reporting

Systems & Data Management

Maintain Monday.com boards, Google Sheets, Workable, and reporting dashboards

Keep shared Google Drive files organized and consistent across systems

Identify duplicate, missing, outdated, or inconsistent information

Assist with data cleanup and system migrations

Maintain templates, checklists, and operational documentation

Process, Documentation & Reporting

Maintain SOPs and process documentation; create and update checklists and templates

Document recurring workflows and track process changes

Identify gaps where ownership or documentation is unclear

Prepare recurring operational reports and update dashboards and KPI trackers

Compile information across multiple systems, prepare meeting agendas, and follow up on action items

Test new workflows, recommend practical improvements, and support special projects

3+ years in Operations, Business Administration, HR Operations, Recruitment Operations, or Customer Operations, with strong organizational and follow-through skills

Strong spreadsheet and data-management skills with excellent attention to detail

Comfortable working across multiple systems and managing recurring deadlines independently in a fast-paced remote environment

Strong problem-solving and critical-thinking skills — notices when something looks wrong and investigates rather than blindly processing

Strong written English and professional communication; high integrity and discretion with financial and personal data; warm and people-oriented in contractor interactions

Preferred Requirements

Experience supporting a U.S.-based company, startup, or professional services firm

Experience with contractor engagement, HR administration, or people operations

Experience with Monday.com, Google Sheets, Workable, HubSpot, QuickBooks, Xero, Wise, or Stripe

Experience supporting payroll, billing, recruiting, HR, or contractor operations

Experience creating or maintaining SOPs and using automation/AI tools (Zapier, Make, Google Apps Script)
