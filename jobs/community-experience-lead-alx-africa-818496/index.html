About ALX Africa

ALX Africa, a non-profit organisation under the ALX Foundation, is dedicated to unlocking the potential of Africa's digital future. Formerly part of Sand Tech Holdings, we've embarked on an independent journey to provide world-class tech skills training and career acceleration programmes. Our mission is to bridge the digital divide, upskill and re-skill talent, and create a generation of innovative leaders. By 2030, we aim to empower 2 million Africans to secure sustainable tech careers.

With hubs in 8 cities across Africa and counting, we provide safe access to quality learning and a dedicated network of expert instructors. Our innovative programmes equip learners with the practical skills and knowledge needed to succeed in today's rapidly evolving tech industry. Through a combination of rigorous coursework, industry partnerships, and hands-on projects, we prepare our students for in-demand roles in software engineering, data science, and cybersecurity.

We achieve this by

Providing young professionals with access to the most in-demand tech skills that will power the future.

Empowering the next generation of technology innovators, entrepreneurs, and business leaders through challenging, real-world coursework.

Building a lifelong, impactful Community of tech professionals that support them at all stages of their career journey.

Role Summary

As the Community Experience Lead, you will own the long-term vision and strategy for Community across ALX, ensuring it is embedded throughout the entire talent journey, from selection and recruitment through learning, transition, and lifelong alumni engagement. You will lead a small, high-performing team, currently a Community Program Specialist and a Community Operations Associate, setting direction, unblocking execution, and developing their capability. You will represent

Community experience at the leadership table

aligning goals with broader organisational priorities, managing relationships with cross-functional stakeholders (Brand, Alumni Relations, Product, Regional teams, and more), and reporting on Community performance and impact to drive continued investment in the function.

Specific Responsibilities

Strategy & Vision

Define

and drive the long-term Community Experience strategy, ensuring it stays member-led, city-driven, and embedded across the full talent lifecycle, from marketing to lifelong engagement.

Set

the roadmap for major strategic initiatives (e.g. unifying the Learner and Alumni community, engagement tracking, ambassador programs, resource and knowledge hubs) and prioritise investment across them.

Translate

organisational goals into a clear Community roadmap, and adjust strategy as the business, budget, and community needs evolve .

Champion

what Community is and isn't: a decentralised, member-led function that advises, curates, and enables rather than dictates, and that lives throughout the talent journey rather than as a stand-alone function.

Team Leadership & People Management

Manage and develop the Community Program Specialist and Community Operations Associate, setting clear goals, giving regular feedback, and supporting their growth.

Model

and reinforce a high-performance, high-accountability team culture grounded in data and continuous improvement.

Conduct

regular 1:1s and performance reviews, identify development and growth opportunities, and manage performance issues directly and fairly.

Recruit

, onboard, and scale the Community team as the function grows, defining roles and hiring needs in line with the strategy.

Unblock

the team's day-to-day execution: resolving cross-functional dependencies, prioritisation conflicts, and resourcing gaps before they stall delivery.

Extend

leadership beyond direct reports by supporting and coaching City-based Community Managers and Ambassadors on standards, tools, and ways of working.

Stakeholder Management

Act as the primary point of contact and central voice for Community-related initiatives across the organisation.

Build

and maintain strong relationships with cross-functional teams, including Loyalty & Rewards, Alumni Relations, Regional/City teams, Brand, Product, and Learning Delivery, aligning on shared goals and initiatives.

Represent

the Community function in leadership and planning conversations, securing buy-in and resourcing for Community priorities.

Proactively

surface and manage risks such as community outreach fatigue, ensuring member-facing communication stays coordinated across teams.

Negotiate

priorities and trade-offs with other functions when their goals compete for member attention, time, or Community team capacity.

Build

a network of relationships with Leads and Regional teams to keep the central strategy grounded in local, on-the-ground realities.

Reporting & Performance

Own reporting on Community engagement, member satisfaction, and program impact to senior stakeholders, using data to make the case for continued investment.

Track

team and program performance against goals, identifying trends and opportunities for continuous improvement.

Run regular

business reviews on Community performance, translating metrics into a clear narrative of impact, risk, and recommended next steps.

Own

the Community experience function's budget: forecasting costs, tracking spend against plan, and prioritising investment when resources are constrained.

Benchmark

ALX's Community engagement and practices against comparable organisations, feeding external insight back into strategy.

Skill Requirements-Must Have

5+ years of experience in community management, program or operations management, or a related field, with at least 2 years in a people-management or team-lead capacity, ideally in education, tech, or a membership-based organisation.

Proven experience

setting strategy and translating it into an executable roadmap across a small team.

Track record

of managing and developing direct reports, including goal-setting, feedback, and coaching.

Strong

stakeholder management skills, with experience influencing cross-functional partners and senior leadership without direct authority.

Comfortable

owning, reporting, and KPIs: defining metrics, tracking performance, and communicating impact to non-technical audiences.

Hands-on

understanding of online community platforms (e.g. Discourse, Circle, Slack, Discord, or similar) and member communications (newsletters, email tools such as Mailchimp or HubSpot).

Excellent

written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to represent Community's voice and priorities across the organisation.

Ability

to operate at both the strategic and operational levels, moving comfortably between long-term planning and day-to-day problem solving.

Ability

to work with cross-functional teams across multiple locations and time zones, including virtually.

Skill Requirements - Nice To Have

Passion for community growth, collaboration, and creating meaningful connections.

Experience

building or scaling a community-led growth, ambassador, or referral program.

Experience with

data visualization or engagement analytics tools (e.g. Tableau, Power BI, or similar).

Background in budget management or making the business case for program investment.

Knowledge

of behavioural psychology or engagement strategies to improve retention and participation.

Experience

operating in a multi-market, distributed organisation.

Employment Type

This role is a Full-time position.

As this is a fully remote role, the successful applicant should be highly self-motivated, organized, and comfortable working in a digital-first environment. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams using tools such as Zoom, Slack, and Gmail. Strong communication is essential, and success in this role will depend on your ability to stay connected, work proactively, and take initiative.
