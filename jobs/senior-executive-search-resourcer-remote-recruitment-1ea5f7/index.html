Senior Executive Search Resourcer

Remote Recruitment Our UK-based client is hiring a full-time, remote Senior Executive Search Resourcer based in South Africa. Full-time Admin & Virtual Assistance Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

Anyone can find candidates. Finding the one candidate a board will fight to hire - that is a different profession. Job Overview Our UK-based client is hiring a full-time, remote Senior Executive Search Resourcer based in South Africa. You will drive the research engine behind senior-level searches: mapping markets, identifying exceptional people and opening conversations that headhunters build placements on. This is a long-term role on UK business hours. About Our Client The client is a UK search and recruitment business operating at the senior end of the market, where quality of research decides the outcome. They want a resourcer who thinks like a consultant: rigorous, discreet and genuinely curious about what makes a great leader. Key Responsibilities - Map target markets, companies and talent pools for each search mandate - Identify and qualify senior candidates through research and headhunting outreach - Build accurate longlists with evidence, not guesswork - Conduct first-stage conversations to test fit, appetite and availability - Maintain immaculate records of outreach, responses and market intelligence - Prepare candidate summaries and market insight for consultants and clients - Protect confidentiality on sensitive searches at all times What You Will Be Doing Day-to-Day Your day blends detective work and conversation. Mornings are for research: mining LinkedIn, databases and industry sources to build target lists that actually reflect the brief. Afternoons are for outreach - crafted messages and calls that senior people respond to because they are relevant and respectful. You will debrief with consultants on progress, sharpen search strategies based on what the market says, and keep every mandate moving with visible momentum. This suits a South African professional with search or recruitment research experience who enjoys the hunt, writes crisp summaries, and understands that at senior level, reputation is everything. If you are based in South Africa with excellent English and a rigorous research mindset, this is a stable remote career in executive search with a UK employer that prizes quality. Apply today.
