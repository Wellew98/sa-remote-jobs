Role Overview

We are seeking a senior, high-agency Head of Finance and Operations to serve as the founder's right hand and lead our finance delivery engine. Sitting at the intersection of fractional CFO advisory, accounting operations, team management, and financial software development, you will establish firm-wide delivery standards, manage our multidisciplinary delivery squad (FP&A, accounting, and engineering), serve as senior finance advisor on select high-growth accounts, and help build proprietary internal tools and software.

This is an executive operating role for someone who has previously built, managed, and scaled finance delivery at an outsourced accounting firm, finance consulting practice, or specialized BPO. The ideal candidate possesses deep accounting and FP&A mastery—capable of building complex 3-statement models, leading board-level discussions, and auditing close packets line-by-line while leveraging AI tools ( Claude Code ) and internal engineering resources to automate workflows and scale practice operations.

Key Responsibilities

1. Delivery Standards & Quality Assurance

- Delivery Playbook Architecture: Author and maintain our core operational playbook—establishing standardized financial model architectures, month-end close checklists, reconciliation protocols, and chart-of-accounts conventions tailored by business model (e.g., DTC, eCommerce, B2B SaaS).

- Quality Control Program: Enforce a rigorous review program where every financial model, 13-week cash flow forecast, month-end close packet, and lender package undergoes documented line-by-line review before reaching client founders.

- Standardized Deliverable Templates: Design and maintain master templates for recurring client deliverables, including Budget vs. Actual (BVA) analysis, unit economics dashboards, board decks, and debt/equity fundraising packages.

2. Team Leadership & Client Delivery Management

- Squad Management: Direct day-to-day operations across our FP&A analysts, staff accountants, and software engineers—setting priorities, allocating client workloads, and running weekly 1:1 check-ins.

- Performance KPI Tracking: Define and monitor team performance metrics, including on-time close rates, review finding frequencies, and deliverable turnaround times.

- Team Expansion: Lead hiring, onboarding, and training as the finance delivery team grows across remote and offshore regions.

3. Client Finance Leadership (Fractional CFO)

- Founder Advisory: Serve as fractional CFO or Head of Finance for select client accounts, working directly alongside founders, executive teams, and boards.

- Capital & Transaction Readiness: Prepare client companies for capital raises—building dynamic fundraising models, managing data rooms, assembling lender packages (SBA, asset-based lending, venture debt), and leading diligence reviews.

- Strategic Financial Modeling: Construct decision models for key founder inflection points, including hiring plans, pricing restructures, channel expansions, capital allocation, and runway extension strategies.

4. Systems, AI Workflows & Product Ownership

- Client Tech Stack Administration: Oversee client software stacks and integrations across QuickBooks Online, Xero, Shopify, Amazon, Stripe, Ramp, and payroll engines.

- Product Ownership: Act as product owner for our internal software tools and client portal—partnering with engineering from technical requirement gathering through production rollout.

- AI-Assisted Operations: Leverage AI development platforms ( Claude Code ) daily to build working prototypes, custom automation scripts, internal mockups, and data workflows.

5. Practice Operations & Margin Optimization

- Utilization & Scope Governance: Track client utilization, scope parameters, and gross margins—flagging scope creep proactively before unbilled work occurs.

- Onboarding & Security Protocols: Manage client onboarding workflows, service tier standards, and enterprise data security across all managed client accounts.

Required Skills & Qualifications

- Experience Level: 8+ years of progressive finance and accounting experience, including 3+ years leading delivery at an outsourced accounting firm, FP&A consultancy, BPO, or finance advisory practice.

- Executive Leadership Track Record: Proven experience serving as CFO, VP of Finance, or Head of Finance (in-house or fractional)—working directly with founders, boards, venture investors, and lenders.

- Deep Accounting Technicals: Advanced mastery of month-end close procedures, accrual accounting, complex reconciliations, ASC 606 revenue recognition, inventory accounting, and COGS structures.

- Advanced FP&A & Modeling: Expert-level proficiency in Excel and Google Sheets—building driver-based 3-statement financial models, 13-week cash flow forecasts, unit economic models, and fundraising models from scratch.

- AI Tool Fluency: Practical fluency using AI tools ( Claude Code or similar LLM developer environments) for workflow automation, rapid prototyping, and financial scripting.

- Tech Stack Fluency: Direct, hands-on experience with QuickBooks Online, Xero, Shopify, Stripe, spend management platforms (Ramp/Brex), and cloud payroll engines.

- Communication & Presence: Exceptional written and spoken English with the executive presence required to advise venture-backed founders and corporate boards.

Preferred Qualifications

- Professional credential such as CPA, ACCA, CFA , or foundational experience within Big 4 / public accounting.

- Direct experience managing a delivery P&L, pricing service packages, or building software-enabled service models.

- Exposure to lender diligence processes (SBA loans, term debt, asset-based lending).

- Working knowledge of SQL or Python for financial data extraction and analytics.

Success Metrics (KPIs)

- On-Time Close & Deliverable Velocity: Maintaining a high percentage of on-time month-end closes and scheduled client reporting packages.

- Quality Assurance Integrity: Minimizing post-review findings and client-reported errors through strict QA playbook adherence.

- Account Gross Margin & Utilization: Optimizing team utilization rates and maintaining healthy gross margins across client accounts.

- Software & Automation Rollout: Converting manual, repetitive finance workflows into automated internal tools and engineering features.
