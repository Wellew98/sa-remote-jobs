Job Title: Campaign Manager (SMS & Email Marketing) Location: Remote (South Africa or LATAM) Employment Type: Full-Time Company Overview Our client is a growing marketing company specializing in SMS marketing campaigns targeting homeowners. With a lean, high-performing team, they are focused on streamlining campaign management processes, improving operational efficiency, and expanding into email marketing. This is an exciting opportunity to join a growing business and play a key role in scaling its marketing operations. Position Overview We are seeking a detail-oriented and analytical Campaign Manager to oversee the planning, execution, optimization, and reporting of SMS and email marketing campaigns. The ideal candidate is data-driven, highly organized, and passionate about improving campaign performance through continuous testing and optimization. You will work closely with leadership to manage campaign execution, monitor performance metrics, identify process improvements, and help automate workflows as the company continues to grow. Key Responsibilities Campaign Management - Plan, launch, and optimize SMS marketing campaigns targeting homeowners. - Assist in building and executing email marketing campaigns to support overall marketing objectives. - Ensure campaigns are delivered accurately and on schedule. Performance Analysis - Monitor campaign performance using key metrics such as: - Revenue Per Thousand Messages (RPM) - Conversion Rates - Click-Through Rates (CTR) - Open Rates - Analyze campaign results and provide actionable recommendations to improve performance. Workflow Optimization - Improve campaign planning, tracking, and reporting processes. - Utilize Google Sheets and automation tools to streamline campaign management. - Identify inefficiencies and implement scalable solutions. Reporting & Collaboration - Prepare regular campaign performance reports for leadership. - Collaborate with multiple stakeholders across the business. - Present data-driven insights and recommendations for future campaigns. Required Qualifications - 3+ years of experience in SMS marketing, campaign management, or digital marketing. - Fresh graduates with a Marketing degree will also be considered if they demonstrate strong aptitude in email marketing, campaign optimization, and independent work. - Experience using SMS and/or email marketing platforms. - Advanced proficiency with Google Sheets. - Strong analytical skills with the ability to interpret campaign data and optimize performance. - Excellent written and verbal English communication skills (C1 level or higher). - Highly organized with exceptional attention to detail. - Able to work independently with minimal supervision after onboarding. - Comfortable receiving constructive feedback and working with multiple leaders. Preferred Qualifications - Experience marketing to homeowners or within the real estate/home services industry. - Experience improving marketing workflows and automation. - Curious, proactive mindset with a passion for continuous optimization. - Experience managing both SMS and email marketing campaigns. Why Join This Opportunity? - Join a fast-growing marketing company with significant growth potential. - Work remotely from South Africa or LATAM. - Receive ongoing training and development. - Play a key role in building scalable marketing systems and automation. - Competitive compensation with opportunities for career growth. - Work in a collaborative, entrepreneurial environment where your ideas and impact will be valued.
