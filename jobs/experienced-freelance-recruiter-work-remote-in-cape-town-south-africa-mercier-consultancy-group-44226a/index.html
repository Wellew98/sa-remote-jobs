About Mercier Consultancy Group

Mercier Consultancy Group is a leading European recruitment and business process outsourcing firm with a distinguished reputation for connecting exceptional talent with world-class organisations across a wide range of industries. With a strong international footprint and a client portfolio spanning multiple continents, we pride ourselves on delivering recruitment solutions that are strategic, efficient, and people-first. At Mercier Consultancy Group, we believe that great recruitment starts with great recruiters — and that is precisely why we are expanding our freelance network in South Africa.

Job Overview

We are seeking a highly motivated and seasoned Experienced Freelance Recruiter to join our on-site team in Cape Town, South Africa, and play a pivotal role in our international talent acquisition efforts within the Recruitment & Staffing industry. This is an outstanding opportunity for a recruitment professional who thrives in a dynamic, results-driven environment and is ready to leverage their expertise on a flexible freelance basis. If you are looking for one of the most rewarding English-speaking jobs in South Africa, this Experienced Freelance Recruiter position in South Africa could be exactly what you have been searching for.

Key Responsibilities

Manage the full recruitment lifecycle, from sourcing and screening candidates to coordinating interviews and facilitating successful placements across a variety of industry sectors.

Develop and maintain a robust pipeline of qualified candidates through proactive headhunting, database searches, job boards, LinkedIn, and professional networking within the Recruitment & Staffing industry.

Partner closely with hiring managers and key stakeholders to gain a deep understanding of role requirements, team culture, and candidate profiles.

Craft compelling, SEO-optimised job advertisements in English to attract high-calibre talent for vacancies across multiple markets, including jobs in South Africa and international roles.

Conduct thorough candidate assessments, including competency-based interviews, skills evaluations, and cultural fit reviews, ensuring only the strongest profiles are presented to clients.

Build and nurture long-term relationships with both candidates and clients, establishing yourself as a trusted recruitment partner within the Recruitment & Staffing space.

Accurately maintain candidate records, activity logs, and placement data within the company's applicant tracking system (ATS), ensuring full compliance and data integrity.

Remain up to date with market trends, talent availability, and competitive intelligence within the Recruitment & Staffing industry to provide informed counsel to clients and internal teams.

Native or fluent English language proficiency, both spoken and written, is essential for this role — strong communication skills in English are non-negotiable.

A minimum of 2–4 years of proven experience as a recruiter, talent acquisition specialist, or staffing consultant, ideally within the Recruitment & Staffing industry.

Demonstrated ability to manage multiple open vacancies simultaneously while maintaining a high standard of candidate and client experience.

Proficiency with applicant tracking systems (ATS), LinkedIn Recruiter, and other leading sourcing platforms commonly used in the Recruitment & Staffing sector.

Excellent interpersonal skills with a consultative, candidate-first approach and the ability to build trust quickly with diverse stakeholders across different cultures and time zones.

Strong organisational skills, self-motivation, and the discipline to perform consistently and independently in a freelance capacity — this role is on-site in Cape Town, South Africa, and no relocation package is provided.

Life in South Africa

South Africa is one of the world's most breathtaking and culturally rich destinations, offering a quality of life that is truly difficult to match. Cape Town, nestled between the iconic Table Mountain and the sparkling Atlantic Ocean, is consistently ranked among the globe's most beautiful cities, and South Africa as a whole delivers an extraordinary blend of natural wonder, warm hospitality, and cosmopolitan energy. With over 300 days of glorious sunshine per year, a thriving arts and entertainment scene, and world-class beaches just minutes away, South Africa is not simply a place to work — it is a place to truly live.

South Africa's food culture is vibrant and diverse, ranging from celebrated fine-dining restaurants in Cape Town's trendy neighbourhoods to bustling local markets brimming with fresh produce and flavour. The cost of living in South Africa is highly competitive compared to many European and North American cities, allowing professionals to enjoy an elevated lifestyle without compromise. Whether you are exploring the Cape Winelands on a weekend, hiking a scenic trail, or immersing yourself in South Africa's rich multicultural heritage, every day here brings a new and rewarding experience that complements a fulfilling professional career.
