About the Role

We are looking for a detail-oriented and proactive Prior Authorizations Specialist to manage the initial assessment authorization process for a healthcare service provider.

In this role, you will be responsible for preparing and submitting authorization requests, verifying insurance requirements, tracking pending applications, following up with insurance providers, and ensuring approvals are accurately recorded.

The ideal candidate has experience in healthcare authorizations, insurance verification, or medical billing, with a strong understanding of payer requirements and authorization workflows. You should be comfortable working independently, managing multiple requests, meeting deadlines, and taking ownership of the authorization process from start to finish.

This role also requires someone with leadership qualities who can organize priorities, identify issues early, and support the wider team when needed

Key Responsibilities

Prior Authorization Management

Manage the full initial assessment authorization process, from preparing supporting documentation through submission, follow-up, and recording final approvals.

Review authorization requirements and ensure all necessary patient information and documentation are included before submission.

Submit authorization requests through insurance payer portals or via fax.

Monitor pending requests and follow up with insurance providers to avoid unnecessary delays.

Track authorization statuses, approval details, reference numbers, and expiration dates where applicable.

Identify incomplete, delayed, or denied authorization requests and ensure they are escalated appropriately.

Promptly hand over denied requests to the US office for further investigation and follow-up.

Insurance Verification & Payer Coordination

Verify insurance coverage and identify relevant authorization requirements.

Work with Medicaid and commercial insurance payers to confirm submission procedures and documentation requirements.

Navigate payer portals to submit requests, retrieve updates, and maintain accurate records.

Communicate professionally with insurance representatives to resolve routine authorization queries.

Ensure all authorization activities follow established procedures and payer guidelines.

Systems & Administrative Support

Maintain accurate authorization records within CentralReach or similar practice management systems.

Update patient records with submission details, approval outcomes, and relevant correspondence.

Monitor authorization trackers and outstanding requests to ensure nothing falls through the cracks.

Maintain organized documentation and ensure information is accessible to the relevant internal teams.

Support improvements to authorization workflows and administrative processes.

Team Coordination & Ownership

Take ownership of assigned authorization requests and ensure consistent follow-through.

Organize daily priorities according to deadlines, outstanding requests, and operational requirements.

Identify potential issues or bottlenecks and communicate them proactively.

Support colleagues with authorization-related queries and workflow coordination when needed.

Maintain clear communication with the US office regarding approvals, outstanding requests, and denied applications.

Minimum Requirements

Previous experience in healthcare authorizations, insurance verification, medical billing, or a related healthcare administrative role.

Practical experience managing initial assessment authorization requests from preparation through submission and follow-up.

Experience submitting authorization requests through payer portals and/or fax.

Familiarity with Medicaid and commercial insurance authorization processes.

Experience using CentralReach or a similar healthcare practice management system. CentralReach experience is strongly preferred.

Familiarity with GAMMIS and commercial insurance payer portals is advantageous.

Strong understanding of healthcare documentation and authorization workflows.

Excellent attention to detail and accurate recordkeeping.

Ability to manage multiple authorization requests, deadlines, and follow-ups simultaneously.

Strong written and verbal communication skills.

Ability to work independently, take ownership, and support team members when required.

Ability to handle confidential patient and healthcare information appropriately.

Required Software & Tools

Experience with the following systems and platforms is preferred:

CentralReach

For managing patient records, authorization information, and practice administration.

GAMMIS

Georgia Medicaid Management Information System for Medicaid-related authorization processes.

Commercial Insurance Payer Portals

For submitting and tracking authorization requests.

Fax Platforms

For submitting supporting documentation and authorization requests.

Microsoft Office / Google Workspace

For tracking requests, maintaining records, and managing administrative communication.

Experience with similar healthcare authorization and practice management platforms is welcomed.

Essential Skills & Competencies

Authorization Knowledge

Understanding of healthcare authorization processes, payer requirements, and supporting documentation.

Attention to Detail

Ability to review documentation carefully and ensure accurate submissions.

Organization

Strong ability to manage multiple requests, deadlines, and outstanding follow-ups.

Communication

Professional communication with insurance providers, internal teams, and the US office.

Problem-Solving

Ability to identify missing information, submission issues, and potential delays.

Accountability

Takes ownership of requests and ensures consistent follow-through.

Leadership

Able to organize workloads, support colleagues, and proactively identify workflow improvements.

Confidentiality

Understands the importance of protecting sensitive patient and healthcare information.

Important Considerations

This role requires more than general healthcare administration experience. The successful candidate must understand the practical process of obtaining prior authorizations, navigating payer portals, preparing documentation, tracking submissions, and following up on outstanding requests.

The position focuses primarily on initial assessment authorizations. Denied requests must be identified and promptly escalated to the US office rather than independently managed through the appeals process.

Candidates should be comfortable working in a structured, deadline-driven environment where accuracy, accountability, and proactive communication are essential.

What Success Looks Like

Initial assessment authorization requests are prepared and submitted accurately.

Supporting documentation is complete and meets payer requirements.

Pending requests are monitored and followed up on consistently.

Approvals and authorization details are accurately recorded.

Denied requests are identified and escalated promptly.

Patient authorization records remain organized and up to date.

The team receives timely updates and support.

Authorization workflows run efficiently, with minimal delays and avoidable errors.

If you have a strong background in healthcare authorizations, understand insurance payer processes, and enjoy taking ownership of detailed administrative workflows, we would love to hear from you.
