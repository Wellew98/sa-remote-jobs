Company Overview

An advertising and communications agency that helps brands tell their story and grow through strategy, creative and media. It operates alongside several related businesses that share a finance function. We are hiring a remote Bookkeeper/Back Office Admin to keep the accounting for all of them accurate and on schedule.

Role Overview

You will run day-to-day accounting operations remotely across several related businesses. That means keeping payables, receivables, reconciliations and recurring reports accurate and on schedule in QuickBooks Online, Stripe and the team's order management software.

You will report to the CFO and work closely with the owner, who receives weekly and monthly reports and sends invoicing requests. The role suits someone who is highly organized, keeps each business's books cleanly separate and is comfortable meeting fixed weekly and monthly reporting deadlines.

Duties and Responsibilities

  • Record customer payments received by card, ACH and online payment processors, and post them to the accounting system
  • Create and send customer invoices on request, including recurring monthly retainer invoices ahead of their due dates
  • Reconcile payment processor payouts to recorded payments weekly or as needed, recording processing fees correctly
  • Collect vendor invoices from email and mail, file them in each entity's shared AP folder and enter them in the accounting system
  • Record bill payments once approved and run, and move paid invoices to the paid folder
  • Send payment remittance details to vendors when required or when a vendor has many open invoices
  • Compare vendor statements against internal records and resolve any differences
  • Collect company credit card receipts from team members, upload them and categorize each expense
  • Reconcile bank and credit card accounts, matching transactions and categorizing expenses appropriately
  • Research the correct combined sales tax rate when a new jurisdiction is needed, and set it up in both the order management and accounting systems
  • Send the preliminary AP/AR report to the CFO every Wednesday
  • Send the AP/AR report to the CFO and the owner every Friday
  • Run the monthly Profit and Loss report ("This year to last month") after the 1st and send it to the CFO and the owner
  • Manage utility accounts for the businesses that have them
  • Collect and maintain vendor documentation such as ACH forms and W-9s
  • Keep each entity's records, folders and reports separate

Minimum Qualifications

Experience: 2 to 5 years of hands-on bookkeeping experience, including accounts payable, accounts receivable, and bank and credit card reconciliation

  • Working proficiency in QuickBooks Online
  • Comfortable managing several entities at once, keeping each one's records, folders and reports separate
  • Strong organization and the discipline to meet fixed weekly and monthly deadlines
  • Clear written communication for emailing reports and following up with vendors and teammates
  • Discretion with financial information

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with sales tax setup across multiple states
  • Familiarity with shop-management or order management software
  • Experience reconciling Stripe payouts
  • Background in a small-business, multi-entity or accounting-firm environment
