We are in search of a Project Manager to join our Project Management Department. The candidate will oversee project coordination and drive efficiency to ensure project success. From Concept to Completion: Lead Projects That Shine!

We consider candidates from anywhere in the world

Employment Type

  • Full-time 8.30 am - 5 pm, New York time.
  • Remote work.
  • B2B contract only.

Highlights

Training: Comprehensive product training lasting one week.

Responsibilities

  • Written and verbal communication with native speakers (B2C and B2B).
  • Execute and manage the complete project lifecycle from initiation to completion in ClickUp.
  • Maintaining customer data in HubSpot
  • Ensure high-quality project execution and adherence to deadlines.
  • Coordinate with clients to ensure their needs are met and resolve any issues.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including engineering, sales, and scheduling departments.

Requirements

  • Strong skills in maintaining and enhancing client relationships.
  • Engineering education OR strong engineering experience
  • Capable of identifying and resolving issues promptly and efficiently.
  • Strong client communication and relationship skills.
  • Capable of overcoming client objections.
  • Tech Savvy: Proficient with various software and tools.

Conditions

  • Combined compensation: an hourly rate plus performance-based commissions.
  • A wide range of performance-based bonuses.
  • Paid sick leave.
  • Paid vacation.
