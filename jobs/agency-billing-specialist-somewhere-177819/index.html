Agency Billing Specialist

Employment Details

  • Employment Type: Full-Time Remote Contractor
  • Work Arrangement: Fully Remote
  • Industry: Insurance / Risk Management / Insurtech
  • Benefits: Competitive PTO and paid holidays
  • Seniority Level: Mid-Level
  • Schedule: Full-Time

About the Role

A fast-growing, technology-driven insurance and risk management company is seeking an experienced and highly detail-oriented Agency Billing Specialist to own the full billing and cash management lifecycle across a complex portfolio of insurance programs.

This role is critical to ensuring accurate premium processing, carrier payments, invoicing, reconciliation, and premium trust management. The successful candidate will work cross-functionally with internal operations teams, Risk Advisors, clients, and insurance carriers to maintain billing accuracy and operational efficiency.

This is an ideal opportunity for someone who thrives in a structured, high-accountability environment and enjoys working within modern, tech-enabled operational systems. The role offers strong long-term growth potential within a rapidly scaling organization transforming the insurance industry through technology and AI.

Why This Role Is Exciting

  • Work with a modern, tech-forward insurance organization redefining traditional brokerage operations
  • Gain exposure to complex insurance billing workflows and specialty insurance programs
  • Be part of a fast-scaling environment with significant career growth opportunities
  • Collaborate with a high-performing, operationally excellent remote team
  • Develop expertise using advanced billing and payment management platforms

Key Responsibilities

Client Billing & Invoicing

  • Prepare and send client billing communications, including payment instructions and payment links through Ascend
  • Generate payment links for agency-billed policies, admitted carriers, and platform-related fees
  • Process invoices for:
  • Financed policies
  • Carrier installment plans
  • Flat fees
  • Performance fees
  • One-time invoices
  • Handle billing for:
  • Monetary endorsements
  • Tail policies
  • Policy cancellations
  • Issue and track direct-bill payment instructions to insureds, including carrier portal details
  • Maintain accurate billing records and policy billing data within internal systems
  • Upload carrier invoices and statements into WC Admin and Ascend platforms

Premium Trust Management

  • Manage and maintain the premium trust account with a high degree of accuracy
  • Reconcile carrier agency-billed statements to determine:
  • Net amounts payable to carriers
  • Commission amounts payable to the company
  • Review and approve outbound premium and commission payouts in Ascend prior to disbursement
  • Ensure all inbound and outbound cash flows are routed accurately and on time
  • Process return premiums for policy cancellations and premium adjustments
  • Upload and maintain all related return premium documentation within internal systems

Payment Reconciliation & Accounts Payable

  • Manually reconcile client payments received through:
  • Bill.com
  • Tipalti
  • Ramp
  • Justworks
  • Other payment platforms
  • Process accounts payable transactions and ensure accurate payment routing
  • Monitor payment statuses across multiple billing channels
  • Follow up with clients regarding outstanding invoices and overdue balances

Client & Carrier Billing Support

  • Respond to billing-related inquiries from clients and carriers
  • Provide:
  • Payment instructions
  • Billing summaries
  • Invoice copies
  • W-9 documentation
  • Remittance confirmations
  • Handle payment disputes and assist with resolution
  • Guide clients to access invoices and billing information through administrative dashboards

Required Hard Skills & Experience

  • Prior experience in:
  • Insurance billing
  • Brokerage operations
  • Accounting
  • Premium finance administration
  • Strong understanding of:
  • Premium trust accounting
  • Insurance payment workflows
  • Accounts payable processes
  • Experience using billing and payment platforms such as:
  • Ascend
  • Bill.com
  • Tipalti
  • Ramp
  • Similar accounting or payment systems
  • Strong reconciliation and financial tracking capabilities
  • Experience managing high transaction volumes across multiple accounts and carriers
  • Strong data entry and database management skills
  • Proficiency in remote collaboration and digital workflow systems

Required Soft Skills

  • Exceptional attention to detail
  • Strong organizational and time management skills
  • High level of accountability and ownership
  • Excellent written communication skills
  • Ability to work independently in a remote environment
  • Proactive and solution-oriented mindset
  • Ability to prioritize effectively in a fast-paced environment
  • Strong reliability and follow-through
  • Eagerness to learn and grow within a scaling organization

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience working within insurance brokerages or insurtech environments
  • Familiarity with agency-billed insurance processes
  • Exposure to carrier billing operations and premium trust reconciliation
  • Experience supporting high-growth or operationally complex businesses

What’s Offered

  • Stable, long-term remote opportunity
  • Competitive PTO and paid holidays
  • Training and detailed process documentation
  • Collaborative and supportive remote team environment
  • Opportunity for career advancement and expanded responsibilities
  • Exposure to modern insurance technology and AI-enabled operational systems
