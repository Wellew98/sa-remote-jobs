Project Analyst (Infrastructure Consultancy)

Employment TypePart-time, Independent Contractor

Work ModelRemote

Work HoursUS Business Hours alignment

Role Overview

We are seeking a sharp, proactive, and tech-native Project Analyst to help build and operate the operational backbone of a growing energy and infrastructure consultancy.

In this seat, you will support long-lifecycle capital projects—ranging from energy infrastructure, power generation, and natural gas midstream to powered land and commercial site developments. Operating as an AI-empowered operator rather than a traditional virtual assistant, you will set up automated data extraction flows, synthesize meeting insights, track multi-year project milestones, prepare client-facing status reports, and interface directly with executive stakeholders, local officials, commissioners, and judges.

Key Responsibilities

1. AI-Assisted Reporting, Automation & Data Extraction

- Workflow Automation: Configure and oversee automated ingestion workflows (using LLMs, email parser tools, and automated meeting assistants) to extract action items, key decisions, and critical risks from raw project communications.

- Executive Report Synthesis: Digest complex, multi-source inputs to package clean, concise, and structured weekly/monthly progress reports for leadership and external clients.

2. Stakeholder Engagement & Multi-Year Project Management

- Pipeline & Schedule Governance: Track project milestones, budgets, permitting deadlines, and deliverables across multi-year infrastructure lifecycles (14–36 month horizons).

- High-Level Stakeholder Liaison: Proactively follow up with municipal leaders, county judges, utility commissioners, local officials, and corporate clients to coordinate schedules, clear regulatory bottlenecks, and maintain project momentum.

3. Market Intelligence & Industry Research

- Regulatory & Market Tracking: Monitor industry news, FERC/PUC regulatory shifts, policy updates, and market trends across energy, natural gas, renewables, and powered land development.

- Executive Briefings: Prepare actionable brief summaries highlighting market shifts, land acquisition trends, or policy changes affecting client portfolios.

4. Client Meeting Operations & Financial Administration

- Meeting Synthesis: Attend client discovery calls and project working groups—leveraging AI scribes to capture technical notes, assign action items, and ensure zero details slip through the cracks.

- Operational Admin: Manage client invoicing schedules, billable hour tracking, expense logging, and core operational workflows.

Required Skills & Qualifications

- Tech & AI Native Capability: Advanced proficiency leveraging modern AI productivity tools (LLMs, automated meeting assistants, email data extraction flows, and custom task automations).

- Critical Thinking & Data Vetting: Strong analytical skills with the ability to review, verify, and structure raw information logically before presenting it—ensuring zero reliance on unverified "robot outputs."

- Flawless Communication & High EQ: Exceptional written and spoken English with the executive presence to speak directly with C-suite clients, public officials, and project partners.

- Project Management Aptitude: Meticulous organizational skills with proven ability to track multi-variable, long-term project schedules.

- Remote Independence: High agency, self-direction, and personal accountability in a remote environment.

Preferred Qualifications

- GIS & Spatial Literacy: Basic familiarity with Geographic Information Systems (GIS), spatial mapping tools, or land survey analysis.

- Domain Knowledge: Direct background or strong functional interest in energy infrastructure, power generation, natural gas midstream, renewables, or commercial real estate site development .

Success Metrics (KPIs)

- Reporting Velocity & Quality: Delivering error-free, AI-assisted executive progress reports on time every week.

- Stakeholder Response Cadence: Resolving scheduling bottlenecks and maintaining clear communication cadences with public officials and clients.

- Action Item Tracking: 100% resolution tracking of call-derived action items and meeting deliverables.
