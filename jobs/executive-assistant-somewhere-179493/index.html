About the company

We are a fast-growing, science-backed consumer wellness brand. We sell direct to consumers, on online marketplaces, and in select retailers. We’re a small team building a brand people love, and we’re looking for someone to help our founder and CEO make the most of her time.

The role

You’ll be the founder’s right hand, owning her calendar, inbox, and follow-through so she can focus on growth, fundraising, and leadership. This is a high-trust role for someone who is proactive, highly organized, and comfortable working closely with a founder in a fast-moving startup. You’ll work remotely and need to overlap several hours a day with US Pacific Time.

What you’ll do

  • Manage the CEO’s calendar end to end: scheduling, rescheduling, prioritizing, and protecting focus time across time zones
  • Triage her inbox, draft replies, and flag what needs her attention
  • Prep her for meetings with agendas, briefing notes, and relevant docs, then capture notes, action items, and owners afterward
  • Track follow-ups and deadlines and make sure nothing falls through the cracks
  • Support investor and board work: scheduling, investor update prep, data room organization, and meeting logistics
  • Coordinate travel and events, including itineraries and expenses
  • Keep internal docs, trackers, and project boards organized and current
  • Act as a point of contact for partners, vendors, and investors on the CEO’s behalf
  • Take on special projects and research as needed

What we’re looking for

  • 3+ years supporting a founder, CEO, or senior executive, ideally at a startup or consumer brand
  • Excellent written and spoken English, with a polished, warm communication style
  • Very organized and detail-oriented; you anticipate needs before you’re asked
  • Sound judgment and discretion with confidential information
  • Comfortable with ambiguity and shifting priorities
  • Fluent in Google Workspace, Slack, and Notion (or able to learn quickly)
  • Able to reliably work a schedule that overlaps with US Pacific Time
  • Experience in CPG, wellness, or DTC ecommerce
  • Exposure to fundraising, investor relations, or board support
  • Experience with AI tools for notetaking, drafting, and research
