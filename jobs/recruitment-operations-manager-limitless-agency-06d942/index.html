We need a hands-on recruiting lead who can interview, headhunt and fill roles every day, and run the process and the team that makes it all faster.

The opportunity

Limitless Talent places closers, setters, client success managers, media buyers and marketing managers for the agency owners in our programs, and hires for Limitless itself. Demand is already there: more than a dozen paid searches are open right now. We need someone who can carry real recruiting work and put a system around it.

About Limitless

Limitless Agency helps digital marketing agency owners build businesses that scale and sell. Our core program, Limitless Black Card, combines one-on-one strategy with the founders, implementation support, weekly group coaching and a dedicated account team. We launched Limitless Talent in September 2026 so our clients could hire A-players fast, and it's become one of our fastest-growing divisions.

The role

This is a player-coach seat. Most of your week is real recruiting: screening, interviewing, headhunting and closing candidates for client and internal roles. You also own how recruiting runs: the pipeline, the process, capacity, client updates and the weekly numbers, and you lead the recruiters who work alongside you.

No task is beneath this role. You'll post jobs, chase no-shows and update Workable yourself when that's what it takes.

What you will do

Hands-on recruiting (every day)

Run first-round screens and interviews for client and Limitless roles.

Headhunt every week on LinkedIn Recruiter and other channels. Don't wait for inbound.

Write and post job ads, review applicants and keep candidates moving.

Present shortlists and one-page candidate profiles, and close candidates through to offer and start date.

Fill Limitless's own sales, client success and recruiting roles with a weekly screening quota.

Delivery and speed

Get every new search live within 48 hours of the client's hiring alignment call.

Deliver a first qualified shortlist within 10 business days (sooner for sales roles).

Own the search queue: set capacity per recruiter, sequence new searches and give clients honest timelines.

Pipeline and process

Run Workable like a sales pipeline: every candidate in the right stage, every interview logged as held, no-show, pass or fail within 24 hours.

Standardize 20 to 25 minute first-round screens with pass/fail scorecards.

Write and maintain recruiting SOPs, interview guides and job description templates.

Use AI and automation to cut admin work.

Talent bench, team and reporting

Build and run our talent community, a vetted pool of strong candidates we stay in touch with so new roles fill from the bench.

Coach the recruiters, hold them to weekly targets and help hire new ones as we grow.

Run client hiring alignment calls and be the escalation point when a search is off track.

Send leadership a weekly scorecard: open searches, days to shortlist, interviews held, pass-through rates and starts.

A track record hiring sales, client success and marketing roles for U.S. companies, including remote and international hires.

Power user of an ATS (Workable strongly preferred) and LinkedIn Recruiter. Comfortable using AI tools every day.

Fluent English, written and spoken. Available full-time during U.S. Eastern hours, with no other jobs or side clients.

Nice to have

Experience with behavioral assessments (Predictive Index or similar).

Prove it

Finalists will be asked to show real numbers and real work, for example:

Searches you ran at the same time, with time-to-shortlist and time-to-fill.

A pipeline, scorecard or SOP you built, and what it changed.

A live headhunting exercise: find five strong candidates for one of our open roles.

Who thrives here

You're driven, organized and fast. You do the work yourself and fix the system around it. You hold people to a standard, side with the company's standards when a client pushes, and tell leadership the truth even when the numbers are bad. If you only want to manage and not recruit, this isn't the seat for you.
