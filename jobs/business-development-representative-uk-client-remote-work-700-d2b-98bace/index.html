ABOUT THE COMPANY

The client is a pair of sister engineering and technical support businesses serving the UK industrial, energy, and offshore sectors. Led commercially by their Commercial Director and supported by their technical leadership, both companies operate with a relationship-first approach—rejecting high-volume, pushy telesales in favor of consultative partnerships that solve genuine operational, resourcing, and project mobilisation challenges.

While operating as distinct brands with equal commercial priority, they share a unified mission: connecting UK industrial and energy clients with specialized engineering expertise, technical labour, and project support.

Brand A (Technical Resourcing): Focuses on technical resourcing, engineering support, and solving skilled labour shortages across industrial and energy projects.

Brand B (Engineering & Mobilisation): Delivers specialized engineering, technical scoping, and project mobilisation services—including offshore and broader industrial operations.

ROLE OVERVIEW

This is a dedicated, full-time outbound B2B (Business-to-Business) relationship-building role reporting directly to the Commercial Director. The representative is responsible for researching prospect lists , engaging key decision-makers across UK (United Kingdom) businesses, and uncovering genuine operational, labour, or project needs across two sister engineering and technical support companies equally—rather than relying on pushy, script-based telesales. Ultimately, the role acts as a strategic gatekeeper who protects leadership's time by qualifying opportunities and handing over well-researched, high-value meetings to the Commercial Director, supported technically by the offshore operations lead and wider engineering team.

Key Responsibilities

Work through the prospect lists for both businesses and find the person who actually makes or influences the buying decision.

Pick up the phone, introduce the clients properly, and have a sensible conversation about the work the company does and any help it may need.

Ask good questions about upcoming projects, busy periods, labour shortages, existing suppliers, and who is responsible for buying support.

Build relationships over time. Follow up by phone and email without pestering people or using a generic sales script.

Keep the client contact records up to date. If a company is relevant to both, flag it and coordinate the approach so we do not contact them twice unnecessarily.

Book meetings for the client when there is a real reason for him to speak to the prospect, then send him a clear note before the meeting.

When a meeting is ready for the client

The client has commitments and is often working offshore, so we need to protect their time. The BDE should not book a meeting just because someone agrees to a chat. Before handing it over,  we would expect them to know:

Who we are speaking to and what they are responsible for.

What the company is working on and which Company A or Company B service might help.

Whether there is a genuine need, an upcoming requirement or a sensible reason to stay in touch.

What the next conversation with Martin is supposed to achieve.

The handover should be short and useful: company, contact details, project or site, what the prospect has told us, likely services, timing and agreed next steps. The client will take the commercial relationship forward. And the other manager and the relevant technical people will get involved where we need their experience on resourcing, scope, or mobilisation.

Confident spoken and written English, with a clear and friendly telephone manner that works well with UK  businesses.

Previous experience making outbound B2B calls and finding the right decision-makers.

Engineering,  recruitment, or industrial experience would be useful, but is not essential if they can learn quickly.

Someone who listens, asks sensible questions and can talk naturally without reading from a script.

Organised and persistent, with good follow-up habits and accurate notes in Excel or a CRM.

Reliable working from home, with a quiet space, good internet and the ability to work a full-time week during UK business hours.

Someone who wants to be part of our businesses and build accounts over time, rather than move from one  short-term calling campaign to another.
