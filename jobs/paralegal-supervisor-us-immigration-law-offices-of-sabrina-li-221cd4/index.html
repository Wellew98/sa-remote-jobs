About Us

The Law Offices of Sabrina Li, P.C. is a leading, full-service U.S. immigration law firm with offices in Los Angeles, Pasadena, Santa Ana, and Dallas, as well as international operations across Asia, Africa, and Central and South America. We are dedicated to helping individuals, families, and businesses navigate complex legal challenges with skill, compassion, and integrity.

We are proud to be recognized as a 2025 Inc. Best Workplaces honoree, a distinction that reflects our intentional investment in our people and workplace culture. Our firm prioritizes professional development, clear career pathways, and a culture of accountability and respect, creating a workplace where high performers can build meaningful, long-term careers.

About the Role

We are seeking an experienced and proactive Paralegal Supervisor to lead our growing legal support team across various areas of U.S. immigration law, including family-based immigration, waivers, humanitarian relief, employment-based immigration, consular processing, and removal defense.

The ideal candidate will have extensive hands-on experience handling U.S. immigration cases, coupled with strong leadership, performance management, and process improvement skills. This is primarily a leadership role, with success measured by the quality, productivity, and overall performance of the paralegal team.

Reporting to the Managing Attorney and working closely with operations leadership, you will oversee case production, develop team members, and contribute to the continuous improvement of our legal workflows, systems, and processes. As our firm continues to grow, we are looking for someone who embraces change, takes initiative, and can build structure within an evolving environment.

Key Responsibilities

Oversee the day-to-day operations of the paralegal team, ensuring timely and accurate case progression from assignment through filing.

Assign, prioritize, and redistribute cases based on complexity, team capacity, and operational needs.

Monitor case deadlines, turnaround times, and outstanding assignments, proactively addressing backlogs and workflow bottlenecks.

Conduct quality control reviews of immigration filing packages to ensure accuracy, completeness, and compliance with filing requirements.

Maintain a reduced caseload of complex immigration matters, including hardship waivers, RFE and NOID responses, and advanced legal drafting assignments under attorney supervision.

Coordinate attorney reviews and external service providers, ensuring timely completion of case-related deliverables.

Lead weekly case status meetings, establish clear action items, and hold team members accountable for deadlines and deliverables.

Monitor individual and team performance, provide constructive feedback, and address concerns related to quality, productivity, and accountability.

Oversee productivity point allocation and prepare monthly paralegal performance reports.

Facilitate the onboarding, training, and professional development of paralegals and legal interns.

Develop, implement, and maintain standard operating procedures (SOPs), case management workflows, and training materials.

Ensure accurate and consistent use of case management and document management systems, supporting ongoing system improvements and automation.

Utilize legal technology and AI-assisted tools to improve productivity and workflow efficiency, subject to firm confidentiality and review requirements.

What Success Looks Like

Within Your First 90 Days

Maintain accurate oversight of all active matters, including case ownership, current stages, and upcoming deadlines.

Establish structured weekly case status meetings with clear action items, responsibilities, and deadlines.

Ensure paralegals independently maintain accurate and up-to-date case records.

Successfully deliver at least two monthly paralegal performance reports, with accurate productivity point allocations.

Demonstrate a measurable reduction in overdue cases, prioritizing the oldest outstanding matters.

Within Your First Year

Ensure most cases are filed within established turnaround times, with timely follow-up on outstanding reviews.

Establish effective onboarding and training processes that enable new team members to work independently within defined timelines.

Maintain high-quality filing standards with no avoidable rejections or inconsistencies.

Build a reliable and accountable paralegal team that operates with minimal attorney intervention in administrative and case production matters.

Qualifications

Minimum of 3 years of hands-on experience handling U.S. immigration cases (required).

Minimum of 2 years of experience supervising or leading paralegals in a formal leadership capacity (required).

Bachelor's degree or paralegal certification is preferred.

Demonstrated experience preparing Forms I-130, I-485, I-601, and I-601A, as well as RFE and NOID responses.

Familiarity with EOIR filings, PERM, VAWA, T visas, U visas, and consular processing is highly preferred.

Strong legal drafting skills, particularly in preparing hardship waiver cover letters and responses to complex immigration matters.

Demonstrated ability to manage team performance, reduce case backlogs, and improve operational efficiency.

Experience developing legal workflows, SOPs, and structured training materials.

Experience using legal case management and project management software is required, including platforms such as Monday.com, Clio, Lawmatics, Docketwise, or similar systems.

Experience developing productivity reports, case aging reports, and performance dashboards is highly preferred.

Strong proficiency in utilizing AI tools to enhance work product and efficiency is highly preferred.

Excellent leadership, organizational, problem-solving, and English communication skills.

Proven ability to adapt to changing systems and processes, exercise sound judgment, and work independently.

Candidates must have a reliable home office setup, including high-speed internet and a backup power solution to ensure consistent availability during power outages.

Why Join Our Team?

Competitive compensation, performance-based bonuses, and periodic remuneration reviews.

Fully remote work with flexible scheduling and paid company-observed U.S. holidays.

Comprehensive onboarding and ongoing professional development opportunities.

Opportunity to lead a growing legal team and shape departmental systems and processes.

Clear career advancement opportunities within a collaborative, innovative, and growth-oriented global law firm.

Working Hours

The scheduling for this role is flexible; however, contractors are expected to maintain a minimum of 6 hours of overlap with the firm's operating hours in Pacific Time (PT), subject to operational needs and team coordination.

Application Process

As part of the application process, candidates will be asked to provide a redacted legal writing sample and a brief description of a previous experience in which they successfully improved a team or process, outlining the situation, actions taken, and measurable results.
