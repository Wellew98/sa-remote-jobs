About the company

Our client is an established property management and estate agency based in Manchester, specializing in the residential and commercial sectors. With over 20 years of experience in the local market, the team manages an extensive portfolio of more than 800 properties, ranging from luxury professional accommodations to student housing and commercial lettings. They pride themselves on a bespoke approach to real estate, focusing on delivering high yields for landlords and providing high-quality modern living spaces for tenants in key urban areas.

The team operates with a unique, proven methodology that prioritizes expert market knowledge and a seamless customer experience. By combining deep local expertise with a commitment to minimal-hassle property management, they have built a reputation for excellence in sales, lettings, and valuations. This is an excellent opportunity to join a high-performing environment that values results, industry innovation, and a personalized service model within the vibrant UK property market.

Job Summary

We are seeking a data-driven and systems-oriented Marketing & Funnel Growth Coordinator to lead the digital expansion of a premier Manchester-based real estate agency. This is a pivotal role where you will own the end-to-end marketing ecosystem—from social media engagement and high-converting lead funnels to sophisticated CRM automation and nurture sequences. If you are a proactive marketer who thrives at the intersection of creative content and technical operations, this is your opportunity to drive measurable growth for a fast-paced property business.

Key Responsibilities

Social Media Management

Execute and schedule consistent, high-quality content across Facebook and Instagram to maintain brand authority and engage local audiences.

Funnel & Conversion Optimization

Build and manage high-performing landing pages, lead capture forms, and qualification workflows, utilizing A/B testing to maximize conversion rates.

CRM & Operations

Oversee lead routing and attribution within the CRM, ensuring all segments are organized and campaign-to-conversion performance is tracked.

Automation & Nurture

Design and manage automated email sequences, SMS workflows, and missed-call automations to ensure rapid engagement with inbound leads.

Database Marketing

Execute targeted email campaigns, newsletters, and database reactivation strategies to keep the brand top-of-mind for landlords, investors, and tenants.

AI & Systems Support

Implement and assist with AI-powered response systems and chatbots to improve operational efficiency.

Performance Tracking

Monitor key metrics including cost per lead, funnel throughput, and marketing-attributed appointments.

Proven experience in Real Estate marketing and lead generation.

Expertise in Social Media Management and Email Marketing.

Strong proficiency in CRM systems and Marketing Automation platforms.

Hands-on experience managing and optimizing digital marketing funnels.

Proficiency in Microsoft Office and Google Workspace.

Exceptional written and verbal English communication skills.

Ability to work strictly within 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM GMT hours.

A proactive, "owner" mindset with the ability to manage technical systems independently.

Nice-to-Haves

Experience with Landing Page Builders (e.g., ClickFunnels, Unbounce, or similar).

Knowledge of Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) principles.

Familiarity with AI automation tools and chatbot integration.

Experience with SMS marketing and property portal management.

Advanced marketing analytics and reporting skills.
