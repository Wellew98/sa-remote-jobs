About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a Senior Full Stack Engineer to architect and build a modern tax filing platform from the ground up.

This isn’t just another software engineering role.

You’ll own the architecture, backend services, frontend experience, and key technical decisions behind a mission-critical platform where security, scalability, accuracy, and reliability are essential.

You’ll work extensively with AI-assisted development tools such as

Cursor

and Claude Code to accelerate development while maintaining exceptional code quality and engineering standards.

If you enjoy solving complex technical problems, building scalable software, and taking ownership from architecture through deployment, this role is a strong fit.

What Happens After You Apply

After submitting your application, you’ll receive an email invitation from

Spark Hire

to record a short 3–5 minute Intro Video . This is the first step in our hiring process and can be completed whenever it’s convenient for you.

Instead of multiple initial screening calls, you’ll have one opportunity to introduce yourself, discuss your full stack engineering experience, walk through projects you’ve built, explain your experience with Python, FastAPI, modern frontend frameworks, and share how you’ve used AI-assisted development tools such as Cursor or Claude Code to improve software development. Your video will be reviewed by the hiring team before moving to the technical assessment.

You can record your video as many times as you’d like before submitting it—only your final version will be reviewed.

Please keep an eye on both your inbox and spam folder for your Spark Hire invitation after applying.
