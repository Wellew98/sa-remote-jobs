JOB TITLESALES MANAGER

LOCATIONREMOTE TIME ZONE: US TIME ZONES (EST–PST)

Role Overview

We are looking for an experienced Sales Manager on behalf of our client, a fast-growing US-based call center that provides outsourced sales and appointment-setting teams to growing companies. Our client needs a hands-on leader who can coach Account Executives and SDRs, own revenue targets, and build a repeatable sales process. Experience leading sales in a call center, BPO or outsourced services environment is a strong plus.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead, coach and develop a team of Account Executives and SDRs
  • Own team revenue targets, pipeline health and forecasting
  • Run weekly pipeline reviews, deal coaching and call reviews
  • Build and refine sales playbooks, scripts and processes
  • Step into key deals and senior client conversations when needed
  • Track team KPIs and report performance to leadership
  • Partner with operations and delivery on smooth client handoffs

Required Qualifications

  • 5+ years in B2B sales, including 2+ years managing a sales team
  • A proven track record of leading a team to hit or exceed revenue targets
  • Experience selling to US-based companies, founders or executives
  • Strong coaching, forecasting and pipeline management skills
  • Prior experience working remotely, with fluency in remote collaboration tools such as Slack, Zoom, Google Workspace, Asana, or similar; candidates without prior remote work experience will not be considered
  • Reliable internet connection and a professional remote work setup

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience leading sales in a call center, BPO or outsourced services company
  • Experience building a sales team or process from early stage
  • Experience managing distributed, international sales teams

Tools & Technology

  • CRM platforms (HubSpot, Salesforce, or similar)
  • LinkedIn Sales Navigator
  • VoIP and dialer tools
  • Sales reporting and dashboard tools
  • Google Workspace or Microsoft 365
  • Slack, Zoom, and task management tools

Please NOTE It is crucial that you complete the application form in full. As part of the application process, you will be required to record a video. If your application is successful, you will receive an email confirming next steps — the video is the first step of the interview process. If you do not record a video, we will not be able to consider you for ANY open roles.
