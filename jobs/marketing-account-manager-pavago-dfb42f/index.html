About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a Marketing Account Manager to manage client relationships, oversee marketing execution, and ensure projects are delivered accurately and on time.

This is more than an account management role.

You’ll serve as the primary point of contact for multiple client accounts while coordinating email marketing campaigns, WordPress updates, SEO initiatives, reporting, and cross-functional marketing projects.

You’ll work closely with leadership and internal marketing teams to keep campaigns moving, maintain clear client communication, and deliver an exceptional client experience.

If you’re highly organized, client-focused, and enjoy balancing marketing execution with relationship management, this role is a strong fit.

What Happens After You Apply

After submitting your application, you’ll receive an email invitation from Spark Hire to record a short 3–5 minute Intro Video . This is the first step in our hiring process and can be completed whenever it’s convenient for you.

Instead of multiple initial screening calls, you’ll have one opportunity to introduce yourself, discuss your experience managing client relationships, coordinating marketing campaigns, working with email marketing platforms, WordPress, SEO tools, and analytics, and share how you’ve kept multiple client accounts organized while delivering exceptional service. You’ll also have the opportunity to highlight how you’ve used AI tools to improve marketing execution and client communication. Your video will be reviewed by the hiring team before moving to the client interview.

You can record your video as many times as you’d like before submitting it—only your final version will be reviewed.

Please keep an eye on both your inbox and spam folder for your Spark Hire invitation after applying.
