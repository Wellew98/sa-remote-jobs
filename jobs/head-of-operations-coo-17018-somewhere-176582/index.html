Head of Operations (COO)

LocationRemote (Preference for Western / Western-aligned time zones)

TypeFull-Time

Reports toFounder & CEO

CompensationTop-decile base + aggressive performance / equity upside

The Role – In One Sentence

You are the operational engine and the Founder’s right hand.

You own everything that isn’t vision, brand, or new product creation — and you turn a fast-growing, high-potential DTC brand into a predictable, high-velocity revenue machine that can scale past $100M+ without breaking.

First 90 Days – Non-Negotiable Outcomes

If you can’t see yourself doing this in 90 days, this isn’t your seat.

-

Full Team & Systems Audit Complete

-

Every seat gradedKeep / Upgrade / Remove

-

Weak links identified and addressed

- A-player talent pipeline live and active

- Company Operating System Locked In & Enforced

- Daily SOD/EOD cadence, weekly “war room,” real-time dashboards

- Clear KPIs, ownership, and follow-through

- Founder never has to chase execution again

- Friction Identified and Eliminated Across the Business

- Supply chain, fulfillment, Amazon operations, finance, creative pipeline, cross-functional communication

- Bottlenecks diagnosed and resolved with durable solutions

- The Company Feels Like a Weapon, Not a Startup

- Rhythm, clarity, and tempo across all teams

-

Problems surfaced early and solved quickly

- Everyone knows what “great” looks like and how they contribute

Note: A lot of this already exists. Your job is to make it sharp, scalable, and unbreakable .

What You Own

Everything that touches money, execution, and operational performance :

-

End-to-end supply chain and manufacturing

- Amazon Seller Central (listings, inventory, PPC execution, compliance)

- DTC fulfillment (Shopify, subscriptions, CX, LTV, churn reduction)

- People and org build-out (current team ~8 → 25+ in 18 months)

- Hiring, performance management, and when necessary — firing fast

-

Operating systemNotion (or similar), SOPs, KPI dashboards, meeting cadences

- Margin defense and velocity offense – you protect profit and unlock growth

You don’t just “keep things running.” You design the machine that keeps running.

Mandatory Prerequisites – No Exceptions

- You are (or were) a serious athlete.

- Competitive background (sport, martial arts, endurance, etc.)

- You train hard, eat clean, and treat health as non-negotiable.

- This is a lifestyle, not a phase.

- You have already scaled operations for a $20M–$100M+ e-commerce / CPG / supplement brand (or a division of similar size).

- You’ve seen the complexity and know where things break.

- You’ve been in the room when hard decisions were made.

- You have personally built and led remote teams of 15–40+ people.

- Hiring plans, scorecards, onboardings, performance management, exits.

- You have fired underperformers and upgraded seats quickly and humanely.

- You can have hard conversations without drama.

- You are decisive under pressure.

- Calm when things are on fire, allergic to excuses, obsessed with solutions.

- You work in Western or Western-aligned time zones

- You live the health optimization lifestyle.

- You don’t just understand the customer — you are the customer.

Cultural Fit (More Important Than Your CV)

- Extreme ownership is your default mode.

- You move with controlled aggression — fast but never sloppy.

- Mediocrity genuinely bothers you; you can’t ignore it.

- You push back (respectfully but firmly) on bad ideas — including the Founder’s.

- You want legacy and wealth , not another comfortable job.

- You think in systems, not tasks , and you hate recurring problems.

This is a seat for someone who thinks “How do we 10x this?” not “How do we maintain this?”

How You’ll Be Measured

You will be judged on hard numbers , not vibes

-

Operational efficiency≥ 92%

-

On-time delivery≥ 97%

-

Founder time freed from operations≥ 85%

- Margin improvement directly attributable to your initiatives

- Zero meaningful revenue lost due to operational failures

If these metrics excite you (not scare you), you’re in the right place.

How to Apply

Send your CV + a short Loom video

In your Loom (5–7 minutes max), answer:

-

Hardest operational turnaround you’ve led

- What was the situation, what was the scale, and what were the exact results?

-

Your current training routine

- Show us you live the athlete life (training logs, competition history, or other proof).

- One thing you’re certain the Founder of a brand like this is probably doing right now that’s slowing the company down — and how you’d fix it.

This is not “a role.”

This is the second-highest operational seat in a brand that intends to dominate its category.

Apply only if you’re built for it.
