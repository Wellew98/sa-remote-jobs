Management Consultant Location: Remote (US Based Work Hours) Compensation: Competitive with the market (USD) Reports to: Founder & Executive Team Start Date: ASAP About the Opportunity We’re seeking a sharp, resourceful, and execution-oriented Management Consultant to serve as a strategic right hand to the Founder of a growing portfolio company builder actively acquiring and scaling businesses across sectors like construction, manufacturing, and tech-enabled services. This role offers a rare opportunity to apply top-tier consulting skills in a fast-moving, entrepreneurial environment. You’ll work across multiple business units and contribute directly to financial analysis, strategic planning, board-level communications, and M&A integration. If you’ve worked at McKinsey, BCG, Bain, or in a similar high-performance strategy role—and want to go beyond slide decks to implement real change —this is your moment. What You’ll Do Strategy & Analysis - Develop clear, actionable insights from data to support founder-led decision-making - Own financial modeling , forecasting, and scenario planning across multiple business units - Conduct deep-dive market research , competitive analysis, and operational benchmarking - Design and deliver board-level presentations, investor reports , and strategic updates Business Operations & Execution - Work across 2–3 core businesses (e.g., luxury wood manufacturing, construction services, tech-enabled consulting ) - Translate strategic priorities into playbooks, SOPs, and cross-functional projects - Support key metrics tracking and executive dashboards to guide performance M&A & Integration Support - Contribute to acquisition modeling, financial due diligence , and integration planning - Analyze new business opportunities and risks during pre-acquisition review - Assist in aligning new entities to group-wide financial and operational standards What We’re Looking For - 5+ years in management consulting, corporate strategy, FP&A, or a similar analytical role - Mastery of financial modeling, Excel, PowerPoint , and business case development - Strong business acumen with a deep understanding of P&L drivers, ROI, and valuation - Excellent English communication skills , especially in crafting executive-level content - Proven ability to work autonomously and handle shifting priorities in entrepreneurial settings - Based in South Africa or Latin America , with overlap to U.S. business hours Bonus Points - Experience working directly with founders, PE-backed companies, or SMB leadership - Exposure to acquisition integration, operational turnaround, or growth playbook development - Familiarity with tools like Notion, Airtable, HubSpot, Monday.com , or BI platforms - Background in verticals like manufacturing, construction, or tech-enabled services Why Join Us We don’t just advise—we build. This role is perfect for someone who wants to step out of the consulting ivory tower and make a tangible impact on real businesses with real teams. You’ll operate side-by-side with decision-makers, influence outcomes, and gain a deep, hands-on view of entrepreneurial operations and investment strategy. If you’re hungry for ownership, sharp in your thinking, and excited to operate across industries— you’ll thrive here.
