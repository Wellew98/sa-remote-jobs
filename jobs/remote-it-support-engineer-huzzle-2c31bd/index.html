About Huzzle

At Huzzle, we connect exceptional talent with top opportunities at leading companies across the UK, US, Canada, Europe, and Australia. Our clients include startups, digital agencies, and tech platforms in industries such as SaaS, MarTech, FinTech, and HealthTech. Unlike an outsourcing agency, we place you directly with a client where you’re hired in-house as a valued member of their team.

About the Company

Our client is a well-established UK-based technology company with over 40 years of experience delivering innovative, reliable, and secure IT solutions to businesses.

Specialising in flexible IT leasing, managed IT services, and technical support, they empower startups, SMEs, and growing organisations to streamline their technology infrastructure and improve operational efficiency.

Their comprehensive service offering includes device deployment, mobile device management (MDM), Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace administration, cloud backup, cybersecurity, and technical helpdesk support.

As an ISO 27001-certified company, Microsoft Partner, and member of the Apple Consultants Network, they have built a strong reputation for technical excellence, information security, and exceptional customer service.

Job Summary

We are seeking a technically skilled and customer-focused IT Support Engineer based in South Africa to join an established and growing UK-based technical support team.

In this fully remote role, you will deliver high-quality IT support to UK business clients, troubleshooting technical issues across Microsoft 365, Microsoft Intune, Windows, macOS, iOS, networking, and server environments.

You will take ownership of helpdesk tickets, manage device configurations and deployments, resolve technical incidents, and ensure consistently high service standards within an SLA-driven environment.

The ideal candidate has hands-on experience within a Managed Service Provider (MSP) or IT services environment, strong technical troubleshooting capabilities, and the ability to communicate confidently with international clients.

This is an excellent opportunity for an IT professional looking to advance their technical career while working directly with an established international technology company.

Key Responsibilities

Provide exceptional remote IT support and technical assistance to UK business clients and internal teams.

Manage, prioritise, troubleshoot, and resolve helpdesk tickets in accordance with agreed service level agreements (SLAs), including response times of 30–60 minutes where applicable.

Diagnose and resolve hardware, software, operating system, and connectivity issues across Windows, macOS, iOS, and server environments.

Administer and maintain Microsoft 365 environments, including Exchange Online, Microsoft Teams, OneDrive, SharePoint, user accounts, and licensing.

Configure, enrol, deploy, and manage devices using Microsoft Intune, Windows Autopilot, and Apple Business Manager.

Implement and support Mobile Device Management (MDM), Mobile Application Management (MAM), and device compliance policies.

Assist with device provisioning, pre-configuration, deployment, wiping, and reprovisioning.

Identify and troubleshoot networking issues involving DHCP, DNS, NAT, and WAN connectivity.

Provide technical support for Windows and Linux servers, Active Directory, and Group Policy.

Troubleshoot printing, peripheral, hardware, software, and general end-user support issues.

Maintain accurate helpdesk ticket records, technical documentation, and internal knowledge base articles.

Communicate technical solutions clearly and professionally to both technical and non-technical users.

Collaborate with experienced IT engineers to resolve complex technical issues and maintain exceptional client service standards.

Take ownership of technical support requests and ensure timely resolution and effective client communication.

2–3 years of professional experience in IT support, technical support, desktop support, or an IT helpdesk role.

Previous experience within a Managed Service Provider (MSP) or IT services company is strongly preferred.

Strong hands-on experience administering Microsoft 365 (Office 365), including Exchange Online, Microsoft Teams, OneDrive, SharePoint, user management, and licensing.

Practical experience with Microsoft Intune (Endpoint Manager), Windows Autopilot, and Apple Business Manager.

Strong understanding of Mobile Device Management (MDM), Mobile Application Management (MAM), and device compliance policies.

Solid technical knowledge of PC, Mac, and server hardware.

Strong understanding of networking fundamentals, including DHCP, DNS, NAT, and WAN technologies.

Experience supporting Windows and Linux servers, Active Directory, and Group Policy.

Proficiency in troubleshooting Windows 10/11, macOS, and iOS operating systems.

Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and technical troubleshooting skills.

Excellent written and spoken English, with a professional and customer-focused communication style.

Ability to communicate confidently with UK business clients via phone, email, and video calls.

Strong organisational and time management skills, with the ability to manage multiple support tickets and competing priorities.

Ability to work independently, take ownership of technical issues, and collaborate effectively within a remote team.

Availability to work full-time during UK business hours.

Must be based in South Africa.

Nice to Have

Microsoft certifications such as MS-900, MD-102, or MS-102.

CompTIA A+ or Network+ certifications.

Experience with Jamf or alternative Apple device management solutions.

Familiarity with cybersecurity and backup solutions such as Sophos, Barracuda, or Microsoft Defender.

Previous experience delivering remote IT support to UK-based or international business clients.
