Senior Video Editor / Creative Production Lead

Remote Recruitment Reliable internet connection and a suitable remote working environment in South Africa. Design & Creative Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

About the Company Our client is a growing UK-based video marketing agency specialising in high-volume paid advertising content. The agency supports businesses with performance-focused video content across platforms such as Meta, Google and YouTube. With a strong referral pipeline and increasing client demand, the business is looking for a highly capable creative production professional who can significantly increase its content delivery capacity. The Role This is not a standard video editing position. We are seeking a senior-level video editor and creative production professional who can manage the full content workflow—from receiving raw footage through to delivering approved advertising assets. The successful candidate will be responsible for running an efficient, high-volume content pipeline while maintaining strong creative and technical standards. You must be confident making appropriate creative decisions within an agreed brief without requiring constant direction or micromanagement. The role offers a clear progression opportunity, with the successful candidate expected to eventually oversee and manage a team of additional video editors.
