We are seeking a highly experienced and motivated PFM Consultant to join our dynamic team. The ideal candidate will possess a deep understanding of development economics and public finance management (PFM), coupled with proven experience working with African Ministries of finance or major donor organizations that provide technical assistance to Ministries of finance. This role requires a minimum of five years of relevant professional experience.

Key responsibilities

Economic Analysis & Strategic Reporting: Conduct rigorous quantitative analysis on macro-economic trends and public finance issues. You will prepare high-quality, policy-relevant briefs and presentations that translate complex data into actionable advice for senior government officials.

PFM Diagnostics and Technical AssistanceLead the design of reforms across the entire budget cycle, including:

Medium-Term Expenditure Frameworks (MTEF) and the expenditure chain.

Revenue generation and Domestic Resource Mobilization (DRM) and fiscal space analyses.

Fiscal decentralization and innovative financing.

Budget tagging for cross sector thematics.

Open Budget/Transparency

Stakeholder Engagement: Act as a high-level advisor and interlocutor with senior government counterparts and major donor representatives (e.g., World Bank, AfDB, EU).

Business Development & Bidding: Proactively identify opportunities and lead the preparation of competitive technical and financial proposals. You will be expected to co-design technical assistance programs with international partners.

Capacity Building: Design and facilitate advanced training packages for government and donor staff on budgeting, fiscal transparency, and expenditure management.

Service Line Evolution: Play a key role in co-developing the structure, team, and project pipeline for our PFM service line.

Lead with Insight: Manage complex workstreams and small, high-performance teams, translating intricate financial data into compelling narratives for ministers, multi-lateral donors, and private foundations.

Based in Sub-Saharan Africa, Middle East or Asia
