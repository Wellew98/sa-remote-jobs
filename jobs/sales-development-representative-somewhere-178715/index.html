Job Title: Sales Development Representative (SDR). Employment Type: Full-Time, Independant contractor Working Hours: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM Pacific Time, Monday through Friday. Location: Remote, open to candidates in South Africa, Latin America, or the Philippines. Industry : Healthcare / Chiropractic services. About the Role We are looking for an energetic, confident, and highly motivated Sales Development Representative (SDR) to join our team in a fully remote, client-facing sales role. This is a phone-heavy position requiring someone who is naturally outgoing, comfortable speaking with prospects , and able to confidently guide conversations from initial contact through discovery and the next stage of the sales process. You will engage with both outbound and inbound prospects, as well as potential customers through Instagram DMs. Because you will be communicating with U.S.-based prospects in a medical-related environment, fluent English and exceptionally clear spoken communication are essential. What You'll Do - Make outbound calls to prospective and colder leads. - Handle inbound calls from prospects who have expressed interest. - Conduct discovery-style conversations to understand prospect needs, challenges, and fit. - Build rapport quickly and establish trust over the phone. - Follow up with prospects and move qualified opportunities through the sales process. - Engage with prospects through Instagram DMs and sell through chat. - Respond professionally and promptly to prospect questions. - Maintain accurate notes and follow-up activity in the relevant CRM/sales systems. - Learn and follow established sales scripts, processes, and messaging. - Continuously improve your sales approach based on coaching and performance feedback. What We're Looking For Required: - Fluent, highly proficient English with an accent that is easy for U.S. callers to understand. - Strong telephone presence and communication skills. - Confident, outgoing, and personable personality. - Comfortable making outbound calls and speaking with new people throughout the day. - Driven by targets, results, and personal performance. - Able to handle rejection and maintain a positive, persistent approach. - Strong listening and discovery skills. - Professional and reliable in a fully remote environment. - Comfortable communicating through social media and chat. Preferred: - Previous SDR, BDR, inside sales, telesales, appointment-setting, or similar experience. - Experience selling to U.S.-based customers. - Familiarity with GoHighLevel. - Experience working with CRMs and sales pipelines. - Experience in healthcare, medical services, or another client-facing professional environment. Required Tools: - Familiarity with GoHighLevel is a plus. Ideal soft skills required: - O utgoing and energized by cold calling, not intimidated by it. Client Assessment Process: - A short email introduction (why you want the role and what success looks like). - A talking-head video answering 3 questions. - A short phone conversation. - A working interview/role-play session. To move forward successfully share the following to Sammy@somewhere.com : A short cover letter: - Use this as an opportunity to pitch yourself directly to the client by highlighting your relevant experience, achievements, and motivation for applying. - Include the reasons for leaving your last 3 roles and share your long-term career plans. We value long-term commitment. Additionally share: - What is your notice period and expected monthly salary in USD? - An updated CV. Ensure it highlights your key skills and experience you have relevant to the role. - Upload a short video introduction here where you introduce yourself and summarize your experience and key achievements.
