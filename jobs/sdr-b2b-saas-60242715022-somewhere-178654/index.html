Strategic Sales Development Representative (SDR) Location: Fully Remote Hours: Full-Time | Monday–Friday | EST Business Hours Target Candidates: Global / Offshore (e.g., South Africa, LATAM)  Target Target Compensation: $2,000–$3,000/month Base Salary + Performance Commission About the Company We are an innovative, fast-growing B2B SaaS startup backed by seed funding. Our platform helps enterprise and mid-market organizations maximize revenue by identifying hidden revenue leaks, quantifying upside potential, and delivering actionable insights that fuel data-driven growth. Selling directly to VP and C-level decision-makers with average contract values (ACV) around $100k/year , we operate at the intersection of high-value solution selling and modern go-to-market technology. Role Overview This is not a traditional transactional appointment-setting role. We are seeking a high-caliber, strategic Sales Development Representative (SDR) to act as a true partner to our founding team. As a early member of our sales organization, you will own the end-to-end top-of-funnel engine. You will be responsible for taking early-stage outbound playbooks, executing them with surgical precision, analyzing performance, and iterating to optimize conversion. Beyond outbound prospecting, you will partner directly with the co-founders to launch, test, and sequence high-intent inbound channels (e.g., post-conference leads, paid digital campaigns). The ideal candidate possesses deep executive presence, strong business acumen, and a passion for building systems from scratch in an agile, startup environment. Key Responsibilities Strategic Outbound Execution & Playbook Optimization - Own top-of-funnel pipeline generation, engaging enterprise and mid-market buyers (Directors, VPs, and C-Suite) across strategic target accounts. - Execute multi-channel outreach utilizing email, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, phone, and cold touches tailored to high-value decision-makers. - Analyze initial outreach playbooks, gather market feedback, and continuously jam with the founders to refine targeting, value props, and messaging sequences. - Experiment with outbound variations, identify what resonates, and help build repeatable, scalable GTM playbooks. Inbound Channel Sequencing & Lead Engagement - Partner with the leadership team to rapidly follow up with and convert leads generated from new acquisition channels (e.g., event/conference lists, paid media, strategic initiatives). - Design, implement, and run tailored follow-up sequences to convert high-intent inbound inquiries into qualified executive demos. Account Research & Consultative Outreach - Conduct deep research on target organizations to identify revenue leakage points and align our platform's value proposition with executive-level priorities. - Craft highly customized, business-outcome-focused messaging—moving past superficial feature pitches to establish immediate credibility. Modern Sales Tech Stack Optimization - Drive daily prospecting workflows using Amplemarket , Attio CRM , and LinkedIn Sales Navigator . - Leverage advanced AI productivity tools (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) to enhance research depth, personalize outreach at scale, and streamline operational tasks. - Maintain strict CRM hygiene and maintain clean data to support accurate pipeline forecasting. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) - Qualified Demos Booked: Number of high-fit executive meetings held on founder calendars. - Pipeline Value Created: Total dollar value of qualified sales opportunities generated. - Sequence & Campaign Conversion Rates: Continuous improvement of open, reply, and conversion metrics across playbooks. - Playbook Development: Tangible contribution to testing, documenting, and scaling outbound and inbound GTM motions. Required Qualifications - 2–5 years of experience in Sales Development, Business Development, or B2B SaaS Sales. - Proven track record of cold outreach to enterprise or mid-market executive buyers ( VP/C-Level ). - Exceptional written and spoken English communication skills with strong executive presence and consultative phrasing. - Hands-on experience executing outbound campaigns in early-stage or rapidly evolving environments. - Proficiency with modern sales engagement tools (Amplemarket, Apollo, Outreach, or similar) and CRMs (Attio, Salesforce, HubSpot). - Comfortable operating in a fast-paced seed-stage environment with shifting priorities and unstructured playbooks. - Willingness to work aligned with US Eastern Standard Time (EST) hours. Preferred Qualifications - Prior experience in early-stage/seed startups where you helped build or refine GTM processes rather than just following existing scripts. - Hands-on experience leveraging AI platforms (ChatGPT, Claude) to accelerate prospecting, account research, and copy drafting. - Experience selling high ACV platforms ($50k–$100k+ ARR). What We Offer - Direct Access & Mentorship: Work side-by-side with our founding team to shape the company's core GTM strategy. - Impact & Growth: Foundational position with substantial autonomy and career growth potential as the team scales. - Modern Tooling: Full company-sponsored access to premium AI platforms and cutting-edge sales tech tools. - Flexibility: Fully remote work culture.
