About the Client

The client operates in the career-technology space, providing tools and resources designed to help job seekers navigate the hiring process more effectively.

Its platform supports key stages of the job search including

  • Resume building
  • Interview preparation
  • Job applications
  • Career development
  • Job searching
  • Hiring-process navigation

Digital content plays an important role in educating job seekers, communicating product value, building credibility, and helping users better understand the hiring process.

About the Role

As a Content Creator for the company, you’ll become a key face and voice behind the brand’s digital content.

You’ll be a strong fit if you are highly confident on camera, communicate exceptionally well in English, and know how to turn career and hiring-related topics into content that feels:

  • Practical
  • Engaging
  • Credible
  • Conversational
  • Approachable
  • Easy to understand
  • This role is not intended for a generalist creator with no meaningful exposure to careers or hiring .
  • We are specifically prioritizing candidates who already understand or have created content around:
  • Careers
  • Recruiting
  • HR
  • Talent acquisition
  • Education
  • Professional development
  • Resume writing
  • Interviews
  • Job searching
  • Hiring
  • Job applications
  • Career coaching
  • Career technology

Role Responsibilities

On-Camera Content Creation

  • Serve as a recognizable and credible on-camera presence for the company.
  • Record engaging short-form video and other digital content.
  • Deliver scripted and semi-scripted content naturally.
  • Use a teleprompter while maintaining strong eye contact and conversational delivery.
  • Maintain energy and authenticity across multiple takes.
  • Adjust tone and messaging depending on the topic and audience.
  • Make prepared scripts feel natural and relatable.

Career, Recruiting & Job-Search Content

Create content around topics such as

  • Job searching
  • Resume development
  • Resume mistakes
  • Interview preparation
  • Interview questions
  • Job applications
  • Recruiting
  • Hiring
  • HR
  • Candidate experience
  • Professional development
  • Career growth
  • Career changes
  • Working with recruiters
  • Job-search strategy
  • Career technology
  • AI tools for job seekers

Content Production

You’ll spend approximately 10 hours per week , t his includes, meetings preparing, recording, and producing content.

The remainder of your day may include

  • Research
  • Content ideation
  • Script development
  • Content planning
  • Performance review
  • Audience research
  • Reviewing hiring trends
  • Developing hooks
  • Testing different content approaches
  • Coordinating with leadership and marketing

Content Ideation & Creative Development

  • Develop original ideas based on the challenges job seekers face.
  • Create compelling hooks and opening lines.
  • Turn complex recruiting or career topics into easy-to-understand content.
  • Contribute to scripts and talking points.
  • Bring personality and creativity to existing concepts.
  • Identify opportunities to turn one topic into several pieces of content.
  • Stay informed about hiring, recruiting, career, and social-media trends.
  • Recommend improvements based on audience response.

Requirements

  • 2–5 years of relevant experience in content creation, social media, video production, digital marketing, presenting, recruiting, HR, education, career development, or a related field.
  • Strong professional English proficiency.
  • Confident, engaging, energetic, and credible on-camera presence.
  • Meaningful experience or knowledge related to careers, recruiting, HR, education, professional development, job searching, resumes, interviews, or hiring.
  • Strong understanding of the job-seeker or candidate journey.
  • Strong verbal communication and storytelling skills.
  • Ability to make career-related topics practical and relatable.
  • Ability to simplify complex subjects without sacrificing accuracy.
  • Experience creating short-form digital content.
  • Ability to deliver scripted and semi-scripted content naturally.
  • Comfortable using or quickly learning a teleprompter.
  • Strong research and ideation skills.
  • Strong organization, reliability, and consistency.
  • Ability to maintain a structured content-production schedule.
  • Ability to work independently in a remote environment.
  • Comfortable collaborating with leadership and marketing.
  • Comfortable receiving feedback and quickly adapting.

Strongly Preferred

Experience creating content related to

Additional advantages include

  • Experience producing content for LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube.
  • Strong understanding of hooks, audience retention, pacing, and storytelling.
  • Experience contributing to scripts or creative concepts.
  • Basic video-editing or production knowledge.
  • Experience testing different messaging approaches and content formats.
  • Startup or technology-company experience.

A strong general content portfolio is valuable, but relevant subject-matter experience should be prioritized

You'll Be a Strong Fit If

You're a camera-confident Content Creator who understands the job seeker and hiring experience , not simply someone who is comfortable reading scripts.

You understand the questions, frustrations, and challenges candidates face when:

  • Building resumes
  • Searching for jobs
  • Applying
  • Preparing for interviews
  • Speaking with recruiters
  • Navigating hiring processes
  • Advancing their careers

You can turn those topics into content that feels useful, relatable, modern, credible, and engaging .
