What You'll Learn - How blockchain analytics are powered through data and service layers - The inner workings of complex DeFi applications - Nuances of international accounting and crypto tax considerations How You’ll Work - 100% remote — work from anywhere - Fully flexible schedule — we care about output, not hours Technical Requirements Required: - 3–5 years of Python development experience, contributing to production systems - Familiarity with FastAPI and SQLAlchemy (preferred) - Solid understanding of REST API design and development - Experience building scalable, event-driven, microservices-based architectures - Proficiency with relational databases (PostgreSQL preferred), schema design, and transactions - Strong coding standards and Unix-based development environment experience Nice to Have: - Experience with Google Cloud Platform - Kubernetes cluster setup and maintenance - DevOps tools and shell scripting (Linux) - Infrastructure-as-code (e.g., Terraform, Ansible) What You'll Be Doing This is an individual contributor role with significant growth potential into leadership.
