Remote • English Voice Recording • Under 60 Minutes • Earn $15–$27+ Per Completed Task

Your voice can help us better understand how people from different language backgrounds naturally speak and interact with voice technology.

Volga Partners is inviting multilingual participants to join a Global Language Research Project focused on understanding natural English speech across different languages, accents, generations, regions, and devices.

Participants will complete a short, guided English voice-recording activity remotely using their own device.

Most participants finish in approximately 20–35 minutes, and the entire task is designed to take less than 60 minutes.

No professional voice, acting, or research experience is required.

🎙️

ImportantRecordings Are in English

Although we are seeking participants from different language and cultural backgrounds, all recording prompts for this project will be in English.

Participants should

Have strong English reading and comprehension skills

Be comfortable reading short English sentences and conversational prompts aloud

Be able to understand and follow written instructions in English

Speak clearly and naturally when recording

You do not need a specific English accent. We want to hear natural voices from people with different language backgrounds.

🌎

What You’ll Do

Selected participants will

Read and record short conversational prompts in English

Use their natural speaking voice and pronunciation

Complete the recording remotely from a quiet location

Follow simple recording and audio-quality instructions

Submit completed recordings through the designated project platform

Respond to the project team if clarification or a rerecording is needed

The goal is clear, natural, everyday speech — no professional recording experience is necessary.

📱

What You’ll Need

Participants must have access to a clear, properly working personal device capable of completing voice-recording activities.

Compatible devices may include

Apple computer, including MacBook or iMac

iPad and Apple computer users are especially encouraged to apply — additional incentive available!

You will also need

Reliable internet access

A quiet indoor recording environment

Strong English reading and comprehension skills

Ability to clearly read English prompts aloud

Fluency or strong proficiency in at least one of the listed language groups
