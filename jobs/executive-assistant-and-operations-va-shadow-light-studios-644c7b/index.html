Executive Assistant and Operations VA

About SLS

Shadow Light Studios accelerates brands, creators, and entrepreneurs with a high-performance content engine built for the scroll. We specialize in viral short-form, disruptive visual design, and full-stack production that drives awareness, trust, and revenue.

We’ve delivered for culture-defining brands including Beats by Dre, Apple Music, Monster, Samsung, X Games, JuneShine, Poppi, Jay Shetty, and Machine Gun Kelly.

Recent wins

Scaled Nectar from $2M → $5M in 12 months

Grew Johnny Chang to 1M followers in under 8 months

Drove 105M+ organic views for Lisa Stelly & Fancy Sprinkles

Produced 79M+ paid ad views for Golden Goose

The Role

We’re looking for a proactive Executive Assistant and Operations VA to be the right hand to operations and leadership. This role removes friction across sales, production, and finance, keeping pods moving fast and leadership focused. You’ll handle outreach, scheduling, finance prep, candidate coordination, and day-to-day organization to keep everything running smoothly.

What You’ll Do

Run podcast and partner outreach using trackers and templates

Own daily PodSync logisticsagendas, notes, and action item capture

Maintain editor capacity dashboards and send clear daily snapshots

Prepare finance categorization in QuickBooks with a clean monthly checklist

Draft and send team notices; maintain communication templates

Manage hiring adminjob posts, challenges, Loom submissions, and interviews

Keep ClickUp organized with accurate owners, dates, and statuses

Record Looms for repeatable processes and maintain an SOP index

What You Bring

3+ years of EA or VA experience supporting an operations lead or founder

Strong English writing and professional tone

Detail-oriented with confidence in Google Sheets and ClickUp

Familiarity with QuickBooks basics and meeting operations

Comfortable recording Looms and following SOPs

Availability during Americas working hours with reliable internet

Experience working within ClickUp

Agency or content production background

QuickBooks Online month-close prep experience

Understanding of video production pipelines (short and long form)

Key Outcomes

Outreach and follow-ups run independently without leadership intervention

Daily PodSync and same-day action items captured consistently

Editor dashboards reflect real-time status and help balance capacity

Finance prep accurate and review-ready with minimal rework

All-team notices are clear, timely, and professional

ClickUp hygiene maintained with no missing owners or overdue tasks

Location & Schedule

Remote

Full-time with strong overlap to CST (Must work in US Time Zone)
