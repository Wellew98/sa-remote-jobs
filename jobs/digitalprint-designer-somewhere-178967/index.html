Digital/Print Designer

  • Experience : At least 5-8 years
  • Employment Type : Full-Time, Fully Remote
  • Sourcing Location : South Africa
  • Schedule : Monday–Friday 9:00 AM–6:00 PM CST
  • Compensation : $2 ,000/month
  • Primary Focus : Digital performance graphics, print collateral design, direct-response ad creative, AI-assisted visual production, and platform asset integration

About the Company

Our client is a full-service marketing agency specializing in high-impact, direct-response digital marketing strategies, media planning, performance creative, and funnel optimization. By combining strategic visual design with data-driven execution, the agency helps businesses scale their brand presence, acquire high-value leads, and maximize customer conversion across diverse digital and print touchpoints.

As the agency continues to grow, it is expanding its creative team to ensure clients receive eye-catching, strategic, and polished multi-channel visual assets that drive engagement and business growth.

Role Overview

We are seeking a versatile Digital/Print Designer to lead the visual execution of high-performing marketing assets across digital platforms and traditional print media.

In this role, you will take ownership of the design lifecycle—translating strategic concepts and brand guidelines into visually compelling paid ads, landing page graphics, marketing collaterals, print assets, and campaign deliverables. You will leverage modern AI-assisted visual and layout tools alongside standard design software to streamline creative production, while maintaining high aesthetic and conversion-oriented design standards.

The ideal candidate is a creative problem-solver with a background in both direct-response digital graphics and print production, proficient in collaborative project management environments and marketing platforms.

Key Responsibilities

Digital & Print Design Execution

  • Design conversion-focused visual assets for paid ad campaigns (Meta, Google, Display), sales funnels, email templates, and landing pages.
  • Create print-ready assets including promotional materials, collateral, brochures, signage, and direct mail assets adhering to strict pre-press specifications.
  • Transform raw messaging and wireframes into polished, high-engaging visual layouts aligned with client brand identity.
  • Maintain visual consistency, hierarchy, typography, and brand standards across all online and offline deliverables.
  • Perform image retouching, color correction, resizing, and asset formatting for multi-channel campaign rollouts.

AI-Enhanced Design & Creative Iteration

  • Utilize AI-driven design tools (such as ChatGPT, Claude, and visual AI generators) for concept generation, layout brainstorming, asset variation, and design ideation.
  • Refine and elevate AI-assisted design drafts to ensure output meets professional standards and exact client branding.
  • Build rapid creative iterations and variations of top-performing ad designs for ongoing campaign testing.
  • Stay updated on emerging AI visual design tools and techniques to maximize production speed and visual quality.

Funnel & Marketing Platform Asset Support

  • Upload, configure, and align visual elements directly within marketing workflows and landing pages in Go High Level.
  • Ensure all digital graphics are optimized for web performance, speed, and cross-device responsiveness.
  • Collaborate with content developers and media buyers to ensure visual creative aligns with direct-response messaging and offer structure.

Workflow & Project Management

  • Track, organize, and manage design task pipelines and project deliverables inside ClickUp.
  • Organize and maintain a clean digital asset management system for graphics, templates, and client design files.
  • Prioritize design deliverables to meet strict campaign timelines and client launch schedules.
  • Receive creative feedback constructively and execute revisions efficiently.

Required Qualifications

  • At least 5-8 years of professional experience as a Graphic Designer, Visual Designer, Digital/Print Designer, or similar agency role.
  • Strong portfolio demonstrating expertise in both direct-response digital asset design (ads, landing page graphics) and print design (pre-press, marketing collateral).
  • Practical experience utilizing AI tools (such as ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney, or platform-integrated AI design features) to accelerate creative workflows.
  • Hands-on experience using ClickUp for project tracking, task organization, and deadline management.
  • Experience integrating design assets within Go High Level (CRM) or similar landing page/marketing platforms.
  • Advanced visual composition, typography, color theory, and layout skills.
  • Thorough understanding of print production processes, print preparation, CMYK color profiles, and file specifications.
  • Strong written and verbal English communication skills for team collaboration and creative alignment.
  • Strong organizational skills and the ability to handle multiple design projects simultaneously.
  • Reliable internet connection and a dedicated remote workspace equipped with professional design software.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Previous experience in a direct-response or performance marketing agency environment and preferably with Healthcare industry experience.
  • Knowledge of basic UI/UX principles for web and mobile landing pages.
  • Experience creating quick motion graphics or short video assets for social media ads.
  • Track record of designing visual assets specifically engineered for high ad conversion rate performance.

Technology & Tools

Candidates should be proficient with or capable of quickly mastering:

  • Adobe Creative Cloud (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign) or equivalent professional design suites
  • ClickUp (Project Management)
  • Go High Level (CRM & Asset Deployment)
  • ChatGPT & Claude (AI Prompting & Visual Conceptualization)
  • AI Image Generation / Enhancement Tools
  • Google Workspace / Microsoft Office

What This Role Offers

  • The opportunity to design key visual assets for a wide range of growing client brands.
  • Direct ownership of both digital performance creative and traditional print media production.
  • A forward-thinking agency environment leveraging modern AI workflows and organized management systems.
  • Consistent full-time hours within standard Central Time business hours.

Ideal Candidate Profile

The ideal candidate is a visually skilled, production-minded designer who understands how design influences user action and campaign performance.

They are equally comfortable preparing print files for production as they are designing high-converting digital ads and landing page elements inside Go High Level. They utilize modern project systems like ClickUp to keep work organized and employ AI platforms like ChatGPT and Claude to speed up ideation, layout planning, and asset generation.

Most importantly, they take pride in visual excellence, speed of execution, and delivering functional designs that elevate client brands while getting real results.

Success Metrics

Success in this role will be measured by

  • Visual quality, brand consistency, and aesthetic impact of digital and print assets.
  • Accuracy and precision in print preparation and pre-press file setup.
  • On-time completion of design deliverables within ClickUp.
  • Seamless integration and formatting of visual assets within Go High Level.
  • Smart, effective use of AI tools to accelerate design workflows without sacrificing quality.
  • Collaboration effectiveness with copywriters, media buyers, and account managers.
  • Contribution of design assets toward campaign conversion and engagement goals.
