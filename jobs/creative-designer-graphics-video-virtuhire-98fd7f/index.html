Read the posting in full · 196 words

Our client in the UK is looking for someone with strong graphic design skills, confidence in video editing and an interest in AI-powered creative tools. You will work closely with the creative and e-commerce teams to produce high-quality product imagery, adapt infographic templates for new launches and edit engaging short-form video content for online marketplaces, websites, and social media channels.

Key Responsibilities

Adapting existing infographic templates for new product listings.

Editing product and promotional videos for e-commerce channels.

Creating new visual assets using AI image generation tools.

Using AI creative tools such as Midjourney, ChatGPT, and Nano Banana to support content creation and workflow efficiency.

Maintaining brand consistency across all creative content.

Following detailed creative briefs and working to deadlines.

Supporting the wider creative team with additional design projects when required.

REQUIREMENTS

Experience using design tools such as Adobe, Canva, Figma, or similar.

Video editing experience with Premiere Pro, After Effects, CapCut, or equivalent software.

Interest or experience using AI creative tools.

Strong attention to detail and ability to follow brand guidelines accurately.

Comfortable working within structured processes and templates.

A positive attitude and willingness to learn quickly.

Interest in e-commerce, digital content, and creative trends.
