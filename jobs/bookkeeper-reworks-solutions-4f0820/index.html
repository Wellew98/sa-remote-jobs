Working Hours9am - 5pm EST

LocationRemote - South Africa

About the Role

We are looking for a detail-oriented and reliable Bookkeeper with hands-on experience using QuickBooks . The ideal candidate will have strong bookkeeping skills, excellent communication abilities, and a professional manner when communicating with clients and colleagues.

Key Responsibilities

Maintain accurate financial records and bookkeeping entries.

Process accounts payable and accounts receivable.

Reconcile bank accounts, credit cards and financial statements.

Manage invoices, payments and expense records.

Assist with month-end reporting and financial documentation.

Maintain organised and up-to-date financial records in QuickBooks.

Identify and resolve discrepancies in financial transactions.

Communicate professionally with clients, suppliers and internal teams.

Assist with additional accounting and administrative tasks as required.

Minimum Requirements

Previous experience working as a Bookkeeper or in a similar bookkeeping role.

Hands-on experience using QuickBooks is essential.

Good understanding of bookkeeping principles and financial recordkeeping.

Strong attention to detail and accuracy.

Excellent verbal and written English communication skills.

Clear, confident and professional telephone manner.

Good organisational and time-management skills.

Ability to work independently, meet deadlines and manage multiple priorities.

Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and other relevant office applications.

Ideal Candidate

The ideal candidate is organised, trustworthy and comfortable handling financial information. They should be confident using QuickBooks, communicate clearly and professionally, and take pride in maintaining accurate financial records.
