Maintenance Coordinator  Location:  South Africa  Schedule:  Full-time, U.S. working hours (CST or EST overlap) Industry:  Property Management Salary Range:  $1,300 – $2,000 USD/month About the Role A growing U.S.-based property management firm, is hiring 2 Maintenance Coordinators to support its expanding portfolio of residential properties. This is a long-term, full-time remote role with a U.S. company that values proactive communication, reliability, and critical thinking. As a Maintenance Coordinator, you'll be the first point of contact for tenants reporting maintenance issues. You’ll triage, prioritize, and assign work orders through platforms like AppFolio and Property Meld — ensuring that repairs are handled quickly and efficiently. Responsibilities: - Respond to maintenance requests from tenants and owners in a timely and professional manner - Triage and prioritize work orders based on urgency and severity - Dispatch service vendors or in-house technicians - Communicate with residents and vendors to follow up on repairs and completion timelines - Use AppFolio and Property Meld to manage and track maintenance issues - Document all communications and status updates within the system - Support the property management team with reporting and vendor coordination Requirements: - 2+ years of experience in a Maintenance Coordinator or similar role within property management - Excellent written and spoken English - Strong customer service orientation — calm, friendly, and solutions-focused under pressure - Detail-oriented and organized — able to juggle multiple requests and priorities - Critical thinker — able to assess maintenance requests and determine the best course of action - Familiarity with urgency ranking of maintenance issues (e.g., emergency vs. routine) - Proficient in AppFolio and Property Meld (strongly preferred) - Able to work full-time during U.S. business hours (CST/EST overlap) Bonus: - Experience supporting U.S. property managers - Familiarity with U.S.-based housing laws or maintenance standards
