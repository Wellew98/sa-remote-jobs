Our client in the UK is looking for a versatile designer to join the team permanently. This is a broad role that touches almost every part of the business - customer-facing creative work, production graphics, UI designs for our data visualisation products, and working on developing the design systems that underpin them. It's a role that rewards genuine range: the ability to shift between a brand campaign brief and a stat pack template, between a motion graphics package and a Figma component, without losing quality in the transition. Everyone on the team has real ownership of their work and real impact on the product and the brand - this role is no different.

Responsibilities

Customer graphics — campaign assets, social graphics, broadcast-ready visuals, and branded content for football clubs, media companies, and athletes.

T3 Studios and video production

— graphics packages, motion-ready assets, templates, and visual support for our studios and production team.

Stat pack design

— our stat packs are used by some of the world's leading broadcasters and rights holders as on-air production tools. Data-rich, visually precise, and built to perform in a broadcast environment.

Toolbox template design

— our Toolbox platform lets clients generate their own graphics. You'll design templates that sit inside the product, balancing creative quality with the constraints of a data-driven system.

Design systems

— we have two design systems: one for our stat packs and one for the Toolbox UI. You'll contribute to both by building out components, maintaining consistency, and helping us scale without losing quality.

Strong proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite — Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects.

Proficiency in Figma.

Experience designing for multiple output formats — social, digital, and broadcast.

Comfortable working across multiple projects and briefs simultaneously without dropping quality.

High standards visible in portfolio detail; receptive to feedback and able to iterate.

Self-directed — doesn't need to be managed to get things done.

Good communicator — can take a brief, ask the right questions, present their thinking.

Collaborative across design, product, and production teams.

Strongly Valued (Differentiators)

Motion graphics or video production experience.

Experience working within or contributing to a design system.

Previous work in a creative agency, sports organisation, or media environment.

Ability to pick up new tools and workflows quickly.

Genuine passion for sport — particularly football.

Platforms & Tools

Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects).

Figma (UI, prototyping, design systems / components).

Motion / video production tooling (After Effects core; bonus for Premiere / Cinema 4D / Lottie exposure).

Awareness of broadcast-spec output (frame rates, safe areas, colour profiles, deliverable specs).

Character

High standards - you care about the detail and it shows in your work.

Good communicator - you can take a brief, ask the right questions, and present your thinking.

Efficient - you know when to spend time and when to move fast.

Receptive to feedback and able to iterate.

Self-directed - you don't need to be managed to get things done.

Collaborative - you're comfortable working across design, product, and production teams.

What You'll Get

A role with genuine breadth and ownership — no two weeks look the same.

Work that reaches real audiences in professional sport and media.

A close-knit, high-quality team that cares about doing things well.
