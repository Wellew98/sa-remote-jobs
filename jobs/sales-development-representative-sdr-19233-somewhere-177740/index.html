Sales Development Representative (SDR)

Employment Type:  Full time, independent contractor

Work Model:  Remote

Work Hours:  US work hours

Role Overview

We’re hiring high-energy, assertive SDRs to re-activate a large base of warm and dormant clinic accounts. These are not cold leads - every account has previously signed up or ordered.

Your role is to

  • Re-establish contact
  • Identify why accounts have gone inactive
  • Drive them back to ordering
  • Route qualified opportunities to our sales reps

This is a discovery-first role, not full-cycle sales. You’ll uncover insights, remove simple blockers, and tee up opportunities for closers.

Key Responsibilities

  • Conduct high-volume outbound calls (80–120/day) to a portfolio of ~1,000 accounts
  • Re-engage clinics that have lapsed (60–90+ days inactive)
  • Deliver promotions and guide customers back to the ordering portal
  • Identify key blockers (pricing, product availability, convenience, etc.)
  • Answer common Tier 1 questions (login help, product info, ordering process)
  • Log all activity, notes, and dispositions in HubSpot CRM
  • Escalate complex opportunities or objections to assigned sales reps
  • Collaborate daily via Slack, email, and phone with the internal team

What Success Looks Like

  • 10+ reactivations per week = baseline performance
  • 20–25 reactivations per week = strong performance
  • ~100 calls/day with 3–5 meaningful conversations
  • Consistent contribution to pipeline for sales reps

Ideal Candidate Profile

  • Assertive, confident communicator (not passive or overly soft-spoken)
  • Accent-neutral English with strong clarity and presence
  • Comfortable speaking with:
  • Admin staff
  • Clinic managers
  • Doctors and business owners
  • Proven experience in:
  • SDR / outbound sales / call center roles
  • High-volume dialing environments
  • Strong discovery skills—can quickly identify the “why” behind inactivity
  • Tech-savvy and HubSpot proficient (or quick to learn)
  • Reliable internet + backup power setup

Tools & Systems

  • HubSpot CRM (primary workflow and tracking)
  • Slack (team communication)
  • Google Workspace (email, docs)
  • VoIP dialer integrated with HubSpot
  • SMS tools (e.g., Salesmsg / Sendinblue)

What We’re Looking For

This role requires someone who can

  • Be direct and persistent in conversations
  • “Lean in” and confidently ask why a client isn’t ordering
  • Create urgency and bring energy to every call
  • Operate independently after onboarding

We are specifically not looking for overly passive communicators - this role requires presence and conviction.
