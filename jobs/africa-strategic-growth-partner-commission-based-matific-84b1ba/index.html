Matific is a fast-growing, AI-first global EdTech company delivering a world-class, research-driven mathematics learning platform for schools, built to be localised quickly to a country's curriculum and language. Used by millions of students, teachers and families in over 70 countries, Matific empowers learners through interactive, adaptive and pedagogically rigorous digital experiences.

Recognised internationally for excellence, Matific has received top industry honours including the GESS, Global Edtech Prize, CODiEs, Academics' Choice, and EdTech Digest awards. It is also part of UNICEF's Blue Unicorn Portfolio and a member of UNESCO's Global Education Coalition.

With over $50M USD invested and a global team of 200+ employees, Matific operates as a modern SaaS organisation – leveraging AI, data, and adaptive learning technology to help schools and ministries improve outcomes at scale. Our mission is to deliver equitable access to high-quality mathematics education and to support meaningful systemic improvement worldwide.

The Opportunity

We're looking for someone who believes in education at scale, for whole school systems and not just a few classrooms, and who can make that happen across Africa. This is a partnership, not a salaried job: you open the doors and close the deals, and you share substantially in the revenue you bring in.

Africa is one of our growth priorities. We have a product, evidence, a regional team and live projects. Matific already works with UNICEF in Ghana and Egypt, with a state government programme in Nigeria, and with delivery partners in South Africa, and ministries across the continent are talking to us about pilots. The groundwork is done. Now we want to turbocharge it.

Location

Africa or MENA, selling into African markets ·

Type

independent partner or small firm, non-exclusive to start

Pay

100% performance-based, uncapped

Key Responsibilities

Open and close deals in the African countries you know well

Sell to ministries, state and county education bodies, and donor-funded programmes (World Bank, UNICEF, GPE and others)

Bring in publishers, distributors and school groups that can carry Matific in their catalogue

Run each deal from first meeting to signature, with our team handling demos, pricing, contracts and delivery

Register your deals with us and keep the pipeline up to date in our CRM

Based in Africa or MENA, with real relationships in African education: ministries, education agencies, publishers, distributors, or donor programmes

You have closed government or institutional deals before, and can say who, what and how much

You understand how education gets bought in your market: who holds the budget and where the money comes from

You are comfortable working on commission only

What You Get

A team behind every deal. Our Africa team and senior leadership join your ministry and partner meetings, and we bring the demos, evidence, pricing, contracts and delivery, so you can focus on the relationships and on moving each deal forward

A large share of the revenue on every deal you originate, well above standard agent rates, uncapped, and agreed per market and deal type

The chance to grow into a longer-term role in the region as the business grows

There is no salary or retainer. Expenses are not covered by default, but we can agree to exceptions in advance, such as representing Matific at a conference.
