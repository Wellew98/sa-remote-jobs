Join Hire Hangar and work with fast-growing global companies while building a long-term career.

Lead Data Analyst

LocationRemote

Working HoursUS Time Zones (PST - EST)

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead the development and execution of analytical frameworks to support business goals
  • Collaborate with stakeholders to translate business questions into data projects
  • Design and maintain dashboards, reports, and advanced analytics models
  • Mentor junior analysts and ensure high standards in data quality and storytelling
  • Recommend process improvements based on data-driven insights
  • Stay ahead of industry trends, tools, and best practices

Qualifications

  • 3+ years of experience in a data analysis or business intelligence role
  • Strong command of SQL and Python/R for analysis and modeling
  • Hands-on experience with BI tools like Tableau, Power BI, or Looker
  • Proven track record of leading projects and teams
  • Excellent communication skills with the ability to explain complex data to non-technical stakeholders

Tools & Technology

  • Python, SQL, or Scala
  • Apache Airflow, dbt, or equivalent pipeline orchestration tools
  • Snowflake, BigQuery, or Redshift
  • AWS, GCP, or Azure
  • Kafka or Spark (advantageous)
  • Google Workspace
  • Slack, Zoom, Jira, and other remote collaboration tools

Please NOTE

It is crucial that you complete the application form in full. As part of the application process, you will be required to record a video. If your application is successful, you will receive an email confirming next steps—the video is the first step of the interview process. If you do not record a video, we will not be able to consider you for ANY open roles
