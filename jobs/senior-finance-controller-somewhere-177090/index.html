Senior Finance Controller

Full time, Independent Contractor

LocationRemote  ​(based in  South Africa)

U.S. business hours (PST time zone)

Role Overview

The Role

This is a department leadership role. You will take full ownership of accounting operations, supervise four offshore team members, and serve as the single accountable owner of month-end close, trust accounting integrity, and financial reporting.

You will work directly with the VP of Operations and CEO, alongside a fractional CFO. The objective is clear: install structure, improve systems, and reduce executive involvement in day-to-day accounting.

This is not an accounting role, it is an ownership role.

Core Responsibilities

Month-End Close & Financial Reporting

  • Own monthly close
  • Ensure complete balance sheet reconciliations
  • Review and approve journal entries
  • Prepare accurate financial statements for leadership

Trust & Owner Accounting

  • Oversee trust account reconciliations
  • Validate owner statement accuracy and payout logic
  • Supervise ~90 monthly owner distributions
  • Ensure fiduciary discipline and financial accuracy

Team Leadership

  • Manage four accounting team members
  • Run weekly cadences and performance management
  • Improve accountability, responsiveness, and output quality
  • Recommend hiring and performance actions (with leadership approval)

Systems & Process Improvement

  • Strengthen internal controls
  • Standardize close procedures and documentation
  • Improve workflow efficiency and automation
  • Demonstrate comfort leveraging AI and technology tools
  • Partner on automation initiatives where appropriate

Budget & Reporting Support

  • Own budget vs actual reporting
  • Maintain and update financial models built by leadership/CFO
  • Provide clear variance analysis

Tax & Compliance Coordination

  • Coordinate with external CPA
  • Manage documentation and deadlines
  • Oversee 1099 compliance and compliance calendar

Qualifications

Required

  • 7–10 years accounting experience
  • 3+ years managing at least 2 direct reports
  • Experience owning full month-end close
  • Reconciliation and internal controls expertise
  • Experience managing remote/offshore teams
  • Strong English communication skills
  • Willingness to work PST hours

Preferred

  • Big 4 or strong mid-tier accounting background
  • Property management, hospitality, or trust accounting experience
  • High-transaction accounting environment
  • Experience leveraging AI tools in accounting workflows

Culture & Reporting

This role reports to the VP of Operations and CEO. We operate in a high-accountability, founder-led environment that values ownership, initiative, and systems thinking.
