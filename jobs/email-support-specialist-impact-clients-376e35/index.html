Impact Clients is a fast-growing coaching and AI education company supporting thousands of members across multiple products. We're hiring a Client Support Specialist to own our email/ticket support desk, the first point of contact for every client question, issue, and request that comes through.

You won't just be responding to and resolving tickets. Our support philosophy is simple: every reply either protects or erodes the client's trust in us. You'll follow a detailed internal playbook covering refunds, billing, access, and technical issues, but you'll also use real judgment to personalize responses, de-escalate frustrated clients, and know exactly when to solve something yourself versus escalate it to the right person.

What you'll do

Monitor and respond to tickets across our support inboxes, hitting response-time targets (4 business hours standard, 1 hour for high-priority issues like billing disputes or threats)

Correctly categorize and tag every ticket for accurate reporting

Handle common cases directly using our documented playbook and canned response library — access issues, billing/commission questions, refund requests, subscription changes, while personalizing every reply rather than pasting a stock response.

Recognize sensitive situations, refund threats, chargeback risk, legal/review mentions — and escalate immediately per our clear escalation ladder

Keep the queue cleanno ticket left without a first response by end of day

Work primarily out of our Helpdesk platform, with Slack for team coordination and Trello for client/refund tracking

1+ years in a customer support, help desk, or email/chat support role

Excellent written English, clear, warm, and typo-free; you can write like a helpful human, not a script

Experience with a ticketing/helpdesk platform (Zendesk, Freshdesk, Gorgias, HelpScout, or similar) — we'll train you on our specific stack

Comfortable working independently on a remote team, following detailed SOPs without needing constant oversight

Strong judgment under pressure — able to read when a situation needs empathy, when it needs firm policy, and when it needs immediate escalation

Reliable internet and a distraction-free home setup

Available to work full time 9 hours per day Mon-Fri

Bonusprior experience in online education, coaching, subscription, or membership-based businesses

Fully remote, work from anywhere within [insert accepted regions/timezones]

Compensation between $1,000–$1,500/month

Clear, well-documented processes, you're never guessing what to do, we have a full internal playbook for nearly every scenario

Real ownership over the client experience, not just ticket-clearing

Direct access to leadership when you need a decision, no bureaucracy

Room to grow as the company scales into new products.
