Junior Graphic Designer (Digital Marketing & E-commerce)

LocationRemote

Employment TypeFull-Time (Contractor)

Working HoursU.S. business hours overlap preferred

Reports toMarketing / Creative Team

About the Role

We are seeking a creative and detail-oriented Junior Graphic Designer to support our marketing team in producing high-quality digital assets.

This role goes beyond basic design execution—we are looking for someone with a strong design eye, creative intuition, and attention to aesthetic quality . You should be able to take direction and elevate it, not just execute tasks mechanically.

You will play a key role in creating performance-driven visuals across digital channels, particularly within e-commerce and Amazon environments.

Key Responsibilities

1. Digital Marketing Asset Creation (60%)

  • Design high-quality assets for:
  • Paid ads (Meta, Google, etc.)
  • Social media (Instagram, TikTok, etc.)
  • Email marketing and landing pages
  • Translate creative direction into visually compelling designs
  • Ensure brand consistency across all assets
  • Iterate on designs based on performance feedback

2. Amazon & E-commerce Graphics (25%)

  • Create Amazon listing images, including:
  • Product feature graphics
  • Infographics
  • Lifestyle-enhanced visuals
  • Optimize designs for clarity, conversion, and user experience
  • Collaborate with the team to align visuals with product positioning

3. Creative Ideation & Design Support (15%)

  • Contribute ideas to improve creative output and campaigns
  • Bring a strong sense of design quality and visual storytelling
  • Stay up-to-date with design trends and apply them appropriately
  • Support light brand and packaging design work (as needed)

Ideal Candidate Profile

Must-Have

  • 3+ years of experience in graphic design
  • Strong proficiency in:
  • Adobe Photoshop
  • Adobe Illustrator
  • Experience creating digital marketing assets (ads, social, e-commerce)
  • Familiarity with AI tools for design support (e.g., Midjourney, ChatGPT, Firefly, etc.)
  • Strong portfolio showcasing:
  • Clean, modern design
  • Marketing-focused visuals
  • Excellent English communication (no language barrier)

Nice-to-Have

  • Experience with Amazon listing graphics
  • Exposure to packaging design
  • Experience working with U.S.-based brands or agencies
  • Basic understanding of performance marketing (what converts)

What Success Looks Like

  • Designs are visually strong, polished, and aligned with brand standards
  • Creative output improves engagement and performance metrics
  • You contribute ideas—not just execute instructions
  • Assets are delivered quickly and with high attention to detail
  • The team trusts your creative judgment and taste

Key Traits for Success

  • Strong design eye and aesthetic judgment
  • Creative thinker with attention to detail
  • Ability to take feedback and iterate quickly
  • Self-starter who takes ownership of work
  • Balances speed with quality
