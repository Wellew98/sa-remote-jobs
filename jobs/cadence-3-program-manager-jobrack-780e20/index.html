Own the Cadence 3 Momentum program end-to-end and keep three busy founders aligned and moving.

$3,000-3,500 USD/month | Full-time | 1pm-9pm CET

We’re

Cadence 3 , a full-service marketing and technical consulting firm working with B2B tech companies. We build tailored marketing content and experiences, and we consult on how to communicate what a product actually does for a customer, through solution-focused storytelling.

Inside Cadence 3 there’s a newer subsidiary program called Cadence 3 Momentum, run by three founders. It’s moving quickly and it’s currently being supported by the wider Cadence 3 team, which is why we need someone dedicated to it. One more thing worth knowing about us: we’ve been remote-friendly since the beginning, long before most companies were working remotely.

We’re looking for a Program Manager to own and run the Cadence 3 Momentum program end-to-end. You’ll be the central connection point between the three founders - pulling what’s needed from each of them, keeping everyone informed and making sure nothing has to be chased. This is a senior role for someone who builds their own context, comes prepared and is confident enough to push busy people for what they need.

📋 Manage the Cadence 3 Momentum program end-to-end from an operations and PM perspective

🤝 Act as the central connection point between the three founders and keep everyone informed

🎯 Keep the program aligned with the broader Cadence 3 operations and company goals

📞 Stay on top of incoming lead calls and deliverables so nothing slips

💻 Support client work operationally (the three founders handle clients for now, though the role may grow into client-facing work over time)

💡 Build context proactively, do your own research and come to the founders with narrowed-down asks rather than open-ended questions

Working hours are 1pm-9pm CET.

To be successful in this role, you need

✅ 5+ years of relevant experience at a senior level

✅ Strong program and project management skills, with a track record of owning something end-to-end

✅ To be very organized and analytical, comfortable juggling many moving parts

✅ To be proactive and confident enough to tell founders what you need and by when

✅ The ability to build your own context and come prepared, rather than needing everything handed over

✅ Experience working with tech clients

✅ To be fluent and comfortable with technology and AI (this will be tested)

✅ To be available 1pm-9pm CET

The person who does well here is open-minded, warm and genuinely good to work with. Communication matters a lot - you’ll be the thread between three founders, so being outgoing and clear really counts. A bit of leadership instinct helps too, because sometimes the job is telling a busy founder that a deliverable will slip unless something lands tomorrow. What won’t work is a rigid “this is my job and no further” mentality; we’d much rather have someone willing to stretch and support across the team.

What’s in it for you?

💰 $3,000-3,500 USD per month

💼 Full-time

🌍 Fully remote

📅 Unlimited PTO

🎓 $500 USD per year professional development and training budget

📱 Mobile stipend

🏥 Healthcare coverage stipend (also for offshore team members)

🤝 A long-term, full-time role where you’re treated as part of the core team

✈️ We aim for a once-a-year full-team offsite (not guaranteed every year) and there are potential travel opportunities

Please note that this is a gross salary and that you are responsible for any tax-related procedures in your country of residence. We kindly ask you to apply only if you are happy to work remotely as an independent contractor.
