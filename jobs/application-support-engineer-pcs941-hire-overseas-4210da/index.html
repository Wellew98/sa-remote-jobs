We're looking for an

Application Support Engineer to help the top customers of a fast-growing AI app builder successfully build and scale their apps. The platform lets business owners describe the software they need in plain language and get a working product. Those customers are not technical, so when the platform's AI agent fails them, they are stuck. You are the person who gets them unstuck, whether that means fixing a bug, advising on architecture, or sorting out an integration.

This is a support role for engineers. You will debug real production issues in customer apps, then take what you learn and fix the underlying platform or AI agent so the same issue never happens again. You will also build automations so your support workflow can scale to hundreds of customers a day. If you are a strong TypeScript and React engineer who likes talking to customers, moves fast, and cares about getting the details right, this role is a strong fit.

What You'll Work On

Customer App Resolution

Fully resolve issues with customer apps, from fixing bugs to advising on architecture to helping with integrations

Work with non-technical customers and explain problems and fixes in clear, simple language

Debug production issues using logs, traces, repros, and minimal test cases

Close tickets at high volume without sacrificing quality or leaving customers half-helped

Product and Platform Improvement

Turn every customer fix into a platform improvement by updating the underlying platform or AI agent so the issue does not recur

Spot patterns across tickets and flag the product gaps that cause the most customer pain

Work with the engineering team to ship fixes quickly and verify they work

Knowledge Base and Documentation

Keep public and internal documentation up to date, including known issues

Write guides and answers that are clear, concise, and tailored to the audience

Turn repeated questions into self-serve resources

Support Automation

Automate your support workflow so you can help hundreds of customers a day

Build tooling, scripts, and AI-assisted workflows that cut repetitive work and speed up resolution

Continuously improve how support runs rather than just working the queue

Incident Response

Be the first point of contact for platform incidents during your working hours

Manage the public status page and customer communication during incidents

Coordinate with engineering on diagnosis and resolution, and follow up with clear updates until it is closed

What You Bring

Strong TypeScript and React background

Solid modern web app fundamentals including HTTP, APIs, authentication, and databases

Proven ability to debug production issues using logs, traces, repros, and minimal test cases

Strong systems and architecture thinking, including scalability, reliability, performance, and security basics

Excellent written communication that is clear, simple, concise, and specific to the audience

Prior experience in a customer-facing role such as support, sales, or pre-sales

A track record of closing 50 or more tickets a day

A bias to ship fast, iterate, and correct mistakes quickly

Good taste and attention to detail, with pride in work that is simple and self-serve

Hunger and a willingness to work hard and operate at any level to get things done

Available to work standard Sydney business hours

Nice to Have

Experience with Postgres, Redis, ClickHouse, Hono, Drizzle ORM, Temporal, or Tailwind

Experience with Cloudflare or AWS and infrastructure as code tools such as CDK or Terraform

Experience building with AI SDKs and LLM-based applications

Experience building automations or internal tooling to scale a support function

Experience managing platform incidents and public status communication

Experience supporting non-technical or small business customers
