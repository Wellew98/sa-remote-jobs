Assistant Controller (Hands-On Senior Accountant)

Priority LocationSouth Africa, Latin America, or Global Remote

Working HoursMonday – Friday, Full-Time (Aligned with US Pacific Time)

Salary Range: $4,000 – $6,000 USD per month (The final offer is at the client’s discretion and depends on the candidate’s interview result, skills, and experience.)

Type of contractIndependent Contractor (Full-time)

Type of jobRemote

About the Company

Our client is a well-established, ~22-attorney U.S. law firm headquartered in Washington State with a substantial international client base across Asia and Europe. The firm values financial rigor, low-ego execution, and long-term stability.

About the Role

We are seeking a sharp, low-ego, hands-on Assistant Controller to join the firm’s confidential finance function and partner directly with the current Head of Finance. This role requires a rare blend of senior accounting expertise and "roll-up-your-sleeves" operational execution—you will be equally comfortable processing high-volume daily invoicing and bookkeeping as you are building attorney-level profitability reports for the CEO. Over a 12-month onboarding and transition roadmap, you will master the firm’s custom-built financial workflows with minimal hand-holding and progressively assume full day-to-day operational ownership of the accounting function as current leadership steps back into an oversight and audit role.

Key Responsibilities

  • High-Volume Invoicing & Operational Bookkeeping: Execute and manage daily client billing workflows (75 to 100 invoices per day), accounts receivable, accounts payable, general ledger entries, and monthly bank/account reconciliations with speed and accuracy.
  • Attorney Profitability & Mixed-Fee Analysis: Build and deliver actionable management and business intelligence reports for the CEO—analyzing attorney productivity and profitability across complex mixed fee models (both hourly billable and fixed-fee engagements) to inform compensation and performance decisions.
  • Timekeeping & Billing Systems Improvement: Audit and strengthen attorney timekeeping discipline, billing accuracy, and realization tracking across domestic and international client matters.
  • Financial Statement Preparation & Review Support: Prepare accurate monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements and maintain clean, audit-ready schedules to support external CPA financial reviews.
  • AI-Assisted Financial Reporting: Leverage modern AI tools alongside Excel and accounting software to streamline variance analysis, management dashboards, and recurring reporting (with strict human verification).
  • 12-Month Operational Ownership Transition: Collaborate closely and respectfully with current finance leadership to absorb institutional system knowledge, maintain strict confidentiality over sensitive firm financials, and ensure seamless continuity across the accounting function (note: payment/disbursement authority remains exclusively with firm ownership) .

Qualifications

Required

  • Senior Accounting Experience: 5+ years of progressive experience as a Senior Accountant, Accounting Manager, or Assistant Controller with strong command of full-cycle accounting and financial statement preparation.
  • Humble, Hands-On "Player-Doer" Mindset: Zero ego—genuinely willing and energized to execute daily bookkeeping and 75–100 daily invoices alongside higher-level financial analysis.
  • Complex Billing & Profitability Modeling: Proven ability to track and analyze profitability across mixed revenue structures (hourly vs. fixed-fee) and improve timekeeping workflows.
  • Self-Directed Learner: Able to step into established, custom-built accounting systems, document workflows, and ramp up quickly without requiring heavy day-to-day training.
  • Excel, Accounting Software & AI Fluency: Advanced Excel skills and practical comfort using AI tools to enhance financial reporting and data analysis.
  • Confidentiality, Availability & Communication: Fluent, clear English communication, consistent availability during US Pacific Time business hours, absolute discretion with sensitive financial data, and a reliable remote setup with backup internet and power.

Preferred

  • Prior accounting, billing, or controller experience inside a U.S. law firm or professional services partnership.
  • Familiarity with legal timekeeping/billing platforms (e.g., Clio, TimeSolv, Juris, BigTime) and international/cross-border client billing (Asia/Europe).
  • CPA, CA, ACCA, or Bachelor’s/Master’s degree in Accounting or Finance.
