About Company

We are an award-winning boutique branding and design agency based in New York City. We build brands, websites and campaigns for high-profile companies across healthcare, aerospace, infrastructure, technology and beyond.

We're a small, fast-moving team that values great design, resourcefulness, ownership and efficiency. You will work directly with agency leadership, strategists, designers and developers.

About the Role

We're looking for an exceptionally proactive, tech-savvy Creative Project Manager who combines strong client management skills with digital production experience and advanced AI proficiency.

This is not a traditional project management or administrative role. We need someone who takes full ownership of accounts, anticipates problems, makes decisions and moves work forward without being chased.

We also want someone who actively uses technology and AI to challenge and improve the way an agency operates.

Key Responsibilities

Project & Account Ownership

  • Own multiple client accounts and creative projects from kickoff through delivery.
  • Serve as a primary point of contact for clients.
  • Translate client requests into clear, actionable creative briefs.
  • Manage deadlines, deliverables, approvals and team resources.
  • Anticipate client needs and proactively identify next steps.
  • Lead efficient, focused internal and external meetings.

Website & Digital Production

  • Manage website projects from initial design through development and launch.
  • Coordinate directly with designers and external developers.
  • Manage staging environments, content population, QA, bug tracking and launch checklists.
  • Understand CMS platforms, website functionality and digital production workflows.
  • Explore emerging tools that make website production faster and more efficient.

AI & Workflow Innovation

  • Use Claude, ChatGPT and other AI tools daily to accelerate project management and production.
  • Build repeatable prompts, templates and automations.
  • Identify opportunities to eliminate unnecessary manual work.
  • Improve how the agency handles briefs, scopes, meeting recaps, reporting and documentation.
  • Share new tools and workflows with the broader team.

Financial & Operational Management

  • Maintain accurate, up-to-date project information in Monday.com.
  • Track project hours, budgets, deliverables and profitability.
  • Identify scope creep before it becomes a problem.
  • Anticipate resource and bandwidth issues.
  • Identify opportunities to grow existing client relationships.

Required Experience

  • 4+ years of project management or production experience at a creative agency, branding studio or digital agency.
  • Experience working directly with US-based clients.
  • Exceptional written and verbal English communication skills.
  • Demonstrated experience independently managing website projects through launch.
  • Advanced, practical experience using AI tools beyond basic copywriting.
  • Proficiency in Monday.com or similar project management platforms.
  • Familiarity with Figma, Slack, Google Workspace and CMS platforms.
  • Experience managing multiple concurrent projects in a fast-paced environment.
  • Strong understanding of creative and digital production processes.

Ideal Candidate

  • Naturally proactive and highly resourceful.
  • Moves quickly without sacrificing attention to detail.
  • Communicates clearly, confidently and concisely.
  • Comfortable making decisions independently.
  • Technically curious and constantly exploring better ways to work.
  • Appreciates exceptional design and understands how to protect the creative process.
  • Can lead a client meeting and then execute the resulting action items themselves.
  • Takes ownership of outcomes, not just tasks.

Interview Process

Candidates should be prepared to

  • Walk us through a website project they personally managed from kickoff to launch.
  • Demonstrate an AI-powered workflow or automation they have built.
  • Share examples of client communication and project documentation.
  • Complete a paid, practical working exercise.

Why Us

Big-agency clients without the big-agency layers. Real ownership, direct access to leadership and an opportunity to help shape how a modern creative agency operates.

We're looking for a long-term team member who wants to grow with us and continually raise the standard of how we work.
