Patient Acquisition Specialist (Inbound Sales & Booking), Part-Time

Location & Employment Details

  • Location: Remote.
  • Schedule: Part-time, approximately 20 hours fixed per week, worked within U.S. Eastern business hours (core window 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM ET, exact shift block to be confirmed).
  • Engagement: Independent contractor, ongoing.
  • Reports to: Clinic leadership.
  • Team: Joins an existing inbound sales and booking team as an additional front-line seat.

About the Company

We are a rapidly growing U.S.-based physical therapy and performance clinic serving athletes and active adults. Every patient works one-on-one with a Doctor of Physical Therapy, starting with an in-depth evaluation. We are an out-of-network, cash-pay practice. That lets us spend real time with each patient instead of working around insurance limits, and it means the first conversation a patient has with us matters a great deal. Patients pay with out-of-network benefits, HSA or FSA cards, or a payment plan.

About the Role

Our patients find us through targeted campaigns and injury consult requests, and every one of these people has already raised their hand. The difference between a lead that books and a lead that goes cold is usually measured in minutes, and inbound volume has grown past what one person can cover at that speed. This role exists to make sure no interested patient waits. You will respond fast, understand each person’s injury and goals, and book them into an evaluation with a clinician. This is not cold calling and it is not pressure selling. It is consultative, helpful conversation with people who want to know if we can help. You will also re-engage past patients around new goals and flare-ups.

Key Responsibilities

  • Respond to every new lead within 5 minutes during your shift by SMS, email, or phone, and follow up persistently and professionally using a multi-touch sequence.
  • Run short discovery conversations using our scripts: injury type and duration, training background, prior physical therapy experience, and goals.
  • Explain our out-of-network, cash-pay model clearly, and handle common objections (price, insurance, timing) calmly and without pressure.
  • Book in-person and virtual evaluations directly into the clinician’s calendar, collect deposits when required, send reminders, and rebook no-shows.
  • Run reactivation outreach to past patients and book follow-up evaluations.
  • Log every conversation in our CRM, keep scheduling accurate, tag lead sources, and write clean intake notes for the clinician.
  • Handle all patient information confidentially and in line with HIPAA.
  • Track and report weekly on response time, booking rate, show rate, and reactivation bookings.

Required Qualifications

  • 1+ year in appointment setting, inbound sales, sales support, or a booking role with a conversion or booked-appointment target.
  • Clear, confident spoken English on the phone and polished written English for SMS and email.
  • Comfortable discussing pricing, payment options, and deposits professionally and directly.
  • Experience working in a CRM and keeping records clean (any platform, our systems will be trained).
  • Persistent follow-up habits: comfortable reaching out several times without becoming pushy.
  • Available for a consistent part-time shift within U.S. Eastern business hours.
  • Reliable remote setup: stable internet, a backup power and connectivity plan, a quiet workspace, and a quality headset.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Background in a healthcare, physical therapy, chiropractic, wellness, fitness, or coaching business.
  • Experience selling a premium or cash-pay service where the client pays out of pocket.
  • Hands-on experience with a CRM or clinic scheduling system.
  • Prior work for U.S.-based clients or patients.
  • Familiarity with HIPAA or handling sensitive personal information.
  • Personal connection to running, strength training, or sport.

Ideal Candidate Profile

  • Fast and dependable: when a lead comes in during your shift, you are on it within minutes.
  • A natural listener who makes people feel heard before talking about price or booking.
  • Confident with money conversations.
  • Persistent without being pushy.
  • Highly organized, with clean notes.
  • Coachable, tracking your own numbers and wanting to improve week over week.

Why Join Us

  • Warm leads only: every person you talk to has already asked for help.
  • Direct line to clinic leadership, with clear scripts, clear targets, and quick feedback.
  • Visible impact on patients and on the business.
  • Room to grow as the clinic expands.
