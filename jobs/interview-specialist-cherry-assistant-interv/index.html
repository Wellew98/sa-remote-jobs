We are hiring an Interview Specialist for a fast-paced, high-volume recruiting client operating in the recruitment and talent acquisition industry. This is a full-time, fully remote position with a U.S.-based company, ideal for someone who enjoys working with people, communicating confidently, and staying productive in a structured, fast-moving environment. You do not need previous recruitment experience to apply. We are particularly interested in candidates coming from backgrounds such as retail, hospitality, customer service, sales, call centres, administration, or other roles that involve regular interaction with people. If you are confident speaking to strangers, comfortable asking questions, able to follow a process, and enjoy working towards daily targets, this could be an excellent opportunity to transition into a professional remote career.

Key Responsibilities

  • Make outbound calls to job applicants using a streamlined dialing system.
  • Conduct multiple first-round interviews throughout the day, averaging approximately one interview every 20 minutes.
  • Welcome candidates, build rapport quickly, and guide them through a structured interview process.
  • Follow provided interview scripts and evaluation criteria to ensure consistent candidate assessments.
  • Ask relevant questions to understand candidates' experience, availability, qualifications, and suitability.
  • Record accurate interview notes and update candidate information in the client's applicant tracking system (ATS).
  • Maintain a professional, friendly, and engaging communication style.
  • Manage a busy interview schedule while maintaining punctuality and efficiency.
  • Communicate with internal team members regarding promising candidates and potential concerns.
  • Consistently work towards daily productivity and performance targets.

Required Qualifications

  • At least 2 years of experience in a customer-facing, people-focused, sales, service, retail, hospitality, administrative, or similar role.
  • Previous experience working in a structured, target-driven or high-volume environment.
  • Strong verbal communication skills and confidence speaking with people from different backgrounds.
  • Fluent English with strong business-level communication skills.
  • Excellent listening skills and the ability to ask questions while maintaining natural conversations.
  • Comfortable following structured processes, scripts, and guidelines.
  • Strong organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple conversations throughout the day.
  • Reliable, self-disciplined, and able to work independently.
  • Based in South Africa and available to work full-time U.S. business hours, Monday to Friday.

Recruitment experience is beneficial but not required.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Previous experience in retail, hospitality, sales, customer service, call centres, or other high-interaction environments.
  • Experience working towards daily or weekly performance targets.
  • Comfortable using computers, digital platforms, and online communication tools.
  • Previous remote or work-from-home experience.
  • Experience handling customer enquiries, conducting consultations, or explaining products and services.

Required Skills & Tools

  • Confident telephone communication and professional call handling.
  • Ability to navigate phone or VoIP calling systems.
  • Zoom or similar video conferencing platforms.
  • CRM systems or other platforms for recording and tracking interactions.
  • Email and calendar tools, including Gmail, Outlook, or Calendly.
  • Accurate typing and the ability to capture information while managing conversations.

Schedule & Pay

  • Full-time position.
  • Monday to Friday, working U.S. business hours (8am–5pm EST/CST/PST depending on assignment).
  • Fully remote position supporting a U.S.-based company.
  • Monthly compensation: R15,000 – R18,000 .

Additional earning opportunities include

  • Weekly performance incentives of up to 50% additional earnings.
  • Stable income and consistent working hours.
  • Annual performance reviews and opportunities for progression.

System Requirements

  • Fibre internet connection with a minimum speed of 25 Mbps.
  • Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher.
  • Minimum 8 GB RAM.
  • Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer.
  • HD 720p webcam.
  • Headset with microphone.

Technical specification verification is required before proceeding with your application.

Benefits

  • Fully remote, stable full-time employment.
  • Opportunity to transition into the recruitment and talent acquisition industry.
  • Exposure to U.S.-based business operations.
  • No commuting and location flexibility.
  • Consistent working hours and structured workflows.
  • Performance-based earning opportunities.
  • Long-term engagement and career development potential.
  • Supportive and structured working environment.

Who Should Apply? We are looking for people who are confident communicators, naturally comfortable interacting with others, and capable of keeping up with a busy daily schedule. Whether your background is in retail, hospitality, sales, customer service, or another people-focused industry, we are interested in the skills you bring to the table. If you are reliable, personable, organized, and motivated to build a career in a professional remote environment, we'd love to hear from you.
