We are seeking a creative and detail-oriented

Graphic Designer

to join our team at D2B. In this remote role, you will be responsible for designing visually compelling graphics and assets that promote our brand, products, and services. You’ll play a key role in enhancing brand identity, supporting marketing campaigns, and engaging our audience across digital and print channels.

Position

Senior Graphic Designer

Salary

PHP 50,000 - PHP 90,000/month

Working Hours & Conditions

Mondays to Fridays, 7:00AM to 4:00PM; full-time (remote)

Holidays

: TBD

Responsibilities

Create and adapt designs across POS, print, digital, and social platforms

Produce high-quality finished art for press, digital, and social outputs

Perform advanced retouching for campaign imagery

Prepare press-ready and platform-specific exports

Develop distinctive brand assets and campaign visuals that are both on-brief and creatively original

Go beyond the brief where possible to add value to client objectives

Collaborate closely with creative, production, and account teams

Contribute conceptually to ideas and design direction, not just execution

Lead and mentor junior designers when needed

Leverage automation tools, templates, and workflows for high-volume rollouts

Suggest and implement AI tools and process improvements to drive efficiency

Maintain brand consistency across formats and markets

Ensure all work is world-class, user-friendly, and aligned with both business and consumer needs

Qualifications

3–5 years’ experience in an agency or high-volume design environment

A strong portfolio showcasing brand and campaign work across digital and print

Expertise in Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign) and Figma

Advanced retouching and finished art preparation skills

Familiarity with automation scripts, asset management, and AI-enhanced design workflows

A solid understanding of digital-first design principles across web, mobile, and social

Exceptional attention to detail and ability to meet tight deadlines

Strong communication and collaboration skills for remote teamwork

A genuine passion for creativity, innovation, and continuous improvement

Portfolio

is required.

Benefits

Competitive salary based on experience and skill set

100% remote role — work from home anywhere in the Philippines

Paid local holidays aligned with the Australian business calendar

Opportunities for training and professional growth

Work directly with a supportive Australian team — no agency middleman

Long-term engagement with a stable and growing business
