We are hiring an Executive Assistant for an independent creator/personal brand in the creative media and publishing industry. This is a Part-Time remote role for a United States (Pacific Time) based client. The role is focused on executive support, operational coordination, and launch execution for a coffee-table book, including social media hygiene, vendor/brand outreach, and production logistics.

The ideal candidate is proactive, outspoken, highly organized, comfortable building SOPs from scratch, and professionally mature—especially when working with artistic content that may include mature/NSFW themes.

Key Responsibilities

  • Own executive calendar, email, daily priorities, and scheduling; coordinate meetings, events, photo shoots, and international travel across PT/ET time zones
  • Build and maintain administrative systems, SOPs, and workflows from the ground up inside Microsoft Teams and Planner; document processes and improve operational efficiency
  • Organize and manage the coffee-table book project and associated creative assets, files, and deliverables across shared drives and project tools
  • Research the client’s existing digital footprint and lead brand cleanup efforts, including managing appropriate content takedown requests
  • Support social media organization, content calendars, posting coordination, profile hygiene, and basic photo/video editing when needed
  • Coordinate book launch planning, promotion, events, and outreach; liaise with photographers, studios, printers, press, and other creative vendors
  • Research and manage brand collaborations, press opportunities, and strategic partnerships; draft briefs and coordinate outreach
  • Maintain and optimize CRM contacts, pipelines, and structured follow-ups
  • Prepare agendas, capture action items, provide concise visual/video updates, and ensure timely follow-through
  • Track basic budgets, invoices, and expenses; coordinate contracts/NDAs and release forms with vendors/talent as needed
  • Grow into a trusted right-hand role, eventually helping coordinate other contractors and team members as the business scales

Required Qualifications

  • 2+ years as an Executive Assistant, Operations Coordinator, or similar client-facing role supporting a founder or creative executive
  • High level of autonomy and resourcefulness; proven ability to take ownership and move projects forward with minimal supervision
  • Demonstrated comfort, discretion, and professional maturity working with fine art photography and figure modeling that includes artistic nudity (non-explicit/non-pornographic)
  • Strict commitment to confidentiality, privacy, and maintaining clear professional boundaries across multiple business identities
  • Hands-on proficiency with Microsoft Teams (including Planner, Calendar, task management, and file collaboration)
  • Comfortable with visual and asynchronous communication, including receiving and recording Loom videos, voice messages, and screen shares
  • Hands-on experience managing calendars, inboxes, and travel/shoot logistics across US time zones
  • Strong organizational skills with the ability to create SOPs and operating systems from scratch
  • Excellent written and verbal communication in English; confident, clear, and respectfully direct communicator
  • Familiarity with social media coordination (Instagram, YouTube, TikTok) and basic content editing
  • Ability to work US Pacific Time core hours and manage deadlines in a fast-paced, creative environment

Preferred Qualifications

  • Background in creative industries (publishing, fashion, modeling, fine art photography, entertainment, or influencer marketing)
  • Experience supporting product or content launches (books, campaigns, events)
  • Experience handling online brand cleanup, digital footprint audits, or content takedown requests
  • Knowledge of release forms, NDAs, and basic contract coordination
  • Experience with CRM administration (e.g., GoHighLevel or similar)

Required Skills & Tools

  • Microsoft Teams (Planner, Calendar, Tasks, File Collaboration)
  • Loom & asynchronous video/voice communication tools
  • Google Workspace (Gmail, Drive, Docs/Sheets)
  • CRM tools (GoHighLevel or similar)
  • Basic photo/video editing and social media scheduling tools

Schedule & Pay

  • Part-Time position; 20 hours/week, Monday–Friday, core hours 9:00 AM–1:00 PM PT (with flexibility as needed); potential to scale to Full-Time
  • Fully remote role for a United States (Pacific Time) based client
  • Pay ranges from R8,000 - R 11,000 per/month depending on experience
  • High-ownership role with process-building opportunities and strong long-term growth potential

System Requirements

  • Internet speed of at least 20 Mbps upload and download
  • Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher
  • 8 GB of RAM or higher
  • Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer
  • HD 720p webcam
  • Headset with microphone

Benefits

  • Competitive pay rates
  • Consistent hours and predictable workload
  • Fully remote work with long-term potential
  • Direct collaboration with the Founder/Creator, making a real impact
  • Supportive and inclusive work environment
  • Opportunity to grow alongside the brand and book launch program
