About Huzzle

At Huzzle, we connect exceptional talent with top opportunities at leading companies across the UK, US, Canada, Europe, and Australia. Our clients include startups, digital agencies, and tech platforms in industries such as SaaS, MarTech, FinTech, and EdTech. Unlike an outsourcing agency, we place you directly with a client where you’re hired in-house as a valued member of their team.

Job Summary

We are looking for a high-energy, results-driven Sales Account Executive to join our growing team. In this role, you will be the driving force behind our revenue growth, taking ownership of the sales cycle from initial discovery to final signature. This is an opportunity for a true "closer" who thrives in a fast-paced environment and is passionate about solving customer problems with innovative solutions. If you are a persuasive communicator with a track record of smashing quotas, we want to talk to you.

Key Responsibilities

Manage the end-to-end sales process, focusing on converting qualified leads into long-term partners.

Conduct high-impact product demonstrations and presentations tailored to the specific needs of potential clients.

Navigate complex organizations to identify key stakeholders and decision-makers.

Negotiate contracts and pricing to ensure mutually beneficial outcomes.

Maintain a robust and accurate pipeline within the CRM to ensure consistent monthly and quarterly forecasting.

Collaborate with the lead generation and customer success teams to ensure a seamless transition for new accounts.

Proven experience as an Account Executive with a heavy emphasis on closing new business.

Demonstrated track record of meeting or exceeding sales targets and KPIs.

Exceptional communication, presentation, and negotiation skills.

Strong ability to build rapport quickly and maintain professional relationships.

High level of self-motivation and a "hunter" mentality.

Resilience and the ability to handle objections gracefully.

Nice-to-Haves

Previous experience selling Software as a Service (SaaS) or technology solutions.

Familiarity with modern CRM tools and sales engagement platforms.

Experience selling into diverse industries or enterprise-level accounts.

💰 Competitive salary

🌎 Fully remote role with flexible work environment

🚀 Work with high-growth global companies and innovative products

📈 Clear progression path into senior sales or leadership roles

🎯 Performance-based incentives and bonuses

🤝 Collaborative, high-performance sales culture
