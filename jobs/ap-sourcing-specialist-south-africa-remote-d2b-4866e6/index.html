We are seeking a dedicated and detail-oriented

Sourcing Specialist

to join our team at Pear Tree. In this remote role, you will be responsible for identifying and engaging qualified candidates through various sourcing channels, including LinkedIn, job boards, and talent databases. You will work closely with our recruitment team to understand hiring requirements, develop effective sourcing strategies, build talent pipelines, and identify candidates who closely match our clients’ needs. Strong research, communication, organizational skills, and the ability to proactively source niche talent are essential for success in this position.

Position

Recruitment Consultant

Salary

ZAR 9,240 to 10,560 ZAR per month

Working Hours & Conditions

SA business hours; Mondays to Fridays, 8:00AM to 5:00PM SA Time; full-time (remote)

Holidays

: TBD

RESPONSIBILITIES

Proactively source and engage candidates using Workable and other sourcing platforms, applying targeted search strategies for current and upcoming hiring needs.

Conduct initial outreach, pre-screening conversations, and resume reviews to assess candidate fit, interest, and qualifications against role requirements.

Maintain accurate and organized candidate profiles and pipelines within the ATS.

Collect and manage candidate requirements such as introduction videos, portfolios, assessments, and other supporting materials.

Coordinate and track the completion of candidate assessments to ensure timely processing.

Collaborate closely with recruiters to ensure the smooth progression of candidates through the early stages of the hiring process.

QUALIFICATIONS

Minimum 2+ years of experience in talent sourcing or recruitment, preferably in an outsourcing or offshore staffing environment.

Experience with an IT service-based business, with Microsoft industry experience being a plus .

Hands-on experience using ATS tools, preferably Workable.

Proven and hands-on experience using

LinkedIn for candidate sourcing is required .

Proven ability to source candidates using LinkedIn, job boards, and other sourcing platforms.

Strong communication and people skills, with confidence in candidate outreach, engagement, and screening.

Highly organized, detail-oriented, and capable of managing multiple sourcing pipelines and tasks simultaneously.

Basic familiarity with typing assessments and candidate evaluation methods.

Ability to accurately review resumes and assess candidate fit against role requirements.

Self-driven, proactive, and able to work effectively in a fast-paced, team-oriented environment.
