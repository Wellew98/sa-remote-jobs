About Us

We are leading the charge in AI technology and no-code solutions, revolutionizing industries such as health, wellness, and enterprise performance. Our mission is to deliver cutting-edge, human-centered technology that empowers businesses to innovate and thrive in a rapidly changing landscape.

Opportunity – No Code and AI Agencies

We are actively seeking partnerships with innovative no-code and AI agencies that share our vision of creating impactful, scalable solutions. This collaboration will enable us to enhance our portfolio of services, driving value for our clients through the power of AI and no-code solutions.

Your Role

As a valued agency partner, you will work closely with us to deliver high-quality, user-friendly solutions that incorporate AI technologies without complex code development.

Responsibilities

Develop and implement no-code applications that effectively utilize AI technology.

Coordinate with our internal teams to ensure alignment on project goals and deliverables.

Engage in brainstorming sessions to enhance our services and create innovative solutions.

Ensure seamless integration of new solutions within our ecosystem.

Stay updated on the latest trends and technologies in no-code and AI development.

A proven track record in no-code development and experience with AI tools.

Demonstrated experience delivering successful projects that leverage AI within no-code frameworks.

Strong collaboration and communication skills to work effectively with our team and stakeholders.

Ability to adapt techniques and methodologies to meet diverse project requirements.

A creative approach to problem-solving and delivering innovative solutions.

Ability to work independently while being a part of a collaborative team environment.

Preferred Qualifications

Experience with tools such as Bubble, Xano, or Supabase.

Familiarity with AI platforms like OpenAI, TensorFlow, or similar.

A portfolio showcasing your work in no-code and AI.

Corporate Culture & Work Ethos

We foster a culture of innovation, performance, and purpose. Success is measured by outcomes, and we thrive in a fast-paced, AI-first environment. Transparency, collaboration, and high standards guide how we work, while our mission is to use technology for good, advancing health, well-being, education, and human performance.
