About Huzzle

At Huzzle, we connect exceptional talent with exciting career opportunities at leading companies across the UK, US, Canada, Europe, and Australia. Our clients range from fast-growing startups and digital agencies to SaaS companies and innovative tech-driven businesses across industries such as MarTech, FinTech, EdTech, and eCommerce. Unlike traditional outsourcing agencies, we place you directly within these companies, where you become an integral part of their team and contribute to their growth.

Role TypeFull-time

Job Summary

We are seeking a driven, energetic, and resilient Sales Development Representative to play a key role in driving our growth. This is a dedicated outbound sales role suited to someone who thrives on making calls and enjoys the challenge of converting cold leads into qualified opportunities. If you have a clear, professional South African accent, a strong work ethic, and the motivation to consistently exceed high-volume targets, this is an excellent opportunity to join a team that values performance, determination, and results.

Key Responsibilities

Conduct high-volume outbound cold-calling campaigns to generate new business opportunities.

Develop and manage a strong sales pipeline, securing a minimum of 20 qualified calls per month.

Confidently handle objections and navigate gatekeepers to connect with key decision-makers.

Accurately record sales activities, interactions, and lead statuses in the CRM to ensure smooth handovers to the sales team.

Keep up to date with industry developments and product knowledge to serve as a knowledgeable and credible first point of contact for prospective clients.

Demonstrated experience in B2B sales is preferred.

Native or fluent, near-native English proficiency.

Excellent communication skills, with the ability to quickly establish rapport over the phone.

A proven ability to perform in a high-volume outbound cold-calling environment.

Strong self-discipline and a consistent work ethic.

A proactive, results-driven hunter mindset with the resilience to handle rejection and stay motivated.
