Join One of the Fastest-Growing Online Education Companies in the World

At ELVTR, we're transforming online education by connecting ambitious professionals with industry leaders from companies like Nike, Netflix, Google, Amazon, and more.

Since launching in 2020, we've grown into a global learning community with over 300 expert instructors, delivering more than 200 live online courses across 12 industries. To date, we've helped 22,000+ graduates worldwide gain the skills, confidence, and connections needed to advance their careers.

Now we're looking for a highly organized and creative Instructional Designer to help us bring exceptional online learning experiences to life. If you're passionate about education, thrive in a collaborative environment, and enjoy turning ideas into impactful courses, we'd love to meet you.

This is a full-time, remote role for candidates based in South Africa.

Why This Role Is Different

At ELVTR, you'll work at the intersection of education, innovation, and collaboration. You'll partner with industry-leading instructors and cross-functional teams to develop engaging online courses that help professionals around the world advance their careers.

From the earliest stages of course planning through production and launch, you'll play a key role in ensuring every learning experience meets our high standards for quality, relevance, and learner engagement.

You'll Love This Role If You

Enjoy managing projects from concept to completion

Thrive in a fast-paced, collaborative environment

Are highly organized and detail-oriented

Love working with experts and helping bring their knowledge to life

Enjoy solving problems and finding creative solutions

Stay curious about learning, education, and emerging technologies

Believe education has the power to transform careers and lives

What You'll Do

Coordinate the end-to-end production of live online courses, from planning through launch

Conduct market research and help define course objectives, learning outcomes, and target audiences

Manage production timelines, priorities, and deliverables to ensure projects stay on schedule

Partner closely with instructors, guiding them through course preparation and rehearsals

Collaborate with Marketing, Creative, Sales, and Student Success teams to deliver high-quality learning experiences

Review course materials, marketing content, and sales collateral for clarity, accuracy, and educational value

Identify and resolve production challenges while maintaining project momentum

Stay informed about industry trends, instructional design best practices, and emerging learning technologies

Continuously share ideas and feedback to improve our courses and production processes

Experience in instructional design, course production, project management, or a related field

Excellent organizational and project management skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities

Strong analytical thinking and creative problem-solving abilities

Outstanding English communication skills, both written and spoken

Comfortable collaborating with senior professionals and subject matter experts

Ability to work independently while thriving in a remote, collaborative environment

Flexibility to collaborate across multiple time zones

Passion for education, lifelong learning, and creating engaging learning experiences

Experience with online education or EdTech is a plus

Why Join ELVTR?

Make an Impact:Every course you help create empowers professionals to build new skills, advance their careers, and unlock opportunities they never thought possible.

Grow Your Career:We're committed to developing our people. As ELVTR continues to expand globally, you'll have opportunities to grow professionally, take on new challenges, and shape the future of online education.

Join a Winning Team:Work alongside passionate educators, creators, marketers, and innovators who are committed to delivering world-class learning experiences and making education more accessible around the world.

Ready to Help Shape the Future of Online Learning?

If you're looking for more than just another instructional design role—if you want to collaborate with industry experts, create meaningful educational experiences, and make a lasting impact on learners around the world—we'd love to hear from you.

Apply today and become part of ELVTR's next chapter of growth.
