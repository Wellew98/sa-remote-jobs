LocationRemote (South Africa)

Contract: ​Independent Contractor. Minimum 6-month contract with potential for indefinite extension. We provide equipment.

ScheduleFull-Time, Monday-Friday, CST

Role OverviewThe Strategic Partnership Hunter

We are a premier insurance technology firm specializing in a niche luxury retail market. We are seeking a high-caliber SDR to drive growth within our retail partnership channel. This is a "Zero-to-One" growth role designed for a "hunter" who possesses exceptional relationship-building skills and the technical acumen to eventually master software demonstrations.

This is a unique B2B sales motion. You aren't selling a tangible product with a high price tag; you are selling a free, high-value referral partnership. We provide retailers with a world-class, free appraisal software that simplifies their daily operations. In exchange, when they issue an appraisal, we gain the opportunity to offer specialized insurance solutions to their customers. When those customers sign up, the retailer earns a referral fee.

Your mission is to get retailers to care about this value-add, cut through the noise of their busy workdays, and secure the partnership.

Key Responsibilities

I. Outbound Prospecting & "Ice Breaking"

  • Strategic Outreach: Manage an intentional, high-quality outbound volume (approx. 30 touchpoints per day) via phone, email, and auto-sequencing tools.
  • Gatekeeper Mastery: Demonstrate the ability to "cut through the ice" with gatekeepers to get the right decision-maker on the phone.
  • Appointment Setting: Introduce the "Free Appraisal Software" hook and the referral fee revenue stream to secure qualified product demos for the leadership team.
  • Persistence: Maintain a high level of perseverance, understanding that retailers are niche, busy, and often hard to catch during standard retail hours.

II. Pipeline & CRM Management

  • Database Hygiene: Keep the CRM (Ontraport) meticulously organized, ensuring all lead statuses, follow-up tasks, and notes are up to date.
  • Nurture Loops: Manage rigorous email follow-up cadences to stay top-of-mind with retailers who have expressed initial interest.
  • Process Optimization: Collaborate with leadership to refine outreach messaging based on real-world feedback from the industry channel.

III. The Growth Path: From SDR to AM

  • Product Mastery: During the first 6–9 months, you will focus on prospecting and appointment setting. As you master the industry nuances, you will receive full training to conduct independent product demos.
  • Account Management: Transition into a full-cycle Account Executive/Account Manager role (9–12 months), owning the revenue-generating relationships and managing the ongoing success of your retail partners.

Who You Are

Experience & Must-Haves

  • South African Advantage: We have found that the South African accent resonates exceptionally well within the luxury retail and global trade market. We are specifically looking for SA-based talent with world-class English and a refined, professional phone presence.
  • Sales Acumen: 4+ years of experience in outbound sales, SDR/BDR roles, or appointment setting.
  • Tech-Inspired Brain: You have a natural curiosity for software and automation. You understand how technology can solve operational pain points for a traditional business owner.
  • Resilience: You have a "thick skin" and the drive to persevere through "no" to find the partners who see our value.
  • CST Alignment: You are 100% committed to working US Central Standard Time hours to stay in sync with retail partners.

Nice-to-Haves

  • Industry Context: Previous experience in luxury retail, insurance, or relationship-driven industries is a significant advantage.
  • Software Sales: Experience selling SaaS or supporting a product-led growth motion.

Why This Role?

  • Elite Training: Receive deep onboarding on the industry and modern software sales processes.
  • Career Trajectory: This is a clear "step-up" role designed to turn a high-performing phone professional into a full-cycle Account Manager.
  • Stable, Cool Culture: We are a smaller, high-energy organization that values professional longevity and makes work fun.
