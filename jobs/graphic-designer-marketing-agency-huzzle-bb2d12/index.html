About the company

Our client is a dynamic creative agency specializing in full-service digital marketing solutions tailored exclusively for the real estate industry. By integrating high-end architectural renders, custom web development, and strategic social media management, the team helps property developers and agents elevate their digital presence and drive lead generation. The agency focuses on visual storytelling and technical precision to bridge the gap between physical spaces and digital experiences, ensuring their clients stand out in a competitive luxury market.

Job Summary

We are seeking a precise, reliable, and Figma-savvy Graphic Designer to join a fast-paced real estate marketing agency. In this role, you will be the engine behind the "Blueprint Studio" deliverables, transforming concepts into high-quality branding, marketing collateral, and digital assets. This is a perfect opportunity for a designer who prides themselves on clean execution and quick turnarounds, offering a clear path to step into creative leadership as the team expands.

Key Responsibilities

Execute a consistent queue of design tasks including branding assets, social media graphics, print materials, and floorplans.

Adapt and build out marketing collateral within Figma based on established brand guidelines.

Manage light web design and presentation work to support client launches.

Handle revisions quickly and accurately, ensuring feedback is implemented with minimal back-and-forth.

Maintain highly organized file structures and workflows within Figma to ensure seamless team collaboration.

Communicate daily with leadership to track progress on deliverables and meet strict deadlines.

Proficiency in Figma: You must be an expert in the tool, from layout design to component management.

Professional Graphic Design Experience: A solid portfolio demonstrating clean, on-brand work (ideally within a marketing or agency setting).

Reliability & Consistency: We value "low-drama" execution - hitting deadlines and delivering polished work every time.

Excellent English Communication: Ability to understand complex briefs and explain design choices clearly.

Nice-to-Haves

Experience in Brand Strategy or the Real Estate industry.

Proficiency in Portuguese.

Experience with Slack for team communication.

Multi-disciplinary skills: Web design, motion graphics, or video editing.

💻 Fully Remote: Work from anywhere with international teams

💰 Tailored Compensation: Salaries vary by client and candidate preference — we’ll match you with options that fit your goals
