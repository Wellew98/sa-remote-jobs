Operations & Digital Content Assistant

Cherry Assistant Eligible locations: South Africa Part-time Admin & Virtual Assistance Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

We are hiring a Operations & Digital Content Assistant for a growing small-business contractor in the Construction & Remodeling industry. This is a Part-Time-to-Full-Time remote role for a California, USA based client. The role is focused on end-to-end administrative support for construction projects, including estimates, proposals, invoicing, change orders, scheduling, cost tracking, and organizing project documentation - plus light content posting. The ideal candidate is detail-oriented, proactive, construction-savvy , an excellent communicator, and comfortable working US Pacific Time hours. Key Responsibilities: - Prepare, edit, and format estimates and proposals based on project scope and inputs from the field - Handle paperwork and submission of fleet account invoices online, ensuring accuracy and compliance (estimates, invoicing, A/R, change orders, customer comms, scheduling, follow-ups, post-job follow-ups (customer satisfaction sign-offs) - Draft, process, and log change orders; ensure scope, pricing, and approvals are captured accurately - Coordinate customer communications (calls, texts, emails) for scheduling, confirmations, and updates - Conduct post-job follow-ups and manage customer satisfaction sign-offs/documentation - Track project costs, budgets, and vendor/subcontractor invoices in spreadsheets/CRM - Maintain organized digital files and folders (e.g., Google Drive) and standardize document templates - Enter, update, and maintain records, pipelines, and tasks in the CRM (GoHighLevel) - Support light marketing tasks: posting photos/videos, organizing media, and basic website updates - Assist with digital marketing campaigns (Google Ads, Meta Ads, SEO testing, newsletters) - Create and schedule engaging content for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and other relevant platforms - Collaborate with the owner/leadership to improve SOPs, streamline workflows, and maintain accurate reports - Design and manage graphics and posts using Adobe and Canva - Manage relationships with influencers, and community networks to expand brand visibility - Provide direct executive support, including calendar management, inbox organization, meeting coordination, and document preparation Required Qualifications: - 2+ years of administrative/operations experience; exposure to construction, remodeling, or trades - 2+ years of hands-on experience preparing estimates/proposals, invoices, and change orders - 2+ years of experience in social media management, digital marketing, or brand account management. - 2+ years experience using Canva, Adobe and CapCut for image and video content creation - Strong customer communication skills with clear, neutral spoken and written English - Proven ability to track costs, manage spreadsheets, and maintain organized project files - High attention to detail, follow-through, and ability to meet deadlines in a fast-moving environment Preferred Qualifications: - Prior experience supporting residential or light commercial remodeling/ADU projects - Familiarity with time-tracking and screen-tracking tools (e.g., Hubstaff) and VoIP phone systems - Experience posting to social media or organizing photo/video assets; basic content editing is a plus Required Skills & Tools: - GoHighLevel (CRM) or similar pipeline/task management tools - Google Workspace (Docs, Sheets, Drive) with strong spreadsheet skills - E-sign and PDF tools (e.g., DocuSign, Adobe Acrobat, or equivalent) Schedule & Pay: - Part-Time-to-Full-Time position; Part-time to start (20 hours/week) with core collaboration during 8:00 AM–4:30 PM Pacific Time (Mon–Fri), potential to scale to Full-Time based on performance and business needs - Fully remote role for a California, USA based client - Pay range: R7,000 - R8,500 per/month - Includes onboarding support, SOP development assistance, and growth potential into a broader role System Requirements: - Internet speed of at least 20 Mbps upload and download - Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher - 8 GB of RAM or higher - Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer - HD 720p webcam - Headset with microphone Benefits: - Competitive pay rates - Consistent hours and predictable workload - Fully remote work with long-term potential - Direct collaboration with the client’s leadership team, making a real impact - Supportive and inclusive work environment - Opportunity to grow alongside a dynamic construction contractor If you’re highly organized, construction-savvy, and excited about blending operations with creative digital support, we’d love to hear from you. This is an opportunity to work directly with a growing contractor, play a key role in streamlining projects, and help elevate a brand during an exciting growth phase.
