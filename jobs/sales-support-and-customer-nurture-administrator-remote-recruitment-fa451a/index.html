Sales Support and Customer Nurture Administrator

Remote Recruitment South Africa (remote) Full-time Admin & Virtual Assistance Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

Role Overview We are looking for an organised, confident and customer-focused Sales Support and Customer Nurture Administrator to support a remote sales team working across two established brands. The successful candidate will take ownership of the administrative and customer-service tasks surrounding the sales process, allowing the sales team to focus on developing opportunities and closing deals. This is a remote role suited to someone who enjoys speaking with people, is comfortable using the telephone and can manage multiple follow-ups, appointments and reporting requirements accurately. Key Responsibilities Provide day-to-day administrative support to the sales team. Confirm sales calls, meetings and appointments with prospective clients. Follow up with contacts after events, enquiries and initial conversations. Contact prospects to gather feedback and establish whether they would like to book a further meeting with the sales team. Identify potential sales opportunities during customer conversations and refer qualified prospects to the appropriate salesperson. Maintain accurate customer, lead and appointment records. Update sales reports, spreadsheets and internal tracking systems. Support the sales team across multiple reporting requirements. Ensure follow-up actions are completed accurately and on time. Assist with customer nurturing throughout the sales journey. Communicate professionally with estate agents, clients, prospects and other stakeholders. Work closely with the Sales Manager and remote sales representatives to keep the pipeline organised. Support the introduction and adoption of new systems, including Salesforce, when required. Candidate Requirements The ideal candidate will have: Previous experience in sales administration, sales support, customer service or customer success. A confident and professional telephone manner. The ability to build rapport quickly with customers and prospects. Strong organisational and time-management skills. Excellent written and verbal communication skills. The confidence to pick up the telephone and contact people proactively. The ability to recognise a potential opportunity and book an appropriate follow-up meeting. Strong attention to detail. The ability to manage several priorities and follow-up tasks simultaneously. A proactive and dependable approach to work. The ability to work independently in a remote environment. Experience supporting salespeople or a sales team would be advantageous. Systems and Tools The successful candidate may work with: JustCall GoHighLevel Excel Google Docs Notion Salesforce, once implemented Other internal reporting and customer-management systems Previous experience with these specific systems is helpful but not essential. The ability to learn new software quickly is more important. Working Hours This is a full-time remote position. The anticipated working hours are approximately: 10:30 a.m. to 6:30 p.m. South African time Some flexibility may be required to accommodate customers or estate agents who request calls later in the day.
