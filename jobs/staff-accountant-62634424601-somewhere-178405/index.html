Job Overview We are seeking an experienced Staff Accountant to support the preparation of full-scope bookkeepi ng services and accurate financial reporting for a portfolio of clients. This role is focused on producing complete and reliable financial information using internal reporting templates, with final review and presentation handled by the Accounting Manager. Key Responsibilities Bookkeeping & Financial Reporting - Prepare full-cycle bookkeeping for assigned clients, including maintaining accurate and up-to-date financial records - Complete bank, credit card, and balance sheet reconciliations - Prepare monthly financial statements (Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow) - Ensure all supporting schedules and documentation are accurate and complete Internal Review & Support - Submit completed financial reports to the Accounting Manager for review , along with variance analysis and identification of potential client issues - Address feedback, revisions, and adjustments as needed to finalize reporting packages - Assist in identifying discrepancies or issues during the close process and support resolution Collaboration & Client Interaction (Limited) - Collaborate closely with internal team members to ensure deadlines and quality standards are met - Communicate with clients as needed to resolve reconciliation issues, discrepancies, or clarify bookkeeping items - Participate in occasional client discussions to support understanding of transactions or account activity (not responsible for presenting financials) - Proactively identify and resolve issues before they escalate Strong communication skills, accounting knowledge , and sound accounting judgment are essential for success in this role.
