Digital Marketing Manager Salary : $2,500-3,000 Schedule: 9 AM to 5 PM Irish Time Fully Remote Location: South Africa About the Company Our client is a growing mortgage company dedicated to helping individuals and families navigate the home financing process with exceptional service and innovative marketing strategies. As the company continues to expand, they are seeking a well-rounded  Digital Marketing Manager  who can take ownership of all marketing initiatives and proactively drive business growth across multiple digital channels. Role Overview The Digital Marketing Manager will own the company's digital marketing strategy and day-to-day execution across social media, paid advertising, content creation, website management, SEO, AI search optimization, and email marketing. This is a highly autonomous role for someone who enjoys wearing multiple hats and taking ownership of marketing initiatives from concept to execution. The successful candidate will be responsible for creating engaging content, managing advertising campaigns, improving online visibility, maintaining the company website, and continually identifying new opportunities to generate leads and grow the business. The ideal candidate is a proactive self-starter who enjoys bringing new ideas to the table, stays ahead of emerging marketing trends, and is excited to leverage AI-powered marketing tools to improve performance and search visibility.
