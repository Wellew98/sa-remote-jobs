About The Role

We are looking for an experienced and detail-oriented ABA Insurance Authorization Specialist to join our client's team.

The successful candidate must have previous hands-on experience managing ABA insurance authorizations across multiple US states. This role requires a strong understanding of ABA-related authorization processes, insurance requirements, and the ability to navigate varying state-specific payer guidelines.

The ideal candidate will be confident managing authorization requirements, ensuring accuracy in submissions, and keeping authorization processes moving efficiently. Previous experience specifically within the ABA industry is essential.

Key Responsibilities

Manage and process insurance authorization requests for ABA therapy services across multiple US states.

Review insurance requirements and verify authorization criteria for different payers.

Ensure authorization requests are accurately completed and submitted within required timeframes.

Monitor authorization statuses and proactively follow up on pending, approved, or denied requests.

Maintain accurate records of authorization submissions, approvals, expirations, and renewals.

Identify missing documentation or information that may delay authorization approvals.

Ensure compliance with payer-specific and state-specific authorization requirements.

Communicate authorization updates and outstanding requirements to relevant internal teams.

Assist with resolving authorization-related issues and delays.

Minimum Requirements

+2 years previous hands-on experience as an ABA Insurance Authorization Specialist or in a closely related ABA authorization role.

Proven experience handling ABA insurance authorizations across multiple US states (essential).

Strong understanding of ABA insurance authorization processes and requirements.

Experience working with different insurance providers and payer requirements.

Excellent attention to detail and strong administrative and organisational skills.

Ability to manage multiple authorization requests and deadlines independently.

Strong written and verbal communication skills.

Preferred Personality Traits

Highly detail-oriented and accurate.

Organised and proactive.

Analytical with strong problem-solving abilities.

Accountable and dependable.

Able to work independently and manage competing priorities.

Comfortable following up and taking ownership of outstanding authorizations.
