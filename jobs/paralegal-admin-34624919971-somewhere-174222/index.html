About the Role

We are seeking a highly skilled Virtual Paralegal & Administrative Assistant to support a solo practitioner lawyer based in Orlando, Florida. This role is ideal for an experienced legal assistant with strong organizational skills, excellent English proficiency, and a keen eye for detail. The candidate will play a crucial role in streamlining legal operations, managing contracts, and optimizing administrative workflows.

Key Responsibilities

- Legal Administrative Support: Assist with document drafting, proofreading, and contract management, ensuring accuracy and compliance.

- Billing & Time Tracking: Enter billable hours into Clio and Bill4Time accurately and efficiently.

- Client & Case Management: Organize and retrieve client meeting notes, maintain systematic records, and track case progress.

- Task Prioritization & Scheduling: Manage daily schedules, prioritize tasks, and ensure the lawyer stays on top of deadlines.

- Document & Version Control: Maintain an organized system for contracts and legal documents, ensuring proper version tracking.

- Communication & Coordination: Liaise with clients, legal contacts, and internal stakeholders to facilitate smooth operations.

- Software Utilization: Work proficiently with Clio, Bill4Time, Google Suite, and Microsoft Office to execute daily tasks efficiently.

Requirements

- Experience: 1-3+ years of experience in a paralegal or legal administrative role.

- English Proficiency: Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to proofread legal documents accurately.

- Technical Skills: Proficient in Google Suite, Microsoft Office, and legal practice management software (Clio, Bill4Time preferred).

- Typing Speed: Minimum 50 words per minute.

- Time Zone Availability: Able to work during Eastern Time Zone business hours.

- Bonus Skills: Trilingual proficiency in English, Spanish, and Portuguese is a plus but not mandatory.
