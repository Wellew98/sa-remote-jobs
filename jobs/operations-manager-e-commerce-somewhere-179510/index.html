About the Role

We’re looking for a hands-on, high-accountability Operations Manager to help run day-to-day execution across a fast-growing e-commerce brand. This role is both strategic and tactical: you’ll keep the business moving, coordinate across teams, solve problems quickly, and make sure nothing falls through the cracks.

This is not a purely administrative role. We need someone who is proactive, resourceful, comfortable working in Shopify and e-commerce tools, and able to jump in when needed to get things done.

Key Responsibilities

  • Own day-to-day operational tasks and help take work off leadership’s plate
  • Coordinate website updates, including:
  • pricing changes
  • homepage/front-page changes
  • pop-ups and promotional messaging
  • basic Shopify edits and troubleshooting
  • Organize weekly and seasonal promotions with internal teams
  • Communicate campaign updates to customer-facing staff and marketing
  • Coordinate with marketing team members and media buyers to ensure promotions are implemented correctly
  • Track inventory and coordinate biweekly inventory updates
  • Work with suppliers/vendors on inventory and operational needs
  • Help manage website and e-commerce workflows, including working with freelancers when needed
  • Monitor issues as they arise and take initiative to resolve them or escalate quickly
  • Support execution around major sales periods like Black Friday, Veterans Day, and other time-sensitive campaigns
  • Act as a hands-on leader who can keep the team aligned and accountable

Required Skills & Experience

  • Mid-level to senior operations experience
  • Strong e-commerce background
  • Experience with Shopify
  • Comfortable using AI tools to work faster and smarter
  • Strong problem-solving and decision-making skills
  • Hands-on leadership style
  • Able to manage multiple moving parts and stay calm under pressure
  • Good communication skills and team coordination ability
  • Detail-oriented, proactive, and resourceful

Preferred Skills & Experience

  • Experience in niche e-commerce brands
  • Familiarity with Klaviyo
  • Familiarity with marketing coordination and promotions
  • Experience working with small teams and wearing multiple hats

Success in This Role Looks Like

  • Leadership has more bandwidth because the ops load is being handled
  • Promotions and website changes are executed quickly and correctly
  • Inventory is tracked reliably
  • Problems are spotted early and solved before they become bigger issues
  • The team stays aligned and operationally organized during busy sales periods
