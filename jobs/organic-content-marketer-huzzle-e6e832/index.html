About Huzzle

At Huzzle, we connect exceptional talent with top opportunities at leading companies across the UK, US, Canada, Europe, and Australia. Our clients include fast-growing startups, digital agencies, SaaS companies, and tech-enabled businesses across industries such as MarTech, FinTech, EdTech, and eCommerce. Unlike traditional outsourcing agencies, we place you directly with innovative companies where you become a valued member of the team.

About the company

Our client is a high-growth partner agency operating at the intersection of the creator economy and digital marketing. They specialize in helping world-class creators and digital founders transform organic audiences into seven and eight-figure businesses. By acting as an embedded growth team, they provide the essential infrastructure for scaling, including full-funnel development, paid media strategy, and high-quality creative production. Their mission is to bridge the gap between content creation and predictable revenue, moving beyond traditional agency models to offer true performance-based partnerships.

With over $50M in revenue generated for their global partners, this scale-up focuses on data-driven systems that include sophisticated ad frameworks, automated nurturing, and high-ticket sales optimization. The team is composed of seasoned entrepreneurs and growth consultants who value agility, measurable impact, and long-term sustainability. Candidates joining this remote-friendly environment will work alongside experts in creative strategy and product development to shape the future of digital entrepreneurship.

Job Type

Full-time

Job Summary

We are looking for a high-energy, strategic Organic Content Marketer to own the growth engine for some of the most influential educators and experts in the creator economy. This is not a role for a "social media manager" who just posts updates; this is a role for a growth architect who understands how to turn deep expertise into scroll-stopping content that drives millions in revenue. Working directly with founders who have built $50M+ brands, you will have the freedom to test, build, and scale content strategies across LinkedIn, Meta, and YouTube to turn organic audiences into predictable business growth.

Key Responsibilities

Strategy & Growth: Own the end-to-end organic strategy for partner brands, focusing on moving prospects from top-of-funnel authority to bottom-of-funnel conversion.

Content Creation: Script and storyboard short and long-form video content, write high-converting copy, and design visual assets like carousels and thumbnails using Canva.

Research & Trend Analysis: Continuously monitor high-level creator trends and platform shifts to maintain a pipeline of fresh, high-performing content angles.

Lead Generation: Build and grow email lists, craft newsletters, and deploy ManyChat automations (comment-to-DM) to capture and nurture leads.

Leveraging Technology: Utilize AI systems to accelerate research, scripting, and design workflows without sacrificing brand voice or quality.

Data-Led Iteration: Track performance metrics across all channels to identify what works, why it works, and how to scale it.

Proven Track Record: Demonstrated experience growing organic social channels and personal brands, specifically for creators, educators, or experts.

Platform Mastery: Deep expertise in Meta (Instagram/Facebook), LinkedIn, and YouTube.

Strategic Writing: Exceptional copywriting skills with a focus on hooks, storytelling, and direct response.

Creative Eye: Hands-on design ability in Canva and a strong understanding of video storyboarding.

Analytical Mindset: You are comfortable reading data and using insights to pivot or double down on content strategies.

Self-Starter Mentality: You thrive in fast-moving, early-stage environments and can own the organic engine with minimal hand-holding.

Nice-to-Haves

Experience with Substack and TikTok growth.

Advanced knowledge of ManyChat or similar automation tools.

Experience working in a high-growth startup or a creator-led agency.
