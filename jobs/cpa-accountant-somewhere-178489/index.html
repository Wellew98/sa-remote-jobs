RoleCPA Accountant

Priority LocationLatin America (Argentina, Colombia, Panama) or South Africa

Working Hours(40 hours/week), aligned to US Central Time

Type of contractFull-time, Independent Contractor

Type of jobRemote

About the Company

We are a high-growth venture studio and healthcare MSO

(Management Services Organization). Our ventures are growing rapidly.

We operate a lean, fast-moving structure focused on scaling healthcare

and technology businesses for future acquisition.

About the Role

This is a critical, high-autonomy role where we need a CPA-level

professional who can step in immediately to own the treasury, reporting, and

compliance functions. You will take full ownership of this role, transitioning our books to

clean, GAAP-compliant standards to ensure we are audit-ready for future capital raises

or company exits.

Key Responsibilities

This role owns the full financial lifecycle, including multi-entity accounting, treasury

management, GAAP compliance, and partner distributions, while ensuring the business

is prepared for potential future acquisition.

Financial Management & Reporting

● Maintain and improve QuickBooks Online setup.

● Oversee both cash and accrual accounting functions.

● Reconcile financials from multiple payment gateways.

● Prepare and maintain clean GAAP-compliant financials.

Multi-Entity & Treasury Oversight

● Manage complex accounting across one central entity and multiple pass-through legal entities.

● Handle inter-entity transfers and state-by-state compliance.

● Lead the monthly/quarterly distribution calculation for partners based on

drawdowns against profits.

● Manage and improve partner capital accounts, shifting current expensing

methods into proper balance sheet treatments.

Audit & Compliance Management

● Prepare the business for potential future acquisition or capital raises

($10M–$60M deals) by maintaining audit-ready records.

● Interface with external CPAs, legal advisors, and partners as needed.

Required Qualifications

● Certification: CPA certification is a non-negotiable requirement.

● Proven experience in US-based accrual and cash accounting and multi-entity

structures.

● Expert-level QuickBooks Online/Enterprise (classes, categories, reporting).

● Ability to operate independently from day one with an ownership mentality; no

handholding required.

● Exceptional written and verbal English skills; ability to provide concise, high-value

financial summaries.

● Precision in documentation and GAAP compliance is non-negotiable.

Preferred

● Systems: Familiarity with PEO platforms like JustWorks for payroll management.
