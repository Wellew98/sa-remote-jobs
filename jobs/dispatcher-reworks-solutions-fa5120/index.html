About the Role

We are looking for an organised, proactive, and detail-oriented Dispatcher to coordinate non-emergency medical transportation (NEMT) services for patients and members in the United States.

In this role, you will be responsible for coordinating transportation requests, assigning trips to drivers, monitoring journeys in real time, and ensuring that members arrive at their destinations safely and on time. You will act as the central point of communication between drivers, healthcare facilities, scheduling teams, and other stakeholders to keep daily transportation operations running smoothly.

The ideal candidate has experience in dispatch, logistics, or transportation coordination and can think on their feet when schedules change or unexpected issues arise. You should be comfortable working across multiple systems, managing competing priorities, and communicating clearly and professionally in a fast-paced environment.

Key Responsibilities

Trip Intake & Scheduling

Receive and review transportation requests through systems such as Zoho Desk and MOVI.

Verify trip details, including pickup and drop-off locations, appointment times, service requirements, and any special accommodations.

Confirm trip readiness, eligibility, and required authorisations in line with company procedures.

Identify missing information or scheduling conflicts before trips are assigned.

Driver Coordination

Assign trips to drivers based on availability, location, vehicle suitability, and service requirements.

Communicate trip details, special instructions, and schedule changes clearly to drivers.

Monitor driver availability and trip progress throughout the day.

Coordinate adjustments when drivers become unavailable or transportation needs change.

Real-Time Dispatch Management

Monitor active trips and track on-time performance.

Respond promptly to delays, cancellations, driver no-shows, vehicle breakdowns, and missed pickups.

Provide routing guidance and support to drivers when needed.

Communicate transportation updates and estimated arrival times (ETAs) to healthcare facilities, medical offices, staff, and family members.

Coordinate return trips, facility wait times, and same-day transportation changes.

Resolve service concerns professionally while prioritising member safety and continuity of service.

Documentation & System Updates

Update trip statuses accurately in dispatch and transportation management systems.

Record delays, cancellations, incidents, and service-related issues.

Maintain complete trip notes and required compliance documentation.

Ensure records are accurate, up to date, and aligned with company procedures.

Problem-Solving & Operational Support

Identify and resolve scheduling conflicts, coverage gaps, and potential service disruptions.

Escalate urgent or complex issues to the appropriate team members or management.

Collaborate with schedulers, drivers, and operational teams to maintain efficient service delivery.

Assist with urgent or additional transportation requests when required.

Requirements

Previous experience in dispatch, transportation coordination, logistics, or fleet operations is required .

Strong organisational and time-management skills.

Excellent verbal and written communication skills.

Ability to manage multiple trips, requests, and priorities simultaneously.

Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to remain calm under pressure.

Comfortable working with scheduling software, transportation management systems, and digital communication tools.

Strong attention to detail when reviewing trip information and maintaining records.

Ability to work independently in a fully remote environment.

Willingness to work US business hours consistently.

Preferred Experience

Experience with non-emergency medical transportation (NEMT) operations.

Familiarity with healthcare scheduling and patient transportation coordination.

Experience using Zoho Desk, MOVI, PCC, or similar dispatch and transportation management platforms.

Understanding of driver coordination, trip authorisations, and transportation compliance requirements.

Familiarity with basic medical terminology and insurance processes.

Experience communicating with healthcare facilities, medical offices, drivers, or patients.

Key Skills & Competencies

Attention to detail

Ensuring trip information, schedules, and documentation are accurate.

Problem-solving

Responding quickly and effectively to unexpected changes and service disruptions.

Communication

Providing clear updates to drivers, facilities, and internal teams.

Coordination

Managing multiple moving parts while keeping trips on schedule.

Customer service

Handling enquiries and concerns professionally and empathetically.

Adaptability

Adjusting to changing priorities in a fast-paced operational environment.

Technology proficiency

Learning new systems quickly and confidently navigating multiple platforms.

What We Offer

Fully remote work from South Africa.

Full-time employment opportunity.

Consistent working hours aligned with US business operations.

The opportunity to contribute to essential healthcare transportation services.
