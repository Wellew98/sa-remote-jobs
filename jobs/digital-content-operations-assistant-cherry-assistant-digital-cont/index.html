We are hiring a Digital Content & Operations Assistant in the motor industry to support a growing business primarily through high-impact social media content creation and video editing.

This is a part-time remote role for a US-based client, centered on building brand visibility, engagement, and lead generation through compelling digital storytelling. The ideal candidate is creative, trend-aware, and highly skilled in short-form video content , while still being organized enough to support light administrative and operational tasks when needed.

Key Responsibilities

Operations & Administrative Support

  • Assist with basic administrative tasks such as submitting warranty claims, invoicing, documentation, and data entry
  • Support digital sales by responding the client primary through text but also calls and emails.
  • Support internal communication and scheduling when required
  • Help maintain organized digital files and content libraries
  • Provide occasional executive support (calendar, inbox, coordination) as needed

Social Media & Video Content

  • Create, edit, and publish short-form video content (Reels, TikToks, YouTube Shorts) tailored to the motor industry
  • Develop engaging content for Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and emerging platforms
  • Transform raw footage into polished, high-performing videos using CapCut, Adobe Premiere Pro, or similar tools
  • Stay up to date with social media trends, sounds, and formats to maximize reach and engagement
  • Support planning and managing a content calendar, ensuring consistent and strategic posting
  • Write compelling captions, hooks, and calls-to-action to drive engagement and conversions

Required Qualifications

  • 2–3 years of Demonstrated success creating Reels, TikToks, and YouTube Shorts that drive engagement.
  • Strong working knowledge of CapCut, Adobe Premiere Pro (or similar), and Canva for creating high-quality content.
  • Comfortable handling operational tasks such as customer communication, invoicing, documentation, scheduling, and data entry while managing multiple priorities.
  • Strong written and verbal English, a customer-first mindset, attention to detail, and the ability to work independently in a remote environment.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in the motor industry or automotive content creation
  • Familiarity with content performance analytics and optimization
  • Experience supporting digital marketing campaigns (Meta Ads, etc.)
  • Basic understanding of branding and storytelling for social media

Required Skills & Tools

  • CRM tools (GoHighLevel or similar)
  • ShopMonkey (similar store management software)
  • CapCut / Adobe Premiere Pro (video editing)
  • Canva / Adobe Illustrator (graphic design)
  • Social media platforms (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook)
  • Google Workspace

Schedule & Pay

  • Part-time position; 9am – 1pm PST (U.S timezone)
  • Fully remote role for a US-based company
  • Pay ranges from R7,000 – R8,000 per month
  • Annual raises, performance bonuses, and paid holidays

System Requirements

  • Internet speed of at least 20 Mbps
  • Computer with 2.4 GHz processor or higher
  • 8 GB RAM or higher
  • Windows 10+ or Mac OS X 10.10+
  • HD webcam and headset with microphone

Benefits

  • Competitive pay
  • Remote work flexibility
  • No commute
  • Long-term client relationships
  • Career growth opportunities
  • Stable hours and consistent workload
  • Supportive and collaborative team environment
