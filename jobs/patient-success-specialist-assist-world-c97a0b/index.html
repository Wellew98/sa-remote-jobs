Company Overview

A US-based, cash-pay telehealth group offering men's health treatment including testosterone replacement therapy (TRT) on a monthly subscription, alongside other lines of business. Patients are cared for remotely, with prescriptions fulfilled by partner pharmacies and shipped direct to the patient.

Role Overview

The business is seeing patients cancel their TRT subscription early, often for reasons a simple conversation could have solved. The Patient Success Specialist is the friendly, confident voice patients hear from between appointments. You'll reach out at the points where patients tend to drop off, check in around refills and shipments, answer routine questions, and make sure each patient feels supported and confident in their treatment. This is not a sales role, but you'll spot the right moments to recommend moving a patient from monthly to quarterly or 6-month billing. The main focus is TRT patients, with follow-ups expanding to other lines of business over time. Start: as soon as possible.

Key Responsibilities

- Proactively call patients at known drop-off points in their subscription to check in and identify any risk of cancellation.

- Reach out around refill submissions and upcoming shipments to ask how treatment is going and set expectations.

- Answer routine questions clearly, e.g. why a shipment arrives in separate packages, shipping timelines, and portal or billing basics.

- Talk through common concerns (like injection discomfort) and flag anything clinical for a hand-off to the medical team. No medical advice is given.

- Recommend switching from monthly to quarterly or 6-month billing when it suits the patient, explaining the discount and the continued quality of care.

- Hand off warm leads and higher-level questions to sales or medical staff with clear notes.

- Follow up by email and text, and keep accurate notes on every patient interaction.

- Share patterns you're hearing (common reasons for cancelling, recurring confusion) so the team can fix them at the source.

Skills & Tools

- Customer success, account management or retention experience (required).

- Healthcare, pharmacy or patient-facing experience is a strong plus; product and medication knowledge will be trained.

- Excellent spoken English that is clear and easy for US patients to understand, with a calm, confident and warm phone manner.

- Strong written English for patient emails and text messages.

- Intelligent, personable problem solver who's comfortable discussing men's health topics professionally and discreetly.

- Comfortable making recommendations without being pushy; not a quota-driven sales role.

- Organised and self-directed; can manage a steady outreach cadence in a CRM or patient platform.
