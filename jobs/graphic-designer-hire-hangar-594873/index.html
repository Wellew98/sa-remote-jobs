Job TitleGraphic Designer (Mid-Level)

LocationRemote

Time ZoneUS Time Zones (PST - EST)

Role Overview

We are seeking a Mid-Level Graphic Designer to support ongoing design needs across digital marketing, brand assets, and visual content. This role is fully remote and requires close collaboration with US-based stakeholders. The ideal candidate has a strong design foundation, hands-on experience with modern design tools, and the ability to reliably work remotely with excellent internet connectivity.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design high-quality visual assets for digital ads, social media, email campaigns, and marketing initiatives
  • Create and adapt designs for landing pages, marketing funnels, and web-based experiences
  • Collaborate with marketing, growth, and product teams to translate briefs into effective visual solutions
  • Use AI-assisted design tools to improve speed, ideation, and creative output where appropriate
  • Maintain brand consistency across all deliverables
  • Prepare design files for handoff, production, and iteration
  • Manage multiple projects simultaneously while meeting deadlines within PT working hours

Required Qualifications

  • Mid-level experience as a Graphic Designer with a strong, relevant portfolio (portfolio submission is required and mandatory for consideration)
  • Proven experience working remotely in a professional setting; candidates must be fluent with remote collaboration tools (e.g., Slack, Zoom, Google Workspace, Asana or similar) and have prior experience working with US or UK-based companies
  • Strong proficiency in core design tools including Canva, Figma, Adobe Photoshop, and Adobe Illustrator
  • Reliable, high-speed internet connection suitable for daily video calls, file transfers, and cloud-based work
  • Solid understanding of visual hierarchy, typography, color theory, and digital design best practices

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience designing paid ads across platforms such as Meta, Google, LinkedIn, or similar
  • Landing page design experience with a focus on conversion and user experience
  • Familiarity with A/B testing concepts and performance-driven design
  • Experience supporting fast-paced or growth-focused teams

Tools & Technology

  • Design: Canva, Figma, Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator
  • Collaboration & Workflow: Slack, Zoom, Google Workspace, Asana (or similar)

Please NOTE It is crucial that you complete the application form in full. As part of the application process, you will be required to record a video. If your application is successful, you will receive an email confirming next steps — the video is the first step of the interview process. If you do not record a video, we will not be able to consider you for ANY open roles.
