Position :  Appointment Setter/Telesales Executive - UK Client)

Salary : £ 650–£ 1,000 (14,000 - 22,000 rand- Strong Fit) £ 750–£ 850 (16,000 - 18,000 rands- experienced)

Working Hours & Conditions : Monday to Friday - UK Business Hours

ROLE OVERVIEW

We are seeking ambitious, professional, and results-driven Telesales Executives to  generate qualified appointments for our Field Assessors. The role focuses on  engaging business owners, occupiers, property managers, and finance decision makers to identify opportunities for business rates savings and secure appointments  relating to potential Business Rates Appeals under the Valuation Office Agency  (VOA) Check, Challenge, Appeal (CCA) process. The VOA allows ratepayers to  challenge their business rates valuation through the Check, Challenge, Appeal  process in England and Wales. [gov.uk], [gov.uk]

The successful candidate will be responsible for outbound prospecting, identifying  qualifying circumstances, overcoming objections, and creating high-quality  appointment opportunities that convert into client instructions.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Conduct outbound calls to prospective commercial property occupiers and  owners.

Identify businesses that may have grounds for a Business Rates Appeal or  review.

Qualify opportunities against company criteria before arranging appointments.

Book high-quality meetings for Field Assessors with decision-makers and senior stakeholders.

Clearly explain the potential benefits of reviewing a property's rateable value.

Accurately record customer information and call outcomes within the CRM  system.

Manage follow-up activity and nurture prospects through the sales pipeline.

Meet and exceed appointment-setting, conversion, and activity targets.

Maintain a thorough understanding of the Business Rates system and  ongoing valuation developments.

Represent the company professionally and ethically at all times.

QUALIFICATIONS

Looking for South African Based candidates!

Sales Skills

Minimum 2 years' telesales, appointment setting, or outbound sales  experience.

Strong objection handling and closing skills.

Proven track record of achieving KPIs and sales targets.

Excellent telephone manner and communication skills.

Ability to build credibility quickly with senior decision-makers.

Resilient, self-motivated, and target driven.

Technical Knowledge

Understanding of the Non-Domestic (Business) Rates system in England and  Wales.

Knowledge of Rateable Values and how they impact Business Rates  liabilities.

Awareness of the VOA Check, Challenge, Appeal process.

Understanding of circumstances that may justify a Business Rates review or  appeal.

Ability to explain complex rating concepts in straightforward business terms.

IT Skills

CRM experience.

Microsoft 365 proficiency.

What Success Looks Like

A successful Telesales Executive consistently generates qualified meetings with  businesses that have realistic potential for Business Rates savings, provides Field  Assessors with fully qualified opportunities, and contributes directly to securing new  client instructions for Business Rates Appeals and related rating consultancy  services.
