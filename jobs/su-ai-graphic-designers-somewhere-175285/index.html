AI Graphic Designer Location: South Africa Salary Range: 1000 - 2500 USD Role Description: The AI Graphic Designer is responsible for creating high-quality visual assets using a combination of traditional design tools and AI-powered platforms. This role blends creative design skills with the technical know-how to generate, edit, and refine graphics using tools like Adobe Creative Suite, Midjourney, DALL·E, Canva AI, and others. The ideal candidate is both visually creative and technologically curious, leveraging AI to streamline workflows and enhance design output across digital and print platforms. Key Responsibilities: - Design digital and print assets including social media graphics, web banners, presentations, brochures, and advertisements. - Generate and refine AI-created images and illustrations to match brand identity and project goals. - Collaborate with content, marketing, and product teams to translate concepts into visually compelling designs. - Use AI tools (e.g., Midjourney, DALL·E, Adobe Firefly, Canva Magic Studio) to ideate and produce initial concepts, then fine-tune for final use. - Maintain consistency with brand guidelines while exploring innovative design approaches. - Optimize visual assets for different platforms and formats (e.g., web, mobile, email, print). - Stay up to date with the latest AI design tools, technologies, and trends to continuously improve processes and output. - Manage multiple design projects simultaneously and deliver high-quality work within deadlines. - Provide input and creative direction for branding, campaigns, and visual storytelling. Qualifications: - Bachelor’s degree in Graphic Design, Visual Arts, Digital Media, or a related field (or equivalent experience). - 2+ years of experience in graphic design, with a portfolio demonstrating creativity and range. - Proficient in Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign) and other industry-standard design tools. - Experience working with AI-powered design platforms (e.g., Midjourney, DALL·E, Canva AI, Runway, Adobe Firefly). - Strong understanding of design principles including color theory, typography, layout, and branding. - Excellent attention to detail and ability to follow brand guidelines. - Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a fast-paced environment. - Strong communication and time-management skills. Preferred Skills: - Basic knowledge of UI/UX design and tools like Figma or Sketch. - Experience creating animated or video content using tools like After Effects or Runway AI. - Familiarity with web design, HTML/CSS, or low-code/no-code platforms. - Comfort with prompt engineering and crafting effective AI prompts for image generation. Details:   - Independent contractor agreement paid 2x monthly. - Client has a desire for long term commitment.
