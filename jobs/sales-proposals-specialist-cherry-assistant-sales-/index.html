We are hiring an Inside Sales & Proposals Specialist for a small home services company in the holiday lighting/home improvement industry. This is a Part-Time (20 hrs/week) remote role for a U.S.-based client operating on Eastern Time. The role is focused on rapid lead response, consultative discovery, creating AI-aided lighting renderings and professional proposals, and closing deals with deposits while maintaining meticulous CRM hygiene.

The ideal candidate is a persuasive, consultative seller with clear, neutral-sounding English; highly organized; tech-savvy with AI tools and CRMs; KPI-driven; and comfortable thriving in a fast-paced, seasonal environment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Own inbound lead intake: call, text, and email prospects quickly; qualify needs, timeline, and budget; and set expectations.
  • Conduct consultative discovery, educate prospects on service scope (design, install, service, removal, storage), and tailor packages to budget (e.g., roofline vs. full décor).
  • Create simple, polished house-lighting renderings using AI/image tools based on discovery insights.
  • Build accurate, professional proposals using pricing guidelines/templates; send promptly (ideally within 24 hours of discovery).
  • Follow up systematically to secure approvals and collect deposits; schedule next steps in the CRM.
  • Maintain impeccable CRM records (Jobber): notes, status updates, tasks, and communication logs.
  • Track and report KPIs (e.g., conversion/capture rate, response times, pipeline status) and propose improvements.
  • Collaborate on streamlining proposal workflows and templates using AI and spreadsheets.
  • Provide basic social media support during lower lead-volume periods, as needed.
  • Participate in role plays, coaching, and occasional video calls; uphold a professional, camera-ready presence.

Required Qualifications

  • 4+ years of inside sales, SDR/AE, or phone-based B2C sales experience with a consultative approach.
  • Exceptional spoken and written English; clear, easily understood communication style.
  • Demonstrated success managing high lead volume with fast response times and structured follow-ups.
  • Hands-on CRM experience (preferably Jobber or similar field service CRMs) and disciplined documentation.
  • Proficiency creating client-facing proposals and basic visual mockups/renderings.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in home services, home improvement, or seasonal services (e.g., lighting, landscaping, HVAC, cleaning).
  • Familiarity with Jobber; exposure to ServiceTitan, ServiceM8, or similar platforms is a plus.
  • Experience using AI tools for design/rendering and process automation; spreadsheet proficiency for pricing.

Required Skills & Tools

  • Jobber (or similar field service CRM)
  • AI productivity tools (e.g., ChatGPT/Gemini) and AI/image rendering tools for simple visual mockups
  • Google Workspace (Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets) and phone/SMS tools (e.g., Google Voice or similar)

Schedule & Pay

  • Part-Time (20 hrs/week) potential to increase hours
  • Core coverage in U.S. Eastern Time, Monday–Saturday, with occasional Sundays as needed. Mon - Fri 11am - 3pm EST/ 5pm - 9pm SAST, Saturdays 9am - 1pm EST / 3pm - 7pm
  • Fully remote role for a U.S.-based client
  • Pay ranges from R7000 to R9000 per month depending on experience and hours worked
  • Includes rapid onboarding, training on workflows/templates, and strong growth potential (including expansion into permanent lighting offerings)

System Requirements

  • Internet speed of at least 20 Mbps upload and download
  • Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher

8 GB of RAM or higher

  • Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer
  • HD 720p webcam
  • Headset with microphone

Benefits

  • Competitive pay rates
  • Consistent hours and predictable workload
  • Fully remote work with long-term potential
  • Direct collaboration with the Founder, making a real impact
  • Supportive and inclusive work environment
  • Opportunity to grow alongside a scaling home services program
