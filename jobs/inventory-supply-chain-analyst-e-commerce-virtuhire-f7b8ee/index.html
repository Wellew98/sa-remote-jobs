Description

Our client is looking for an experienced Inventory & Supply Chain Analyst to take ownership of stock management, inventory reconciliation and supply chain analysis within a fast-paced international e-commerce environment.

This is a broad, hands-on role sitting across finance, inventory, supply chain and stock management . We are looking for someone who understands how stock moves through an end-consumer/e-commerce business and can confidently investigate discrepancies, reconcile information from multiple systems and identify the underlying reasons behind stock variances.

The ideal candidate does

not necessarily need to come from a traditional accounting background . Strong experience within Amazon, e-commerce, retail, DTC or another end-consumer product business is particularly valuable, alongside practical experience in inventory planning, stock reconciliation, replenishment and supply chain operations.

The role requires someone analytical and naturally curious who will not simply identify that numbers do not match, but will investigate why , engage with the relevant teams and take ownership of resolving the issue.

Key Responsibilities

Take full ownership of stock and inventory reconciliations across multiple systems, warehouses and sales channels.

Reconcile physical and system stock levels and investigate any discrepancies through to resolution.

Reconcile

Amazon inventory and stock movements against internal system records.

Review Amazon ledger reports, stock adjustments and system movement reports to identify missing or incorrect transactions.

Investigate stock variances and provide clear explanations and recommended corrective actions.

Track stock movements between warehouses and ensure inventory is accurately accounted for throughout transfers.

Analyse inventory at

SKU level to identify discrepancies, risks, excess stock, shortages and unusual movements.

Support inventory planning, forecasting and replenishment activities.

Monitor inbound stock, shipments, warehouse movements and stock availability.

Pull together information from finance, supply chain, warehouse and operational teams to support accurate reconciliations.

Produce clear stock and inventory reporting for management.

Structure and analyse large datasets to make information easy to understand and review.

Proactively identify issues within stock data and investigate their root causes.

Support month-end inventory reporting and reconciliation processes.

Work cross-functionally with finance, supply chain, procurement, warehouse and operational stakeholders.

Help improve inventory controls, reporting processes and stock management procedures.

Key Requirements

Strong professional experience across inventory management, supply chain, stock control, replenishment, inventory planning or stock accounting .

Previous experience within an

Amazon, e-commerce, DTC, retail or end-consumer product business is highly desirable.

Practical understanding of

Amazon inventory/FBA environments would be a strong advantage.

Demonstrated experience performing detailed stock reconciliations and investigating variances.

Strong understanding of stock movements, warehouse transfers and inventory controls.

Experience working with large SKU portfolios and high-volume inventory data.

Strong supply chain and stock management knowledge.

Some exposure to the financial/accounting side of inventory, including stock valuation, adjustments or month-end reconciliations, is advantageous.

Advanced Excel skills

with the ability to pull, reconcile, analyse and structure large volumes of data.

Experience working with ERP, inventory management or marketplace systems.

Strong analytical and problem-solving ability.

Excellent attention to detail.

The Type of Person We’re Looking For

You will be someone who is naturally curious and investigative . When a reconciliation does not balance, you will not simply report the difference — you will work through the available data, speak to the relevant people and determine what caused it.

You should be comfortable working autonomously in a remote environment and proactively reaching out to colleagues for information rather than waiting for answers to come to you. The role requires a genuine self-starter who takes ownership and follows issues through to completion.

Ideal Background

Candidates could come from backgrounds such as

Inventory Planning / Inventory Analysis

Supply Chain Planning

Stock Management / Stock Control

Replenishment Planning

E-Commerce Operations

Amazon FBA / Marketplace Operations

Inventory Accounting

Supply Chain Analysis

Warehouse & Inventory Management

A profile combining Amazon/e-commerce exposure, inventory planning, SKU-level analysis, replenishment, stock optimisation and strong analytical capability would be particularly relevant to this position.

What Success Looks Like

Stock reconciliations are fully owned and completed accurately.

Inventory discrepancies are investigated, understood and resolved rather than simply reported.

Amazon and internal system inventory levels are consistently reconciled.

Warehouse transfers and stock movements are accurately tracked from beginning to end.

Inventory risks, shortages, excess stock and unusual movements are identified proactively.

Management receives clear and reliable inventory reporting.

Supply chain, finance and stock information is brought together effectively.

Inventory processes become more accurate, efficient and easier to review.
