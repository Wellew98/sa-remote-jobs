Creative Lead

VirtuHire Agency Lead Design & Creative

A UK-based client is seeking a hands-on Creative Lead to own creative vision, strategy, and production for client accounts while working remotely during UK hours.

Our client in the UK is looking for a Creative Lead. You will own the creative vision and quality bar for every client account. You decide what we make and why, write the angles and scripts, storyboard the ads and sign off the finished work before it goes live. This is a hands-on leadership role.

You will think like a strategist, write like a copywriter and open CapCut to build a quick cut or a set of test variations yourself. Final creative sign-off sits with you. You will have the authority to approve work, reject it or send it back for changes.

Key responsibilities

The role covers four areas, and you will spend time in all of them every week.

Creative vision and quality

Own the creative direction for each client account, in line with their brand and goals.

Set and protect the quality bar for all paid social creative.

Review and sign off all ad creative before it goes live.

Build creative guidelines, brief templates and review checklists the team can work from without you.

Creative strategy

Research each client's audience, customer reviews, competitors and ad libraries to find angles worth testing.

Turn research into clear angles: pain points, desires, objections, social proof, offers and founder stories.

Plan creative testing roadmaps with the paid ads team so every account has fresh concepts in the pipeline.

Read creative performance data (hook rate, hold rate, CTR, CPA, ROAS) and decide what to iterate, scale or retire.

Concepts, scripts and storyboards

Write hooks, scripts and on-screen copy for video and static ads across Meta, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn.

Storyboard concepts so the video editor, designer and any UGC creators know exactly what to produce.

Write clear creative briefs for internal production, UGC creators and client shoots.

Present creative concepts and the thinking behind them to clients when needed.

Hands-on production

Build quick edits in CapCut to prove a concept or get a test live fast.

Create test variations of winning ads: new hooks, openings, captions, CTAs and formats.

Adapt assets for different placements and platforms.

Brief and direct the video editor and designer on polished production.

Who you will work with

You report to the Operations Director. Day to day, you plan tests with the paid ads team and brief the video editor and creative designer, who produce polished work from your storyboards.

What success looks like

By day 90 you own creative on every account, with a testing roadmap and a sign-off process the team runs without you.

1. First 30 days: Learn every client account, including their brand, audience and current ad performance. Audit the existing creative and agree a sign-off process with the Operations Director and paid ads team.

2. First 60 days: Take over creative strategy and sign-off on all accounts. Put a creative testing roadmap in place for each client, with new concepts in production from your briefs and storyboards.

3. First 90 days: Show measurable improvement in creative performance on key accounts. Document creative guidelines, brief templates and a review checklist the team uses day to day.

Ongoing, you will be measured on

Creative performance against each account's targets, such as hook rate, CTR, CPA and ROAS.

The number of new concepts tested each month.

How often new concepts beat the current best performers.

Quality and consistency of creative across all accounts.

Client feedback on creative.

What we are looking for

Strategy and production carry equal weight in this role, so we need you to be strong at both.

Strategy and leadership

Three or more years in creative strategy, creative direction or copywriting for paid social, ideally in an agency or a fast growing DTC brand.

A portfolio of paid social ads you conceived, with the results they drove.

A deep understanding of what makes ads work on Meta, TikTok and YouTube, including hooks, pacing, native formats and direct response principles.

Comfortable reading ad performance data and turning it into creative decisions.

Strong copywriting and scriptwriting skills in English.

Confident giving clear, direct feedback and making sign-off calls.

Experience presenting creative ideas to clients.

Production

Able to storyboard concepts clearly, whether sketched, illustrated or built digitally.

Confident editing short form video in CapCut.

Able to produce quick test variations to a good standard without waiting on the editor.

A sharp eye for design, typography and brand consistency.

Organised enough to manage several client accounts and deadlines at once.

Working remotely

Self managed and reliable as part of a remote team.

Clear, concise written communication.

Working hours that overlap with UK business hours.

Nice to have

None of these are required, but they will help you stand out.

Experience briefing and directing UGC creators.

Experience with AI creative tools for images, video or voiceover.

Knowledge of Google Ads creative, including YouTube and Performance Max assets.

Experience with ecommerce or DTC brands.

Familiarity with Meta Ads Manager and TikTok Ads Manager.

Editing skills beyond CapCut, such as Premiere Pro or After Effects.

Experience leading or mentoring a small creative team.
