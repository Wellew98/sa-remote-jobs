SU - Tax Associate

Somewhere South Africa (Remote) Full-time Finance & Accounting Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

Role Description: The  Tax Associate  is responsible for supporting the preparation, filing, and review of tax returns while ensuring compliance with  U.S. federal, state, and local regulations . This role involves analyzing financial information, assisting clients or internal stakeholders with tax-related matters, and contributing to accurate tax planning and reporting. The ideal candidate is detail-oriented, organized, and eager to build expertise in  U.S. federal taxation  and accounting principles. Key Responsibilities: - Prepare and file individual, corporate, and partnership tax returns in compliance with  U.S. federal and state regulations . - Assist in tax research and stay updated on changes in U.S. federal and state tax laws. - Support senior tax staff in audits, tax planning, and special projects. - Review financial records, reports, and documentation to ensure accuracy and completeness. - Identify potential tax issues and recommend solutions or opportunities for savings. - Maintain accurate records and documentation for audit and compliance purposes. - Communicate with clients, stakeholders, or internal teams to gather necessary tax-related information. - Assist in preparing quarterly and annual tax provisions and reconciliations. - Ensure compliance with deadlines and deliverables in accordance with internal policies. Qualifications: - Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, Economics, or a related field. - 1-5 years of experience in tax, accounting, or auditing (internship or entry-level experience acceptable). - Strong knowledge of U.S. federal tax regulations ; familiarity with state tax compliance is a plus. - Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and tax/accounting software (e.g., QuickBooks, ProSeries, or similar). - Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills with attention to detail. - Strong written and verbal communication skills. - Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet strict deadlines. Preferred Skills: - CPA eligibility or progress toward CPA certification is a plus. - Experience with corporate, international, or multi-state tax filings. - Familiarity with ERP or financial management systems. - Strong interest in continuous learning and professional development in taxation.
