Brand Operations Manager

VirtuHire LOCATION: , South Africa (Remote) Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

<p>A fast-growing D2C skincare brand based in the UK is looking to hire a <strong>Brand Operations Manager </strong>to help build the operating engine behind the brand. This is not a passive coordination role. It is a <strong>high-ownership leadership</strong> role for someone who can bring structure to a fast-moving environment, keep teams aligned, solve problems quickly, and drive execution without letting things slip through the cracks. You will work closely with the founder and play a central role in keeping the business organized, accountable, and moving forward.</p><p></p><p><strong>The Opportunity</strong></p><p>This is a high-impact role for someone who wants real ownership. You will help build and run the systems, processes, and cross-functional workflows that keep the brand operating at a high level. You will be involved in weekly planning, team accountability, product launch coordination, vendor management, cross-functional execution, and operational performance. </p><p>This role is also a rare chance to learn how a D2C brand operates end to end. You will gain direct visibility into the full business flow across product, marketing, operations, launch execution, team management, and growth.</p><p>They are going heavy on AI implementation across all departments of the business, so this role is a front-row seat to that transformation. You will have the opportunity to help lead it, learn fast, and stay on top of what is happening in AI across real business functions.</p><p></p><p><strong>What You Will Own</strong></p><p></p><p><strong>Brand Operations &amp; Execution</strong></p><ul><li>Build and manage the company’s project management system in tools like Asana, Notion, ClickUp, Monday.com, or similar.</li><li>Break company priorities into clear weekly tasks, timelines, owners, and deadlines.</li><li>Track progress across departments and make sure work is moving on schedule.</li><li>Identify bottlenecks early and push for resolution before they become bigger problems.</li><li>Create visibility, accountability, and operating discipline across the team.</li></ul><p></p><p><strong>Team Cadence, Leadership &amp; Performance</strong></p><ul><li>Lead daily huddles and keep the team focused on the highest-priority outcomes.</li><li>Coordinate communication across departments so everyone is aligned on what matters most.</li><li>Follow up consistently to ensure execution is happening, not just discussion.</li><li>Resolve internal friction, remove blockers, and keep momentum high.</li><li>Drive a high-output culture and ensure the team is performing at a strong level under your management and governance.</li><li>Help the founder maintain clarity, structure, accountability, and execution discipline across the business.</li></ul><p></p><p><strong>Growth Accountability</strong></p><ul><li>Keep a close check on whether the brand is moving in the right direction and growing.</li><li>Monitor whether the marketing team is performing well and meeting agreed KPIs.</li><li>Escalate misses early, drive follow-through, and make sure growth-critical priorities are not ignored.</li></ul><p></p><p><strong>AI Implementation &amp; Process Improvement</strong></p><ul><li>Help lead AI implementation across departments and identify practical use cases that improve productivity, output, speed, and quality.</li><li>Support teams in adopting new tools, workflows, and ways of working as the business becomes increasingly AI-enabled.</li><li>Stay current on relevant AI tools and applications so the brand remains ahead of the curve operationally.</li></ul><p></p><p><strong>Product Launch Coordination</strong></p><ul><li>Support building the new product pipeline and help move initiatives from planning into structured execution.</li><li>Coordinate with external partners and specialists involved in product development and launch readiness.</li><li>Post jobs, conduct interviews, and hire contractors or consultants as needed for launches, including regulatory specialists, patent or legal partners, industrial designers, graphic designers, photographers, videographers, and UGC creators.</li><li>Manage timelines, communication, and delivery with contractors and consultants.</li><li>Coordinate packaging design workflows and make sure assets, revisions, and approvals move efficiently.</li><li>Organize testing, feedback collection, revisions, and next steps for samples.</li><li>Ensure products are launch-ready by tracking details, approvals, documentation, and execution across all moving parts.</li></ul><p></p><p><strong>Launch Readiness</strong></p><ul><li>Coordinate photographers, videographers, and other creative vendors needed for launch execution.</li><li>Make sure all required details, assets, packaging, documents, and approvals are in place before launch.</li><li>Hold a high standard for detail and completeness so launches are not delayed by preventable issues.</li></ul><p></p><p><strong>What Success Looks Like</strong></p><ul><li>The business runs with more clarity, structure, and accountability.</li><li>Departments are aligned and working from clear priorities.</li><li>Teams operate at a higher level of output and follow-through.</li><li>Marketing teams are meeting KPIs and the brand is growing.</li><li>Launches move faster and more smoothly.</li><li>Vendors and specialists are coordinated well.</li><li>Fewer things fall through the cracks.</li><li>AI becomes embedded into day-to-day workflows in a practical way.</li><li>The founder and team can operate with more focus and less chaos.</li></ul>
