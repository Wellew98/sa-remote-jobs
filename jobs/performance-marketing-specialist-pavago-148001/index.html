What a Typical Day Looks Like

You might start by reviewing campaign dashboards, spend, conversions, CPA/CPL, and ROAS across active accounts.

From there, you’ll optimize budgets and bids, pause underperforming ads, scale winning campaigns, analyze audience performance, and launch new tests.

Throughout the day, you’ll collaborate with creative teams on new ad concepts, review landing-page performance, troubleshoot tracking issues, and analyze lead or revenue quality with sales and marketing teams.

In short

you turn paid advertising budgets into measurable, scalable business growth.
