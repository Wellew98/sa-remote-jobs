Job TitleYouTube Marketer / Podcast Producer

LocationRemote (U.S. Eastern Time availability required)

Position TypeFull-Time

Reports ToExecutive Leadership

Summary

The YouTube Marketer and Podcast Producer is responsible for owning the end-to-end content engine that spans podcast production, YouTube strategy, and social distribution. This role requires a balance of creative vision, operational precision, and performance-driven execution. The ideal candidate is a strategic thinker with a strong editorial sensibility and a proven ability to translate complex ideas into engaging media across platforms. They are expected to drive channel growth, shape content direction, and work cross-functionally to turn media assets into consistent acquisition and engagement tools.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead podcast production from guest identification and outreach through to scheduling, coordination, and post-recording follow-ups
  • Develop and implement a YouTube-first video strategy focused on discoverability, watch time, and channel growth
  • Oversee post-production workflows, ensuring consistent quality and on-schedule delivery in collaboration with editors and design resources
  • Manage a full-cycle content repurposing strategy across LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, and other platforms, including both organic and paid distribution
  • Own the creation of visual and promotional assets, ensuring brand alignment and platform relevance
  • Write and distribute newsletters that support ongoing subscriber engagement and product education
  • Maintain and optimize blog and video content on WordPress for SEO performance and long-tail engagement
  • Monitor performance metrics (retention, CTR, subscriber growth) and adjust content strategy accordingly
  • Actively engage with podcast and video audiences across channels to foster interaction and community
  • Use HubSpot to manage workflows, automate outreach, and report on content performance and audience behavior
  • Create onboarding videos, tutorials, and customer-facing educational assets to support broader marketing goals

Qualifications

  • Extensive experience in content marketing, podcast production, or social-led media execution
  • Demonstrated ability to grow YouTube channels and social audiences through data-informed experimentation
  • Skilled in storytelling, content repurposing, and campaign execution across multiple channels
  • Proficient with Adobe Creative Suite, Canva, HubSpot, WordPress, and podcast distribution tools
  • Familiar with SEO principles, YouTube analytics, and social media ad platforms
  • Adept at managing multiple projects and stakeholders in a remote, fast-moving environment
  • Strategic mindset with attention to operational detail and consistent follow-through
  • Confident communicator with a clear, audience-first approach to content

Work Hours

This role requires availability during U.S. Eastern Time business hours. Flexibility is expected for interviews, live promotions, and cross-functional collaboration.
