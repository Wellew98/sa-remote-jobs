Company Overview

Our team runs marketing and PR for hospitality and wellness brands, with a strong focus on social media presence, press outreach, and influencer partnerships. We're a small, fast-moving team that values clear writing, sound judgment, and someone who can be trusted to run with the execution side of the work.

Role Overview

We're looking for a Virtual Assistant to take over the social media management and PR execution work currently handled in-house. This person will handle the day-to-day posting, community engagement, and outreach that keeps our social and press efforts running smoothly, freeing up the internal team to focus on strategy. We need someone a little more experienced and detail-oriented than a junior hire — someone who can exercise good judgment on tone and messaging, stay organized across multiple moving pieces, and execute reliably without things slipping through the cracks.

Key Responsibilities

  • Post and manage content across social platforms, including Instagram Reels
  • Handle day-to-day community engagement (comments, DMs, audience interaction)
  • Write and send press releases and outreach emails
  • Draft and send newsletters via MailChimp
  • Conduct influencer outreach: researching Seattle-area influencers (via Instagram and Facebook groups) and negotiating rates and terms for partnerships
  • Create simple static graphics in Canva as needed
  • Manage and track tasks through Teamwork, with day-to-day communication in Slack

Skills & Tools

  • Strong copywriting skills — this is the core skill for the role
  • Excellent organizational skills and sound self-judgment on tone and messaging
  • Comfortable pushing content out independently, with a bias toward getting things done accurately
  • Canva experience a plus (basic static design)
  • Metricool experience a plus — training provided (this is where content is scheduled/posted)
  • Slack knowledge a plus
  • Familiarity with Teamwork or similar project management tools a plus
  • MailChimp experience a plus
  • Creative and strategic thinking is a bonus, though the role is primarily execution-focused
