Working Hours

Key Responsibilities

Outbound Prospecting & Pipeline Generation

Work through assigned target accounts using daily cold calls and personalized email outreach.

Research companies, portfolio sizes, markets, technology platforms, and resident experience challenges before contacting them.

Engage multiple stakeholders within each account, including operations, marketing, regional management, asset management, and IT.

Use power dialers, email sequences, and sales prospecting tools to generate qualified opportunities.

Maintain accurate outreach records and activity updates in HubSpot CRM.

Discovery & Stakeholder Management

Lead structured discovery conversations to understand clients' retention, renewal, reputation, and resident experience goals.

Identify buying committees, economic buyers, champions, influencers, and potential blockers.

Adapt sales messaging to different stakeholders and their business priorities.

Coordinate meetings and maintain momentum across multiple decision-makers throughout the sales process.

Product Demonstrations & Deal Closing

Deliver tailored product demonstrations that connect platform capabilities to customer needs.

Build business cases and ROI proposals around resident retention, turnover savings, and reputation improvement.

Prepare proposals, pricing, pilot programs, and portfolio-wide rollout plans.

Manage negotiations, procurement processes, security reviews, and contract discussions through to signature.

Coordinate closed-won account handoffs to Customer Success and support the expansion of successful pilots into broader portfolio agreements.

Pipeline Management & Forecasting

Keep opportunities updated with stakeholder details, meeting notes, next steps, deal stages, and expected close dates.

Maintain accurate weekly forecasts and participate in pipeline reviews.

Nurture prospects that are not immediately ready to purchase.

Maintain sufficient pipeline coverage to consistently achieve quarterly sales targets.

Cross-Functional Collaboration

Work with leadership, Marketing, Product, and Customer Success to improve sales messaging and targeting.

Share market feedback, customer insights, and competitor information.

Stay informed about product updates, customer success stories, and developments in the multifamily property management industry.

Required Experience & Skills

3–5 years of B2B SaaS sales experience, including at least 2 years as a full-cycle Account Executive.

Proven experience generating your own pipeline through cold calling and personalized email outreach.

Track record of independently closing deals worth $25K+ in Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR).

Experience selling to buying committees and managing multiple decision-makers through longer sales cycles.

Strong discovery, product demonstration, business case development, negotiation, and objection-handling skills.

Ability to learn complex, multi-product software platforms and explain their value to different audiences.

Comfortable with daily outbound prospecting without SDR or inbound lead support.

Excellent spoken and written English.

Strong CRM discipline, organization, forecasting, and follow-through.

Self-motivated, accountable, coachable, and results-oriented.

Preferred Experience

Experience in multifamily real estate, property technology (PropTech), or selling to property management companies.

Familiarity with property management systems such as Yardi, RealPage, Entrata, or AppFolio.

Experience converting pilot programs into portfolio-wide agreements.

Proficiency with HubSpot or other CRM platforms.

Familiarity with LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Apollo, ZoomInfo, or similar prospecting tools.

Previous experience in a startup or fast-growing SaaS environment.

Typical Day / Day-to-Day Responsibilities

Your day starts with reviewing your assigned target accounts, researching potential prospects, and planning your outbound outreach. You will spend a significant part of your day making cold calls, sending personalized emails, and connecting with multiple decision-makers within each company.

You will also conduct discovery calls, deliver product demonstrations, prepare proposals, and negotiate with prospects. Throughout the day, you will update HubSpot, track follow-ups, coordinate stakeholder meetings, and keep opportunities moving toward a signed contract. You will regularly review your pipeline and sales performance to stay on track with your monthly and quarterly targets.
