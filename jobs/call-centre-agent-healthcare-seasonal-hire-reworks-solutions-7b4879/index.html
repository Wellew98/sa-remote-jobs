About The Role

We are looking for a customer-focused and professional Call Centre Agent to join our client's growing healthcare team on a seasonal, remote basis.

The successful candidate will serve as the first point of contact for clients, handling inbound calls, assisting with appointment scheduling, responding to general enquiries, and ensuring an excellent customer experience.

This role is suited to someone with previous call centre or customer service experience who is confident communicating over the phone, highly organised, and comfortable working in a fast-paced environment.

The position requires flexibility to work rotating shifts within US operating hours, including weekends, depending on business needs. Candidates must be available to start immediately.

Key Responsibilities

Answer inbound calls professionally and efficiently.

Schedule, reschedule, and confirm client appointments.

Assist clients with general enquiries and provide accurate information.

Deliver friendly, empathetic, and professional customer service.

Accurately capture, update, and maintain client information.

Coordinate appointment schedules and communicate any changes.

Maintain accurate records and ensure all client interactions are documented.

Handle multiple enquiries while maintaining a high standard of service.

Work collaboratively with the wider team to ensure a seamless client experience.

Follow established processes and procedures when handling client enquiries.

Minimum Requirements

Previous call centre experience is essential.

Previous customer service experience in a fast-paced environment.

Excellent English communication skills with a neutral accent.

Strong verbal communication and professional telephone etiquette.

Good computer literacy and accurate data-capturing skills.

Excellent attention to detail and organisational abilities.

Ability to multitask and remain calm under pressure.

Strong interpersonal skills and a customer-focused approach.

Comfortable working remotely and managing responsibilities independently.

Willingness and availability to work rotating shifts, including weekends.

Must be available to start immediately.

Preferred Personality Traits

Friendly, approachable, and customer-focused.

Patient and empathetic when dealing with clients.

Confident and professional over the phone.

Reliable, punctual, and accountable.

Adaptable and comfortable working in a fast-paced environment.

Organised with strong attention to detail. A proactive team player with a positive attitude

Working Hours

The role operates within US business hours, between 9:00 AM and 9:00 PM EST. Candidates must be flexible to work rotating shifts within this operating window, including weekends on a rotational basis, depending on business needs.
