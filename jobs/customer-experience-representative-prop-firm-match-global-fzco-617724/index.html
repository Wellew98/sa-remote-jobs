About PFM

Prop Firm Match Global FZCO is a leading platform for discovering, comparing, and selecting top proprietary trading firms. We provide traders with tools and features to easily compare challenge details, read verified reviews, see accurate payout data, and much more. We're a fast-moving, fully remote team with members from all around the world caring deeply about the quality of what we build. Our culture values ownership, clear communication, and practical impact over fluff.

About the department

Customer Experience is the front line of PFM. We're the team traders turn to when something isn't working, when they need clarity on a firm, a promotion, or their loyalty rewards, or when a payout question needs a real answer. We work across Intercom, Discord, and email to keep response times fast without losing quality and every ticket we close well is a small deposit in the trust traders place in PFM and in the firms we work with.

About the role

Deliver timely, accurate, and empathetic support that enables traders to confidently use Prop Firm Match while maintaining trust with both customers and partner firms. This role ensures every customer interaction contributes to a positive experience and protects the reputation of PFM.

What we look for

Must have

Minimum 2 years of customer support or other client-facing experience, ideally in a remote, high-volume environment.

Strong written communication: able to explain things clearly and empathetically to non-technical customers.

Hands-on experience with Intercom or a similar helpdesk/ticketing platform.

High attention to detail: comfortable verifying specifics (promo codes, payout terms, policy language) before responding.

Strong time management, with the discipline to hit response-time and handling-time targets without cutting corners on quality.

Comfortable investigating and troubleshooting an issue independently before escalating it.

Professional, accountable, and collaborative: works well across teams without needing heavy oversight.

Available to work CET-aligned hours in a fully remote, async-first environment.

Nice to have

Familiarity with proprietary trading firms or the prop trading industry.

Experience using AI-assisted support tools (e.g., Intercom Fin AI or similar) -  kept here as a nice-to-have rather than dropped entirely, since it's still worth listing as a plus.

Experience managing or responding to public reviews (e.g., Trustpilot).

Background in fintech, SaaS, or another fast-paced digital consumer platform.

Hiring stages

We keep our process simple, transparent, and respectful of your time. Here's what to expect:

Application screening

We carefully review all applications to identify candidates whose background aligns with the role's requirements. You'll receive a timely and transparent update on your application status.

Video intro + role fit questionnaire A short async step -  no scheduling required. You'll record a brief video introduction and complete a written questionnaire, helping us understand your background and fit without either side committing to a live call upfront.

Discovery HR Interview

A conversation with our HR team focused on company and culture match -  how PFM works, what remote ownership looks like day to day, and what success in this role looks like.

Real-world task

A short, scoped exercise based on real PFM support scenarios, giving both sides a realistic view of the work before committing further.

Professional interview

A conversation with the Head of Customer Experience focused on your support experience, problem-solving approach, and how you handle real user situations -  including firm-comparison neutrality and integrity judgment calls.

Peer & values conversation

A conversation with a peer from across the team focused on values, culture, and how you work best. You'll have plenty of space to ask questions and get a real feel for life at PFM.

If there's a strong mutual match, we start with an informal conversation to align on expectations, then follow up with a formal written offer. Full transparency throughout, and time for you to make an informed decision. To keep things fully transparent and mutually fair, we complete a reference check before the formal offer is extended. No surprises - just part of how we make careful, informed decisions on both sides.
