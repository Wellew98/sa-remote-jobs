Executive Virtual Assistant

Location100% Remote OPEN FOR SOUTH AFRICA ONLY

Employment TypeIndependent Contractor

Hours30 hours per week

CompensationUSD $1,400–$1,800 per month (based on experience)

About the Role

We are seeking a highly organized, proactive, and detail-oriented Executive Virtual Assistant to support a founder across multiple ventures, including a SaaS business, property operations, and personal administration.

This is a long-term remote opportunity for someone who thrives in a fast-paced environment, takes ownership of their work, and enjoys supporting both business and personal priorities.

Key Responsibilities

Executive & Administrative Support

  • Manage inboxes and calendars across business and personal accounts
  • Coordinate meetings across multiple time zones
  • Prepare proposals, quotes, and business documents
  • Track tasks, deadlines, and follow-ups
  • Maintain organized records, spreadsheets, and documentation

Operations & Finance Support

  • Assist with project and operational coordination
  • Support invoicing, expense tracking, receipt reconciliation, and payment follow-ups
  • Conduct research and help create processes and SOPs

Property & Personal Support

  • Coordinate with suppliers, contractors, and vendors
  • Assist with property-related administration and communications
  • Manage travel arrangements, appointments, subscriptions, and general life admin
  • Make phone calls on behalf of the founder when required

Tools You'll Use

Examples include

  • Google Workspace
  • ClickUp
  • HubSpot
  • Slack
  • Xero
  • Notion
  • PandaDoc
  • ChatGPT and other AI tools

Experience with all tools is not required, but you should be comfortable learning new technology quickly.

What We're Looking For

  • Excellent spoken and written English
  • Strong organizational skills and attention to detail
  • Ability to work independently and proactively
  • Professional communication skills and confidence making phone calls
  • High level of discretion and confidentiality
  • Experience supporting executives, founders, or small business owners is preferred
  • Familiarity with AI tools such as ChatGPT or Claude is highly desirable
