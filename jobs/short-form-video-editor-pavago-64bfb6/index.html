Short-Form Video Editor – Reels & Premium Brand Content | Remote

Position Type

Fractional, Remote

Function

Creative / Video Production

About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a

Short-Form Video / Reels Editor

to transform raw footage and existing brand assets into polished, premium short-form video content.

This is an

execution-focused creative role , not a social media strategy or management position. You’ll receive footage, assets, and creative direction and be responsible for turning them into refined, brand-aligned Reels and short-form videos .

We’re specifically looking for someone whose work feels premium, intentional, and professionally produced—not the typical influencer or template-driven CapCut aesthetic .

What You’ll Own

Short-Form Video Editing

Edit raw footage into polished

Reels and short-form videos

Create strong pacing and natural visual flow

Select the strongest footage and structure edits effectively

Trim and sequence clips with attention to rhythm and storytelling

Incorporate existing brand assets into finished videos

Prepare final videos for social platforms and short-form placements

Premium Visual Execution

Maintain a sophisticated, premium visual aesthetic

Apply professional

color correction and color grading

Ensure transitions and effects complement rather than overpower the content

Maintain consistency with existing brand direction

Produce clean, intentional edits rather than heavily templated social content

Sound & Music

Select music that complements the brand and creative direction

Edit audio for clean, professional output

Balance dialogue, music, and other audio elements appropriately

Use sound intentionally to improve pacing and overall production quality

Creative Production

Work from provided footage, assets, and creative direction

Turn existing materials into finished, publication-ready content

Incorporate feedback efficiently

Manage multiple edits and revisions as needed

Maintain organized project files and final exports

Help maintain a backlog of completed short-form creative

Requirements

Professional experience editing short-form video, Reels, branded content, or commercial social content

Strong portfolio or reel demonstrating high-quality video editing

Excellent sense of

pacing, rhythm, composition, and storytelling

Strong color correction and color-grading skills

Strong judgment around music and sound selection

Ability to create premium brand content without relying heavily on templates or trendy effects

Strong attention to detail

Ability to work independently from provided creative direction

Comfortable working in a

fractional, remote capacity

Ability to consistently deliver polished creative on deadline

Preferred Skills

Experience producing content for premium, luxury, lifestyle, or design-conscious brands

Strong experience with professional video-editing software

Experience editing Instagram Reels and other vertical short-form formats

Understanding of typography and motion graphics for video

Experience working with existing brand systems and creative direction

Strong color-grading capabilities

Experience selecting and integrating music and sound into branded content

Tools & Technology

Adobe Premiere Pro | DaVinci Resolve | Final Cut Pro | After Effects | Professional Color-Grading Tools | Audio Editing Tools

Equivalent professional editing tools are also welcome.

What Makes You a Strong Fit

You’ll likely thrive in this role if you

Have a strong visual eye and can immediately recognize when an edit feels polished

Understand the difference between

premium branded content and typical influencer-style editing

Know when to keep an edit clean rather than adding unnecessary effects

Have excellent instincts for pacing, music, and sound

Can elevate raw footage through editing and color

Work well from an established creative direction

Are highly execution-oriented and don’t need to own social strategy

Can consistently deliver refined, brand-ready work

Take feedback well and turn revisions around efficiently

What a Typical Project Looks Like

You’ll receive raw footage, existing creative assets, and direction for a piece of short-form content.

From there, you’ll select the strongest footage, build the edit, establish pacing, refine the color, select appropriate music and sound, incorporate brand elements, and deliver a polished final video ready to publish.

In short

we provide the footage, assets, and direction; you turn them into premium short-form content.

Key Metrics for Success

Quality and polish of finished videos

Consistency with the established brand aesthetic

Strength of editing and pacing

Quality of color grading

Effectiveness of music and sound selection

Turnaround time

Accuracy to creative direction

Efficiency incorporating revisions

Consistency across multiple pieces of content

Reliability of fractional creative support
