About Pavago

Pavago helps companies hire top remote talent globally across sales, operations, marketing, customer support, executive assistance, finance, and technical roles. We focus on speed, quality, and long-term fit by delivering fully vetted candidates ready to interview quickly.

We are growing fast and looking for a high-performing Recruiter who can move with urgency, communicate confidently, and identify top talent across multiple industries and regions.

Key Responsibilities

Source and identify qualified candidates through LinkedIn, job boards, databases, referrals, and outbound outreach

Conduct phone screenings and recruiter interviews to evaluate communication, professionalism, technical fit, and overall candidate quality

Manage multiple open roles simultaneously while maintaining speed and organization

Coordinate candidate pipeline stages and maintain accurate updates inside internal systems

Present fully vetted candidate packages including resumes, interview notes, salary expectations, and recordings

Work closely with Account Managers and leadership to align candidate profiles with client expectations

Follow up consistently with candidates throughout the hiring process to maintain engagement and availability

Maintain high standards for candidate experience and communication

Support urgent hiring requests and fast-moving searches across different industries

Requirements

2+ years of recruitment experience, preferably in agency or offshore recruitment environments

Strong English communication skills, both written and verbal

Experience recruiting for roles such as SDRs, EAs, Customer Support, Marketing, Operations, or Technical positions

Ability to evaluate communication skills and culture fit effectively

Strong organizational and multitasking abilities

Comfortable working in a fast-paced, high-volume environment

Experience using LinkedIn Recruiter, job boards, ATS platforms, and sourcing tools

Ability to work independently and take ownership of searches

Nice to Have

Experience recruiting international remote talent

Experience supporting US-based clients

Familiarity with HubSpot, ClickUp, Slack, or recruiting CRMs

Previous experience in staffing, BPO, or offshore recruitment companies

What Success Looks Like

Consistently delivering high-quality shortlisted candidates quickly

Maintaining strong candidate communication and pipeline organization

High interview-to-hire conversion rates

Ability to adapt sourcing strategy based on client feedback and market conditions

Strong collaboration with internal recruitment and account management teams

Why Join Pavago

100% remote environment

Fast-growing international team

Opportunity to work with US startups and growing companies

High ownership and growth potential

Collaborative and performance-driven culture
