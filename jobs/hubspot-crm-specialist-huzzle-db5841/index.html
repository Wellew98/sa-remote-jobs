About Huzzle

At Huzzle, we connect high-performing professionals with global companies across the UK, US, Canada, Europe, and Australia. Our clients include startups, digital agencies, and tech platforms in industries like SaaS, MarTech, FinTech, and EdTech. We match top talent to full-time remote roles where they’re hired directly into client teams and provided ongoing support by Huzzle.

Role TypeFull-time

EngagementIndependent Contractor

About the Company

Our client is an FDA-registered, ISO-accredited independent contract microbiology laboratory providing critical testing and research services across a diverse range of industries. Based in the US, the team specializes in product efficacy, safety screening, and regulatory compliance for sectors including pharmaceuticals, medical devices, cosmetics, and food science. They serve as essential partners to global manufacturers, helping them verify marketing claims and ensure product safety through rigorous analytical methods and microbial identification.

The team consists of highly experienced professionals dedicated to accuracy and rapid turnaround times in a high-stakes, science-driven environment. As an established leader in the microbiology space, the company fosters a culture of technical excellence and professional integrity. Candidates have the opportunity to work at the forefront of microbial research and quality control, supporting the development of safe, innovative products that impact public health and consumer safety..

Job Summary

We are looking for a hands-on RevOps professional to take full ownership of our HubSpot ecosystem and commercial workflows. This is not a basic data-entry role; it is a mission-critical position designed for a HubSpot power user who can bridge the gap between technical CRM administration and high-stakes commercial operations. You will be responsible for ensuring our HubSpot Pro environment is optimized, our reporting is airtight, and our quoting process is flawless. If you thrive on building efficient systems, managing complex pricing logic, and maintaining total data integrity, you will be a vital pillar of our commercial team’s success.

Key Responsibilities

HubSpot Administration

Manage and optimize the HubSpot Professional environment, including deal stages, lifecycle stages, custom properties, and user permissions.

Workflow Automation

Build and maintain sophisticated automated workflows to streamline lead routing, sales notifications, and data management.

Quoting & Pricing Logic

Coordinate the quoting process, ensuring all calculations, margins, and pricing logic are accurate before delivery to clients.

Reporting & Analytics

Develop and manage custom reports and dashboards to provide the leadership team with real-time visibility into sales performance and pipeline health.

Process Documentation

Create and maintain clear, technical documentation for all CRM processes and standard operating procedures (SOPs).

Data Integrity

Conduct regular audits to ensure data completeness, accuracy, and hygiene across the entire CRM database.

System Integration

Assist in managing integrations between HubSpot and third-party tools (e.g., Zapier, Make) to enhance commercial efficiency.

2+ years of experience in Revenue Operations (RevOps), Sales Operations, or CRM Administration.

Hands-on experience managing HubSpot at the Professional tier or higher, including building complex workflows and custom reports.

Advanced spreadsheet skills (Excel/Google Sheets) with the ability to manage complex quote calculations and financial logic.

Strong understanding of sales margins, pricing structures, and pipeline management.

Exceptional attention to detail and a "zero-error" mindset regarding data and documentation.

Professional proficiency in written English, with the ability to communicate technical concepts clearly.

Ability to work full-time with a minimum of 5 hours overlap with US Central Time.

A process-driven personality with the ability to work independently and solve technical problems proactively.

Nice-to-Haves

Active HubSpot Certifications (Reporting, Sales Software, or Admin).

Previous experience in Laboratory services, CRO/CDMO, or highly regulated technical industries.

Experience with attribution reporting and full-funnel marketing analytics.

Familiarity with Zapier, Make, or basic scripting for automation.

Knowledge of vendor qualification processes and audit documentation.
