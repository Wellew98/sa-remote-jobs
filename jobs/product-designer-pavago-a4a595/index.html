Product Designer (UX/UI) – Remote

Full-Time | Remote | U.S. Hours (Flexible)

About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a Product Designer (UX/UI) to create intuitive, modern, and user-friendly digital experiences across web and mobile products.

You’ll own the design process from

user research and wireframing through high-fidelity UI, prototyping, and developer handoff , working closely with Product and Engineering.

We’re looking for someone who combines strong UX problem-solving with polished visual design and knows how to turn complex ideas into simple, engaging product experiences.

What You’ll Do

UX Research & Product Discovery

Conduct user interviews, usability testing, and surveys

Create user flows, personas, and journey maps

Identify friction points and opportunities to improve the product

Use tools such as

Mixpanel, Amplitude, or Hotjar to understand user behavior

UX/UI Design & Prototyping

Create wireframes, user flows, and clickable prototypes

Design polished, responsive interfaces for web and mobile

Work primarily in

Figma , with Adobe XD or Sketch experience also valuable

Iterate designs based on user feedback, testing, and product requirements

Design Systems

Build and maintain reusable components and design patterns

Maintain consistency across typography, spacing, colors, and UI elements

Help scale design systems as products grow

Collaboration & Handoff

Partner closely with Product Managers, Engineers, and stakeholders

Translate product requirements into clear design solutions

Prepare organized developer handoffs using

Figma Dev Mode, Zeplin, or similar tools

Review implementations to ensure design accuracy and usability

What We’re Looking For

2+ years of Product Design / UX/UI experience

Strong portfolio demonstrating both

UX thinking and UI craftsmanship

Strong proficiency with

Figma

Experience designing responsive web and/or mobile products

Strong understanding of

User flows

Wireframing

Prototyping

Usability

Accessibility

Design systems

Strong communication and cross-functional collaboration skills

Nice to Have

SaaS, consumer app, or enterprise product experience

Familiarity with

WCAG accessibility standards

Basic understanding of HTML/CSS

Experience with analytics, usability testing, or A/B testing

Experience building or scaling design systems

What Success Looks Like

Intuitive and polished product experiences

Improved usability, engagement, and user satisfaction

Smooth collaboration and developer handoffs

Consistent adoption of design systems

Fewer design revisions and usability issues

Reliable delivery within product and sprint timelines

Why This Role Stands Out

High ownership over real product experiences

Work closely with Product and Engineering

Modern UX/UI and product design workflows

Opportunity to influence product decisions

Fully remote environment

Growth into

Senior Product Designer, Lead Designer, or Design Systems Lead

Interview Process

Initial Application Review

Spark Hire Intro Video

Portfolio Review + Design Task

Client Interview

Offer & Onboarding

What Happens After You Apply

After applying, you’ll receive an email invitation from Spark Hire to complete a short Intro Video. This gives our hiring team an opportunity to learn more about your design experience, portfolio, UX approach, and communication style before the interview stage.

You can complete the recording on your own time and re-record your responses before submitting if needed.

Please check both your

inbox and spam/junk folder for the Spark Hire invitation.

Apply Now

If you love solving UX problems, creating polished interfaces, and turning ideas into digital experiences users genuinely enjoy, we’d love to hear from you.
