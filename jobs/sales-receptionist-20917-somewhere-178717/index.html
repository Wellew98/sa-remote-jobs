RoleBooking & Reservations Sales Specialist

Priority LocationLatin America or South Africa

Working HoursMonday to Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM US Central Time (40 hours/week)

Salary Range$1,500 – $3,000 USD per month (The final offer depends on experience, phone presence, and market alignment.)

Type of ContractFull-Time | Independent Contractor

Type of Job100% Remote

About the Company

We are a growing US-based outdoor hospitality group managing a portfolio of premier RV resorts and waterfront destinations.

About the Role

As a Booking & Reservations Sales Specialist, you will drive resort occupancy by converting inbound inquiries, web leads, and outbound pipeline prospects into confirmed bookings across our property portfolio. This is an active, performance-driven sales position—not a passive receptionist or administrative desk—centered on live phone calls, rapid text messaging, and disciplined pipeline follow-up. You will play a critical role in company revenue by building immediate rapport, overcoming hesitation, and guiding travelers smoothly through to a confirmed reservation.

Core Performance Metrics & KPIs

- Conversations per Dial: Convert at least 20% of outbound dials into substantive conversations lasting 45 seconds or longer. (Evaluation focuses on real conversations rather than empty dial counts).

- Speed to Lead: Contact 100% of new leads within 5 minutes of arrival during shift hours; initiate contact with off-shift leads within the first hour of the next shift.

- Inbound Coverage: Answer inbound calls live during shift hours to minimize dropped inquiries or rolled voicemails, and return missed calls immediately.

- Pipeline Execution & Conversion: Proactively advance leads through every CRM stage to "Booked," manage consistent SMS follow-ups, and keep the pipeline free of stale leads.

- Shift Consistency: Maintain steady, documented calling, texting, and follow-through across the entire 8-hour shift with no unlogged downtime.

Key Responsibilities

- Convert inbound reservation inquiries and incoming leads into paid bookings, diagnosing guest travel dates, rig specifications, and preferences to close directly in the reservation system.

- Engage new leads within 5 minutes across phone and text channels using our sales CRM, maintaining proactive follow-up until opportunities are booked, closed, or resolved.

- Build authentic, conversational rapport with prospective guests—especially older travelers and working-class American families—using a warm, plain-spoken, and unscripted approach.

- Overcome guest hesitation, rate questions, and booking objections by presenting site amenities, location advantages, and availability to confidently ask for the deposit.

- Recover after-hours voicemails and missed calls during the first hour of each business day, treating every missed contact as an active sales opportunity.

- Maintain clean, real-time CRM hygiene, logging accurate call notes, rig details, follow-up timestamps, and pipeline stages immediately after every customer interaction.

Candidate Qualifications

Required

- Minimum 2 years of proven experience in phone-based sales, inbound reservations, or conversion-focused telesales (Required).

- Near-native English fluency with a warm, natural, and easily understandable conversational phone presence (Required).

- Proven closing ability with comfort directly asking for deposits, handling objections, and navigating payment workflows (Required).

- Demonstrated comfort communicating with diverse US demographics, with the patience and clarity needed for older and non-technical travelers (Required).

- Strong multitasking skills with the ability to navigate active phone calls, text threads, and CRM data entry simultaneously (Required).

- High discipline, punctuality, and self-direction when working in a remote, independent environment (Required).

Preferred

- Hands-on proficiency with GoHighLevel (GHL) or comparable multi-channel sales CRMs (Preferred).

- Experience using campground or hospitality reservation engines such as Campspot or Cloudbeds (Preferred).

- Prior industry background in RV parks, campgrounds, vacation rentals, or outdoor hospitality (Preferred).

Home Office & Technical Requirements (Non-Negotiable)

- Workspace: A dedicated, quiet, and private home office free from household background noise.

- Connectivity: High-speed, stable primary internet with a verified secondary backup connection (e.g., secondary fiber line, LTE mobile hotspot).

- Power Redundancy: A verified Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) or battery inverter capable of powering your PC, monitors, and router throughout local power outages or load-shedding.

- Hardware: A modern, reliable PC/laptop with dual monitors and a professional-grade USB noise-canceling headset.
