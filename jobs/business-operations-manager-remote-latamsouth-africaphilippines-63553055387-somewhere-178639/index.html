Business Operations Manager Position Title: Business Operations Manager (Startup Generalist) Department: Business Operations Reports To: Co-Founder & CEO / Co-Founder Contract Type: Direct Contract (Long-Term Permanent Fit) Location: Remote — Latin America, South Africa, or Philippines Working Hours: U.S. Business Hours Overlap Required Company Overview The client builds high-performing, proprietary technology solutions generating approximately $600K–$700K in monthly revenue . The company operates as a lean, agile team of approximately seven people and is focused on scaling rapidly without unnecessary bureaucracy. The client is seeking a high-agency Business Operations Manager who can take ownership of operational challenges, drive projects independently, and thrive in a fast-paced startup environment. Position Overview This is not a traditional administrative or Executive Assistant role . It is primarily an operations and project management position for a strong startup generalist. The Business Operations Manager will serve as the operational connective tissue across the business, owning recurring processes, developing and improving SOPs, coordinating cross-functional projects, managing vendors, and resolving operational roadblocks. The ideal candidate can take an open-ended goal or problem, determine the required steps, coordinate with the appropriate people, make sound decisions, and execute without needing step-by-step instructions. Key Responsibilities Operations & Project Management - Take ownership of founder-led projects, operational goals, and unexpected challenges from start to completion. - Coordinate with internal team members, technical teams, and external contractors to keep projects on track. - Investigate operational issues, process failures, and business bottlenecks and independently develop and implement solutions. Process, SOP & Vendor Management - Create, document, audit, and continuously improve internal SOPs . - Manage vendor communications, software tools, contractor workflows, and hiring/onboarding logistics. - Handle core business administration, including platform payouts, Mercury wire transfers, and manual financial processes when required. Founder Support & Decision-Making - Proactively remove operational bottlenecks and miscellaneous projects from the co-founders’ workload. - Make practical, well-informed decisions in ambiguous situations without relying on micromanagement. Candidate Qualifications Essential Skills & Mindset - High Agency & Autonomy: Able to identify solutions and execute without being told exactly how to complete a task. - Highly Organized & Reliable: Exceptional follow-through, attention to detail, and ability to manage deadlines. - Comfortable with Ambiguity: Thrives in a lean, fast-moving startup where priorities can change quickly. - Tech-Literate: Comfortable learning new software, custom dashboards, fintech platforms such as Mercury, Slack, and project management tools. Experience - 3–5+ years of experience in Operations Management, Business Operations, Startup Generalist, or Operations Coordination roles. - Experience within a tech company, SaaS startup, or high-growth digital business is preferred. - Exceptional verbal and written English with a polished and professional communication style.
