Billing Coordinator (Managed Services Provider – MSP)

LocationRemote

Employment TypeFull-Time

Reports ToChief Revenue Officer (CRO)

DepartmentRevenue Operations / Finance

About the Role

We are seeking a detail-oriented and process-driven Billing Coordinator to support the revenue operations of a growing Managed Services Provider (MSP).

This role is critical to ensuring billing accuracy, subscription alignment, and contract integrity across managed services, licensing, and project-based work.

You will operate at the intersection of PSA systems, vendor platforms, and customer billing , ensuring that all services delivered are accurately captured, reconciled, and invoiced.

This is not a basic billing role — it requires someone who understands MSP billing complexity , recurring revenue models, and how operational data translates into revenue.

What You’ll Own

1. Billing & Subscription Reconciliation

-

Perform monthly reconciliation of

- Managed services (MRR) contracts

- Licensing subscriptions (e.g., Microsoft 365, security tools)

-

User and device counts across accounts

-

Validate billing accuracy across

-

PSA systems

- Vendor portals

-

Customer invoices

- Identify discrepancies and ensure alignment across all systems

2. PSA Contract Management

- Build, update, and maintain service contracts within the PSA platform

-

Ensure accurate configuration of

-

Recurring billing rules

- Per-user / per-device pricing models

-

Service bundles and add-ons

-

Manage ongoing updates, including

-

User count changes

- Licensing adjustments

-

Contract modifications

3. Time Entry & Ticket Billing Review

-

Review technician time entries for

-

Accuracy

- Completeness

-

Proper billable classification

-

Ensure correct linkage between

-

Tickets and contracts

- Project work and invoices

-

Time & materials billing

- Collaborate with service teams to resolve discrepancies and missing data

4. Customer Billing Support

- Serve as a point of contact for customer billing inquiries

-

Respond to questions related to

-

Invoices

- Subscription changes

-

Contract adjustments

-

Process requests such as

-

User additions/removals

- License updates

-

Service upgrades or downgrades

- Maintain clear, professional communication with clients

5. Documentation & Reporting

- Maintain and improve internal billing documentation and SOPs

-

Support reporting across

-

MRR reconciliation

- Subscription tracking

-

Contract accuracy and changes

- Assist in identifying trends and recurring billing issues

What We’re Looking For

This role requires someone who is highly detail-oriented, process-driven, and comfortable working across multiple systems .

You should understand that small billing errors can scale quickly in an MSP environment — and take ownership of preventing them.

Required Qualifications

- 3+ years of experience in billing, revenue operations, or finance

- Experience working with a Managed Services Provider (MSP) or IT services company

- Strong understanding of recurring revenue (MRR) billing models

-

Hands-on experience with PSA tools such as

-

Autotask

- ConnectWise

-

HaloPSA

- Kaseya BMS

- Experience reviewing technician time entries and ticket-based billing

- Strong reconciliation skills and attention to detail

- Excellent written English for customer communication

Preferred Qualifications

- Familiarity with Microsoft 365 licensing models

- Exposure to cybersecurity tools (EDR, MDR, DNS filtering, backup solutions)

- Experience with QuickBooks Online or similar accounting platforms

- Experience working with remote/offshore MSP teams

Key Success Metrics

- Billing accuracy and invoice reliability

- Timely and complete subscription reconciliation

- Reduction in billing discrepancies and adjustments

- Response time and quality of customer billing support

- Accuracy and cleanliness of PSA contract data

What This Role Is Not

- Not a basic invoicing or data-entry role

- Not siloed — requires cross-functional coordination with finance and service teams

- Not reactive — proactive reconciliation and issue prevention are expected

Why This Role Matters

In an MSP environment, billing complexity scales quickly due to:

- Recurring contracts with variable user/device counts

-

Multiple vendor subscriptions

- Integration between PSA, vendor systems, and accounting

This role ensures revenue accuracy, client trust, and operational efficiency — all critical to growth and retention.
