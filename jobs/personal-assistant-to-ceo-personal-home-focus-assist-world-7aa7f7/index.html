Overview

We are seeking an exceptionally organized, proactive, and detail-oriented Personal Assistant to support a CEO at a fast-moving luxury travel company. This role goes beyond traditional assistant work — you will act as a true operator behind the scenes, ensuring the CEO’s personal and household life runs seamlessly. You will work alongside the Executive Assistant, with a clear division of responsibilities:

This rolePersonal + household + life management

Executive AssistantBusiness operations

The ideal candidate is highly anticipatory, resourceful, and takes pride in solving problems before they arise.
