Company Overview

A well-established US-based bookkeeping and tax practice servicing a diverse client base across multiple industries, primarily within California. The business provides professional accounting, bookkeeping and tax support services to small and medium-sized businesses and is currently expanding following the acquisition of an additional client portfolio. The environment is fast-paced, service-driven and highly detail-oriented, requiring strong technical bookkeeping capability and the ability to manage multiple client accounts independently.

Role Summary

An experienced Senior Bookkeeper is required to manage full-cycle bookkeeping functions across a portfolio of US-based clients. The successful candidate will work primarily within QuickBooks Online and will be responsible for reconciliations, financial reporting support, payroll-related bookkeeping functions and maintaining accurate financial records to US standards. This is a hands-on role requiring strong technical accounting knowledge, excellent organisational skills and the ability to work independently with minimal supervision.

Key Responsibilities

Manage full-cycle bookkeeping across multiple client accounts

Maintain accurate financial records and bookkeeping systems

Perform bank, credit card and loan reconciliations

Manage accounts payable and accounts receivable processes

Prepare and review balance sheet reconciliations and profit & loss reporting

Reconcile undeposited funds and gross up sales reports from net deposits

Work with multiple POS systems and financial data sources

Support payroll journal reconciliation and payroll-related bookkeeping functions

Liaise with clients professionally regarding bookkeeping matters

Maintain bookkeeping accuracy in line with US accounting standards

Assist with month-end and year-end bookkeeping processes

Utilise AI and automation tools effectively to improve efficiency while maintaining accuracy
