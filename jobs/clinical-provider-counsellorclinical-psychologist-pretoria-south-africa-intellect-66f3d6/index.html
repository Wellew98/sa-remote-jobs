At Intellect, we’re a leading global mental health tech company in 100+ markets worldwide. We partner with employers, insurers, and governments to deliver coaching, counselling, and therapy services. We’re expanding our EAP provider network and hiring coaches, counsellors, and psychologists. Whether you’d like to supplement your private practice or take on a full-time career pathway, Intellect offers flexibility to fit your lifestyle.

What You’ll Do

  • Provide 1:1 telehealth and/or onsite coaching, counselling, or therapy-style consultations, offering general advice only (not treatment)
  • Facilitate webinars and in-person seminars (optional)
  • Support employees with wellbeing, stress, and resilience
  • Collaborate with Intellect’s internal clinical team to improve EAP programs

Must Haves

Counsellors & Clinical Psychologists

Counsellors

Master’s in Counselling, Counselling Psychology

1–3 years of supervised counselling experience

Clinical Psychologists

  • Master’s in Clinical Psychology
  • 1–3 years of supervised clinical practice, post Masters.

Nice to Haves

Background in managerial or leadership support (e.g., coaching managers, HR advisory)

Prior EAP or workplace counselling experience

Additional languages

Flexibility for occasional onsite sessions

Let’s Talk

This is more than a job. It’s your chance to:

Work with a global provider network

Broaden your clientele base beyond local users

Grow your career outside of sessions through research, content, and webinars

Access supervision & CE most EAPs don’t provide

If you’re ready to make a meaningful impact and expand your practice with flexibility, we’d love to hear from you. Apply today and join Intellect in transforming workplace mental health across the country and beyond.

Why Join Us?

Flexibility in employment models: freelance, contracted, part-time, or full-time — so you can maintain your own private caseload/clients while expanding with Intellect

Competitive compensation & attractive perks

Minimal admin burden — we handle scheduling, billing, client matching

Regular supervision & CPD included

Diverse client base — from local employees to regional/global Fortune 500 companies
