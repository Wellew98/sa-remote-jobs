Content Marketing Writer (Multi-Industry)

Remote · Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm (Pacific, Central, or Eastern) · $1,250/month for the first 90 days, then $1,500/month

About the role

We're looking for a Content Marketing Writer who knows how to turn ideas into content that gets people to pay attention — and, more importantly, take action.

You'll write across different formats and industries, from SEO blog articles and email campaigns to website and advertising copy. The subject matter may change depending on the business you're supporting, but the goal stays the same: create clear, engaging content that serves a purpose and gets results.

This isn't a role where you're writing simply to fill a content calendar. You'll need to understand the audience, the objective behind the piece, and what we want the reader to do next.

What you'll actually do

Write SEO-friendly blog content that's useful, engaging, and moves readers toward a decision

Create

email sequences and campaigns designed to increase opens, engagement, and action

Write

website copy that's clear, compelling, and keeps visitors engaged

Develop

ad copy for digital platforms around specific conversion goals

Use

ChatGPT or similar AI tools to speed up research and first drafts, while applying your own editorial judgment to produce the final content

Research unfamiliar topics and turn them into content that's easy for the intended audience to understand

Adapt your writing style, tone, and messaging to different businesses, audiences, and industries

What we're actually looking for

2–5 years of professional writing experience , ideally covering blogs, email campaigns, website copy, and/or advertising copy

A portfolio of published work that demonstrates your writing ability

Written English that is genuinely fluent, with strong grammar, clarity, and attention to tone

Comfortable using

ChatGPT or similar AI tools as part of your writing workflow, while being able to critically review, rewrite, and improve AI-generated content

Able to take a complex or unfamiliar subject and explain it clearly in plain language

SEO knowledge is a plus

, but not required

The kind of person who will do well here

You don't need to know every industry we'll work with. You do need to be someone who can learn quickly, ask the right questions, research a topic, and turn what you learn into writing that makes sense to the reader.

You take deadlines seriously, communicate early when something might slip, and don't need someone constantly checking in to make sure a task gets finished.

A few practical things matter too

Reliable internet — you need to be able to work without interruptions during deadlines and meetings

Consistent and reliable attendance

Strong ownership of your work and deadlines

Clear communication when priorities or timelines change

The ability to work independently and follow through without being chased

How the process actually works

Six steps. Same steps whether or not there's a client waiting.

We read your CV. Not a keyword scan, an actual read. If it's clearly AI-generated with nothing specific in it, that's usually obvious and it doesn't help you.

A short intro video. Two minutes of you talking is worth more than a page of text. We're checking if you're clear and comfortable on camera, your English skills, and your professional setup (camera, lighting, sound...)

A call with a recruiter, about 30 minutes. We'll ask you to walk us through something real you've done, so have a specific example ready instead of a general answer.

A couple of short take-home exercises. Nothing sneaky, nothing that takes your whole evening.

A reference check. We'll ask for someone who actually managed or worked closely with you, not a character reference.

If a client's ready: one interview with them. They make the final call on who they hire, but you don't repeat anything you've already done here.

Here's the honest part: sometimes when you finish this process, there's no job waiting yet. That's not a runaround, it's how we work. We'd rather spend the time getting to know good people properly now, so that when a client does come looking for someone like you, we already know you're ready, instead of starting the search from scratch. If that's your situation, you won't be left guessing. We'll tell you where things stand, including when the honest answer is "nothing new yet." And if you take another job while you're waiting, that's completely fine, just tell us, and we'll pick things back up whenever the timing works for you.

What to actually expect from us

If you make it to an interview, you'll get real, specific feedback about how it went either way, not a copy-paste line. If your CV doesn't move forward past the application stage, you'll still hear back, but it'll be a quick automated note rather than a personal one. With how many applications a posting like this gets, that's the only honest way we can get back to everyone fast.
