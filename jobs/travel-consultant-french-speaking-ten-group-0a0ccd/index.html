Travel Consultant - French speaking

Ten Group Employer Mid Customer Support

Ten Lifestyle Group is seeking a French-speaking Luxury Travel Consultant to join their team in South Africa. The role involves advising premium clients by phone and email in French and English, handling inquiries from quote to billing, making reservations, and creating tailored travel itineraries. Candidates must have excellent French and English (C2), several years of travel industry experience, GDS knowledge (preferably Amadeus), and be available full-time (40hrs/week) with flexibility to work Sunday to Thursday between 07:00 and 22:00 SAST. The position offers a hybrid/remote work option with requirements including being located in South Africa, a secure home office, and minimum internet speeds.
