Backlink Outreach / Link-Building Specialist

Assist World South Africa, United Kingdom (Remote) Full-time Marketing & Content Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

We’re looking for a Backlink Outreach / Link-Building Specialist to help grow our website’s authority through high-quality backlink acquisition and editorial outreach. Your primary responsibility will be to secure relevant, authoritative placements and grow the number of high-quality referring domains through channels such as HARO/Qwoted, podcasts, guest posts, and other editorial opportunities. This is a results-driven role . We care about the quality and impact of the placements you secure — not simply how many pitches you send. What You’ll Do - Identify relevant, authoritative websites and media opportunities for backlink acquisition. - Conduct targeted outreach to secure editorial placements. - Pursue opportunities through HARO/Qwoted, podcasts, guest posts, and similar channels . - Build and manage relationships with relevant outlets and publishers. - Manage opportunities from initial outreach through to confirmed placement. - Maintain an active pipeline of upcoming backlink and editorial opportunities. - Track placements and referring-domain growth using Semrush . - Monitor backlink quality and flag potentially spammy, low-authority, or paid-placement-heavy opportunities. - Provide a concise weekly report covering: - Placements landed - Placements currently in progress - New referring domains - Any red flags or issues - Your target outlets/sites for the following week How You’ll Be Measured Your performance will be evaluated against four core KPIs: New Referring Domains 15–25 new referring domains per month Dofollow, Editorial-Relevant Links 70%+ of new links Authority Score Trend +0.5–1.0 average monthly increase Placements Landed 4–8 placements per month These KPIs are designed to measure actual results , rather than activity. Metrics such as total backlinks, pitches sent, or emails opened are not considered success metrics. AI search citations will also be tracked separately through monthly spot-checks of priority queries. What Success Looks Like You’ll thrive in this role if you can consistently: - Secure genuine editorial placements on relevant websites. - Grow the number of high-quality referring domains. - Maintain a strong percentage of dofollow, editorially relevant links. - Build a healthy pipeline of future placement opportunities. - Contribute to consistent growth in Authority Score. - Recognize and avoid low-quality or spammy backlink opportunities. - Take ownership of your outreach pipeline and results. If you have a strong background in backlink outreach, link building, digital PR, editorial outreach, or securing earned media placements and are motivated by measurable results, we’d love to hear from you.
