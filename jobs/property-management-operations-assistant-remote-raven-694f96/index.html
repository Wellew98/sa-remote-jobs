Our client is a US-based property management company managing residential properties across multiple markets. They are hiring a Property Management Operations Assistant to take ownership of the operational backbone of the business: data entry and file auditing, utility coordination, maintenance follow-up, and tenant-facing customer service — including inbound calls and outbound delinquency outreach.

This is not a generalist VA role. The hire will be embedded in day-to-day property operations and will be measured on accuracy of tenant and compliance records, speed of utility and maintenance ticket closure, and the professionalism with which they handle difficult tenant calls. You will work closely with property managers, vendors, and tenants, and you will be expected to flag issues, follow through to resolution, and document every meaningful interaction.

The right person is someone who notices the details others miss, treats an open task in their queue as a personal accountability, and stays composed when a tenant call gets tense. If you are stimulated by clean records, closed loops, and a high standard for follow-through, this role is built for you.

Responsibilities

Data Entry & File Auditing

Perform accurate, timely data entry across property management software and spreadsheets

Audit tenant files, lease records, and compliance documentation to identify missing, outdated, or incorrect information

Ensure files meet internal standards and any applicable regulatory or compliance requirements

Flag discrepancies and missing documents and follow up to resolve them

Maintain organized, up-to-date digital records for active and inactive properties and tenants

Run periodic audits of existing records for ongoing accuracy

Utility Coordination & Follow-Up

Contact utility providers on behalf of the company and tenants to set up, transfer, or close accounts

Follow up on billing discrepancies, service interruptions, account transfers, and outstanding requests

Track every open utility matter so nothing sits unresolved

Document every utility-related call or email in the system

Coordinate with property managers and tenants to handle utilities cleanly at move-in and move-out

Maintenance Coordination & Follow-Up

Follow up on open maintenance requests to keep vendors responsive and tickets moving

Communicate status updates to tenants in a timely, professional manner

Coordinate vendor and contractor scheduling and confirm appointments

Update work orders accurately in the system

Escalate unresolved or urgent maintenance issues to the appropriate property manager

Customer Service & Tenant Communication

Answer inbound calls from tenants, owners, and vendors professionally

Address tenant inquiries on leases, maintenance, utilities, and general property matters

Conduct outbound calls to delinquent tenants on balances, payment arrangements, and account status

De-escalate difficult conversations and document the full interaction

Log every tenant communication and track follow-up actions through to completion

Route calls and issues to the right team member when escalation is required
