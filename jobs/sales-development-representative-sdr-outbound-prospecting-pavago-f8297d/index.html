About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a Sales Development Representative (SDR) to generate qualified sales opportunities through high-volume outbound prospecting and lead qualification.

This is a fast-paced, phone-first sales role where you’ll engage prospects through calls, email, and text messaging, identify qualified opportunities, and help build a healthy sales pipeline. Success in this role comes from persistence, strong communication skills, and the ability to consistently achieve outreach goals.

If you’re coachable, goal-driven, and looking to build a successful career in sales, this is an excellent opportunity to grow.

What You’ll Own

Outbound Prospecting

Conduct high-volume outbound outreach via phone, email, and text messaging.

Identify and engage prospective candidates and leads across assigned campaigns.

Build rapport quickly and maintain professional conversations.

Follow structured outreach cadences and prospecting workflows.

Consistently achieve daily outreach activity goals.

Lead Qualification

Qualify prospects based on predefined campaign criteria.

Conduct discovery conversations to assess fit and interest.

Gather relevant information and determine next steps.

Advance qualified prospects through the sales pipeline.

Deliver a professional and positive prospect experience.

Inbound Lead Follow-Up

Respond promptly to inbound leads generated through job postings and marketing efforts.

Conduct qualification conversations and identify next steps.

Maintain ongoing communication until a clear outcome is reached.

Ensure timely and consistent follow-up.

CRM & Pipeline Management

Maintain accurate records of prospect interactions.

Log calls, emails, texts, and follow-up activities in the CRM.

Keep pipeline stages updated and organized.

Support reporting through accurate activity tracking.

Maintain clean and reliable CRM data.

Team Collaboration

Coordinate qualified lead handoffs to recruiting teams or sales leadership.

Share prospect insights and relevant information with internal stakeholders.

Participate in coaching sessions, training, and team meetings.

Collaborate with leadership to improve outreach performance.

Performance & Target Achievement

Meet daily and weekly outbound activity targets.

Achieve lead qualification and conversion goals.

Follow established sales processes and messaging.

Continuously improve outreach techniques and communication skills.

Maintain consistent performance in a metrics-driven environment.

What Happens After You Apply

Right after you apply, you’ll receive an email invitation from Spark Hire to record your Intro Video . This short, self-recorded video is the next step that completes your application and can be recorded whenever it’s convenient for you.

Instead of repeating yourself across multiple screening calls, you’ll introduce yourself once, and your video will be shared with the hiring team. Don’t overthink it—you can record your video as many times as you’d like before submitting it. Only your final submission will be reviewed.

Please keep an eye on both your inbox and spam folder for your Spark Hire invitation after submitting your application.
