About the Role

We are seeking a highly organised, proactive, and hands-on Real Estate Acquisitions & Entitlements Coordinator to support a US-based real estate business involved in the acquisition and sale of land.

This is an execution-focused role for someone with practical experience in real estate transactions, land acquisitions, zoning, entitlements, permitting, title documentation, and closing processes.

We are not looking for a traditional Project Manager who simply delegates tasks and monitors progress. We need someone who can personally take ownership of transactions, manage documentation, communicate directly with attorneys, title companies, municipalities, utility providers, and other stakeholders, and consistently follow up until outstanding matters are resolved.

The ideal candidate will have a background in US real estate transactions, real estate paralegal work, transaction coordination, or land development and permitting. They should be confident working independently, comfortable navigating unfamiliar processes, and able to keep multiple transactions moving forward.

Key Responsibilities

1. Real Estate Acquisitions & Transaction Coordination

Coordinate land acquisition processes, due diligence requirements, and transaction documentation.

Track acquisition milestones, outstanding requirements, and closing deadlines.

Coordinate title searches, title documentation, and closing requirements.

Liaise directly with attorneys, title companies, sellers, vendors, and other parties involved in property transactions.

Follow up on outstanding transaction requirements to ensure deals continue progressing.

Identify potential delays or issues and take ownership of resolving them.

2. Zoning, Entitlements & Permitting

Research and coordinate zoning requirements, land-use regulations, and entitlement processes.

Assist with preparing, submitting, and following up on permit and approval applications.

Communicate directly with municipalities, planning departments, and relevant authorities.

Track application statuses, outstanding documentation, and approval timelines.

Follow up on delayed applications and identify the appropriate next steps.

Coordinate utility applications and related land development requirements.

3. Legal Documentation & Due Diligence

Organise and maintain property-related legal documentation and transaction records.

Coordinate due diligence documents and ensure all required information is obtained.

Review documentation for completeness and accuracy.

Maintain organised digital filing systems, correspondence, and transaction records.

Work closely with legal professionals and external parties to obtain outstanding documentation.

Ensure important deadlines, requirements, and transaction details are accurately recorded.

4. Stakeholder Communication & Follow-Up

Communicate professionally with attorneys, title companies, municipalities, utility providers, tenants, vendors, and other external stakeholders.

Proactively chase outstanding information, approvals, and documentation.

Build effective working relationships with external parties to keep transactions moving.

Investigate delays, resolve administrative blockers, and escalate complex matters when necessary.

Provide clear and accurate progress updates to internal stakeholders.

5. Administration, Reporting & Organisation

Maintain accurate transaction trackers, spreadsheets, deadlines, and follow-up schedules.

Manage multiple transactions and workstreams simultaneously.

Keep records of communication, outstanding tasks, and transaction progress.

Use digital tools and AI-assisted research or drafting where appropriate.

Verify information independently, particularly when dealing with legal, regulatory, or property-related matters.

Identify opportunities to improve administrative processes and documentation management.

Required Experience & Skills

Previous hands-on experience in US real estate acquisitions, property transactions, entitlements, zoning, permitting, title coordination, or real estate paralegal work.

Practical understanding of real estate transaction processes and property documentation.

Experience coordinating due diligence, title requirements, closing documentation, or property approvals.

Ability to communicate directly with attorneys, title companies, municipalities, and other external stakeholders.

Strong document management, administration, and organisational skills.

Excellent written and spoken English communication.

Strong attention to detail and the ability to manage multiple deadlines.

Confidence working independently and taking ownership of outstanding tasks.

Strong research, problem-solving, and follow-up abilities.

Good judgement and the ability to recognise when something needs to be verified or escalated.

Preferred Experience

Experience with US land acquisitions and land development.

Exposure to zoning regulations, land-use approvals, and municipal permitting.

Experience coordinating title searches, title commitments, and real estate closings.

Familiarity with utility applications and development-related approvals.

Previous experience working as a Real Estate Paralegal, Transaction Coordinator, Acquisitions Coordinator, or Development Coordinator.

Familiarity with digital document management systems, spreadsheets, and AI-assisted productivity tools.

Ideal Candidate Profile

We are looking for someone who is

Hands-on

Enjoys getting involved in the actual work rather than simply delegating tasks.

Proactive

Takes initiative and follows up without waiting to be reminded.

Resourceful

Can investigate unfamiliar issues and find practical solutions.

Detail-oriented

Understands the importance of accurate documentation and deadlines.

Confident

Comfortable communicating with attorneys, municipalities, and other external stakeholders.

Resilient

Can manage pressure, delays, and multiple competing priorities.

Honest and accountable

Comfortable admitting when they don't know something and taking steps to verify the correct information.

Independent

Can take ownership of responsibilities and move transactions forward with minimal supervision.
