About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a Copywriter to create persuasive, conversion-focused marketing content that drives leads, engagement, and revenue.

This is a performance-driven role for someone who understands how words influence customer behavior. You’ll develop high-converting copy across landing pages, email campaigns, paid advertising, and sales funnels while collaborating closely with marketing and design teams to optimize campaign performance.

You’ll support clients across a variety of industries, including healthcare, restaurants, fitness, e-commerce, professional services, SaaS, and B2B businesses.

If you’re passionate about direct-response copywriting, customer psychology, and writing content that delivers measurable business results, this role is for you.

Responsibilities

Landing Pages & Sales Funnels

Write and optimize high-converting landing pages, sales pages, and lead generation funnels.

Develop messaging tailored to each stage of the customer journey (TOFU, MOFU, BOFU).

Improve headlines, offers, calls-to-action (CTAs), and conversion flow.

Collaborate with designers and marketers to ensure messaging aligns with visual design and campaign goals.

Email Marketing & Lifecycle Campaigns

Write cold outreach emails, nurture sequences, newsletters, promotional campaigns, and re-engagement emails.

Develop lifecycle messaging that supports lead generation, customer retention, and conversion.

Create segmentation-based email campaigns for different audience groups.

Continuously optimize subject lines, hooks, messaging, and CTAs based on campaign performance.

Paid Advertising & Campaign Support

Write compelling ad copy for Meta, Google, LinkedIn, and other paid advertising platforms.

Develop high-performing hooks, headlines, and messaging frameworks.

Support ongoing campaign testing by refining copy based on performance metrics and A/B testing results.

Content Strategy & Optimization

Conduct audience research and competitor analysis to strengthen messaging.

Create SEO-aware landing page and website copy that aligns with search intent.

Analyze campaign performance and improve messaging using engagement and conversion data.

Support A/B testing initiatives for headlines, offers, CTAs, and page messaging.

What Makes You a Great Fit

You understand customer psychology and persuasive messaging.

You write copy that balances creativity with measurable business outcomes.

You think strategically and use performance data to improve results.

You can adapt your writing style across multiple industries and audiences.

You’re highly organized and capable of managing multiple projects simultaneously.

You thrive in fast-paced remote environments and collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams.

What Happens After You Apply

Right after you apply, you’ll receive an email invitation from Spark Hire to record your Intro Video . This short, self-recorded video is the final step that completes your application.

Instead of repeating yourself across multiple screening calls, you’ll introduce yourself once, and your video will be shared with the hiring team. This allows hiring managers to evaluate your communication style early, making future interviews more meaningful while reducing unnecessary interview rounds.

Don’t overthink it—you can record your video as many times as you’d like before submitting it. Only your final submission will be reviewed. Please keep an eye on both your inbox and spam folder for your Spark Hire invitation.
