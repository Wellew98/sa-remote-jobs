Our client is a US-based healthcare organisation and they are seeking an experienced and detail-oriented

Accountant

to join the finance team.

This role is ideal for a hands-on Accountant who enjoys working with numbers, solving problems and taking ownership of their work. You will be responsible for supporting accounting operations across multiple entities, ensuring accurate financial records, reconciliations and month-end processes.

We are looking for someone who can work independently, identify and resolve discrepancies, and confidently manage a complex accounting workload with minimal supervision.

PLEASE NOTE

Working hours

Monday to Friday, 15:00–00:00 SAST (subject to US daylight saving changes)

Public Holidays

You will be required to work on all

South African public holidays

(compensated for in accordance with the BCEA).

Internet Requirements

A fixed fibre line with a minimum speed of 25 Mbps (upload & download) and the ability to support a wired Ethernet connection is mandatory. Applicants without a fixed fibre line cannot be considered.

Power Backup

A reliable power backup solution is required to manage load shedding and power outages. Applicants without a power backup cannot be considered.

Work Environment

This is a

fully remote working (must be based in South Africa).

Responsibilities

Work with Sage Intacct and Expensify to manage accounting transactions and reporting.

Perform bank reconciliations across multiple entities and bank accounts.

Process bank transactions, journal entries and general ledger adjustments.

Manage intercompany transactions and expense allocations.

Record payroll journal entries and allocate costs across different entities and departments.

Process revenue, bad debt and month-end accounting entries.

Investigate and resolve accounting discrepancies.

Assist with month-end closing activities and maintain accurate financial records.

Maintain supporting schedules and documentation to ensure accuracy and compliance.

What we’re looking for

Proficiency in Sage Intacct or a comparable ERP system, such as Oracle NetSuite.

Proven experience in an accounting role involving bank reconciliations, journal entries and month-end processing.

Strong experience with multi-entity accounting and intercompany allocations.

Strong understanding of general ledger accounting principles.

Excellent analytical, organisational and problem-solving skills.

Ability to work independently, take initiative and manage competing priorities.

High level of accuracy and attention to detail.

Advantageous

Experience using Expensify.

Exposure to US accounting practices or international finance operations.

If you are not contacted within 14 working days, please consider your application unsuccessful.
