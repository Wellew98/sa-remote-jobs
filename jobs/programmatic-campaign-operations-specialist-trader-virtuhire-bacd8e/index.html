South Africa – Remote

Employment

Permanent, Full-Time

Role Purpose

Execute and maintain programmatic campaigns accurately and efficiently within an established strategy, workflows, and QA standards. This is a hands-on campaign execution role focused on campaign builds, trafficking, pacing, optimisation, troubleshooting, and maintenance.

Key Responsibilities

Build campaigns, line items, targeting structures, and campaign components within assigned DSPs.

Traffic creative assets and third-party tags.

Complete pre-launch campaign QA.

Monitor campaign pacing and delivery on a daily basis.

Identify and escalate pacing or delivery risks before they become client-visible.

Execute routine optimisations and approved campaign changes within defined guidelines.

Troubleshoot campaign, creative, targeting, and activation issues.

Escalate complex technical issues to the Senior Programmatic Operations Lead.

Maintain accurate campaign documentation, naming conventions, and change histories.

Provide screenshots, compliance documentation, and campaign data when required.

Support campaign closeout, reconciliation, and reporting processes.

Work accurately across high volumes of campaigns and line items.

Must-Have Requirements

2–4 years of hands-on digital or programmatic campaign execution experience.

Direct experience building and managing campaigns within at least one major DSP.

Ability to learn and work across additional platforms quickly.

Strong attention to detail.

Comfortable with repetitive, high-volume campaign production where accuracy is critical.

Strong Excel and Google Sheets skills.

Comfortable working from insertion orders, build sheets, and campaign specifications.

Strong written communication.

Disciplined escalation and issue-management skills.

Preferred Experience

Experience with one or more of

DV360

Basis

Simpli.fi

Amazon DSP

Meta

YouTube

VAST and ad-server tags

PMP deals

Verification tools

CTV/OTT

Online video

Agency or managed-service campaign operations

Success in the Role

Campaigns are built correctly the first time.

Campaigns meet agreed launch SLAs.

Pacing and delivery issues are identified early.

Routine changes are completed quickly and accurately.

Campaign documentation remains clean and up to date.

QA and escalation procedures are consistently followed.

The wider programmatic team can rely on accurate, consistent execution.
