PositionAppointment Setter

Work Hours (Client) : 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM EST

Location of SearchSouth Africa

Work LocationREMOTE

Type of ContractIndependent Contractor

Company/Client Overview

We’re hiring an Appointment Setter to generate qualified opportunities for our sales team. You’ll connect with warm and cold leads, qualify prospects, and schedule pre-booked strategy sessions. This role is for motivated, metrics-driven professionals who thrive on outreach and pipeline building.

Duties and Responsibilities

KPIs & Expectations

  • Make 80–120 outbound calls per day (Cold Calls and Warm
  • Book 15–20 qualified appointments per week
  • Maintain a 70%+ show-up rate
  • Convert 20–30% of leads to appointments
  • Keep 100% accurate CRM data in ClickUp/GoHighLevel

Why Join Us?

  • Your role will include cold calling as well as working from a warm list of pre-qualified leads.
  • Competitive salary $1000 plus incentives (top earners $3,500+/month)
  • Remote role with training and career growth opportunities
  • Join a high-energy, supportive sales team

Minimum Requirements

Language requirementExcellent English Communication Skills

EducationBachelor's Degree (ideally)

Experience1+ years of related experience

Non Negotiables

  • 1+ year of experience in appointment setting, cold calling, or business development
  • Proven ability to meet or exceed activity targets (calls, emails, SMS)
  • Strong communication skills in English (written + verbal)
  • Proficiency in CRM systems (GoHighLevel, ClickUp preferred)
  • Highly organized with excellent follow-through
  • Available to work U.S. EST hours (9 AM – 6 PM)

If you don’t have experience in appointment setting or lead qualification, do not apply.
