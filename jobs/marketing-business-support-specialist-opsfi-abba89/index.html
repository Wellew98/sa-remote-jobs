Marketing & Business Support Specialist

OpsFi is a growing, boutique accounting and advisory firm servicing high growth clients in the tech industry and our offices are based in Bree Street, Cape Town.  We have specific openings for fully remote roles (within South Africa) and roles based in Cape Town to build a greater hybrid office presence (where we will expect a certain amount of office days per month for the Cape Town team). Our offshore client is an InsurTech firm based in California in the United States leveraging modern software platforms, AI risk modelling, and state-of-the-art hardware engineering to provide next-generation insurance solutions for B2C and B2B customers in the United States.   We are looking to recruit a Marketing & Business Support Specialist to join their offshore division, that will work exclusively for their Marketing team (i.e. fully integrated into their team and workflows). You would work remotely from South Africa with a combination of SA & US-based teams. This is an exciting opportunity to grow experience in international markets and work alongside a dynamic team who are building out exciting products in the insurance and tech space. We are growing the structure in South Africa, and the titles will evolve as the structure takes shape, so it is a great time to join the team. This opportunity would suit an individual who is looking to progress their career and develop intuition. Key Responsibilities In this role, you will provide comprehensive support across a variety of marketing initiatives and daily operational workflows. Your responsibilities will include but not be limited to: Marketing Aptitude: Proven experience managing social media accounts (LinkedIn, X/Twitter), drafting newsletters, and creating clean, professional marketing collateral (experience with Canva, HubSpot, or Adobe Creative Suite is a plus). Marketing Campaign Coordination: Proven experience with executing and monitoring digital marketing campaigns across social media, email channels, and content platforms to drive brand awareness and engagement. Content Creation & Copywriting: Drafting engaging copy for newsletters, social media posts, and marketing collateral, ensuring all materials align with our brand voice. Client & Systems Support: Experience using CRMs (such as Salesforce or HubSpot) to manage lead tracking, update customer data and assist with client communication workflows. Administrative & Operational Backing: Providing expert oversight for daily administrative tasks, including calendar management, meeting coordination, CRM updates, and data management. Marketing Analytics & Reporting: Tracking, analysing, and reporting on marketing campaign performance metrics to help optimize future outreach strategies. Community & Brand Management: Monitoring digital communities, engaging with our audience online, and ensuring all customer-facing touchpoints maintain a premium standard. Ad Hoc Business Support: Delivering insightful, tailored support to address unique, fast-moving operational challenges as the business scales.
