Director of Development (Nonprofit Fundraising)

We are hiring a Director of Development for a nonprofit organization in the nonprofit and community development industry . This is a full-time remote role for a Washington, D.C.-based company . This role is ideal for an experienced nonprofit development leader who is highly organized, strategic, and operationally focused, with a strong track record of managing development teams, fundraising operations, funding relationships, and multi-million-dollar fundraising portfolios. The ideal candidate is someone who can build systems, develop people, improve processes, and take full ownership of the Development Division's performance. Key Responsibilities: - Lead and manage the overall Development Division and its fundraising operations. - Develop and execute the organization's fundraising and development strategy. - Build a diversified funding portfolio and identify new funding opportunities. - Manage the grant pipeline, proposal workflow, and funding calendar. - Oversee the quality and timely submission of grant proposals and funding applications. - Ensure proposals meet organizational, budgetary, formatting, evaluation, and compliance requirements. - Maintain complete visibility into active grants, pending applications, award amounts, reporting deadlines, renewals, and compliance obligations. - Build and maintain strong relationships with foundations, government agencies, corporate partners, and donors. - Lead donor stewardship, renewal planning, site visit coordination, executive briefings, and recognition activities. - Develop and improve Development SOPs, quality assurance systems, internal approval processes, dashboards, and workflows. - Monitor departmental capacity, delegate effectively, and establish clear performance expectations. - Hire, coach, develop, and manage Development team members. - Provide regular reporting to Executive Leadership on pipeline status, proposal and reporting calendars, revenue forecasts, funding risks, renewal opportunities, and department performance. - Identify risks early and proactively address issues before they become major problems. - Lead the implementation of AI, automation, and technology to improve Development efficiency and quality. - Continuously evaluate and improve Development systems, processes, and operational performance. Required Qualifications: - Minimum 7 years of progressively responsible nonprofit development experience. - 7+ years of demonstrated experience managing Development teams. - Proven success securing foundation, corporate, and government funding. - Experience overseeing multi-million-dollar fundraising portfolios. - Strong project management and organizational skills. - Strong relationship management and stakeholder communication skills. - Experience implementing systems and operational improvements. - Strong financial and budget literacy. - Experience using CRM platforms and development software. - Excellent written and verbal communication skills. - Strong leadership, accountability, decision-making, and problem-solving abilities. Preferred Qualifications: - Experience building or scaling a nonprofit Development Division. - Experience developing fundraising systems, SOPs, dashboards, and quality assurance processes. - Experience with AI-assisted proposal development, research automation, workflow automation, or internal GPT tools. - Experience managing relationships across foundations, government agencies, corporate partners, and donors. - Experience forecasting revenue and identifying funding gaps and growth opportunities. Required Skills & Tools: - Nonprofit Development & Fundraising - Grant & Proposal Management - Grant Portfolio Management - Fundraising Strategy - Team Leadership & Performance Management - Project & Workflow Management - CRM Platforms - Development Software - Budget & Financial Management - Reporting & Compliance - Revenue Forecasting - SOP & Process Development - Quality Assurance - AI & Workflow Automation - Excellent Written & Verbal Communication Schedule & Pay: - Full-time position; working hours aligned with EST. business hours - This is a fully remote job for a Washington, D.C.-based company. - Pay ranges from R56 000 to R72 000, depending on experience and skill. - Annual raises, bonuses, confirmed holidays, and PTO. System Requirements: - Internet speed of at least 25 Mbps - Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher - 8 GB of RAM or higher - Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer - HD 720p webcam - Headset with a microphone Benefits: - Competitive pay rates - The company provides a US phone number and business email address - Enjoy the flexibility of working remotely, from home or any location of your choice - Eliminate commute time - Consistent work with the same clients, fostering long-term professional relationships - Opportunities for career advancement, dependent on the client - Stable work hours and consistent pay - A supportive and inclusive work environment that values diversity and individual growth If you are an experienced nonprofit development leader who enjoys building high-performing teams, creating efficient systems, strengthening funding relationships, and taking ownership of results, we would love to hear from you. Apply today and join a team focused on building a stronger, more scalable Development operation.
