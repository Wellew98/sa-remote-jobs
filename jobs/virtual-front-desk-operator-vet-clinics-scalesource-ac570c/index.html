Virtual Front Desk Operator (Vet Clinics)

Remote · Monday–Friday, overlapping US Central Time, with reliable availability in the 10am–12pm and 4pm–6pm peak windows · $1,000/month for the first 90 days, then $1,500/month

Some of the clinics we place people with are ready for someone right away. Others aren't yet, and we're building a group of people we already trust so we can move fast the moment a clinic does need someone. Either way, you go through the same process. Your recruiter will tell you plainly which situation applies to you.

What this job actually is

When someone calls a vet clinic, they're often worried about their animal. Your job is to answer fast, figure out what's actually going on, and get them to the right next step, whether that's a booked appointment or telling them to get to an emergency vet right now. You're not diagnosing anything. You're the person who keeps the clinic's front door running smoothly so the vets and techs can focus on the animals instead of the phone.

What you'll actually do

Answer calls and figure out quickly what each caller actually needs

Book, confirm, and reschedule appointments accurately in the clinic's practice management system

Ask a few quick questions on every call to catch symptoms that need urgent attention, and know when to send someone to the ER instead of booking a routine visit

Follow up on missed calls the same day, so a call that slipped through doesn't turn into a client who books somewhere else

Log calls and messages in the practice management system so the clinical team has real notes to work from, not gaps

Flag patterns, like a string of complaints or a scheduling gap, to the clinic before they turn into bigger problems

What we're actually looking for

1-5 years in customer-facing phone work: hospitality, dental or medical reception, salon coordination, or something similar

Real hands-on experience using a practice management system, a dialer, and SMS tools

Comfortable staying calm and clear with someone who's anxious or upset on the phone

Genuinely fluent in English, spoken and written. Not "good enough," fluent

Able to reliably work the 10am-12pm and 4pm-6pm Central time windows, five days a week

Veterinary, medical, or dental front desk experience specifically is a plus, not a requirement

A few things that matter more than they sound like they should

Internet that doesn't cut out mid-call

You show up for your shift, every time, on time

If something's going wrong, a caller escalated, a booking went sideways, you say so before it turns into a bigger problem

You follow through on what you said you'd do without someone checking in on you

How the process actually works

We read your CV. Not a keyword scan, an actual read.

A short intro video. Two minutes of you talking is worth more than a page of text. We're checking if you're clear and comfortable on camera, your English skills, and your professional setup (camera, lighting, sound...)

A call with a recruiter, about 30 minutes. We'll ask you to walk us through something real you've done, so have a specific example ready instead of a general answer. This is also when your recruiter will tell you clearly whether a clinic is ready for you now or not yet.

ScaleSource's training academy built specifically for this role. This isn't a formality. It's where you actually learn the job: the systems you'll use, the red flags to listen for, and how to handle a call that's gone sideways. At the end, you sit a certification test. Pass it, and you're certified to work this role.

A reference check. We'll ask for someone who actually managed or worked closely with you, not a character reference.

If a client's ready: one interview with them. They make the final call on who they hire, but you don't repeat anything you've already done here.

Here's the honest part: sometimes when you finish this process, there's no job waiting yet. That's not a runaround, it's how we work. We'd rather spend the time getting to know good people properly now, so that when a client does come looking for someone like you, we already know you're ready, instead of starting the search from scratch. If that's your situation, you won't be left guessing. We'll tell you where things stand, including when the honest answer is "nothing new yet." And if you take another job while you're waiting, that's completely fine, just tell us, and we'll pick things back up whenever the timing works for you.

What to actually expect from us

If you make it to an interview, you'll get real, specific feedback about how it went either way, not a copy-paste line. If your CV doesn't move forward past the application stage, you'll still hear back, but it'll be a quick automated note rather than a personal one. With how many applications a posting like this gets, that's the only honest way we can get back to everyone fast.
