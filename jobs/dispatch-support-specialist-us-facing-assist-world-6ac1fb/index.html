We are hiring on behalf of a premier residential air conditioning and home comfort company serving homeowners across Palm Beach, Broward, and the Treasure Coast.

Unlike high-volume call centers or scripted BPO operations, our client’s 5-star reputation is built on white-glove hospitality, transparency, and a calm, reassuring human touch. Whether a homeowner is calling for an urgent repair, exploring a new AC installation, or booking routine maintenance, your voice is their first impression of the company.

As a Client Coordinator & Dispatch Support Specialist, you will work closely alongside the dispatch team—handling inbound client communications, reading territory maps to route field technicians efficiently, and supporting customers across every stage of AC sales, installation, and maintenance.

What You'll Do

- Be the Warm, Reassuring Voice of the Brand: Handle inbound calls with a clear, natural, accent-neutral English phone voice that puts homeowners at ease—never sounding scripted or rushed.

- Support the Dispatch Team & Read Territory Maps: Actively assist the dispatch team by reading service maps, checking technician availability, and coordinating routes across South Florida to ensure timely arrivals.

- Full-Lifecycle Customer Support: Guide and support clients across the entire service journey—from initial AC sales and system replacement inquiries to installation scheduling and ongoing maintenance appointments.

- Intake & Scheduling Accuracy: Listen attentively to homeowner needs, capture accurate details, and coordinate smoothly between clients, field technicians, and office leadership.

- Proactive Follow-Ups: Keep customers informed regarding arrival windows, installation timelines, and post-service check-ins.

What We Are Looking For

- An Exceptional Phone Voice (Top Priority): A clear, warm, articulate, and easily understood speaking voice. Strong phone presence is non-negotiable—all shortlisted applicants will be asked to submit a brief Loom video introduction.

- Fluent English Comprehension: Effortless spoken and listening fluency (C1/C2 level) to navigate natural conversations with South Florida homeowners without miscommunication.

- Hospitality & High-Touch Background: Experience in boutique hospitality, concierge, medical reception, property management, or upscale client coordination—we value genuine warmth over rigid call-center habits.

- Spatial & Map-Reading Comfort: Confidence reading a digital map, understanding basic geographic territories, and helping the dispatch team organize daily technician routes logically.

- Tech-Ready Setup: Reliable high-speed internet, a quiet dedicated workspace, a quality headset/microphone, and comfort learning scheduling and dispatch software.

- Schedule Availability: Full-time availability Monday–Friday during US Eastern Time (EST) hours, with flexibility for occasional schedule adjustments.

How to Apply & Why Join

- Application Note: Please ensure your resume highlights any client-facing, hospitality, scheduling, or dispatch-related experience. Qualified candidates will be invited to submit a 60-second Loom video so we can hear your communication style firsthand.

- Long-Term Stability: Direct-hire placement with a growing, top-rated US home comfort leader.

- Guaranteed 90-Day Review: Clear path to a compensation increase after your first 90 days.

- Zero Cold Calling: 100% inbound customer care, scheduling, and dispatch coordination.
