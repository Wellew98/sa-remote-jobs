Operations and Customer Support Coordinator

A fast-growing energy procurement company is hiring a remote Operations and Customer Support Coordinator for its lean operations team. You will manage inboxes, own cases, investigate billing discrepancies, and keep account records accurate and complete.

About the role

The company helps clients secure the right energy supply agreements and makes sure those agreements are delivered as contracted. The operations team runs the full RFP lifecycle, from drafting through supplier responses and sales booking. You will keep that process running smoothly behind the scenes. The role combines a high-level virtual assistant with a back-end operations coordinator. Once trained, you work independently: you spot what needs attention, research it, resolve it, and document it, and you escalate only when something truly needs the senior team. You will use AI tools every day to speed up research, summaries, and drafts, while staying responsible for the accuracy of everything you send.

Required Qualifications

  • 2+ years in virtual assistance, operations support, customer service, or account coordination, ideally in a complex industry such as energy, finance, logistics, or utilities
  • A genuine investigative mindset: you dig into why something went wrong and back up your answer with evidence
  • Strong memory for detail across many clients, accounts, and processes
  • Able to be trained once and then complete work correctly and fully without ongoing supervision (non-negotiable)
  • Exceptional written and spoken English for high-stakes communication with clients, suppliers, and the sales team
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office, especially Excel

Preferred Qualifications

  • Hands-on experience researching invoices, reconciling charges, or investigating account discrepancies
  • Exposure to energy, utilities, telecom, or another regulated, contract-driven industry
  • Regular professional use of generative AI tools, with specific examples of how they improved your work
  • Experience with CRM or case management platforms
  • Excel skills including lookups, pivot tables, and basic formulas for billing analysis

Responsibilities

  • Manage team inboxes and act on incoming messages based on context, rather than simply forwarding them
  • Resolve straightforward issues yourself, and escalate early when there is financial, contractual, or relationship risk, with your research already organized
  • Turn incoming inquiries into clear, actionable tickets and follow every case through to resolution
  • Investigate discrepancies by comparing invoices, account records, and service dates to find the cause and the fix
  • Work directly with the sales team to track down invoices, answer questions, and keep sales and operations aligned
  • Confirm that service starts on time for every contracted account, and record and communicate account exits across all systems
  • Keep thorough records of every investigation so anyone can pick up a case without needing to ask questions
  • Build reference notes on supplier quirks, common errors, and processes so recurring issues get solved faster
  • Use AI tools such as Claude, ChatGPT, or Copilot to summarize contracts and email threads, compare documents, analyze billing data, and draft communications, checking every figure against the source documents and following data confidentiality rules
  • Over time, help leadership investigate missing or short payments and match incoming payments against expected revenue

Working arrangement

This is a full-time, fully remote contractor role, Monday to Friday during standard business hours, open to candidates in South Africa or Latin America. You will need:

  • A modern computer (Core i5 or Ryzen 5 or better, 8 GB RAM)
  • Stable internet of at least 25 Mbps download and 10 Mbps upload, with a wired connection preferred
  • A UPS or similar backup power
  • A good-quality headset
  • A quiet, dedicated home office

Who thrives in this role

  • Can be trained once and trusted completely, and never lets tasks sit
  • Remembers supplier quirks and account history, and uses that knowledge to work faster
  • Needs to know why something went wrong and how to stop it from happening again
  • Communicates in an organized, complete way that leaves no room for confusion
  • Uses AI to reach the right answer faster, never to skip checking it
  • Is the quiet backbone the team could not run without
