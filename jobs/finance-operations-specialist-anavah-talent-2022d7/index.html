U.S. Startup talent firm is seeking a full-time Finance & Operations Specialist Hours: Full-time Time-Zone: U.S. Central Time (CST) or significant overlap Terms: Independent Contractor Compensation: 75,000/month negotiable based on experience ABOUT THE ROLE Anavah Talent is a fast-growing U.S.-based staffing and recruiting firm that helps companies build exceptional remote teams across the Philippines, Latin America, and South Africa. We're looking for our first dedicated Finance & Operations Specialist to become the operational backbone of the business. This role owns payroll operations, contractor administration, client invoicing, HR coordination, operational reporting, and internal systems. Beyond execution, you'll help build the operational infrastructure that allows the company to scale efficiently through better documentation, automation, and process improvement. You'll support approximately 30–50 active contractors while partnering closely with leadership, recruiters, clients, and contractors to ensure operations remain accurate, organized, and scalable. ABOUT YOU Do you enjoy bringing order to fast-moving environments? Are you the person who naturally creates checklists, documents processes, and finds better ways of doing things? This role is ideal for someone who enjoys balancing finance, operations, HR administration, and systems improvement. You take pride in accuracy, meet recurring deadlines without constant oversight, and constantly look for opportunities to reduce manual work through better processes and automation. If you're excited about helping build the operational foundation of a growing U.S. startup, we'd love to meet you. RESPONSIBILITIES: Payroll & Finance Operations Own the end-to-end payroll process and financial administration, ensuring contractors are paid accurately and on time. Execute two payroll cycles each month using Wise Review contractor timesheets, invoices, reimbursements, bonuses, deductions, and payroll adjustments Prepare payroll approval summaries for leadership Maintain payroll reports and Contractor Masterfile Process contractor rate increases and compensation changes Support month-end financial reconciliation and reporting Manage Stripe subscriptions and recurring billing Assist with financial recordkeeping and reporting Client Billing & Business Operations Ensure internal operations remain organized, accurate, and scalable while supporting day-to-day business administration. Prepare and reconcile client invoices Investigate and resolve billing discrepancies Maintain Monday.com boards, operational trackers, and shared documentation Maintain organized HR, finance, and operational records Coordinate recurring operational meetings and administrative requests Support leadership with operational projects and cross-functional initiatives Contractor Operations & People Administration Manage the complete contractor lifecycle while providing an exceptional contractor experience. Coordinate contractor onboarding and offboarding Manage Independent Contractor Agreements, tax forms, background checks, and compliance documentation Track PTO, contractor records, and employment changes Conduct scheduled contractor check-ins (7, 30, 60, 90-day, and annual) Serve as the primary point of contact for payroll and operational questions Coordinate contractor engagement initiatives, anniversaries, and recognition Systems, Process Improvement & Automation Continuously improve how the business operates by building scalable systems and reducing manual work. Develop and maintain Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) Improve operational workflows and documentation Identify repetitive tasks suitable for automation Implement automation using tools such as AI, Zapier, Make, or similar platforms Build dashboards and operational reporting Recommend and implement process improvements that increase efficiency and support company growth
