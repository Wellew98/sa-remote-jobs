Hours: Full-time Time-Zone: U.S. Central Time (CST) or significant overlap Terms: Independent Contractor Compensation: $1600 Hiring Process: Application → Interview → Take-Home Assessment → CEO Interview → References & Background Check ABOUT THE ROLE Anavah Talent is a fast-growing U.S.-based staffing and recruiting firm that helps companies build exceptional remote teams across the Philippines, Latin America, and South Africa. We're looking for our first dedicated Operations & Finance Lead to become the operational backbone of the business. This role owns payroll operations, contractor administration, client invoicing, HR coordination, operational reporting, and internal systems. Beyond execution, you'll help build the operational infrastructure that allows the company to scale efficiently through better documentation, automation, and process improvement. You'll support approximately 30–50 active contractors while partnering closely with leadership, recruiters, clients, and contractors to ensure operations remain accurate, organized, and scalable. ABOUT YOU Do you enjoy bringing order to fast-moving environments? Are you the person who naturally creates checklists, documents processes, and finds better ways of doing things? This role is ideal for someone who enjoys balancing finance, operations, HR administration, and systems improvement. You take pride in accuracy, meet recurring deadlines without constant oversight, and constantly look for opportunities to reduce manual work through better processes and automation. If you're excited about helping build the operational foundation of a growing U.S. startup, we'd love to meet you.
