About the Role

Your role is the first and most important step in that journey: you’re the first person a new patient speaks to, and you set the tone for everything that follows

There is no existing sales team, no playbook built by a predecessor, and no long list of SOPs to follow. The owner is looking for a true partner in building this function, not someone who waits to be told what to do.

The right person will

  • Be a hunker-down, roll-up-your-sleeves type who thrives in a small, fast-moving business
  • Come with ideas and suggest better ways to run calls, follow up with leads, and keep the schedule full
  • Be someone the owner can depend on to bring solutions, not just problems
  • Work collaboratively with the owner, who will also be bringing ideas, training, and feedback

If you want a highly structured role with fixed processes from day one, this isn’t the right fit. If you want to help build something from the ground up and grow with it, it could be a great one.

What You Will Do

Inbound Lead Response & Consultative Sales Calls

  • Answer inbound calls and quickly contact new leads from Meta ads, Google ads, email, and the website.
  • Run friendly, consultative phone conversations with prospective patients.
  • Build rapport, listen actively, and ask thoughtful follow-up questions so prospects feel heard and understood.
  • Learn about each prospect’s pain, injury, sport or activity, goals, and expectations without trying to diagnose them.
  • Explain the clinic’s cash-based model, its one-on-one approach, and what to expect at the first visit.
  • Check whether each prospect is a good fit for cash-based physical therapy.
  • Handle common questions and concerns confidently and professionally.

Booking & Deposit Collection

  • Book qualified prospects into in-person consultations with the owner.
  • Collect consultation deposits over the phone.
  • Process bookings and deposits accurately in the clinic’s scheduling and booking system.
  • Make sure every booking includes accurate patient details and notes from the call so the owner is fully prepared.

Pipeline Management & Follow-Up

  • Manage and update leads in the clinic’s CRM.
  • Follow up with leads who haven’t booked yet, by phone and text.
  • Keep lead records clean, organized, and up to date, with accurate statuses and notes.
  • Follow up on missed calls, no-shows, and cancellations to rebook patients.
  • Track lead activity and conversion, and flag trends, problems, and opportunities to the owner.

Scheduling & Patient Coordination

  • Manage appointment scheduling for the clinic.
  • Call or text patients to confirm upcoming appointments.
  • Handle reschedules and cancellations professionally.
  • Help keep the owner’s schedule full and running efficiently.

Building the Process

  • Help create and document call scripts, processes, and FAQs as the role develops.
  • Proactively suggest improvements to the booking process, lead follow-up, and patient experience.
  • Work with the owner to test new ideas and refine what works.

Administrative Support

  • Manage the clinic’s inbox and respond to patient enquiries.
  • Handle day-to-day admin tasks as needed.

Qualifications & Skills

Required

  • 3+ years of experience in inbound sales, appointment setting, SDR work, customer service, or client-facing roles.
  • Knowledge and strong working experience in Go High Level
  • Experience using Jane or a similar ERM
  • Experience in a similar setting is strongly preferred, such as a physical therapy, sports medicine, wellness, fitness, or medical clinic.
  • A physical therapy or healthcare background is a big plus.
  • Excellent spoken and written English, with a clear, natural, accent-neutral phone manner.
  • A genuinely warm, bubbly, and upbeat personality that puts people at ease on the phone.
  • Confident and comfortable on the phone, including collecting payments or deposits.
  • Strong rapport-building and active listening skills, with the ability to guide a conversation through thoughtful questions.
  • Highly self-motivated, proactive, and independent, with a builder’s mindset and the ability to take initiative without constant direction.
  • Comfortable with ambiguity, and able to think critically and solve problems on your own.
  • Strong organization and follow-through, especially with lead follow-up.
  • Experience working for or supporting a US-based company is strongly preferred.
  • Available to work US Pacific Time hours.

Nice-To-Have

  • Experience with cash-pay or private-pay services is a plus.
  • Interest in sports, fitness, or racket sports is a plus.
  • Basic social media skills are a plus.
