About the Role

As Senior Accountant, you will help lead day-to-day accounting operations while strengthening financial reporting, reconciliations, compliance, and month-end close processes. You will work closely with the Finance Director to improve workflows, maintain dependable financial records, and provide insights that support broader business decisions.

This role is ideal for an accountant who understands the pace and complexity of e-commerce and enjoys bringing structure to high-volume financial activity. You will have the autonomy to identify issues, recommend improvements, and contribute to financial projects beyond routine accounting work.

Responsibilities

Own accurate financial data entry, account reconciliations, and supporting schedules for recurring reporting cycles

Support a timely and well-controlled month-end close process

Maintain complete financial records and documentation that are ready for review or audit

Manage or support accounts payable and accounts receivable workflows, ensuring transactions are processed accurately and on time

Reconcile financial activity across e-commerce platforms such as Shopify, Amazon, payment processors, and accounting systems

Assist with tax filings, regulatory compliance, audits, and requests for supporting documentation

Partner with the Finance Director to streamline accounting processes and strengthen internal controls

Analyze financial data, investigate discrepancies, and communicate findings clearly to stakeholders

Support ad-hoc financial analysis, reporting, and strategic initiatives as business needs evolve

Must-Have

At least 3 years of experience in financial accounting, reporting, or financial analysis

Hands-on experience supporting e-commerce or retail businesses, including platforms such as Shopify or Amazon

Proficiency with accounting software such as QuickBooks, Xero, or a comparable platform

Advanced Excel and Google Sheets skills, including the ability to organize, reconcile, and analyze complex financial data

Strong knowledge of reconciliations, financial documentation, and month-end accounting processes

High attention to detail with the ability to manage competing deadlines accurately

Clear written and verbal English communication skills for effective remote collaboration

Nice to Have

Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field

Experience supporting tax filings, compliance reviews, or external audits

Experience improving accounting workflows or implementing stronger financial controls

Familiarity with e-commerce payment processors, inventory accounting, or multi-channel revenue reconciliation
