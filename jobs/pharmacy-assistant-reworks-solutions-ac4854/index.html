Job Title

Pharmacy Assistant

Location

South Africa

Job Type

Full-Time, Remote

Working Hours

US Hours (9am-5pm EST)

Salary

South African Rand (ZAR)

Please note

Occasional weekend work will be required to support business needs and meet operational demands.

We are seeking a highly organized and detail-oriented Pharmacy Assistant to support the day-to-day administrative and operational functions of a busy pharmacy team. The successful candidate will assist with prescription processing, documentation, inventory support, and general pharmacy administration while ensuring accuracy and compliance with established procedures.

The ideal candidate has previous pharmaceutical or pharmacy experience, excellent attention to detail, and the ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced environment. They are dependable, proactive, and committed to maintaining the highest standards of accuracy and confidentiality.

Key Responsibilities

Assist with the preparation and processing of prescription-related documentation.

Maintain accurate patient and pharmacy records.

Support prescription data entry and verify information for accuracy.

Assist with medication inventory management, stock control, and ordering.

Monitor stock levels and report shortages when necessary.

Ensure all pharmacy documentation is complete, accurate, and up to date.

Communicate professionally with patients, healthcare providers, and internal teams.

Support pharmacists with administrative and operational tasks.

Maintain confidentiality of patient information and comply with all healthcare regulations.

Perform general administrative duties to support the efficient operation of the pharmacy.

Assist with additional duties as assigned.

Previous experience working in a pharmacy, pharmaceutical, or healthcare environment is essential.

Pharmacy Assistant experience is highly advantageous.

Strong administrative and organizational skills.

Excellent attention to detail and accuracy.

Ability to prioritize multiple tasks in a fast-paced environment.

Strong verbal and written English communication skills.

Professional, reliable, and dependable.

Ability to work independently and as part of a team.

Comfortable learning new pharmacy systems and software.

Experience processing prescriptions or pharmacy documentation.

Experience with medication inventory or stock management.

Knowledge of pharmacy regulations and best practices.

Customer service experience within a healthcare or pharmacy setting.
