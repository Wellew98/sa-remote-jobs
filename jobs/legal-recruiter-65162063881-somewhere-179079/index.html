Legal Recruiter / Candidate Development Specialist

PositionLegal Recruiter / Candidate Development Specialist

TypeFull-Time, Independent Contractor

Working HoursAligned with US Business Hours (Eastern / Central / Pacific dependent on assignment)

About Our Client

Our client is a boutique executive search firm specializing in high-stakes legal recruitment across the United States. They represent premier law firms—including AmLaw 100/200 practices and prominent boutique firms—to identify and place elite legal talent across key practice areas such as corporate M&A, commercial litigation, private equity, intellectual property, and technology transactions.

Role Overview

We are seeking a highly articulate, polished, and persistent Legal Recruiter / Candidate Development Specialist to drive passive candidate engagement for sophisticated attorney placements.

This is not a high-volume, transactional recruiting role where candidates submit active applications. You will be reaching out directly to well-established, highly compensated attorneys, senior associates, and law firm partners who are comfortable in their current positions and not actively seeking a move.

Your objective is to conduct intelligent, consultative outreach, build immediate professional credibility, and pique the interest of top-tier legal talent—turning cold outreach into meaningful, preliminary career conversations.

Key Responsibilities

- Passive Candidate Sourcing & Research: Conduct targeted research using specialized legal databases (e.g., Firm Prospects, LinkedIn Recruiter, state bar directories) to identify senior associates and partners matching active search specs.

- Consultative Outbound Outreach: Execute tailored, multi-channel outreach strategies (phone, personalized email, LinkedIn) to initiate contact with passive attorneys.

- High-Touch Candidate Engagement: Articulate complex legal career opportunities clearly and compellingly without sounding transactional or script-reliant.

- Objection Handling & Persistence: Engage constructively with busy, skeptical, or defensive professionals; manage objections smoothly and execute professional, multi-touch follow-up cadences.

- Qualification & Handoff: Assess preliminary alignment regarding practice area fit, career motivation, and book of business (where applicable), then seamlessly introduce qualified candidates to our client's senior search principals.

- Pipeline Quality Maintenance: Focus on candidate quality and fit rather than sheer volume, delivering 3–5 well-qualified, highly interested attorney conversations per month.

Candidate Requirements & Qualifications

Must-Have Qualifications

- Flawless Communication Skills: Exceptional written and spoken English with zero robotic phrasing, strong professional presence, and native-level conversational fluency.

- Sophisticated Professional Presence: Confidence and composure when engaging with high-income, highly educated professionals (AmLaw 100/200 partners, senior corporate attorneys).

- Proven Outbound Sourcing Experience: Demonstrated success in executive search, high-ticket consultative B2B sales, or boutique executive recruitment where cold outreach to passive talent is required.

- Active Listening & Natural Dialogue: Ability to steer conversations dynamically without relying on rigid scripts or generic templates.

- Resilience & Grit: High tolerance for initial rejection, delayed responses, and gatekeepers, paired with the discipline to maintain thoughtful follow-up cadences.

Preferred Qualifications

- Background in legal recruiting, professional services search (consulting, finance, accounting), or high-end B2B sales.

- Familiarity with the US legal landscape (AmLaw rankings, law firm structures, associate/partner tracks, billable hour dynamics).

- Experience utilizing legal recruitment platforms (e.g., Firm Prospects, PitchBook, LinkedIn Recruiter).

What Makes Someone a Poor Fit

- Relying heavily on standardized outreach scripts or automated mass messaging.

- Discomfort or hesitation with outbound phone calls.

- Difficulty communicating fluidly or matching the professional cadence of US-based attorneys.

- Preference for high-volume applicant screening over deep, targeted sourcing.

- Giving up early when passive candidates do not respond to initial touches.

What Success Looks Like

- First 30–60 Days: Master our client's target practice areas, terminology, and firm profiles; establish a consistent outreach process.

- Ongoing Target: Consistently introduce 3 to 5 qualified, highly interested candidate opportunities per month to the senior recruitment team.
