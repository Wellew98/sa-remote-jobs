We're looking for an

Internal Customer Success Manager to own existing client relationships at Hire Overseas, a fast-growing offshore staffing platform that helps founders and executives hire full-time overseas talent. You will be the person clients go to after the sale, running check-ins, vetting and presenting candidates, coordinating placements, spotting problems before clients do, and turning happy clients into rehires, reviews, and referrals.

This role was built to free up a co-founder who has been handling it directly. Nearly all of the company's growth comes from referrals and repeat clients, which means the existing client base is the most important thing in the business, and this role exists to look after it. This is a small team with direct access to both founders and no layers between you and the decisions that matter. If you have high critical thinking, take full ownership of client outcomes, and want a role that grows as fast as you do, this role is a strong fit.

Why You'll Want to Join

You will be paid in USD (bi-monthly: every 15th and 30th)

Paid Time Off

in accordance with company policy

Observance of

Holidays per company guidelines

100% remote setup so you can work wherever you're most productive

This role requires availability during

US business hours with responsiveness outside core hours when clients need you

Work directly with both co-founders of a fast-growing offshore staffing company with a strong referral-driven client base

High-ownership role that grows with you as the company scales

What You'll Work On

Client Relationship Management

Be the primary point of contact for existing clients after the sale

Join kickoff, check-in, and escalation calls and own the client relationship through every stage of the engagement

Run regular check-ins across every active account and spot problems with a placed contractor before the client does

Fix issues when they arise without waiting to be told and without letting things drop

If a client messages at 9pm, you reply; responsiveness is a baseline, not a stretch

Candidate Vetting and Placement

Evaluate the top one to five candidates surfaced by the recruiting team per role and decide who is strong enough to present

Tell the difference between a strong candidate and one who just interviews well

Present your picks to clients, coordinate interviews, handle feedback, and close the placement

Own the quality of every candidate that goes in front of a client

Growth and Retention

Spot when a client is ready to hire again before they ask and bring it to them proactively

When a client is happy, ask for a review and an introduction; most of the company's growth depends on it

Mark first placements, milestones, and good moments with thoughtful, personalized gestures

Turn client satisfaction into long-term relationships, repeat business, and referrals

What You Bring

High critical thinking with the ability to form a clear view, defend it, and apply it to both candidate evaluation and client problem-solving

Full ownership mindset; if a client is unhappy, it is your problem until it is solved

Relevant experience in customer success, account management, staffing, or recruiting

Clear, real, personal written communication; a genuine message beats a polished generic one every time

Responsive by nature with the ability to keep clients feeling prioritized without being chained to a desk

Comfortable growing into a role without a playbook being handed to you; the job will get bigger and you want it to

Nice to Have

Background in staffing, recruiting, or offshore hiring

Experience in a small, founder-led company where you had direct access to leadership and real ownership of outcomes

Familiarity with offshore talent markets in the Philippines, Latin America, or Africa

How to Apply

Please include

Your

updated resume

A short Loom video (1 to 2 minutes) introducing yourself, describing your customer success or account management experience, and walking through a situation where you caught and resolved a client problem before it escalated

If you take full ownership of client relationships, can tell a strong candidate from one who interviews well, and want to be the person that a growing offshore staffing company's most important clients rely on, this role gives you the access, the ownership, and the direct impact to do your best work.
