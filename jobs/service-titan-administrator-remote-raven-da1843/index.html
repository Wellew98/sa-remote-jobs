We are hiring a ServiceTitan Integration Specialist for our client — a US-based, PE-backed multi-brand platform in the roofing and exteriors industry with nine operating brands across 60+ locations. This is not a maintenance seat. Our client is looking for a builder who wants to own ServiceTitan end to end and help make the environment measurably better.

You will share accountability for how ServiceTitan is configured, integrated, adopted, and improved — turning the needs of nine distinct brands into one governed, scalable configuration that still respects each brand's identity. You will work with US-based leadership, brand teams, and vendors, and you will present to stakeholders on camera when the work calls for it.

What You Will Own

ServiceTitan, end to end. Configuration, workflows, roadmap, releases, and continuous optimization.

Integrations. Manage and maintain the connections between ServiceTitan and the broader tech ecosystem (Sage Intacct, Smartsheet, Zoho Desk, Microsoft 365).

New-brand onboarding. As the platform acquires companies, map their setup, migrate their data (including from AccuLynx), and stand them up in ServiceTitan.

Multi-tenant administration. Every ServiceTitan function across complex business units — each with its own setup, templates, and contracts.

Environment build-out. Business units, tags, skills, workflows, SBET/pricebook, templates, and reporting, delivered through a governed, tiered rollout.

Data + AI. Evaluate large data sets, clean and map data for migrations, catch quality issues, and use AI tools to work faster and sharper.

Requirements to reality. Gather needs, build a prioritized backlog, and ship against it with clear acceptance criteria.

Governance and adoption. Set the guardrails that keep nine brands coherent, then drive the training that makes it stick.
