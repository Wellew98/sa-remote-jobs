Cherry Assistant is a global recruitment firm based in the US. We help US, UK, Australian, and Canadian companies hire top talent globally. Job Summary: We are seeking a well spoken and organized Recruitment Consultant to join our team on a full-time basis. The ideal candidate should enjoy talking to various people, learning about new business models, and working hands on with clients and candidates to ensure the best match. Key Responsibilities: - Talent Sourcing: Find candidates through job boards, social media, referrals, and professional networks. - Candidate Screening: Review resumes, run initial interviews, and evaluate skills, past experience, and role fit. - Pipeline Management: Keep applicants organized, update our tracking system, and maintain steady movement through each stage. - Relationship Building: Stay in regular contact with candidates and internal teams. Support clear communication throughout the hiring cycle. - Onboarding Support: Coordinate background checks, gather needed documents, schedule training sessions, and help new hires settle in smoothly. - Performance Review: Monitor key recruiting numbers and provide feedback on what is working and what needs attention. - Compliance: Follow all employment rules and guidelines during each step of the recruiting process. Required Qualifications - Minimum of 2 years of experience in recruitment, sales, or account management - Proficiency in applicant tracking systems or CRM softwares - Enjoys meeting people and learning about different businesses - Strong organizational and time-management skills - Ability to work independently and as part of a team - Native level English speaker with fluency in written and verbal communication - Tech-savvy with the ability to quickly learn and utilize new software and tools Nice To Have Qualifications: - Familiarity using LinkedIn and Indeed for recruiting - Experience using AI softwares to automate workflows - College degree Hours & Pay: - Full-time US hours: 8am to 4pm PST - ZAR 15,000 - 20,000 per month (~$860 - $1200 USD) (excluding commissions, bonuses) - Bi-weekly pay with annual bonuses and raises Why this job? - Exposure into various US and UK businesses - Learn the latest in using AI softwares in your work - Fast paced recruiting environment - Meritocracy based work - Fully remote, work from home If you are a driven and passionate individual with a strong background in recruitment, we encourage you to apply for this exciting opportunity. Join our team and help us connect top talent with leading companies.
