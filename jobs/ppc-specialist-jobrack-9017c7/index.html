Manage and optimize paid campaigns across major platforms. $2,500–$4,500/month | Full-time | Remote (EST collaboration hours)

Hi! I’m Jordan, the head of HR at

Mannix Marketing

.

We’re a digital marketing agency based in the U.S, and we’ve been helping businesses grow online since 1996. Our work focuses on delivering real, measurable results - especially in PPC, SEO, and web design - and we’re proud to support a wide range of clients, from local companies to national brands.

What makes Mannix a great place to work?

We’re a tight-knit team of A-players who truly care about what we do. We value clear communication, ownership, empathy, and a passion for growth.

We also embrace new technologies, especially AI, and are constantly improving how we work together.

You’ll be joining a team that’s effective, collaborative, and genuinely excited to deliver great work!

We’re looking for a PPC Specialist.

We need someone highly analytical and detail-oriented, with solid hands-on experience managing PPC campaigns in fast-paced environments.

You should be comfortable working independently while also collaborating closely with strategists to improve performance and execution speed. Experience working with smaller budgets and highly targeted campaigns is especially important.

In this role, you will

🚀 Develop and execute PPC strategies across Google Ads, Microsoft Advertising, and social platforms 🎯 Create, manage, and optimize search, display, video, remarketing, and social campaigns 🔍 Conduct keyword research to identify growth and optimization opportunities ✍️ Write compelling ad copy and support landing page performance improvements 📊 Implement bid strategies to maximize performance and budget efficiency 📈 Monitor, analyze, and report on campaign performance with actionable insights 🤝 Collaborate with SEO, content, and social teams for integrated campaign execution

Working Hours

This is a full-time contract role with flexible hours, but we do ask for at least 4 hours of overlap between 9 AM – 4 PM EST to allow for collaboration and meetings.

Time Tracking

As part of our standard process, we require time tracking through Toggl for client billing. All candidates should be comfortable with logging their time consistently.

You’ll be reporting to Alexis Connor, Digital Marketing Manager at Mannix Marketing. Alexis leads PPC strategy and campaign execution across client accounts and works closely with the wider marketing team to drive performance and growth.

To excel in this role, you need

✅ Strong English verbal and written communication skills ✅ Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Business, Communications, or related field ✅ 3+ years of PPC campaign management experience ✅ Google Ads and Microsoft Advertising certifications ✅ Strong analytical skills with data-driven decision-making ability ✅ Proficiency with Google Analytics and PPC reporting tools ✅ Experience with A/B testing and conversion rate optimization ✅ Strong project and time management skills

✅ Demonstrated ability to drive conversions and achieve ROI goals through effective Meta advertising campaigns

Brownie points for

⭐ Familiarity with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript ⭐ Experience with GA4 and Looker Studio reporting ⭐ Understanding of SEO principles and PPC integration

⭐ Experience in leveraging AI and automation to drive PPC campaign efficiency and performance gains

You’re likely someone who thinks critically, works independently, and pays close attention to detail. You’re a strong problem solver who communicates clearly, stays open to new ideas, and is motivated by growth.

You care about efficiency, approach challenges with a solutions mindset, and take ownership of your work while collaborating effectively with others.

What’s in it for you?

💰 $2,500 – $4,500 per month (based on experience)*

🌍 Full-time contract role, fully remote with flexible working hours

⏰ Freedom to manage your own schedule while aligning with core team hours (9am–4pm EST)

🤖 Work in a team that actively embraces AI and new marketing technologies

*Please note that this is a gross salary and that you are responsible for any tax-related procedures in your country of residence. We kindly ask you to apply only if you are happy to work remotely as an independent contractor.
