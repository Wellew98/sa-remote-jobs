About the Role

We are looking for a highly intelligent, detail-oriented, and articulate Client Intake & Quality Assurance Specialist to join our team. This role is suited to someone who is quick-thinking, organised, and an excellent communicator, with the ability to manage both client-facing intake responsibilities and detailed quality assurance tasks.

The ideal candidate will be equally comfortable communicating with clients, gathering information, and handling administrative processes as they are reviewing documentation, identifying discrepancies, and ensuring accuracy across various processes.

We are looking for someone with a great personality, a proactive attitude, strong attention to detail, and the ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced environment. There may also be opportunities to assist with other operational responsibilities as business needs evolve

Key Responsibilities

1. Quality Assurance & Documentation Review

Review records, documentation and completed work to ensure accuracy, completeness and compliance with established requirements.

Identify errors, inconsistencies, missing information and areas that require correction.

Conduct detailed quality checks and ensure all required information is properly documented.

Maintain high standards of accuracy across records, forms and administrative processes.

Flag recurring errors or process gaps and communicate findings to the relevant team members.

Follow established quality assurance procedures and ensure corrections are completed where necessary.

2. Client Intake & Communication

Handle incoming enquiries and communicate professionally with prospective clients or individuals requiring assistance.

Guide individuals through the intake process by asking relevant questions and gathering the necessary information.

Ensure all information collected is accurate, complete and correctly recorded.

Explain processes, answer questions and provide clear guidance throughout the intake process.

Follow up on outstanding information or documentation where necessary.

Communicate with empathy, confidence and professionalism, ensuring a positive experience.

3. Administrative Coordination & Follow-Up

Maintain accurate records and ensure information is updated in the relevant systems.

Manage multiple tasks while maintaining attention to detail and meeting deadlines.

Identify missing or incomplete information and take the necessary steps to resolve it.

Communicate updates, concerns and potential issues to the relevant team members.

Adapt between quality assurance, intake and other administrative responsibilities as business needs require.

Essential Requirements

Excellent spoken and written English communication skills.

Clear, articulate speech with a neutral or light accent that is easily understood by US-based clients.

Exceptional attention to detail and the ability to identify even minor errors or inconsistencies.

Strong comprehension skills and the ability to process information quickly and accurately.

Quick-thinking, intelligent and able to learn new processes and systems efficiently.

Confident speaking with clients, asking questions and gathering information.

Strong organisational skills and the ability to manage multiple responsibilities.

Comfortable working with detailed documentation, records and quality assurance processes.

Able to work independently, take initiative and follow tasks through to completion.

Professional, approachable and confident communication style.

Advantageous Experience

Previous experience in quality assurance, auditing or documentation review.

Experience in healthcare administration or medical documentation.

Previous intake, admissions, patient coordination or client onboarding experience.

Customer service or call centre experience involving regular client communication.

Experience identifying errors, reviewing records and maintaining quality standards.

Software & Systems

Comfortable working with digital platforms, spreadsheets and documentation systems.

Able to learn new software and internal systems quickly.

Confident using email, online communication tools and data-entry platforms.

Ideal Candidate Profile

The ideal candidate is someone who combines exceptional attention to detail with strong interpersonal and communication skills. They enjoy structured, detail-heavy work and take pride in ensuring that information is accurate, complete and properly documented.

At the same time, they are confident and articulate enough to handle client intake conversations, ask the right questions and communicate clearly with individuals from different backgrounds.

We are looking for someone who is sharp, quick to learn, adaptable and able to switch comfortably between detailed quality assurance work and people-facing responsibilities without compromising on either.
