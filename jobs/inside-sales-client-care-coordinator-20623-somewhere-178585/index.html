Inside Sales & Client Care Coordinator (Remote) Location & Hours Remote. Full-time IC 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM Eastern, Monday through Friday. About the Company We are an established, family-owned custom window treatment, area rug, and wallpaper company serving homeowners across New Jersey. We operate through a mix of showroom locations and a shop-at-home model, pairing expert field salespeople with a personalized, in-home consultative approach. As we grow, we're building out the behind-the-scenes support that keeps our sales team focused on what they do best — being in front of clients. About the Role This role exists to protect our salespeople's selling time and elevate the client experience from the first phone call through post-installation. You'll be the connective tissue between our clients and our field team — running virtual phone consultations, qualifying and nurturing leads while interest is high, coordinating appointments, and keeping back-office operations organized. When you do this well, our salespeople walk into every in-home appointment fully briefed, with warmer and better-informed clients, and nothing slips through the cracks in between. Key Responsibilities Inside Sales & Lead Engagement - Conduct virtual phone consultations with clients assigned through the Hunter Douglas Retail Lead Network (Home Depot and JCPenney accounts) on behalf of the sales team. - Respond quickly to warm inbound leads across all channels — Hunter Douglas, website, and social media — engaging clients while their interest is high. - Qualify leads by uncovering project scope, number of windows, and product interests, then walk clients through their options. - Educate clients on our product lines so they arrive at their in-home appointment informed and ready to move forward. - Send curated product information and links after each call to help clients narrow their choices and set the salesperson up to close. - Document each consultation and hand off clear notes so the assigned salesperson walks in fully briefed. Appointment & Phone Coordination - Provide overflow and backup phone coverage for the Westfield and Tenafly showrooms when managers are unavailable. - Staff the Princeton phone line full-time, as that location runs shop-at-home without a dedicated showroom manager. - Reschedule and adjust client appointments on behalf of salespeople when conflicts arise. - Confirm appointments the day before to reduce no-shows. CRM, Operations & Sales Support - Assist with quoting products and building out project details. - Order materials and samples for our open-market residential clients (direct clients outside the Retail Lead Network) using company vendor accounts. - Nurture aged and cold leads in GoHighLevel so nothing goes stale, and reactivate old quotes that never closed. - Make post-installation follow-up calls to request Google reviews. - Triage the shared inbox and route emails to the correct salesperson. - Maintain data hygiene in GoHighLevel — keeping tags, pipeline stages, and lead sources clean and current. - Prepare quote packets and sample lists ahead of installations so salespeople arrive ready. - Review and refine standard operating procedures on an ongoing basis. Required Qualifications - Excellent spoken and written English with a warm, consultative, and confident phone presence. - Strong organizational skills and the ability to juggle multiple client and sales workflows without dropping details. - Comfort with CRM systems and general tech-savviness (email, scheduling tools, product catalogs). - Proven ability to follow up promptly and persistently without being pushy. - Availability to work 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM Eastern consistently, Monday through Friday. Preferred Qualifications - Prior experience in inside sales, appointment setting, customer service, or client coordination — ideally in home services, interior design, or a consultative retail environment. - Hands-on experience with GoHighLevel or a comparable CRM. - Familiarity with window treatments, home décor, or a product-driven sales process. Ideal Candidate Profile You are: - Highly organized and proactive — you close loops before anyone has to ask. - Genuinely personable on the phone, able to build trust with homeowners quickly. - A natural coordinator who likes being the reliable center point of a busy sales operation. - Detail-oriented enough to keep a CRM clean, and commercially minded enough to spot a stale lead worth reviving. - Comfortable owning back-office work while still enjoying real client conversations. Why Join Us You'll join a close-knit, family-owned business where your work has a visible, direct impact. This is a role with real ownership — not a small cog in a large call center, but the person the sales team relies on to keep clients happy and operations running smoothly. As the company grows, so will the scope and importance of this seat.
