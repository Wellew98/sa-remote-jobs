Who are we?

SupportYourApp

is a global Intelligent Support-as-a-Service leader, partnering with tech companies and industry leaders like Mastercard, Calm and MacPaw in 30+ countries since 2010 to deliver secure customer and technical support. We operate globally, supporting clients in 60 languages across diverse industries. So what if you had a chance to be a part of the world's leading SaaS, software, or hardware solutions?

Join our community as a

Customer Support Consultant on a short-term basis and unlock your potential by mastering new skills and achieving challenging goals in cooperation with our People First management approach.

Excited? Let's see what it takes 💛

What you will do

Provide prompt and accurate support to users by resolving tickets in line with project-specific procedures and quality expectations;

Follow all guidelines related to tone, accuracy, escalation paths, and ticket categorization as outlined in the SOP;

Document all user interactions clearly and professionally within the designated support platform;

Perform daily supplemental tasks as assigned to support operational excellence (e.g., knowledge review, internal communications, task reporting);

Maintain a strong understanding of project workflows, updates, and service goals;

Strive to meet or exceed individual KPIs and project metrics consistently;

Reach out to the Team Lead regularly when facing difficulties understanding procedures or handling specific tickets;

Communicate openly about availability, workload, and schedule-related concerns;

Proactively contribute to a positive and supportive team culture;

Follow the assigned shift schedule as distributed by the Team Lead;

Submit any schedule-related requests (e.g., shift swaps) directly to the Team Lead for review and approval.

What you need to succeed in this role

Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English (C1);

Strong attention to detail and commitment to delivering high-quality support;

Ability to work independently while following established procedures and service expectations;

Reliability in meeting shift schedules and deadlines;

Proactive attitude toward learning and continuous improvement;

Flexibility and adaptability to process, software, and expectation changes;

Previous customer service (email-based) experience;

Previous Zendesk experience;

Previous experience working with KPIs and expectations;

Ability to resolve 80–100 tickets per shift;

An Android or Apple device (a smartphone works), required for signing into accounts.

Compensation in USD;

Responsive leadership interested in your development;

Greenhouse conditions for self-development;

A culture built on trust, with no time-tracking requirements.
