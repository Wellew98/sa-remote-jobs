About Huzzle

At Huzzle, we connect exceptional talents with top opportunities at leading companies across the UK, US, Canada, Europe, and Australia. Our clients include startups, digital agencies, and tech platforms in industries such as SaaS, MarTech, FinTech, and EdTech. Unlike an outsourcing agency, we place you directly with a client where you’re hired in-house as a valued member of their team.

If you're passionate about paid search marketing, data-driven campaign optimization, and scaling digital growth, this remote Google Ads Specialist role offers the opportunity to work with innovative companies and deliver measurable marketing results.

Role TypeFull-time

EngagementIndependent Contractor

Job Summary

We are seeking a Senior Google Ads Specialist with 5+ years of hands-on experience managing high-budget PPC campaigns. This is a high-impact remote marketing role where you will take ownership of Google Ads strategy, execution, and optimization across multiple client accounts.

You will be responsible for driving measurable growth through paid search, improving conversion performance, and scaling campaigns profitably.

Key Responsibilities

Plan, build, and manage Google Ads campaigns (Search, Display, YouTube, Performance Max)

Conduct in-depth keyword research, competitor analysis, and audience targeting

Optimize campaigns for performance metrics including CTR, CPC, CPA, ROAS, and conversion rates

Implement and manage conversion tracking (Google Tag Manager, GA4, tracking pixels)

Continuously test ad creatives, landing pages, and bidding strategies (A/B testing)

Analyze campaign performance and deliver actionable insights and reports

Manage and allocate advertising budgets effectively across campaigns

Collaborate with creative, content, and CRO teams to improve funnel performance

Stay updated on Google Ads updates, algorithm changes, and industry best practices

5+ years of experience in Google Ads / PPC campaign management

Proven track record of managing high-performing campaigns with significant ad spend

Strong understanding of paid search strategies, bidding models, and funnel optimization

Hands-on experience with Google Analytics (GA4), Google Tag Manager, and conversion tracking setup

Experience working with eCommerce, SaaS, or lead generation campaigns

Strong analytical mindset with the ability to interpret data and make strategic decisions

Google Ads certifications are highly preferred

Excellent communication skills and ability to present performance insights clearly

Experience working remotely with international teams is a plus

💻 Fully Remote: Work from anywhere with international teams
