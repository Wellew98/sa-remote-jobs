PositionUI/UX Designer

LocationSouth Africa (Remote)

Working Hours9 a.m. -5 p.m. Central European Time Zone (±2 hours)

Salary1000 - 1500 USD

Responsibilities

  • Product and UX Design: Execute and translate the product vision into product concepts and simple and delightful product/web designs.
  • Create Design Assets: Develop wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity designs using Figma to illustrate design concepts.
  • Shape Design Identity: Play a key role in shaping the design identity of the startup, influencing both product design and overall brand aesthetics.
  • Collaborate with Founders: Work closely with the design-focused founders to understand project requirements and deliver effective design solutions.
  • Ensure Consistency: Maintain consistency and cohesiveness across all design elements to uphold a strong brand identity.
  • Iterate and Improve: Analyze feedback to refine designs, focusing on simplicity and quality to enhance user experience.
  • Attention to Detail: Exhibit an eye for detail and a drive for simplicity in all design work.

Requirements

  • Experience: 3-5 years of experience as a UIUX Designer with a strong portfolio showcasing your design skills and creativity.
  • SaaS and eCommerce: Experience designing for SaaS platforms and/or eCommerce, particularly within the fashion industry, is highly desirable.
  • Tools: Proficiency in Figma is a must; knowledge of Framer is a plus but not required (willingness to learn required).
  • Design Principles: Strong understanding of design principles, typography, and color theory.
  • Communication Skills: Excellent communication skills, both verbal and written.
  • Startup Mindset: Ability to work independently in a remote startup environment, adapting to fast-paced changes.
  • Passion for Quality: Commitment to delivering high-quality designs, with quality above all.
