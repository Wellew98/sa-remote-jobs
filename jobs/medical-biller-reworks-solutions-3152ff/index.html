About the Role

We are looking for a proactive, highly organised and detail-oriented Medical Billing & Accounts Administrator to join a growing healthcare services team.

The successful candidate will be responsible for supporting the financial and administrative operations of a US-based healthcare provider. This includes processing payments, verifying insurance benefits, managing insurance payer portal registrations, coordinating electronic payment and remittance enrolments, and handling family billing and client invoicing.

This role requires someone who is comfortable working with healthcare billing processes, financial records and multiple administrative systems. You will need to take ownership of assigned responsibilities, stay on top of outstanding tasks and ensure that billing and payment administration is handled accurately and efficiently.

The ideal candidate is someone who takes initiative, works independently and can manage multiple ongoing tasks without requiring constant supervision.

Requirements

Key Responsibilities

1. Medical Billing & Payment Administration

Process and post incoming payments accurately to the relevant client accounts.

Manage payment posting and ensure financial records are kept up to date.

Review payment information and identify discrepancies or missing information.

Assist with maintaining accurate billing and payment records.

Monitor outstanding payments and follow up on relevant billing queries.

2. Insurance Benefits & Verification

Conduct insurance benefit checks to confirm patient coverage and available benefits.

Review insurance information and ensure relevant details are accurately recorded.

Identify potential coverage issues or discrepancies and escalate where necessary.

Maintain accurate records of insurance verification activities.

3. Insurance Payer Portal Administration

Set up and maintain insurance payer portal accounts.

Coordinate agency recognition and registration with relevant insurance payers.

Manage Electronic Remittance Advice (ERA) and Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) enrolments.

Monitor enrolment progress and follow up on outstanding registrations or approvals.

Ensure payer portal information and account details remain accurate and up to date.

4. Family Billing & Client Invoicing

Manage billing administration for families receiving healthcare services.

Prepare and issue invoices to clients and families.

Maintain accurate billing records and supporting documentation.

Respond to billing-related queries and assist with resolving discrepancies.

Ensure invoices and payment information are processed within required timelines.

5. General Financial & Administrative Support

Maintain organised records, spreadsheets and billing documentation.

Track outstanding tasks, enrolments, payments and administrative requests.

Manage multiple projects and deadlines simultaneously.

Identify administrative gaps and proactively follow up on outstanding items.

Provide regular updates on completed tasks, pending activities and potential issues.

Support improvements to billing and administrative workflows.

Minimum Requirements

Previous experience in medical billing, healthcare administration or medical accounts administration is strongly preferred.

Understanding of medical billing and payment posting processes.

Experience working with insurance benefits verification.

Familiarity with insurance payer portals and healthcare administration.

Knowledge of ERA/EFT enrolment processes.

Experience with invoicing, payment tracking and financial administration.

Strong numerical and data-entry accuracy.

Excellent organisational and time-management skills.

Strong written and verbal English communication skills.

Good computer literacy and confidence working across multiple systems.

Ability to work independently and take ownership of responsibilities.

Strong attention to detail and ability to manage multiple ongoing tasks.

Advantageous Experience

Experience working with US healthcare providers or medical billing companies.

Exposure to ABA therapy or behavioural healthcare billing.

Experience with insurance payer registration and agency recognition.

Familiarity with electronic payment processing and remittance systems.

Experience managing family billing and patient accounts.

Exposure to healthcare practice management or medical billing software.

Software & Systems

Candidates should ideally have experience working with

Medical billing or practice management systems.

Insurance payer portals.

ERA/EFT enrolment platforms.

Insurance benefits verification systems.

Payment posting and invoicing software.

Microsoft Excel and other administrative tools.

Specific software training will be provided where required.

Working Hours & Holiday Structure

Full-time remote position.

Working hours aligned with the client's US business operations.

US public holidays observed.

No weekend work required.
