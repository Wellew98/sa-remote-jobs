About the Company

My client is a global creative production company — 10+ years, 8,000+ pitches, US$3B+ in secured production budgets across 40+ countries. We deliver pitch decks, campaigns, film development, and AI-hybrid productions through a transparent four-tier service structure, and we pay our creatives fairly by design.

About The Role

We're hiring a dedicated Closer for an unusually clean closing seat: a dedicated outbound Hunter feeds you qualified, decision-maker-level opportunities; a published rate card with named price anchors does the positioning; proposal templates are built; and the Director General co-closes premium engagements with you. Your job is one thing: turn qualified pipelines into signed agreements at full value.

What You’ll Do

  • Own every opportunity from qualified handoff to signature: discovery, scoping, proposal, negotiation, close.
  • Deliver template-based proposals within 48 hours of handoff, priced on the rate card.
  • Defend pricing with warmth and spine — our structure is transparent and we hold it.
  • Hand signed clients to our Client Service Lead with complete context; then go close to the next one.
  • Work directly with the Director General: weekly pipeline review, async deal updates, co-closing on top-tier engagements.

What We’re Looking For

  • 4+ years closing B2B service deals in the $2K–$25K range, ideally creative, agency, media, or professional services.
  • Demonstrated price discipline — you can point to deals you walked from rather than discounted into.
  • Excellent spoken and written English; polished on video calls with senior stakeholders.
  • Comfortable with a 3–6 week consultative cycle; organized tracker and pipeline hygiene.
  • Time-zone overlap with Pacific Time mornings or midday
  • Must be available Mondays 4:30pm - 5:30pm for weekly Team Meeting

We’re Looking For (5 C’s + E):

  • Courage (dominant) - High-volume outreach, comfortable with rejection math, initiates contact with senior decision-makers without hesitation.
  • Critical Thinking (dominant) - Researches and scores prospects, screens reactivation targets against payment history, runs channel experiments and reads the data.
  • Communication - Writes excellent short-form outreach; leads with the tier ladder and a named price anchor.
  • Collaboration - Clean CLCP handoffs to the Closer; disciplined CC protocol with the CSL on any existing-account touch.
  • Creativity Fresh angles per vertical — speaks VC language to VCs, spatial/visual language to architects and developers.
  • Empathy / Energy - Reads prospect readiness; sustains daily activity without burning contacts or brand.
