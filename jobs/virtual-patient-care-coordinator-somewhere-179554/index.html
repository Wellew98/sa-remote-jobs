Location / Employment Details

  • Work setup: Fully remote, full-time
  • Hours: Monday to Friday on U.S. Central time, with the occasional weekend task to be agreed later
  • Target region: Philippines first, with South Africa as a second option
  • Reports to: The practice owner (a sports chiropractor) and the clinic administrator
  • Systems: GoHighLevel (CRM) and Unify (call center)

About the Company

We are a rapidly growing U.S.-based sports chiropractic clinic in the Midwest. We work with athletes and active adults who want to recover from injury, move better and perform at a higher level. Care here is one-on-one and unhurried. Each patient gets a full assessment and a plan built around their goals, not a quick adjustment and a handoff. That approach is why patients stay and refer their teammates, friends and families. It also means the clinic is growing faster than its back office, which is where you come in. About the Role Our sessions run a full hour, so every missed call, unconfirmed appointment or incomplete intake form costs us real clinical time. Today the clinic administrator carries most of the back-end work alone, and the doctor picks up the rest between patients. We need one dependable person to own the front desk from a distance: keeping the schedule full, making sure patients arrive prepared, and following up with people who have gone quiet. This is not a data-entry seat. You'll be the voice and inbox patients meet before they ever step into the clinic, so the way you speak and write sets the tone for their experience. You'll also help guide new and returning patients through our process, which is why a little sales instinct matters here. Key Responsibilities Scheduling & Patient Flow

  • Book, confirm, reschedule and fill cancelled appointments in the clinic calendar
  • Send reminders so patients show up on time and prepared
  • Keep the schedule tight, with no gaps that could have been filled

Patient Outreach & Communication

  • Make outbound calls and answer inbound calls through the call center system
  • Respond to patient emails, calls and texts quickly and professionally
  • Follow up with new inquiries, missed appointments and patients due to return
  • Walk new patients through how the clinic works and what to expect at the first visit

Patient Files & Intake

  • Send intake forms ahead of time and chase any that come back incomplete
  • Keep patient records accurate and up to date, with HIPAA-compliant handling at every step
  • Complete the non-clinical checks and pre-visit prep that need to happen before a patient is seen

CRM & Systems

  • Keep contacts, pipelines and follow-up sequences clean and current in GoHighLevel
  • Flag patterns the doctor should know about, such as no-show trends or stalled inquiries

Social Media & Projects

  • Schedule and post social content using material supplied by the clinic
  • Take on small admin projects as the practice grows, such as updating forms, tidying SOPs and organizing shared files

Required Qualifications

  • 2 to 4 years of experience in an administrative, front-desk, patient coordination or appointment-setting role
  • Working knowledge of HIPAA and comfort handling patient information responsibly
  • Clear, confident spoken English with a neutral accent, suitable for outbound and inbound patient calls
  • Strong written English for patient emails and texts
  • Proven experience managing a busy calendar and chasing paperwork to completion
  • Comfortable working Central time hours, Monday to Friday
  • Reliable internet and a quiet, professional workspace

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in a healthcare, chiropractic, physical therapy, wellness or other appointment-based clinic
  • Hands-on experience with GoHighLevel or a similar CRM, and a cloud call center tool such as Unify
  • Some sales, appointment-setting or patient-conversion experience
  • Social media scheduling and basic content skills
  • Prior experience supporting U.S. clients or working U.S. hours
  • Familiarity with intake form tools and basic process documentation

Ideal Candidate Profile

You are warm on the phone and precise on paper. You like owning a schedule end to end, and you notice when something is about to slip before anyone has to tell you. You're comfortable picking up the phone to follow up with a patient, and you can explain a process clearly enough that people feel looked after, not sold to. You take patient privacy seriously, you ask good questions when something is unclear, and you can work independently with two busy people as your point of contact. You want a long-term seat in a growing practice where your work is visible and valued. Why Join Us You'll be the first dedicated remote hire in a growing, patient-focused practice, working directly with the doctor and clinic administrator. There's room to shape how the front office runs, take on more responsibility as the clinic grows, and see the impact of your work in a full schedule and happy patients.
