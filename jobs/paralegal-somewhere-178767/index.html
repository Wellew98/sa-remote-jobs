Role :  Paralegal

Priority LocationAny

Working Hours10am - 6pm (Mon - Fri) EST

Salary Range : $2,000 - $2,500 USD per month & commission TBD (The final offer is at the client’s discretion and depends on the candidate’s interview result, skills, and experience.)

Type of contractIndependent Contractor

Type of jobRemote

Key Responsibilities

  • Provide day-to-day paralegal and administrative support to a transactional partner.
  • Review, organize, and manage legal documents and supporting materials.
  • Conduct detailed document dives to identify and retrieve relevant information.
  • Organize files, records, correspondence, and matter-related documentation.
  • Assist with preparing, compiling, formatting, and maintaining legal documents and paperwork.
  • Review documents for completeness, consistency, and organizational accuracy.
  • Track important information, outstanding documentation, deadlines, and follow-up items.
  • Maintain organized digital files and ensure documents can be quickly located when needed.
  • Assist the partner with research and information-gathering assignments as required.
  • Summarize or organize findings from large sets of documents for attorney review.
  • Handle sensitive and confidential client and legal information appropriately.
  • Coordinate with attorneys and other members of the firm when necessary.
  • Proactively identify missing information, inconsistencies, or outstanding action items.
  • Provide additional legal and administrative support as the firm's needs evolve.

Qualifications & Skills

Required / Highly Preferred

  • Previous experience as a Paralegal, Legal Assistant, or similar legal support professional.
  • Experience working with a U.S.-based law firm, attorney, or legal department is highly preferred.
  • Familiarity with U.S. legal terminology, processes, documentation, and professional standards.
  • Exceptional organizational skills and attention to detail.
  • Strong ability to work through large volumes of documents and identify relevant information.
  • Excellent written and verbal English communication skills.
  • Strong document management and administrative capabilities.
  • Ability to prioritize multiple assignments and maintain accuracy under deadlines.
  • Comfortable working independently in a remote environment.
  • High degree of professionalism, discretion, and confidentiality.

Nice-to-Have

  • Previous experience supporting transactional attorneys or transactional legal matters.
  • Experience conducting document reviews or managing document-heavy legal matters.
  • Experience working directly with partners or senior attorneys.
  • Familiarity with legal document management, practice management, or case management platforms.
  • Experience working remotely for a U.S.-based legal team.

Key Attributes

The ideal candidate will be

  • Highly Organized: Able to bring structure to significant volumes of documents and information.
  • Detail-Oriented: Notices small discrepancies and understands that accuracy matters in a legal environment.
  • Resourceful: Comfortable digging through documents and information to find what an attorney needs.
  • Reliable: Follows assignments through and consistently delivers accurate work.
  • Proactive: Identifies missing information and next steps rather than waiting to be prompted.
  • Professional: Understands the sensitivity and confidentiality associated with legal work.
  • Independent: Capable of managing responsibilities with limited supervision once expectations are established.
