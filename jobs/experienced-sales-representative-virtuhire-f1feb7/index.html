Experienced Sales Representative – FMCG (Snacks) | Remote, South Africa

Are you a results-driven sales professional with strong connections in the snack industry ? Do you have the drive to expand market reach and build lasting partnerships? If so, we want to hear from you!

About the Role

We are seeking an

Experienced Sales Representative to identify and secure distributors and importers for our client’s snack products in South Africa. This is a remote role that requires frequent travel and a proactive approach to market expansion, relationship-building, and sales growth .

Key Responsibilities

Identify and engage potential importers and distributors for our client’s snack products.

Conduct market research to uncover sales opportunities and assess distributor capabilities.

Build and maintain strong relationships with key stakeholders in the industry.

Negotiate agreements that benefit both the company and its partners.

Ensure smooth distribution of products into target markets.

Existing connections in the snack industry

  • this is essential.

Three or more years of experience

in sales, sales support, or sales operations, ideally in FMCG or manufacturing.

A

Bachelor’s degree

in Marketing, Business, or a related field.

A strong networker and relationship builder with excellent communication skills.

Highly goal-driven, resilient, and proactive in seeking out opportunities.

Willingness to

travel extensively (60% or more) across South Africa.

South African residency

  • this role is only open to candidates currently living in South Africa.
