About the Role

We’re hiring a results-driven Business Development Representative (BDR) to generate qualified sales opportunities through outbound prospecting, lead qualification, discovery calls, and pipeline development .

You’ll be responsible for identifying potential customers, reaching decision-makers, understanding their business needs, and converting conversations into qualified meetings for Account Executives.

This is an ideal opportunity for someone who enjoys B2B sales, cold calling, outbound outreach, relationship building, and creating pipeline in a fast-paced, performance-driven environment.

What a Typical Day Looks Like

You may start by reviewing your pipeline and prioritizing follow-ups before building a new list of target accounts and decision-makers.

Throughout the day, you’ll make outbound calls, send personalized emails and LinkedIn messages, respond to inbound leads, conduct discovery conversations, and schedule qualified meetings for Account Executives.

You’ll document activity in the CRM, coordinate handoffs, review outreach performance, and refine your messaging based on what’s generating conversations.

In short

you create new sales opportunities by finding the right prospects, starting meaningful conversations, qualifying their needs, and moving strong opportunities into the sales pipeline.

What Happens After You Apply

Right after you apply, you’ll receive an email invitation from Spark Hire to record your Intro Video. It’s a short, self-recorded video completed on your own time and is the final step needed to complete your application.

Instead of repeating your background across multiple screening calls, you get to introduce yourself once and show hiring teams how you communicate before the first interview. If there’s a strong fit, your first live conversation can focus on the opportunity rather than starting from scratch.

Don’t overthink it. You can record your responses multiple times, and discarded takes are not shared.
