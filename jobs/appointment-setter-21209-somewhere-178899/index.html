Appointment Setter

About the Company

Our client is a life insurance brokerage focused on helping individuals and families understand their insurance options and secure coverage that aligns with their financial needs and long-term goals.

The company is looking for an experienced Appointment Setter who can engage prospective clients, build rapport, identify interest, and consistently schedule qualified appointments for the insurance broker.

About the Role

We are seeking a proactive and personable Appointment Setter to manage prospect outreach, follow-up, and appointment scheduling.

This is a sales-focused role for someone who is comfortable spending a significant portion of their day communicating with prospective clients by phone, text, and email. The ideal candidate is confident on the phone, persistent without being overly aggressive, and skilled at turning initial conversations into scheduled appointments.

The position will begin part-time for the first six months , with the intention of transitioning into a full-time Monday–Friday position following the initial ramp-up period.

Key Responsibilities

Prospect Outreach & Appointment Setting

- Conduct outbound calls to prospective life insurance clients.

- Engage leads through phone, text, email, and other approved communication channels.

- Introduce the brokerage's services clearly and professionally.

- Identify prospects' initial insurance needs, interests, and readiness to speak with a broker.

- Qualify leads based on established criteria.

- Schedule qualified appointments directly onto the broker's calendar.

- Confirm upcoming appointments and reduce cancellations and no-shows.

- Re-engage leads who previously expressed interest but did not schedule or move forward.

- Maintain consistent outreach volume and follow-up activity.

Lead Follow-Up & Nurturing

- Follow established outreach and follow-up sequences.

- Maintain communication with prospects who are interested but not yet ready to schedule.

- Follow up consistently without allowing viable opportunities to fall through the cracks.

- Handle common initial questions and objections confidently.

- Build trust with prospects while maintaining a consultative, professional approach.

- Recognize when a prospect is ready for a more detailed insurance conversation and transition them appropriately to the broker.

CRM & Pipeline Management

- Maintain accurate and current prospect information within the CRM or lead management system.

- Document calls, conversations, outcomes, follow-up dates, and appointment details.

- Keep the appointment-setting pipeline organized and current.

- Track leads through different stages of the outreach process.

- Maintain accurate notes so the broker has appropriate context before each scheduled appointment.

- Follow established processes for lead disposition and follow-up.

Required Qualifications

- 2–5 years of experience in appointment setting, outbound sales, inside sales, lead generation, telesales, or a similar customer-facing sales role.

- Previous experience conducting outbound calls and scheduling appointments.

- Excellent spoken and written English communication skills.

- Confident and professional phone presence.

- Strong ability to build rapport quickly with prospective clients.

- Comfortable handling objections and maintaining conversations with hesitant prospects.

- Persistent and disciplined approach to follow-up.

- Strong organizational and time-management skills.

- Comfortable working with CRM systems, calendars, email, and other digital communication tools.

- Ability to work independently and remain productive in a remote environment.

- Reliable, accountable, and comfortable working toward measurable activity and appointment-setting goals.

Preferred Qualifications

- Previous experience in life insurance, insurance, financial services, or another consultative sales environment.

- Experience speaking with U.S.-based consumers.

- Familiarity with insurance lead-generation and appointment-setting processes.

- Experience working with both warm and cold leads.

- Understanding of sales pipelines and lead nurturing.

- Experience working in a performance-driven sales environment.

What Success Looks Like

Success in this role means consistently engaging prospective clients, maintaining disciplined follow-up, and generating a reliable pipeline of qualified appointments for the broker.

The ideal candidate will become a dependable extension of the sales process—someone who can take ownership of lead follow-up, communicate professionally with prospective clients, and ultimately grow from the initial part-time position into a long-term full-time member of the team.
