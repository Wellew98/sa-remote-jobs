Overview

We are seeking a proactive and adaptable Virtual Assistant to support a multi-faceted entrepreneur managing a growing skincare brand alongside a career in entertainment and modeling.

This role is ideal for someone who enjoys variety, can handle both admin and light marketing tasks, and thrives in a fast-paced, evolving environment. You will act as a right-hand assistant, taking over day-to-day operational and communication tasks so the founder can focus on growth and creative work.

Key Responsibilities

Admin & Communication

- Manage and respond to emails on behalf of the founder

- Handle routine and “easy” inbound communications

-

Draft and send professional replies

- Make outbound calls when required

Scheduling & Personal Assistance

- Manage calendar and appointments

-

Coordinate bookings and meetings

- Assist with travel planning (flights, accommodation, logistics)

Marketing & Brand Support

- Research and identify PR influencers for product promotion

-

Assist with outreach coordination

- Support execution of ad campaigns (primarily Google & Pinterest ads)

- Upload ads and track basic performance metrics

General Virtual Assistant Support

- Conduct online research

- Assist with ad hoc tasks across the business

- Take initiative in identifying ways to reduce the founder’s workload

Requirements

- Strong written and verbal English communication

-

Previous VA / Personal Assistant experience

- Comfortable handling emails independently

- Basic understanding of digital marketing (ads, influencers)

-

Highly organized and proactive

- Able to work PST hours

Nice to Have

- Experience with Google Ads or Pinterest Ads

- Experience sourcing or working with influencers

- Background supporting entrepreneurs or personal brands

Growth Opportunity

This role will start part-time but has strong potential to transition into a full-time position based on performance and business growth.
