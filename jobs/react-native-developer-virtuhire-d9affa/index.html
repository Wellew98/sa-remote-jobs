React Native Developer

VirtuHire South Africa (Remote) Software & IT Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

Our client is on a mission to revolutionize restaurant bookings, and need passionate developers to help them build the best mobile experience. As a React Native Developer , you’ll be at the heart of their product, crafting seamless and high-performance applications for both iOS and Android. Join their dynamic team and collaborate with product managers, designers, and backend engineers to create intuitive and feature-rich mobile apps that delight users. If you thrive in a fast-paced environment and love solving real-world problems, we’d love to hear from you! What You’ll Do: Lead the design, development, and continuous improvement of mobile apps using React Native. Work closely with product managers and designers to bring innovative ideas to life. Build and maintain reusable, scalable components and libraries. Optimize app performance for smooth animations, efficient resource usage, and fast load times. Integrate seamlessly with APIs, third-party services, and native modules like Apple Pay and Google Maps. Contribute to technical discussions, advocating best practices in mobile development. Identify and solve user experience and development challenges proactively. REQUIREMENTS: Strong experience with React Native and JavaScript/TypeScript . Proven track record of building and maintaining mobile applications on both iOS and Android . Expertise with React Navigation, Expo, or other navigation libraries . A keen eye for performance optimization and debugging. Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines for mobile app deployment. A user-first mindset, with a passion for delivering impactful features. Bonus Points If You Have: Experience with video and map integrations . Knowledge of analytics and A/B testing for mobile apps. Background in marketplace or booking system applications .
