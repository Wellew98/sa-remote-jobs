Project & Operations Coordinator Location: Remote (Latin America or South Africa) Compensation: - LATAM: USD $1,800-$2,000/month - South Africa: USD $1,600-$1,800/month Industry: Nonprofit Foundation | Sports & Community Impact Schedule: Full-Time | Monday-Friday | U.S. Business Hours About the Organization Our client is a mission-driven athlete foundation dedicated to creating meaningful impact through youth initiatives, educational programs, charitable campaigns, and community engagement. As the foundation continues to grow, they're looking for a highly organized Project & Operations Coordinator to help leadership stay focused by bringing structure, accountability, and visibility to day-to-day operations. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys creating order, keeping projects moving, and ensuring that important commitments never fall through the cracks. Role Overview The Project & Operations Coordinator will serve as the organizational backbone of the foundation, partnering closely with leadership to coordinate projects, maintain operational visibility, and keep priorities moving forward. Working primarily within Notion, you'll organize projects, track action items, monitor deadlines, document meetings, and proactively follow up with stakeholders to ensure work is completed on time. Rather than owning programs directly, you'll make sure every initiative has structure, accountability, and momentum. Success in this role comes from being proactive, detail-oriented, highly organized, and comfortable working independently in a fast-paced environment. Key Responsibilities Project Coordination - Maintain and organize projects, tasks, timelines, and priorities within Notion. - Monitor project progress and proactively follow up on outstanding action items. - Keep leadership informed of upcoming deadlines, risks, and operational bottlenecks. - Coordinate internal initiatives across multiple concurrent projects. - Ensure tasks remain visible, assigned, and progressing according to schedule. Executive & Leadership Support - Attend meetings, capture detailed notes, and convert discussions into actionable next steps. - Prepare agendas, maintain documentation, and organize meeting follow-ups. - Track commitments made by leadership and ensure timely execution. - Assist leadership with day-to-day operational priorities and administrative coordination. Operations & Process Management - Improve and maintain organized operational systems and documentation. - Build repeatable workflows that improve efficiency as the foundation grows. - Maintain accurate records and project documentation. - Identify opportunities to improve internal coordination and communication. Cross-Functional Coordination - Communicate regularly with internal stakeholders to keep projects aligned. - Coordinate schedules, meetings, and project deliverables. - Ensure information flows effectively across the organization. - Support the coordination of foundation initiatives, campaigns, and operational activities. Required Hard Skills - 2+ years of experience in Project Coordination, Operations, Executive Assistance, Account Coordination, Project Administration, or similar roles. - Strong project tracking and organizational skills. - Experience managing multiple concurrent priorities. - Proficiency with Notion or comparable project management platforms (Asana, ClickUp, Monday.com, Trello, etc.). - Excellent written and verbal English communication. - Strong proficiency with Google Workspace. - Comfortable creating documentation, meeting notes, and operational processes. Preferred Experience Candidates with experience in one or more of the following environments are highly desirable: - Nonprofit organizations - Executive support - Business operations - Project coordination - Agency or consulting environments - Community organizations - Startup operations - Remote-first organizations Soft Skills - Exceptional organizational skills. - Strong attention to detail. - Naturally proactive and self-directed. - Excellent follow-through and accountability. - Comfortable working independently. - Calm under pressure and adaptable to changing priorities. - Strong interpersonal and communication skills. - High degree of professionalism and discretion. About the Ideal Candidate You enjoy bringing order to busy environments. Rather than waiting for instructions, you naturally ask what needs to happen next, who owns it, and whether anything is falling behind. You're comfortable following up with stakeholders, keeping projects moving, and ensuring important commitments don't get lost. You take pride in being the person others rely on to keep operations organized, deadlines visible, and priorities moving forward. Most importantly, you're proactive, dependable, and genuinely enjoy helping leaders stay focused on the work that matters most. Why This Role Matters Growing organizations generate countless moving pieces, and leadership performs best when someone is ensuring those pieces stay organized. This role provides the operational structure that keeps projects moving, commitments visible, and priorities on track. By maintaining accountability across initiatives and ensuring consistent follow-through, you'll allow the foundation's leadership to spend less time managing tasks and more time advancing its mission. Salary Recommendation Rationale We recommend targeting: - South Africa: USD $1,500-$1,800/month - Latin America: USD $1,800-$2,000/month The role requires a high-agency operations professional with strong project coordination, organizational, and executive support capabilities rather than a senior nonprofit program manager. This compensation remains competitive across both regions while attracting experienced candidates capable of independently managing priorities, coordinating stakeholders, and providing exceptional operational support to foundation leadership. REQUIREMENTS: We are looking for candidates based in South Africa or Latin America.
