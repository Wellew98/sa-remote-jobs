RolePhysical Therapy & Fitness Sales Development Rep (SDR)

Priority Location :South Africa / Latin America / Philippines

Working Hours :  Monday - Friday 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM , Saturday 10am - 2pm

Salary RangeBase Salary + Commissions & Performance Bonuses

Type of contractIndependent Contractor

Type of jobRemote

About the Role: The client is seeking a personable, motivated Sales Development Representative to support lead conversion, outbound follow-up, and client booking efforts for a growing physical therapy and wellness practice.

This role is ideal for someone who is confident on the phone, strong in follow-up, and able to communicate value clearly to prospective client

Key Responsibilities

  • Effectively manage lead tracker and converting new leads into initial evaluations for the PTs
  • 5-Minute Speed-to-Lead : Contact new Meta/Google ad leads and website form inquiries via phone, SMS, and email within 5 minutes of submission.
  • Weekend Lead Monitoring : Check and respond promptly to high-urgency weekend inbound leads to ensure no prospect falls through the cracks.
  • Comfortable with high volume outbound calls and inbound calls for leads (150-200 outbound dials/day)
  • Scheduling appointments, initial evals, follow ups
  • Collecting new client intake information
  • Collect payments - deposits for initial evaluations
  • Build positive relationships with patients, community, and practitioners and manage conflict with care and empathy.
  • Lead clients through the sales process to meet new client conversion goals
  • Promote conversion by providing excellent onboarding and customer experience.
  • Answer phone calls, text messages, instagram DM, and instant messages in a fast, timely, and professional manner
  • Willingness to learn new technologies & frameworks
  • Manage patient feedback escalation process and ensure that conflicts are resolved and the patient feels heard.
  • Analyze new client metrics to optimize client communication and onboarding process with clinical director
  • Emotional Intelligence & Deep Discovery : Conduct intake calls that uncover a prospect’s true underlying motivation ( "the why behind the why" )—navigating emotional health challenges while remaining firmly in control of the sales conversation.
  • Value Differentiation & Objection Handling if any: Articulate the distinct value of our cash-based, one-on-one therapy model versus traditional insurance-based clinics, overcoming financial and logistical objections effortlessly.

Qualifications

  • Excellent phone and customer service skills
  • Strong sales background
  • Comfortably communicating in English
  • Great at multi-tasking
  • Strong computer skills (google suites, Office 365, project management tools, CRM system)
  • Organizational skills, Problem solving skills
  • 2+ years of experience in sales, appointment setting, lead follow-up, or customer-facing outreach
  • Strong verbal communication and phone presence
  • Comfortable working in a fast-paced environment with high follow-up volume
  • Ability to build trust quickly and explain services in a persuasive but professional way
  • Experience with CRM systems and call/VoIP tools preferred
  • Self-motivated, organized, and reliable
  • Ability to work during Eastern Time business hours

Ideal Candidate Qualifications

  • Experience handling objections and converting leads into booked appointments
  • Familiarity with Instagram DMs, email outreach, and light marketing follow-up
  • Fitness, boutique med sales background
  • Managerial and multi-tasking fast pace experiences
