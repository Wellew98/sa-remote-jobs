About Stranger Soccer

Stranger Soccer (www.strangersoccer.com) is a Singapore-based company experiencing explosive growth. In a few short years, after launching a revolutionary platform and Mobile App for on-demand football, the company has amassed over 50,000 registered users playing hundreds of games a week. It has solved the problem that football is the world's #1 sport, but is difficult for an individual to play. Stranger Soccer makes it as easy as going to the gym or for a jog. Browse. Book a slot. Show up and play. Today, it is the #1 way people play football and futsal in Singapore, and is rapidly expanding internationally via a license partner model, with locations in Australia, India, USA, UAE, and South Africa.

Role

The selected candidate will work closely with the UAE Business Owner and a small operations team to manage and grow our social media presence, drive organic search performance, and support key marketing functions. We are looking for an experienced content, social media, and SEO operator.

Responsibilities

Video editing (short-form reels, match highlights, promo content) using CapCut Pro / Descript

Graphic design (game announcements, promos, sponsor content) via Canva + AI tools

Copywritingpost captions, community messages (AI-assisted via Claude/ChatGPT)

Content scheduling across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook (Buffer / Later)

Community managementDMs, comments, brand voice consistency

Monthly/weekly content calendar planning aligned with our game schedule

Coordinating with our UAE-based videographer for match-day capture assets

Google SEO ownership: keyword research, on-page optimisation, and long-form blog content aimed at ranking Stranger Soccer UAE #1 for high-intent search terms (e.g., "pickup football Dubai," "5-a-side Abu Dhabi," "football pitches near me UAE"). Directly own our organic search performance against key competitors

Content-for-SEO output2-4 SEO-optimised blog articles per month on our website targeting UAE-specific football search intent

Google Business Profile and local SEO maintenance across our venue locations

3+ years in social media / content role

2+ years hands-on SEO experience — keyword research, on-page optimisation, content strategy, proven track record ranking content on Google

Working knowledge of SEO tools (Ahrefs, SEMrush, Google Search Console, Google Analytics)

Proven portfolio of short-form video and design output

Fluent English

Ability to work independently and must be available for weekly sync at UAE morning time

Hands-on experience with CapCut Pro or Descript for video editing, Canva and AI tools for graphic design, and Claude or ChatGPT for AI-assisted copywriting

Experience using Buffer or Later to schedule content across Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook

Experience managing online communities and coordinating with external creatives such as videographers

Knowledge of the UAE market is a plus.
