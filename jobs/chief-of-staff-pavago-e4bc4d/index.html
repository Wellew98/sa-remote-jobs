About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a Chief of Staff to serve as the strategic partner to the CEO and executive leadership team.

This is a high-impact leadership role focused on driving company strategy, operational execution, and cross-functional alignment.

You won’t simply coordinate projects—you’ll help shape company priorities, ensure strategic initiatives move forward, improve operational efficiency, and provide executive-level support that enables leadership to scale effectively.

You’ll work closely with the CEO, department leaders, and key stakeholders to translate strategy into execution while building systems that improve accountability, communication, and business performance.

If you’re an exceptional operator who thrives in fast-paced environments, enjoys solving complex business challenges, and excels at leading through influence, this role is a strong fit.

Strategic Planning & Execution

Partner with the CEO to define and execute company strategy.

Drive accountability across strategic initiatives and cross-functional projects.

Identify execution risks and proactively develop solutions.

Lead special projects and company-wide initiatives from planning through completion.

Executive Partnership

Support the CEO with strategic planning, prioritization, and decision-making.

Prepare executive briefings, reports, presentations, and leadership communications.

Assist with board meetings, investor updates, and executive reporting.

Align departments around company priorities and strategic objectives.

Facilitate communication and collaboration across teams.

Remove operational bottlenecks and improve execution across the organization.

Build strong relationships with internal and external stakeholders.

Operations & Business Performance

Improve internal systems, workflows, and operating procedures.

Support quarterly planning, annual planning, and business reviews.

Develop executive dashboards and performance reports.

Track KPIs, business metrics, and organizational performance.

Recommend operational improvements that increase efficiency, accountability, and scalability.

Communication & Organizational Alignment

Draft executive communications and company-wide updates.

Ensure consistent messaging across leadership and departments.

Reinforce company culture, values, and organizational alignment.

What Happens After You Apply

After submitting your application, you’ll receive an email invitation from Spark Hire to record a short 3–5 minute Intro Video . This is the first step in our hiring process and can be completed whenever it’s convenient for you.

Instead of multiple initial screening calls, you’ll have one opportunity to introduce yourself, discuss your experience supporting executive leadership, driving strategic initiatives, leading cross-functional projects, improving operations, and partnering with CEOs or founders. You’ll also have the opportunity to share how you’ve used AI tools to improve productivity, decision-making, or business execution. Your video will be reviewed by the hiring team before moving to the client interview.

You can record your video as many times as you’d like before submitting it—only your final version will be reviewed.

Please keep an eye on both your inbox and spam folder for your Spark Hire invitation after applying.
