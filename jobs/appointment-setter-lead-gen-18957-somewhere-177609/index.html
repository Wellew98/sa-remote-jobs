Appointment Setter / Lead Generation

RoleAppointment Setter / Outbound Lead Generation Specialist

IndustrySecurity Services (B2B)

LocationRemote (LATAM / South Africa / Philippines)

LanguageEnglish (Fluent – Strong Verbal & Written Required)

About the Role

We are hiring a high-output Appointment Setter / Lead Generation Specialist to support outbound business development for a growing security services company .

This role is focused on hunting for new business through outbound campaigns, while also maintaining light touchpoints with existing customers.

You will be responsible for filling the sales pipeline by identifying prospects, reaching out via email (and occasionally phone), and booking qualified meetings for the sales team.

Core Responsibilities

Outbound Lead Generation (Primary Focus)

  • Execute cold outbound email campaigns
  • Identify and research prospects using tools like:
  • ZoomInfo
  • ALN
  • CRM databases
  • Build targeted prospect lists across multiple markets
  • Personalize outreach to increase response rates
  • Consistently generate qualified meetings for the sales team

Appointment Setting

  • Engage with prospects via email and occasional calls
  • Qualify leads based on basic criteria (fit, interest, timing)
  • Schedule meetings for sales reps / leadership
  • Ensure smooth handoff of leads to closers

Customer Touchpoints (Secondary)

  • Maintain light communication with existing customers
  • Send follow-ups, check-ins, and relationship touchpoints
  • Help ensure continued engagement and retention

CRM & Reporting

  • Track outreach activity and performance in CRM
  • Maintain clean, organized lead data
  • Report on:
  • Emails sent
  • Responses
  • Meetings booked
  • Conversion rates

Ideal Candidate Profile

Must-Haves

  • 1–3+ years experience in:
  • Appointment setting
  • Lead generation
  • SDR / BDR roles
  • Strong written English (this role is email-heavy )
  • Comfortable with outbound / cold outreach
  • Familiar with tools like:
  • CRMs (HubSpot, Salesforce, etc.)
  • Prospecting tools (ZoomInfo, Apollo, etc.)
  • Highly organized and self-motivated

Strong Pluses

  • Experience in B2B services or security industry
  • Experience running outbound campaigns independently
  • Comfortable hopping on calls when needed
  • Experience supporting U.S.-based teams

Success Metrics (First 60–90 Days)

  • Consistent outbound activity (emails + prospecting)
  • Steady pipeline of qualified meetings booked weekly
  • Clean CRM tracking and reporting
  • Positive response rates and engagement
