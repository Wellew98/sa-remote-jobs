Rockstar is recruiting for a global brand that provides essential business support services. The client is a dynamic, fast-growing company focused on delivering high-quality solutions through a remote-first, outcome-driven culture. They are seeking a skilled professional to join their team and help shape their digital foundation.

In this pivotal role, the Website Manager will shape the digital foundation of a fast-growing global brand. As the Website Manager, the individual will own the day-to-day operations, governance, and performance of the HubSpot-hosted website. By partnering with senior leaders, design teams, and external agencies, the successful candidate will ensure the website remains a high-performing, conversion-ready platform that reflects the brand.

About the Role

The client is seeking a Website Manager to own the end-to-end operation of the website, ensuring updates are executed efficiently, quality standards are upheld, and the site remains accurate, scalable, and conversion-ready. This role acts as the central point of coordination across content, design, development, SEO, growth, analytics, and QA.

This is not a design or development role. It is an operational leadership role focused on execution, governance, and performance.

Key Responsibilities

Website Operations & Governance

  • Own the full website maintenance lifecycle, from request intake through deployment
  • Manage prioritization, SLAs, and scheduling for website updates (urgent vs. routine vs. strategic)
  • Enforce website governance standards, including templates, modules, permissions, and publishing rules
  • Serve as the primary point of contact for all website-related requests across Marketing, Sales, and Leadership
  • Manage user access, permissions, and CMS training

Cross-Functional Coordination

  • Coordinate work across Content, Design, Development, SEO, Growth/CRO, Analytics, and QA
  • Ensure clear handoffs, timelines, and accountability at each stage of execution
  • Identify and resolve blockers, dependencies, and resourcing issues
  • Facilitate alignment between stakeholders on scope, priority, timing, and expectations

Release Management & QA Oversight

  • Oversee staging, QA, and production releases
  • Support CRO initiatives including A/B testing, personalization, and experiment QA
  • Ensure all changes pass required checks:
  • Content QA
  • Visual QA
  • Technical QA
  • Accessibility compliance
  • Own post-publish validation and coordinate rollbacks or hotfixes when needed
  • Maintain a consistent release cadence and deployment process

CMS & Technical Stewardship (HubSpot)

  • Maintain a clean, scalable HubSpot CMS environment, including:
  • Page templates
  • Modules
  • HubDB usage
  • Permissions and publishing workflows
  • Ensure technical best practices are followed for performance, responsiveness, and maintainability
  • Partner with the cybersecurity team to ensure website security, compliance (cookie consent, GDPR), and access hygiene
  • Partner with developers on upgrades, fixes, and CMS improvements

Content & Asset Updates

  • Implement content and copy updates in partnership with the Content team and subject matter experts
  • Coordinate visual asset updates with the Design team
  • Monitor pages for outdated content or messaging and trigger refresh cycles as needed
  • Maintain long-term website architecture and scalability across services and verticals

SEO Oversight

  • Oversee the implementation of SEO updates, with support from an external agency
  • Partner with the SEO Specialist and web developer on audits and recommendations
  • Ensure site updates do not negatively impact organic search performance

Vendor Management

  • Serve as the primary point of contact for website-related vendors, including external SEO and CRO agencies
  • Coordinate vendor requests by routing action items to the appropriate internal teams and owners
  • Ensure alignment on scope, priorities, and timelines, and track vendor-driven work through completion

Documentation & Continuous Improvement

  • Maintain website documentation, including:
  • Website changelog
  • SOPs for updates and QA
  • Page inventory and ownership
  • Lead retrospectives on major launches or incidents
  • Continuously improve workflows, tools, and standards to increase speed and reliability

Required Qualifications

  • 3–5+ years of experience managing websites in a marketing or growth environment
  • Hands-on experience with HubSpot CMS (or comparable CMS platforms)
  • Strong understanding of:
  • Website governance and QA processes
  • Cross-functional project coordination
  • SEO and CRO fundamentals
  • Proven ability to manage multiple concurrent requests and priorities
  • Experience coordinating with distributed or remote teams
  • Excellent organizational, communication, and stakeholder management skills

Work Conditions

LocationRemote (South Africa, India, or Philippines), with availability for U.S. Pacific Standard Time collaboration as needed

EmploymentFull-time role with competitive salary and benefits

Why Join?

In this role, the candidate will shape the digital foundation of a fast-growing global brand. The individual will:

  • Build structure where it matters by shaping scalable website operations, governance, and workflows
  • Work in a remote-first, outcome-focused culture that values ownership, clarity, and results
  • Collaborate with global teams, partnering with senior leaders and creative teams to align technical execution with strategic marketing goals
