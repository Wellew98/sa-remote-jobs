Job TitleDigital Marketing Content Producer (Short-Form Video & Social Media)

LocationRemote

Time ZoneUS Time Zones (EST–PST)

Role Overview

We are seeking a Digital Marketing Content Producer to lead the creation of short-form, social-first video content that supports brand awareness, engagement, and digital marketing campaigns. This role focuses on producing high-quality, platform-optimized content aligned with marketing objectives and performance goals.

Key Responsibilities

  • Produce short-form video content for platforms such as TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and LinkedIn
  • Own the end-to-end production process including ideation, scripting, filming, editing, and publishing
  • Translate marketing campaigns and messaging into engaging video content
  • Collaborate with Marketing and Social teams on content planning and launches
  • Adapt content for different platforms while maintaining brand consistency
  • Monitor content performance and optimize based on engagement metrics and trends

Required Qualifications

  • Proven hands-on experience producing short-form video content for digital marketing or social media
  • Strong video editing and storytelling skills tailored to social platforms
  • Prior experience working remotely is mandatory, including fluency with remote collaboration tools such as Slack, Zoom, Google Workspace, Asana (or similar), and experience working with US or UK-based companies; applications without this experience will not be considered
  • Solid understanding of social media platforms and digital marketing fundamentals
  • Ability to manage multiple content deliverables independently

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience creating performance-driven or campaign-based video content
  • Familiarity with basic motion graphics, captions, or animated text
  • Experience working with digital-first or growth-focused brands

Tools & Technology

  • Video Editing: CapCut, Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, or similar
  • Design: Canva, Adobe Creative Suite
  • Social & Scheduling: Hootsuite, Buffer, Sprout, or native tools
  • Collaboration: Slack, Zoom, Google Workspace
