Read the posting in full · 422 words

Are you a dedicated Maths and English teacher looking for a rewarding opportunity to inspire the next generation? We're looking for energetic, patient, and passionate educators ready to make a meaningful impact on young learners from the comfort of their own home.

Position Details

Subjects

Mathematics and Conversational English & Exam Preparation

Students

Work with motivated young learners aged 6–18 years, based in Ukraine, in small group settings

Class Size

5–8 students for Maths | 7–12 students for English

Class Duration

55-minute sessions

Teaching Platform

Zoom

Schedule

Maths

Courses run 2 days per week (Mon & Wed or Tue & Thu) for 34 lessons per course. Classes are scheduled between 16:00–20:00 GMT+3 / 15:00–19:00 SAST. Teachers may take on more than one course. (Note: Schedule may shift to Mon–Sun from September 2026)

English

5 days per week, including one weekend day.

Rate

$7.00 USD per hour (Maths) | $6.50 USD per hour (English)

Contract Type

Independent Service Provider with The Really Great Teacher Company — enjoy the flexibility of working remotely from home.

Key Responsibilities

Deliver engaging, age-appropriate Maths and English lessons aligned with the Cambridge curriculum

Teach conversational English and support students with exam preparation

Foster a positive, patient, and energetic classroom environment

Provide constructive feedback to support each student's progress

Use existing lesson materials effectively

National Diploma in any field (minimum)

120-hour TEFL or equivalent

Maths

Minimum 6–12 months of Maths teaching or tutoring experience; knowledge of the Cambridge curriculum is essential

English

Minimum 2 years of English teaching experience

Patient, friendly, and energetic — you'll be working with children and teenagers

Strong communication, adaptability, time management, and problem-solving skills

Willingness to teach both Maths and English is a strong advantage

Technical Requirements

PC specifications

Intel® Core™ i5 CPU @2.0GHz (4 CPUs) or greater, 8GB RAM or greater

Backup power

Uninterrupted Power Supply (UPS) with a battery life of 4-5 hours (2000VA)

Internet connection

Stable fiber connection, minimum 20 Mbps upload and download speeds

Accessories

Noise-cancelling headsets, HD web camera, and a tidy, well-lit, and quiet teaching environment

For more information, view our online ESL teacher's tech checklist .

Why Join Us?

Teach motivated, curious young minds in a structured small-group environment

Flexible scheduling that fits around your life

Supportive team and clear onboarding process

Opportunity to take on multiple courses and grow your teaching hours

Be part of a global community of educators making a real difference

If you are enthusiastic about shaping young minds and helping students excel in Maths and Science, we’d love to hear from you!
