About Huzzle

At Huzzle, we connect exceptional talent with top opportunities at leading companies across the UK, US, Canada, Europe, and Australia. Our clients include fast-growing startups and established businesses in SaaS, MarTech, FinTech, and EdTech.

Unlike traditional outsourcing, you’ll be placed directly with a client as a core member of their team—giving you real ownership, career progression, and long-term stability.

Role TypeFull-time

EngagementIndependent Contractor

Location: On-site (Lisbon, Portugal; Stellenbosch, South Africa; Cairo, Egypt; Buenos Aires, Argentina).

About the Company

Our client is a fast-growing global financial technology company operating within the payments industry. The business provides payment processing, local payment methods, merchant services, recurring billing solutions, fraud prevention technology, and cross-border payment infrastructure to businesses worldwide.

Serving clients across SaaS, e-commerce, gaming, travel, and enterprise sectors, the company supports merchants in over 200 regions globally and continues to expand its international commercial team.

Job Summary

We are seeking a highly motivated Senior Sales Development Representative (SDR) with proven experience in fintech, payments, financial services, payment gateways, merchant services, or related industries.

This is an excellent opportunity for a polished and commercially driven sales professional who thrives in outbound prospecting, lead generation, and enterprise relationship building. You will work closely with Business Development Managers to identify opportunities, engage merchants and decision-makers, and contribute to the growth of a leading global payments organization.

This is a fully onsite role and requires international travel throughout the year for conferences, trade shows, and industry events.

Key Responsibilities

Research target accounts, merchants, partners, and key decision-makers across priority markets.

Build, manage, and maintain a pipeline of qualified prospects through proactive outbound prospecting.

Generate new business opportunities through cold calling, email outreach, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, networking, and other sales channels.

Conduct discovery conversations to identify merchant needs, payment challenges, and business opportunities.

Qualify leads and schedule meetings for Business Development Managers and senior sales stakeholders.

Support the sales process by assisting with presentations, proposals, service agreements, and merchant onboarding activities.

Develop and maintain strong relationships with prospective clients, merchants, and strategic partners.

Accurately manage prospect data, activities, and pipeline stages within Salesforce and other CRM platforms.

Track prospect engagement, sales activities, and opportunity progression to ensure accurate forecasting and reporting.

Collaborate closely with Business Development Managers and cross-functional teams to drive revenue growth.

Represent the company at international fintech conferences, trade shows, networking events, and industry gatherings.

Generate new merchant and partnership opportunities through conference attendance and relationship-building activities.

Maintain a strong understanding of payment processing, payment gateways, merchant services, fintech solutions, and industry trends.

Consistently achieve and exceed outreach, pipeline generation, and sales performance targets.

Deliver a professional, consultative, and solution-oriented sales experience throughout the buyer journey.

1–3 years of experience in B2B sales, business development, account development, or Sales Development Representative (SDR) roles.

Proven experience within FinTech, payments, financial services, payment gateways, merchant services, Payment Service Providers (PSPs), banking technology, or SaaS companies with strong financial exposure.

Demonstrated success in outbound prospecting, lead generation, lead qualification, and pipeline development.

Experience engaging with merchants, business owners, decision-makers, and enterprise stakeholders.

Strong understanding of payment processing, merchant acquiring, payment infrastructure, digital payments, or related financial technology solutions.

Excellent verbal and written English communication skills with strong presentation and relationship-building abilities.

Experience working with CRM platforms such as Salesforce and sales engagement tools such as LinkedIn Sales Navigator and Outreach is preferred.

Proven track record of working toward sales targets, KPIs, and performance metrics.

Comfortable working in a fast-paced, high-growth, target-oriented environment.

Stable employment history with demonstrated career progression preferred.

Must be based in, or willing to relocate to, the assigned hiring location and work onsite full-time (Lisbon, Portugal; Stellenbosch, South Africa; Cairo, Egypt; Buenos Aires, Argentina).

Must be willing and able to travel internationally approximately 5–6 times per year for conferences, trade shows, and industry events.

Bachelor's Degree required; Master's Degree highly advantageous.
