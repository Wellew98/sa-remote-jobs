Genesis Analytics is an Africa-headquartered impact firm that advises governments, businesses, and development organizations on economic, social, financial, and technological challenges across Africa and other young-population regions. Working at the intersection of strategy, policy, and implementation, the firm helps clients tackle complex issues that shape inclusive growth and long-term development. In management consulting, its work spans high-impact engagements that connect deep sector knowledge with practical execution.

This role sits at the center of Genesis Analytics' work in

Payments and digital finance

, with a focus on helping clients navigate transformation across African markets. As Head of Payments & Digital Financial Services, you will shape the direction of a strategic service line, guide senior client engagements, and help expand the firm's influence across the payments ecosystem. The role brings together thought leadership, commercial leadership, and delivery oversight in support of work with public- and private-sector stakeholders.

Responsibilities

Lead the Payments and Digital Financial Services service line across Africa.

Set strategy and oversee delivery of complex payments transformation engagements.

Build partnerships with regulators, financial institutions, fintechs, and development organizations.

Drive business development, client relationships, proposals, and service-line revenue growth.

Higher degree in economics, finance, engineering, or a related field.

10+ years of leadership experience in payments, digital finance, or financial inclusion consulting.

Proven success growing consulting revenue, managing senior client relationships, and leading multidisciplinary teams through complex transformation programs.

Deep knowledge of African payment ecosystems, including regulation, infrastructure, and market dynamics.

Hands-on experience with payment rails and switching infrastructure, digital wallet platforms, core banking integrations, or regulatory and market-infrastructure systems.

Strong relationships with regulators, banks, fintechs, donors, and development organizations.

Fluency in English for cross-market consulting work; fluency in French or Portuguese for work across Francophone or Lusophone markets is a plus.
