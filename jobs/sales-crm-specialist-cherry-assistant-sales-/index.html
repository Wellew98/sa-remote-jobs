We are hiring a Sales & CRM Specialist for a fast-growing virtual assistant staffing agency in the remote staffing and recruitment industry. This is a full-time remote role for a US-based company. This role is ideal for someone who is a confident, high-energy closer with strong organizational habits, capable of owning inbound consultation calls and executing disciplined, multi-week CRM follow-ups to turn prospects into long-term clients.

Key Responsibilities

  • Conduct consultative, high-energy inbound discovery and sales calls with prospective clients across general industries and home service businesses.
  • Confidently guide prospects through our placement process, handle objections, and secure initial deposits.
  • Own the full post-consult follow-up pipeline, ensuring zero leads fall through the cracks over multi-week or multi-month decision windows.
  • Maintain strict CRM hygiene by updating pipeline stages, logging call notes, and tracking lead statuses accurately in GoHighLevel (GHL).
  • Utilize internal AI and bot tools to prioritize daily follow-up lists and focus outreach on the highest-converting prospects.
  • Execute a seamless onboarding handoff to the recruitment and client success teams.

Required Qualifications

  • 2+ years proven track record in B2B sales, high-ticket closing, or recruitment/staffing account management.
  • Exceptional communication skills with the confidence and presence to lead video sales calls without over-talking.
  • Strong experience managing sales pipelines and executing disciplined, persistent follow-up sequences.
  • High attention to detail and strong organizational skills for maintaining clean CRM records.
  • Self-motivated and target-driven, taking full ownership of personal close rates and pipeline health.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Prior experience working in an agency, staffing firm, or remote-first B2B service business.
  • Familiarity selling to US and UK small-to-medium business owners
  • Experience using AI tools to streamline daily workflows, lead prioritization, and outreach.

Required Skills & Tools

  • GoHighLevel (GHL) or comparable CRM platforms
  • Video conferencing software (Zoom / Google Meet)
  • Payment and subscription link workflows (e.g., Stripe)
  • AI prompting/assistant tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Grok)
  • Google Workspace (Docs, Sheets, Gmail, Calendar) & Slack/Discord

Schedule & Pay

  • Full-time position; US business hours in the PST time zone
  • This is a fully remote job for a US-based company
  • Pay ranges from R14,500–R18,500 , depending on experience and skill, plus an uncapped, flat-fee commission for every confirmed deposit that converts into an active subscription.
  • Annual raises, bonuses, confirmed holidays PTO

System Requirements

  • Internet speed of at least 20 Mbps
  • Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher
  • 8 GB of RAM or higher
  • Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer
  • HD 720p webcam
  • Headset with a microphone

Benefits

  • Competitive pay rates
  • The company provides a US phone number and business email address
  • Enjoy the flexibility of working remotely, from home or any location of your choice
  • Eliminating commute time
  • Consistent work with the same clients, fostering long-term professional relationships
  • Opportunities for career advancement, dependent on the client
  • Stable work hours and consistent pay
  • A supportive and inclusive work environment that values diversity and individual growth

If you thrive in a dedicated closing seat, love turning warm conversations into long-term partnerships, and take pride in running a spotless CRM pipeline, we want to hear from you.
