Overview

We are hiring a Sales & Operations Coordinator for a founder-led residential cleaning company in the home services industry. This is a Full-Time remote role for a New York, USA based client. The role is focused on sales-first lead handling, rapid lead follow-up, booking and closing cleanings, scheduling, cleaner coordination, customer service, and day-to-day operations support. The ideal candidate is highly responsive, sales-driven, personable, organized, process-oriented, coachable, tech-savvy, and proactive, with strong conversational English and a clear, professional phone presence.

Key Responsibilities

  • Respond quickly to inbound calls, texts, emails, and web leads; deliver fast, professional responses (speed-to-lead within 5–10 minutes)
  • Qualify prospects, provide quotes, overcome objections, and convert inquiries into booked cleanings
  • Own the CRM pipeline (GoHighLevel preferred): log activities, update stages, set tasks, and maintain clean data and accurate records
  • Schedule jobs, assign cleaners, coordinate routes, and manage calendar changes, reschedules, and same-day updates
  • Send confirmations, reminders, and follow-ups; rebook recurring services and drive review requests
  • Monitor and action leads from platforms such as Google LSA and Angi; track source and conversion metrics
  • Conduct outbound follow-ups on warm leads; maintain target close rates and responsiveness SLAs
  • Handle customer service issues (service complaints, adjustments, refunds) with empathy and a resolution-focused approach
  • Collaborate daily with the founders; provide clear status updates and recommend process improvements
  • Track KPIs (lead response time, close rate, show rate, repeat bookings, reviews) and prepare daily/weekly reports
  • Create and maintain SOPs, call scripts, templates; suggest improvements to processes
  • Escalate complex issues promptly and deliver exceptional customer experience at every touchpoint
  • Learn and adapt quickly to new CRM tools and business processes

Required Qualifications

  • 2+ years experience in phone-based sales or customer support within home services (cleaning preferred)
  • 2+ years experience scheduling appointments, coordinating contractors/ service teams and managing day-to-day operations
  • 2+ years hands-on experience managing a sales or service pipeline in a CRM (GoHighLevel preferred or similar)
  • 2+ years scheduling/ field-service software experience (e.g., BookingKoala, Housecall Pro, Jobber)
  • Proven ability to convert leads by phone/text with excellent, friendly spoken English and a neutral accent
  • Strong organizational skills; able to juggle multiple leads, schedules, and follow-ups with high accuracy
  • Confident problem solver with customer-first mindset and clear, concise written communication
  • Comfortable working US business hours (Eastern Time) in a fully remote environment

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in residential cleaning or related home services (e.g., HVAC, plumbing, landscaping)
  • GoHighLevel automation/optimization experience (pipelines, workflows, triggers, templates, automations, nurture sequences)
  • Familiarity with Google Local Services Ads (LSA), Angi/Angie’s List, or similar lead platforms

Required Skills & Tools

  • GoHighLevel or comparable CRM (e.g., HubSpot, Pipedrive)
  • BookingKoala, Housecall Pro, Jobber, or similar scheduling/field-service software
  • Quo (OpenPhone) or similar VoIP phone systems for inbound/outbound calls and texting
  • Google Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets)

Schedule & Pay

  • Full-Time position; 9:00 AM–5:00 PM US Eastern Time (Monday–Friday; some flexibility based on call volume)
  • Fully remote role for a New York, USA based client
  • Pay ranges from R14,000 - R22,000 per month depending on experience and skill
  • Includes training, growth potential, and the opportunity to optimize tools and processes

System Requirements

  • Internet speed of at least 20 Mbps upload and download
  • Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher
  • 8 GB of RAM or higher
  • Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer
  • HD 720p webcam
  • Headset with microphone

Benefits

  • Competitive pay rates
  • Consistent hours and predictable workload
  • Fully remote work with long-term potential
  • Direct collaboration with founders, making a real impact
  • Supportive and inclusive work environment
  • Opportunity to grow alongside a fast-growing residential cleaning company

If you are a highly responsive, personable, sales-driven professional who thrives in a fast-paced environment, communicates confidently with customers, and can take ownership of both sales and day-to-day operations, we’d love to hear from you.
