Job TitleContent Creator (Social Media & Video)

LocationRemote

Time ZoneUS Time Zones (EST–PST)

Role Overview

We are hiring a Content Creator to produce engaging, creative video and social media content designed to connect with audiences and build brand presence. This role is highly hands-on and creator-led, with a strong emphasis on originality, trends, and audience engagement.

Key Responsibilities

  • Create engaging short-form video and social content for social platforms
  • Ideate and execute content aligned with brand voice and social trends
  • Film, edit, and publish content in a consistent and timely manner
  • Adapt creative concepts for different platforms and formats
  • Collaborate with Marketing and Social teams on content themes and campaigns
  • Monitor engagement and iterate on content ideas based on performance

Required Qualifications

  • Demonstrated experience creating social media and video content for brands or businesses
  • Strong creative instincts and understanding of social media trends
  • Prior experience working remotely is mandatory, including fluency with remote collaboration tools such as Slack, Zoom, Google Workspace, Asana (or similar), and experience working with US or UK-based companies; applications without this experience will not be considered
  • Comfortable managing content creation independently from concept to publish
  • Strong attention to detail and brand alignment

Preferred Qualifications

  • Comfort appearing on camera or directing on-camera talent
  • Experience creating UGC-style or creator-led content
  • Familiarity with social analytics and engagement metrics

Tools & Technology

  • Video Editing: CapCut, InShot, Adobe Premiere Pro, or similar
  • Design: Canva
  • Social Platforms: TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn
  • Collaboration: Slack, Zoom, Google Workspace

Please NOTE

It is crucial that you complete the application form in full. As part of the application process, you will be required to record a video. If your application is successful, you will receive an email confirming next steps, the video is the first step of the interview process. If you do not record a video, we will not be able to consider you for ANY open roles.
