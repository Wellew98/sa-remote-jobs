About the Company

The client is a wholesale importer and exporter of ingredients. The business operates with a lean but established team, including existing VA support across specialised functions. This role reports directly to the company owner and supports him personally with day-to-day operations, organisation, and communication management.

About the Role

We’re looking for a reliable, organised, and tech-savvy executive and admin assistant to work closely with the owner. This is a part-time starting role (20 hours/week) with the expectation of growing to full-time as trust is established. You’ll be the person who keeps the small things from slipping through the cracks — freeing the owner to stay focused on higher-level priorities.

Key Responsibilities

From day one

  • Perform daily/weekly checks to confirm automations and data imports are running correctly; log and flag any issues
  • Apply routine website content updates (copy-paste level; custom PHP/HTML backend — no coding required)
  • Check and maintain marketing material stock levels across warehouse and office locations via phone/email
  • Serve as a communication liaison — checking in with internal team members on behalf of the owner and relaying anything that needs his attention
  • Handle general admin tasks as assigned (Google Workspace — Docs, Sheets, Gmail)

As trust develops

  • Manage the general company inbox (info@) — sorting, forwarding to relevant departments, and sending holding/clarification replies where needed
  • Act as a first filter for inbound inquiries, distinguishing buy-side vs sell-side contacts and routing accordingly
  • Support calendar management for the owner

What We’re Looking For

  • Strong attention to detail — this role is all about consistency and follow-through
  • Comfortable working independently with minimal hand-holding
  • Tech-savvy; comfortable with Google Workspace and confident learning new tools
  • Basic familiarity with HTML is a plus (not required, but handy)
  • Good written English for professional email correspondence
  • Reliable availability during EST business hours (approx. 9am start)
  • Long-term mindset — the owner values loyalty and stability over short engagements

Nice to Have

  • Experience supporting a senior executive or business owner
  • Familiarity with AI tools or automation workflows (Zapier, Make, etc.)
  • Prior experience in e-commerce, wholesale, or import/export environments
