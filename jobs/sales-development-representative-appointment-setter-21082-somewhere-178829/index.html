Sales Development Representative (Appointment Setter)

Remote — Commercial Pavement & Property Services

Location: Fully remote (preferred for U.S. time-zone alignment) Employment Type: Full-time IC  Working Hours: U.S. Eastern business hours Reports To: Owner / Founder Compensation: [Base + commission on booked estimates / closed work — to be finalized]

About the Company

We are a rapidly growing U.S.-based commercial pavement and property maintenance company backed by a national franchise brand with a strong reputation for quality and communication. We help property managers, facility managers, HOAs, and commercial, retail, and industrial clients protect and improve their properties through services like parking lot striping, sealcoating, asphalt and concrete repair, warehouse floor markings, and epoxy coatings.

We operate in a fast-moving region of the Southeast and are actively expanding into new markets. Our clients count on us for dependable work and clear, responsive service — and we hold ourselves to that same standard internally.

About the Role

This role exists to solve a specific problem: our owner is currently the driving force behind sales, and that limits how fast we can grow. We need a dedicated Sales Development Representative to build a steady flow of qualified estimate opportunities so the owner can focus on what he does best — closing deals and running the business.

You are the tip of the spear. Working from a structured lead list and a mix of inbound inquiries, your job is to turn cold and warm prospects into interested, open conversations and to book estimates. You are not expected to close. You are expected to hand off "layups" — prospects who are warmed up, qualified, and ready to talk. The owner takes it from there.

If you like the chase, live comfortably on the phone, and take pride in a full calendar of booked appointments, this is a role where your work is directly visible and directly rewarded.

Key Responsibilities

Outbound Prospecting & Lead Generation

  • Work through a structured lead list to reach property managers, facility managers, and commercial decision-makers by phone, email, and LinkedIn.
  • Open conversations, build quick rapport with practical, non-technical buyers, and identify real maintenance needs.
  • Reactivate dormant leads and follow up on past quotes that never closed.

Inbound Lead Response & Qualification

  • Respond quickly to inbound inquiries and web leads while interest is high.
  • Qualify prospects for fit, timing, and scope before advancing them.

Appointment Setting & Estimate Coordination

  • Book qualified estimate appointments and site visits for the owner.
  • Coordinate scheduling so the owner walks into every conversation already warm.
  • Confirm appointments and reduce no-shows through timely follow-up.

Pipeline & CRM Management

  • Log all activity, notes, and next steps in the CRM with discipline.
  • Maintain clean, accurate records and organized follow-up cadences.
  • Keep the pipeline moving so no lead goes cold from neglect.

Collaboration & Reporting

  • Hand off warm, well-documented prospects to the owner with the context he needs to close.
  • Share what you're hearing in the market and report on activity and booked-appointment metrics.

Required Qualifications

  • 2+ years of outbound B2B sales, appointment setting, or SDR experience, ideally cold-calling U.S. businesses.
  • Excellent spoken and written English with a clear, neutral accent that lands well on the phone with U.S. buyers.
  • Comfort with high-volume phone outreach and handling objections without losing momentum.
  • Experience working in a CRM to manage a pipeline and follow-up.
  • Reliable availability during U.S. Eastern business hours, a quiet home-office setup, and stable high-speed internet.
  • Self-directed and dependable in a lean environment without heavy oversight.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in home services, construction, facilities, property management, real estate, or commercial services.
  • Track record of booking estimates or site visits for a field-services business.
  • Familiarity with tools such as HubSpot, Jobber, ServiceTitan, power dialers, or LinkedIn Sales Navigator.

Ideal Candidate Profile

You are

  • Highly organized and diligent about follow-up — nothing falls through the cracks.
  • Proactive and self-motivated, comfortable owning your day without someone directing every step.
  • Resilient on the phone — brush-offs don't rattle you, and you keep dialing.
  • Personable and grounded, able to talk plainly with busy, practical buyers.
  • Coachable and eager to learn directly from an owner who will invest in you.
  • Results-driven and energized by clear targets and a full appointment calendar.

Why Join Us

  • A ground-floor sales role in a growing company where your impact is immediate and visible.
  • Direct access to and mentorship from the owner.
  • A structured lead list and tools already in place — you focus on conversations, not building the machine from scratch.
  • Clear metrics and real reward for the appointments you book and the deals they turn into.
  • Room to grow as the sales function expands.
