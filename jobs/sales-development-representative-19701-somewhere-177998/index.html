SALES DEVELOPMENT REPRESENTATIVE  ABOUT THE ROLE  We are seeking a contract Sales Development Representative (SDR) for a fully remote position on behalf of our client. They are a 3-year-old SaaS platform in the contractor collections space, currently scaling their growth by transitioning from inbound referrals to outbound sales. DAILY GOALS & KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS  • Making 120+ Cold Calls A Day (600+ Per Week)  • 40+ Conversations Decision-Makers Per Day (200+ Per Week)  • 30 live conversations per day (continue calling until reached)  • 3+ hours of talk time  • 1+ Qualified Demo Show Up a Day (5+ Per Week)  THREE THINGS YOU’LL NEED TO SUCCEED  • Self-driven, hustler personality type (you don’t need anyone to motivate you) • Incredible self-confidence (you have zero doubt you’ll hit quota)  • Impeccable time management (you can already see yourself doing more activities in a day)  WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR  • You must have prior SaaS sales experience (you will not be considered for this role unless you meet this criteria)  • High cold calling activity per day experience (over 120 calls in a day). 90-Day Ramp-Up Quota  All new SDRs are placed on a 90-day probationary ramp period. During this period, performance is evaluated based on  qualified demo show-ups — demos that meet all qualification criteria: QuickBooks Online, decision-maker/owner present,  20+ open invoices  Period Minimum Qualified Demo  Show-Ups  Expectation  - Days 1– 30  - Days 31– 60  - Days 61– 90  15 Qualified Demo Show-Ups  20 Qualified Demo  Show-Ups  25 Qualified Demo  Show-Ups  Learn systems, scripts, and HubSpot workflows while  producing early pipeline activity.  Show measurable progress, steady daily activity, and growing  consistency.  Demonstrate full competence, independence, and readiness  for the ongoing quota.
