Inbound Chat Closer (US hours)

VirtuHire LOCATION: , South Africa (Remote) Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

<h3>Role Mission</h3><p>Own and convert inbound and outbound conversations across email, LinkedIn, and Instagram. Qualify potential leads for the $1k-$25k+ programs to the standard required to book a call where price is revealed and the close happens.</p><p>This role exists to take the CEO out of the DM grind and turn inbound attention into a predictable, measured sales engine. You own the conversation, the qualification, and the cash that comes from it.<br></p><h3>Responsibilities</h3><p><strong>Daily conversation ownership</strong></p><ul><li>Respond to every inbound message within coverage hours on both platforms.</li><li>Qualify in order using ARISE. Never skip or re-ask an answered question.</li><li>Confirm raise amount, status of raise, timeline, and at least one real gap before any offer.</li><li>Keep communication direct, concise, and in the CEO's voice. No hype, no manufactured urgency.</li></ul><p></p><p><strong>Systems (low lift, non-negotiable)</strong></p><ul><li>Tag every lead accurately by source and stage in real time.</li><li>Keep note fields current.</li><li>Flag red-alert conversations to the CEO or the right team member immediately.</li><li>Maintain the weekly KPI log.</li></ul><p></p><p><strong>Campaign execution</strong></p><ul><li>Run DM blitzes after webinars, viral posts, launch weeks, and announcements.</li><li>Follow the SOPs: The 24-Hour Close, Warm Lead Sprint, Founder Funding Week.</li></ul><p></p><h3>Disqualify Cleanly</h3><p>Not every conversation is a sale. You will be measured on clean exits as much as closes. Disqualify and park (or route to webinar) when: anti-equity stance, pre-product with no near-term timeline, no clear raise amount, no timeline, no visible pain. Survival-stage founders are not Momentum Pro. Probe once, then exit neutral.</p>
