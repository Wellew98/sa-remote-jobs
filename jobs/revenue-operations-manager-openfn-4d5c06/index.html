OpenFn is the digital public good for public service automation & AI orchestration. Our platform automates data exchange and digital workflows so that governments and NGOs can deliver public services more efficiently, achieve interoperability across fragmented systems, and scale critical public services across health, humanitarian, and government programs.

Our team,

Open Function Group

, the primary steward of this open-source platform, is a lean, fully-remote team of integration specialists, solution architects, and implementation consultants working across 15+ countries.

We are not making marginal improvements to already comfortable lives. We are building the infrastructure that allows governments to function, clinics to operate, and vulnerable populations to access services they depend on.

The role

We need a remote, systems-minded, commercially sharp Revenue Operations Manager who can give OpenFn a trusted view of its revenue and its customers: whether pipeline is on track, whether each customer is receiving everything their subscription includes, which accounts are healthy or at risk, and what is coming up for renewal. You will set and track the targets our go-to-market team works toward, run the deal desk, keep our CRM data clean, and use AI and automation to build the reporting and tools that keep a growing team running smoothly.

We are hiring our first Revenue Operations Manager as we transition to a subscription model built around packaged product-and-services bundles, and as we grow a new go-to-market team of business development, partnerships, and account management roles.

As our first Revenue Operations Manager, you will own the tools, data, and processes behind how OpenFn finds, wins, prices, contracts, and renews its customers. You will be the person leadership turns to for a trusted view of pipeline, revenue, and customer health, and the person our sellers turn to when they need a quote approved, a contract reviewed, a territory clarified, or a proposal template that is current.

OpenFn is a technical product, and our own operations should reflect that. You do not need to be an engineer, but you should be comfortable configuring the systems you own rather than relying on others to do it: setting up fields, reports, and dashboards, keeping data clean with rules and simple automations, and knowing when a problem needs an integration or a developer. If you can do some light scripting or integration work yourself, that is a bonus, and since we build workflow automation for a living, you will have the chance to use OpenFn's own platform to connect our internal systems.

Because this is a foundational hire, you will both build and execute. You will not inherit a mature playbook; you will help create it, establishing the processes, cadences, and tooling that a strong revenue operations function depends on, while supporting live deals and a growing pipeline from day one.

What you'll be doing

Oversee our CRM and revenue data

Administer our CRM (Salesforce / Odoo) day to day, including user access and simple automations (flows, formulas, validation rules) that reduce manual work

Set data standards for accounts, contacts, opportunities, and subscriptions, and train the go-to-market team to follow them

Make sure every customer, prospect, partner, and funder is in the CRM, categorized, and assigned to an owner, with opportunities tagged consistently (for example by type, channel, region, tier, and whether they are partner-sourced or funder-backed)

Own requirements and data quality when outside partners take on larger CRM configuration or migration projects

Build the funnel and reporting

Design and maintain a lead-to-revenue funnel with conversion rates and cycle times by stage

Use our CRM, Claude. and other AI tools to build pipeline, forecast, bookings, and subscription dashboards (annual recurring revenue, net revenue retention, churn, and expansion) for leadership and the board, covering sales, partnership, and fundraising pipeline

Run the weekly pipeline and forecast review with the go-to-market team

Report on CRM coverage and data quality (unassigned accounts, untagged or stale opportunities) and follow up with owners until gaps are closed

Track subscription delivery and customer health

Track what each customer's subscription includes (tier, included service days, features, and service commitments) and whether sales, services, and product have delivered it

Maintain a customer health score that combines product usage (such as monthly active users), service day use, delivery status, and support signals, and rate each customer green, yellow, or red

Maintain a renewal calendar and flag at-risk renewals early enough for account managers to act

Run a regular customer health review with the Growth, Services, and Product leads so every at-risk account has an owner and a plan

Run sales planning and set targets

Design territories and account assignments across our regions and channels, including direct and partner-led sales

Set quarterly targets with leadership for each go-to-market role, covering bookings and revenue plus leading indicators such as meetings, proposals, partner-sourced pipeline, and renewal activity

Set quotas and capacity plans, and report attainment into our monthly OKR review

Administer variable compensation plans, including calculations, attainment statements, and payout inputs to finance

Run the deal desk and quote-to-cash process

Own quoting, making sure deals follow our pricing and packaging model across subscription tiers and services

Manage approvals for discounts and non-standard terms in line with our approval policies

Review contracts, order forms, and partner agreements for commercial terms, bringing in leadership and legal counsel where needed

Ensure a clean handoff from closed deal to invoicing, onboarding, and renewals

Equip the go-to-market team

Maintain proposal templates and pricing sheets so they are current, on-brand, and easy to find

Build AI-assisted workflows for the go-to-market team (renewal account briefs, deal summaries, pipeline hygiene checks, logging conversations in the CRM), in line with OpenFn's AI and data policies

Support partner program operations, including partner onboarding, deal registration, and tracking of partner-sourced pipeline

Own the sales and marketing tool stack

Own the tools around the CRM (email and calendar integrations, marketing automation, e-signature, and the accounting connection), adding new tools only when needed

Build integrations between our CRM, finance, and marketing systems so data flows without manual re-entry

Who you'll work with

You will report to our Chief Operating Officer and also work closely with:

Our Head of Growth, on pipeline, reporting, forecasting, targets, and go-to-market planning

Our Managing Director, Services, on subscription delivery, customer health, and the handoff from sale to delivery

Our Product Lead, on product usage data and what each subscription tier includes

Our go-to-market team (business development, account managers, and our Partner Program Director), who rely on you for reporting, deal support, tools, and process

Our finance & ops team, who you will partner with on invoicing, financial modeling, and variable compensation

Required Qualifications

5-8+ years of experience in revenue operations, sales operations, or business systems at a SaaS or subscription business

Hands-on experience with Odoo and Salesforce, including data management, reporting, and ideally basic automations

A track record of building pipeline, forecast, and customer health reporting that leadership used to make decisions

Experience with sales planning (targets, territories, quotas, and variable compensation) and running a deal desk (pricing approvals and contract review)

Strong analytical and spreadsheet skills, with a high standard for data accuracy

Hands-on use of AI tools (such as Claude) to analyze data, build reports, or automate tasks, with examples you can show us

Fluent professional English, and a home or shared office setup with fast, stable internet in Sub-Saharan Africa

Preferred Qualifications

Experience supporting a CRM migration, or integrating a CRM with accounting systems such as QuickBooks

Experience supporting a channel or partner sales program

Experience helping a business transition from project or services-based revenue to a subscription model

Experience with government, NGO, or international customers, ideally in the open source/ digital public goods or international development ecosystem

Additional language skills relevant to our customer base (e.g. French, Spanish, Portuguese)

Compensation and Benefits

Financial compensation includes a competitive base salary. Total pay is commensurate with experience, per OpenFn's Equal Pay for Equal Work framework.

N.B.: Selling to NGO and government customers, we don't offer Silicon Valley pay scales, but high quality of life and fair, transparent, and competitive compensation is important to us.
