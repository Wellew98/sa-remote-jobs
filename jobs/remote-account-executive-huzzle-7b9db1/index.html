About Huzzle

At Huzzle, we connect exceptional talent with top opportunities at leading companies across the UK, US, Canada, Europe, and Australia. Our clients include startups, digital agencies, and tech platforms in industries such as SaaS, MarTech, FinTech, and EdTech. Unlike an outsourcing agency, we place you directly with a client where you’re hired in-house as a valued member of their team.

Job Summa

ry

We’re hiring an experienced Account Executive with a proven track record in B2B sales, high-volume calling, and closing deals.

This is a hands-on sales role for someone who is confident on the phone, commercially sharp, and comfortable managing a fast-moving pipeline. You’ll speak with a high volume of prospects, run discovery and sales conversations, overcome objections, follow up consistently, and turn qualified opportunities into paying customers.

We’re looking for a strong closer who takes ownership of their numbers and thrives in a performance-driven environment.

Key Responsibilities

Manage B2B prospects through the sales process from initial conversation to close

Handle a high volume of outbound and/or inbound sales calls each day

Conduct effective discovery calls to uncover prospects’ needs, pain points, budgets, and buying intent

Deliver persuasive pitches and clearly communicate the value proposition

Confidently handle objections, negotiate, and ask for the sale

Follow up persistently with prospects across phone, email, and other channels

Maintain an organized, accurate pipeline within the CRM

Consistently work toward and exceed monthly sales and revenue targets

Accurately forecast opportunities and maintain strong pipeline hygiene

Collaborate with SDRs, marketing, and other team members to maximize conversion rates

4+ years of professional B2B sales experience

Demonstrable track record of closing deals and hitting or exceeding sales targets

Significant experience working in a high-call-volume sales environment

Excellent phone presence with strong discovery, pitching, negotiation, and objection-handling skills

Comfortable directly asking prospects for the sale and progressing deals toward a decision

Strong understanding of B2B sales cycles and pipeline management

Experience using CRM platforms such as HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, or similar

Highly organized with disciplined follow-up habits

Excellent spoken and written English

Self-motivated, resilient, competitive, and comfortable being measured against clear KPIs

Reliable remote-working setup with a stable internet connection

💰 Competitive salary

🌎 Fully remote role with flexible work environment

🚀 Work with high-growth global companies and innovative products

📈 Clear progression path into senior sales or leadership roles

🎯 Performance-based incentives and bonuses

🤝 Collaborative, high-performance sales culture
