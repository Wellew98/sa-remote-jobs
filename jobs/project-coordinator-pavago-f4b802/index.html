Project Coordinator – Project Tracking & Operations

Full-Time | Remote | U.S. Business Hours

About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a Project Coordinator to keep multiple projects organized, on schedule, and moving forward.

This is a hands-on coordination role for someone who is highly organized, proactive, and strong at follow-through. You’ll manage project trackers, timelines, documentation, stakeholder communication, and action items , ensuring deadlines are visible and nothing falls through the cracks.

What You’ll Do

Project Tracking & Coordination

Maintain project plans in

Asana, Trello, Monday.com, Smartsheet, or similar tools

Track

deadlines, milestones, dependencies, deliverables, and ownership

Follow up proactively on outstanding tasks

Keep project schedules and trackers accurate and current

Coordinate multiple projects and priorities simultaneously

Communication & Follow-Up

Act as a coordination point between Project Managers, team members, leadership, and stakeholders

Schedule and support project meetings

Capture meeting notes, decisions, and action items

Communicate project updates and follow up on deliverables

Escalate blockers and delays before they impact deadlines

Reporting & Documentation

Prepare weekly project status reports covering progress, risks, blockers, and next steps

Maintain organized documentation in

Google Drive, SharePoint, or Confluence

Build and maintain project trackers using

Excel or Google Sheets

Ensure project information remains easy to find and up to date

Risk & Resource Tracking

Identify bottlenecks, missed dependencies, and execution risks

Maintain risk and issue trackers

Monitor workloads and resource availability

Review deliverables for completeness before submission

Vendor Coordination

Communicate with vendors and external partners

Track external deliverables and deadlines

Follow up on outstanding requirements and escalate issues when necessary

What We’re Looking For

2+ years of experience in Project Coordination, Operations, or Project/Administrative Support

Hands-on experience with

Asana, Trello, Monday.com, or similar project management tools

Strong

Excel / Google Sheets skills, including trackers, Pivot Tables, and conditional formatting

Excellent written and verbal English communication

Strong documentation and organizational skills

Ability to manage multiple projects and deadlines simultaneously

Proactive approach to follow-up and problem-solving

Availability during

U.S. business hours

Nice to Have

PMP, CAPM, Agile, or Scrum certification/in-progress

Familiarity with

Agile/Scrum and sprint workflows

Experience in

SaaS, Marketing, Professional Services, Construction, or Operations

What Success Looks Like

Projects and milestones stay on schedule

No missed deadlines due to coordination gaps

Project trackers remain accurate and current

Risks and blockers are identified early

Status reporting is clear and consistent

Stakeholders have strong visibility into priorities and progress

Why Join?

Fully remote opportunity

High ownership across multiple projects

Direct exposure to cross-functional teams and leadership

Hands-on experience with modern project management systems

Clear growth path into

Project Manager, PMO, or Operations roles
