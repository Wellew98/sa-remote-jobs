About the Client

Our client operates in the education and career development space, helping students achieve meaningful professional and personal outcomes through structured programs, personalized coaching, and ongoing support.

The company emphasizes a highly personalized student experience, where each Student Success Manager takes direct ownership of student progression, engagement, program completion, retention, and long-term relationships.

Unlike traditional customer support positions, this role requires someone who can independently guide students throughout structured programs while identifying opportunities to retain, reactivate, and upsell clients.

About the Role

We are seeking an experienced, commercially driven Student / Customer Success Manager with proven experience in structured education, preferably B2C coaching, career development, EdTech, or similar program-based services.

The successful candidate will independently manage a portfolio of approximately 30–60 students , guiding them from onboarding through program completion while maintaining engagement, monitoring progress, and ensuring successful outcomes.

This is a high-ownership position combining student success, program management, retention, reactivation, renewals, upselling, and payment management. The ideal candidate must demonstrate measurable success in retaining students, reactivating disengaged clients, and independently closing additional program sales.

This is not a traditional customer support or tutoring role. Candidates must have direct experience managing structured student journeys alongside commercial responsibilities.

Role Responsibilities

Student Portfolio & Program Management

  • Independently manage 30–60 students throughout structured coaching or education programs, from onboarding through completion.
  • Establish student goals, milestones, and success plans while monitoring progress, engagement, and overall satisfaction.
  • Conduct regular check-ins, provide accountability, and proactively address challenges affecting student progress.
  • Coordinate with coaches or internal teams to resolve issues and maintain a personalized, high-quality student experience.

Retention, Reactivation & Student Success

  • Take direct ownership of student retention, identifying disengaged or at-risk students before cancellations or refund requests occur.
  • Independently manage difficult conversations, resolve concerns, and implement targeted retention strategies.
  • Reactivate inactive students through personalized outreach, objection handling, and structured follow-ups.
  • Track and report retention, reactivation, and program completion performance.

Renewals, Upselling & Revenue Growth

  • Independently identify, present, and close opportunities for renewals, advanced coaching programs, and additional services.
  • Conduct consultative sales conversations, address objections, and recommend relevant continuation programs.
  • Own individual revenue targets and track upsell conversions, closed sales, renewals, and revenue generated.
  • Maintain strong client relationships while balancing student outcomes with commercial objectives.

Payments, CRM & Relationship Management

  • Manage payment follow-ups, overdue balances, failed payments, and payment arrangements professionally.
  • Maintain accurate CRM records covering student progress, engagement, retention risks, renewal opportunities, and sales activities.
  • Identify satisfied students and generate testimonials, reviews, and referrals.
  • Provide regular performance updates and maintain disciplined follow-ups across the assigned portfolio.

Requirements

  • 3–4+ years of relevant Student Success or Customer Success experience , preferably within structured education, B2C coaching, EdTech, career development, or premium membership programs.
  • Proven experience independently managing approximately 30–60 students or clients through structured programs, including onboarding, milestone tracking, engagement, and completion.
  • Demonstrated ownership of retention, upselling, and reactivation , supported by specific examples, individual performance metrics, and measurable results.
  • Strong consultative sales skills, with proven ability to independently close renewals and upsells without relying on a separate sales team.
  • Practical experience handling billing, failed payments, overdue accounts, and payment arrangements.
  • Excellent spoken and written English, with clear, professional communication suitable for video calls with students and parents.
  • Experience working with US-based clients or students, with availability during US business hours.
  • Strong organizational skills, CRM proficiency, and the ability to independently manage multiple student relationships and commercial priorities.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in high-ticket B2C coaching, career accelerators, educational memberships, or structured professional development programs.
  • Proven track record of meeting individual retention, reactivation, upselling, or revenue targets.
  • Background in software engineering, career coaching, technical education, or related fields. A Computer Science or Programming background is an advantage.

Not a Fit

  • Candidates whose experience is primarily limited to English tutoring, teaching, customer support, or ticket resolution without structured student success responsibilities.
  • Individuals who have not independently managed student portfolios throughout defined coaching or education programs.
  • Candidates without direct commercial ownership of retention, reactivation, renewals, or upselling.
  • Individuals who cannot provide specific examples and measurable results demonstrating their personal contributions.
  • Candidates uncomfortable handling sales conversations, pricing objections, payment collections, or independently closing upsell opportunities.

Candidates should be prepared to provide specific examples of

  • Structured Program Experience: Types of programs managed, duration, student milestones, and responsibilities.
  • Portfolio Ownership: Number of students independently managed and how engagement and progress were monitored.
  • Retention & Reactivation: Strategies used to prevent cancellations, re-engage inactive students, and measurable outcomes.
  • Upselling & Renewals : Additional programs personally sold, deal values, conversion rates, and revenue generated.
  • Individual Performance : Retention rates, renewal targets, upselling achievements, and other measurable KPIs.
