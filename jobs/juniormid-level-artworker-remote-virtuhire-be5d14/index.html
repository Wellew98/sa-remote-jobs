About the Company

Our client is a leading specialist retailer and manufacturer within the pet care industry, supplying a wide range of products for specialist animal care. They develop, manufacture, and distribute branded products across multiple markets, with a strong focus on quality, compliance, and product innovation. The business operates across multiple brands and regularly launches new products, requiring accurate packaging, artwork, and marketing materials to support both retail and digital channels.

Job Title

Junior/Mid-Level Artworker

Reports To

Head of Marketing

Role Overview

Our client is seeking a detail-oriented Junior/Mid-Level Artworker to support two established brands across a high volume of packaging artwork, compliance updates, point-of-sale materials, and marketing collateral for new product launches.

This is primarily a production-focused design role where accuracy, consistency, and technical execution are just as important as creativity. The successful candidate will ensure all packaging, labels, launch materials, and compliance updates are produced accurately, remain on-brand, and are print-ready. The role also involves translating approved product concepts into final production artwork while maintaining strict adherence to brand guidelines.

This position plays a key role in ensuring products remain legally compliant and market-ready across international markets.
