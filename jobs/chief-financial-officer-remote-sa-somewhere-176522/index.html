Finance Leader / Controller (Hospitality & Tourism)

LocationRemote (must overlap with U.S. Pacific Time)

Employment TypeFull-Time

ROLE OVERVIEW

We are seeking a proactive, operator-oriented Finance Leader to oversee and elevate the financial operations across two hospitality and tourism businesses

This is a hands-on role where you will design, manage, and own the entire finance and accounting function — from forecasting and cash flow to payroll accuracy, POS integrations, COGS analysis, debt schedules, and operational reporting

You will collaborate with an existing low-cost bookkeeping provider (Pilot) or supervise a part-time offshore bookkeeper, ensuring financial accuracy while maintaining cost efficiency.

This is not a passive reporting job — it is a strategic and operational leadership role.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

1. Financial Leadership & Forecasting

- Build and maintain 12-month rolling forecasts for both businesses.

- Lead annual budgeting across revenue, labor models, COGS, overhead, and capital planning.

- Conduct scenario analyses (e.g., additional operating days, menu price changes, staffing shifts).

- Develop financial dashboards for Owner and GM.

2. Weekly & Monthly Reporting

- Deliver weekly flash reports: sales, labor %, COGS %, prime costs, cash trends, variances.

- Complete accurate monthly close within 10 days.

- Prepare monthly financial package: P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, KPI commentary.

- Reconcile results against POS, payroll, inventory, and vendor activity.

3. Cash Flow & Debt Management

- Build and oversee 8–12-week cash flow forecasts.

- Maintain SBA loan amortization schedules, seller notes, and debt service projections.

- Prepare lender-required financial reporting and ensure covenant compliance.

- Advise on capital allocation, reserves, and retained earnings strategy.

4. Payroll, POS & Systems Oversight

-

Oversee payroll accuracy for both businesses, including

- Hours, overtime, tips, PTO, adjustments.

- Approval and monthly reconciliation.

- Own integrations between POS (Aloha/Toast/FareHarbor), payroll, MarginEdge, and QBO.

- Maintain labor codes, job categories, tip structures, and reporting fields.

5. COGS, Inventory & Vendor Management

-

Build a robust COGS reporting framework

- Audit vendor invoices and monitor price changes.

- Oversee inventory processes and theoretical vs. actual food cost analysis.

- Recommend vendor optimization and purchasing efficiencies.

6. Labor Modeling & Operational Support

- Build labor models by role, daypart, and season.

- Track labor efficiency, productivity, and server-to-cover ratios.

- Support GM with schedule planning aligned to sales forecasts.

- Audit overtime, breaks, and compliance as needed.

7. Menu Engineering & Profitability

-

Conduct quarterly menu engineering

-

Contribution margin

- Popularity index

-

Pricing elasticity

- Recommend pricing changes, portion controls, and product mix improvements.

8. Controls, Compliance & Financial Hygiene

- Implement financial controls across cash handling, comps/voids, tips, vendor payments, and inventory.

- Review POS reports daily for irregularities.

- Maintain audit-ready files for CPA and lenders.

- Support tax, legal, and compliance workflows.

9. Owner & GM Partnership

- Meet weekly with Ownership and GM to review performance, risks, and opportunities.

- Provide proactive recommendations — not just reporting.

- Train operational leaders on financial literacy as needed.

- Assist with future HoldCo entities or new business lines.

RECOMMENDED FINANCIAL WORKFLOW (OWNED BY THIS ROLE)

CFO Responsibilities

- Own QBO, POS reconciliation, payroll approvals, and financial systems.

- Review Pilot’s (or bookkeeper’s) categorizations and reconciliations.

- Make month-end adjustments and finalize close.

- Deliver weekly and monthly financial reporting.

- Build all forecasts, KPIs, dashboards, and budgets.

- Ensure COGS and labor reporting accuracy — essential in restaurant finance.

Pilot / Offshore Bookkeeper Responsibilities

- Daily/weekly categorization of transactions.

- Bank and credit card reconciliations.

- Vendor bill entry and receipt management.

- Draft monthly close files for review.

- POS deposit matching and cleanup.

Goal of the Workflow

- CFO focuses on analysis, strategy, accuracy, and operational insight — not clerical work.

- Books remain timely, accurate, and meaningful for operational decisions.

- Avoid unnecessary systems like U.S. EOR payroll or high-cost bookkeeping.

REQUIRED EXPERIENCE

- 5–10+ years in finance roles: Controller, FP&A, CFO, Senior Analyst .

- Hospitality/restaurant/tourism or multi-entity SMB experience highly preferred .

- Advanced proficiency in QuickBooks Online , payroll systems, and POS integrations.

- Experience with MarginEdge is ideal.

- Strong financial modeling and forecasting ability.

- Excellent written communication and ability to present insights clearly.

- Must have overlapping availability with U.S. Pacific Time .

IDEAL ATTRIBUTES

- Highly proactive and anticipatory — identifies risks before they surface.

- Detail-oriented with high accuracy standards.

- Comfortable building structure in an evolving, scaling environment.

- Collaborative, supportive of operations, and able to translate numbers into action.
