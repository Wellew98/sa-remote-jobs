Role Overview

We are a high-performing real estate team based in San Jose, California, serving a sophisticated residential market where precision, follow-through, and client experience matter. We are seeking an experienced Real Estate Virtual Assistant to act as the operational backbone of the business. This role is responsible for end-to-end listing coordination, marketing execution, transaction support, and administrative systems management, ensuring that listings launch on time, transactions stay on track, and clients experience a seamless, professional process from start to finish. The position requires strong project-management skills, attention to detail, and the ability to proactively manage multiple workflows while coordinating with local on-the-ground support in the U.S. This is not a task-only VA role. The ideal candidate thinks in systems, anticipates next steps, and owns outcomes—not just to-do lists.

Core Responsibilities

1. Administrative & Business Operations ● Own inbox triage and calendar management ● Schedule all appointments, inspections, photography, and meetings ● Maintain CRM accuracy: data entry, tagging, segmentation, notes ● Build and manage checklists for listings, escrows, and repeat processes ● Track deadlines, contingencies, and escrow milestones ● Manage DocuSign, disclosures, and compliance files ● Perform transaction coordination for buyer and seller transactions ● Track expenses, invoices, and receipts ● Create and maintain SOPs and internal documentation ● Prepare weekly status reports and priority summaries for the agent

OutcomeThe agent never wonders “What’s outstanding?”

2. Listing Management (Pre-Market → Live → Close) ● Coordinate all pre-listing activities remotely ● Order inspections, staging consults, photography, video, drone ● Gather and organize seller disclosures ● Enter and update MLS listings accurately ● Draft MLS descriptions and marketing copy for agent approval ● Build property websites and upload all media ● Proof all marketing materials before release ● Manage showing instructions and feedback ● Prepare neighborhood stats, disclosures packets, and pricing docs ● Track listing timelines and ensure deadlines are hit Outcome: Listings launch clean, on time, and professionally—every time.

3. Marketing Execution & Brand Support ● Create, schedule and publish social media posts across all channels ● Write marketing captions and copy ● Create graphics and marketing assets using tools like AI, Canva, and Adobe ● Using Opusclip, edit videos and upload to all platforms like YouTube and other social channels ● Format and send email newsletters and market updates ● Coordinate print materials and farming mailers ● Maintain website updates and blog posts ● Manage Google Business profile updates ● Request, track, and post client reviews ● Track basic marketing performance metrics Outcome: Consistent marketing presence without agent micromanagement.

4. Client, Lead & Sphere Support ● Handle inbound leads (Zillow, Realtor.com, website, referrals) ● Pre-qualify leads via email/text and schedule consults ● Prepare CMAs and buyer/seller packets for review ● Draft follow-up emails after client meetings ● Manage client touchpoints (birthdays, anniversaries, home value updates) ● Coordinate closing gifts (ordering, shipping, timing) ● Prepare buyer tour itineraries and moving checklists ● Track referrals and follow-up timelines Outcome: Clients feel cared for. Leads don’t fall through cracks.

5. Open House & Field Coordination (Remote Side Only) ● Coordinate open house scheduling ● Prepare open house marketing materials ● Assign and schedule local runner or open house host ● Collect sign-in data and enter into CRM ● Execute follow-up campaigns after open houses Outcome: Open houses generate value, not just foot traffic.

6. Strategic & Reporting Support ● Maintain pipeline dashboards and deal tracking ● Track KPIs and production metrics ● Monitor listing inventory and market activity ● Research competitors and comparable listings ● Assist with listing presentations and pricing strategy docs ● Research tools, vendors, and tech platforms ● Act as project manager across all active deals Outcome: The agent has visibility and leverage—without doing the work.
