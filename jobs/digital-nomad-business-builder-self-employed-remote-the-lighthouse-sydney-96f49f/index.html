Are you a senior professional or corporate leader ready to trade the boardroom for complete time freedom? This is not a traditional employment role. It is an independent business partnership for motivated individuals who want to build a location-independent business under the umbrella of an established global leadership and self-mastery brand. It can be built full-time or part-time.

We are looking for self-motivated individuals with a strong executive track record, personal presence, excellent communication skills and the drive to work autonomously. If you have been looking for a vehicle that allows you to work from anywhere in the world while creating an asset that works for you, this opportunity is your next step.

Key Responsibilities

Business Development: Implement proven marketing and lead generation strategies to generate active enquiry (no cold calling).

Brand Representation: Act as a professional ambassador for high-demand success education, leadership and personal expansion programmes.

Team Expansion: Mentor, guide and connect with other senior professionals who are looking to establish their own independent paths.

System Efficiency: Leverage a fully established global business model, back-office infrastructure and training system to scale your operations.

What We Look For

Professional Track Record: A background in marketing, sales, leadership, management or general business operations is highly regarded.

Autonomy: A self-starter mindset with the discipline to manage your own schedule and work unsupervised.

Growth Mindset: A strong personal alignment with self-mastery, personal expansion and conscious leadership.

Technical Setup: Access to a reliable laptop, smartphone, high-speed internet connection and a quiet workspace, wherever that may be in the world.

Why Partner With Us?

True Location Freedom: Work from anywhere and design a genuine digital nomad lifestyle.

Pure performance-based Income: What you put in is what you get back in financial return. Your income reflects your dedication and results, with no ceiling on your growth.

Full Support and Mentorship: Access comprehensive training and daily support from a global network of successful business builders.

Simple Business Model: No inventory to hold, no staff to manage and no logistics to handle.

Based in Sydney, Australia, we work with leaders worldwide. If you are ready to move away from the traditional, run-ofd-the-mill ways of earning and living in business that aligns with your lifestyle, apply below. Shortlisted applicants will be contacted directly. Please do not apply if you do not wish to be interviewed.
