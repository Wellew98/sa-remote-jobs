Own email, Shopify & Amazon execution for a growing skincare brand.

$3,500-$3,750 USD/month | Full-time | 2-3 hrs PST overlap

I'm Leslie, and I founded Almond Clear to bring effective acne and folliculitis treatment to people who've tried everything else. Our products use mandelic acid to treat breakouts on the face, back, chest, thighs and butt, and they also work beautifully for fine lines, ingrown hairs, sun damage, hyperpigmentation and fading dark spots. What makes us different is that our formulations are safe for all skin tones and gentle enough for sensitive skin, which matters to people who've been told they have limited options.

We're a small, fast-growing team based mostly in Portland and Arizona, and we've seen serious momentum over the last two years across Shopify, Amazon and TikTok Shop. Right now we're about five core people plus a handful of agencies and contractors, so everyone wears multiple hats and you'll get real ownership over your work. If you love the energy of a startup where things move quickly, processes evolve as you go, and your ideas actually get implemented, you'll feel at home here.

We're looking for an

E-Commerce Marketing Specialist who can take full ownership of our day-to-day marketing execution so our Marketing Manager can finally step back and focus on strategy. You'll be the go-to person for email marketing, Shopify updates, content creation and Amazon support, working closely with Tamara but running with things independently once you understand the brief. This isn't a role where you wait to be told what to do next - you'll need to be proactive, resourceful and comfortable solving problems on your own.

📋 Own day-to-day email marketing campaigns and automations in Omnisend

✏️ Build, maintain and optimize email flows

💡 Develop advanced customer segments and targeted campaigns

📊 Monitor email performance metrics and make data-informed improvements

💻 Create email and marketing assets independently

✍️ Write marketing copy that reflects our brand voice and product value

📦 Support content creation and marketing execution across e-commerce channels

🛒 Make content and product updates to the Shopify website

📱 Support organic social media activities where required

🤝 Collaborate closely with the Marketing Manager on priorities and campaigns

🧩 Help systemize and improve marketing processes as we grow

🤖 Use AI and research to solve problems and increase productivity

📈 Potentially support Amazon backend and related e-commerce activities

🎯 Be prepared to take on varied marketing tasks within a small, fast-growing team

You'll work full-time with 2-3 hours overlap with PST, Monday through Thursday.

To be successful in this role, you need

✅ Approximately 3-5+ years of relevant e-commerce marketing experience

✅ Deep, hands-on email marketing expertise

✅ Experience building and optimizing email flows

✅ Advanced understanding of email segmentation

✅ Ability to analyze campaign metrics and independently make adjustments

✅ Experience with an email marketing platform such as Omnisend, Klaviyo or similar

✅ Shopify experience

✅ Experience with ratings & review tools (Okendo, Judge.me) and loyalty programs

✅ Amazon experience

✅ Strong marketing copywriting skills

✅ Good understanding of branding, brand voice and product positioning

✅ Ability to create or coordinate marketing assets without relying on the existing team for every element

✅ Strong problem-solving and research skills

✅ Ability to hit the ground running with limited training

✅ Confidence using AI tools as part of the working process

✅ Strong written and spoken English

✅ Experience working in a small, fast-moving e-commerce company

Brownie points for

⭐ Direct Omnisend experience

⭐ Experience managing or supporting the Amazon backend

⭐ Familiarity with organic social media marketing

⭐ Experience creating content and updating e-commerce websites

⭐ TikTok Shop knowledge or an interest in developing expertise in this area

⭐ Experience using Gmail, Slack and Asana

⭐ Previous experience working with a growing consumer-product brand

⭐ Basic design or creative asset production skills

⭐ Experience improving and documenting marketing processes

The ideal candidate is proactive and resourceful, someone who researches and attempts solutions before reaching out for help. You're comfortable stepping outside your formal job description and working in an environment where not every process is perfectly documented yet. A startup-style setting energizes you rather than stresses you out. Change doesn't throw you - you lean in, get your hands dirty and actively improve or systemize processes as you go. Collaboration comes naturally to you, and you communicate clearly with a genuine growth mindset.

What’s in it for you?

💰 $3,500-$3,750 USD per month*

💼 Full-time

🌍 Fully remote

📅 22 paid days off per year, including sick days and vacation

⏰ 2-3 hours overlap with PST, Monday through Thursday

*Please note that this is a gross salary and that you are responsible for any tax-related procedures in your country of residence. We kindly ask you to apply only if you are happy to work remotely as an independent contractor.
