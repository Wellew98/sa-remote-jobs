We're looking for an

Email Marketing Specialist to manage campaign scheduling, deploy creatives and landing page links, follow QA checklists, and manage email flows for a data-first growth marketing agency serving e-commerce brands. You will own the operational side of email marketing execution — keeping campaigns on schedule, assets correctly deployed, and flows working as intended across a portfolio of clients.

This is an execution and operations role. You do not need to write copy or design assets — those come to you. Your job is to take what has been produced, deploy it correctly, QA it thoroughly, and ensure every campaign goes out on time and on spec. Clear communication and an eye for detail are the most critical traits for this role. If you are organized, process-driven, comfortable manipulating email flows in a major ESP, and take pride in clean, error-free deployments, this role is a strong fit.

High-ownership email operations role with direct impact on campaign performance and delivery quality

What You'll Work On

Campaign Scheduling and Deployment

Schedule and deploy email campaigns accurately and on time across client accounts

Add creatives, copy, and landing page links into the ESP and ensure all assets are correctly placed before deployment

Coordinate campaign timing across multiple clients and ensure nothing launches late or with incorrect assets

Maintain a clear campaign calendar and flag scheduling conflicts or risks proactively

QA and Pre-Send Checklists

Follow QA checklists thoroughly before every send to catch errors in links, creative placements, subject lines, preview text, segmentation, and send time

Test emails across devices and email clients to ensure rendering is correct

Flag any issues to the relevant team member before deployment and confirm resolution before sending

Maintain a zero-error standard on all campaign deployments

Flow and Automation Management

Build, update, and manage email flows and automations inside the ESP

Understand how flows are structured and make changes to triggers, conditions, content, and timing as directed

Troubleshoot flow issues and ensure automations are running correctly across all client accounts

Document flow logic and changes clearly so the team has full visibility

Communication and Coordination

Communicate clearly and proactively with internal team members on campaign status, deployment timelines, and open issues

Flag blockers, missing assets, or unclear briefs early rather than waiting until a deadline is at risk

Maintain organized records of campaign deployments, QA outcomes, and flow changes

What You Bring

Proven hands-on experience managing email campaigns and flows inside a major ESP such as Klaviyo, HubSpot, Mailchimp, Attentive, or similar

Strong understanding of how email flows and automations are structured and how to manipulate them confidently

Excellent attention to detail with a track record of zero-error campaign deployments

Comfortable following QA checklists and pre-send processes rigorously without cutting corners

Clear written and verbal English communication skills with a proactive approach to flagging issues

Highly organized with the ability to manage campaign schedules across multiple client accounts simultaneously

Available Monday through Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern Time

Nice to Have

Experience with Klaviyo specifically for e-commerce email marketing

Background in a marketing agency managing email across multiple client accounts

An eye for creative quality and the ability to flag obvious design or copy issues before deployment

Familiarity with e-commerce platforms such as Shopify or BigCommerce as they relate to email integration

Experience with SMS marketing platforms alongside email
