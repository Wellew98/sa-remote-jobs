About Huzzle

At Huzzle, we connect high-performing B2B sales professionals with global companies across the UK, US, Canada, Europe, and Australia. Our clients include startups, digital agencies, and tech platforms in industries like SaaS, MarTech, FinTech, and EdTech. We match top sales talent to full-time remote roles where they’re hired directly into client teams and provided ongoing support by Huzzle.

Role TypeFull-time

EngagementIndependent Contractor

Job Summary

We are looking for an experienced CMS Developer with hands-on expertise in Wix, WordPress, and/or Shopify to join one of our clients remotely.

In this role, you will build, customize, maintain, and optimize websites using modern content management systems and e-commerce platforms. You'll work closely with designers, marketers, and other team members to turn designs and business requirements into responsive, high-performing websites.

This is an excellent opportunity for a detail-oriented web developer who is comfortable managing CMS-based websites independently and enjoys combining technical development with strong visual execution.

Key Responsibilities

Build, customize, and maintain websites using Wix, WordPress, and/or Shopify

Convert designs and mockups into responsive, functional web pages

Customize themes, templates, layouts, and CMS functionality

Implement website updates, landing pages, forms, integrations, and new features

Troubleshoot and resolve technical, layout, and compatibility issues

Optimize websites for speed, mobile responsiveness, usability, and SEO

Install, configure, and maintain plugins, apps, and third-party integrations

Manage website content and ensure consistency across pages

Perform ongoing website maintenance, updates, testing, and quality assurance

Collaborate with designers, marketers, and other stakeholders to deliver projects accurately and on time

Follow web development best practices for performance, accessibility, security, and maintainability

Proven professional experience as a CMS Developer, Web Developer, WordPress Developer, Shopify Developer, or Wix Developer

Strong hands-on experience with at least one of the following: Wix, WordPress, or Shopify

Solid knowledge of HTML and CSS

Familiarity with JavaScript and basic front-end development principles

Experience customizing CMS themes, templates, plugins, or applications

Understanding of responsive and mobile-first web development

Familiarity with website performance optimization and SEO best practices

Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills

Excellent attention to detail and ability to accurately implement designs

Ability to manage multiple website tasks and deadlines independently

Strong written and verbal English communication skills

Reliable internet connection and ability to work effectively in a remote environment

A portfolio or examples of websites you have built or maintained

Nice to Have

Experience across multiple platforms, particularly WordPress, Shopify, and Wix

Experience with page builders such as Elementor or similar tools

Knowledge of Shopify Liquid

Experience with Wix Studio or Velo

Familiarity with Figma and translating designs into live websites

Experience working with digital agencies, SaaS companies, startups, or e-commerce businesses

Knowledge of analytics, conversion tracking, and marketing integrations

💻 Work on meaningful WordPress development projects with real ownership and impact.

🚀 Join a collaborative, high-performing team with opportunities to grow your technical expertise.
