Creator Partnerships & Affiliate Growth Lead

Salary:  $2500-$4000

Schedule :  Mon-Fri EST (Normal Business Hours 8-4 or 9-5)

Independent Contract

Remote Job About the Company

Our client is building a next-generation publishing platform at the intersection of AI, creators, books, and social commerce. Their mission is to help individuals transform ideas into published books and distribute them through modern social commerce channels, creating new opportunities for authors, creators, and consumers alike.

As the company scales, they are investing heavily in creator-led growth and affiliate partnerships. They are seeking a  Creator Partnerships & Affiliate Growth Lead  to own and scale a high-touch VIP creator program focused on driving product awareness, creator engagement, and revenue growth.

Role Overview

The Creator Partnerships & Affiliate Growth Lead will serve as the primary owner of a VIP creator program designed to cultivate relationships with high-performing TikTok Shop affiliates, BookTok creators, and social commerce influencers.

This is not an administrative or traditional virtual assistant role. The ideal candidate is a creator-facing revenue operator who understands partnerships, affiliate growth, relationship management, and creator engagement. They will be responsible for identifying opportunities, activating creators, managing campaigns, and helping creators maximize performance through data-driven insights and ongoing support.

The successful candidate will combine elements of account management, affiliate marketing, partnerships, creator relations, and growth operations into one highly impactful role.

Key Responsibilities

Creator Partnerships & VIP Program Management

- Own the day-to-day execution of the VIP creator program

- Manage a pipeline of high-potential creators, affiliates, and social commerce partners

- Track creator status, commitments, follow-ups, sample requests, and campaign participation

- Move creators through the lifecycle from prospect to active partner

- Ensure every creator has clear next steps and ongoing engagement

- Build strong long-term relationships with top-performing creators

Creator Outreach & Recruitment

- Identify and recruit high-quality TikTok Shop affiliates and creator partners

- Send personalized outreach and follow-up communications

- Re-engage creators who have previously participated in campaigns

- Qualify creators based on audience fit, performance metrics, content quality, and engagement

- Clearly communicate the value proposition and benefits of participation

- Convert creator interest into active campaign participation

Campaign & Product Launch Management

- Build and manage creator launch groups for new product releases and campaigns

- Match creators with products best suited to their audience and content style

- Coordinate sample requests and campaign onboarding

- Provide creators with briefs, talking points, content angles, and performance insights

- Track campaign performance, posting activity, and creator engagement

- Encourage repeat posting and long-term participation

Creator Relationship Development

- Serve as a trusted point of contact for creators

- Provide product recommendations and performance insights

- Help creators identify opportunities to improve engagement and results

- Escalate creator concerns or opportunities to leadership when necessary

- Identify high-performing creators deserving of expanded opportunities and support

Reporting & Program Optimization

- Maintain accurate CRM records and creator tracking systems

- Monitor creator activity, campaign performance, engagement, and outcomes

- Report on creator pipeline health and campaign performance

- Analyze performance trends and identify improvement opportunities

- Create and refine SOPs, outreach frameworks, and campaign processes

- Continuously improve creator activation and retention strategies

What This Role Offers

- Opportunity to build and scale a creator growth engine from the ground up

- High ownership and visibility within a fast-growing business

- Direct impact on revenue, creator growth, and product launches

- Exposure to social commerce, affiliate marketing, creator partnerships, and AI-powered commerce

- Collaborative entrepreneurial environment with significant growth potential

Ideal Candidate Profile

  • Excellent written English that feels natural for U.S. creator communication
  • Clear performance metrics from prior roles
  • Creator, influencer, affiliate, or partnerships experience tied to business outcomes
  • Strong attention to detail in their resume, summary, and communication
  • Evidence of being a top performer, not just someone with relevant responsibilities
  • Comfort with data, GMV, attribution, and performance-based incentives
  • Strong follow-up discipline
  • Scrappiness and willingness to grind
  • Excitement about helping creators grow their revenue

-

GMV Growth Incentive

The shop is currently doing approximately $350K/month in GMV.

There is a credible path to scaling meaningfully over the next 90 days by pitching, onboarding, and managing a small number of high-performing creators. A single high-performing creator can be worth approximately $100K/month in GMV, so onboarding and managing several of them could materially change the business.

We are open to a significant GMV-growth incentive for the right candidate.

A potential bonus structure would be approximately 1% of incremental GMV from managed creators, subject to attribution and margin guardrails.

For example

  • $200K/month in incremental managed-creator GMV = $2K bonus
  • $500K/month in incremental managed-creator GMV = $5K bonus
  • $700K/month in incremental managed-creator GMV = $7K bonus
  • It is fair to say that the right candidate should have a credible path to consistently earning $2K/month or more in bonus commission, with substantially higher upside if they help us scale the creator program aggressively.

The right person should be excited by this upside. We want someone who is motivated by performance and wants to help build a creator revenue engine, not simply execute administrative tasks.
