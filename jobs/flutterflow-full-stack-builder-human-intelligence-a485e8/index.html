About Human Intelligence

At Human Intelligence, we believe human intelligence is one of the most valuable forces in the world, shaping better decisions, stronger systems, and more meaningful outcomes. Our work in human intelligence AI is focused on helping people, teams, and institutions operate with greater clarity, precision, and impact through technology that is thoughtful, practical, and responsibly designed.

We are building AI-first products that support real-world human needs across digital health, wellness, and intelligent systems. This is not about building AI for its own sake. It is about applying modern AI in ways that are useful, reliable, secure, and genuinely valuable.

About the Role

We are looking for a FlutterFlow Full-Stack Builder to help build our next-generation consumer app experience.

This is a hands-on builder role for someone who can create polished user interfaces, connect APIs cleanly, and ship reliably. You will work closely with technical and product leadership to turn AI-powered product ideas into real screens, flows, and features.

This role is best suited to someone who is highly practical, detail-focused, fast-moving, and confident using AI tools in their daily workflow.

What You’ll Do

Build and maintain the FlutterFlow frontend for our app

Create pixel-perfect UI with strong layout, interaction, animation, and responsive behavior

Integrate backend APIs, authentication, tenant awareness, and error handling

Help turn AI-powered product features into usable app experiences

Use AI tools daily for development, design, QA, and speed of delivery

Keep work organized, communicate clearly, and document key decisions

Collaborate closely with our AI Tech Lead, Intelligence Officer, and Product team

What We’re Looking For

Strong experience building and shipping real FlutterFlow apps to production

Strong UI and UX skills with a high standard of visual quality

Good understanding of

REST APIs

auth flows

state management

caching

loading, error, and empty states

Comfortable using custom code when needed, while staying FlutterFlow-first

Strong English communication

Strong availability for live build sessions and team collaboration

Confident using modern AI tools for development, design, and testing

Organized and reliable in documenting work and keeping ClickUp updated

Nice to Have

Experience with schema-driven UI rendering

Experience with AI-powered app experiences such as chat, streaming, or voice patterns

Basic backend skills for debugging, light API work, or data transformation

Why Join Human Intelligence

Help build meaningful AI-first products for real human needs

Work closely with leadership in a high-trust, high-impact builder role

Join a fast-moving team that values clarity, quality, and practical execution

Grow through a KPI-based performance and reward structure

Contribute to a product environment where AI is part of how work gets done every day
