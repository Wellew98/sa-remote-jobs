About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring an experienced General Manager to lead and scale their International Moving Division , specializing in international household goods relocations and freight forwarding.

This is a senior, hands-on leadership role for someone with deep international moving industry experience . You’ll oversee the full operation — from sales, pricing, and quoting through international shipping, customs, destination services, operations, and customer experience.

You’ll lead multiple departments, improve operational performance, coach teams, establish KPIs, strengthen processes, and work directly with ownership on growth and strategic initiatives.

This role requires significant direct experience in international household goods moving. Candidates without this industry background will not be considered.

What a Typical Day Looks Like

You may start by reviewing sales, operational, and customer service KPIs before meeting with department leaders to address priorities.

Throughout the day, you could review complex international moves, troubleshoot shipment or customs issues, evaluate pricing, coach sales representatives, resolve a customer escalation, improve an operational workflow, or review team performance.

You’ll also work directly with ownership on growth initiatives, technology improvements, and the long-term direction of the division.

In short

you own the people, performance, processes, customer experience, and growth of the International Moving Division.
