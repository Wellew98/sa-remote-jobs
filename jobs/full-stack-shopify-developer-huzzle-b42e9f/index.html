About Huzzle

At Huzzle, we connect high-performing B2B sales professionals with global companies across the UK, US, Canada, Europe, and Australia. Our clients include startups, digital agencies, and tech platforms in industries like SaaS, MarTech, FinTech, and EdTech. We match top sales talent to full-time remote roles where they’re hired directly into client teams and provided ongoing support by Huzzle.

Role TypeFull-time

EngagementIndependent Contractor

About the company

Our client is a premier solution provider supporting the entire electronics lifecycle through a comprehensive range of test and measurement equipment alongside specialty technical supplies. As an established North American leader, the team serves engineers and manufacturers by offering vital resources including environmental test chambers, precision soldering tools, and advanced measurement systems. By providing a diverse suite of services—from calibration and rentals to inventory management—they ensure that organizations can maintain high-volume workflows and precision performance across the technology and defense sectors.

The team fosters an innovative, solutions-oriented environment dedicated to empowering the creators of tomorrow's technology. Operating with a focus on impact and reliability, the company has grown into a massive distribution powerhouse that balances technical expertise with a customer-centric approach. Candidates joining this fast-paced organization will contribute to a legacy of technical excellence, helping to deliver essential tools and services that drive progress within the electronics and aerospace industries.

Job Summary

We are seeking a technically-minded and detail-oriented eCommerce Content Development Specialist to serve as the architect of our digital storefront. In this role, you will bridge the gap between technical development and content strategy, ensuring our online store is not just functional, but high-performing and conversion-optimized. If you are a Shopify expert who enjoys working with APIs, Liquid, and front-end technologies to solve complex integration challenges and enhance the customer journey, this is the perfect opportunity for you to make a tangible impact on a thriving B2B environment.

Key Responsibilities

Develop, manage, and optimize digital content and site architecture across the Shopify platform.

Build and customize themes using HTML, CSS, and Liquid to maintain a premium brand aesthetic.

Integrate and manage third-party applications and plugins to extend site functionality.

Connect Shopify with critical external systems (ERP, CRM, PIM, and payment gateways) via APIs.

Maintain accurate, SEO-optimized product listings to drive organic traffic and consistency.

Audit and improve site speed, mobile responsiveness, and overall usability.

Troubleshoot technical issues related to site functionality and third-party integrations.

Partner with Marketing and Sales teams to execute promotional campaigns and product launches.

Utilize data insights from site analytics to recommend and implement UX improvements.

Proven experience in Shopify store management and theme customization.

Strong technical understanding of APIs and hands-on experience with system integrations.

Proficiency in front-end technologies including HTML, CSS, and basic JavaScript.

Working knowledge of Shopify Liquid templating language.

Demonstrated experience with Shopify apps, plugins, and third-party tool ecosystems.

Solid understanding of SEO principles and eCommerce best practices.

Analytical mindset with a proactive approach to problem-solving.

Exceptional attention to detail and the ability to manage multiple technical projects simultaneously.

Previous experience in a B2B eCommerce environment.

Knowledge of Google Analytics and data-driven optimization.

Experience specifically integrating eCommerce platforms with ERP or CRM systems.

Basic understanding of UX/UI design principles.
