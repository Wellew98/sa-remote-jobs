About RemotePass

RemotePass exists because talent is distributed equally, but opportunity is not. Backed by EBRD and trusted by 800+ companies across 150+ countries, RemotePass is the platform that gives businesses the infrastructure to hire anyone, anywhere - and gives workers in emerging markets the financial tools and global access they deserve.

RemotePass is the only platform that combines Employer of Record, contractor management, direct employment, and corporate spend cards in a single product - rated 4.8/5 on G2, trusted by Spotify, Logitech, and Deloitte, and operating across 150+ countries.

We are backed by leading global investors, including Oraseya Capital, Endeavor Catalyst, Khwarizmi Ventures, Flyer One Ventures, Access Bridge Ventures, A15, Swiss Founders Fund, and Plug & Play.

The role

Instagram and TikTok are blank canvases at RemotePass, and we want someone who's itching to fill them. Up to now we've mostly cross-posted LinkedIn content over; we're done with that. We want native, scroll-stopping, made-for-the-platform video, and we want the person behind it to be the face of it.

You'll report to our Social & Content lead, who'll shape strategy with you, but the execution, the ideas, the trends, the camera time, is all yours. Editing is covered by our in-house multimedia designer, so you're not stuck behind a timeline; you're out finding the story, filming it, and being in it.

It's a fully remote role, which means part of the job is figuring out how to make great content happen without an office full of people to grab. If you're resourceful, persuasive, and genuinely can't stop scrolling TikTok, this is your shot to build something from zero and watch it take off.

What you'll do

Launch and grow Instagram and TikTok from scratch, shaping strategy with our Social & Content lead and owning execution day-to-day

Film native short-form content and appear on camera yourself, think street interviews, trend-jacks, and behind-the-scenes moments

Live and breathe trends, sounds, and formats, and turn them into ideas that actually fit RemotePass

Pull teammates and other departments into content, and get creative sourcing footage in a remote-first company

Come up with static post ideas as well as video, and brief them clearly to our design team

Jump in on quick, easy LinkedIn posts (like event coverage) alongside your Instagram/TikTok work

Track performance and use it to sharpen what you make next

Be a face of RemotePass as we expand into new markets

What we're looking for

2-5 years creating content for Instagram and/or TikTok, in-house, agency, freelance, or as a creator

A strong portfolio or reel that shows you on camera and shows you get what makes short-form actually work, this is the single most important thing we'll look at

Genuinely plugged into Instagram/TikTok trends and culture

Comfortable and natural on camera, and confident directing others who may be less comfortable than you

A self-starter who drives ideas forward, with the follow-through to not let details slip

Resourceful and persuasive, able to make content happen remotely without a studio or office full of people on tap

Great at taking and acting on feedback

Experience creating for international or multi-market audiences
