About Us

HireHawk helps U.S. and Canadian companies build high-performing global teams by connecting them with skilled professionals across Latin America, the Philippines, South Africa, Europe, and beyond. We believe exceptional talent exists everywhere, and businesses should be able to access it without geographic limitations.

We're growing our global recruitment business and looking for people who want to take ownership, solve meaningful challenges, and help shape how we scale.

About the Role

We're looking for a hands-on Head of Growth Marketing to lead HireHawk's B2B client acquisition strategy, from the first search or ad click through qualified discovery calls, signed clients, and completed placements.

You'll own SEO, paid acquisition, conversion optimization, marketing analytics, and AI-powered automation. This is a leadership role for someone who enjoys building and executing, not just setting strategy or managing agencies.

You'll have the opportunity to shape our growth infrastructure, identify scalable acquisition channels, and work closely with Sales to turn marketing investment into measurable business results.

Your success will be measured by the quality of the pipeline you generate, the clients we acquire, and the efficiency of the growth engine you build.

Responsibilities

Own and scale B2B client acquisition across Google Ads, paid search, SEO, and other high-potential channels, prioritizing qualified pipeline and revenue over traffic volume.

Build and optimize paid media campaigns, including budget allocation, keyword targeting, search-term analysis, creative testing, and landing page performance.

Lead HireHawk's SEO strategy, combining technical improvements, high-intent keyword research, and content that attracts companies searching for staffing and recruitment solutions.

Improve conversion rates across the acquisition funnel by testing landing pages, messaging, forms, calls to action, and email nurture sequences.

Partner closely with Sales to improve lead quality, increase discovery-call bookings, and understand which acquisition sources produce signed clients and completed placements.

Build reliable attribution and reporting that connects advertising platforms, website analytics, and CRM data to qualified leads, pipeline, revenue, and customer acquisition costs.

Develop AI-powered and automated marketing workflows to improve campaign analysis, lead scoring, lead routing, reporting, and operational efficiency.

Design and execute growth experiments, using measurable business outcomes to decide which campaigns to scale, refine, or discontinue.

Establish clear marketing KPIs and reporting that provide visibility into acquisition costs, conversion rates, pipeline contribution, and return on marketing investment.

Must-Have

Proven hands-on experience leading both SEO and paid media for B2B customer acquisition, with measurable improvements in qualified leads, pipeline, or revenue.

Advanced Google Ads and paid search expertise, including budget management, keyword strategy, conversion tracking, optimization, and performance analysis.

Strong technical SEO, organic content strategy, and conversion rate optimization skills.

Experience integrating advertising platforms, website analytics, and CRM systems to track lead quality, pipeline progression, and revenue attribution.

Practical experience implementing AI tools and marketing automation workflows that improve acquisition performance or operational efficiency.

Strong analytical and commercial judgment, with the ability to translate marketing data into clear investment decisions.

Ability to independently execute marketing initiatives, communicate findings clearly in English, and collaborate effectively with a distributed Sales team.

Nice to Have

Experience driving growth for staffing, recruitment, talent solutions, or other B2B professional services businesses.

Familiarity with platforms such as HubSpot, Salesforce, GA4, Google Tag Manager, and Looker Studio.

Experience with offline conversion tracking and sending CRM-qualified lead or revenue data back into paid advertising platforms.

Experience building automation workflows using tools such as Zapier, Make, n8n, or similar platforms.

Experience scaling acquisition programs across multiple markets or customer segments.
