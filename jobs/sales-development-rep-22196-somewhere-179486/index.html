Outbound Sales Development Representative

Job Title

Outbound Sales Development Representative (Phone-First, Small Business Owners)

Location / Employment Details

  • Location: Fully remote
  • Engagement: Full-time, permanent independent contractor role.
  • Schedule: Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM U.S. Central Time (4:00 PM to midnight South Africa time, or 3:00 PM to 11:00 PM during U.S. daylight saving time).
  • Reports to: The sales team lead. You'll book discovery calls for the company's sales representatives.
  • Tools: HighLevel CRM, a calling platform, and video for internal communication.

About the Company

We are a rapidly growing U.S.-based digital marketing agency that works exclusively with independent automotive repair shops. Our team builds websites, runs search and paid advertising, manages social media, and shapes the messaging that helps shop owners stand out in their local markets.

Our approach is personal. We get to know each shop owner and their business, then build marketing around what makes their shop different. Clients stay with us because we communicate often, show them real results, and treat them as partners rather than accounts.

We're a team of more than 50 people across the U.S. and several other countries. We work in a highly collaborative, video-first remote environment, and our core values guide how we hire, how we work, and how we treat our clients.

About the Role

Most of our new business comes from education: webinars, classes, industry events, and referrals. That works, but it arrives in waves. We're building a steady outbound engine so our sales team has a reliable flow of qualified conversations every week.

You'll be the first voice many shop owners hear from us. Independent shop owners are busy, skeptical of marketing agencies, and often reached through a service advisor guarding the phone. The job is to earn a few minutes of their trust, learn about their shop, and book a discovery call when there's a real fit.

This is a phone-first role for someone who already knows how to cold call well and enjoys talking with small business owners. You'll work from prospect lists we provide and follow up with owners who have already engaged with our events and content.

Key Responsibilities

Outbound Prospecting

  • Make daily outbound calls to independent auto repair shop owners and decision-makers across the U.S.
  • Get past gatekeepers professionally and reach the owner or the person who makes marketing decisions.
  • Introduce our agency and clearly explain the value of our digital marketing services.
  • Open conversations with curiosity: ask about the shop, its customers, and what's working in its marketing today.
  • Work through provided prospect lists efficiently, prioritizing the most promising follow-ups.

Warm Lead Follow-Up and Event Promotion

  • Follow up with shop owners who attended webinars, classes, conferences, and other company events.
  • Invite prospects to upcoming webinars and educational sessions.
  • Stay in consistent contact with interested prospects until a conversation turns into a booked call or a clear no.

Qualification and Appointment Setting

  • Identify marketing needs and qualify fit before booking.
  • Schedule discovery appointments for the sales team, with clear notes so the rep walks in prepared.
  • Hand off context on each prospect: what they said, what they care about, and any objections raised.

CRM, Reporting, and Feedback

  • Log every call, conversation, appointment, and follow-up accurately in the CRM.
  • Hit daily and weekly targets for calls, conversations, and qualified appointments.
  • Share common objections, prospect feedback, and market trends with the sales team.
  • Help refine call scripts, messaging, and objection handling based on what's working.
  • Join regular sales meetings, training sessions, and performance reviews.

Required Qualifications

  • 2+ years of outbound cold calling and appointment setting, with numbers to back it up (dials, connect rate, appointments booked).
  • Excellent spoken English with very clear pronunciation and a natural American accent.
  • The ability to build rapport with U.S. business owners quickly, including owners who have never heard of us.
  • Proven ability to handle objections and steer a conversation toward a booked meeting.
  • Experience working in a CRM and calling software, with disciplined record-keeping.
  • Comfortable working toward daily and weekly activity and appointment goals.
  • Self-directed in a remote setting, without needing constant supervision.
  • Based in South Africa and available to work 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM U.S. Central Time, Monday to Friday.
  • Minimum typing speed of 40 WPM at 95% accuracy.

Preferred Qualifications

  • B2B phone sales to U.S. small business owners, especially owner-operated service businesses.
  • Experience selling digital marketing, websites, software, or other professional services.
  • Familiarity with the automotive repair or aftermarket industry.
  • Hands-on experience with HighLevel CRM.
  • Experience following up with webinar attendees, event participants, and other warm leads.

Ideal Candidate Profile

You are

  • Warm on the phone. Owners relax when they talk to you, because you sound like a person who's interested in their business, not someone reading a script.
  • Resilient. A brush-off on call 40 doesn't change how you sound on call 41.
  • Curious. You ask good questions and listen for the real need before you pitch a meeting.
  • Organized. Your follow-ups happen on time and your CRM notes let anyone pick up where you left off.
  • Coachable. You want feedback on your calls and you adjust quickly based on results.
  • Values-driven. Integrity matters to you, and you'd rather lose a meeting than mislead a prospect to get one.

Why Join Us

  • Time to recharge. Unlimited PTO after 90 days, eight paid local holidays, and paid time off from Christmas Eve through New Year's Day.
  • Health support. Monthly HMO reimbursement after 90 days.
  • A team that invests in you. Regular coaching, sales training, and a team that helps refine your approach.
  • Work you can be proud of. You'll represent an agency that clients trust and stay with.
  • Culture that's more than words. A collaborative remote team that takes its values seriously and has fun working together.
