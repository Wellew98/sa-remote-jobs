Are you a tech-savvy marketing professional passionate about leveraging automation and AI to drive results? We’re looking for a Marketing Automation Specialist to join our dynamic team and take the lead in creating innovative, data-driven campaigns using GoHighLevel or Instantly, alongside cutting-edge AI tools.

As our Marketing Automation Specialist, you’ll be responsible for designing and executing integrated marketing campaigns that deliver results. Your key responsibilities will include:

  • Mastering GoHighLevel or Instantly to:
  • Build and optimize high-performing sales funnels.
  • Design and implement targeted email marketing campaigns.
  • Create and manage automated workflows.
  • Integrate with CRMs and other marketing tools.
  • Leveraging AI tools (e.g., Jasper, Copy.ai, MidJourney) to enhance creativity, efficiency, and personalization.
  • Driving multi-channel marketing efforts (email, social media, SMS, etc.) with precision and innovation.
  • Analyzing campaign performance and delivering actionable insights to optimize results.
  • Troubleshooting technical issues within the marketing stack and ensuring seamless platform integrations.
  • Staying on top of the latest trends and technologies in marketing automation to keep us ahead of the curve.

What You’ll Bring

To excel in this role, you should have

  • Proven experience with GoHighLevel or Instantly—this is non-negotiable.
  • A solid understanding of marketing automation principles and best practices.
  • Hands-on experience with AI tools and their applications in marketing.
  • Proficiency in email marketing platforms, social media schedulers, CRM systems, and related tools.
  • Exceptional technical skills, including software integration and troubleshooting.
  • Strong analytical abilities with a data-driven mindset.
  • Outstanding communication and interpersonal skills.
  • The ability to stay organized and juggle multiple projects with ease.

Bonus Points If You Have

  • Experience with other marketing automation platforms like ActiveCampaign or Zapier.
  • API integration expertise.
