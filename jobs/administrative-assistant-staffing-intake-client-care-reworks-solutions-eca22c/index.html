About Able Minds ABA

Able Minds ABA is a growing healthcare organisation providing Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) therapy and support services for children with autism and their families .

The team is looking for a warm, organised, and proactive

Administrative Assistant

to join their growing remote team. This person will initially support both the Staffing/Recruiting team and the Intake/Client Care team , while also assisting with general administrative and clerical responsibilities.

As the organisation grows, there may be an opportunity for the role to develop into a more specialised position within either Staffing/Recruiting or Intake/Client Care.

About the Role

This is a varied role that combines administration, coordination, communication, and people-facing responsibilities .

You will work with both internal staff and families, so the ability to communicate clearly, stay organised, and create a friendly and positive experience is extremely important.

We are looking for someone who doesn't simply wait to be told what to do — someone who notices what needs attention, takes ownership, and is happy to jump in wherever support is needed.

Key Responsibilities

Staffing & Recruiting Support

Provide administrative support to the Staffing/Recruiting team.

Assist with candidate coordination, communication, scheduling, and follow-ups.

Help maintain accurate candidate information and records.

Assist with recruitment-related documentation and administrative processes.

Intake & Client Care Support

Provide administrative support to the Intake/Client Care team.

Assist with coordinating information and follow-ups with families.

Communicate professionally and warmly with families and internal staff.

Help ensure information is accurately recorded and followed up on.

General Administration

Assist with day-to-day clerical and administrative tasks.

Maintain accurate records, spreadsheets, and documentation.

Assist with scheduling and coordinating appointments or meetings.

Manage follow-ups and ensure outstanding tasks are completed.

Support different members of the team as required.

Take on ad-hoc administrative tasks and projects.

Requirements

Previous healthcare experience is required .

ABA experience is not necessary

and training can be provided.

Excellent spoken and written English.

Clear and professional communication that is easy for U.S.-based families and staff to understand.

Strong organisational skills and attention to detail.

Good communication and interpersonal skills.

Comfortable handling both administrative and people-facing responsibilities.

Strong computer literacy and comfortable learning new systems.

Able to manage multiple priorities and follow through on tasks.

Reliable, consistent, and able to work independently in a remote environment.

A team-oriented attitude and willingness to support colleagues.

Personality & Culture Fit

Personality is extremely important for this role.

Able Minds ABA has a close-knit and collaborative team and is looking for someone who will contribute positively to that environment.

The ideal person is

Warm and friendly

  • someone people naturally feel comfortable speaking with.

Kind and empathetic

  • particularly when communicating with families.

Easy to talk to

and approachable.

Proactive

  • notices what needs to be done without always waiting for instructions.

Reliable

  • follows through and can be trusted to get things done.

Team-oriented

  • enjoys supporting colleagues and working collaboratively.

Adaptable

  • comfortable switching between administrative and people-facing tasks.

Long-term focused

  • looking to build something with a company rather than frequently moving between jobs.

Long-Term Opportunity

Able Minds ABA is looking for someone who wants to become a long-term member of the team .

This isn't intended to be a short-term stepping-stone role. The successful candidate will have the opportunity to learn the business, build relationships with the team, and potentially move into a more specialised role as the organisation grows.

Software & Systems

Important Note

ABA experience is not required. Healthcare experience, strong communication skills, organisation, reliability, and the right personality are the key priorities for this position.
