About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a Digital Marketing / Performance Marketing Manager to plan, execute, and optimize performance-driven digital campaigns that generate leads, customers, and measurable revenue.

This is a hands-on, data-driven role for someone who understands how to turn advertising budgets into business results. You’ll manage paid campaigns, analyze performance, improve conversion efficiency, and continuously test audiences, creatives, messaging, and channels.

You’ll be accountable for metrics such as CPL, CAC, ROAS, conversion rates, and revenue impact — not just clicks and impressions.

What a Typical Day Looks Like

Your day may start by reviewing campaign spend, CPL, CAC, ROAS, and conversion performance across active channels.

You’ll optimize campaigns, research new audiences or keywords, review creative performance, collaborate on landing pages and messaging, verify conversion tracking, and launch new tests.

You’ll also prepare performance reports and identify opportunities to scale successful campaigns or improve underperforming ones.

In short

you turn paid media budgets into measurable leads, customers, and revenue through continuous testing and optimization.
