About the Company

Our client is an innovative and fast-growing organization driven by high-performing, decentralized teams across the globe. Their culture is built on transparency, high autonomy, and a relentless focus on results, giving team members the independence to excel without micromanagement. Partnering with our client offers independent contractors stable, competitive USD compensation, full remote flexibility, and a direct line of impact on company revenue and growth.

About the Role

As a Remote Sales Development Representative (SDR), you will be the spearhead of top-of-funnel pipeline growth, engaging high-value commercial prospects and qualifying business opportunities. In this role, you will leverage enterprise intelligence tools to research key decision-makers and execute targeted outbound phone campaigns across North America. Your primary mission is to convert cold prospects into high-intent sales meetings for dedicated outside sales representatives physically stationed on the ground in each target region. This is an exciting opportunity for an articulate, persistent sales hunter looking to earn competitive USD compensation while collaborating with a world-class sales organization.

Key Responsibilities

  • Execute High-Volume Outbound Cold Calling: Conduct 60+ targeted cold calls daily to key enterprise decision-makers across designated US sales territories to generate top-of-funnel sales momentum.
  • Secure High-Quality Sales Meetings: Book qualified, high-intent meeting appointments for regional outside sales representatives, ensuring seamless handoffs and complete pre-meeting briefing notes.
  • Leverage Market Intelligence Tools: Utilize CivicIQ and Google Alerts to monitor public sector triggers, municipal budgets, and breaking industry news, identifying high-priority sales triggers before competitors.
  • Conduct Advanced Prospecting: Leverage ZoomInfo and sales intelligence tools to build clean, targeted lead lists, map decision-maker hierarchies, and identify key stakeholders.
  • Maintain Rigorous Salesforce Hygiene: Track all outreach activities, call dispositions, contact updates, and meeting confirmations within Salesforce with 100% data integrity and zero missed follow-ups.
  • Drive Pitch Mastery & Objection Handling: Deliver compelling value propositions, navigate gatekeepers effectively, and overcome objections to convert neutral prospects into active sales opportunities.

Qualifications

  • Minimum 2+ years of experience in outbound phone sales, cold calling, or B2B SDR/BDR roles within enterprise or commercial sectors (Required).
  • Near-native / C2-level English proficiency with exceptional verbal articulation, active listening skills, and zero hesitation initiating phone conversations with C-level/VP executives (Required).
  • Demonstrated hands-on experience using CRM and intelligence platforms, specifically Salesforce and ZoomInfo (Required).
  • Familiarity or prior usage of CivicIQ or similar public intelligence/government tracking software and Google Alerts for trigger-based sales outreach (Preferred).
  • Proven track record of meeting or exceeding monthly outbound call volumes and qualified meeting setting quotas (Required).
  • Reliable remote setup , including a quiet, dedicated home office space, high-speed fiber internet, and a professional noise-canceling headset (Required).
  • Ability to work full-time US business hours (e.g., EST/CST) consistently from South Africa (Required).
