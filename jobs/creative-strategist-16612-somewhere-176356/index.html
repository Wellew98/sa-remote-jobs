eCommerce Creative Strategist

(Performance Ads – VSL, UGC, Scripts, Visuals)

Full-Time | Remote | Salary + Bonus Potential

We are a fast-growing direct-to-consumer (DTC) brand portfolio seeking a high-performance Creative Strategist who lives and breathes performance marketing. If you understand what makes a scroll-stopper convert, love scripting hooks that hit, and can direct UGC, statics, and motion assets with strategic precision — this role is for you.

What You’ll Do

As our Creative Strategist, you’ll lead end-to-end creative strategy for several of our top-performing SKUs — spanning categories such as wellness, personal care, pet health, supplements, and lifestyle accessories. You’ll turn insights into thumb-stopping, revenue-driving creative across Meta, YouTube, and additional performance channels.

Core Responsibilities

Creative Strategy

- Develop and lead multi-SKU ad creative across the full customer journey.

- Build, test, and refine messaging angles, frameworks, and formats that drive conversions.

- Use customer reviews, performance data, and market trends to guide prioritization of concepts and iterations.

Scripting & Concepting

Regularly script and develop concepts for

- VSLs (Video Sales Letters)

-

UGC and VO-led ads

- Short-form social scripts & hooks

- Static ad copy, angles, and concepts

- Motion briefs (GIFs, animations, micro-motions)

Each concept must be emotionally resonant, platform-native, and conversion-optimized.

Review & Feedback Loop

- Participate in creative review rounds with in-house production and editing teams.

- Provide clear, constructive feedback to elevate creative quality and ensure alignment with brand tone and performance goals.

Cross-Functional Collaboration

- Join weekly syncs with creative, production, and growth teams.

- Support creator casting and brief development (e.g., using influencer/UGC platforms such as Insense).

- Recommend workflow improvements, testing strategies, and creative iteration opportunities.

What Success Looks Like

- Consistent delivery of high-impact creative that improves ROAS and lowers CPA.

- Strong understanding of ecommerce trends, ad psychology, and performance-driven hooks.

- Efficient output with fast turnaround times without sacrificing creative quality.

- Ability to lean in during major campaign pushes, launches, or high-velocity testing sprints.

Your Toolkit

- Experience developing creative for DTC brands in wellness, personal care, pet, or similar verticals.

- Familiarity with performance metrics via Meta Ads Manager or equivalent analytics.

- Ability to script, concept, direct, and brief editors, animators, and UGC creators.

- Strong communication — responsive and collaborative across Slack and team calls.

Deliverables & Compensation

- 11 scripts per week , including:

-

Static image concepts

- ElevenLabs / UGC scripts

-

Iterations

- VSLs

-

Bonus potential tied to performance impact

- Creative tests may be requested prior to long-term engagement

- Flexibility to scale output quickly if the partnership hits its stride

Nice to Have

- Experience writing emotionally resonant, storytelling-driven copy

- Experience guiding or collaborating with creative teams

Next Steps

If you’re obsessed with performance-driven ad creative, want to scale with a growing brand portfolio, and love building creative that wins — we want to hear from you.

Please include links to ads, scripts, or VSL examples you’ve developed.
