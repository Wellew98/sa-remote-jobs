Job TitleClient Success Manager (Mental Health)

LocationRemote | Type: Full-Time

About Our Client

Our client is a mission-driven mental health organization committed to providing innovative and accessible therapeutic solutions. As they continue to grow, they are seeking a dedicated and empathetic leader to take ownership of their client experience and drive long-term retention.

The Opportunity

We are seeking an experienced and data-driven Client Success Manager to lead our client's retention efforts. This is a foundational role for a professional who is passionate about building systems that support long-term client wellness and engagement.

You will be responsible for developing and implementing the strategies that ensure clients not only have a great onboarding experience but also stay engaged and achieve their therapeutic goals. This role bridges intake, therapist communication, and client follow-up, ensuring no client falls through the cracks.

Key Responsibilities

-

Retention & Engagement Strategy

- Track client attendance, session frequency, and dormant accounts to proactively flag at-risk clients.

- Proactively follow up with clients who have missed or have not scheduled sessions.

- Coordinate with therapists to ensure continuity of care and a smooth client re-engagement process.

-

Client Tracking & Reporting

- Build and maintain retention dashboards using data from HubSpot, SimplePractice, and billing systems.

- Provide weekly reports on key metrics such as retention rates, client churn, and therapist performance, identifying trends and making recommendations to leadership.

-

Therapist Support & Accountability

- Partner with therapists to monitor caseloads, especially when a therapist transitions out of the practice.

- Collaborate with the Clinical Director to identify therapists with higher client drop-off rates and develop corrective strategies.

-

Client Experience & Communication

- Act as a key liaison between clients and the practice for questions related to scheduling, therapist transitions, or reassignments.

- Support the onboarding of new clients to ensure a seamless and positive first experience.

What You Bring

- 3+ years of experience in a client success, patient coordination, or healthcare operations role, with a strong preference for experience in the mental health or medical field .

- A strong analytical mindset, with the ability to identify patterns in client behavior and recommend data-driven solutions.

- Proficiency with CRM platforms ( HubSpot preferred ), EHR systems ( SimplePractice experience is a plus ), and data reporting.

- Excellent communication and relationship management skills, with a high degree of empathy.

- A highly organized, proactive, and detail-oriented approach to your work.
