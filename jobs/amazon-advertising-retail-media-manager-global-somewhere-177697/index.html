Amazon Advertising / Retail Media Manager

Location100% Remote (Open Worldwide)

Contract TypeIndependent Contractor Agreement

Compensation:   $2.000 - $5.000 Usd Monthly  Based on Experience

About the Role

We are seeking a highly skilled Amazon Advertising / Retail Media Manager to take full ownership of our Amazon advertising function and bring it in-house. This is a critical, high-impact role focused on replacing external agency support with a dedicated expert who can drive performance, efficiency, and long-term growth.

You will be responsible for managing and optimizing large-scale Amazon advertising campaigns across a diverse product catalog, while aligning closely with broader business objectives such as inventory planning, pricing, and promotions.

Key Responsibilities

Campaign Strategy & Execution

  • Build and manage full-funnel Amazon advertising strategies across a catalog of 50+ SKUs
  • Structure campaigns effectively, including keyword segmentation, match type allocation, and campaign hierarchy across Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, and Sponsored Display
  • Adapt campaign structures and strategies based on platform differences (Vendor Central vs. Seller Central)
  • Continuously optimize campaigns to improve performance, scalability, and efficiency

Search Term & Keyword Management

  • Conduct ongoing search term harvesting to identify new keyword opportunities
  • Implement and maintain robust negative keyword strategies to reduce waste and prevent campaign cannibalization
  • Establish clear thresholds and performance benchmarks to guide optimization decisions
  • Regularly audit and refine keyword structures to maximize relevance and return

Budget Management & Performance Optimization

  • Manage and allocate significant monthly advertising budgets across multiple campaign types
  • Monitor key performance metrics such as ACOS, TACOS, and ROAS, and communicate insights effectively to stakeholders
  • Make data-driven decisions on budget reallocation to maximize return and support growth objectives
  • Identify scaling opportunities while maintaining efficiency targets

Ranking Strategy & Product Lifecycle Management

  • Develop tailored advertising strategies for new product launches versus established ASINs
  • Support organic ranking improvements through strategic paid media efforts
  • Align advertising initiatives with broader catalog growth and lifecycle strategies

Retail & Cross-Functional Alignment

  • Collaborate with internal teams to align advertising with inventory levels, pricing strategies, and promotional calendars
  • Provide insights that inform retail decisions, ensuring advertising supports overall business performance
  • Coordinate closely with stakeholders to ensure seamless execution across all channels

Systems & Process Development

  • Build and document scalable processes, workflows, and best practices for in-house advertising operations
  • Analyze performance data and translate insights into actionable strategies
  • Continuously test, learn, and implement improvements across campaigns and systems

Qualifications & Requirements

  • Proven experience managing Amazon advertising for CPG or multi-SKU brands (50+ SKUs preferred)
  • Strong expertise across Sponsored Ads (Products, Brands, Display) and retail media strategy
  • Experience working within both Seller Central and/or Vendor Central environments
  • Demonstrated ability to manage and optimize large advertising budgets
  • Deep understanding of keyword strategy, search term analysis, and campaign structure
  • Strong analytical mindset with the ability to interpret performance metrics and drive decisions
  • Experience aligning advertising strategy with broader business functions such as inventory, pricing, and promotions
  • Highly organized, detail-oriented, and capable of operating independently in a remote environment

What We’re Looking For

This role is ideal for a hands-on operator and strategic thinker who thrives in ownership-driven environments. You should be comfortable building systems from the ground up, optimizing performance at scale, and making data-driven decisions that directly impact revenue and profitability.

If you’re excited about taking full control of Amazon advertising, replacing an agency, and building a high-performing in-house function — this is a unique opportunity to make a meaningful impact.
