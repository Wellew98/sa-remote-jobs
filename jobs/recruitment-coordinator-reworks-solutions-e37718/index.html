Recruitment Coordinator

Working Hours

9:00 AM–5:00 PM EST

Location

South Africa | Fully Remote

SalaryR26 500 - R30 000

About The Role

We are looking for a highly organised, responsive, and detail-oriented Recruitment Advertising Coordinator to manage job postings and recruitment advertising across a network of approximately 34–35 nursing home facilities in the United States.

This role will focus heavily on Indeed, including managing employer job postings, coordinating sponsored advertisements, monitoring vacancy activity, and ensuring each facility has the appropriate recruitment advertising in place to attract qualified applicants.

The successful candidate will work closely with facility administrators, corporate HR, and senior management to maintain accurate vacancy records, monitor applicant flow, and identify opportunities to improve recruitment advertising performance.

This is not simply a job-posting role. We are looking for someone who understands how Indeed works, particularly its employer advertising and sponsorship features, and who can take ownership of multiple vacancies and advertising campaigns simultaneously.

The client is open to considering candidates at different experience levels. While hands-on Indeed sponsorship experience is strongly preferred, candidates with solid recruitment coordination experience, strong administrative skills, and the ability to learn quickly will also be considered.

Key Responsibilities

1. Indeed Job Posting & Advertising Management

Manage and maintain job postings across Indeed for approximately 34–35 nursing home facilities.

Create, publish, update, and remove job advertisements as vacancies change.

Manage Indeed sponsored advertisements and authorised advertising budgets.

Monitor sponsored job activity and make adjustments to improve visibility and applicant flow.

Ensure job advertisements are accurate, current, and aligned with each facility's hiring requirements.

Identify vacancies that require additional advertising attention or adjustments.

Monitor posting performance and flag advertisements that are not generating sufficient interest.

2. Vacancy & Recruitment Tracking

Maintain a centralised vacancy tracker or spreadsheet covering all facilities.

Monitor active vacancies, job postings, sponsorship status, and advertising activity.

Keep records updated to reflect changes in hiring needs and recruitment progress.

Identify gaps, outdated postings, and vacancies requiring attention.

Ensure accurate reporting across all facilities.

Track advertising activity and provide regular updates on posting status and performance.

3. Facility & Stakeholder Coordination

Communicate with facility administrators to gather vacancy updates and confirm recruitment requirements.

Follow up on outstanding information and ensure job advertisements reflect current hiring needs.

Maintain regular communication with corporate HR and relevant stakeholders.

Escalate recruitment advertising issues, delays, or barriers to Todd and corporate HR.

Provide prompt responses and ensure requests are actioned within reasonable timeframes.

Build effective working relationships with administrators across multiple facilities.

4. Reporting & Recruitment Advertising Improvements

Monitor applicant flow and identify vacancies that are struggling to attract candidates.

Identify opportunities to improve job visibility and advertising effectiveness.

Recommend adjustments to job postings and sponsorship strategies where appropriate.

Report on active vacancies, advertising status, sponsorship activity, and recruitment barriers.

Proactively communicate challenges rather than waiting for issues to be raised.

Take ownership of the overall advertising workflow and ensure consistent follow-through.

Essential Requirements

Previous experience using Indeed, particularly the employer platform.

Familiarity with creating, managing, editing, and removing job advertisements.

Strong organisational and administrative skills.

Experience maintaining spreadsheets, trackers, or recruitment dashboards.

Excellent attention to detail and accuracy.

Strong written and verbal English communication skills.

Confident communicating with administrators, hiring managers, and other stakeholders.

Ability to manage multiple vacancies and competing priorities.

Responsive, proactive, and reliable with follow-ups.

Quick learner who can adapt to new processes and systems.

Ability to work independently and take ownership of responsibilities.

Advantageous Experience

Hands-on experience managing Indeed sponsored advertisements and recruitment budgets.

Experience monitoring, adjusting, and optimising sponsored job campaigns.

Previous recruitment coordination or talent acquisition experience.

Experience managing recruitment advertising across multiple locations or facilities.

Exposure to healthcare, nursing homes, senior living, or long-term care recruitment.

Experience improving applicant flow through job advertising strategies.

Familiarity with applicant tracking systems (ATS) and recruitment reporting tools.

Software & Systems

Indeed Employer Platform

Job postings, sponsored advertisements, campaign management, and vacancy advertising.

Google Sheets / Microsoft Excel

Vacancy tracking, advertising records, and reporting.

Recruitment Dashboards

Monitoring vacancies, posting activity, and sponsorship status.

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS): Advantageous.

Ideal Candidate Profile

The ideal candidate is someone who is highly organised, responsive, and comfortable managing a large volume of recruitment advertising activity across multiple locations.

They should understand that this role requires more than simply uploading job descriptions. They need to be able to monitor vacancies, manage sponsored advertisements, keep information accurate, communicate with stakeholders, and proactively identify when advertising is not delivering the desired results.

We are looking for someone who

Takes ownership of their workload and follows through without constant supervision.

Responds promptly to requests and communicates proactively.

Is comfortable managing multiple facilities and vacancies simultaneously.

Understands the importance of effective job advertising and applicant flow.

Can identify problems and take initiative to resolve them.

Is confident using Indeed and other recruitment platforms.

Can become fully autonomous after an initial onboarding and training period.
