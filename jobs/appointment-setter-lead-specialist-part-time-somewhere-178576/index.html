Appointment Setter / Lead Specialist — Part-time Location: Fully remote Type: Part-time to start (~20 hours/week, roughly 4–5 hours/day), with a clear path to full-time as the account grows Coverage window: CST Time Zone coverage  About the Role When a campaign goes out, most responses land within the first couple of hours. Some homeowners book directly on the calendar. Others text back with a question, reply to a voicemail, or stall out mid-conversation with our AI. Those are the moments that need a real person — someone who can step in, answer clearly, handle the back-and-forth, and get the appointment on the calendar. You will be the human layer on top of our automation. Working inside our platform, you will move between multiple client accounts, monitor incoming conversations, respond quickly and professionally, and convert interested homeowners into booked appointments. Most of this happens over text, with some live inbound calls from homeowners returning voicemail drops or asking to speak with a person. You are representing premium home improvement brands to their prospective customers, so how you write and how you carry a conversation directly reflects on them. Key Responsibilities Lead Response & Conversation Management - Monitor incoming conversations across multiple client accounts in our platform throughout your shift - Respond promptly to homeowner texts, questions, and replies generated by active marketing campaigns - Pick up and re-engage conversations where an AI thread has stalled or a homeowner has asked for a person - Keep responses clear, on-brand, and professional for each client you represent Appointment Setting - Guide interested homeowners to a booked appointment, primarily over text - Handle basic objections and questions to move the conversation toward a scheduled slot - Book directly into client calendars and confirm details accurately Inbound Call Handling - Take live inbound calls from homeowners returning ringless voicemail drops or requesting a representative - Answer questions and set appointments by phone when the conversation calls for it Account & Platform Coordination - Switch cleanly between client sub-accounts without mixing up context or brand voice - Keep notes and conversation records accurate so nothing falls through the cracks - Flag high-volume or high-priority situations as accounts scale Required Qualifications - Excellent written English — strong grammar, comprehension, and the ability to hold a natural, professional text conversation (this is the single most important requirement) - Prior experience setting appointments or working leads (SDR, appointment setter, inside sales, or similar) - Comfort managing conversations over text/SMS as the primary channel - Clear, professional spoken English for inbound calls (a light accent is fine; clarity and comprehension are what matter) - Ability to work the U.S. morning coverage window for your region and respond quickly during peak hours - Reliable internet, a quiet call environment, and a strong, self-directed work ethic Preferred Qualifications - Experience in a CRM / marketing automation platform, ideally GoHighLevel (or similar conversation-and-calendar systems) - Background in home improvement, home services, or another appointment-based sales environment - Experience managing multiple client accounts or brands at once - Track record of hitting appointment or conversion targets - Typing speed of 50+ WPM at high accuracy (all candidates are tested; 40+ WPM at 95% accuracy is the minimum) Ideal Candidate Profile - A strong, natural communicator in writing — the kind of person whose texts read like a sharp, helpful human, not a script - Quick on your feet in a conversation and comfortable handling questions and light objections - Organized enough to juggle several client accounts without losing the thread - Self-directed and dependable during your coverage window, with minimal hand-holding - Motivated by results and comfortable in a role where booked appointments are the scoreboard - Professional enough to represent large, established brands to their customers
