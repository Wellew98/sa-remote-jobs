About Huzzle

At Huzzle, we connect exceptional B2B sales professionals with top opportunities at leading companies across the UK, US, Canada, Europe, and Australia. Our clients include startups, digital agencies, and tech platforms in industries such as SaaS, MarTech, FinTech, and EdTech. Unlike an outsourcing agency, we place you directly with a client where you’re hired in-house as a valued member of their team.

About the Company

With a strong operational foundation and an expanding portfolio in premium markets such as coastal California, Florida, and Arizona, the company is now scaling its internal team to support continued growth and operational excellence.

Job Summary

We are a fast-growing performance lead generation company specializing in the legal vertical. We provide high-intent leads and signed retainers to personal injury law firms across the U.S. As we scale, we are looking for a high-level Client Operations Manager to own the entire lifecycle of a client relationship—from the moment a contract is signed to the successful, ongoing delivery of high-quality results.

This is a critical hire designed to remove operational bottlenecks. You will be the primary engine driving campaign launches, managing technical lead distribution, and serving as the authoritative point of contact for our law firm clients and wholesale partners. If you are a data-driven operator who thrives at the intersection of client success and technical performance marketing, this role is for you.

Key Responsibilities

Client & Account Ownership

Serve as the primary point of contact for law firm clients and partners; own the tone, quality, and frequency of all client-facing messaging.

Campaign Launch Management

Build the bridge between sales and delivery by driving new orders from paperwork to first lead, ensuring all criteria, delivery methods, and intake requirements are documented and met.

Technical Distribution

Act as the hands-on owner of LeadDistro, managing campaign builds, routing logic, filters, caps, integrations, and daily QA/QC.

Data-Driven Dispute Resolution

Independently manage lead quality disputes by reconciling delivery exports against client data, classifying defects, and deciding on credits or replacements based on established policy.

Team Leadership

Manage a small team including an Account Manager and Admin Support to ensure a high-velocity daily operating cadence where no task sits without an owner.

System Integrity

Maintain Monday.com as the live source of truth for all campaign statuses and keep SOPs current as products and processes evolve.

Supplier Coordination

Liaise with fulfillment partners and call centers to monitor volume, pacing, and quality, holding them accountable to strict specifications.

Industry Experience

4+ years in client-facing operations or account management within performance marketing, lead generation, or a call center environment.

Technical Proficiency

Professional experience administering lead distribution or call routing software (e.g., LeadDistro, Boberdoo, LeadsPedia, Phonexa, Trackdrive, etc.).

Professional Communication

Exceptional written English skills; you must be comfortable communicating with attorneys and law firm owners with high levels of polish.

Analytical Rigor

Advanced spreadsheet skills; ability to analyze large CSV exports to produce defensible, data-backed answers to client queries.

Leadership

Prior experience managing at least 1–2 direct reports.

Operational Demeanor

Calm under pressure, particularly during quality disputes, with a focus on leading with data rather than apologies.
