Sales-Driven Call Center Representative

We are seeking a high-energy, persuasive, and results-oriented Sales & Support Representative to join our team. In this role, you aren’t just answering questions—you are a brand ambassador and a closer. You will turn every customer inquiry into a sales opportunity by identifying needs and recommending the perfect solutions.

The ideal candidate is a "people person" with a competitive spirit who thrives on hitting targets and turning a "no" into a "yes" while maintaining a world-class customer experience.

Key Responsibilities

- Revenue Generation: Proactively identify opportunities to upsell or cross-sell products and services during inbound support calls.

- Consultative Selling: Actively listen to customer needs to recommend tailored solutions that drive sales growth and increase average order value.

- Lead Conversion: Follow up on inquiries via phone, email, and chat to convert potential leads into loyal, paying customers.

- Problem Resolution & Retention: Resolve customer concerns with a "save" mindset, turning negative experiences into opportunities for brand loyalty and renewed sales.

- CRM & Pipeline Management: Maintain meticulous records of customer interactions and sales progress in the CRM to ensure effective follow-ups and lead nurturing.

- Performance Targets: Consistently meet or exceed Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), including sales conversion rates , revenue targets , and CSAT scores.

Required Skills & Qualifications

Skill Category Requirements

Sales Acumen

Proven ability to handle objections, negotiate, and close a sale confidently.

Communication

Exceptional verbal and written skills; ability to build rapport quickly and "smile through the phone."

Tech Savvy

Proficiency in CRM software (Salesforce, Zendesk) and the ability to multitask across multiple digital platforms.

Resilience

A high degree of emotional intelligence; ability to handle rejection and remain motivated in a fast-paced environment.

Experience

High school diploma or equivalent. 1–2 years of experience in outbound/inbound sales, retail sales, or commission-based roles is highly preferred.

Reliability

Ability to work flexible shifts, including peak sales periods such as weekends or holidays.
