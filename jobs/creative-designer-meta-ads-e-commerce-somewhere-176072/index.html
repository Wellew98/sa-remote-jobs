Creative Designer – Meta Ads & E-Commerce

LocationRemote (South Africa or LATAM)

Compensation

  • South Africa: $2,000 – $2,500 USD/month
  • LATAM (Brazil, Colombia, Mexico): $2,000 – $3,000 USD/month

IndustryBoutique Growth Consultancy / Direct-to-Consumer E-Commerce

ScheduleFull-time, U.S. business hours overlap (10 AM – 6 PM EST)

About the Company

Our client is a boutique growth consultancy partnering with a select group of high-growth ecommerce brands generating between $2M–$20M in annual revenue. Our globally distributed team collaborates closely with direct-to-consumer brands to produce high-performing creative across paid media, email marketing, and ecommerce storefronts.

We operate in a fast-moving, execution-focused environment where strong creative output directly impacts growth, conversion, and revenue performance.

Role Overview

We are hiring a Creative Designer with strong experience supporting direct-to-consumer ecommerce brands through high-quality creative execution across Meta ads, email marketing, and ecommerce storefronts.

This is a highly execution-focused role centered around producing polished, performance-driven creative assets at scale. The ideal candidate combines strong visual design fundamentals with an understanding of performance marketing creative and modern AI-assisted workflows.

You will work across multiple ecommerce brands simultaneously, collaborating closely with media buyers, account managers, and marketing teams to deliver high-performing creative quickly and consistently.

Key Responsibilities

Meta Ad Creative Design

  • Design static and motion-based creatives for Meta platforms including Facebook and Instagram
  • Create high-converting direct response ad creatives aligned with campaign goals
  • Adapt creative styles across multiple ecommerce brands while maintaining brand consistency

Creative Production & Asset Execution

  • Produce high volumes of creative assets across multiple client accounts
  • Execute creative variations and iterations efficiently based on performance feedback
  • Maintain organized design workflows and asset management systems

Video Editing & Motion Design

  • Edit short-form ad creatives, UGC-style videos, motion graphics, and promotional content
  • Produce engaging video assets optimized for paid social performance
  • Support rapid creative testing and iteration cycles

Email Marketing Design (Klaviyo)

  • Design email marketing assets and campaigns within Klaviyo
  • Ensure responsive layouts, clean formatting, and visual consistency
  • Collaborate with marketing teams to support campaign launches and promotional calendars

Shopify Design Support

  • Assist with Shopify storefront design updates
  • Create homepage banners, landing page assets, promotional graphics, and product visuals
  • Support ecommerce merchandising initiatives through visual design execution

AI-Assisted Creative Workflow

  • Utilize AI tools to accelerate creative ideation, asset generation, editing, and production workflows
  • Incorporate modern AI creative tools into daily execution processes to improve efficiency and output quality

Collaboration & Project Management

  • Manage design requests and project tickets within Notion or similar project management systems
  • Collaborate cross-functionally with media buyers, copywriters, and account managers
  • Ensure all deliverables are completed accurately and on schedule

Required Hard Skills

  • Proven experience designing Meta ads for direct-to-consumer ecommerce brands
  • Prior experience working within a DTC ecommerce agency environment strongly preferred
  • Strong portfolio showcasing paid social creative work
  • Experience with:
  • Static ad design
  • Video editing and motion graphics
  • Ecommerce creative production workflows
  • Typography and visual composition
  • Proficiency with:
  • Adobe Creative Suite
  • Figma
  • Shopify
  • Klaviyo
  • Notion
  • Slack
  • Strong familiarity with AI creative tools including:
  • ChatGPT
  • Claude
  • Gemini
  • Manus
  • Higgsfield
  • Nano Banana or similar AI image/video generation platforms
  • Ability to manage multiple projects and deadlines simultaneously
  • Strong English communication skills

Soft Skills

  • Highly organized and detail-oriented
  • Fast and reliable executor
  • Comfortable operating in fast-paced agency environments
  • Strong ownership mentality and accountability
  • Collaborative and adaptable team player
  • Comfortable receiving feedback and iterating quickly
  • Proactive communicator in remote work environments

About the Candidate

The ideal candidate is a high-output creative executor who understands how ecommerce creative impacts performance. They are comfortable working across multiple brands, producing assets quickly without sacrificing quality or consistency.

This person thrives in execution-heavy environments, embraces AI-assisted workflows, and enjoys working closely with performance marketing teams to continuously improve creative output.

Why This Role Matters

This role is central to maintaining the speed, quality, and consistency of creative production across multiple ecommerce brands. Strong creative execution directly impacts ad performance, customer engagement, conversion rates, and overall revenue growth.

The right candidate will become a critical contributor to the agency’s ability to scale creative production efficiently while maintaining high design standards.
