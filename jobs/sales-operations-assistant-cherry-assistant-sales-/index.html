Overview

We are hiring a Sales Operations Assistant for a founder-led small business in the residential and commercial cleaning industry. This is a full-time remote role for a United States (Eastern Time) based client. The role is focused on lead intake and conversion, scheduling/dispatch, recruiting coordination, and CRM/data management to keep daily operations running smoothly. The ideal candidate is clear and confident on the phone, organized, fast-paced, detail-oriented, and experienced in home services operations, with the ability to follow SOPs and work independently.

Key Responsibilities

  • Own speed-to-lead: respond to new inquiries within minutes via phone, text, and email; qualify prospects, prepare quotes, and convert to booked jobs
  • Schedule and dispatch jobs in BookingKoala (or similar); manage calendar changes, confirmations, reschedules, and service reminders
  • Coordinate with field teams/cleaners to ensure on-time arrival, coverage, and route efficiency; manage day-of changes and customer updates
  • Maintain the sales pipeline and contact records in GoHighLevel (or similar); perform ongoing data cleanup and list management
  • Migrate data from spreadsheets into the CRM; keep all systems in sync and up to date
  • Generate daily/weekly reports on leads, conversion, jobs scheduled, and revenue KPIs
  • Support recruiting: post job ads, screen resumes, conduct initial phone screens, schedule interviews, and move candidates through the hiring pipeline
  • Communicate with property management portals as needed; enter work orders and update job statuses
  • Document processes and follow SOPs; propose practical improvements as you learn the business
  • Provide general administrative support and customer service as required

Required Qualifications

  • 2+ years in sales operations, dispatcher/coordinator, or administrative support within a home services environment (cleaning, HVAC, plumbing, landscaping, etc.)
  • Proven experience handling inbound/outbound calls and converting leads to bookings
  • Hands-on experience scheduling jobs and coordinating field teams
  • Strong English communication skills (clear, confident, and professional on calls and in writing)
  • Demonstrated ability to follow SOPs, manage priorities, and work independently during US business hours

Preferred Qualifications

  • Direct experience in residential/commercial cleaning operations
  • Working knowledge of BookingKoala, Housecall Pro, Jobber, or similar service management software
  • Experience with GoHighLevel or similar CRMs and basic workflow/automation setup
  • Experience supporting recruiting for field service roles
  • Familiarity with US geography and the ability to quickly learn local service areas

Required Skills & Tools

  • Google Workspace (Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets)
  • BookingKoala or similar (Housecall Pro, Jobber)
  • CRM such as GoHighLevel (or similar)
  • VoIP/softphone systems (e.g., Quo, OpenPhone, Dialpad)
  • Slack or similar team communication tools
  • Intermediate spreadsheet/reporting skills

Schedule & Pay

  • Full-time position; Monday–Friday, approximately 9:00 AM–5:00 PM Eastern Time (flexibility for occasional Saturday coverage as the business scales)
  • Fully remote role for a United States (Eastern Time) based client
  • Pay ranges from USD $5.00–$8.00 per hour (starting around $6.50, depending on experience)
  • Includes structured onboarding, SOPs, training, and clear growth potential with milestone-based increases

System Requirements

  • Internet speed of at least 20 Mbps upload and download
  • Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher
  • 8 GB of RAM or higher
  • Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer
  • HD 720p webcam
  • Headset with microphone

Benefits

  • Competitive pay rates
  • Consistent hours and predictable workload
  • Fully remote work with long-term potential
  • Direct collaboration with a founder-led team, making a real impact
  • Supportive and inclusive work environment
  • Opportunity to grow alongside a rapidly scaling cleaning company
