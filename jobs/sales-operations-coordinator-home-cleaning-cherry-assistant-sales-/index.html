Overview

We are hiring a Sales & Operations Coordinator (Home Cleaning) for an owner-operated small business in the residential cleaning industry. This is a Full-Time remote role for a US-based client (Central Time).

The role is focused on lead response and conversion, scheduling and dispatch, customer service/retention, and light marketing and hiring support. The ideal candidate is customer-obsessed, persuasive yet empathetic, highly organized, tech-savvy, and a fast learner with clear, neutral English communication.

Key Responsibilities

  • Own speed-to-lead: respond to new inquiries via phone/SMS/email within minutes to maximize conversions
  • Qualify prospects, provide estimates/quotes, and close one-time and recurring cleaning bookings
  • Manage calendars and schedules in BookingKoala (or similar): create jobs, assign teams, handle reschedules/cancellations, and optimize utilization
  • Handle inbound calls, texts, and voicemails; manage VoIP inboxes to ensure no lead or customer message is missed
  • Send confirmations, reminders, and follow-ups; nurture warm leads and re-engage past customers
  • Deliver exceptional customer service: de-escalate complaints, coordinate re-cleans or adjustments, and process refunds/discounts within guidelines to protect reviews and retention
  • Maintain accurate CRM and scheduling records, updating lead stages, notes, tags, and tasks.
  • Produce daily/weekly KPI reports (e.g., speed-to-lead, close rate, bookings, revenue, CSAT/retention)
  • Assist with basic marketing tasks: simple social posts, review requests, email/SMS campaigns, and reactivation sequences (e.g., in GoHighLevel)
  • Provide as-needed hiring support: post roles, screen applicants, coordinate interviews, and assist with onboarding
  • Collaborate with the owner to improve SOPs and document processes; suggest workflow enhancements
  • Support payment collection and resolve billing questions professionally and promptly

Required Qualifications

  • 2+ years in sales & operations/customer service for a US-based business, ideally in home services/ residential cleaning
  • Excellent phone communication with clear, neutral English; confident closer with high empathy
  • Hands-on experience with scheduling software and CRMs to manage leads, quotes, and jobs
  • Strong problem-solving and de-escalation skills with a customer-first mindset
  • High attention to detail; proficient typing, data entry, and documentation
  • Comfortable working Monday–Friday during US Central business hours
  • Reliable remote work setup and ability to follow SOPs while working independently

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in residential cleaning or field service operations
  • Basic automation experience (e.g., simple workflows or Zapier)

Required Skills & Tools

  • CRM: GoHighLevel (or HubSpot/Salesforce)
  • Scheduling: BookingKoala (or Jobber/Housecall Pro/Launch27)
  • Lead Sourcing: Thumbtack & Google LSA
  • Productivity: Google Workspace (Meet, Gmail, Calendar, Sheets, Docs)
  • VoIP and texting tools (e.g., OpenPhone, RingCentral)

Schedule & Pay

  • Full-Time position; 8 hours/day, Monday–Friday, core hours aligned to 8:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. US Central Time (some flexibility; occasional Saturday coverage by prior arrangement)
  • Fully remote role for a US-based client
  • Pay ranges from R18,000 - R25,000 per month depending on skills and experience
  • Includes structured onboarding, SOPs, performance-based incentives, and growth potential

System Requirements

  • Internet speed of at least 20 Mbps upload and download
  • Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher
  • 8 GB of RAM or higher
  • Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer
  • HD 720p webcam
  • Headset with microphone

Benefits

  • Competitive pay rates
  • Consistent hours and predictable workload
  • Fully remote work with long-term potential
  • Direct collaboration with the Founder, making a real impact
  • Supportive and inclusive work environment
  • Opportunity to grow alongside a fast-growing home cleaning company

If you are a highly motivated, sales-driven professional who thrives in a fast-paced, remote environment and is eager to make an impact in a growing residential home cleaning company, we’d love to hear from you.
