Staff Accountant

Working arrangement

This is a remote role. Hours should align with Pacific time, or fall within one to two hours of it. There is room to grow into a longer-term, full-time position.

A US-based manufacturer is hiring a remote Staff Accountant to be the hands-on support for its plant controller on month-end close, reconciliations, inventory, and cost accounting.

About the role

You will help keep the plant's books accurate, complete, and on time so the controller and plant leadership can make decisions on numbers they trust. You will work closely with production, purchasing, and materials teams, flagging cost and inventory issues early. Success means a clean, on-time close every month and balance sheet accounts that tie out.

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Accounting or Finance, or equivalent progressive accounting experience
  • 2+ years of general accounting experience, including month-end close and balance sheet reconciliations
  • Hands-on experience with an ERP system
  • Intermediate Excel skills, including lookups, pivot tables, and reconciliation schedules
  • Working knowledge of U.S. GAAP and accrual accounting
  • Able to work Pacific time zone hours, or within one to two hours of Pacific time

Preferred Qualifications

  • Manufacturing plant experience, ideally in automotive or industrial components
  • Experience with inventory, standard costing, and physical inventory or audit support
  • Progress toward a CPA or CMA designation

Responsibilities

  • Prepare and post accruals, prepaids, depreciation, payroll, and reclass entries to deliver an accurate, on-time month-end close
  • Reconcile cash, inventory, fixed assets, accrued liabilities, and intercompany accounts each month, and clear reconciling items
  • Support cycle counts and the annual physical inventory, reconcile counts to the general ledger, and maintain scrap and obsolescence reserves
  • Analyze standard-to-actual variances for material, labor, and overhead, and flag trends to the controller
  • Maintain accounts payable controls, including three-way match, vendor setup, and payment run review, resolving exceptions with Purchasing and Receiving
  • Produce recurring plant reports on labor and overtime, scrap, freight, cost center spending, and KPIs
  • Support the annual budget and monthly forecast, including headcount and salary labor spend
  • Maintain documented internal controls and audit-ready schedules for external audit, fixed assets, capital projects, and sales/use and personal property tax filings

Who thrives in this role

  • Detail-obsessed, the kind of person who reconciles to the cent and sleeps better for it
  • Calm under fixed month-end deadlines and quarter-close pressure
  • Comfortable asking clarifying questions rather than guessing
  • Able to explain numbers plainly to colleagues outside finance
  • Discreet and trustworthy with confidential payroll and financial information
