About The Role

CoverGo is hiring an ISMS Manager to own our internal and external audit programme and give management independent assurance on the state of our cybersecurity compliance and risk management. The Security team is small and growing, and works AI-first: everyone uses AI to automate their own work and to scale security across a large engineering organisation.

What You Will Do

Own the internal and external audit programme, including ISO 27001 and SOC 2 certification and surveillance audits.

Set up and run an internal audit framework: audit plan, scoping, control testing, findings and follow-up.

Design custom control frameworks that establish clear ownership and accountability in a matrix organisation, where several teams share responsibility for the same systems.

Define technical controls for the engineering organisation and test that they operate as designed.

Report to management on compliance status, control effectiveness and risk, with clear findings and named owners.

Maintain the risk register and drive remediation through to closure.

Prepare audit evidence for customer security reviews and due diligence.

Use AI to automate evidence collection, control testing and reporting.

What We Need

3+ years in information security governance, risk and compliance, or audit.

Hands-on experience leading ISO 27001 and SOC 2 certifications through to a successful audit.

A track record of building internal audit frameworks with custom controls, not just adopting a standard template.

Experience designing control frameworks that assign accountability across a matrix organisation.

Enough technical depth to define and test controls for engineering teams, such as access management, change management, secure development and cloud configuration.

Practical experience using AI to automate compliance and audit workflows.

Clear writing for both engineers and executives.

Nice to have

ISO 27001 Lead Auditor or CISA certification.

Experience in insurance, financial services or another regulated SaaS environment.
