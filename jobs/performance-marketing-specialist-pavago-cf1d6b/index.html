About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a Performance Marketing Specialist to manage, optimize, and scale paid advertising campaigns across Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, and other digital channels .

This is a hands-on paid media role focused on measurable growth and acquisition performance — not simply campaign coordination.

You’ll own campaign execution, budget optimization, conversion tracking, A/B testing, attribution, and performance reporting, with a focus on improving ROAS, CPA, CPL, and conversion rates .

You’ll collaborate with creative, content, sales, and marketing teams to turn advertising spend into qualified leads, customers, and revenue.

If you’re a data-driven marketer who enjoys testing, optimizing, and scaling campaigns, this role is a strong fit.

What a Typical Day Looks Like

You might start by reviewing campaign dashboards, spend, conversions, CPA, CPL, and ROAS across active accounts.

From there, you’ll optimize budgets and bids, pause underperforming ads, scale winning campaigns, analyze audience performance, and launch new tests.

Throughout the day, you may collaborate with creative teams on new ad concepts, review landing-page performance, troubleshoot tracking issues, and analyze lead or revenue quality with sales and marketing teams.

In short

you turn paid advertising budgets into measurable, scalable business growth.
