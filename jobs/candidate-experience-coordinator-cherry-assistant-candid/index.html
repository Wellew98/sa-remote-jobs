We are hiring a Candidate Experience Coordinator for a fast-paced, high-volume recruiting client operating within the recruitment and talent acquisition industry.

This is a full-time, fully remote position with a U.S.-based company. The role is ideal for someone who is highly organized, proactive, confident in communicating with people, and comfortable working in a structured, performance-driven environment.

You do not necessarily need previous recruitment experience to apply. We welcome candidates from backgrounds such as retail, customer service, sales, hospitality, call centres, administration, and other roles involving regular customer interaction, outbound communication, or high-volume operations. You'll support multiple recruiting teams by contacting applicants, managing candidate pipelines, coordinating interviews, and ensuring candidates remain engaged throughout the hiring process. If you're comfortable speaking with people, working towards daily targets, managing multiple priorities, and learning new systems, this could be an excellent opportunity to transition into the recruitment industry.

Key Responsibilities

  • Provide recruiting support to assigned markets when primary recruiters or administrators are unavailable or require additional assistance.
  • Conduct high-volume outbound candidate outreach through phone calls, text messages, and email.
  • Manage candidate pipelines and maintain consistent follow-up with applicants throughout the hiring process.
  • Schedule, coordinate, and confirm interviews while ensuring a positive candidate experience.
  • Contact candidates ahead of scheduled interviews to confirm attendance, answer questions, and address potential concerns.
  • Support interview retention by maintaining regular communication and reducing candidate no-shows.
  • Manage calendars, send Zoom invitations, coordinate interview schedules, and respond to candidate inquiries.
  • Maintain accurate candidate records and document all interactions within the client's applicant tracking system (ATS).
  • Monitor recruiting activity and performance metrics, identifying opportunities to improve candidate engagement and market performance.
  • Collaborate with recruiters, administrators, and market leadership to support hiring goals.
  • Maintain professional and timely communication across multiple platforms.
  • Consistently meet daily activity targets while maintaining accuracy and quality in all recruiting activities.

Required Qualifications

  • At least 2 years of experience in recruitment, talent acquisition, customer service, retail, sales, hospitality, call centres, administration, or another high-volume, people-focused environment.
  • At least 1 year of experience working remotely.
  • Fluent English with excellent verbal and written communication skills.
  • Confident speaking with unfamiliar people and comfortable conducting frequent outbound calls.
  • Strong organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously.
  • Comfortable working towards daily activity targets, KPIs, and measurable performance expectations.
  • Strong attention to detail and the ability to maintain accurate records.
  • Ability to follow structured processes while using initiative to resolve routine issues.
  • Computer literate and comfortable learning new software and digital platforms.
  • Reliable, proactive, and able to work independently in a fast-paced environment.
  • Based in South Africa with availability to work full-time U.S. business hours, Monday to Friday.

Previous recruitment experience is beneficial but not essential. We are particularly interested in candidates who can demonstrate strong communication skills, consistency, resilience, and the ability to manage high volumes of daily interactions.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Previous experience in outbound sales, telesales, customer service, retail, hospitality, or high-volume administration.
  • Experience working towards daily or weekly performance targets.
  • Familiarity with Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), CRM platforms, or candidate management databases.
  • Experience coordinating appointments, managing calendars, or handling high volumes of customer enquiries.
  • Comfortable using Zoom, Slack, WhatsApp, Discord, and other digital communication platforms.
  • Previous experience supporting multiple teams, departments, or operational functions.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to work independently.

Required Skills & Tools

  • Phone and VoIP calling systems for high-volume outbound communication.
  • Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) for candidate tracking and pipeline management.
  • CRM platforms and digital databases.
  • Zoom or similar video conferencing software.
  • Email and calendar management tools, including Gmail, Outlook, and Calendly.
  • Slack, WhatsApp, Discord, or similar communication platforms.
  • Fast and accurate typing skills, with the ability to update systems and record candidate interactions in real time.

Performance Expectations

This is a performance-driven role with clear daily and weekly activity expectations.

Successful candidates will be expected to

  • Maintain a minimum 50% Call List Completion Rate for assigned markets.
  • Maintain a minimum 50% Interview Retention Rate .
  • Complete a minimum of 200 outbound phone calls per day .
  • Maintain timely, professional communication with candidates and internal teams.
  • Accurately document recruiting activities, candidate interactions, and follow-ups.
  • Demonstrate consistency, accountability, and ownership of assigned recruiting responsibilities.

Schedule & Pay

  • Full-time position, Monday to Friday.
  • U.S. business hours (8:00 AM – 5:00 PM EST/CST/PST depending on assignment).
  • Fully remote role supporting a U.S.-based company.
  • Monthly compensation: R12,000 – R15,000 .

Additional earning opportunities include

  • Weekly performance incentives of up to 50% additional earnings.
  • Stable income and consistent working hours.
  • Annual performance reviews and career progression opportunities.

System Requirements

  • Fibre internet connection with a minimum speed of 25 Mbps.
  • Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher.
  • Minimum 8 GB RAM.
  • Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer.
  • HD 720p webcam.
  • Headset with a microphone.

Technical specification verification is required before proceeding with your application.

Benefits

  • Fully remote, stable full-time employment.
  • Opportunity to transition into the recruitment and talent acquisition industry.
  • Exposure to U.S.-based business operations.
  • No daily commute and location flexibility.
  • Performance-driven environment with measurable earning opportunities.
  • Consistent working hours and structured workflows.
  • Supportive and collaborative recruiting team.
  • Opportunities to develop recruitment, communication, and operational skills.
  • Long-term career development potential.

Who Should Apply? Perhaps you've worked in retail, sales, hospitality, customer service, or another fast-paced environment where you regularly interacted with people, handled enquiries, managed competing priorities, or worked towards daily targets.

We're looking for someone who is confident picking up the phone, comfortable following up with people, organized enough to manage multiple ongoing conversations, and motivated to deliver consistent results.
