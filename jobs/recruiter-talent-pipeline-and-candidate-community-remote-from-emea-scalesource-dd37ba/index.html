Recruiter - Talent Pipeline and Candidate Community (remote from EMEA)

This job is open to candidates working remotely from anywhere Schedule:  Full-time, Monday to Friday | US business hours Compensation:  $1,250 USD/month for the first 90 days / $1,500 USD/month after that. About the Role Yes, we post jobs, screen applications, and run interviews. But the real business we are in is readiness. A client tells us on a Tuesday that their bookkeeper resigned, and the only thing that matters is whether we already know four people who could do that job well. Most recruiters start from zero when a role opens. Two weeks disappear into sourcing before the first real conversation happens, the client waits, and the shortlist ends up being whoever was available rather than whoever was best. We are not building that. We are building a live, warm, continuously vetted talent community across customer support, executive assistance, virtual reception, accounting and bookkeeping, and sales operations, so that when a seat opens we are presenting in days. If you need a requisition before you will talk to a candidate, this is not the role. If you have already run a bench, kept it warm, and watched a client's jaw drop at how fast you moved, you may be exactly who we need. The Big Picture The standard is simple: when the role opens, the people are already there. We do not need someone who says "I will start sourcing as soon as the requisition is approved." We need someone who says: "I have nine vetted virtual receptionists I have spoken to in the last three weeks, four can start inside ten days, two of them I would put in front of a client tomorrow, and here is why the other five are still worth keeping warm." That is the difference. Role Mission Own a full recruiting desk and the talent community behind it. Fill live roles fast, and build the bench that makes the next ones faster. You are responsible for the pipeline existing before anyone asks for it. What You Will Own 1. The talent community The reason we are hiring this seat. Proactively source, interview, and vet candidates for roles that are not open yet Segment the community by role, readiness, and seniority, and keep the record current Run a real re-engagement cadence so candidates stay warm, honest about their availability, and still interested Set and hold a freshness standard per pool, and know what has gone stale before someone asks A bench nobody touches is not a bench. It is a list of people who already took another job. 2. Full-cycle hiring across multiple verticals Live roles, several at once, closed in 14 days. Write postings that attract the right people and repel the wrong ones Screen hard and early, structure your interviews, and defend your shortlist with evidence Partner with hiring managers on the real bar, not the wish list Learn unfamiliar roles quickly and get to a defensible bar within days Better shortlists, not bigger ones. 3. The candidate experience Including, and especially, the people we reject. Respond to every candidate within 24 business hours, every time Give specific, honest, useful feedback to candidates who reach interview stage Keep rejected candidates warm enough that they apply again and send us their friends The benchmark: would this person still refer a friend to us after we told them no? 4. Making the function better You are not just working the system. You are improving it. Own at least one recruitment improvement initiative that changes how other recruiters work Use AI and automation to remove manual steps from your own desk first Bring your data to the weekly scorecard and argue from it Strong opinions, held loosely, backed by numbers. Targets Time to fill: 14 days or less (from requisition received to candidate start date) Time to present candidates to a new client: 24 hours Candidates invited for interviews after presenting to a client: 75% Reply to every incoming application within 24 hours ScaleSource is hiring 100+ new joiners through the end of 2026. You will carry a real share of that. What Success Looks Like at 90 Days You have a live, segmented talent community you can speak to from memory, and you have filled at least one role out of it in under a week You have hired 30+ candidates, with full support to get there Hiring managers trust your shortlists enough to stop second-guessing them Candidates, including rejected ones, are telling us the process was better than anywhere else they applied You own one internal improvement initiative that is already in use Core Values and Mindset Have Fun You will talk to people all day, in a lot of countries, about their lives and their work. If that energises you, this job is a good time. If it drains you, it will show in the first month. Try New Things Fast New channel, new AI tool, new screening format: test it this week, measure it, keep it or kill it. We would rather you run five small experiments than write one long proposal. Best in the World Anybody can send a client ten CVs. That is not a skill, it is a surrender. The bar is a shortlist so good the hiring manager struggles to choose. Put in the Work Warm benches are built by unglamorous, repeated outreach when nothing is on fire. The recruiters who win here do the passive work in the weeks when there is no pressure to. Hold Accountability Your numbers are visible weekly. When a role slips, you name it first, with the reason and the fix.
