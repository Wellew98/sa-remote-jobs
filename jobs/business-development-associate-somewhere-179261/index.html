About the Opportunity

Our client, a US-based commercial equipment dealership, is looking for a Business Development Specialist to lead customer reactivation and drive new revenue across its parts and service departments. The business sells commercial-grade equipment to construction, landscaping, and demolition companies, owner-operators, municipalities, and commercial accounts.

The company maintains a substantial database of past accounts and key market contacts. This role is dedicated to proactively building relationships, identifying latent service and parts needs, and winning back high-value commercial accounts. Getting started now is critical to pipeline development ahead of seasonal shifts in commercial equipment maintenance cycles.

The ideal candidate is a proactive business development professional who communicates with executive polish, thrives on outbound outreach, and brings a systematic approach to pipeline management. High resilience, strong commercial acumen, and relentless pursuit of targets matter most.

Duties

Strategic Outbound Development & Account Reactivation

  • Target and re-engage dormant commercial accounts, identifying equipment usage patterns and upcoming maintenance or parts requirements.
  • Conduct consultative discovery calls with business owners, fleet managers, and buyers to position our client as their primary parts and service partner.
  • Articulate clear value propositions, service agreements, and commercial incentives to drive account reactivation.
  • Prioritize outreach based on high-margin locations and key geographic targets.

Opportunity Handoff & Pipeline Management

  • Qualified sales/service opportunities: warm-transfer or schedule introductions directly with regional parts and service advisors.
  • Provide comprehensive account briefs and detailed CRM notes to local service teams to ensure seamless onboarding.
  • Maintain ongoing touchpoints with dealership location managers to ensure pipeline conversion and lead follow-through.

Multi-Channel Sales Campaigns

  • Execute targeted multi-touch campaigns (phone, email, and SMS) using the company’s CRM platform.
  • A/B test messaging strategies across different customer segments (e.g., municipalities vs. private contractors) to optimize response rates.

Market Intelligence & Reporting

  • Methodically log call activity, conversion milestones, and account revenue impact in the CRM.
  • Analyze key account churn factors, competitive dynamics, and lost-sales trends to inform regional strategy.
  • Provide weekly pipeline forecasts and activity metrics to executive leadership.

Qualifications

  • Proven track record in business development, B2B outbound sales, or account management.
  • Exceptional spoken and written English, with the confidence to engage business owners, operations managers, and municipal directors.
  • Demonstrated ability to navigate high call volumes, cold outreach, and complex sales objections.
  • Highly organized, structured, and metrics-driven approach to pipeline tracking.
  • Available for a full-time, long-term remote role matching US Central Time.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Prior experience in commercial equipment, heavy machinery, automotive, marine, or industrial dealership B2B sales.
  • Conversational or business Spanish skills.
  • Advanced proficiency in CRM platforms, cloud phone systems, and Microsoft Excel.
