About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a Sales Development Representative (SDR) to generate qualified pipeline through cold calling, outbound email, LinkedIn prospecting, and consistent follow-up .

This is a high-activity, performance-driven sales role. You’ll identify prospects, initiate conversations, handle objections, and book qualified meetings for Account Executives.

If you’re confident with cold outreach, motivated by targets, and know how to turn prospecting activity into meetings, this role is for you.

What You’ll Own

Outbound Prospecting & Lead Generation

Build targeted prospect lists using

LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Apollo, ZoomInfo, and similar tools

Research companies, decision-makers, and prospect needs

Execute multi-channel outreach across

email, LinkedIn, and phone

Personalize messaging to improve response and conversion rates

Generate qualified meetings and pipeline opportunities

Cold Calling & Outreach

Make 30–40+ cold calls daily

Execute

60–100 outbound touchpoints per day

Confidently navigate gatekeepers and handle objections

Book qualified meetings for Account Executives

Maintain disciplined outreach and follow-up cadences

CRM & Pipeline Management

Maintain accurate records in HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho CRM, or similar platforms

Log calls, emails, conversations, notes, and next steps

Track lead stages and follow-up activity

Maintain clean, accurate pipeline data

Outreach Optimization

A/B test email copy, subject lines, CTAs, and outreach sequences

Monitor reply rates, conversion rates, and meetings booked

Identify what messaging and channels perform best

Continuously improve prospecting effectiveness

Requirements

1–3+ years of experience in SDR, BDR, outbound sales, appointment setting, or lead generation

Strong written and verbal English communication

Confident objection-handling and prospecting skills

Organized and disciplined with follow-ups

Comfortable working U.S. business hours

Nice to Have
