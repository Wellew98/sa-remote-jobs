Overview

We are hiring a Contract Administration Coordinator for a B2B supplier company in the Commercial Interiors/Construction industry. This is a part-time (10 hours per week) role for a US based company. This role is ideal for someone who is proactive, detail-oriented, a self-starter, tech-savvy, process-driven, and comfortable navigating varying requirements across multiple stakeholders.

Key Responsibilities

  • Review contract, compliance, and onboarding requirements from multiple general contractors (GCs) and create clear submission checklists per project
  • Collect, organize, and submit required documentation (e.g., vendor forms, W-9, pay applications, SOVs, lien waivers, certificates requested from internal teams)
  • Complete vendor registration and onboarding tasks in GC/vendor portals; upload forms, certifications, and supporting documents accurately
  • Assist with schedules of values (SOV), change orders, and progress billing submissions per contract terms and deadlines
  • Track approvals, due dates, and portal tasks; maintain logs and follow up with internal and external stakeholders until completion
  • Coordinate with internal teams to source missing information and resolve discrepancies; escalate blockers promptly
  • Maintain an organized digital filing system and ensure version control and audit readiness
  • Standardize templates/SOPs and recommend process improvements to increase speed and accuracy
  • Provide status updates to the account team lead and prepare summary reports as needed

Required Qualifications

  • 2+ years of experience in administrative support, contract administration, or project coordination (construction/commercial interiors or related B2B environment preferred)
  • Demonstrated success managing documentation across multiple stakeholders and portals
  • Excellent written and verbal English communication skills
  • Strong attention to detail, organization, and follow-through under tight deadlines
  • Tech-savvy with the ability to quickly learn new web portals and client-specific processes

Preferred Qualifications

  • Prior experience supporting US-based teams and working across time zones
  • Familiarity with schedules of values, progress billing, and lien waiver processes
  • Experience with GC/vendor portals (e.g., Textura, Ariba, Coupa, Procore) and pay application workflows
  • Experience in administrative support, contract administration, or project coordination (construction/commercial interiors or related B2B environment preferred)
  • Demonstrated success managing documentation across multiple stakeholders and portals
  • Strong attention to detail, organization, and follow-through under tight deadlines

Required Skills & Tools

  • Microsoft 365 (Excel, Word, Outlook) and PDF tools (e.g., Adobe Acrobat or similar)
  • Cloud file management (e.g., SharePoint, OneDrive, Google Drive)
  • Comfort navigating client/vendor web portals and forms

Schedule & Pay

  • Part-Time position; 10 hours per week with 2–4 hours overlap within 9:00 AM–5:00 PM Pacific Standard Time
  • Pay ranges from USD $258–$387 / R4198 - R6297 ZAR per month
  • Includes onboarding/training, skills development, and potential to increase hours as the program grows

System Requirements

  • Internet speed of at least 20 Mbps
  • Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher
  • 8 GB of RAM or higher
  • Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer
  • HD 720p webcam
  • Headset with a microphone

Benefits

  • Opportunity to grow alongside a national B2B program in the commercial interiors/construction space
  • Fully remote work with long-term potential
  • Competitive pay rates

Closing Statement

If you are organized, proactive, and ready to support a growing B2B program, we encourage you to apply and join a collaborative remote team.
