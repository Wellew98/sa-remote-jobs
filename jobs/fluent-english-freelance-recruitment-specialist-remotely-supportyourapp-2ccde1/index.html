About position

We’re looking for a

Freelance

Recruiter

to conduct full cycle recruitment process from the the first conact to offer stage. In this role, you’ll play a key part in evaluating talent and ensuring a smooth candidate experience.

To be successful in this role, you need to ensure we offer a positive candidate experience and meet our hiring needs.

Excited? Let’s see what it takes 💛

What you will do

Manage the full recruitment cyclefrom application to job offer;

Monitor automated hiring flows and step in where a personalized approach is needed;

Conduct interviews for roles that follow the standard hiring process;

Assess candidates' communication skills, experience, and overall fit for the role;

Provide detailed feedback on interviewed candidates;

Keep candidate data accurate and up to date in the ATS;

Communicate with candidates throughout the hiring process, ensuring a positive candidate experience;

Be an ambassador of SupportYourApp and promote the company’s brand and values.

What you need to succeed in this role

Excellent communication skills of written and spoken English (C1+);

Experience with Workable ATS is a

must ;

At least 1−2 years of the Talent Acquisition or Recruiter experience;

Good time management skills with the ability to juggle various open positions;

Experience with recruiting candidates through different job websites;

Sourcing skills;

Open, easy-going personality.

Will be a great plus

Knowledge of AI recruiting tools;

Any other foreign language knowledge is a plus.
