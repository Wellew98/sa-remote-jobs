About the Role

We’re hiring a Customer Service & Dispatch Coordinator to manage inbound customer communication and coordinate field service operations for a U.S.-based home services business.

This is not a basic support role — you are the operational hub of the business. You will handle high-volume calls, schedule jobs, and ensure technicians are dispatched efficiently in real time.
