Head of Client Delivery – Client Success, Implementation, AI & GTM Systems

Full-Time | Remote | U.S. Business Hours

About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a Head of Client Delivery to own the post-sales journey from onboarding and implementation through adoption, retention, and expansion.

This is a senior, hands-on role for someone who can manage complex client implementations, build trusted relationships, and translate business goals into effective AI, automation, and GTM workflows .

You’ll work closely with Sales, Product, Engineering, and leadership to ensure clients launch successfully, see value quickly, and remain successful long term.

What You’ll Own

Client Delivery & Implementation

Own client onboarding, implementation, adoption, and ongoing success

Manage multiple implementations, timelines, milestones, and deliverables

Translate client goals into clear workflows and execution plans

Drive faster time-to-value and successful adoption

Identify risks, blockers, and delivery issues before they escalate

Client Success & Retention

Own client satisfaction, retention, and account health

Build strong relationships with key client stakeholders

Lead client reviews, implementation updates, and strategic conversations

Manage escalations and drive issues through resolution

Identify opportunities for expansion and deeper adoption

AI, Automation & GTM Systems

Support implementations involving

AI, CRM, GTM, automation, and integrations

Translate business requirements into technical workflows

Partner with Product and Engineering on integrations and custom use cases

Optimize client workflows for efficiency, scalability, and measurable outcomes

Process & Leadership

Build and improve onboarding playbooks, SOPs, and delivery processes

Standardize implementation workflows as the client base grows

Improve visibility into client health, milestones, risks, and outcomes

Partner with leadership on retention, expansion, and Client Delivery strategy

What We’re Looking For

Senior experience in Client Delivery, Customer Success, Implementation, Solutions Engineering, or Technical Account Management

Proven experience managing complex client implementations

Strong understanding of

GTM systems, CRM platforms, automation, and integrations

Strong client-facing and stakeholder management skills

Ability to work effectively with both technical and non-technical teams

Excellent written and spoken English

Strong project management, problem-solving, and ownership skills

Comfortable working remotely during U.S. business hours

Nice to Have

Experience with ChatGPT, Claude, Clay , or similar AI/GTM tools

SaaS, AI, technology, or high-growth startup experience

Experience scaling Client Success or Implementation functions

Experience with complex technical integrations and automation workflows

What Success Looks Like

Faster client onboarding and time-to-value

Strong implementation and adoption rates

High client satisfaction and retention

Fewer delivery risks and escalations

Increased account expansion and long-term client value

Scalable and consistent Client Delivery processes

Why This Role Stands Out

Own the complete post-sales client lifecycle

Work at the intersection of

AI, automation, GTM, and client success

Direct impact on retention, expansion, and client outcomes

High visibility across Product, Engineering, Sales, and leadership

Fully remote with significant ownership and growth potential
