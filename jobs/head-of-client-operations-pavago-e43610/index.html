Head of Client Operations (Cold Email & Client Delivery) – Remote

Position Type

Full-Time, Remote

Working Hours

Flexible (Aligned with U.S. Client Time Zones)

About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a Head of Client Operations to take full ownership of client delivery, campaign performance, client retention, and operational execution for a fast-growing outbound lead generation business.

This is not a traditional account management or project coordination role. You will be responsible for ensuring clients achieve measurable outcomes by improving campaign performance, optimizing operational systems, and maintaining exceptional delivery standards.

Working directly with the founder, you’ll thrive in a fast-paced startup where priorities evolve quickly, ownership is expected, and execution matters more than theory. You’ll combine strategic thinking with hands-on execution—solving problems, improving systems, and building long-term client relationships.

If you’re experienced in cold email operations, outbound lead generation, and client success, and enjoy building scalable operational processes, this role is for you.

Responsibilities

Client Ownership & Retention

Manage a portfolio of 20–30 client accounts.

Serve as the primary point of contact for client delivery, campaign performance, and strategic discussions.

Lead client meetings, quarterly reviews, escalation management, and account planning.

Build trusted relationships while proactively managing client expectations.

Identify account risks early and implement retention strategies before issues escalate.

Own client satisfaction, retention, and long-term account success.

Campaign Performance & Delivery

Monitor and optimize cold email campaign performance.

Diagnose and resolve deliverability issues, inbox placement challenges, low reply rates, messaging performance, and list quality concerns.

Conduct quality assurance reviews before campaign launches.

Continuously improve deliverability, engagement, reply rates, and conversion performance.

Ensure clients consistently receive measurable business outcomes.

Hands-On Cold Email Execution

Refine outbound messaging, campaign strategy, and email sequences.

Optimize subject lines, value propositions, calls-to-action, and email copy.

Improve outbound infrastructure and inbox placement.

Work directly within platforms including

Apollo.io

Smartlead.ai

Instantly.ai

Troubleshoot campaigns and implement recovery and optimization strategies.

Systems, SOPs & Operational Scaling

Design and improve scalable operational systems and delivery workflows.

Develop SOPs, playbooks, and standardized processes.

Track and improve SLAs, client health metrics, delivery quality, and operational efficiency.

Continuously refine workflows to support company growth.

Drive practical process improvements through ongoing iteration.

Cross-Functional Collaboration

Collaborate with outreach teams, developers, infrastructure teams, and leadership.

Coordinate the resolution of technical issues, automation challenges, and infrastructure concerns.

Maintain visibility into campaign performance, operational risks, and delivery bottlenecks.

Ensure smooth communication and accountability across teams.

What Makes You a Great Fit

You thrive in fast-paced startup environments.

You take ownership without requiring constant direction.

You enjoy solving complex operational problems.

You balance strategic thinking with hands-on execution.

You communicate confidently with clients, founders, and cross-functional teams.

You focus on measurable results and continuous improvement.
