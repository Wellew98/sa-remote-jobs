Role Overview

We're looking for an Operational Accountant with 5+ years of experience to own day-to-day accounting operations in a fast-paced, evolving environment. This is a hands-on role for a builder—someone who can run the close, keep the books clean, and improve the systems and processes around them as we scale. This is a hybrid role with accounting focus and light operational support.

Key Responsibilities

Accounting (70%)

  • Own the full month-end close process, including journal entries, accruals, and account reconciliations
  • Manage daily bookkeeping and maintain accurate, audit-ready financial records
  • Perform weekly bank and credit card reconciliations
  • Process accounts payable and accounts receivable, ensuring timely and accurate payments and collections
  • Run payroll, including commission calculations and contractor payroll
  • Administer 401(k) processing and ensure compliance
  • Prepare and file 1099s
  • Produce recurring and ad hoc financial reports for leadership
  • Identify gaps in existing processes and build scalable workflows and controls

Operations (30%)

  • Support employee onboarding and offboarding workflows
  • Process salary changes and employee record updates
  • Coordinate everyday HR and people-ops processes
  • Help maintain smooth internal operations across a growing in-person team
  • Support communication and coordination between departments
  • Assist with scheduling, administrative follow-up, and internal process management
  • Maintain organized records and documentation for employee-related changes
  • Collaborate with finance and leadership on people ops administration

Requirements

  • 5+ years of hands-on accounting experience, ideally in a high-growth or fast-paced setting
  • Strong command of month-end close, reconciliations, AP/AR, and payroll
  • Experience with 401(k) administration, 1099 filing, and commission/contractor payments
  • Proficiency across: Deel, QuickBooks Online (QBO), Campfire, Ramp, HubSpot, and Claude AI
  • Comfortable working independently with shifting priorities and minimal hand-holding
  • High attention to detail and accuracy
  • Strong critical-thinking skills and a builder's mindset—you improve what you touch
  • Flexible, adaptable, and comfortable wearing multiple hats

You’ll Thrive If You

  • Like building systems, not just maintaining them
  • Move fast without sacrificing accuracy
  • Ask "why" and spot problems before they become fires
  • Want ownership over operational finance end to end

  • Unlimited PTO policy (with approval from your Direct Support) + 8 paid US holidays
  • On-time direct deposit pay 2x/month
  • Option to enroll in a 401(k) plan via Vestwell with competitive match options
  • Learn expert deal-making and how to buy businesses with 100% free access to all of our courses and our flagship Contrarian Community
