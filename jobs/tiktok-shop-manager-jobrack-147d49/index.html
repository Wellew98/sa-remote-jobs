Own TikTok Shop for a portfolio of children’s gift brands. Recruit creators, run launches, grow the channel.

$3000-$3500 USD/month | Full-time | Flexible hours, PST preferred

We own and run a portfolio of 3 children’s gift brands - the kind of things parents and grandparents buy for the little ones in their lives. We sell on Amazon across North America, Europe and Australia, on TikTok Shop in the US, on our own websites, and through major big-box retailers in North America.

We’re a small, fully remote team spread across time zones. Everyone owns their channel end to end, and we move fast, especially in our busy season of November and December. TikTok has taken off faster than we expected. An agency ran the launch and still handles it, and they’ve done a good job - but paying agency fees for every new product line and every new brand doesn’t scale. We want that expertise on the team instead.

We’re looking for a TikTok Shop Manager.

You’ll own our TikTok channel end to end. The heart of the job is affiliate and creator marketing - the part we know least about, because it works nothing like Amazon. You’ll shadow the agency for a couple of weeks, then take over.

🤝 Find, recruit and manage affiliates and creators, and build strategies that keep new ones coming in

📦 Send samples, follow up with interested creators and keep a steady flow of videos live

🚀 Run the cold start for every new product line: market and competitor research, creator briefs, sample programs, first ~50 videos, first sales

💻 Oversee the TikTok Shop account day to day: listings, pricing, promotions and campaign registrations for Black Friday, holiday and other TikTok events

📊 Watch inventory and sales, and react fast when a video takes off so stock never runs out

💡 Run GMV Max and other TikTok ads once there’s enough content, against target return and margin

🌍 Launch our other brands on TikTok Shop US from scratch, then expand to markets outside the US where we already hold stock

📈 Send a weekly written report: sales, margin after commissions, samples and ads, ad return, active creators and videos, stock risk and next week’s plan

Hours are flexible, and we’d ideally like some overlap with PST.

To be successful in this role, you need

✅ Proven affiliate and creator marketing experience - this is the top priority

✅ A track record of owning and running a channel yourself, end to end

✅ Deep, practical understanding of how TikTok works

✅ Confidence reaching out to creators cold and building relationships that last

✅ Comfort working to numbers: commissions, margin, ad return, video volume

Brownie points for

⭐ TikTok Shop experience (strongly preferred over affiliate experience on other platforms)

⭐ TikTok ads: GMV Max and video shopping ads

⭐ Launching or running TikTok Shop outside the US

⭐ Fulfilled by TikTok and TikTok Shop inventory management

⭐ Amazon seller experience

⭐ Toys, gifts or kids’ products

⭐ Sample platforms such as JoinBrands

⭐ Social media experience beyond TikTok, such as Instagram

⭐ A second European language

This role suits a self-starter who creates their own work. Nobody will hand you a task list - you’ll decide what moves the channel and then do it. Our buyers are mothers and grandmothers shopping for children aged 3 to 7, so an instinct for that audience matters. Growth-minded and genuinely excited about TikTok is the right energy here, as long as it comes with a practical, reasonable head for numbers and incentives.

💰 $3000-$3500 USD per month

🎯 Performance bonus on top of base salary, with a structure we’ll agree together and tie to the growth you drive

💼 Full-time

🌍 Fully remote - work from wherever you do your best work

🕒 Flexible hours, PST overlap preferred

🔑 Real ownership of a growing channel, with direct access to the founders

Please note that this is a gross salary and that you are responsible for any tax-related procedures in your country of residence. We kindly ask you to apply only if you are happy to work remotely as an independent contractor.
