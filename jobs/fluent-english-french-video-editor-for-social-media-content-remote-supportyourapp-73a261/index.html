We're looking for a creative and detail-obsessed

Video Editor

to join the team of one of our clients. In this role, you'll turn raw footage and a creative brief into ready-to-post Instagram and TikTok content: from matching the pace and effects of a reference video to delivering clean, polished files. The ideal candidate has a proven portfolio of social media content that was actually published and performed well, moves fast without cutting corners, and uses the client's editing tools to deliver more videos, sooner.

Ready to create something great? Let's see what it takes 💛

What you will do

Transform raw rushes and a brief (reference content and specific guidelines) into ready-to-post social media content;

Match the editing pace and effects of the reference content, ensuring a polished, high-quality result;

Apply corrected color grading to keep the visuals consistent and appetizing;

Ensure clean audioreuse or replace a soundtrack, clean and mix voiceovers;

Generate and verify subtitles, including French subtitles and embedded text;

Deliver content in the appropriate format, file type, and size;

Increase daily output by using the tools provided by the client, such as auto-derush and starting from an auto-edited v1 of the videos;

Keep clear track of your activities and progress, and stay open to additional on-demand tasks that fit the position.

What you need to succeed in this role

Excellent English and French B2;

A portfolio of 10+ Instagram and TikTok videos that you edited and that were actually posted and successful on social media. This proof of experience matters more than years of experience or degrees;

Solid common sense about food and dishes;

Proven ability to increase output (number of videos delivered per day) using client-provided tools;

Strong operational excellence with a detail-oriented approach;

Excellent adaptability and the ability to work in fast-paced environments;

An enthusiastic, driven, proactive, and solution-oriented mindset, with the ability to take initiative;

A flexible, reliable, and organized approach: you adapt quickly, act fast, and keep clear track of activities and progress.

Will be a plus

The ability to edit carousels for social media (a sequence of photos, besides videos).

Benefits

Fixed scheduleMonday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 (UTC +1); ;

Fully remote cooperation;

Cooperation on a fast-growing B2B SaaS product;

Inclusive and international environment;

Compensation in USD;

Trust-based management with no micromanagement, focused on your growth and long-term collaboration.
