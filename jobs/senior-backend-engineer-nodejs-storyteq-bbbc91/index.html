Hi, we are Storyteq! 👋🏼

Storyteq helps marketing and creative teams in top brands to scale their creative production and gain control over their marketing campaigns. Through our platform, our clients can streamline campaign workflows, automate their creative production through templates & AI and activate engaging campaigns that go to market faster.

We believe creativity takes time, but creative production shouldn’t. Since 2016 our mission has been to revolutionize the way creative assets are created and used. Magic happens when we let technology empower creativity. Our endless curiosity and relentless commitment to our customers lies at the heart of our problem-solving approach. This shared mission is woven into our values: we dream big, think differently, and are stronger together.

About the job

You will be a part of the domain where we work to enable global enterprises to efficiently scale up their content creation. Taking in content from sources like Photoshop, Figma and After Effects, or designing it in our AI enabled web driven editor and taking it to the next level. Working directly with other Developers, Designers, Quality Engineers, DevOps Engineers, and Product Owners to deliver the best platform for our users.

Your role will revolve around all the different elements that will bring our product to new heights. This includes implementing new features, scaling existing capabilities, and making our product more amazing every day. The team works with strong goals and a good balance of product & engineering roadmap features. That way we make sure we always keep our technology and product on track!

Key responsibilities

Build and ship

robust backend services with real product impact

Ensure systems are scalable, performant and easy to maintain

Collaborate across teams to integrate services and deliver end-to-end value

Model business domains into clean, performant data structures

Coach and mentor other engineers on best practices to help them grow

Collaborate with other engineers and lead technical delivery on initiatives within a domain or project

Lead by example in engineering excellence, pragmatism and shared ownership.

Key Requirements

5+ years of hands-on experience delivering products with TypeScript (and JavaScript)

Strong backend development skills with

Node.js

Solid experience designing and integrating

REST and/or GraphQL APIs

Comfortable working in

monorepo environments and modular architectures

Proficient in

data modeling and working with SQL/NoSQL databases

Familiar with testing practices, performance tuning, and scalable design principles

You’re proactive, result-driven, and excited to work in a fast-growing tech scale-up

Confident in making architectural decisions and communicating trade-offs

Comfortable mentoring and supporting engineers to help them grow

You’re a strong team player who values collaboration and shared success

Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English

Nice to have

Have experience supporting Keycloak (or other identity management solutions)

Have experience with Vue.js (or other front-end frameworks) to occasionally support frontend tasks

Have affinity with

rendering technologies (like ffmpeg)

Have affinity with

motion graphics and visual effects software (like After Effects)

Experience with Gitlab CI/CD, DevOps, public cloud (like GCP).
