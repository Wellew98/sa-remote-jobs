About the Role

About the Role

We are looking for an experienced

Recruiter to join our team and support the recruitment of healthcare professionals for our clients in the US.

You will primarily be recruiting for

Registered Behavior Technicians (RBTs) and Board Certified Behavior Analysts (BCBAs) within the ABA healthcare space.

You do not need previous ABA experience. If you have a background in recruitment, healthcare recruitment, medical recruitment, staffing, or agency recruitment, you'll be trained on the basics of ABA and the specific roles you're recruiting for.

We're looking for someone who understands the fundamentals of recruitment, is comfortable sourcing and approaching candidates, and has strong follow-through from initial outreach through to placement.

What You'll Be Doing

Source candidates for RBT and BCBA positions using LinkedIn and Aploy.

Identify and approach suitable candidates based on the requirements of each vacancy.

Conduct candidate outreach and follow up consistently.

Use tools such as

SignalHire to find candidate contact information when required.

Screen candidates against basic role requirements.

Keep candidate information and recruitment activity organized and up to date.

Manage multiple candidates and vacancies at the same time.

Work closely with the team to ensure positions are staffed within required timelines.

Assist with onboarding coordination when required.

Support the wider recruitment team with additional sourcing and administrative tasks.

We'd love to hear from you if you have experience as a

Healthcare Recruiter

Previous

ABA recruitment experience is a bonus but not required .

You'll ideally have

2+ years of recruitment experience .

Experience sourcing candidates through LinkedIn or other recruitment platforms.

Experience with candidate outreach and follow-up.

Good written and verbal English communication skills.

Strong organization and attention to detail.

The ability to manage multiple vacancies and candidates.

Good follow-through and the ability to work independently.

A willingness to learn new industries, roles, and recruitment processes.
