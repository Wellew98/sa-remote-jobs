Own and scale growth, community, partnerships, and operations for a fast‑growing legal coaching brand.

$1,500–$2,000/month | ~20 hrs/week | Flexible remote schedule

BE THAT LAWYER

helps ambitious attorneys build sustainable books of business through coaching and training, Rainmaker Roundtables, the BE THAT LAWYER Community, podcasts, books, speaking, and practical business development systems. We have strong content, a recognized brand, and multiple growth channels. We are now looking for the person who can turn those assets into more members, more qualified prospects, and ultimately, more revenue.

This is not a traditional marketing role. It is a hands-on growth and execution leadership role for someone who can move quickly, manage people and projects, create momentum, and own measurable outcomes.

We're looking for a Fractional Head of Growth & Operations.

Run and grow the marketing engine that allows Steve Fretzin to focus on the work only he should be doing: creating thought leadership, working with clients, meeting qualified prospects and developing high-value strategic relationships.

In this role, you will

🚀 Leverage content and events into qualified leads 🌱 Grow and engage an online community — from its current base of 100 toward 200, 300, 500 members and beyond 🤝 Manage our marketing team and outside vendors ⚙️ Build and manage a new CRM to automate follow-through 🎯 Identify, develop and activate new strategic partnerships (for example, Bar Associations) 📈 Turn Steve's existing content into more leads and measurable business opportunities 📋 Keep the virtual assistant and vendors accountable and clear on ownership ✅ Launch projects on time without Steve chasing people or managing the details

Working Hours

This is a fractional engagement of approximately 20 hours per week, with flexibility on schedule and a bias toward getting things done.

Reporting

You'll be working directly with Steve Fretzin, founder of BE THAT LAWYER, and will own the monthly goals and Issues List that protect the team from constant priority changes.

To excel in this role, you need

✅ Experience growing a founder-led professional services, coaching, consulting, membership, education or personal brand business (legal background a bonus)

✅ Demonstrated success generating leads, growing memberships, building partnerships or creating measurable pipeline

✅ Strong project and people management skills, including experience managing contractors, virtual assistants or vendors

✅ Comfort with email marketing, LinkedIn, podcasts, webinars, CRM systems, content repurposing, SEO or AEO and basic marketing analytics

✅ An entrepreneurial mindset with a bias toward action, experimentation and results

✅ A "doer first, strategist second" approach — able to take an idea, build a practical plan, assign the work and follow through

✅ Comfort working independently, juggling multiple priorities and changing direction when the data says something isn't working

Brownie points for

⭐ Prior experience in the legal or professional-services space

⭐ A track record of scaling an online community or membership program

⭐ Experience negotiating and activating association or institutional partnerships

The strongest candidate will be confident, persuasive, adaptable, and results-oriented. You should be comfortable moving quickly, handling several projects at once, questioning the status quo, making decisions, and working independently. You need enough structure to run systems and track results without allowing perfection or process to slow down execution.

What Success Looks Like

📊 The BE THAT LAWYER Community grows consistently from its current base of 100 toward 200, 300, 500 members and beyond

🎯 Qualified coaching and roundtable opportunities enter the pipeline on a consistent basis

🤝 New strategic partnerships are identified, developed, and activated (for example, Bar Associations)

📈 Steve's existing content produces more leads and measurable business opportunities

👥 The virtual assistant and vendors know what they own and are held accountable for getting it done

🚀 Projects launch on time without Steve chasing people or managing the details

✅ Monthly goals are completed and the Issues List protects the team from constant priority changes

⏱️ Steve spends less time managing marketing and operations and more time creating, selling, coaching and building relationships

Meet Your Manager

You’ll report directly to Steve Fretzin, President of BE THAT LAWYER. Steve is a plane crash survivor and a five-time author.

He’s driven, resilient, and deeply committed to helping lawyers succeed - and he’s looking for a true marketing partner to stand shoulder-to-shoulder with him as the company scales.

What's in it for you?

💰 $1,500–$2,000 per month base (based on experience), plus meaningful performance bonuses tied to agreed growth and revenue outcomes

🌍 Fractional engagement, fully remote with flexible working hours

📈 Real ownership over growth strategy and execution for a recognized, founder-led brand

🚪 Room to grow — the role may expand over time based on results and business needs

*This role is expected to begin as a fractional engagement of approximately 20 hours per week. Base compensation is based on experience and scope, plus performance bonuses tied to agreed growth and revenue outcomes.

*Please note that this is a gross salary and that you are responsible for any tax-related procedures in your country of residence. We kindly ask you to apply only if you are happy to work remotely as an independent contractor.
