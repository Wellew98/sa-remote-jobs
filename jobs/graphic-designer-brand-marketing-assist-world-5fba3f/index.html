About the Company

Our client is a rapidly growing coffee roasting and manufacturing business supplying private-label and branded coffee to major retailers and businesses across the United States. As the company continues to grow its portfolio of brands and customer base, they are looking for a talented Graphic Designer to support their marketing and sales teams with high-quality creative work.

The Role

This is a varied creative role combining graphic design, AI-generated imagery, brand consistency and marketing presentations. You will be responsible for creating and adapting visual assets across the company's brands, preparing artwork for film and packaging suppliers, and developing polished marketing decks and presentations that the sales team can use with customers. The ideal candidate will be both creative and technically minded, with strong attention to detail and the ability to work confidently with design files and templates.

Key Responsibilities

● Create and edit high-quality graphic assets for the company's brands ● Develop and work with AI-generated images and creative assets ● Combine images, graphics and design elements into polished marketing materials ● Work with existing brand templates and ensure visual consistency across different brands ● Prepare artwork and files for submission to film, packaging and print suppliers ● Review artwork and layouts to ensure they meet production requirements ● Communicate with external suppliers regarding artwork, layouts and file requirements ● Adapt designs for different brands, products and customer requirements ● Create professional marketing decks and presentations for the sales team ● Develop customer-facing presentations that clearly showcase products and brands ● Work closely with the sales and marketing teams to understand creative requirements ● Organise and maintain design files and templates ● Help improve and standardise the company's existing creative processes and templates
