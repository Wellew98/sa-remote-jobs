We are seeking an experienced and creative Digital Video Editor to join our growing team at The Soar Group. You will be responsible for producing high-quality video content tailored for social media ads, promotional content and performance marketing across multiple brands. This role requires a strong understanding of e-commerce, short-form video trends and platform-specific best practices.

The Soar Group is one of the UK's fastest-growing performance agency groups, built around a specialist paid media core — with Meta as our primary growth channel — backed by an in-house creative execution arm and a dedicated TikTok Shop implementation capability. With over £100M+ in annual ad spend under management and a team of 130+, we partner with ambitious 7-9 figure DTC and e-commerce brands across Meta, Google and TikTok. Our capabilities span full-funnel performance media, in-house creative execution delivering high-production content, UGC and statics at scale, and a dedicated TikTok Shop and social commerce capability. We were nominated for Meta's Creative Diversity Award 2025 and recognised by Meta as the UK's #1 independent Meta agency group.

What you'll do

Edit high-quality videos that align with each brand's style and tone of voice.

Specialise in social media ads (UGC, motion graphics, high-production content).

Optimise content for TikTok, Meta (Facebook & Instagram) and other platforms to maximise engagement.

Work efficiently under tight deadlines while maintaining high-quality standards.

Collaborate with team members to implement client feedback effectively.

Stay up to date with video content trends and conversion-driven editing techniques.

What you'll bring

Proven experience in video editing and production, preferably within e-commerce.

Expert proficiency in Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects; ability to use other video editing tools is a plus.

A deep understanding of TikTok, Meta ads and short-form video best practices.

Proficiency in applying motion graphics, transitions, captions and colour grading.

A self-motivated, proactive approach, able to work independently or as part of a team.

Excellent written and spoken English communication skills.

Full ownership of both your successes and failures, with an entrepreneurial mindset and the ability to see the bigger picture.

A proactive, solution-oriented and adaptable approach, with exceptional attention to detail and strong problem-solving skills.

Confidence building long-term relationships, and the ability to self-manage and prioritise effectively in a fast-paced environment.

Please submit a valid portfolio or showreel that demonstrates your expertise.

Why Contract with Soar?

Incredible client base: work with some of the fastest-growing and most established DTC e-commerce brands globally.

Skill development: broaden your skill set with diverse projects, ongoing learning opportunities and regular mentoring from senior management.

Start-up sensibilities: be part of a rapidly growing agency where you'll have a direct impact on shaping how we do things.
