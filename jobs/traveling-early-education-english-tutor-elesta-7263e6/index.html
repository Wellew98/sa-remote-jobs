Family office is seeking a highly engaging

Native English-speaking

Traveling Tutor to provide personalized early education for three siblings, following the British National Curriculum . This is a travel-based position supporting a private family and requires flexibility, adaptability, and a genuine passion for working with children.

The initial engagement will be based in

Costa Rica

, from approximately 10 October through early November (approximately 4 weeks) . The successful candidate will be expected to travel with the family and provide a consistent, engaging, and age-appropriate educational experience throughout the assignment.

Key Responsibilities

Provide lessons to three siblings (aged 4, 6, and 9)

Adapt lesson plans to each child's learning pace and academic goals

Track progress and provide regular written reports to the Head of Education

Travel internationally with the family as required

Native English-speaker

Bachelor's degree or higher in Early Education; teaching qualification preferred

Minimum 3 years of tutoring or teaching experience within the British curriculum

Ability to teach

Numeracy & Literacy to a 4-year-old.

Ability to teach

Maths, Science & Humanities to a 6-year-old.

Strong ability to teach

English as a First Language to ages 6–9, including comprehension, grammar, and creative writing, aligned with UK independent school standards .

Strong

1-to-1 teaching skills across different ages and ability levels.

Ability to

differentiate and adapt teaching strategies to individual learning needs.

Ability to

assess progress, identify gaps and plan learning accordingly .

Ability to build

engagement, independence and strong learning habits .

Comfortable with frequent international travel (Europe, LatAm, South Africa, United Arab Emirates)

Stable, long-term employment with a young and progressive family that values loyalty, professionalism, and mutual respect.

Opportunity to travel internationally

, experiencing diverse cultures and environments.

All travel expenses covered

when accompanying the family, including flights, accommodation, transportation, and meals.

A supportive working environment

, with collaborative teamwork alongside other traveling tutors and nannies.

A safe, respectful, and well-organized household with clear communication and expectations.
