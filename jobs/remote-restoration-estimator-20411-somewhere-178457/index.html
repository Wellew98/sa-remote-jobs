Remote Estimator

About the Company

Our client is a Tennessee-based water and fire restoration company providing professional restoration solutions to residential and commercial customers. The company helps property owners recover from water, fire, and other property damage by delivering responsive service, accurate project assessments, and high-quality restoration work.

As the company continues to grow, they are seeking an experienced Remote Estimator to support the estimating process and help ensure restoration projects are accurately scoped, priced, and documented.

Role Overview

The Remote Estimator will be responsible for preparing accurate and detailed estimates for water, fire, and property restoration projects using Xactimate. This individual will review project documentation, assess restoration scopes, develop comprehensive estimates, and communicate directly with clients and internal team members throughout the estimating process.

The ideal candidate has at least two years of hands-on Xactimate experience, a strong understanding of restoration or construction estimating, and excellent English communication skills. This is an immediate hiring need for someone who can quickly integrate into the company's workflow and contribute with minimal training.

Key Responsibilities

Restoration Estimating

- Prepare accurate and detailed estimates for water, fire, and property restoration projects

-

Build comprehensive estimates using Xactimate

- Review project scopes, photographs, measurements, documentation, and supporting information

- Ensure estimates accurately reflect required labor, materials, equipment, and restoration activities

- Revise and update estimates as project scopes or requirements change

Xactimate Management

- Utilize Xactimate to create detailed and professionally structured estimates

- Maintain accurate line items, pricing, and supporting documentation

- Review estimates for completeness and accuracy before submission

- Stay current with Xactimate processes and restoration industry estimating practices

Documentation & Quality Control

- Maintain organized records and documentation for each estimate

- Review project information for missing or inconsistent details

- Ensure estimating documentation meets internal quality standards

- Coordinate with internal teams to gather additional information when necessary

Client Communication

- Communicate directly and professionally with clients regarding estimates and project details

- Explain estimating information clearly when questions arise

- Follow up promptly on outstanding information needed to complete estimates

- Maintain high-quality written and verbal communication throughout the estimating process

Team Collaboration

- Work closely with project managers, operations teams, and other internal stakeholders

- Support the transition of approved estimates into active restoration projects

- Communicate changes or potential estimating issues proactively

- Learn and utilize the company's construction management software and internal workflows

Required Qualifications

- Minimum of 2 years of experience using Xactimate

- Previous experience in restoration, construction, insurance, or property damage estimating

- Strong understanding of estimating principles and project scopes

- High-level written and verbal English proficiency

- Ability to communicate professionally and confidently with clients

-

Excellent attention to detail and accuracy

- Strong organizational and time management skills

- Ability to manage multiple estimates and priorities simultaneously

- Ability to work independently in a fully remote environment

Preferred Qualifications

- Previous experience estimating water damage restoration projects

- Previous experience estimating fire damage restoration projects

- Familiarity with insurance restoration or property damage claims

- Experience working with construction management software

- Knowledge of restoration industry processes and terminology

- Experience working directly with property owners, insurance professionals, or restoration teams

Technology & Tools

- Xactimate ( 2+ years of experience required )

- Dash construction management software (company uses this platform; prior experience is not required)

Microsoft Office

Remote communication and collaboration tools
