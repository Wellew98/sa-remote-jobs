Sales Development Representative (SDR) Location: Remote Department: Sales & Client Success Reports to: Sales Manager, Outbound Team About the Company We are a rapidly growing U.S.-based cost segregation firm that helps real estate investors unlock accelerated depreciation and meaningful tax savings through engineering-backed studies. With the reinstatement of 100% bonus depreciation and newly opened data channels, we are scaling our outbound sales team quickly and building the pipeline engine that fuels that growth. About the Role This role sits at the very top of our sales funnel. As an SDR, you are the first human contact a property owner has with our firm, and the quality of the meetings you book directly determines what our Account Managers can close. This is not a script-reading seat. You will own the early conversation, qualify real fit, and hand off meetings that convert. It is a proving ground: strong performers move into Team Lead or Account Manager roles as the team expands. Key Responsibilities Outbound Prospecting - Make 80–100 dials per day with a focus on quality conversations, not just volume - Use CoStar, RB2B, ZoomInfo, HubSpot, and LinkedIn Sales Navigator to identify and reach the right property owners Qualification & Meeting Booking - Qualify prospects against defined criteria (location, asset class, property value) and book meetings that meet the bar - Hit monthly meeting quotas and stretch goals tied to commission Pipeline Hygiene - Maintain accurate, detailed lead notes and dispositions in HubSpot so Account Managers walk into every meeting prepared Collaboration & Iteration - Partner with sales managers and data analysts to sharpen targeting, messaging, and outreach cadence based on what is converting Required Qualifications - 1–2 years in outbound B2B lead generation or appointment setting - Strong written and spoken English, with a neutral to North American accent - Comfort in a high-volume, metric-driven environment - Self-starter who performs well with autonomy Preferred Qualifications - Experience in financial or real estate services - Familiarity with HubSpot, CoStar, ZoomInfo, RB2B, or LinkedIn Sales Navigator Ideal Candidate Profile You are resilient on the phone and do not lose energy on the 60th dial. You listen closely enough to spot real fit versus a polite brush-off. You are organized, so nothing falls through the cracks in the CRM, and you treat quotas as a floor rather than a ceiling. You want to grow into a closing role and are willing to earn it. Compensation - Base: depending on experience  - Commission: Tiered, meeting-based with accelerators linked to revenue generated - Realistic on-target earnings: $2,000–$2,500/month and up for high performers, with additional upside as the team scales Why Join Us - Training and call frameworks built from our top closers - Daily coaching and QA support - A clear path into Team Lead and Account Manager roles - A sharp, driven team working across multiple time zones
