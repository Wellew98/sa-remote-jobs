What a Typical Day Looks Like

You may start your day by reviewing performance across your client portfolio, identifying campaigns with changes in CPL, CTR, conversions, or lead quality.

Throughout the day, you’ll adjust budgets and bids, refine audience targeting, launch new creative tests, conduct competitor research, and analyze campaign performance.

You’ll also prepare reports, communicate recommendations to clients, and collaborate with creative and strategy teams on new campaigns and optimization opportunities.

In short

you ensure LinkedIn Ads campaigns consistently generate measurable B2B outcomes while maintaining strong campaign execution and client satisfaction.
