Ready to drive profitable growth for subscription eCommerce? Own Google Ads end-to-end for health-focused brands.

Independent Contractor (Remote) | 40 hours/week | $4,000-$4,500/month (gross) | CEST preferred

Are you a growth-minded media buyer who wants to drive real change?

At

Paleovalley

& Wild Pastures, we aren’t just selling products - we’re revolutionizing the food industry through regenerative agriculture and radical transparency. We are looking for a Senior Google Media Buyer to architect the next phase of our growth, scaling our impact from millions to tens of millions.

We've been growing at a rapid pace, and our goal is to extend that momentum, maximising our positive impact on a global scale. Our team of 200+ passionate people works across the U.S. and internationally in areas like marketing, media buying, design, and logistics. As a family-owned company, we pride ourselves on a work culture that's both challenging and fun, prioritising physical and mental well-being. Here, respect, unity, and empowerment drive us forward every day. If you're looking for a supportive environment that truly cares about its team, keep on reading!

We're looking for a Google Media Buyer. You'll be the kind of person who can step into a high-performing account, take ownership from day one, and drive profitable customer acquisition for subscription-based e-commerce brands.

This role is all about balancing efficiency and scale across multiple Google Ads campaign types while using data to guide every decision.

In this role, you will

📋 Manage Google Ads end-to-end (Search, Shopping, Performance Max, YouTube, Display)

🚀 Step into a fast-moving account and own the strategy from day one

🛍️ Drive paid acquisition for our subscription-based eCommerce brands (Paleovalley & Wild Pastures)

📈 Hit efficiency targets (CPA, ROAS, MER) while maintaining scale across all campaign types

📊 Leverage data (GA4, Mixpanel, Haus) to evaluate true performance and guide budget allocation

✏️ Translate insights into actionable creative briefs for designers and creators; refine keywords

🛠️ Diagnose & Turnaround underperforming metrics quickly

To collaborate effectively with our team, we're looking for someone who can generally provide approximately four hours of overlap with our Mountain Time team (typically between 9:00 AM and 1:00 PM MT). Outside of those collaboration hours, you're welcome to structure your workday in a way that best supports your productivity and the needs of the role.

To be successful in this role, you need

✅ Google Ads Mastery: Deep proficiency in Search, Shopping, Performance Max, YouTube with 3+ years of experience

✅ Agile Execution: Ability to jump into a new, fast-paced account and hit the ground running

✅ Subscription Expertise: Proven experience in subscription eCommerce—you know the unique dynamics vs. one-time purchases

✅ High-Budget Track Record: Proven history managing significant monthly ad spend while crushing CPA, ROAS, or MER targets

✅ Experience with third-party data measurement and attribution tools like GA4, Mixpanel, and Haus (or similar) to guide budget optimisation

✅ Workflow for analysing performance data and translating insights into actionable briefs for creators and designers, plus search term and keyword refinement

Brownie points for

⭐ Food, wellness or DTC experience

⭐ Experience with AI-driven campaign optimisation

⭐ Merchant Center expertise

⭐ Experience in high-growth eCommerce brands

⭐ Creative testing experience

⭐ Interest in health, nutrition or regenerative agriculture

The ideal candidate thinks like an owner and operates with a highly proactive mindset. Data drives your decisions, but curiosity keeps you continuously improving.

You're someone who challenges assumptions with data, communicates clearly, and thrives in a collaborative environment while managing your work autonomously. A long-term mindset matters to you, and you're looking for a role where you can truly make an impact.

What’s in it for you?

💰 $4,000-4,500 per month*

💼 Approximately 40 hours per week & 100% remote independent contractor engagement

📅 Supportive work culture that prioritises physical and mental well-being

🤝 Work with a passionate team of 200+ people across the U.S. and internationally

🌱 Make a positive impact on health and the planet through sustainably sourced products

*Compensation is based on experience and geographic location

*Please note that this is a gross salary and that you are responsible for any tax-related procedures in your country of residence. We kindly ask you to apply only if you are happy to work remotely as an independent contractor.
