Sales & Customer Experience Representative

Employment Type: Full-time, IC  Location: Remote   Hours: U.S. Eastern business hours (approx. 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM ET) Compensation: Base salary + commission on closed sales

About the Company

We are a rapidly growing U.S.-based ecommerce company specializing in premium home wellness products, with a focus on high-value saunas and sauna heaters. Our customers are making considered, high-ticket purchases, which means every conversation matters. We are building a dedicated remote team to own the customer relationship end to end and are looking for our first sales and customer experience hire to help lead that effort.

About the Role

This role exists because our growth is outpacing our current ability to give every prospect and customer the attention a high-value purchase deserves. You will be the voice of the brand across phone, chat, and email, guiding buyers through a meaningful purchase decision and supporting them long after the sale.

This is a blended position. On the sales side, you will handle both inbound interest and proactive outbound outreach, building trust and closing deals. On the service side, you will resolve returns, order issues, and product questions in a way that keeps customers coming back. Success here is measured by revenue you help generate and by the relationships you build along the way.

Key Responsibilities

Sales (Inbound & Outbound)

  • Respond promptly to inbound inquiries across phone, live chat, and email, and guide prospects through a considered, high-ticket purchase decision
  • Proactively follow up on leads, warm inquiries, and abandoned carts to move buyers toward a decision
  • Build genuine rapport quickly and establish the trust needed to close higher-value orders
  • Meet and exceed individual sales targets

Customer Service & Support

  • Handle post-purchase questions, order issues, and returns with patience and professionalism
  • Resolve customer problems in a way that protects the relationship and drives repeat business
  • Turn service interactions into opportunities to reinforce customer loyalty

Product Expertise & Relationship Management

  • Develop deep knowledge of the product line so you can advise customers with confidence
  • Maintain accurate notes and records of customer interactions
  • Represent the brand consistently and warmly across every touchpoint

Required Qualifications

  • 2+ years in a blended sales and customer service role, ideally in ecommerce/DTC or high-ticket phone sales
  • Exceptional spoken and written English, with a warm, natural, trust-building phone presence
  • Availability to work full U.S. Eastern business hours
  • Comfort working toward sales targets in a base-plus-commission structure
  • Reliable remote work setup: quiet environment, stable high-speed internet, and a quality headset

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience selling considered or high-average-order-value products
  • Background in home, wellness, or lifestyle product categories
  • Familiarity with ecommerce platforms and help desk / CRM tools (e.g., Shopify, live chat, ticketing systems)

Ideal Candidate Profile

You are

  • Warm, outgoing, and naturally skilled at building rapport and trust
  • Consultative rather than pushy, especially on higher-value purchases
  • Resilient and level-headed when handling frustrated or difficult customers
  • Self-directed and proactive, comfortable owning outbound outreach without being told
  • Motivated by the earning potential of commission, but genuinely service-minded
  • Looking for a long-term home, not a short-term stop

Why Join Us

  • Fully remote role with consistent U.S. business hours
  • Direct-hire stability with a growing brand
  • Base salary plus meaningful commission on the sales you close
  • The chance to be the first dedicated hire on a team you can help shape and grow
