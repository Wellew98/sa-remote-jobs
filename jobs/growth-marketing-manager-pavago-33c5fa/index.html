At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring an AI-first Growth Marketing Manager to lead hands-on marketing execution, organic growth, content production, SEO, and scalable marketing infrastructure.

You’ll work closely with designers, developers, and marketing stakeholders to build repeatable growth systems across social media, SEO, content, websites, and marketing operations.
