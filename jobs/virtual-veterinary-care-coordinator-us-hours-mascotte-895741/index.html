Location

Remote — work from anywhere in South Africa

Hours

3pm – 11pm SAST or 6pm – 2am SAST (U.S. business hours) — your mornings and early afternoons are completely free. No weekends.

The Role

You'll be the dedicated remote front-desk for one U.S. veterinary clinic — their care coordinator, their client's first voice, part of their team every day. This is real veterinary work, done from home.

What You'll Be Doing

Client Communication: Answer and initiate calls, texts, chats, and emails from pet parents with empathy and clarity.

Appointment Scheduling: Book, reschedule, and confirm visits.

Care Navigation: Help pet parents understand what comes next — triage inquiries, explain services, coordinate follow-ups.

Estimates & Treatment Plans: Build estimates in partnership with the veterinary team.

Records: Keep medical records, notes, and workflows tight and up to date.

Why Mascotte

Mascotte is a fast-growing, tech-enabled company transforming how U.S. veterinary practices operate behind the scenes. We're not a staffing agency — our team members are dedicated, long-term extensions of the clinics they support. Everyone here has lived the vet clinic hustle.

Who We're Looking For

Veterinary experience: 2+ years in a veterinary clinic or hospital — front desk, reception, practice administration, or client care, with clinic names and employment dates shown in your CV work history.

Scheduling experience: You've handled appointment booking, rescheduling, and confirmations in a veterinary clinic or hospital.

Spoken English: You'll be on live calls with American pet owners all day. Fluent, professional English is essential — and we assess it based on evidence, not self-rating, including prior phone-based roles serving English-speaking clients and fluent, well-written free-text application answers.

Tech-comfortable: Hands-on with veterinary practice management software — you can name the systems you've used and describe your day-to-day work in them. Experience with VoIP tools is a plus.

Team-oriented: Experience coordinating with veterinarians and clinic teams to keep communication and follow-up moving.

Remote-ready: You can describe a specific remote-work setup: fibre or fixed internet of at least 25 Mbps, backup power that lets you work through load shedding (inverter, UPS, or generator), and a quiet dedicated space for taking calls.

Fixed-shift availability: You can work the fixed shift you're assigned — either 3pm–11pm SAST or 6pm–2am SAST, Monday to Friday — on a permanent, long-term basis. We assess this based on the evidence in your CV, including your current commitments and any prior sustained evening or night shift work.
