ISTA Personnel Solutions

is a dynamic and fast-growing BPO company. We are not a recruitment agency. We partner with international US clients and integrate our staff members directly within our clients' operations. We focus on long-term placements where you contribute directly to the client's success.

We are partnering with a growing U.S.-based construction company to recruit a

Property Maintenance Coordinator

for residential refurbishment projects (including apartment complexes and multifamily properties) together with light commercial projects across the United States.

This is a fully remote opportunity for an organised and proactive individual with experience in property maintenance, property services, facilities management or building maintenance.

You will coordinate day-to-day maintenance across multiple properties, liaising with property managers, contractors, vendors and suppliers to schedule repairs, manage work orders and ensure outstanding jobs are followed up through to completion.

The role requires strong administrative and coordination skills, with the ability to manage multiple maintenance requests, track job progress and maintain clear communication with all stakeholders.

PLEASE NOTE

Working hours

Monday to Friday, 16:30–01:30 SAST (subject to US daylight saving changes)

Public Holidays

You will be required to work on both South African and U.S. public holidays (compensation for SA public holidays in accordance with the BCEA).

Internet Requirements

A fixed fibre line with a minimum speed of 25 Mbps (upload & download) and the ability to support a wired Ethernet connection is mandatory. Applicants without a fixed fibre line cannot be considered.

Power Backup

A reliable power backup solution is required to manage load shedding and power outages. Applicants without a power backup cannot be considered.

Work Environment

This is a

fully remote working role and you must be based in South Africa.

Job Responsibilities (but not limited to):

Coordinate day-to-day maintenance and repair activities across multiple residential properties.

Receive, review and coordinate incoming property maintenance requests and work orders.

Liaise with external contractors, vendors and service providers, including plumbers, electricians, general maintenance teams and other trades.

Arrange and schedule contractor visits, inspections, repairs and maintenance work.

Follow up with contractors and field teams to monitor job progress and ensure work is completed within agreed timeframes.

Coordinate maintenance activities for both occupied and vacant properties, ensuring that work is arranged appropriately.

Track outstanding work orders, identify delays and ensure unresolved maintenance issues are followed up.

Request and review contractor quotations, assist with purchase orders and monitor job-related costs.

Maintain accurate property records, work orders, contractor details, quotations and maintenance updates within internal systems.

Communicate regularly with property managers, clients, contractors and internal teams regarding job progress and outstanding requirements.

Escalate maintenance issues, delays, cost concerns or service-related problems where necessary.

Manage multiple properties and maintenance requests simultaneously while ensuring that all tasks are tracked through to completion.

At least 2 years' experience in property maintenance coordination, property administration, facilities management, building maintenance or a similar environment.

Previous experience working for a property management, property services, property preservation, facilities management or building maintenance company.

Experience coordinating external contractors, vendors or subcontractors, ideally within plumbing, electrical, HVAC or general building maintenance.

Experience arranging maintenance visits, scheduling repairs and following up on outstanding work.

Understanding of work orders, maintenance requests, quotations, purchase orders and contractor invoices.

Strong administrative and organisational skills, with the ability to manage multiple properties, requests and deadlines simultaneously.

Excellent written and spoken English.

Comfortable working with computer systems, spreadsheets and task management platforms.

Strong communication and problem-solving skills.

A proactive approach to following up on outstanding tasks and ensuring that work is completed without constant supervision.

Beneficial Experience

Exposure to single-family rental portfolios, multifamily properties, apartment complexes or residential property portfolios.

Experience coordinating maintenance for occupied and vacant properties.

Previous experience working with a national network of contractors or vendors.

Experience with property management systems, maintenance management platforms or work order software.

Familiarity with property inspections, repair reports, code compliance or property registration processes.

Experience working with US-based clients or international teams.

If you are not contacted within 14 working days, please consider your application unsuccessful.
