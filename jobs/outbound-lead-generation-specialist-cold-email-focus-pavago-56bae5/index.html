About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a Cold Email Outreach Specialist / Outbound Lead Generation Representative to generate qualified pipeline through targeted, data-driven outbound email campaigns.

This is a hands-on role with ownership of the full cold email workflow — from prospect research and list building to campaign setup, copywriting, deliverability, optimization, reply management, and lead qualification.

This is not a passive list-sending role. You should understand how targeting, messaging, domain reputation, inbox health, and deliverability work together to drive outbound performance.

If you know how to build targeted lists, write cold emails that generate responses, maintain healthy sending infrastructure, and continuously optimize campaigns based on data, this role is a strong fit.

What a Typical Day Looks Like

You may start your day reviewing campaign performance, bounce rates, reply rates, and inbox health before checking replies and routing qualified prospects to sales.

From there, you’ll research new target accounts, build and clean prospect lists, write personalized sequences, launch campaigns, and run A/B tests.

Throughout the day, you’ll monitor deliverability, optimize underperforming campaigns, update CRM records, and ensure outbound systems remain organized and healthy.

In short

you own cold email execution from prospect data to qualified opportunity.
