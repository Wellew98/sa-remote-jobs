Video Editor (Digital Marketing) Location: Remote (Philippines preferred, or South Africa) Hours: Full-time, M-F US hours The Opportunity We're seeking a creative, efficient, and trend-savvy Video Editor to join our marketing team. This full-time, remote role is open to talented professionals in the Philippines (preferred) or South Africa You will be the engine behind our digital content, transforming long-form video into high-impact, short-form content that drives engagement and growth. This is a fast-paced startup environment where urgency is high and autonomy is key. We are looking for a self-directed individual who has a passion for digital games and collectibles and a strong ability to produce visually compelling content that resonates with our community. Key Responsibilities - Content Editing & Repurposing: - Edit long-form video into high-impact, short-form content for platforms like TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook. - Cut, caption, and format videos for multiple aspect ratios (9:16, 1:1, 16:9). - Reformat old footage and adjust clips for platform-specific requirements to create new, engaging promotional content. - Collaboration & Quality Control: - Work closely with the digital marketer to ensure a consistent brand aesthetic and style across all videos. - Meet tight deadlines for scheduled posting and be able to pivot creative direction based on engagement metrics. - Maintain a consistent brand aesthetic and optimize pacing, transitions, and sound design for social platforms. Required Skills & Experience - Proficiency in a Major Editing Suite: You must be highly proficient in at least one major video editing suite, such as Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, or DaVinci Resolve . - Social Media Savvy: Strong understanding of social media trends for video performance and the ability to handle a high volume of editing with tight deadlines. - Creative Storytelling: A sharp eye for visual appeal and creative storytelling skills. - Self-Direction: The ability to work independently and manage multiple projects in a startup environment with minimal micromanagement. - Passion for the Niche: A genuine passion for digital games, card games, or collectibles is a plus, as it aligns with our community. - Bonus: - Familiarity with AI-assisted editing tools (Descript, Runway, Opus Clip) and CapCut for mobile/social-specific editing is a plus. - Experience with multi-skilled hires who can evolve with the business. - Paid Leave: 15 days paid leave (note: no US public holidays as sales are big on those days). Interview & Hiring Process The hiring process will include an initial interview followed by a take-home brief skills assessment. We are looking for multi-skilled hires who are adaptable and can quickly evolve with the business.
