About Us

We’re a fast-growing premium D2C skincare brand building more than just products.

Our mission is to bring clinic-grade skincare into everyday homes — delivering professional-level results without the high cost, complexity, or intimidation of in-clinic treatments.

We combine advanced formulations with strong branding and performance-driven marketing. Creative is a core driver of growth.

Role Overview

We’re hiring a hybrid creative who can edit high-converting videos, design premium visuals, and generate hyper-realistic AI content.

This is a performance-driven role. You won’t just execute — you’ll own outcomes, contribute to strategy, and help scale creative output across the business.

Key Responsibilities

Edit high-performing D2C ad creatives (Meta, TikTok, YouTube)

Turn raw/UGC footage into conversion-focused videos

Design static creatives (ads, thumbnails, landing visuals)

Generate hyper-realistic AI videos and visuals

Create scroll-stopping hooks and compelling storytelling

Repurpose content across multiple platforms

Rapidly iterate on winning creatives

Use AI tools to accelerate production and ideation

Collaborate on creative strategy (not just execution)

Ensure high output and creative velocity across projects

What We’re Looking For

1. Experience

Minimum 3+ years of relevant experience in video editing/design

Proven exposure to D2C / performance marketing creatives

2. Performance + Psychology Understanding

Strong understanding of human psychology and buyer behavior

Ability to translate insights into high-converting creatives

Understands hooks, retention, and ad fatigue

3. Strong Editing + Design Skills

VideoPremiere Pro / CapCut / Final Cut

DesignPhotoshop (or equivalent)

Ability to create clean, premium, brand-aligned visuals

4. Advanced AI Capability

Able to generate hyper-realistic AI videos and creatives

Experience with AI video, image generation, voiceovers, and scripting

Strong interest in AI and willingness to lead AI adoption within the creative function

5. Ownership, Speed & Execution

Highly productive with a strong bias toward getting things done

Self-motivated (“batteries included”)

Ensures high output — both personally and within the team

Able to take ownership and drive work independently

6. Growth Mindset

Open to learning new tools, skills, and responsibilities

Thrives in a fast-moving, agile environment

Actively keeps up with trends in creative and AI

Nice to Have

Experience with D2C / skincare / beauty brands

Understanding of direct response marketing

Experience working with creative strategists or media buyers

How to Apply

Please include

Portfolio (D2C ads + design + AI-generated work)

2–3 high-performing creatives you’ve worked on (with results if possible)

A short Loom video covering

Your introduction

Your editing & creative approach

Your AI workflow (especially hyper-realistic content)

Why you’re a strong fit

Applications without relevant D2C work will not be considered.

Role Details

Full-time

Remote

Compensation based on experience and performance

Why Join Us

Work directly on high-impact, revenue-driving creatives

Gain exposure to the entire D2C business ecosystem

Be at the forefront of AI implementation in a fast-scaling brand

Continuous learning and growth across creative, marketing, and AI
