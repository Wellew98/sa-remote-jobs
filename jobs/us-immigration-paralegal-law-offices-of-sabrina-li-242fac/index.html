About Us

The Law Offices of Sabrina Li, P.C. is a leading, full-service U.S. immigration law firm with offices in Los Angeles, Pasadena, Santa Ana, and Dallas, as well as international operations across Asia, Africa, and Central and South America. We are dedicated to helping individuals, families, and businesses navigate complex legal challenges with skill, compassion, and integrity.

About the Role

We are seeking an experienced and detail-oriented U.S. Immigration Paralegal to join our growing remote legal team.

The ideal candidate will have proven experience handling U.S. immigration matters and possess a strong understanding of immigration processes, case preparation, and document management. This role requires a highly organized professional who can effectively manage a high-volume caseload, meet strict deadlines, and deliver exceptional client service in a fast-paced legal environment.

Working closely with attorneys and legal support staff, you will play a key role in preparing immigration filings, maintaining case records, communicating with clients, and ensuring matters progress efficiently throughout the immigration process.

Key Responsibilities

Prepare, organize, and file immigration petitions, applications, and supporting documentation.

Draft and review legal documents, declarations, affidavits, recommendation letters, and client correspondence.

Communicate with clients to gather information, request supporting documentation, provide case updates, and respond to inquiries in a professional and timely manner.

Maintain organized and accurate digital case files to support efficient case management and document retrieval.

Manage and update legal case management and CRM systems, including platforms such as PracticePanther, Lawmatics, Docketwise, Pipefile, and Monday.com.

Track filing deadlines, monitor case progress, and assist with scheduling and administrative coordination.

Collaborate closely with attorneys and legal support staff on case preparation, workflow management, and case strategy implementation.

Utilize legal technology and AI-assisted tools to improve productivity, organization, and workflow efficiency.

Consistently meet established performance expectations related to quality, productivity, responsiveness, and client service.

Qualifications

Bachelor of Laws (LLB) degree is required.

Minimum of 2 years of hands-on experience handling U.S. immigration cases (required).

Demonstrated experience preparing and managing U.S. immigration petitions, applications, and supporting documentation.

Experience using legal case management and CRM software is required , including platforms such as Docketwise, PracticePanther, Lawmatics, Pipefile, or similar systems.

Strong drafting, organizational, and multitasking abilities.

Proven ability to manage multiple deadlines while maintaining a high degree of accuracy and attention to detail.

Demonstrated ability to meet performance goals and productivity expectations in a fast-paced legal environment.

Strong proficiency in utilizing AI tools to enhance work product and efficiency is highly preferred.

Excellent written and verbal English communication skills.

Candidates must have a reliable home office setup, including high-speed internet and a backup power solution to ensure consistent availability during power outages.
