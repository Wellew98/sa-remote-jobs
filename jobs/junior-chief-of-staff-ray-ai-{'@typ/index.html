The full posting

Reproduced from the employer’s own listing, unedited.

Read the posting in full · 282 words

You'll work directly with a founder or senior executive running a high-growth company in the US or Europe. Not as a task-taker. As the operational layer that keeps everything moving.

You'll own the full picture: priorities, people, open loops, and decisions. You'll know what's coming before they do. You'll handle what you can without asking, and brief them cleanly on what you can't. You'll be the reason things don't fall through the cracks.

This is not an admin role. It's a high-trust, high-ownership position for someone who wants to build a career in operations, chief of staff, or founder-adjacent leadership.

What you'll own

Track and close open loops across the executive's full workload

Calendar, inbox, and communications management

Meeting prep, decision briefs, and post-call follow-up

Task and priority management across the executive and their team

Research, structured recommendations, and scenario planning

CRM management and reporting

Proactive identification of problems before they surface

Long-term projects with real business impact

Who this is for

3 to 4 years of experience in operations, EA, founder's associate, or a similarly high-ownership role

You move before being asked, and you close the loop without being reminded

Strong written and spoken English, you communicate clearly at executive level

Comfortable managing upyou push back when something doesn't make sense

What you get

Monthly salary up to R 30,000

Performance-based bonuses that can add up to 3 months salary

Modern laptop/hardware provided by us

Specialized training in AI and LLM tools

Access to a community of automation engineers, designers, and senior operators

How we hire

Selection is rigorous. We care about judgment, ownership, and the ability to operate in ambiguity. If you meet the bar, you'll know it quickly.
