Junior Chief of Staff

You'll work directly with a founder or senior executive running a high-growth company in the US or Europe. Not as a task-taker. As the operational layer that keeps everything moving. You'll own the full picture: priorities, people, open loops, and decisions. You'll know what's coming before they do.
