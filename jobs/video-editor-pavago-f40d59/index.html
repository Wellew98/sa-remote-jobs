About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a Video Editor to create engaging, high-performing video content across social media, digital marketing, and training.

This is more than a technical editing role. We’re looking for someone who understands storytelling, hooks, pacing, audience retention, and content performance .

You’ll take raw footage and transform it into polished content including:

Instagram Reels

TikTok videos

YouTube Shorts

YouTube and long-form videos

Paid marketing creatives

Training and educational videos

Brand and corporate content

If you know how to capture attention in the first few seconds, maintain viewer interest, and turn average footage into content people actually want to watch , this role is a strong fit.
