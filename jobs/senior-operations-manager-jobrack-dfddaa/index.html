Lead operations and scale a fast-growing UK content agency as Senior Ops Manager. £3,000–£4,000 | Full-time | UK timezone

We're Jamal and Ali, the founders of

Your Social Currency

  • a fast-growing social media agency working with some of the biggest founders, entrepreneurs, and CEOs in the UK.

Our team helps high-level personal brands create content that actually performs - from raw footage all the way through to publishing and growth. We're building something serious, and we're proud of the calibre of clients we work with and the results we deliver.

We care about clean execution just as much as creative quality, and we're building a team of switched-on people who feel the same way. If that sounds like your kind of environment, you'll fit right in!

We’re looking for a

Senior Operations Manager.

This is a high-level strategic leadership position, not an execution-heavy or admin role. As Jamal and Ali step back from day-to-day operations, you will essentially run the operational engine of the business, leading systems, strategy, and accountability across the agency.

You will architect and optimise agency workflows, standard operating procedures (SOPs), AI-driven automations, and financial oversight. You won't be doing the task-level delivery work yourself—instead, you'll manage and empower our Account Managers and production team leads to deliver exceptional results reliably.

You will serve as the final point of escalation for operational or client delivery issues before they ever reach the founders. Your goal is to build an autonomous, scalable business structure that runs smoothly without founder friction.

You will

own overall operational architecture, agency efficiency, and operational profitability

build, refine, and enforce SOPs, quality benchmarks, and automated workflows across the agency

manage, mentor, and hold Account Managers accountable for team output and client satisfaction

leverage AI tools, integrations, and modern software to streamline production cycles

maintain financial oversight, monitoring margins, capacity planning, and resource allocation

act as the final firewall and escalation point for major client or delivery risks before founder involvement

drive strategic growth and scaling initiatives in tandem with executive vision

You are not

doing day-to-day editing, clipping, or content task execution

managing individual task cards or acting as a project coordinator

a frontline account manager dealing with daily client queries

an executive assistant or admin support worker

You are the operational leader ensuring the business operates with complete stability, profitability, and accountability.

If you naturally think

“How do we automate this?” / “Is this process scalable?” / “Are our margins healthy?” / “How do I empower my team to solve this without me?” …then you’ll thrive in this role.

In this role, you will

📋 Design and oversee scalable operational systems and agency-wide SOPs

🤖 Implement AI tools and custom workflow automations to eliminate manual overhead

👥 Lead, manage, and hold Account Managers accountable for client health and team delivery

💰 Oversee operational finances, resource allocation, software costs, and delivery margins

🛡️ Serve as the final escalation buffer for high-level risks before they reach founders

🔄 Optimize end-to-end workflows from client onboarding through to ongoing retention

📅 Lead strategic operational reviews and high-level leadership alignment meetings

📈 Forecast capacity needs and plan recruitment strategy alongside company growth

🧹 Drive operational excellence, governance, and accountability culture cross-functionally

⚡ Enable the founders to focus on strategic vision while you run day-to-day operations

This is a full-time role aligned with UK hours (9am–5pm, Monday to Friday).

What Success Looks Like

The team starts Monday with complete clarity

Work moves daily instead of in bursts of panic

Deliverables don’t sit unassigned or forgotten

Editors and support staff know exactly what they own

Clients aren’t chasing for updates

Meetings become shorter because the operational reality is already known

Deadlines are hit consistently

Problems are surfaced before they become emergencies

The business feels calm, controlled, and operationally sharp

To excel in this role, you need

✅ 3–7+ years of operations leadership experience in small to mid-sized agency environments

✅ Proven track record of designing SOPs, scaling systems, and driving organizational accountability

✅ High fluency with AI tools, workflow automation platforms (e.g. Make, Zapier), and modern tech stacks

✅ Experience managing leaders and Account Managers rather than direct execution staff

✅ Strong financial oversight skills (margins, budgeting, capacity planning, cost management)

✅ Exceptional executive communication, conflict resolution, and problem-solving abilities

✅ Decisive leadership style with the confidence to run the company operations autonomously

Brownie points for

⭐ Direct experience in fast-moving social media, video content, or creative production agencies

⭐ Experience aiding founder transition from operational management to strategic guidance

This role is a great fit for someone who dislikes vague ownership, messy processes, and unfinished loops.

The kind of person who naturally askswho owns this, when is it due, and what’s blocking progress?

Someone who stays calm under pressure while still creating urgency when needed, and who brings structure to fast-moving environments without creating confusion or drama.

You won’t wait for problems to become obvious before stepping in. You spot risks early, keep things moving, and take pride in clean execution, clear communication, and smooth operations.

If managing lots of moving parts at once sounds energising rather than overwhelming, this role will likely feel very natural to you!

You’ll Probably Struggle In This Role If…

You dislike chasing people or enforcing accountability

You prefer brainstorming over operational execution

You wait for founders to create structure for you

You struggle to manage lots of moving pieces simultaneously

You avoid difficult follow-ups when deadlines slip

You rely on reminders instead of proactively driving workflow forward

You confuse being busy with actually being in control

What’s in it for you?

💷 £3,000–£4,000 per month*

💼 Full-time role, fully remote (with UK time zone alignment)

🏖️ Minimum 20 days paid holiday + public holidays

🩺 Paid sick leave

📚 Investment in your growth - training, courses, and ongoing development

🚀 Genuine progression opportunities as the company scales - we promote from within

🌍 Flexibility around working location, as long as UK hours are maintained

*Please note that this is a gross salary and that you are responsible for any tax-related procedures in your country of residence. We kindly ask you to apply only if you are happy to work remotely as an independent contractor.
