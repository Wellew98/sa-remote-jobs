About the Role

Step into a high-impact role as a Cold Caller for a dynamic real estate investment firm, where your charisma and confidence turn leads into lucrative property deals. You will act as the vital first point of contact, using your "think-on-your-feet" intuition to identify motivated sellers and qualify opportunities that fit our strategic "buy box." This is a thrilling opportunity to master high-level acquisitions, handle objections with ease, and directly drive the growth of our portfolio by securing key appointments for our principals.

Key Responsibilities

  • Source and qualify potential property leads by using existing lists and researching new prospects that fit the company’s specific "buy box" criteria.
  • Set high-quality appointments for the acquisitions team by identifying motivated sellers and gathering essential property details.
  • Handle and overcome objections with ease, using rapport-building techniques to keep conversations productive and professional.
  • Communicate clearly and effectively with property owners to build trust and create a positive first impression of the company.
  • Manage all lead data and appointment scheduling accurately within the GoHighLevel (GHL) CRM system.
  • Capture critical information regarding owner motivation and property condition to ensure the follow-up team is prepared to make an offer.

Key Qualifications

  • 3+ years of experience in real estate, ideally commercial real estate
  • Familiarity with real estate CRMs and platforms, GoHighLevel preferred
  • Strong phone presence and ability to strategize to get leads to sign up
  • Maintain a professional and resilient attitude, consistently representing the company’s brand values during every interaction.
  • Availability for full-time, exclusive work with our company (not juggling multiple clients)
  • Excellent English communication skillsIdeally have prior experience in sales development, lead generation, or real estate
  • Skills You Should Have:

Project natural charisma and confidence on every call to build immediate rapport with property owners.

  • Think on your feet to navigate conversations naturally without relying strictly on a script.
  • Maintain resilience and a positive attitude when handling objections or challenging callers.
  • Demonstrate coachability by eagerly learning the company’s specific real estate processes and CRM tools.
  • Adapt quickly to feedback to constantly improve your outreach and appointment-setting results.
  • Prioritize a friendly demeanor to uncover seller motivations and build long-term trust.
