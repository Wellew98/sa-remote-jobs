Performance Marketing Specialist (Google Ads, Meta & SEO)

Location / Employment Details

  • Location: Fully remote, open to candidates in any region
  • Schedule: 20 hours per week for the first 3 months, moving to full-time (40 hours per week) from month 4
  • Hours: Flexible. No U.S. time-zone requirement, with a weekly sync and responsiveness around campaign launches
  • Reports to: Company leadership
  • Brands supported: Multiple brands within one portfolio, with more expected to be added

About the Company

We are a U.S.-based company that operates a small portfolio of online businesses, including an e-commerce brand and a digital services brand.

We run lean, so every hire carries real ownership. We are growing, and new brands and ad accounts are expected to join over time. That means more accounts for this role to build and manage.

About the Role

We are moving our paid marketing in-house from an outside agency, and this role owns that work. Our ad accounts exist but are not yet mature. Tracking, campaign structure, and spend efficiency all need a strong operator to sharpen them.

You will take over the existing Google Ads and Meta accounts, rebuild what is not working, and launch new accounts as the portfolio grows. You will also own SEO so each brand builds organic traffic alongside paid.

The role starts at 20 hours a week for the first 3 months. That gives you time to audit the accounts, take over from the agency, and show early wins. From month 4 it moves to full-time as the portfolio and workload grow. This is a hands-on role for someone who can manage several brands and budgets at once and report clearly on what the spend is producing.

Key Responsibilities

Agency Transition & Account Audit

  • Take over account access, history, and active campaigns from the outgoing agency without losing performance
  • Audit each Google Ads and Meta account for structure, tracking, wasted spend, and quick wins
  • Deliver a 30-day plan for each brand covering priorities, budget split, and target metrics

Paid Search (Google Ads)

  • Build, manage, and optimize Search, Shopping, Performance Max, and remarketing campaigns
  • Own keyword research, negative keyword lists, ad copy testing, and bid strategy
  • Manage product feeds and Google Merchant Center for the e-commerce brand

Paid Social (Meta)

  • Plan and run Facebook and Instagram campaigns across prospecting, retargeting, and retention
  • Build audiences, test creative and offers, and scale what performs
  • Brief and source ad creative, and give clear direction on what to make next

SEO

  • Run technical SEO audits and fix site issues that hurt rankings
  • Lead keyword research and on-page optimization for product, category, and service pages
  • Plan content that builds organic traffic for each brand

Tracking, Reporting & Budget

  • Set up and maintain conversion tracking, Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, and Meta Pixel / Conversions API
  • Manage ad budgets across brands and move spend toward the best-performing channels
  • Report weekly on spend, ROAS, CPA, and revenue, with clear recommendations

Channel Expansion

  • Support email campaigns, TikTok, and Pinterest as priorities allow
  • Launch and set up ad accounts for new brands as they join the portfolio

Required Qualifications

  • 3+ years managing Google Ads and Meta campaigns hands-on, not only overseeing an agency
  • Proven results you can show with numbers, such as ROAS, CPA, or revenue growth on accounts you ran
  • Experience managing more than one brand or client account at the same time
  • Hands-on SEO experience, including technical audits, keyword research, and on-page optimization
  • Working knowledge of conversion tracking with GA4, Google Tag Manager, and Meta Pixel / Conversions API
  • Strong written and spoken English for clear weekly reporting
  • Available for 20 hours per week now, and for full-time from month 4

Preferred Qualifications

  • E-commerce experience, especially Shopping campaigns and Google Merchant Center
  • Experience with email marketing platforms (a plus)
  • TikTok Ads and Pinterest Ads experience
  • Has taken over accounts from an agency or rebuilt underperforming accounts before
  • Background in e-commerce or consumer products marketing (a plus)
  • Google Ads and Meta certifications

Ideal Candidate Profile

You are

  • A hands-on operator who builds and fixes campaigns yourself
  • Numbers-driven, judging every dollar of spend by what it returns
  • Organized enough to run several brands and budgets without dropping details
  • Proactive. You spot what is broken, propose the fix, and act on it without waiting to be asked
  • A clear communicator who explains results in plain language and flags problems early
  • Trustworthy with access to ad accounts and budgets, and transparent about where the money goes
  • Comfortable in a lean company where you own the outcome, not just the tasks

Why Join Us

  • Real ownership: You will be the in-house marketing lead, not one voice among an agency team
  • A clear path to full-time: The role grows from 20 hours to full-time in month 4
  • Growing scope: New brands and ad accounts will be added over time for you to build from the ground up
  • Direct line to leadership: Your recommendations go straight to the people who make the decisions, with no layers in between
  • Flexible and remote: Work from anywhere, on a schedule that fits your time zone
