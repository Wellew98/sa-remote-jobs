About the Role

We are a fast-growing US-based search firm building a high-performing offshore recruiting team to support large-scale sales hiring. This role is focused on identifying, engaging, and pre-screening top sales talent to support high-volume hiring.

Key Responsibilities

- Conduct cold outreach to sales candidates via LinkedIn, job boards, and databases

- Perform 15–20 minute phone screens assessing sales aptitude, communication skills, and cultural fit

- Shortlist qualified candidates and schedule interviews with US-based hiring managers

- Maintain accurate candidate notes and ensure pipeline hygiene

- Support high-volume recruiting with speed and consistency

-

Provide insights on candidate market trends

Required Hard Skills

- 2–3+ years recruiting experience, sales recruiting experience mandatory

- Proven high-volume sourcing and outreach capability

- Strong phone screening and candidate assessment skills

-

Confident spoken English

- Experience recruiting US-based candidates

- Emotional intelligence and candidate-read skills

-

Comfortable with aggressive outreach cadence

Preferred

- Experience in home services, pest control, or blue-collar sales

- Familiarity with screening tools or structured scorecards

Required Soft Skills

- Confident communicator and self-starter

- High energy, resilience, and ability to work under pressure

-

Comfortable pushing conversations forward

- Strong judgment under speed and volume

Professional US-facing demeanor
