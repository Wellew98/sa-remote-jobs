Operations Director

(Healthcare Subscription Business – Confidential)

LocationRemote

Employment TypeFull-Time

Reports ToExecutive Leadership

Confidential Search

Role Overview

We are seeking a highly experienced Operations Leader to fully own and scale a high-volume healthcare subscription business line . This individual will act as the operator of record , with end-to-end accountability across patient onboarding, technology systems, team management, and recurring monthly subscription fulfillment.

This is a hands-on leadership role for someone who has previously run complex, recurring operations at scale—owning both people and systems , not just executing tasks. The ideal candidate is calm under pressure, deeply process-oriented, metrics-driven, and comfortable operating in regulated, workflow-heavy environments.

Key Responsibilities

End-to-End Operational Ownership

-

Own the full operational lifecycleintake → onboarding → subscription setup → recurring fulfillment → delivery

- Ensure accurate, timely, and error-free execution of monthly subscriptions at scale

- Act as the single point of accountability for operational outcomes and performance

Systems & Technology Leadership

- Own and optimize CRM systems, automations, and operational tooling

- Design, document, and enforce workflows across patient onboarding and subscription management

- Ensure data accuracy, system reliability, and seamless handoffs between teams and systems

- Identify and implement process improvements to increase efficiency and reduce operational risk

Team Leadership & Management

- Build, manage, and scale cross-functional operational teams

- Set clear expectations, KPIs, and accountability structures

- Coach, develop, and performance-manage team members

- Create SOPs, training materials, and documentation to support consistent execution

Performance, Metrics & Quality Control

- Own operational KPIs including volume, accuracy, turnaround times, and retention

- Monitor and report on operational performance and bottlenecks

- Proactively identify issues before they impact patients or revenue

- Drive continuous improvement through data-driven decision making

Compliance & Process Discipline

- Operate effectively within regulated or process-heavy environments

- Ensure workflows align with internal standards and external requirements

- Maintain operational rigor while scaling volume

Required Experience & Qualifications

- 8+ years of experience in operations leadership roles

- Proven experience running high-volume, recurring subscription or service operations

- Strong background owning both systems and people , not just execution

- Hands-on experience with CRMs, workflow design, and automation tools

- Demonstrated ability to build and manage teams at scale

- Strong process design, documentation, and operational problem-solving skills

- Metrics-driven mindset with clear ownership mentality

Preferred Backgrounds

- Healthcare, insurance, medical services, logistics, or other regulated industries

- Subscription-based or recurring revenue business models

- Experience managing complex onboarding and fulfillment workflows

- Prior “operator of record” or general manager-style responsibility

What Success Looks Like (First 6–12 Months)

- Fully owned and stabilized onboarding and subscription fulfillment operations

- Clear, documented workflows with minimal errors and escalations

- Scaled team operating efficiently with defined KPIs

- Improved operational throughput, accuracy, and patient experience

- Leadership team confident in operational visibility and predictability

Why This Role

This is a high-impact leadership opportunity for someone who wants true ownership , not a support role. You will be trusted to run the operation, improve it, and scale it—while directly impacting patient experience and business growth.
