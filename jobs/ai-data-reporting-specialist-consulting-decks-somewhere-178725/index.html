Opportunity Overview

We are looking for an analytical, highly technical AI & Data Reporting Specialist who operates like a blend between a data engineer, a management consultant, and a visual storybuilder.

In this role, you will go into the "data lab"—taking raw, complex organizational data, feeding it into advanced AI models (specifically Claude ), and transforming the output into hyper-polished, executive-ready presentation decks and financial/operational reports for leadership to present to enterprise clients.

You will not be required to present to clients initially; your focus is raw execution—cleaning complex data sets, prompting AI engines, maintaining data accuracy, and crafting visually stunning, institutional-grade PowerPoint/Google Slides decks.

Key Responsibilities

1. Claude Master Data Integration & Prompt Architecture

  • AI & Claude Mastery: Leverage Claude and advanced prompt engineering to synthesize master data sets, extract high-level insights, and generate structured report outlines.
  • Process & Skill Optimization: Own, refine, and upgrade our internal AI prompts, data skills, and custom reporting workflows to continuously elevate quality and speed.

2. Visual Storytelling & Deck Building

  • Executive Presentation Design: Build detailed, highly aesthetic, client-ready presentation decks based on internal templates and brand guidelines.
  • Data Visualization: Translate raw metrics, charts, and AI-driven analyses into clean, easy-to-read, visual slide decks.
  • Precision QA: Conduct rigorous quality audits on all data points, formulas, and slide formatting to guarantee 100% accuracy before leadership presentations.

3. Data Infrastructure & Continuous Improvement

  • Data Cleanliness: Organize, clean, and structure raw data inputs coming from various operational platforms.
  • System Enhancements: Identify technical gaps in how data is processed, proposing systemic upgrades to make internal reporting faster and more robust.

Candidate Profile & Qualifications

  • Professional Background: 3+ years in data analytics, management consulting (e.g., Big 4 / Deloitte-style rigor), quantitative finance, or data engineering.
  • Advanced AI Skillset: Power user of Claude , ChatGPT, and LLM frameworks for data analysis and document generation.
  • Presentation Mastery: World-class skills in PowerPoint / Google Slides—able to make complex data look elegant, clean, and professional.
  • Methodical Precision: Uncompromising attention to detail regarding data accuracy, visual alignment, and consistency.
  • Fluent English: C1/C2 professional English fluency.
