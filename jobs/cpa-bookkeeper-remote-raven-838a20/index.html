We are seeking a highly disciplined, detail-driven

Bookkeeper

to take full ownership of financial records across a growing, multi-location studio environment. This is not a transactional or data-entry role — it requires end-to-end control of the books, from daily journal entries through executive-level monthly financial reporting.

You will be the single point of accountability for the integrity of our client's books. You will manage QuickBooks Online as the system of record, reconcile multiple bank, credit card, and loan accounts, match Stripe payment processor deposits against revenue reports from Mariana Tek (the studio management platform), and deliver a complete monthly financial statement package to C-Suite leadership. Your work directly supports decisions about expansion, pricing strategy, and investor reporting.

This role is best suited for a bookkeeper who has already closed books independently for at least three years, who treats reconciliation discrepancies as puzzles to solve rather than items to defer, and who can produce a clean Balance Sheet and Income Statement without being walked through it. You will operate with significant autonomy — leadership expects the books to be current, accurate, and audit-ready without prompting.

Multi-location accounting is central to this role. Revenue must be allocated correctly across studio locations, and the bookkeeper must understand how to track membership income, class pack sales, retail, events, and teacher training separately. Familiarity with fitness or wellness CRM platforms is strongly preferred because the revenue allocation work is where most candidates without prior service-business experience struggle.

Responsibilities

Financial Management & Reconciliation

Own and manage QuickBooks Online (QBO) including all journal entries and account categorization

Reconcile bank accounts and credit cards monthly with full accuracy and supporting documentation

Perform monthly balance sheet account reconciliations with complete workpapers

Reconcile Stripe transactions and match deposits to revenue reports from Mariana Tek

Manage and reconcile loan account activity on a monthly basis

Reporting & Insights

Prepare and deliver a full Monthly Financial Statement Package: Balance Sheet, Income Statement, Trailing 12-Month Income Statement, and location-level breakdowns

Flag and clearly explain variances, trends, and anomalies to management in writing

Support cash flow reporting and forecasting

Develop and maintain budgets in collaboration with leadership

Prepare financials for investor packages and strategic planning as needed

Revenue & Subscription Allocation

Review and enter Mariana Tek revenue reports into QBO accurately and consistently

Allocate subscriptions across studio locations with precision

Track and categorize income from memberships, class packs, teacher training, events, and retail

Vendor & Expense Management

Process and schedule vendor payments on time

Track recurring subscriptions and ensure timely payment

Maintain organized digital records of all vendor invoices and payment history

Monitor expense categories and identify efficiency opportunities

Payroll & Contractor Oversight

Prepare and enter payroll data into Paychex and QBO for timely processing

Track contractor payments and support year-end 1099 reporting

Ensure compensation aligns with studio policies

Compliance & Special Projects

Support audit requests and tax preparation in collaboration with the CPA

Assist with licensing, insurance, and sales tax filings as needed

Maintain audit-ready financial records

Lead financial components of studio expansion, new programming, or pricing model changes
