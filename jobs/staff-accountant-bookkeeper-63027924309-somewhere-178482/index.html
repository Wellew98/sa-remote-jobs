About The Role

We are looking for an experienced Staff Accountant / Bookkeeper to take ownership of the company's day-to-day accounting operations, billing, and accounts receivable processes.

This is a highly impactful role for someone who enjoys both hands-on bookkeeping and process improvement. The right person will streamline existing workflows, manage high transaction volumes, improve billing operations, and become a trusted member of the leadership team as the company continues to grow.

While the immediate priority is bookkeeping and AR, there is long-term opportunity to expand into more advanced accounting, reporting, and financial planning responsibilities.

What You’ll Do

Bookkeeping & Accounting

  • Own the day-to-day bookkeeping function
  • Maintain accurate financial records in QuickBooks
  • Classify and reconcile transactions
  • Perform monthly bank and account reconciliations
  • Assist with month-end close activities
  • Maintain organized financial documentation

Accounts Receivable & Billing

  • Create and distribute client invoices
  • Manage ongoing accounts receivable
  • Follow up on outstanding invoices and collections
  • Monitor payment activity and ensure timely collections
  • Set up new customer accounts and billing profiles
  • Support billing inquiries from internal stakeholders

Process Improvement

  • Help simplify and improve existing accounting workflows
  • Work across multiple invoicing platforms during the company's system transition
  • Identify opportunities for automation and operational efficiencies
  • Build scalable finance processes that support company growth

Financial Operations

  • Support expense tracking and reporting
  • Assist leadership with financial reporting as needed
  • Partner closely with the Co-founder to gradually transition ownership of finance operations
  • As the company grows, provide support for budgeting, forecasting, and financial planning initiatives

Qualifications & Skills

Required

  • 3–5+ years of bookkeeping or accounting experience
  • Strong QuickBooks experience
  • Experience managing accounts receivable and billing
  • Experience reconciling high transaction volumes
  • Strong understanding of accounting principles
  • Excellent organizational skills and attention to detail
  • Comfortable working independently
  • Excellent written and verbal English communication skills

Preferred

  • Staff Accountant experience
  • CPA designation (or equivalent accounting qualification)
  • Experience supporting a marketing agency, advertising agency, or professional services business
  • Experience improving accounting processes and workflows
  • Exposure to financial reporting, budgeting, or FP&A
  • Experience with multiple billing or invoicing systems is highly preferred, E.G.:
  • Fat Merchant Stax (Client Billing)
  • Hyphen (Client Billing Platform)
  • Heartland Payroll (Payroll)

Why Join Us

  • Join a growing, established digital marketing agency
  • Work directly with the company's founders and leadership team
  • Make an immediate impact by improving core business operations
  • Own a critical function with significant autonomy
  • Opportunity to grow into broader accounting, finance, and FP&A responsibilities as the company continues to scale
  • Be part of a collaborative, data-driven organization that values continuous improvement

What Success Looks Like

Within your first few months, you will

  • Successfully transition bookkeeping responsibilities from the current external accounting partner
  • Take ownership of day-to-day bookkeeping and AR operations
  • Reduce the amount of finance administration handled by the Co-founder
  • Improve billing accuracy and consistency
  • Create more efficient, scalable accounting processes
  • Become the go-to person for the company's accounting operations
