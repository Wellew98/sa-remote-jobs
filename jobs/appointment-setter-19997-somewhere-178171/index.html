Appointment Setter (Health & Life Insurance)

Overview

Our client is a Health and Life Insurance Broker focused on helping individuals find the right coverage through a consultative, high-touch sales process. They are seeking a dedicated Appointment Setter to support their book of business by handling high-volume outreach and scheduling qualified prospects directly onto their calendar.

This is a pure appointment-setting role—no closing required. The ideal candidate thrives in a fast-paced, phone- and SMS-heavy environment, can move quickly through lead lists, and is skilled at creating strong first impressions that lead to booked meetings.

Key Responsibilities

  • Conduct high-volume outbound calling and SMS outreach to warm and purchased leads
  • Engage prospects quickly and confidently to secure scheduled appointments
  • Gather basic client information (name, DOB, address, phone, etc.)
  • Collect light qualifying details to help guide plan fit (no underwriting or disqualification)
  • Book appointments directly onto the broker’s calendar with clean handoffs
  • Maintain a simple, efficient pitch focused on getting prospects scheduled
  • Work exclusively within one broker’s book of business
  • Track activity and manage follow-ups within the CRM
  • Maintain consistent daily outreach volume and performance metrics

Qualifications

  • 2–5 years of experience in appointment setting, sales development, or outbound sales
  • Strong phone presence and confidence in high-volume calling environments
  • Comfortable with SMS-based communication and fast response times
  • Excellent communication and rapport-building skills
  • Organized and detail-oriented with strong follow-up habits
  • Ability to work independently in a remote environment
  • Results-driven with a focus on booking meetings, not selling

Preferred Experience (Not Required)

  • Health or life insurance industry exposure
  • Experience working with purchased lead sources (QuoteWizard, etc.)
  • Familiarity with VanillaSoft dialing systems
  • Experience using Ringy CRM
  • Exposure to NextGen or Benepath lead systems

Working Hours

  • Monday–Friday
  • Must start at 8:00 AM EST
  • Split shift preferred:
  • 8:00 AM – 12:00 PM EST (core hours)
  • Flexible afternoon/evening hours (e.g., 1:00–5:00 PM or 2:00–6:00 PM EST)
  • ~40 hours per week

Tools & Systems

  • CRM/Communication: Ringy (primary), SMS-heavy outreach
  • Dialer/Leads: VanillaSoft with purchased leads (primarily QuoteWizard + other vendors)

What the Client Provides

  • Consistent flow of purchased leads
  • Established systems and dialing infrastructure
  • Clear expectations and streamlined booking process
  • Remote-first work environment (broker is primarily WFH)
  • Focused role with no closing responsibilities
