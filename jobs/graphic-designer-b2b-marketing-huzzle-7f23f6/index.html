About Huzzle

At Huzzle, we connect exceptional talent with top opportunities at leading companies across the UK, US, Canada, Europe, and Australia. Our clients include fast-growing startups, digital agencies, SaaS companies, and tech-enabled businesses across industries such as MarTech, FinTech, EdTech, and eCommerce. Unlike traditional outsourcing agencies, we place you directly with innovative companies where you become a valued member of the team.

About the company

Our client is a premier B2B growth agency that bridges the gap between high-level strategy and tangible sales results. Based in the UK with a global reach across major hubs like London, New York, and Dublin, the team provides an integrated "go-to-market as a service" model. They specialize in deep market research, creative brand positioning, demand generation, and expert lead generation for B2B founders and GTM leaders in the SaaS, Fintech, and professional services sectors. By combining intelligence-driven insights with proactive outreach, they help businesses in the £1M to £25M ARR range scale their pipelines and secure high-quality meetings with global enterprises.

The company operates with a focus on transparency, methodology, and senior-led strategy, moving away from templated campaigns in favor of research-backed execution. The team thrives on high-impact work, delivering measurable growth for clients in emerging and established markets alike. Joining this organization means working in a collaborative, fast-paced environment where data-driven decisions and creative execution meet to redefine how modern B2B organizations acquire customers and build long-term market influence.

Role TypeFull-time

Job Summary

As a Graphic Designer at the heart of this creative team, you will be the visual storyteller responsible for elevating brand presence across every digital touchpoint. This isn't just about making things look good; it’s about designing high-impact visual content that stops the scroll, drives demand, and generates real results for a global portfolio of ambitious clients. If you are a creative problem-solver who thrives in a fast-paced environment and wants to see your work define the visual identity of industry-leading campaigns, this is the role for you.

Key Responsibilities

Social Media Design

Conceptualize and execute high-quality graphics for LinkedIn and other major platforms, including carousel posts, branded visuals, and thought leadership assets.

Brand & Campaign Design

Develop creative concepts for demand generation activities, designing visuals for webinars, events, lead-gen reports, and promotional content.

Website & Digital Design

Support web projects by creating page designs, custom icons, illustrations, and visual elements that bridge the gap between aesthetics and user experience.

Guide & Asset Creation

Transform complex ideas into polished marketing assets, such as professional eBooks, whitepapers, and downloadable resources with a focus on readability and layout.

Creative Development

Continually push the boundaries of the team's creative standards by testing new formats, staying ahead of design trends, and evolving client brand aesthetics.

Collaboration & Delivery

Manage projects from concept to completion, working closely with marketers, videographers, and leadership to meet deadlines in a high-growth environment.

Design Excellence

A strong portfolio demonstrating a modern aesthetic and an eye for high-end, engaging visual design.

Technical Mastery

High proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign) and a deep understanding of Canva for collaborative workflows.

Social Media Savvy

A proven understanding of what captures attention on professional platforms like LinkedIn and how to design for engagement and performance.

Detail-Oriented

A meticulous approach to design, ensuring every pixel is aligned and every asset is polished before delivery.

Organization Skills

The ability to prioritize multiple projects and meet tight deadlines without compromising on quality.

Growth Mindset

An ambitious and curious nature, with a desire to learn new tools and contribute to the overall creative strategy of the business.

Nice-to-Haves

Experience working within a fast-paced agency environment.

Knowledge of UI/UX principles and how they apply to landing page design.

Experience with motion graphics or video editing elements to enhance static designs.

Prior experience working on B2B marketing or demand generation campaigns.
