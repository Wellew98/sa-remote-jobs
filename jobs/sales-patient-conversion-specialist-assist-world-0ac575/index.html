About the Role

We are recruiting on behalf of a rapidly growing medical aesthetics and wellness practice looking for a  high-performing Remote Sales & Patient Conversion Specialist  to join the team.

This is  not a traditional appointment-setting role . The primary objective is to generate revenue by following up with leads, converting consultations into high-value treatments, and reactivating existing patients who have previously expressed interest but never booked.

The ideal candidate will have  proven sales-closing experience within the med spa, aesthetics, beauty, or healthcare space  and be confident discussing treatments and guiding prospective clients toward making a purchasing decision.

The role will initially be  part-time (approximately 20 hours/week) , with the potential to grow into a full-time position as the practice continues to scale.

Key Responsibilities

  • Follow up promptly with new inbound leads via phone, SMS, email, and CRM
  • Qualify prospective patients and understand their treatment goals
  • Conduct consultative sales conversations and confidently close high-ticket treatment packages
  • Follow up with prospects who have not yet committed and overcome common objections
  • Reactivate dormant and previously lost leads from the existing patient database
  • Identify opportunities to upsell and cross-sell appropriate treatments and packages
  • Maintain consistent follow-up sequences to maximize conversion
  • Manage and update lead and patient information within the CRM
  • Track prospects through the sales pipeline and ensure no opportunities are missed
  • Coordinate appointments once a client has committed
  • Maintain excellent communication and a premium client experience throughout the sales process
  • Provide feedback on lead quality, objections, conversion rates, and opportunities to improve the sales process
  • Assist with general administrative tasks related to sales and patient coordination when required

What We're Looking For

  • Proven experience as a sales closer , preferably within a med spa, aesthetics, cosmetic, beauty, wellness, or healthcare environment
  • Demonstrated ability to sell  high-ticket services or treatment packages
  • Comfortable handling sales conversations over the phone and through messaging
  • Confident, persuasive, and personable without being overly "salesy"
  • Strong objection-handling and negotiation skills
  • Highly proactive with excellent follow-up habits
  • Comfortable working toward revenue and conversion targets
  • Able to work independently and take ownership of the sales pipeline
  • Strong written and spoken English
  • Excellent organizational and administrative skills
  • Experience working with a CRM;  GoHighLevel (GHL)  experience is highly preferred
  • Comfortable learning and discussing aesthetic treatments and services

Ideal Candidate

The ideal candidate is a  natural closer who understands the med spa customer journey .

You should be commercially minded and motivated by results, while still being empathetic and professional when dealing with patients. You understand that selling aesthetics is about understanding what a client wants, building trust, educating them appropriately, and helping them feel confident in their decision.

We're particularly interested in candidates who have previously sold  high-value aesthetic treatments, packages, memberships, or procedures , rather than candidates whose experience is limited to booking appointments.

Performance & Growth

This position has a strong  performance-based component , with commission opportunities tied directly to closed revenue.

The structure is designed to reward candidates who consistently generate results:

  • 5% commission  on closed revenue
  • 8% commission  when achieving 10+ deals per month
  • 10% commission  on successfully reactivating leads from the existing patient database

There is significant opportunity for the right person to grow with the business and transition from part-time to full-time as patient volume increases.

Schedule

  • Part-time: approximately 20 hours/week to start
  • Flexible scheduling around the practice's needs, with availability during key sales and patient-contact hours
  • Potential to progress to  full-time  as the business grows

What Success Looks Like

Success in this role means

  • New leads are contacted quickly and consistently
  • More consultations turn into paying patients
  • High-value treatment packages are being closed
  • Old and dormant leads are being reactivated
  • The practice has fewer missed sales opportunities
  • The owner is no longer the bottleneck for sales and follow-up
  • Revenue generated by the role consistently exceeds the cost of the hire

This is an excellent opportunity for an experienced med spa sales professional who wants to take ownership of a growing practice's sales pipeline and be directly rewarded for the revenue they generate.
