Wholesale and Operations Manager

Hire Overseas Johannesburg, South Africa (Remote) Sales & Business Development Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

We're looking for a Wholesale and Operations Manager to own the entire wholesale function end to end for a fast-moving, design-led greeting cards and gifting brand. This is the single most important operational hire in the business. You will manage existing retail accounts across the UK, EU, USA, Canada, and Australia, open new ones, develop product with the design team, source and negotiate with suppliers, process orders, book freight, invoice buyers, and forecast revenue — all while using AI tools daily to work faster and to a higher standard. This is a rare blend of commercial sales, product development, and operational execution. You need to be equally comfortable charming a buyer, negotiating a quote with an overseas supplier, and getting a tax code right on an invoice. Attention to detail is non-negotiable. A single wrong SKU, currency, or missed booking deadline can bounce an invoice or delay a shipment. If you are commercially sharp, operationally precise, highly responsive, and genuinely enthusiastic about using AI in your daily workflow, this role is a strong fit. Why You'll Want to Join You will be paid in USD (bi-monthly: every 15th and 30th) Paid Time Off in accordance with company policy Observance of Holidays per company guidelines 100% remote setup so you can work wherever you're most productive This role requires availability during UK business hours Own a substantial, multi-region wholesale revenue line with direct impact on business growth Work inside a lean, AI-first company with a strong consumer brand and a growing global retail footprint What You'll Work On New Business Development and Outreach Consistently reach out to new retail buyers and wholesale contacts by email week in and week out, working to a target of dozens of fresh contacts per week Build and maintain a structured prospect pipeline and outreach tracker across Hot, Warm, Cold, Stalled, and Won stages Share product catalogues tailored to each buyer's vertical and encourage sample requests, coordinating production, labelling, and delivery through to buyer feedback Follow up relentlessly so no warm lead ever goes cold Target and open at least two significant new retail accounts per year across the UK, EU, and USA Retail Buyer and Account Management Own day-to-day relationships across a portfolio of established retail accounts spanning major off-price chains, department stores, book and gift retailers, specialty retailers, and online wholesale marketplaces across multiple regions Present card, gift bag, gift wrap, puzzle, notebook, and colouring book ranges to buyers proactively and in response to buyer briefs Adapt communication and range strategy to each buyer's calendar, price architecture, and ordering patterns Manage multi-country and multi-banner accounts with separate order streams, specs, and requirements per retailer Product Development and Design Briefing Take buyer requests and market opportunities and brief the design team with clear specs, sizing, mock-ups, and references Build catalogue and range presentations, mark up mock-ups, and approve production templates and physical samples before bulk production Drive new product development into the wholesale range and spot new category opportunities Manage seasonal design pipelines to strict timelines so samples are ready for buyer review well ahead of each buying season Supplier Sourcing, Quotes, and Negotiation Work with and get quotes from suppliers in China and the UK and source new suppliers when needed Negotiate and confirm pricing on each order accounting for quantity, special finishes, and exchange rates Manage supplier deposits and balance payments and maintain responsive communication across time zones Source new factories against quality and ethical standards including valid ethical audit certification such as BSCI, SMETA, or WRAP Unit Economics, Pricing, and Margin Calculate the true landed cost and margin of every product and order, layering in logistics, freight, forwarder charges, duties, labels, and packaging Validate every supplier quote against the margin model before confirming an order Present numbers, margin, and recommendations to the CEO clearly so commercial decisions can be made quickly Order Processing and Purchase Order Management Review purchase orders from buyers the moment they arrive, checking SKU codes, agreed prices, currency, quantities, and delivery and cancel dates before processing Handle multi-part and multi-destination purchase orders and sequence order flow correctly when multiple POs land together Complete sales orders and reconcile linked purchase orders in the order and inventory system Ensure every shipment is correctly invoiced and nothing ships un-invoiced Logistics, Freight, and Retailer Compliance Book freight to strict, KPI-tracked deadlines across multiple freight forwarder and retailer portals Manage export documentation and proof of delivery, matching shipping documents to the correct orders before invoicing Own retailer compliance including submitting supplier and factory documentation, tracking certificate expiries, and meeting each retailer's packing, labelling, and specification requirements Invoicing and Financial Reconciliation Run the end-to-end invoicing process including completing sales orders, pushing through to the accounting system, and submitting via retailer portals with required documentation Handle account-specific nuances including correct tax treatment, currency, self-billing customers, and portal submissions Carry out month-end reconciliation to ensure every completed order is invoiced in the correct accounting month Reconcile supplier and forwarder invoices, convert currencies where needed, and avoid double-payments Forecasting and Reporting Run projections throughout the year to forecast revenue by account and by product against the annual wholesale goal Maintain a live forecast versus actual view, flag gaps and opportunities early, and project what will ship and invoice the following month Feed clear, numbers-led updates to the CEO and weekly wholesale reporting AI-Enabled Workflow Use Claude and other AI tools daily for drafting and personalising buyer outreach at scale, extracting data from purchase orders, generating purchase orders and import-ready files, researching competitors and new product opportunities, and drafting buyer communications Always double-check AI output for accuracy before use Continuously identify ways to simplify and automate admin so more time goes to selling and growing accounts What You Bring Very strong, fast, and reliable internet connection — a
