At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a Digital Marketing Specialist to execute multi-channel campaigns that generate leads, improve conversions, and drive measurable business growth.

email marketing, paid advertising, social media, landing pages, marketing automation, and analytics . You’ll work closely with sales to improve lead quality and campaign performance.
