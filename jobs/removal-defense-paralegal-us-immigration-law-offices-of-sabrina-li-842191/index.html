About Us

The Law Offices of Sabrina Li, P.C. is a leading, full-service U.S. immigration law firm with offices in Los Angeles, Pasadena, Santa Ana, and Dallas, as well as international operations across Asia, Africa, and Central and South America. We are dedicated to helping individuals, families, and businesses navigate complex legal challenges with skill, compassion, and integrity.

We are proud to be recognized as a 2025 Inc. Best Workplaces honoree, a distinction that reflects our intentional investment in our people and workplace culture. Our firm prioritizes professional development, clear career pathways, and a culture of accountability and respect, creating a workplace where high performers can build meaningful, long-term careers.

About the Role

We are seeking an experienced and detail-oriented Removal Defense Paralegal to join our growing remote legal team, supporting U.S. immigration attorneys in matters before the Executive Office for Immigration Review (EOIR).

The ideal candidate will have hands-on experience handling U.S. immigration court cases, including preparing applications for relief from removal, drafting legal documents, organizing supporting evidence, and managing court filing requirements. This role requires a strong understanding of removal defense procedures, excellent legal drafting skills, and the ability to manage a high-volume caseload while meeting strict deadlines.

Working closely with U.S. immigration attorneys and legal support staff, you will play a key role in preparing cases for immigration court proceedings, maintaining accurate case records, communicating with clients, and supporting attorneys throughout the removal defense process.

Key Responsibilities

Prepare, organize, and file applications, motions, and supporting documentation for removal defense proceedings before the Executive Office for Immigration Review (EOIR).

Assist with the preparation of applications for relief from removal, including asylum (Form I-589), cancellation of removal (Forms EOIR-42A/42B), withholding of removal, and protection under the Convention Against Torture (CAT), as applicable.

Draft and review legal documents, client declarations, affidavits, motions, supporting statements, and client correspondence under attorney supervision.

Prepare and organize evidence packages, country conditions documentation, exhibits, and other materials required for immigration court hearings.

Assist attorneys with case preparation for Master Calendar Hearings, Individual Hearings, and other immigration court proceedings.

Monitor EOIR filing deadlines, hearing schedules, court notices, and case developments to ensure timely submission of required documentation.

Communicate with clients to gather information, request supporting documentation, provide case updates, and respond to inquiries in a professional and timely manner.

Maintain organized and accurate digital case files to support efficient case management and document retrieval.

Manage and update legal case management and CRM systems, including platforms such as PracticePanther, Lawmatics, Docketwise, Pipefile, and Monday.com.

Coordinate immigration court filings and electronic submissions through applicable EOIR systems, including the EOIR Courts & Appeals System (ECAS), where appropriate.

Collaborate closely with attorneys and legal support staff on case preparation, workflow management, and implementation of attorney-directed legal strategies.

Utilize legal technology and AI-assisted tools to improve productivity, document preparation, organization, and workflow efficiency, subject to firm confidentiality and review requirements.

Consistently meet established performance expectations related to quality, productivity, responsiveness, and client service.

Qualifications

Bachelor of Laws (LLB) degree is required.

Bilingual proficiency in Mandarin and English is highly preferred; strong English communication skills are essential.

Minimum of 2 years of hands-on experience handling U.S. immigration cases (required).

Prior experience handling U.S. immigration court (EOIR) or removal defense cases is required.

Demonstrated experience preparing applications, motions, declarations, and supporting documentation for immigration court proceedings.

Strong understanding of EOIR procedures, immigration court filing requirements, and removal defense case preparation.

Familiarity with asylum applications, cancellation of removal, and other forms of relief from removal is highly preferred.

Experience preparing evidence packages and supporting attorneys with immigration court hearing preparation is highly preferred.

Experience using legal case management and CRM software is required, including platforms such as Docketwise, PracticePanther, Lawmatics, Pipefile, or similar systems.

Strong legal research, drafting, organizational, and multitasking abilities.

Proven ability to manage multiple deadlines while maintaining a high degree of accuracy and attention to detail.

Demonstrated ability to meet performance goals and productivity expectations in a fast-paced legal environment.

Strong proficiency in utilizing AI tools to enhance work product and efficiency is highly preferred.

Excellent written and verbal English communication skills.

Candidates must have a reliable home office setup, including high-speed internet and a backup power solution to ensure consistent availability during power outages.
