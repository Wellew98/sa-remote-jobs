Overview

We are hiring a B2B Appointment Setter (Cold Caller) for a boutique deal-origination company in the Private Equity/M&A industry.

This is a Part-Time remote role for a New York, USA based client. The role is focused on high-volume outbound calling to small and mid-sized business owners, qualifying sell-side interest, and scheduling introductions with investment partners.

The ideal candidate is a persuasive, resilient, and professional communicator with excellent English, a neutral accent, proven cold-calling success, and strong CRM discipline.

Key Responsibilities

  • Place high-volume outbound calls to SMB owners (e.g., HVAC, plumbing, roofing, and related sectors) to gauge interest in exploring a company sale
  • Follow a call framework and adapt messaging; professionally navigate gatekeepers and handle objections
  • Qualify prospects against defined criteria; capture discovery details and next steps
  • Schedule and confirm intro calls between owners and investment partners; coordinate availability
  • Log all activities, outcomes, and notes accurately in the custom CRM; maintain pipeline hygiene
  • Leave compelling voicemails and conduct timely phone-based follow-ups
  • Track daily/weekly targets (dials, connects, qualified leads, booked meetings) and report results
  • Collaborate with the client to refine scripts, talk tracks, and qualification criteria
  • Adhere to US calling etiquette and compliance best practices

Required Qualifications

  • 3+ years of B2B cold calling or appointment setting experience, preferably targeting US-based SMBs
  • Demonstrated success meeting or exceeding call and meeting-booked KPIs
  • Professional maturity and confidence when speaking with business owners
  • Strong objection handling, active listening, and qualification skills
  • Excellent spoken English with a clear, neutral accent and confident executive phone presence
  • Ability to work independently and take direction during onboarding and training
  • Strong attention to detail when recording lead and call information
  • Reliable and consistent attendance during scheduled calling hours
  • Comfortable making a high volume of outbound calls
  • Proficient with CRM data entry and workflow management

Preferred Qualifications

  • Previous experience working remotely for a US-based company
  • Experience in B2B cold calling with US-based customers, prospects, or businesses
  • Experience booking executive-level meetings
  • Familiarity with private equity, business brokerage, mergers and acquisitions, or business sales
  • Prior success calling business owners in HVAC, plumbing, roofing, home services, or other similar industries
  • Familiarity with VoIP dialers and US area-code strategies

Required Skills & Tools

  • VoIP calling tools (e.g., RingCentral, Aircall, 8x8) or similar
  • CRM systems (HubSpot, Salesforce, or custom CRMs)
  • Google Workspace or Microsoft Office for notes and reporting
  • Excellent spoken English and telephone communication skills
  • Strong cold calling and prospecting abilities
  • Rapport-building and conversational sales skills
  • Objection handling and active listening
  • Lead qualification and appointment-setting skills
  • Basic CRM proficiency and willingness to learn a custom CRM
  • Reliable internet connection suitable for VoIP calling
  • Ability to work with a US-based VoIP phone system
  • Computer suitable for remote work
  • Comfortable learning industry-specific terminology during training

Schedule & Pay

  • Part-Time position; Monday - Friday; 9 AM - 1 PM Eastern time (EST)
  • Fully remote role for a US-based company (New York)
  • Pay ranges from R7,000 - R8,000 per month depending on skills and experience
  • Annual raises and confirmed holidays

System Requirements

  • Internet speed of at least 20 Mbps
  • Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher
  • 8 GB of RAM or higher
  • Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer
  • HD 720p webcam
  • Headset with a microphone

Benefits

  • Fully remote work from South Africa
  • Consistent part-time schedule
  • Opportunity to work directly with a growing US-based business
  • Training provided on the company's custom CRM, processes, terminology, and qualification criteria
  • Access to a US-based calling number/VoIP system at no additional cost
  • Opportunity for additional work as the company scales its virtual assistant team
  • Work directly with a growing business brokerage operation connecting business owners with private equity firms

If you are a confident, professional, and personable communicator who enjoys cold calling and knows how to build genuine connections with business owners, apply to join the team!
