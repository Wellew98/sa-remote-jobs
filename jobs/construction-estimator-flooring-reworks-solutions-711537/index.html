About the Role

CC Flooring is looking for a detail-oriented and technically capable Junior Construction Estimator to join its team.

The ideal candidate will have at least one year of practical construction estimating experience and be comfortable working with digital takeoff software such as PlanSwift, Bluebeam, or similar platforms.

This role is suited to someone with a solid understanding of construction drawings, measurements, quantities, and estimating processes who can independently complete digital takeoffs with minimal supervision.

You do not need to be an experienced flooring specialist. General construction estimating experience is welcome, and training can be provided on flooring-specific requirements.

The most important thing is that you already understand the estimating process, know how to use the relevant software, and are comfortable getting hands-on with the work.

Key Responsibilities

1. Construction Estimating & Digital Takeoffs

Review and interpret construction drawings, plans, and project specifications.

Perform digital takeoffs to calculate measurements, quantities, and material requirements.

Use estimating software such as PlanSwift, Bluebeam, or comparable digital takeoff tools.

Accurately measure floor areas, dimensions, and other construction elements.

Prepare detailed quantity calculations to support project estimates.

Ensure all measurements and takeoffs are completed accurately and efficiently.

2. Drawing Interpretation & Technical Assessment

Read and interpret architectural and construction drawings.

Identify relevant measurements, dimensions, and project requirements.

Understand basic construction preparation processes, including floating, levelling, and subfloor preparation.

Identify potential discrepancies or missing information within drawings.

Apply construction knowledge when assessing project requirements and quantities.

3. Estimating Support & Project Coordination

Assist with preparing accurate material and quantity estimates.

Organise and maintain digital takeoff files and estimating documentation.

Review calculations to ensure accuracy and consistency.

Work through estimating tasks independently while meeting deadlines.

Communicate any discrepancies, questions, or challenges identified during the estimating process.

Required Experience & Skills

Minimum of 1 year of practical construction estimating experience .

Hands-on experience using digital takeoff software such as

PlanSwift

Bluebeam

Similar construction estimating or digital measurement software.

Ability to independently complete digital takeoffs without requiring software training from scratch.

Good understanding of construction drawings, measurements, dimensions, and quantities.

Basic knowledge of construction processes, including floating, levelling, and subfloor preparation.

Strong numerical and analytical skills.

Excellent attention to detail and accuracy.

Ability to work independently and manage daily estimating tasks.

Good organisational and time-management skills.

Preferred Experience

Previous flooring, tiling, or construction-related estimating experience.

Exposure to quantity surveying or construction measurement.

Experience preparing material estimates and calculating project quantities.

Familiarity with flooring preparation and installation processes.

Flooring-specific experience is advantageous but not essential. Candidates with general construction estimating experience and strong digital takeoff skills are encouraged to apply.
