Creative Strategist / Paid Media

LocationRemote

Employment TypeFull-Time or Contractor

Working HoursU.S. overlap required (EST)

About the Company

We are a rapidly scaling direct-to-consumer health and wellness brand operating across major digital advertising platforms.

As the brand continues to grow, leadership is shifting focus from purely technical media buying toward creative-led growth , recognizing that modern paid media performance is driven primarily by creative strategy.

Role Overview

We are hiring a Creative Strategist with Paid Media experience to own the intersection between creative direction and performance marketing .

This is a hybrid role — ideal for someone who started as a media buyer and evolved into a creative thinker who understands:

- why ads work

- how hooks drive performance

- how messaging impacts conversion

- how creative testing fuels scalable growth

You will work closely with leadership and creative resources to develop, test, and iterate high-performing ad concepts.

Key Responsibilities

Creative Strategy

- Develop ad concepts and creative testing frameworks

- Create briefs for video, UGC, and static creatives

- Identify winning hooks, angles, and messaging themes

- Analyze creative performance and iterate based on data

-

Build structured creative testing roadmaps

Paid Media Collaboration

- Collaborate with media buying execution (Meta, TikTok, etc.)

- Understand performance metrics and attribution trends

- Translate data into actionable creative insights

- Support scaling winners and refreshing fatigued creatives

Performance Analysis

- Review ad-level data to identify creative trends

-

Understand why creatives succeed or fail

- Optimize messaging for CTR, CPA, and ROAS

- Partner with leadership on growth experimentation

Ideal Candidate Profile

Required

- 3–7 years of experience in paid media or performance marketing

- Strong background in Meta and/or TikTok advertising

-

Demonstrated creative strategy experience

- Ability to analyze performance data and extract insights

- Experience briefing creatives and working with editors/designers

-

Strong English communication skills

- Organized, analytical, and proactive mindset

Strongly Preferred

- Experience in DTC, eCommerce, or CPG brands

- Experience with UGC-driven creative strategies

- Understanding of funnel-based creative (TOF / MOF / BOF)

- Health-conscious lifestyle alignment with wellness brands

- Experience working with fast-scaling ad accounts

What This Role Is Not

- Not purely a media buyer role

-

Not purely a graphic design role

- Not influencer management

This role owns creative strategy that drives paid media performance .

What We Offer

- Competitive compensation based on experience

-

High ownership over creative direction

- Ability to directly impact revenue performance

- Long-term growth opportunity within a scaling brand

- Collaborative and fast-paced remote environment
