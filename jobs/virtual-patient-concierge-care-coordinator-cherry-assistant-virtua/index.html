We are hiring a Virtual Patient Concierge & Care Coordinator for a boutique private practice in the aesthetics, functional medicine, and sexual health industry. This is a Full-Time remote role for a Virginia, USA-based client.

The role is focused on delivering high-touch patient support, managing scheduling and EMR workflows, coordinating patient communications, and ensuring a seamless concierge-style experience from inquiry through appointment completion. The ideal candidate is proactive, discreet, empathetic and confident discussing intimate health topics.

Key Responsibilities

  • Serve as the first point of contact for calls, texts, emails, and portal messages, providing concierge-level patient support and service education
  • Schedule, confirm, reschedule, and manage appointments, including accurate intake, consents, and documentation
  • Use Symplast (EMR/CRM) to manage patient records, bookings, communications, and workflows
  • Ensure correct provider assignment so charts and portal messages route appropriately
  • Manage provider calendars, appointment availability, and scheduling workflows
  • Set reminders and track labs, follow-ups, treatments, and other patient requirements
  • Prepare daily schedules and next-day readiness, including confirmations and pre-visit instructions
  • Follow up with patients regarding scheduling, forms, labs, follow-ups, and outstanding requirements
  • Triage post-procedure and urgent messages, escalating to the clinical team per protocol
  • Maintain accurate EMR/CRM documentation of patient interactions, appointments, and follow-ups
  • Maintain strict HIPAA compliance, patient confidentiality, NDA, and security protocols
  • Deliver a high-touch, discreet, and professional experience when handling sensitive health topics
  • Contribute to SOPs and the practice knowledge base to improve efficiency and consistency
  • Collaborate with the founder and clinical team on workflow and patient-service improvements

Required Qualifications

  • 2+ years of experience in patient coordination, medical front office, care navigation, medical scheduling, or concierge healthcare support within aesthetics/med spa, dermatology, urology/sexual health, plastic surgery, functional medicine, or related healthcare settings
  • Hands-on experience managing appointments, EMR workflows, patient communications, and administrative processes in a medical environment
  • Strong verbal and written English communication skills with a clear, professional phone presence
  • Comfort and professionalism discussing sensitive sexual health topics using accurate clinical terminology
  • Exceptional attention to detail, follow-through, organization, and task ownership in a fully remote setting
  • Strong ability to work independently while following established clinical and administrative protocols

Preferred Qualifications

  • Direct experience with Symplast (EMR/CRM) strongly preferred
  • Experience in a high-touch concierge medical, aesthetics, or wellness environment
  • Experience supporting high-profile or VIP clientele
  • Hospitality, luxury service, or patient-experience background
  • Experience working remotely with a U.S.-based medical practice
  • Strong calendar management and administrative coordination skills

Required Skills & Tools

  • Symplast EMR/CRM (strongly preferred) or comparable EMR/CRM platforms
  • VoIP/call management systems and secure messaging tools
  • Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 for documentation and scheduling
  • Strong calendar management and digital organization skills

Schedule & Pay

  • Full-Time position; Monday–Thursday 10:00 a.m.– 6:00 p.m. EST; Friday 10:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. EST
  • Fully remote role for a Virginia, USA-based client
  • Pay ranges from R14,000 - R17,500 ZAR ( $935 - $1110) - dependent on experience
  • Includes structured onboarding, HIPAA certification support if needed, and strong long-term growth potential

System Requirements

  • Internet speed of at least 20 Mbps upload and download
  • Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher
  • 8 GB of RAM or higher
  • Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer
  • HD 720p webcam
  • Headset with microphone

Benefits

  • Competitive pay rates
  • Consistent hours and predictable workload
  • Fully remote work with long-term potential
  • Direct collaboration with the founder and clinical team, making a real impact
  • Supportive and inclusive work environment
  • Opportunity to grow alongside a high-touch, concierge-style practice

If you are passionate about delivering exceptional patient care, creating a seamless patient experience, and thrive in a high-touch, fast-paced environment, we’d love to hear from you.
