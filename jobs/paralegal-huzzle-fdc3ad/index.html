About Huzzle

At Huzzle, we connect exceptional talent with top opportunities at leading companies across the UK, US, Canada, Europe, and Australia. Our clients include fast-growing startups, legal firms, and global organizations across industries such as LegalTech, SaaS, FinTech, and Professional Services.

Unlike traditional outsourcing agencies, we place you directly with a client where you’re hired in-house as a valued member of their team.

Job Summary

We are hiring a detail-oriented Remote Paralegal to support high-performing legal teams across international clients. This is an excellent opportunity for candidates looking for paralegal jobs from home, offering exposure to global legal processes, contract management, and compliance support.

You will play a key role in assisting attorneys, managing documentation, conducting legal research, and ensuring smooth case operations.

Role TypeFull-time

EngagementIndependent Contractor

Key Responsibilities

Conduct legal research and summarize findings for attorneys and stakeholders

Draft, review, and proofread legal documents, contracts, and correspondence

Organize and maintain case files, legal records, and documentation systems

Assist with contract lifecycle management and compliance tracking

Coordinate with clients, internal teams, and external legal professionals

Manage calendars, deadlines, and court filing schedules

Support litigation preparation, including discovery and document review

Ensure confidentiality and compliance with legal standards and procedures

2+ years of experience as a
