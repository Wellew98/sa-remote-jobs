About the Company

A startup founded by Stanford graduate students to accelerate the interconnection of new generation and large loads to the US grid. We are building a platform that shows developers how different interconnection queue scenarios affect their projects.

Key Responsibilities

  • Build and run shadow interconnection studies in US ISOs and utilities, with an initial focus on PJM and WECC.
  • Replicate utility load flow, contingency, and short circuit studies from posted models and reports.
  • Test how results change under alternative queue assumptions.
  • Perform transmission planning modeling, including power flow modeling, using software such as PSS/E, PSLF, PowerWorld, ASPEN OneLiner, and/or TARA.
  • Evaluate the interconnection impacts of new generation projects on the grid.

Qualifications & Skills

  • 3+ years of experience supporting interconnection studies for utilities, developers, or renewable energy projects
  • Hands-on experience with PSS/E and PowerWorld required
  • Familiarity with TARA and PSLF strongly preferred
  • Experience performing load flow / thermal overload, voltage stability, and short circuit studies
  • Knowledge of PJM and/or NV Energy interconnection standards and study requirements preferred
  • Strong understanding of grid modeling, transmission planning, and utility interconnection workflows
  • Able to serve as the technical domain lead for power systems analysis
  • Comfortable collaborating with software engineers on automation and workflow improvement
  • Strong communication skills and ability to work independently in a fast-moving startup environment
