Amazon Implementation Specialist Location: Remote in South Africa Salary Range: 1300 - 2500 USD Role Description: The Amazon Implementation Specialist is responsible for successfully launching and optimizing product listings on the Amazon marketplace (Seller Central and/or Vendor Central). This role ensures that product data, images, SEO content, and compliance requirements are correctly implemented and aligned with Amazon’s best practices. The specialist works closely with cross-functional teams such as marketing, product, logistics, and customer service to ensure a seamless onboarding process and sustained performance of the brand’s presence on Amazon. Key Responsibilities: - Coordinate and manage the onboarding process for new products and brands on Amazon. - Create, update, and optimize product listings, including titles, bullet points, descriptions, backend keywords, and A+ content. - Ensure all listings are fully compliant with Amazon's policies, category guidelines, and technical requirements. - Upload product catalogs via flat files or manual methods, ensuring accurate and complete data entry. - Troubleshoot and resolve listing issues, suppressed listings, and upload errors. - Monitor and maintain content integrity and accuracy across all listings. - Collaborate with internal teams to gather assets such as images, videos, and product information. - Optimize product pages for search visibility (SEO) and conversion rate. - Manage relationships with Amazon support for case resolution and technical escalations. - Assist with setting up variations, FBA settings, and inventory tracking. - Stay updated on Amazon policy changes, tools, and industry trends. Qualifications: - Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Business, E-commerce, or a related field (or equivalent experience). - 2+ years of experience working with Amazon Seller Central and/or Vendor Central. - Solid understanding of e-commerce fundamentals and digital merchandising. - Proficiency in flat file uploads and Amazon templates for product listings. - Knowledge of Amazon SEO and keyword optimization strategies. - Familiarity with Amazon tools (Brand Registry, A+ Content, Vine, FBA dashboards, etc.). - Strong attention to detail and problem-solving skills. - Excellent written and verbal communication skills. - Ability to manage multiple projects and meet tight deadlines. Preferred Skills: - Experience with Amazon advertising (PPC campaigns), brand analytics, or reporting tools. - Proficiency in Excel and data analysis. - Knowledge of other marketplaces (eBay, Walmart, Shopify, etc.) is a plus. - Familiarity with e-commerce platforms (e.g., Shopify, Magento, or ERP systems). Details - Independent contractor agreement paid 2x monthly. - Client has a desire for long term commitment.
