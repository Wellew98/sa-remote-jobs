About the Company

Our client is an agile, founder-led business group focused on driving rapid footprint expansion, commercial growth, and operational scale across physical, service-based brands. Built on principles of extreme ownership, high autonomy, and direct accountability, the culture prioritizes fast execution, structured systems, and commercial rigor.

The initial operational anchor for this role is CG , a high-growth physical brand currently expanding its footprint. While CG serves as the primary starting place and core testing ground, this role will progressively expand to oversee and drive operational initiatives across other businesses, ventures, and strategic projects across the founder's portfolio.

About the Role

As Operations Lead/Chief of staff you will serve as the core operational growth driver, partnering directly with the founder to scale the business's physical footprint and maximize operational efficiency.

Starting with CG as your central operational focus, you will take ownership of unit economics, build operational playbooks, and leverage automation to scale the model. In addition to core operations, you will actively steer growth levers—including customer acquisition, membership programs, client retention, and marketing performance.

This is a high-autonomy seat for a pragmatic, numbers-driven operator who can oscillate between strategic high-level planning and boots-on-the-ground execution. We do not expect you to check every single box on day one: if you bring unmistakable strengths in execution, commercial acumen, and high adaptability, you will have the room and support to develop across the rest.

Key Responsibilities

1. Operational Scale & Unit P&L Ownership

- Manage unit-level financial performance, scrutinizing labor allocation, supply costs, and operational expenditures to optimize gross margins and profitability.

- Build preliminary cash-flow forecasts, labor models, and unit economics analyses to inform expansion decisions.

- Establish clear accountability frameworks, KPIs, and operational standards across on-site teams.

2. Growth, Retention & Marketing Performance

- Act as an operational partner on customer acquisition, collaborating with marketing teams and external partners to ensure campaigns translate into foot traffic and revenue.

- Own and optimize membership programs: track recurring member churn, lifetime value (LTV), member engagement, and retention strategies.

- Monitor marketing performance metrics (CPA, conversion rates, local demand generation) to ensure marketing spend converts efficiently at the unit level.

3. Practical AI & Workflow Automation

- Implement practical AI tools and workflow automations (e.g., Zapier, Make, LLM workflows, CRM logic) to eliminate administrative friction and streamline operations.

- Continuously identify manual bottlenecks in reporting, scheduling, and communication, replacing them with automated, repeatable systems.

4. Footprint Expansion & Playbook Development

- Help coordinate location rollout pipelines—assisting with market research, demographic analysis, site comparison, vendor procurement, and setup schedules.

- Codify operational knowledge into clear, scalable Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), onboarding checklists, and quality benchmarks.

5. Cross-Portfolio Special Projects

- Anchor your focus on CG’s expansion, while remaining agile enough to take on cross-functional initiatives and special projects across other group companies as needs evolve.

- Act as an operational right hand to the founder, triaging complex challenges and driving strategic initiatives from concept through execution.

Qualifications

Required

- 2+ years of operational leadership experience driving growth, systems-building, or operational management in a fast-paced environment.

- Demonstrated P&L and commercial literacy: Comfortable analyzing unit economics, dissecting financial statements, optimizing labor/inventory, and identifying margin-improvement opportunities.

- Practical AI & Automation experience: Proven track record of using modern AI tools and no-code automation platforms to streamline workflows, speed up execution, and automate routine tasks.

- Familiarity with growth engines: Practical exposure to customer acquisition channels, membership/subscription models, customer retention tactics, and marketing performance metrics.

- Flawless executive communication: Native-level written and spoken English, comfortable presenting data, synthesizing complex updates, and communicating with US-based leadership.

- Remote readiness: Proven experience working effectively in a remote capacity with dedicated daily overlap during US Eastern Standard Time (EST) business hours.

Preferred (Not Mandated):

- Hands-on experience with physical site rollouts, retail/studio openings, vendor buildouts, or launch coordination from inception to steady-state.

- Experience within physical, multi-unit consumer service businesses (e.g., boutique fitness, wellness clinics, medspas, personal care, hospitality, or membership-based concepts).

- Prior background as an Operations Manager, General Manager, Founder’s Associate, or Chief of Staff in an early-stage or rapidly scaling company.
