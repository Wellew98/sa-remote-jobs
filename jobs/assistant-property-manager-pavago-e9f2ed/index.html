About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring an Assistant Property Manager to support the day-to-day operations of residential, commercial, or mixed-use properties.

This is a hands-on property operations role focused on

Tenant relations and communication

Leasing and renewals

Rent collection and delinquency tracking

Maintenance and vendor coordination

Property management systems

Compliance and tenant documentation

Owner and portfolio reporting

You’ll serve as the right hand to the Property Manager , ensuring tenants receive timely support, vendors stay coordinated, records remain accurate, and property operations run smoothly.

If you have

2+ years of property management or real estate operations experience and hands-on experience with AppFolio, Yardi, Buildium, or similar property management software , this role is a strong fit.

Tenant Relations & Communication

Rent and payments

Property policies

Maintenance requests

Respond professionally and promptly to tenant concerns

Document tenant interactions within AppFolio, Yardi, Buildium, or similar systems

Manage communication for

Move-ins

Track rent payments and outstanding balances

Track maintenance requests from submission through completion

Follow up with vendors to prevent delays

Confirm work has been completed satisfactorily

Verify vendor invoices against completed work before routing them for approval

Leases
