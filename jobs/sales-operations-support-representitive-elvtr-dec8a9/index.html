About ELVTR

At ELVTR, we're transforming online education by connecting ambitious professionals with industry leaders from companies like Nike, Netflix, Google, DreamWorks and more.

Since launching in 2020, we've grown into a global learning community with over 300 expert instructors, delivering more than 200 live online courses across 12 industries. To date, we've helped 22,000+ graduates worldwide gain the skills, confidence, and connections needed to advance their careers.

Why This Role Exists

Sales Operations Support Representative will support the efficiency, scalability, and day-to-day operations of our sales function.

This is

a full-time remote position with working hours from 9 AM - 6 PM UK time (with potential shift to 12 PM - 9 PM UK time in the future to accommodate US-based customers).

What You Will Do

Conduct daily checks to ensure smooth functioning of core systems (CRM, operational tools, integrations), maintaining high uptime and data integrity

Respond to customer inquiries via email, ensuring timely and exceptional customer service.

Handle incoming customer calls, addressing questions and assisting with enrollments when needed.

Conduct sales conversations with customers to support the sales team during high-volume periods.

Prepare sales materials and documentation for new course launches.

Prepare and maintain essential sales enablement materials, including sales templates, payment links, product documentation, and related resources

Own and continuously update the centralized library of sales resources, processes, procedures, product documentation, and regulatory information

Collaborate with cross-functional teams (Marketing, Finance, Tech, and Operations) to ensure smooth sales processes.

Proactively identify bottlenecks and inefficiencies across the sales cycle and operational workflows

Suggest and implement automation and optimization initiatives to improve speed, accuracy, and scalability

At least C1 level of English proficiency

2+ years of experience in sales, customer service, sales operations, or a related role

Excellent phone and email communication skills, with the ability to build strong relationships with customers and internal teams

Strong organizational skills with high attention to detail

Proficiency with CRM systems (e.g., Salesforce, HubSpot) and sales analytics tools

Positive attitude, self-starter mindset, and strong team collaboration skills

Nice to haveAI proficiency for sales data analysis, process automation, or workflow optimization

You Are a Strong Fit If

Enjoy optimizing processes and making systems run smoothly

Are comfortable supporting sales teams in fast-paced, high-growth environments

Like combining operational work with direct customer interaction

Are detail-oriented, organized, and proactive in solving problems

Are curious about using AI and automation to improve efficiency

Competitive Salary and Bonuses: We value your contributions and offer competitive compensation and performance-based incentives.

Comprehensive Benefits: Enjoy gym membership reimbursements, mental health benefits, generous vacation allowances, and complimentary access to all our courses.

Flexible Work Environment: Work remotely with a schedule that accommodates your personal and professional needs.

Growth Opportunities: Join a company committed to your professional development, offering abundant opportunities for career advancement.

Join Us

If you are a detail-oriented professional ready to contribute to a leading online education platform, we encourage you to apply. Elevate your career with ELVTR.

ELVTR is committed to providing equal opportunities and fostering a diverse, inclusive environment for all, whether you join us as an employee or a contractor.
