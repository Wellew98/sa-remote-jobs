Role Overview

We are building a global network of Independent Sales Agents to help individuals and families explore transparent and reliable healthcare options in India.

This opportunity is ideal for proactive, community-oriented individuals who can build local networks, develop business relationships, and connect prospective patients with healthcare services. As an Independent Sales Agent, you will represent a medical travel facilitation platform within your country or community and help people understand available services and access further information about treatment options in Chandigarh, India.

The role offers an opportunity to develop your own market, build professional relationships, and contribute to improving access to cross-border healthcare services.

Key Responsibilities

Business Development

Represent the healthcare services platform within your local market and identify potential business opportunities.

Network Development

Build relationships with hospitals, clinics, healthcare professionals, dental practices, specialist centres, and medical service providers.

Lead Generation

Identify and connect with individuals and families exploring second opinions, specialist consultations, or treatment options abroad.

Community Outreach

Develop relationships with patient support groups, community organisations, wellness networks, and relevant travel and care-service providers.

Client Engagement

Introduce prospective patients to the platform, explain available services, and connect interested individuals with the central coordination team.

Relationship Management

Maintain strong relationships with healthcare partners, community representatives, and prospective clients.

Market Development

Identify local market opportunities, understand community requirements, and expand the referral network.

Referral Management

Use the designated agent portal to manage referrals, patient enquiries, clinic relationships, and communications.

Ethical Representation

Ensure transparent communication, protect patient privacy, and avoid making medical recommendations or pressuring individuals into treatment.

Field Outreach

Dedicate approximately 3–5 hours per day to networking, prospecting, and relationship-building, depending on the local market and agreed arrangements.

Candidate Requirements

Strong networking and relationship-building abilities within a local community or professional network.

Experience in sales, business development, client acquisition, or independent representation will be an advantage.

Ability to connect with healthcare providers, community organisations, and prospective patients.

Professional, trustworthy, proactive, and comfortable working independently.

Strong verbal and written communication skills.

Willingness to learn about international healthcare coordination and medical travel services.

Ability to dedicate approximately 3–5 hours per day to outreach and business development.

Commitment to ethical communication, patient privacy, and responsible handling of enquiries.

Medical qualifications are not required. The role focuses on business development, networking, referrals, and helping people discover available healthcare options. Medical advice and treatment decisions remain the responsibility of qualified healthcare professionals.

Why Join This Opportunity?

Global Exposure

Build a network of healthcare and medical travel contacts within your country and community.

Independent Working Model

Develop your own local market and professional network.

Training and Support

Receive onboarding, training, platform guidance, and ongoing support.

Dedicated Agent Portal

Approved agents receive a unique agent ID and access to tools for managing referrals, enquiries, and communications.

Transparent Processes

Agents never collect patient payments. Patients make payments through authorised platforms or approved payment channels.

Patient-First Approach

Patients receive clear information, written estimates, and the freedom to make informed decisions without pressure.

Meaningful Social Impact

The initiative supports healthcare access and contributes to social-impact programmes focused on people facing financial barriers to care, as well as women and girls in Himalayan communities.

Compensation Structure

Independent agent opportunity with performance-based payouts.

Earnings depend on eligible referrals, applicable programme terms, and actual performance.

No fixed monthly salary or guaranteed earnings.

Opportunity to grow earnings by developing relationships and generating qualified referrals.

Application Process

Interested candidates can apply through the designated online agent onboarding portal. The process includes application submission, verification, onboarding, and training.

Approved agents receive a unique agent ID and access to the agent portal. All patient information must be handled responsibly, with appropriate consent and in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.
