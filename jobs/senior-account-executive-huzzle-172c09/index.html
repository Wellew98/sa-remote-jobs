About Huzzle

At Huzzle, we connect exceptional talent with top opportunities at leading companies across the UK, US, Canada, Europe, and Australia. Our clients include fast-growing startups, digital agencies, SaaS companies, and tech-enabled businesses across industries such as MarTech, FinTech, EdTech, and eCommerce. Unlike traditional outsourcing agencies, we place you directly with innovative companies where you become a valued member of the team.

About the company

Our client is a results-driven marketing agency specializing in reputation management and AI visibility for the automotive and medical sectors. The team serves as a fractional marketing powerhouse for high-growth operators, focusing on the intersection of search discovery and consumer trust. By moving beyond traditional advertising, they provide a comprehensive system that monitors reputation, optimizes brand signals for AI-driven search, and executes high-impact strategy.

Job Summary

This is a high-impact, autonomous role for a seasoned sales professional who thrives on owning the entire revenue cycle. This digital marketing agency, a leader within the automotive and collision repair sectors, is poised for aggressive expansion into new verticals. We are looking for a "hunter" who combines strategic depth with a relentless drive to build a pipeline from scratch. You will not just be closing deals; you will be a key architect of the agency’s growth, identifying new market opportunities, cultivating long-term referral partnerships, and leveraging cutting-edge AI tools to scale your impact.

Key Responsibilities

Identify and research high-potential clients through multi-channel outreach, industry events, and strategic partnerships.

Own the full sales lifecycle, from initial cold outreach and discovery to proposal development, negotiation, and closing.

Lead deep-dive discovery calls to uncover prospect pain points and articulate tailored marketing solutions that drive ROI.

Manage and maintain a meticulous pipeline within HubSpot, ensuring accurate forecasting and high data integrity.

Collaborate closely with the Growth & Revenue Director to align sales strategies with the agency’s expansion goals.

Facilitate seamless handoffs to the delivery team post-close, acting as the client’s internal advocate to ensure long-term satisfaction.

Build and nurture a network of referral partners and complementary agencies to diversify lead sources.

Actively integrate AI tools into the sales workflow to enhance productivity and outreach personalization.

5+ years of B2B sales experience, specifically within a digital marketing agency or a closely related professional services environment.

Proven track record of full-cycle sales ownership, with documented success in meeting or exceeding revenue quotas.

Mastery of the discovery process, with the ability to move beyond generic pitching to value-based selling.

Advanced proficiency in HubSpot CRM (or equivalent) with a disciplined approach to pipeline management.

Excellent presentation and communication skills, capable of commanding a room and tailoring messages to business owners.

A proactive approach to technology, with genuine comfort and experience using AI tools to optimize sales workflows.

South African based with a focus on regional market dynamics.

Nice-to-Haves

Experience selling retainer-based or recurring revenue service models.

Familiarity with the automotive aftermarket or collision repair industries.

Background in developing or executing Go-to-Market (GTM) strategies for new territories or verticals.

An existing network of professional contacts or referral partners.

Foundational knowledge of core digital marketing channels including SEO, SEM, paid social, and content strategy.
