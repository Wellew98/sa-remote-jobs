Conversion-Focused UI/UX Designer (Web & Digital Creative)

Location / Employment Details

  • Location: Fully remote
  • Employment type: Full-time
  • Hours: Our team works in Eastern Time (ET). You need a reliable daily overlap with ET for reviews and collaboration; full ET hours are not required.
  • Reports to: The founder, working closely with our in-house web developer
  • Language: Fluent English required; French is a plus, not a requirement

About the Company

Marie is a North American based serial entrepreneur leading several businesses (most of which are in the health care space). She leads a small lean virtual team that uses a lot of automation tools and AI.

Digital presence is a core part of how businesses grow. Patients find us through our websites, ads, and educational webinars, and every step of that journey is something we design, test, and improve. We already have a developer building our automation and integrations. Now we want the design side to match.

About the Role

We have worked with plenty of designers who say they are strong in UX. Their portfolios usually tell a different story. We are hiring this role to close that gap: one person who owns how our web experiences look, flow, and convert.

You will design the pages and journeys that turn a visitor into a booked consultation. That means clear flows, less text, obvious calls to action, and layouts that work just as well on mobile. You will also bring a testing mindset: when something underperforms, your first instinct is to form a hypothesis and run an A/B test, not guess.

We use AI tools heavily, and we expect you to as well. The goal is speed with taste. You will use AI to build out a design system and produce ad variations at volume, while making sure everything that ships still looks intentional and on-brand. Output that looks machine-generated is exactly what we are hiring you to prevent.

Key Responsibilities

UX & Conversion Design

  • Map and design user flows for websites and landing pages, from first visit to booked consultation
  • Simplify pages for conversion: tighter copy, clear hierarchy, and obvious calls to action
  • Design responsive, mobile-first layouts that hold up on every screen size
  • Review existing pages and recommend specific changes that would lift conversion

Testing & Optimization

  • Propose and set up A/B tests on pages, forms, and calls to action
  • Read conversion data, explain what it says, and turn findings into the next round of design changes
  • Keep a simple log of tests run, results, and what was learned

Design System & Brand Consistency

  • Build and maintain a design system (type, color, components, templates) that keeps every asset consistent
  • Use AI design tools to speed up production without lowering the quality bar
  • Hand off clean, build-ready designs to our web developer and support implementation

Digital Creative

  • Produce ad creative in multiple variations for testing, including animated and short-form formats
  • Design visual assets for webinars and email campaigns
  • Prepare occasional print materials when needed

Required Qualifications

  • A portfolio of live websites or landing pages with a strong, polished visual standard. This is the first thing we review.
  • 3+ years of UI/UX design for web, including responsive and mobile design
  • Demonstrated UX thinking: you can show the flows behind your designs and explain why they convert
  • Hands-on experience with A/B testing or conversion analysis
  • Daily use of AI design and production tools, plus a professional design tool such as Figma
  • Fluent written and spoken English

Preferred Qualifications

  • Motion and video editing skills for animated ads and short-form creative
  • Experience designing for healthcare, wellness, or local service businesses
  • Experience building or maintaining a design system from scratch
  • Comfort working in a CRM or website builder alongside a developer
  • Print production experience
  • French language skills

Ideal Candidate Profile

You are

  • Picky about aesthetics. You notice when spacing, type, or color is off, and you fix it before anyone asks.
  • Conversion-minded. You judge a page by whether it works, and you can explain the reasoning behind every design choice.
  • Fast without being sloppy. You use AI to move quickly, then apply your own judgment so the result never looks generated.
  • Proactive. You spot what could be better and bring ideas, rather than waiting for a detailed brief.
  • A clear communicator. You present work confidently, take feedback well, and keep projects moving with minimal supervision.

Why Join Us

You will own the visual and UX side of a business that is growing through digital channels, with direct access to the founder and real influence over what ships. Your work goes live quickly and shows up in the numbers. You will also work in an AI-first environment that values good design, so you will have room to experiment with new tools without anyone asking you to trade quality for volume.
