THE ESSENTIALS

💰 Pay : $2,000 USD/month, a flat monthly rate

🌎

Location

: 100% remote from South Africa, Kenya, Nigeria, Morocco, the Philippines, or India

🕘

Schedule

: Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm US Pacific, Central, or Eastern time (this means evening or overnight hours in your time zone)

🗣️

Languages

: Flawless English and fluent Spanish, both required

📊

Experience

: 2–5 years of hands-on campaign management

If any of these don't work for you, this role isn't the right fit, and that's okay.

LAUNCH DAY IS WHERE THE REAL WORK STARTS

A campaign doesn't stop needing you once it goes live. That's when it gets interesting.

We're looking for a bilingual marketer who can

📈 launch a campaign and watch what the numbers are really saying,

🔧 fix what isn't working while there's still budget to save,

🧪 test, learn, and scale what is working.

You'll work across paid ads, email/SMS, copy, and creative, and the mix will change from client to client. What won't change: you own what you run. We don't need someone who launches and waits for the next reporting meeting to find out what happened.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

🎯 Paid ads : Plan, launch, and optimize campaigns on Meta, Google, and LinkedIn, adjusting budget, targeting, and creative based on real results.

🎨

Creative

Work with writers and designers on ads, then track which versions actually perform.

📧

Email & SMS

Build and send campaigns and flows in Klaviyo or Mailchimp, testing subject lines, messaging, and timing.

🌐

Bilingual campaigns

: Make sure messaging feels natural to both English- and Spanish-speaking audiences, not just translated.

📊

Reporting

Turn the numbers into clear next steps: keep, change, test, or stop.

🤝

Collaboration

Keep content, design, and project managers in the loop so campaigns keep moving.

🤖 You'll use ChatGPT or similar tools to move faster. Your judgment decides what reaches customers.

YOU'RE A FIT IF YOU HAVE

2–5 years of hands-on digital marketing, running campaigns from planning to optimization and reporting

Direct, hands-on experience in Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads (LinkedIn Campaign Manager is a

strong plus)

Experience building email or SMS campaigns in Klaviyo or Mailchimp

Flawless written and spoken English for client-facing work

Fluent Spanish, confident enough to write and review customer-facing campaigns

Basic Photoshop and Premiere skills for quick edits and asset prep

The ability to read campaign data and make practical decisions from it

NICE TO HAVE

SEO · Google Analytics (GA4) · SEMrush · Basic HTML/CSS for email templates · Video editing

NON-NEGOTIABLES

📶 Internet and power that won't cut out mid-launch, with a backup for both

⏰ You show up when you say you will. Every time, not most of the time

📣 If something is underperforming, you say so early instead of hoping it fixes itself

🤝 You follow through without anyone needing to remind you

OUR HIRING PROCESS

📝Apply → 👀 Profile review → 🎥 Intro video → 📞 Screening interview → 💬 Interviews → 🚀 Selection & onboarding

1.

Apply

Submit your CV, campaign examples, and answers. Your answers matter more than your CV.

2.

Profile review

Our team reviews every application personally.

3.

Intro video

: If you're a match, we'll ask for a short video in English and Spanish.

4.

Screening interview

: A 30-minute conversation about your experience and the role.

5.

Interviews

Practical marketing exercises and a final interview with the client.

6.

Selection & onboarding

Selected candidates complete a background check and employment verification before starting.

⚠️ We read every application. Answers that are generic, copied, or fully AI-generated are declined. We want to hear from you.

WHAT YOU GET

💵 A clear, fixed rate: $2,000/month, no guesswork

🏡 Fully remote with a stable Monday–Friday schedule

🔑 Real ownership: you'll manage budgets, make decisions, and test ideas

👀 Visible impact: your campaigns and their results are yours
