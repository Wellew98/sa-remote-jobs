Own paid acquisition end to end for a growing dog brand and scale it from near-zero to $1M+ per month in profitable spend.

$4,000-$4,500/month | Full-time | 9-5 EST

We're Happy Consumer, a Direct Response Digital and Supplement Seller that has dominated the DR space for the last 2.5 yrs. We are looking for an experienced Media buyer to join our team and own our marketing campaigns and scaler with us for 2027 and beyond. This position is fully remote, offer flexibility and has a lot of room for professional and personal growth.

We're looking for a Media Buyer who has already taken a brand from near-zero to serious monthly spend and done it profitably. You'll own everything downstream of the creative: Jason makes the ads, you make them work. You'll work closely with him on strategy and report KPIs to Madlyn, our COO.

📋 Run and scale paid acquisition on Meta first, then expand to Google and AppLovin

✏️ Build the testing system: the dashboard, testing cadence and a clear pipeline where Jason drops in new ads, VSLs and videos on set days and you handle everything after

💡 Diagnose quickly when performance drops or CPMs spike, explain why, and shift the approach

💻 Build and launch landers fast using AI tools and Shopify, with tracking set up properly (pixels, CAPI), often live within the hour of an idea

📊 Report KPIs to Madlyn and work with Jason on strategy day to day

You'll need to be available between 9-5 EST.

To be successful in this role, you need

✅ Experience scaling a brand from roughly $0 to $800K+/month in ad spend profitably, built from the bottom rather than inherited at scale (most likely at a strong performance agency or in-house at a DTC ecom brand)

✅ Industry proximity in this order: dog (ideal), pet, nutra/supplements DTC, consumables DTC, or general DTC ecom

✅ Elite-level skills on Meta, and strong experience on AppLovin and TikTok

✅ Technical and AI-fluent: tracking, pixels/CAPI, Shopify, AI tools for ads, video and landers

✅ Coachability: strong opinions but willing to be directed by Jason

✅ Availability for overlap with US Eastern Time until at least 3 to 4pm ET

Brownie points for

⭐ Google Ads experience

⭐ Already active in ecom communities and following what's working day to day

The person who thrives here is competitive in the good way. You've seen performance fall off a cliff plenty of times, you already have the playbook, and you have a plan B ready when plan A stops working. Strong opinions are welcome, and so is taking direction from Jason, who owns creative. Systems-building comes naturally to you: you'd rather build the dashboard and the cadence than wing it every week. Startup pace, shifting priorities and getting your hands dirty are all part of the deal, and you're looking to grow long term inside one company.

What’s in it for you?

💰 $4,000-$4,500 USD per month*

💼 Full-time

🌍 Fully remote

📅 Available between 9-5 EST

*Please note that this is a gross salary and that you are responsible for any tax-related procedures in your country of residence. We kindly ask you to apply only if you are happy to work remotely as an independent contractor.
