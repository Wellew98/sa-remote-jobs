RSB - Roundtable on Sustainable Biomaterials Association seeks an independent consultant operating under a consultancy contract for an initial 9‑month engagement to support a growing portfolio of work in GHG accounting, corporate Scope 3 emission reporting, environmental attribute accounting (i.e. Book and Claim systems) in the context of sustainable chemicals and fuels. Subject to organisational needs, funding availability and mutual interest, this engagement may create opportunities for future employment.

About Us

Our growing, dedicated team of committed professionals works globally to develop solutions to some of the world’s biggest challenges. We operate remotely in many countries worldwide, with a robust and collaborative working environment fostering professional development and a healthy work-life balance. Our team allows for individual autonomy and the opportunity to engage with a full spectrum of roles within our stakeholder base's diverse range of organisations.

RSB is a global, multi-stakeholder organisation dedicated to driving the sustainable development of the bioeconomy based on our best-in-class sustainability framework. Frequently noted as industry leaders in navigating the complexities of supply chain sustainability, we take pride in applying our robust sustainability framework in diverse sectors, including aviation, chemicals, materials, and shipping.

For more information, visit www.rsb.org

Your Role

As Consultant – GHG Accounting, you will play a key role in driving a just and sustainable energy transition in hard-to-abate sectors such as transport, chemicals and materials, by guiding the development of GHG accounting principles across projects.

The consultant will be responsible for delivering the services described in this Terms of Reference, under the guidance of the Book & Claim Expert and in coordination with relevant members of the Projects and RSB Secretariat teams.

You will be responsible for these main areas of work:

Project Delivery

Support the delivery of multiple client-facing projects focused on sustainable fuels, Book and Claim systems, and claims integrity, including:

Development of accounting approaches for biomethane, sustainable aviation fuel (SAF), HVO, and other low-carbon fuels.

Assessment of Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3 accounting implications associated with Book and Claim and other market-based instruments.

Evaluation of risks related to double counting, double claiming, additionality, and claims governance.

Development of technical reports, guidance documents, white papers, and client recommendations.

Corporate GHG Accounting

Cross-cutting methodology and advisory support:

Provide technical expertise on the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard and Corporate Value Chain (Scope 3) Standard.

Advise on emissions accounting methodologies for fuel switching, renewable fuels, biomethane procurement, and supply-chain interventions.

Assess corporate claims and reporting approaches against evolving best practices.

Support clients in understanding accounting, reporting, and disclosure implications of climate-related initiatives.

Lifecycle Assessment and Bioenergy Analysis

Review and interpret life cycle assessment (LCA) studies and carbon intensity calculations.

Assess lifecycle emissions associated with biomass, biomethane, biofuels, e-fuels, and circular materials.

Contribute technical expertise on feedstock sustainability, emissions factors, allocation methodologies, and GHG quantification approaches.

Support the development of technical methodologies and guidance documents.

Standards and Framework Development

Monitor and interpret developments in

GHG Protocol

Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi)

ISO greenhouse gas and Chain of Custody (CoC) standards

EU Renewable Energy Directive (RED)

ICAO CORSIA

Corporate climate disclosure frameworks

Contribute to stakeholder consultations and technical engagement with standard-setting organizations, industry initiatives, and corporate partners.

Stakeholder Engagement

Participate in workshops, technical working groups, and client meetings.

Present technical findings to corporate partners, NGOs, industry stakeholders, and policymakers.

Support consensus-building around credible accounting and claims practices.

Required - Education, experience, and qualifications

Master's degree or equivalent experience in environmental science, environmental engineering, or a related field.

5-10 years of relevant professional experience.

Demonstrated expertise in corporate GHG accounting.

Strong knowledge of the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard and Scope 3 Standard.

Experience with, or strong working knowledge of, ISO greenhouse gas and Chain of Custody standards.

Familiarity with Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) requirements and guidance.

Experience conducting or reviewing life cycle assessments and carbon footprint studies.

Experience working in one or more of the following sectors

Bioenergy / Biofuels

Chemicals and materials

Strong analytical and technical writing skills.

Fluency in English.

Required - Personal attributes

Ability to translate complex technical issues into practical business recommendations.

Strong stakeholder engagement skills.

Ability to work independently in a remote, international environment.

Comfortable operating in evolving methodological landscapes.

Strong attention to detail and commitment to scientific rigor.

Excellent project management and prioritization skills.

Desired

Interest in market transformation, sustainable fuels, and high-integrity climate solutions.

Experience with Book and Claim systems, environmental attribute markets, renewable energy certificates, SAF certificates, or similar market-based instruments.

Knowledge of emerging GHG Protocol Actions and Market Instruments (AMI) developments.

Familiarity with sustainability certification systems such as RSB.

Understanding of claims frameworks and green claims governance.

Experience working with multinational companies on climate strategy or sustainability reporting.
