We are hiring a Virtual Customer Service Representative (CSR) & Appointment Setter for a home services SMB in the Painting industry. This is a Full-Time remote role for a United States (Colorado/Mountain Time) based client. The role is focused on lead follow-up, scheduling free on-site estimates, managing the CRM and calendar, and providing excellent customer support with light admin/data entry. The ideal candidate is service-oriented, organized, detail-driven, and confident on the phone with strong English communication skills and a bias for quick follow-up.

Key Responsibilities

  • Handle inbound calls, texts, and emails from prospects and customers with a friendly, professional demeanor
  • Conduct timely outbound follow-up to new leads (no cold calling) to qualify and book free on-site estimates
  • Schedule, confirm, and reschedule estimate appointments on the salesperson’s calendar using the CRM
  • Maintain accurate CRM records: update lead stages, notes, tags, and next steps; trigger templates and reminders
  • Coordinate daily with the salesperson to align on route/availability and ensure a smooth estimate pipeline
  • Send appointment confirmations and reminders; manage no-shows, cancellations, and rebooking
  • Track and report KPIs such as speed-to-lead, booked estimates, show/cancel rates; provide daily summaries
  • Follow written SOPs precisely; suggest improvements to enhance efficiency and customer experience
  • Perform light admin tasks: convert approved estimates to work orders per SOP; complete data entry into job sheets
  • Update profitability spreadsheets by entering sales, labor, and materials data from the CRM (no payment processing)

Required Qualifications

  • 2+ years of customer service and/or appointment setting experience, ideally in home services or field operations
  • Excellent spoken and written English; strong phone etiquette and active listening skills
  • Proven experience scheduling appointments and managing calendars in a CRM
  • High attention to detail and consistency in following SOPs and documenting interactions
  • Comfortable working US business hours (Mountain Time) and collaborating daily with a small team

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in painting, roofing, landscaping, cleaning, or similar home services trades
  • Hands-on experience with DripJobs CRM; or similar platforms like Housecall Pro, Jobber, or ServiceTitan
  • Proficiency with spreadsheets for data entry and simple reporting (Google Sheets or Excel)

Required Skills & Tools

  • CRM proficiency
  • Google Sheets/Microsoft Excel for accurate data entry and tracking
  • VOIP/softphone call handling and basic email/calendar tools (e.g., Google Workspace)

Schedule & Pay

  • Full-Time position; Monday–Friday, approximately 8:00 AM–5:00 PM Mountain Standard Time (with some flexibility) 16h00 - 01h00 South African Standard Time.
  • Fully remote role for a United States (Colorado) based client
  • Pay ranges from R14 000 - R18 000 per month
  • Includes paid training on tools and SOPs, long-term role stability, and growth potential into project coordination/operations support.

System Requirements

  • Internet speed of at least 20 Mbps upload and download
  • Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher
  • 8 GB of RAM or higher
  • Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer
  • HD 720p webcam
  • Headset with microphone

Benefits

  • Competitive pay rates
  • Consistent hours and predictable workload
  • Fully remote work with long-term potential
  • Direct collaboration with the Founder and sales team, making a real impact
  • Supportive and inclusive work environment
  • Opportunity to grow alongside a home services SMB in the painting industry
