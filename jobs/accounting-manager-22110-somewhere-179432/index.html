Accounting Manager, Multi-Entity E-Commerce: Job Description

Location / Employment Details

Location

Remote (international)

Employment type

Full-time, independent contractor

Hours

9:00 am to 6:00 pm U.S. Eastern Time, Monday to Friday

Reports to

Chief Financial Officer, working day to day alongside the Controller

Scope

Accounting operations across multiple brand and fund entities

About the Company

We are a rapidly growing U.S.-based e-commerce acquisition company. Our founders started as Amazon sellers, built their own brands into category leaders, and now acquire established consumer product brands from founders and larger aggregators.

Once a brand joins our portfolio, we run it with standardized workflows and a disciplined, cash-flow-first operating model. We have closed several multi-brand portfolio acquisitions over the past two years, funded by accredited investors, and the portfolio keeps growing.

We are a lean, high-performance team. People here own outcomes, move fast, and hold a high bar for the quality of their work.

About the Role

Every acquisition we close adds brands, entities, and sets of books. Our accounting workload grows with each deal, and the bar stays the same: a fast, clean close and numbers we can put in front of investors without a second pass.

Our Controller owns financial reporting, controls, and the close calendar. This role is the engine underneath that. You will run day-to-day accounting across our brand and fund entities, keep every ledger reconciled and current, and deliver close work that is accurate the first time.

This is a hands-on manager role for someone who wants ownership. You will be trusted with real responsibility from your first month, and as the portfolio grows, the role grows with it.

Key Responsibilities

Multi-Entity Accounting Operations

  • Own the general ledger for multiple brand and fund entities, keeping each set of books current, reconciled, and audit-ready
  • Record and reconcile marketplace sales activity, including Amazon settlement reports, fees, refunds, and reserves
  • Manage intercompany activity and allocations between entities so balances tie out every month
  • Set up and onboard the books for newly acquired brands, including opening balances and chart of accounts mapping

Payables, Receivables & Cash

  • Run the bill payment process end to end: invoice intake, coding, approvals, and scheduled payments
  • Manage receivables and cash application across entities
  • Complete bank, credit card, and merchant account reconciliations on a set schedule

Month-End Close & Reporting

  • Execute the month-end, quarter-end, and year-end close for your entities against the Controller's close calendar
  • Prepare GAAP-compliant financial statements and the supporting schedules behind them
  • Prepare clean audit and tax support packages and answer auditor and tax advisor requests

Investor & Leadership Support

  • Support investor reporting, including capital account activity, distributions, and investor portal updates
  • Pull together analysis for leadership on brand performance, margins, and cash, and explain the numbers clearly in writing
  • Support budgeting, forecasting, and acquisition due diligence with accurate historical data

Process & Controls

  • Document and improve accounting procedures so the close gets faster as the portfolio grows
  • Follow and strengthen internal controls over payments, approvals, and account access
  • Review and guide the work of junior or offshore accounting support as the team expands

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field
  • 4+ years of progressive accounting experience, including owning the month-end close for one or more entities
  • Strong working knowledge of U.S. GAAP and financial statement preparation
  • Hands-on experience with QuickBooks Online, NetSuite, or a comparable accounting system
  • Advanced Excel skills: fast, accurate, and comfortable with large data sets and reconciliations
  • Fluent, polished English, written and spoken, with the ability to explain your work clearly and confidently to leadership
  • Full availability for 9:00 am to 6:00 pm U.S. Eastern Time
  • A reliable home office setup: dependable computer, stable high-speed internet, and a quiet, dedicated workspace

Preferred Qualifications

  • CPA, ACCA, CA, or equivalent professional designation
  • Audit experience at a Big 4 or mid-tier firm, or experience supporting financial statement audits from the company side
  • Multi-entity or holding company accounting, including intercompany and consolidations
  • E-commerce or Amazon seller accounting, including marketplace settlement reconciliation
  • Fund accounting or investor reporting experience (capital accounts, distributions, investor portals)
  • Experience in a high-growth, acquisitive, or private equity-backed company

Ideal Candidate Profile

You are

  • A confident owner. You take a set of books from messy to clean and stand behind the numbers without needing someone to check every line.
  • Fast and precise. You move quickly through data and Excel, and your work products are complete and tidy the first time.
  • A clear communicator. You explain what you did and why in a few crisp sentences, and you are comfortable presenting your work to senior leadership.
  • Highly organized. You juggle multiple entities and deadlines without letting anything slip.
  • Motivated by challenge. You want a bigger scope as the company grows and you thrive on a team where the bar is high and performance is noticed.

Why Join Us

  • A growing portfolio. Each acquisition adds new brands and new problems to solve, so your scope expands with the business.
  • Real ownership. You will run accounting for real entities from day one, not one narrow slice of a process.
  • Direct access to leadership. You will work closely with the CFO and Controller and see how your work shapes acquisition and investor decisions.
  • A team that values performance. We move fast, hold a high standard, and recognize the people who deliver.
