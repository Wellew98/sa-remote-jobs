Job Title

Website Editor (WordPress)

Industry

Digital Marketing / Web Development / Lead Generation (Property, Construction, Built Environment sectors)

Job Type

Freelance / Deliverables-based

Compensation

Approx. earning potential: £500–£700 depending on deliverables

Role Overview

The Website Editor is responsible for updating, editing, and maintaining client websites built primarily on WordPress. The role focuses on content updates, layout adjustments, and ensuring websites remain accurate, visually consistent, and aligned with marketing objectives.

Key Responsibilities

Content Updates

Edit and update website text, images, and page content

Upload blog posts, case studies, and landing page content

Ensure all content is formatted correctly and published accurately

WordPress Editing

Use WordPress CMS to manage pages, posts, and media

Work with page builders (e.g. Elementor or similar) to make layout edits

Adjust spacing, alignment, and structure of existing pages

Page Maintenance

Maintain consistency across pages (fonts, styles, formatting)

Update existing pages without altering core design unnecessarily

Ensure brand guidelines are followed

Basic Quality Assurance

Check pages before and after publishing

Identify and correct formatting issues, broken links, or visual inconsistencies

Ensure responsiveness across devices

Task-Based Delivery

Work through assigned website edit tasks individually

Deliver updates accurately and within agreed turnaround times

Follow briefs and instructions without requiring heavy supervision

Requirements

Proven experience using

WordPress

Experience with page builders such as

Elementor (or similar)

Strong attention to detail

Ability to follow instructions and implement changes precisely

Basic understanding of layout, typography, and web formatting

Ability to manage multiple small tasks efficiently

Prior experience in website editing or CMS-based roles preferred
