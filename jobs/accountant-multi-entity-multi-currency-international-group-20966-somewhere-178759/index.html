General Accountant – Multi-Entity, Multi-Currency (International Group)

Location / EmploymentRemote  Reports to: Financial Controller   Compensation: TBD

About the Company

We are a NASDAQ-listed international group operating across multiple legal entities and currencies, with a parent company, a European subsidiary, and a Middle East (UAE) operation. Our accounting runs on NetSuite, and our work spans several banking and investment platforms across jurisdictions.

About the Role

This role exists to run the day-to-day accounting engine for a multi-entity international group. The Controller owns technical accounting, consolidation, and reporting; this person owns the transactions, reconciliations, and schedules that make all of that possible and audit-ready.

The work is broad by design. In a single month you will move from recording payables and running payments, to reconciling banks and credit cards across entities, to preparing accruals and prepaid schedules, to revaluing foreign-currency accounts. Because the group spans multiple currencies and jurisdictions, precision and a strong audit trail matter more than speed alone.

The person who succeeds here is a full-cycle accountant who is genuinely comfortable holding many entities and currencies at once without letting details slip, and who takes ownership of chasing down what is missing rather than waiting to be told.

Key Responsibilities

Accounts Payable & Payments

  • Record vendor bills, invoices, and bank transactions in NetSuite, with supporting backup filed to the system and server for audit
  • Prepare weekly payment runs: obtain department-head and approver sign-off, compile the AP list, and confirm vendor banking details
  • Set up wires and ACH payments in the banking portal, and maintain complete payment documentation (confirmations, ACH summaries, approvals) for audit

Month-End Close & Reconciliations

  • Reconcile bank accounts to the general ledger across entities
  • Record and reconcile credit card transactions, and calculate and record bank, wire, and payment-processing fees
  • Prepare and record management fee income accruals using AUM data
  • Review statements proactively for missing or unrecorded bills, invoices, and payments

Multi-Entity & Intercompany Accounting

  • Maintain the books for multiple entities across North America, Europe, and the Middle East, including entity-specific salaries, expenses, and transfers
  • Record intercompany management fee expenses and calculate intercompany management fee invoicing
  • Reconcile intercompany accounts across all entities

Quarter-End Schedules & Technical Entries

  • Prepare and reconcile prepaid and accrual schedules (tax, audit, bonuses, director fees, consulting fees, and missing-bill accruals)
  • Record interest on cash-equivalent investments (GICs, T-Bills, brokerage holdings)
  • Calculate the quarterly director fee cash payment and RSU issuance using period-end pricing
  • Revalue foreign-currency accounts across USD, CHF, EUR, GBP, and AED

Year-End & Audit Preparation

  • Maintain current records of employee and vendor contracts, amendments, and board resolutions
  • Track RSU, DSU, and stock option exercise forms and payroll records
  • Review and adjust End of Service Benefit (EOSB) accruals for the UAE entity
  • Reconcile management fees and operating expenses, and support the external audit with clean, complete documentation

Required Qualifications

  • Degree in Accounting, Finance, or related field
  • Several years of full-cycle accounting experience (AP, bank and credit card reconciliations, accruals, close)
  • Multi-entity accounting experience
  • Multi-currency experience, including foreign-currency revaluation
  • Hands-on NetSuite experience
  • Strong documentation discipline and an audit-ready mindset
  • Clear written English and reliable communication with a distributed North America / Europe team

Preferred Qualifications

  • Intercompany accounting experience across international entities
  • Exposure to investment or asset management (management fee income, AUM-based accruals)
  • Experience supporting an international group with European and/or Middle East operations
  • Familiarity with UAE payroll and End of Service Benefits (EOSB)
  • CPA / ACCA / CA (completed or in progress)
  • Experience working through an ERP transition

Ideal Candidate Profile

You are meticulous and process-driven, and you take pride in a clean, well-documented set of books that an auditor can follow without questions. You can hold several entities and currencies in your head at once without dropping detail, and you treat "review for anything missing" as a real part of the job, not an afterthought.

You are self-directed in a remote setting, you keep to the close calendar, and you flag issues early. You would rather ask the sharp question now than explain a variance later.
