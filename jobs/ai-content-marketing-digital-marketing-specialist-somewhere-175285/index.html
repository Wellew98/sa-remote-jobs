AI Content Marketing & Digital Marketing Specialist Location: South Africa Salary Range: 1000 - 3000 USD Role Description: The AI Content Marketing & Digital Marketing Specialist is responsible for planning, creating, and optimizing content across digital platforms using both traditional strategies and AI-powered tools. This role combines creative storytelling with data-driven digital marketing to drive brand awareness, engagement, lead generation, and conversions. The ideal candidate is a tech-savvy marketer who understands how to use AI tools to streamline content creation, SEO, campaign execution, and performance tracking. Key Responsibilities: - Develop and implement content marketing strategies using a mix of human and AI-assisted tools. - Plan, create, and publish engaging content across platforms (blogs, social media, email, landing pages, video scripts, etc.). - Use AI content generation tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Jasper, Copy.ai, Writesonic) to accelerate content ideation and production. - Optimize content for SEO using AI tools like Surfer SEO, SEMrush, or Clearscope. - Manage digital campaigns across email, social media, search, and display channels. - Analyze campaign data and adjust strategies to improve performance (traffic, engagement, conversion). - Coordinate with designers, video editors, and developers to execute campaigns. - Monitor trends in AI and marketing to implement new strategies and tools. - Maintain content calendars, campaign timelines, and reporting dashboards. - Ensure brand consistency, voice, and compliance across all digital assets. Qualifications: - Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Communications, Digital Media, or a related field (or equivalent experience). - 2–4 years of experience in content marketing, digital marketing, or a similar role. - Strong copywriting and content creation skills across different formats and channels. - Proficiency in using AI tools for content creation and optimization (e.g., ChatGPT, Jasper, Grammarly, Canva Magic Write). - Solid understanding of SEO, social media algorithms, email marketing, and content performance metrics. - Experience with marketing platforms like HubSpot, Mailchimp, Meta Ads, Google Ads, and Google Analytics. - Ability to manage multiple projects and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment. - Strong analytical, creative, and communication skills. Preferred Skills: - Experience with WordPress, Webflow, or CMS platforms. - Familiarity with AI image generation tools (e.g., Midjourney, DALL·E) for visual content support. - Knowledge of A/B testing, CRO, and marketing automation. - Basic understanding of HTML/CSS or landing page builders. - Experience working in B2B SaaS, e-commerce, or tech-driven industries is a plus. Details:  - Independent contractor agreement paid 2x monthly. - Client has a desire for long term commitment.
