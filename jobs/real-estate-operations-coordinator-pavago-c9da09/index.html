Real Estate Operations Coordinator (Property Management, Leasing & Tenant Coordination) – Remote

Property Management | Leasing | Tenant Coordination | Operations

Position Type

Full-Time, Remote

Working Hours

U.S. Client Business Hours (flexibility required for leasing cycles, rent collections, and maintenance coordination)

About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a Real Estate Operations Coordinator to support the day-to-day operations of residential and commercial property portfolios.

This is a fast-paced, operations-focused role where you’ll help ensure tenant communication remains organized, leasing processes stay compliant, maintenance requests are resolved efficiently, and property records remain accurate and up to date.

You’ll serve as the operational link between tenants, property owners, vendors, and internal property management teams to ensure smooth portfolio operations and an exceptional tenant experience.

If you’re highly organized, detail-oriented, experienced with property management systems, and enjoy coordinating multiple moving parts, this role is an excellent fit.

What You’ll Own

Tenant Communication & Support

Respond to tenant inquiries regarding rent payments, lease terms, maintenance requests, and property policies.

Maintain detailed communication records within property management platforms.

Send reminders for rent due dates, lease renewals, inspections, and compliance notices.

Deliver professional, timely, and customer-focused support throughout the tenant lifecycle.

Leasing & Documentation

Process new lease agreements, renewals, amendments, and tenant onboarding documentation.

Maintain organized tenant files, including applications, identification, inspection reports, and signed agreements.

Ensure leasing documentation remains complete, accurate, and compliant.

Assist with property listings across platforms such as Zillow, Apartments.com, MLS, and similar listing services.

Property Management Systems

Maintain accurate records within platforms such as Yardi, AppFolio, Buildium , or similar systems.

Track rent collections, delinquencies, occupancy changes, late fees, and lease expirations.

Generate operational reports including rent rolls, occupancy reports, delinquency summaries, and leasing activity.

Maintenance Coordination

Create and assign maintenance work orders to vendors.

Monitor maintenance requests from submission through completion.

Coordinate communication between tenants and vendors.

Verify completed work and reconcile vendor invoices against approved work orders.

Ensure maintenance issues are resolved promptly and professionally.

Owner Reporting & Operational Support

Prepare owner statements, cash flow summaries, vacancy reports, and occupancy updates.

Provide regular updates on rent collections, maintenance activity, leasing progress, and property performance.

Ensure reports are accurate, organized, and delivered on schedule.

Compliance & Quality Control

Maintain compliance with landlord-tenant regulations, Fair Housing standards, and internal company procedures.

Ensure all documentation remains organized, complete, and audit-ready.

Support operational consistency through accurate recordkeeping and process adherence.

Requirements

Must-Have Qualifications

Experience

2+ years of experience in property management, leasing coordination, real estate operations, or property administration.

Hands-on experience using

Yardi, AppFolio, Buildium , or similar property management software.

Experience with lease administration, tenant communication, rent collection tracking, and maintenance coordination.

Technical Skills

Strong proficiency with Microsoft Office and Google Workspace.

Familiarity with lease documentation, property operations, and reporting.

Excellent written and verbal English communication skills.

Strong organizational skills with exceptional attention to detail.

Ability to manage multiple priorities in a high-volume environment.

Nice-to-Have

Experience managing portfolios of 100+ units.

Multi-state property management experience.

Familiarity with owner statements, trust accounting, and security deposit management.

Experience supporting residential, commercial, or mixed-use property portfolios.

What Makes You a Strong Fit

Excellent communicator with tenants, vendors, and property owners.

Highly organized with strong follow-through.

Detail-oriented and process-driven.

Comfortable managing multiple operational workflows simultaneously.

Solutions-focused with strong operational judgment.

Thrives in fast-paced property management environments.

What a Typical Day Looks Like

Respond to tenant inquiries and maintenance requests.

Process leases, renewals, and tenant documentation.

Update rent payments and occupancy records.

Coordinate vendors and monitor maintenance work orders.

Prepare owner reports and operational summaries.

Maintain accurate records within property management systems.

Ensure compliance across leasing and operational processes.

In short

You keep property operations organized, compliant, and running efficiently by coordinating leasing, maintenance, tenant communication, and operational reporting.

Key Metrics for Success (KPIs)

Rent collection percentage

Vacancy fill time

Maintenance resolution turnaround time

Accuracy of lease and tenant documentation

Owner satisfaction with reporting and communication

Operational accuracy and system data integrity

Why This Role Stands Out

Fully remote opportunity supporting U.S. property portfolios.

High ownership across leasing, maintenance, reporting, and operations.

Direct impact on tenant experience and property performance.

Exposure to residential and commercial real estate operations.

Career growth opportunities into Property Manager, Portfolio Coordinator, or Operations Manager roles.
