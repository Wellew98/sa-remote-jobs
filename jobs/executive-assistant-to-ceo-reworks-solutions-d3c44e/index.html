Job Title

Executive Assistant to CEO

Location

South Africa

Job Type

Full-Time, Remote

Working Hours

US Hours (9am-5pm EST)

Salary

South African Rand (ZAR)

A highly capable and proactive Executive Assistant to CEO is sought to serve as a trusted right-hand partner to a fast-paced executive. This is not a traditional administrative role; the successful candidate will play a critical role in bringing structure to ambiguity, protecting executive priorities, managing follow-through, and ensuring important initiatives are completed effectively. The ideal candidate possesses exceptional organizational skills, strong emotional intelligence, excellent judgment, and the ability to operate independently while supporting a high-performing executive.

Key Responsibilities

Manage and optimize the CEO’s calendar, priorities, and daily schedule

Protect executive time by prioritizing requests, redirecting interruptions, and ensuring focus on high-value activities

Manage inboxes, communications, and follow-up activities

Coordinate meetings, agendas, action items, and executive communications

Track open tasks, projects, and commitments to ensure completion and accountability

Prepare reports, summaries, dashboards, meeting notes, and follow-up trackers

Support project coordination, operational initiatives, and executive priorities

Anticipate challenges, identify risks, and proactively resolve issues before they escalate

Maintain accurate records, documentation, and executive reporting materials

Assist with administrative, operational, HR, payroll, and business support activities as required

Support executive decision-making through research, organization, and information management

Build systems and processes that improve efficiency and create structure within a fast-paced environment
