About The Role

We are looking for an experienced and results-driven ABA Healthcare Recruiter to join our client's growing team. The successful candidate will be responsible for managing the full recruitment lifecycle, with a strong focus on sourcing and recruiting qualified professionals within the Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) industry.

This role will focus on identifying, attracting, screening, and hiring Behaviour Technicians (BTs), Registered Behavior Technicians (RBTs), Board Certified Behavior Analysts (BCBAs), and other ABA-related healthcare professionals across the United States.

The ideal candidate will have previous experience recruiting within the US healthcare or ABA industry, a strong understanding of clinical and behavioural health recruitment, and the ability to manage high-volume recruitment pipelines while maintaining a positive candidate experience.

This is a hands-on recruitment role requiring someone who is proactive, organised, target-driven, and comfortable working in a fast-paced remote environment.

Key Responsibilities

1. Full-Cycle Recruitment

Manage the end-to-end recruitment process for ABA and behavioural healthcare positions.

Work closely with hiring managers to understand staffing requirements, role expectations, and candidate profiles.

Source, attract, and recruit qualified candidates for BT, RBT, BCBA, and other ABA-related positions.

Review applications and CVs to assess candidate suitability.

Conduct initial screening interviews to evaluate experience, qualifications, communication skills, and overall fit.

Coordinate interviews between candidates and hiring managers.

Manage candidate communication and provide regular recruitment updates.

Facilitate offers, follow-ups, and onboarding coordination.

2. Candidate Sourcing & Pipeline Management

Proactively source candidates through platforms such as Indeed, LinkedIn, and other relevant recruitment channels.

Develop and maintain candidate pipelines for current and future hiring requirements.

Identify passive candidates and implement targeted sourcing strategies.

Build relationships with qualified ABA professionals.

Maintain accurate candidate records and recruitment activity within the ATS.

Monitor recruitment progress and ensure candidates move efficiently through the hiring process.

3. ABA Industry & Clinical Recruitment

Recruit across key ABA-related positions, including

Board Certified Behavior Analysts (BCBAs).

Registered Behavior Technicians (RBTs).

Behavior Technicians (BTs).

Clinical Supervisors.

ABA Case Managers.

Clinical Directors.

Other behavioural health professionals.

Understand the differences between ABA-related roles, qualifications, and certification requirements.

Identify candidates with the appropriate credentials, clinical experience, and relevant state-specific requirements.

Verify candidate qualifications and certifications where required.

4. Recruitment Performance & Reporting

Manage multiple open vacancies and recruitment priorities simultaneously.

Monitor sourcing activity, candidate conversion rates, and recruitment progress.

Work towards agreed recruitment targets and hiring deadlines.

Provide regular updates to hiring managers on candidate pipelines and recruitment challenges.

Identify bottlenecks and recommend improvements to recruitment processes.

5. Candidate Experience & Relationship Management

Maintain professional and positive relationships with candidates throughout the recruitment process.

Ensure timely communication and consistent follow-ups.

Provide candidates with clear information about roles, expectations, and recruitment stages.

Deliver a professional and engaging candidate experience.

Minimum Requirements

Previous experience recruiting within the US healthcare, ABA, or behavioural health industry.

Experience recruiting for positions such as BCBAs, RBTs, BTs, or other clinical healthcare professionals.

Strong understanding of the ABA industry and relevant clinical roles.

Hands-on experience with candidate sourcing through Indeed, LinkedIn, or similar recruitment platforms.

Experience managing the full recruitment lifecycle.

Strong candidate screening and interviewing skills.

Experience managing multiple vacancies and recruitment pipelines.

Excellent English communication skills, both written and verbal.

Strong organisational and time-management skills.

Ability to work independently in a remote environment.

Strong follow-up, relationship-building, and candidate management abilities.

Preferred Personality Traits

Proactive and highly self-motivated.

Results-driven and comfortable working towards recruitment targets.

Confident communicator with strong interpersonal skills.

Persistent and resourceful when sourcing difficult-to-find candidates.

Highly organised and detail-oriented.

Adaptable and comfortable managing changing recruitment priorities.

Strong sense of ownership and accountability.

Able to build relationships with candidates and hiring managers.

Resilient and able to work in a high-volume recruitment environment.
