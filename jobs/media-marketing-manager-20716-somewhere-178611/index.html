Media & Marketing Manager About the Company Our client is a growing faith-based organization committed to expanding its reach through engaging digital content, impactful media, and strategic marketing. They are seeking a creative and highly organized Media & Marketing Manager to oversee their digital presence, strengthen audience engagement, and ensure consistent brand messaging across all platforms. Role Overview The Media & Marketing Manager will be responsible for developing, managing, and executing the organization's digital media and marketing strategy across multiple platforms. This individual will oversee content creation, social media management, website optimization, graphic and video production, and marketing campaigns while collaborating closely with leadership to ensure all communications align with the organization's mission and brand standards. The ideal candidate is a proactive marketer with strong creative instincts, exceptional organizational skills, and experience managing multiple digital projects simultaneously.
