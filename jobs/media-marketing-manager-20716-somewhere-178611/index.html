Media & Marketing Manager

About the Company

Our client is a growing faith-based organization committed to expanding its reach through engaging digital content, impactful media, and strategic marketing. They are seeking a creative and highly organized Media & Marketing Manager to oversee their digital presence, strengthen audience engagement, and ensure consistent brand messaging across all platforms.

Role Overview

The Media & Marketing Manager will be responsible for developing, managing, and executing the organization's digital media and marketing strategy across multiple platforms. This individual will oversee content creation, social media management, website optimization, graphic and video production, and marketing campaigns while collaborating closely with leadership to ensure all communications align with the organization's mission and brand standards.

The ideal candidate is a proactive marketer with strong creative instincts, exceptional organizational skills, and experience managing multiple digital projects simultaneously.

Key Responsibilities

Digital Marketing & Brand Management

- Develop and execute digital marketing strategies that increase brand awareness and audience engagement

- Manage the organization's online presence across multiple digital platforms

- Ensure consistent branding, messaging, and visual identity across all communications

- Identify opportunities to improve digital reach through SEO, social media, and content marketing initiatives

- Monitor marketing performance and recommend improvements based on analytics and engagement metrics

- Repurpose content into engaging short-form videos and graphics

Content Creation & Creative Production

- Design graphics, promotional materials, and digital assets for events and ongoing initiatives

- Edit and produce video content for social media, web, and digital campaigns

- Review and organize recorded footage into engaging highlight clips

- Coordinate creative projects and communicate project status with internal stakeholders

- Maintain organized creative asset libraries and project tracking systems

Website & Mobile App Management

- Update website content and optimize pages for SEO

- Manage metadata, keywords, page titles, and overall website performance

- Update and maintain the organization's mobile application as needed

- Ensure digital platforms remain current, accurate, and user-friendly

Marketing Operations

- Maintain project lists and track creative deliverables from concept through completion

- Coordinate marketing initiatives and production timelines

- Audit digital assets regularly to ensure accuracy and brand consistency

- Maintain marketing documentation and creative workflows

- Support special projects and organizational events as assigned

Administrative Support

- Manage marketing-related email communications

- Maintain organized digital files and documentation

- Coordinate with leadership regarding project priorities and deadlines

- Assist with preparation for events, presentations, and organizational initiatives

- Handle confidential information with professionalism and discretion

- 2–5 years of experience in Digital Marketing, Social Media Management, Content Creation, or a related field

- Experience managing multiple social media platforms for an organization or brand

- Strong graphic design and video editing skills

- Experience developing and executing content calendars

- Excellent written and verbal English communication skills

- Strong organizational and project management abilities

- Professional, dependable, and proactive work ethic
