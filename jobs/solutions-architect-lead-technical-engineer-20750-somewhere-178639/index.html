Role Overview

We are seeking a high-energy, high-agency Staff Lead Engineer / Solutions Architect to own technical architecture, integration design, and active codebase delivery across our core platform operations processing $600K–$700K/month in transaction volume.

This is a hands-on seat for an AI-forward engineer who thrives on taking projects from 0 to 1 in fast-paced startup environments. Sitting at the intersection of technical strategy and execution, you will serve as the technical authority on our infrastructure—architecting integration roadmaps, aggressively leveraging modern AI development tools (Cursor, Claude Code, GitHub Copilot) to accelerate build velocity, and contributing directly to the production codebase. You will mentor and direct a squad of 2–3 execution engineers, translating executive vision into production-grade systems while holding ultimate accountability for code quality, architectural integrity, and delivery momentum.

Key Responsibilities

1. 0-to-1 Architecture & Hands-On Codebase Contribution

- System Design & Roadmapping: Lead technical discovery and architect scalable, end-to-end integration roadmaps across our proprietary platform ecosystem.

- Active Production Engineering: Serve as a primary hands-on contributor to the production codebase—building functional integration prototypes, REST APIs, secure data pipelines, and core feature implementations.

- AI Tool Integration: Aggressively integrate modern AI coding workflows, LLMs, and developer tooling into the engineering process to maximize team output and accelerate development cycles.

- Constraint & Security Mapping: Evaluate platform constraints, performance bottlenecks, database security parameters, and scalability limits to maintain high system reliability.

2. Startup Leadership, AI Forwardness & Team Governance

- Technical Supervision: Lead, mentor, and direct a focused team of 2–3 execution engineers on daily project builds, sprint goals, and feature releases.

- High-Agency Execution: Take complete ownership of complex technical initiatives from concept through production deployment—proactively identifying edge cases and resolving technical debt.

- Quality & Code Reviews: Conduct rigorous code reviews, enforce clean code standards, and preserve architectural integrity across monorepo and database environments.

3. Cross-Functional Technical Strategy

- Executive Liaison: Translate high-level commercial objectives from leadership into precise technical specifications, database schemas, and sprint backlogs.

- Platform Optimization: Architect long-term technical resolutions for recurring platform bugs, database bottlenecks, and scaling challenges.

Required Skills & Qualifications

- Experience Level: 6–10+ years of software engineering, backend architecture, or systems integration experience, with 2+ years operating as a Staff Engineer, Lead Engineer, or Solutions Architect within a fast-paced startup environment.

- Startup Track Record & High Agency: Demonstrated experience taking technical projects from 0 to 1 as a major contributor or technical lead in early-to-mid stage startups.

- AI-Forward Mindset: Heavy, active reliance on modern AI developer tools (e.g., Cursor, Claude Code, Codex, Copilot) to multiply engineering throughput and accelerate prototyping.

- Mandatory Technical Stack: Deep proficiency in TypeScript , modern database design with PostgreSQL / Supabase , and Monorepo architectures.

- API & Prototyping Mastery: Hands-on experience building REST APIs, microservices, secure data pipelines, and rapid proof-of-concept prototypes.

- Engineering Leadership: Proven experience managing, mentoring, and driving technical output across direct reports (2–3 execution engineers).

- Communication & Polish: Exceptional written and verbal English skills with the executive presence to brief C-suite leaders and guide engineering teams.

Preferred Qualifications

- Working familiarity with modern cloud environments, serverless architectures, and infrastructure-as-code best practices.

Success Metrics (KPIs)

- 0-to-1 Build Velocity: Rapidly prototyping, validating, and shipping high-quality production integrations from initial concept to launch.

- Codebase Contribution & AI Leverage: Consistently shipping high-grade code while elevating team-wide output through AI-assisted workflows.

- Architectural Integrity: Building resilient, scalable system structures within Supabase/PostgreSQL and monorepo environments with minimal technical debt.

- Squad Throughput & Leadership: Successfully guiding direct reports (2–3 engineers) to hit sprint milestones on schedule with high code quality.
