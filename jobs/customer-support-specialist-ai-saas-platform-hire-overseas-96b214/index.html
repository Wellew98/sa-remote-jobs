We're looking for a

Customer Support Specialist to deliver fast, thoughtful, and technically informed support for users of a growing AI voice automation platform. You will handle inbound questions, troubleshoot issues, guide developers and non-technical users, and help shape a smooth end-to-end customer experience across a global user base including European customers.

This role requires availability that overlaps with European business hours to ensure timely support coverage for customers across the EU. If you enjoy solving problems, communicating clearly, and working inside a product-focused, fast-moving AI environment, this role is a strong fit.

What You'll Work On

Customer Support Across Channels

Manage and respond to inbound support requests through email and internal tools from customers across North America and Europe

Provide clear, empathetic, and accurate responses to both technical and non-technical users

Troubleshoot platform issues by reviewing logs, referencing documentation, and reproducing customer scenarios

Escalate complex or urgent issues to product and engineering teams

Support real-time inquiries inside community spaces such as Discord

Documentation and Insights

Identify recurring questions, bugs, and product gaps and document them clearly

Contribute updates to help center articles, troubleshooting guides, and internal documentation

Provide structured feedback to cross-functional teams on customer sentiment and platform usability

Internal Collaboration

Work with engineering, product, and operations teams to close support loops

Share insights that improve onboarding, documentation, and user experience

Help refine internal support systems, workflows, and templates

What You Bring

2 to 3 years of experience in customer support, ideally within SaaS or technical environments

Strong written English with a clear, concise, and customer-centered communication style

Ability to manage multiple tickets or conversations simultaneously without losing accuracy

Comfortable using tools such as Plain, Intercom, Zendesk, or similar support platforms

Tech-curious and able to learn APIs, integrations, or workflow systems quickly

Available to work with overlap during European business hours to support EU-based customers

Nice to Have

Experience supporting developer tools, AI products, or community-based support environments

Prior experience supporting customers across multiple time zones or international markets

Familiarity with European customer expectations and communication norms

How to Apply

Please include

Your

updated resume

A short Loom video (1 to 2 minutes) introducing yourself and describing your experience supporting SaaS or technical products

Only candidates who submit a Loom video will be moved to the next step of the hiring process.

If you are motivated by helping users succeed, enjoy technical problem-solving, and want to support a global customer base at a company pushing the boundaries of voice automation and AI, this role gives you the scope and the direct impact to do your best work.
