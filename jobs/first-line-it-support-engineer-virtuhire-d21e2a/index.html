Our client in the UK is looking for a First-Line IT Support Engineer. This person will be the day-to-day IT contact for their managed IT customers, around 130 UK dental practices. They'll own first-line support for those customers from start to finish: tickets, monitoring alerts, and keeping each practice informed and happy. Volume is currently around 14 tickets a day (about 11 from customers and 3 from monitoring), with Mondays the busiest.

Responsibilities

Own the first-line (and, where possible, second-line) ticket queue for the IT customers: triage, first response, fix, and keep the customer updated until it's closed.

Microsoft 365 / Entra IDnew users, leavers, licences and mailboxes

Remote support for Windows PCs, printers and remote troubleshooting of networks.

Respond to monitoring alerts and daily backup checks, following up failures and escalating server issues to the UK team quickly

Be the friendly, reliable voice of the IT service: clear updates, follow-ups, and spotting unhappy customers or recurring problems early.

Work from the growing runbook of known fixes. They need someone who can be productive from week one, not a trainee.

Third-line work, site visits and dental imaging support stay with the UK team, who also provide backup cover.

Support hours

The UK team works on a rota, and you'd join it on one of four shifts (UK time): 08:00 to 16:00, 08:30 to 16:30, 09:00 to 17:00 or 09:30 to 17:30. You would need to be comfortable with an early start and later finish to ensure the job is completed.

Proven first-line service desk or MSP support experience, ideally supporting UK or other offshore customers, and ready to work with little hand-holding.

Microsoft 365 and Entra ID administration: new users, leavers, licences and mailboxes.

Remote support for Windows PCs and printers, plus remote network troubleshooting.

Comfortable handling monitoring alerts and backup checks, and knowing when and how to escalate.

Fluent, native-level spoken and written English is essential. You will speak to UK practice staff by phone and email every day.

A naturally friendly, patient and reliable phone manner, who spots unhappy customers or recurring problems early.

Nice to have: NinjaOne, Ahsay, HaloPSA. Zendesk or other PSA/ticketing experience also counts.

Reliable home setup: fibre connection, quiet workspace and backup power, as the role is phone heavy.
