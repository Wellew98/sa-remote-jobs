LocationRemote

Role Overview

We are seeking a Staff Accountant to support accounting operations across our property management portfolio. This role will primarily focus on accounting activity within Yardi, with exposure to Entrata and RealPage during transition periods. The ideal candidate is detail-oriented, dependable, and able to work independently in a fast-paced, multi-system environment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Perform day-to-day accounting support for assigned properties/portfolios
  • Maintain and update accounting records in Yardi and other systems as needed
  • Support monthly close activities, including reconciliations, journal entries, and reporting
  • Assist with accounts payable, accounts receivable, and general ledger tasks as assigned
  • Review financial data for accuracy and completeness
  • Work collaboratively with internal teams and client contacts as needed
  • Support transition work as properties move between systems
  • Ensure timely completion of assigned accounting tasks and deadlines
  • Follow internal controls, documentation standards, and client requirements

Required Qualifications

  • 2+ years of accounting experience preferred
  • Experience with property management accounting strongly preferred
  • Working knowledge of Yardi required or highly preferred
  • Experience with Entrata and/or RealPage is a strong plus
  • Strong understanding of general accounting principles
  • High attention to detail and strong organizational skills
  • Ability to work independently and manage deadlines
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills
  • Comfortable working in a remote, cross-functional environment

Preferred Qualifications

  • Prior experience working with U.S.-based companies
  • Multi-entity or portfolio accounting experience
  • Experience supporting month-end close and reconciliations
  • Exposure to outsourced or third-party property management operations

Work Expectations

  • Must be available to work U.S. business hours
  • Must have reliable internet and own equipment
  • Must be comfortable working in secure systems and following data confidentiality requirements

What Success Looks Like

  • Accurate and timely accounting support
  • Strong ownership of assigned tasks
  • Ability to learn internal processes quickly
  • Reliable support during portfolio transitions
  • Clear communication and dependable follow-through
