We're looking for a

Performance Marketing Specialist to handle the day-to-day execution of paid social ad operations for a data-first growth marketing agency managing paid media across a focused roster of e-commerce and DTC brands. You will own ad operations across Meta and TikTok, with additional support across Snapchat, keeping campaigns built, QA'd, and running correctly at all times.

This is not a budget management or creative role. You will not be writing copy or designing assets, and you will not be making strategic media buying decisions. Your job is to build ads correctly, QA everything thoroughly, keep campaigns organized, and ensure nothing goes live with errors. If you are detail-oriented, fast, experienced across Meta and TikTok, and take pride in clean, organized campaign builds with zero errors, this role is a strong fit.

What You'll Work On

Ad Building and Campaign Management

Build and set up ads across Meta (primarily Facebook and Instagram), TikTok, and Snapchat following client-provided parameters and naming conventions

QA creative, copy, and landing pages submitted by clients before building out campaigns

Create ad names using the internal naming builder and maintain naming convention consistency across all accounts

Upload preview links for client approvals and duplicate approved ads into target campaigns

Manage administrative and data entry touchpoints including promotional code creation and UTM setup

Optimization and Monitoring

Monitor campaign analytics and make adjustments according to client direction and optimization parameters

Conduct basic QA on live campaigns to flag issues early

Track keyword positions and conduct targeted keyword research for Google search campaigns

Support templated reporting and dashboarding using client-provided templates

Reporting

Create monthly creative performance reports using Atria to build Google Slides decks

Support ad hoc digital marketing tasks as directed with training provided by the client as needed

What You Bring

2 or more years of hands-on Facebook Ads experience including building and optimizing campaigns

1 to 2 or more years of experience with Google Ads specifically keyword search

1 or more years of Shopify experience

Proven experience with TikTok Ads and Snapchat Ads

Basic experience with Google Analytics or a similar analytics tool

Familiarity with DTC and e-commerce brands and how paid media supports their growth

Experience managing campaigns across multiple clients simultaneously

Proficient in Google Suite including Sheets, Slides, and Docs

Detail-oriented with strong organizational skills and a zero-error standard on ad builds and QA

Nice to Have

Google or Meta certifications

Experience with Google Data Studio or a similar data visualization platform

Familiarity with Motion or a similar creative and media buyer communications platform

Experience with Atria or similar creative analytics tools
