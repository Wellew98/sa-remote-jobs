Job TypeFull-Time

LocationRemote (UK hours)

About the Company

Our client is an established UK-based company specialising in premium stone worktops and bespoke surfaces for residential and commercial projects. Operating within the construction and home improvement industry, the business is seeking an experienced Financial Controller to take full ownership of its accounting and financial operations.

Job Summary

We are looking for a highly experienced, fully qualified Financial Controller to independently manage the complete finance function of a UK-based business.

This is a hands-on, client-facing role requiring strong expertise in UK accounting, management accounts, VAT, payroll, credit control, cost accounting, and financial reporting.

Fluent written and spoken English is mandatory, as you will communicate directly with UK-based directors, customers, suppliers, and HMRC.

The ideal candidate is proficient in Xero, has experience working with UK limited companies, and can take ownership of financial operations from day one with minimal supervision.

Key Responsibilities

Financial Reporting: Prepare monthly management accounts, P&L statements, balance sheets, cash-flow forecasts, and year-end financial reports.

UK Tax & Compliance: Manage VAT returns, PAYE payroll, RTI submissions, pensions, CIS, and HMRC compliance.

Credit Control: Manage outstanding payments, chase overdue invoices directly with customers, and maintain accurate debtor reports.

Account Reconciliations: Perform bank, credit card, ledger, and control account reconciliations accurately and on time.

Cost Accounting: Track project profitability, job costs, stock usage, material wastage, and supplier expenditure.

Accounts Payable: Process supplier invoices, manage payments, and maintain accurate purchase ledgers.

Financial Systems: Maintain accurate records in Xero or similar accounting software and improve accounting processes through automation.

Stakeholder Communication: Work directly with UK directors, customers, suppliers, external accountants, and HMRC on financial matters.

F ully qualified accountant (ACCA, CIMA, ACA, or equivalent recognised qualification).

3+ years of hands-on UK accounting experience, ideally managing the full finance function of a UK limited company.

Fluent English (mandatory): Excellent written and spoken English, with confidence communicating directly with UK clients, customers, suppliers, and directors.

Strong experience preparing management accounts, P&L statements, balance sheets, and cash-flow forecasts independently.

Proven knowledge of UK VAT, Making Tax Digital, PAYE payroll, RTI submissions, and workplace pensions.

Strong account reconciliation, credit control, and cost accounting skills.

Proficiency in Xero, Sage, or QuickBooks, with advanced Excel skills.

Ability to work independently, solve problems, and manage deadlines without extensive training or supervision.

Reliable computer, high-speed internet, and a quiet, secure remote workspace.

Nice to Have

Experience in construction, real estate, manufacturing, or the kitchen and bathroom industry.

Knowledge of CIS and the UK construction VAT domestic reverse charge.

Experience with job costing, inventory management, and tracking material wastage.

Familiarity with HMRC enquiries and corporation tax.

Experience integrating accounting software with CRM or job-costing systems.

Knowledge of vehicle finance, fleet costs, and financial process automation.

🚀 Opportunity to take ownership of the finance function and improve financial operations.

🎯 High-impact role working directly with company directors.
