Position Details

Start Date

Immediate, upon completion of recruitment and onboarding

Location

100% remote — must be based in South Africa

Students

Young learners aged 4–16, based in China, proficiency A1–C1

Class Type

One-on-one and one-on-two (max two learners per class)

Language of Instruction

English

Platform

ClassIn

Rate

US$6.50 per hour

Class Duration

25 minutes (regular); 21–25 minutes (demo)

Contract Type

Independent Contractor (remote)

Hours

Minimum 14 hours per week, within the teaching window of 12:00–15:30 SAST (18:00–21:30 BJT), Monday to Sunday. You decide how to spread your hours — up to a maximum of six days a week.

Bookings

Hours run on student bookings rather than fixed guaranteed hours. Reliable teachers with regular students tend to build steady, recurring schedules.
