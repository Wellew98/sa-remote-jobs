About Huzzle

At Huzzle, we connect exceptional talent with top opportunities at leading companies across the UK, US, Canada, Europe, and Australia. Our clients include fast-growing startups, digital agencies, SaaS companies, and tech-enabled businesses across industries such as MarTech, FinTech, EdTech, and eCommerce. Unlike traditional outsourcing agencies, we place you directly with innovative companies where you become a valued member of the team.

About the Company

Our client is a results-driven growth marketing partner specializing in end-to-end strategy and execution for high-growth tech companies and startups. With a mission to redefine growth through community and deep partnerships, the team supports a global portfolio across B2B SaaS, robotics, consumer tech, and retail. They provide comprehensive services including omnichannel paid media, ad creative sprints, conversion rate optimization, and website design to help businesses scale from early traction to eight-figure revenues.

The team is composed of seasoned experts with backgrounds from industry giants and agile startups, bringing a high-performance, "startup-to-scale" mindset to every project. Operating with a focus on precision and speed, they collaborate directly with founders and marketing directors to build robust acquisition channels across North America, Europe, and Asia. This is an opportunity to join a fast-paced, innovative environment where data-driven strategies and creative execution drive measurable global impact.

Job Type

Part-time (with potential for Full-time)

Job Summary

We are looking for a detail-oriented Finance Operations expert to take full ownership of the day-to-day financial health of a scaling agency. You will be the backbone of our back-office operations, ensuring every receipt is captured, every invoice is paid, and our profitability is crystal clear. This is a role for a QuickBooks power user who enjoys the intersection of precision accounting and proactive operations. If you are someone who takes pride in a perfectly reconciled month-end and has the professional persistence to manage accounts receivable effectively, we want to hear from you.

Key Responsibilities

Maintain impeccable records in QuickBooks Online, including receipt management, categorization, and reconciliations.

Manage full-cycle payroll for Canadian employees and coordinate timely contractor payments.

Oversee multi-currency accounts and handle complex currency conversions.

Generate client invoices and lead the Accounts Receivable process with professional, persistent follow-ups on overdue payments.

Perform monthly ad spend reconciliations and calculate management fee billing.

Lead the month-end close process and produce comprehensive monthly P&L statements.

Generate weekly AR reports and track client-specific profitability to support leadership decisions.

Expert-level proficiency with QuickBooks Online.

Proven experience working within an agency or service-based business model.

Deep comfort managing multi-currency environments and international transactions.

Strong professional communication skills with a track record of successfully managing collections and payment chasing.

Radical attention to detail and a self-starting nature; you meet deadlines without needing reminders.

Solid understanding of Canadian payroll regulations and tax requirements.

Experience with advanced financial reporting and data visualization tools.

Previous experience in a high-growth startup or expansion-stage company.

Familiarity with specific agency billing software or project management tools.
