Senior Project Manager / Operations & Product Lifecycle Manager

LocationRemote

Employment TypeFull-Time

HoursEastern Standard business hours (flexible as needed for remote work)

Seniority LevelSenior

Start DateImmediate / as agreed

Role Overview

We are seeking a highly capable Senior Project Manager / Operations & Product Lifecycle Manager to support a growing entrepreneurial company in scaling its operations and product offerings. Working closely with the founder, you will take ownership of operational processes, product lifecycle tracking, and vendor coordination.

This role is ideal for a detail-oriented, process-driven professional who thrives in a fast-paced environment, proactively identifies improvements, and confidently communicates with leadership and external stakeholders.

By maintaining operational systems, spreadsheets, and project management tools, you will play a pivotal role in ensuring the business runs efficiently, maintaining quality, and supporting innovation and growth.

Key Responsibilities

  • Own and manage the product lifecycle , ensuring accurate tracking and documentation across all stages.
  • Develop, implement, and optimize operational processes to support business growth and efficiency.
  • Maintain and manage complex spreadsheets for tracking products, performance metrics, and operational workflows.
  • Coordinate with external vendors , managing deliverables, timelines, and quality standards.
  • Utilize project management tools (e.g., Asana) to organize tasks, projects, and workflows.
  • Support the founder by managing operational details and reducing administrative workload .
  • Ensure quality control across processes, documentation, and deliverables.
  • Leverage AI tools where appropriate to improve workflow efficiency and automation.
  • Monitor operational performance and provide insights through data tracking and reporting .

Required Skills & Qualifications

Hard Skills

  • Excellent English proficiency (5/5) — written and verbal communication.
  • Expert-level proficiency in Google Workspace (Sheets, Docs, Drive, etc.).
  • Expert-level proficiency in Microsoft Office (Excel, Word, PowerPoint).
  • Advanced spreadsheet creation and management (data tracking, organization, analysis).
  • Experience with Asana or similar project management tools.
  • Strong process development and operational improvement capabilities.
  • Ability to work independently in a fast-paced, growing company .

Soft Skills / Personality Traits

  • Exceptional attention to detail and organizational skills.
  • Comfortable communicating directly and confidently with leadership and vendors.
  • Outspoken and confident — willing to speak up and challenge inefficiencies.
  • Highly structured and organized, with a proactive problem-solving mindset.
  • Takes ownership of outcomes and drives process improvements.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience working in a startup or early-stage company .
  • Familiarity with AI tools to enhance productivity and workflows.
  • Prior experience managing product or operational lifecycles .

Compensation & Benefits

  • Holiday and PTO (offered at the company’s discretion).
  • Monthly health stipend to assist with healthcare costs.
  • Access to a perks program provided by the company.

Why This Role is Exciting

This is a high-impact position within a growing, entrepreneurial business where you will have direct influence on operational efficiency and product success . You will work closely with the founder, implement meaningful process improvements, and contribute to scaling the business while maintaining high standards of quality and innovation.
