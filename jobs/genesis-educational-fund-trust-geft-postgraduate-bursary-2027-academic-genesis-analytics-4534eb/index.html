About The Bursary

Location

: South Africa (Recognised South African Universities)

Fields of Study

Economics (and related fields) & Public Health

Academic Level

Postgraduate only (Honours or Master’s Degree)

*Note: GEFT does not provide funding for undergraduate degrees

The Genesis Educational Fund Trust bursary is the educational arm of Genesis Analytics - a leading impact and strategy consultancy. The principal objective of GEFT is to support initiatives that promote the educational and professional development of young people from previously disadvantaged backgrounds, as described in the Employment Equity Act 55 of 1998, primarily in the fields of economics (and related disciplines) and public health. The GEFT bursary is offered to eligible young South African students to study towards their Honours or Master’s degree in these overarching fields at a recognised South African university.

Eligibility Criteria

Citizenship

: Must be a South African Citizen

Background

: The bursary is earmarked for candidates who come from previously disadvantaged backgrounds as described by the Employment Equity Act 55 of 1998

Academic Performance

: A consistent academic average of 65% or higher across your undergraduate (and Honours, if applying for a Master's) studies.

What the Bursary Covers

The bursary primarily covers tuition fees, books, and required stationery. On a case-by-case basis, and depending on a number of factors, GEFT may also provide support for additional needs such as accommodation, living allowances, and transport.

Key Dates and Timelines

Applications Open3 August 2026

Applications Close21 August 2026

Shortlisted Candidateswill be contacted by 26 August 2026

Bursary Awarded For: Successful candidates will be awarded bursaries to study approved degrees as mentioned above in the academic year of 2027.

To be considered, your application submission must include the following uploaded:

Comprehensive CV

Copy of South African ID

Motivational Letter (1 page): Detailing your goals and the specific Genesis practice area(s) you are interested in (explore the "What We Do" section on our website to learn more).

Full Academic Transcripts: Consolidated into a single PDF document covering all undergraduate (and postgraduate, if you will be applying for a Master's degree) results to date.

Writing Sample: A recent academic submission (assignment, essay, or research proposal) from the current academic year, solely authored by you.

How to Apply

Apply Online Only: Applications must be submitted directly through the Career Portal on the Genesis Analytics website.

No Email Applications: To ensure data privacy compliance and efficient processing, emailed CVs or applications will not be accepted.

Multiple Roles: Your application will be evaluated specifically for this bursary programme. If you wish to apply for other opportunities at Genesis Analytics, please submit separate applications through those specific job listings.

Confirmation: Upon successful submission via the portal, you will receive an automated email confirmation.
