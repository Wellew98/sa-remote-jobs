We're looking for a

Forward Deployed Engineer to work directly with customers of a fast-growing AI app builder for business users, helping them get unstuck, fix their apps, and build with confidence. The platform lets business owners describe the software they need in plain language and get a working product. Those customers are not technical, so when the platform's AI agent falls short, they need an engineer who can step in and finish the job.

Please read this part carefully: this is a customer-facing technical support role. It is not a feature development role, and you will not be building the product roadmap. You will spend your days working directly with customers, debugging their apps, advising on architecture and integrations, and explaining the answer in plain language. If you are a software developer who enjoys working directly with customers and solving their real problems, and you are comfortable with this being a support role, this role is a strong fit.

What You'll Work On

Customer Problem Solving

Work directly with customers to resolve technical issues in their apps, including bugs, authentication, databases, payments, and integrations

Debug production issues using logs, traces, repros, and minimal test cases

Advise customers on architecture and next steps, and help them choose the right approach

Explain problems and fixes in clear, simple language that a non-technical business owner can follow

Feeding Back to the Product

Spot patterns across customer issues and share them with the engineering team so root causes get fixed

Write clear bug reports and reproductions for the product team

Help keep documentation and known-issue notes accurate and current

Support Workflow

Work through a steady queue of customer issues and keep every case moving until it is resolved

Build small tools and automations to speed up your own workflow

Escalate complex issues to the right engineer with a clear summary

Requirements

What You Bring

Strong TypeScript and React skills

Experience working directly with clients, ideally US clients, including communicating technical issues clearly

Solid web app fundamentals, including HTTP, APIs, authentication, and databases

Ability to read, debug, and fix production code and reason about how a system is failing

Excellent written and spoken English

Patience and empathy when working with non-technical customers

Comfort with this being a support role, and a genuine interest in solving customer problems directly

Available to work full-time on US Pacific Time (PST) business hours

Nice to Have

Prior experience in technical support, customer engineering, or solutions engineering

Experience with Postgres, Redis, Hono, Drizzle ORM, or Cloudflare and AWS

Experience building with AI SDKs and LLM-based applications

Experience building automations or internal tools to scale support

Experience working in a high-volume customer queue

If you are an engineer who likes solving real problems for real customers and wants to be the person who gets them unstuck, this role gives you the variety and the direct impact to do your best work.
