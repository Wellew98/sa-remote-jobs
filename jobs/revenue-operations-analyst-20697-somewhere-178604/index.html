About the Company

A fast-growing advisory group supporting biotech and pharmaceutical companies in finance, HR, operations, and strategy. The company operates multiple entities and continues to expand through acquisitions. They value curiosity, humility, strong execution, and continuous improvement.

Role Overview

The Revenue Operations Analyst plays a critical role in supporting the systems,

processes, and reporting that enable the commercial organization to operate efficiently

and scale effectively. This individual will serve as a Salesforce administrator, support

commission administration and deal operations, and provide reporting and analytics that

drive informed business decisions.

Working cross-functionally with Sales, Finance, and Business Operations, the Revenue

Operations Analyst will help optimize revenue-generating processes, ensure data

integrity, and improve operational efficiency across the customer lifecycle.

Key Responsibilities

  • Serve as a primary administrator for Salesforce, supporting day-to-day platform management and user needs.
  • Lead new user onboarding, role provisioning, and end-user training.
  • Configure and maintain operational workflows, including opportunity management, commercial contracting, and client staffing processes.
  • Monitor and maintain CRM data quality through ongoing data governance, validation, and hygiene initiatives.
  • Support and troubleshoot key system integrations to ensure accurate and reliable data across commercial platforms.
  • Partner with business stakeholders to identify opportunities for process improvements and Salesforce enhancements.
  • Administer commission-related processes, including tracking client referrals and commission eligibility.
  • Serve as the primary point of contact for commission-related inquiries, issue resolution, and escalations.
  • Partner with the Finance team to support commission calculations, validation, and reporting.
  • Assist in maintaining commission policies, documentation, and supporting processes.
  • Develop, maintain, and enhance pipeline, bookings, and sales performance reporting.
  • Produce recurring management reports and dashboards that provide visibility into commercial performance and key operational metrics.
  • Analyze sales pipeline trends and business performance to identify opportunities for process improvements and operational efficiencies.
  • Support ad hoc reporting and analysis for Sales Leadership, Finance, and Executive Management.

Key Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Business, Finance, Information Systems, or a related field.
  • 2–5 years of experience in Revenue Operations, Sales Operations, Business Operations, Financial Operations, or a related analytical role.
  • Experience administering Salesforce CRM required.
  • Strong analytical skills with proficiency in Excel; experience with reporting and BI tools such as Power BI or Tableau is a plus.
  • Excellent organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills with the ability to collaborate across departments.
  • Detail-oriented with a passion for improving processes and driving operational excellence.
