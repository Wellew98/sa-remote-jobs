Senior Manager – US Corporate Tax (CPA) Location: Remote (International) Hours: Full-Time with overlap during US business hours Employment Type: Full-Time, Independent Contractor About the Company Our client is a specialized financial services firm serving the rapidly growing climate technology sector. They provide non-dilutive financing and strategic financial advisory services to venture-backed startups and small-to-medium businesses, helping innovative companies scale while supporting sustainable economic growth. Their mission is to partner with founders building the future by delivering high-quality financial expertise and long-term strategic support. About the Role We are seeking an experienced US Certified Public Accountant (CPA) with an active license to join our team as a Senior Manager – US Corporate Tax . This is a senior, client-facing role responsible for managing complex US corporate tax engagements, ensuring compliance, advising on tax planning strategies, and providing technical leadership across a portfolio of US-based clients. The ideal candidate has 5–10+ years of experience in US corporate taxation, thrives in a fast-paced remote environment, and enjoys partnering with growing businesses to solve complex tax and accounting challenges. Key Responsibilities Corporate Tax Compliance - Manage and review federal and state corporate income tax returns, including Forms 1120, 1120S, 1065, and related filings. - Oversee corporate tax compliance processes while ensuring timely and accurate submissions. - Ensure compliance with IRS and state tax regulations. Tax Advisory & Planning - Advise clients on US corporate tax matters, including tax planning, entity structuring, and regulatory compliance. - Research and interpret changes in tax legislation and communicate impacts to clients and internal stakeholders. - Provide guidance on R&D tax credits and other available tax incentives. Financial Reporting - Review financial statements to assess tax implications and ensure reconciliation between accounting and tax records. - Support ASC 740 tax provision calculations and related documentation where required. - Maintain strong documentation standards and internal controls. Client & Project Management - Serve as the primary point of contact for assigned tax engagements. - Proactively identify, escalate, and resolve project issues while maintaining clear communication with clients and internal teams. - Manage multiple deadlines across concurrent engagements while ensuring exceptional client service. Leadership - Provide technical guidance and mentorship to junior tax professionals. - Contribute to process improvements and best practices across the tax function. What We're Looking For Required Qualifications - Active and unrestricted US CPA license (required). - California CPA or California state tax filing experience is highly preferred. - 5–10+ years of experience in US Corporate Tax. - Experience working with US-based companies or public accounting firms. - Strong knowledge of US federal and state corporate tax regulations. - Experience with corporate tax compliance, tax planning, and tax research. - Strong understanding of US GAAP (knowledge of Canadian or European GAAP is an advantage). - Experience with accounting platforms such as QuickBooks, Sage, Xero, Google Sheets, or similar systems. - Exceptional analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills. - Experience reviewing complex tax returns and managing multiple client engagements. - Excellent written and verbal English communication skills. - Ability to work remotely with regular overlap during US business hours. Preferred Qualifications - Experience supporting venture-backed or mid-market US companies. - Public accounting or Big Four experience. - Familiarity with tax software such as Intuit ProConnect Tax, CCH Axcess Tax, or Thomson Reuters UltraTax CS. - Experience working with clients in climate technology, sustainability, renewable energy, or related industries.
