PositionHead of Customer Delight

Work Hours (Client) : 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM EST

Location of SearchSouth Africa / Latin America

Work LocationREMOTE

Type of ContractIndependent Contractor

Company/Client Overview

Client is a brand platform focused on acquiring, scaling, and revitalizing high-potential consumer brands in the U.S. market. The organization operates with a hands-on, operationally driven approach, aiming to build a diversified portfolio of performance-focused direct-to-consumer brands.

The team values ownership, attention to detail, and long-term brand building. Customer experience is viewed as a core driver of brand loyalty and sustainable growth.

Duties and Responsibilities

Day-to-Day Support

  • Handle customer inquiries across email, SMS, and phone channels
  • Serve as the escalation point for complex or high-sensitivity cases
  • Maintain high standards for response quality, tone, and resolution

Team Management

  • Manage and develop a team of Customer Experience (CX) agents across multiple brands
  • Set clear performance expectations and provide continuous coaching
  • Conduct regular quality assurance reviews with structured, actionable feedback
  • Oversee scheduling, coverage, and capacity planning

Operations and Performance

  • Own and monitor key CX metrics (response time, resolution rate, CSAT, etc.)
  • Build and optimize macros, scripts, and support processes
  • Identify recurring customer issues and coordinate with Brand Managers on root cause resolution
  • Evaluate and implement tools, including AI-assisted support solutions, to improve efficiency and quality

Cross-Brand Collaboration

  • Partner with Brand Managers to align on brand voice, product updates, and customer sentiment
  • Translate customer insights into actionable business improvements
  • Represent the voice of the customer in operational discussions

Minimum Requirements

Language RequirementExcellent English communication skills (5/5), written and verbal, including phone support

EducationBachelor’s Degree (ideally)

Who You Are

You started as a CX agent. You know what it feels like to be in the queue, to handle a frustrated

customer, to find the right words when someone is upset. That experience is what makes you

good at leading a team, because you've been there.

You're empathetic by nature, but you're also analytical. You track metrics, spot patterns, and

translate what you're seeing into improvements. You don't accept "good enough" when it comes

to how customers are treated -- and you hold your team to the same standard.

You're a clear communicator in English, comfortable on the phone, and confident leading a

remote team. You take ownership seriously. If something isn't working, you fix it.

Experience

  • 3+ years of related experience in Customer Experience / Customer Support
  • Proven experience as a CX agent and later as a CX team lead or manager
  • Demonstrated success in tracking and improving CX performance metrics
  • Experience implementing quality assurance processes
  • Familiarity with Gorgias or similar helpdesk platforms
  • Experience with AI-assisted support tools
  • Experience managing remote teams

IndustryDirect-to-Consumer (DTC), eCommerce, Consumer Brands
