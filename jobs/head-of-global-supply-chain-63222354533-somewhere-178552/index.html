About the Company

Our client is a rapidly growing global sourcing and supply chain organization that partners with food manufacturers to solve complex procurement, sourcing, and logistics challenges. Working with suppliers across more than 15 countries, the company supports fast-growing brands through a high-touch, relationship-driven approach to global supply chain management.

As the business continues to scale, they are seeking a  Head of Global Supply Chain  to lead and evolve their global procurement, planning, and logistics operations while building the systems, processes, and team needed to support long-term growth.

Role Overview

The Head of Global Supply Chain will own the end-to-end global supply chain strategy, overseeing procurement, international logistics, integrated planning, supplier management, and cross-functional operational alignment.

This is both a strategic leadership and hands-on execution role. The successful candidate will build scalable systems, mentor a growing remote team, and collaborate closely with leadership across sales, finance, and operations to ensure the supply chain supports the company's continued growth.

The ideal candidate has extensive experience leading global supply chain functions within fast-paced, entrepreneurial organizations and enjoys building processes that improve efficiency, visibility, and operational performance.

Key Responsibilities

Sales & Operations Planning (S&OP)

- Lead the company's Sales & Operations Planning (S&OP) process

- Drive alignment across sales, finance, procurement, and operations

- Oversee demand planning, supply planning, forecasting, and budgeting activities

- Develop scenario planning models to support business decisions

- Create dashboards and reporting tools that improve planning accuracy and decision-making

- Continuously improve forecasting processes and operational visibility

Procurement Leadership

- Oversee daily procurement operations across a global supplier network

- Ensure purchase orders are executed accurately and on schedule

- Build and maintain strong supplier relationships

- Establish supplier performance metrics and review processes

- Develop communication cadences and issue resolution procedures for suppliers

- Partner with commercial teams to anticipate demand and align procurement strategies

- Support customer success by ensuring reliable product availability

Global Logistics Strategy

- Lead international logistics operations from purchase order through final delivery

- Oversee container management, freight coordination, customs, warehousing, and final-mile delivery

- Build and maintain relationships with freight forwarders, logistics providers, and warehouse partners

- Develop standard operating procedures for logistics exceptions and escalation processes

- Improve visibility into shipments, inventory movement, and logistics costs

- Design systems that support efficient transportation planning and cost control

- Identify opportunities to improve delivery performance and reduce operational risk

Supply Chain Strategy & Process Improvement

- Design scalable supply chain processes that support continued business growth

- Evaluate existing workflows and identify opportunities for operational improvement

- Build systems that improve efficiency, visibility, and cross-functional collaboration

- Establish best practices across procurement, planning, and logistics

- Develop long-term supply chain strategies aligned with company growth objectives

- Drive continuous improvement initiatives throughout the organization

Technology & Systems

- Evaluate and recommend improvements to the company's supply chain technology stack

- Support implementation and optimization of ERP, demand planning, transportation management, and related systems

- Improve reporting capabilities through automation and data visibility

- Develop dashboards and KPIs that support operational decision-making

- Ensure systems support scalability and long-term operational excellence

Leadership & Team Development

- Build, mentor, and develop a high-performing remote supply chain team

- Establish clear performance expectations and accountability

- Define KPIs across procurement, logistics, planning, and supplier management

- Coach team members while fostering a culture of ownership and continuous improvement

- Support hiring and organizational growth as supply chain operations expand

- Promote collaboration across departments and geographic regions

Cross-Functional Leadership

- Partner closely with sales, finance, sourcing, and executive leadership

- Participate in strategic planning discussions related to growth and operational risk

- Align supply chain priorities with commercial objectives

- Communicate operational risks and opportunities proactively

- Serve as a key advisor on supply chain strategy and operational excellence
