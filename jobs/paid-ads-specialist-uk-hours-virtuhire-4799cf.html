Paid Ads Specialist | UK hours

VirtuHire LOCATION: , South Africa (Remote) Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

<p>Our client in the UK is looking for a Paid Media Specialist to manage campaigns across Google, Meta, TikTok, and Microsoft (Bing) Ads for their client portfolio, reporting to the Director of Operations. <br><br>You'll take new clients from first access through to a fully live, tracked, reporting-ready account, then keep those accounts healthy, accountable to agreed KPIs, and report on every week and month. This is an ownership role suited to someone with hands-on paid media experience who wants real accountability for client budgets and results. <br><br>Onboarding follows the agency's Paid Media Specialist SOP, which sets out the non-negotiable standards for setup, tracking, and ongoing account management.</p><p></p><h3><strong>Key responsibilities</strong></h3><p><strong>New client setup </strong></p><ul><li>Secure and document correct access under the client's own business accounts — never build on a personal profile or ad account. </li><li>Establish the website access level (full / partial / none) and either install tracking directly or write a clear tracking spec and hand it to the Project Lead for implementation.</li><li>Install and live-verify conversion tracking across platforms — pixels, server-side events (CAPI, TikTok Events API, enhanced conversions), deduplication, and consent/privacy configuration. </li><li>Run discovery with the client: agree KPIs, unit economics (margins, AOV, LTV), audience definitions, USP and competitor set, and budget split before building a single campaign. </li><li>Build accounts with clear, documented structure, consistent naming conventions, platform-native creative, and a full pre-launch QA pass before going live. </li></ul><p></p><p><strong>Ongoing campaign management </strong></p><ul><li>Carry out daily checks on spend and pacing, disapprovals or billing issues, and tracking integrity — treating any unexplained conversion drop as a tracking failure until proven otherwise. </li><li>Review performance weekly against agreed KPIs, manage creative fatigue and search terms, and make considered, logged optimisations within the agreed strategy and budget. </li><li>Apply core optimisation discipline: respect each platform's learning phase, reach statistical significance before killing or scaling, scale budgets gradually, and cut underperformers on data, not emotion. </li><li>Escalate material changes — budget shifts, pausing/scaling, strategy changes — to the Director of Operations and MD/Founder for sign-off before acting, and document decisions in email. </li></ul><p></p><p><strong>Reporting &amp; communication </strong></p><ul><li>Produce the monthly performance report against the Monthly Report Standard, ready at least 2 working days before each client's end-of-month meeting. </li><li>Bring numbers and recommendations to the weekly internal catch-up with the Director of Operations and MD/Founder, flagging risks before the client ever has to. </li><li>Maintain a running change log for every account, and produce a handover document ahead of any planned leave.</li></ul><p></p><p><strong>Performance and targets</strong></p><p>Targets are agreed per client (typically CPA, ROAS, or CPL) and reviewed monthly with the Director of Operations. A realistic ramp-up period is agreed before launch, in line with each platform's learning phase, so performance is judged on statistically meaningful data rather than week-two noise.</p><ul><li>Full launch checklist complete (access, tracking verified end-to-end, KPIs agreed in writing) before any client spend goes live.</li><li>Accounts trending toward agreed KPIs (CPA/ROAS/CPL) once each platform has exited its learning phase.</li><li>Monthly client reports delivered on time, every time, at least 2 working days ahead of the client's EOM meeting.</li><li>Change log and reporting cadence consistently maintained across the account portfolio.</li></ul><p></p><p><strong>Progression</strong></p><p>Paid Media Specialists can progress to Senior Paid Media Specialist and, in time, Paid Media Lead — taking on larger or more complex accounts, mentoring junior specialists, and expanding ownership across TikTok, Bing, and platform partner relationships. </p>
