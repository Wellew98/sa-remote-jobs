Account Executive Job Description Join Our Client’s Team! Our client is an industry-leading web design & development agency based out of Phoenix, AZ. They transform digital presence by forging cutting-edge, custom web solutions that are firmly rooted in user experience and extensive data. You’ll have the tools to continue building your sales career by solving complex and thought-provoking business problems for great brands and good people you’ll be proud to help succeed beyond their business objectives. A few brands supported through award-winning solutions include: Google, Voss Water, General Electric, Arizona State University and many others! If you have a passion for digital media and technology, then join a growing team of talented individuals! Position Objective: The Account Executive is a bonafide hunter and closer responsible for driving the sales pipeline and crushing quotas. Utilizing excellent communication and tenacity, the AE will qualify leads, drive a consultative sales process, and ultimately negotiate and close complex deals at an executive level. Essential Functions and Responsibilities: - Hunter mentality to negotiate complex deals as a direct seller - Meet or exceed sales and profitability quotas on a quarterly and annual basis - Maintain accurate sales forecasting, thoughtful engagement of customers/prospects, and resource utilization against any leads - Participate in/advice on new go-to-market solutions and messaging - Craft and deliver account plans for prospective clients through telephone, email, video meetings, and all other available methods - Work with relevant Directors to develop client offers, proposals, and presentations — ultimately setting relationships and engagements up for long-term success - Manage the overall relationship and communication with key client(s) and have full command of their business model, web development needs, and how our client may deliver value - Develop and manage key selling relationships to drive new business and ensure proper communication on account delivery - Conduct outbound prospecting to net new target accounts - Expertly drive a complex, consultative sales process from beginning to end - Build rapport and uncover true pain points to align services for client success - Contribute to compelling propositions and the vision for each opportunity - Create and execute a strategic sales plan, identifying key stakeholders, need development, and strategic approach - Eagerness to learn, coach, and be coached within an AE team - Experience selling to Marketing Departments, Directors, VPs, and C-Level executives - Confidently and tactfully challenge a prospect’s way of thinking to build value - Experience selling technology solutions: web, SaaS, eCommerce, IT, cloud services, etc. - Thrive in a remote environment - Educate, influence, and deliver compelling sales presentations/demos - Prepare and submit activity reports, pipeline forecasts, and conversion rates Need to Have: - Hunter & closer mentality - Successful track record of achieving and exceeding revenue objectives in complex solution environments - Be a top sales executive within current/previous teams - 5+ years owning a consultative sales process - 3+ years closing complex negotiations - Experience crafting and executing plans to close small to mid-size web development projects - Success selling complex, multi-faceted web development projects into matrixed organizations - Knowledge of/experience with structured sales methodologies - Deep knowledge of digital development/website landscapes - Strong verbal, written communication, and listening skills - Strong customer service and interpersonal skills -
