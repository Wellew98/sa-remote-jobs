ISTA Personnel Solutions South Africa is a dynamic and fast-growing BPO company based in South Africa, specializing in providing top-tier operational support to U.S.-based companies. We are not a recruitment agency—we are a dedicated extension of our clients’ teams, delivering high-quality service with precision, efficiency, and a personal touch.

Our client, a growing ABA therapy provider, is looking for an engaging, organised, and people-oriented individual to join their team as an Interview & Onboarding Specialist.

This role is ideal for someone who enjoys working with people, conducting interviews, and helping new employees successfully transition into their roles. Previous recruitment or interviewing experience will be advantageous, but it is not essential. We are equally interested in candidates with strong communication skills, a confident and engaging personality, and the ability to connect with people.

PLEASE NOTE

Working hours

Sunday, Monday & Wednesday, 16:00–01:00 SAST; Tuesday & Thursday, 19:00–04:00 SAST (subject to US daylight saving changes).

Public Holidays

You will be required to work on both South African and U.S. public holidays (compensation for SA public holidays in accordance with the BCEA).

Internet Requirements

A fixed fibre line with a minimum speed of 25 Mbps (upload & download) and the ability to support a wired Ethernet connection is mandatory. Applicants without a fixed fibre line cannot be considered.

Power Backup

A reliable power backup solution is required to manage load shedding and power outages. Applicants without a power backup cannot be considered.

Work Environment

This is a

fully remote working role, and you must be based in South Africa.

Key Responsibilities

Interviewing & Candidate Selection

Conduct scheduled candidate interviews and engage with applicants in a professional and approachable manner.

Assess candidates’ communication skills, personalities, and overall suitability for available positions.

Make informed hiring decisions based on the client's requirements.

Ensure candidates have a positive and professional interview experience.

Communicate effectively with candidates throughout the interview process.

Onboarding & Administration

Coordinate the onboarding process for successful candidates.

Ensure new employees complete and submit the required employment documentation.

Assist with setting up new employees on CentralReach and other relevant client systems.

Maintain accurate candidate and employee records.

Follow up on outstanding documentation and onboarding requirements.

Ensure new employees are prepared for a smooth transition into their roles.

Communication & Team Collaboration

Work closely with the supervisor and the wider team to support recruitment and onboarding requirements.

Provide updates on interview outcomes and onboarding progress.

Manage candidate information and documentation confidentially.

Take ownership of assigned tasks and follow through to completion.

Tools & Systems

CentralReach

Microsoft Office

Outlook

Other internal and client-specific systems

Requirements

Previous recruitment, interviewing, or candidate selection experience will be advantageous.

Excellent verbal communication skills and clear, confident spoken English.

An engaging, cheerful and approachable personality.

Comfortable interacting with different people and conducting interviews.

Ability to assess candidates and make sound hiring decisions.

Strong organisational and administrative skills.

Good attention to detail and the ability to manage multiple tasks.

Basic computer literacy and confidence navigating different systems.

Working knowledge of Microsoft Office and Outlook.

A team player who is easy to work with, proactive and able to work independently.

Previous experience in ABA, behavioural health, or healthcare will be advantageous.

If you do not hear from us within 14 working days of submitting your application, please consider your application unsuccessful.
