Our client in the UK is looking for a Project Manager to own the delivery of their clients' marketing projects, making sure each one lands on time, on brief and with no surprises. You are the link between clients, the delivery teams and the Operations Director: planning the work, orchestrating the people, communicating clearly and holding everyone, including yourself, to account.

Key Responsibilities

Project delivery

Own the end-to-end delivery of multiple concurrent marketing projects, from kick-off through to sign-off and review.

Turn client briefs and agreed scopes into clear plans with tasks, owners, dependencies, milestones and deadlines.

Deliver to strict and tight deadlines by tracking progress daily and spotting risks before they become delays.

Protect scope: flag out-of-scope requests early and agree changes with the account lead and Operations Director.

Client communication

Act as the main point of contact for project progress, with regular, proactive and clear updates.

Set and manage client expectations, and raise problems early, alongside a proposed solution.

Prepare for and attend client meetings, capturing actions and following them through.

Orchestrating teams

Brief and coordinate the right people for each project across strategy, creative, paid media, SEO, influencer, social and web development.

Balance workload across a team of 11–12, flagging capacity clashes to the Operations Director in good time.

Run regular check-ins so every team member knows what is due, when, and what depends on them.

Accountability and performance

Hold team members to their deadlines and quality standards in a direct, constructive and respectful way, and hold yourself to the same standard.

Escalate blockers and risks to the Operations Director promptly, with options and a recommendation.

Report on project health, on-time delivery and workload each week.

Systems and process

Own the project management tool (such as Asana), keeping it structured, current and the single source of truth.

Build and improve project templates, workflows and checklists so delivery becomes more consistent and efficient.

Essential

Proven experience managing multiple concurrent marketing projects in an agency or similar environment (approximately 5 years).

Broad, all-round knowledge of digital marketing services, including social media, paid advertising, SEO, influencer marketing, content and web, enough to understand each element of a project and set realistic timelines.

Strong command of project management tools such as Asana, Monday.com, ClickUp or Trello.

A track record of delivering to tight deadlines under pressure.

Excellent written and verbal communication, with confidence managing clients at all levels.

Strong prioritisation, planning and resource management skills.

Experience coordinating cross-functional teams, including remote or home-based colleagues, without relying on seniority to get things done.

Desirable

Experience in a small to mid-sized agency of a similar scale.

Experience with time tracking, budgets and resource forecasting.

Familiarity with Google Workspace.

Who we are looking for

Beyond experience, these behaviours matter most

Accountable

: you hold the team to account, and yourself first. You own your commitments and say so early when something is at risk.

Proactive

: you show initiative, spot what needs doing and act without waiting to be asked.

Prioritising

: you separate what is urgent from what is important, and reset priorities when they change.

Constructive

: you identify challenges early and bring practical solutions, not just problems.

Resilient

: you stay calm, clear and decisive under pressure and tight deadlines.

Influential

: you get results through clear communication and credibility, bringing people with you.

What success looks like

Projects are delivered on time and on brief.

Clients hear from us proactively and are never surprised by a delay.

Every team member knows their priorities and deadlines.

The project management tool is accurate and up to date, and the Operations Director has a clear view of delivery at any time.

What we offer

A full-time, home-based role in a friendly, growing full-service agency.

Holiday entitlement in line with your contract.

The chance to shape how projects are planned and delivered across the agency.
