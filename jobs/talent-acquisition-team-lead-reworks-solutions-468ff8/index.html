About the Company

Our client is dedicated to providing quality ABA therapy services and supporting individuals and families through compassionate, effective behavioural healthcare. The organisation relies on a strong and effective recruitment function to ensure the right talent is placed in the right roles to support its growing operations.

Key Responsibilities

1. Recruitment Operations & Delivery

Oversee daily recruitment activities and ensure vacancies are progressing efficiently through the hiring pipeline.

Manage multiple open vacancies and competing recruitment priorities.

Monitor recruitment targets, hiring progress, and team productivity.

Ensure the team consistently delivers quality candidates within agreed timelines.

Step in and support hands-on recruitment activities when needed.

Identify recruitment bottlenecks and implement solutions to improve hiring outcomes.

2. Team Leadership & Development

Lead, support, and develop the recruitment team.

Allocate workloads and ensure recruitment responsibilities are distributed effectively.

Conduct regular performance check-ins and provide ongoing coaching and feedback.

Monitor individual and team performance against recruitment targets.

Identify training needs and support continuous team development.

Foster a positive, accountable, and collaborative team environment.

3. HR & Employee Relations

Provide HR support and guidance to the team and wider organisation.

Assist with employee relations matters and performance management.

Support the consistent application of company policies and procedures.

Address performance concerns and workplace challenges professionally.

Promote accountability, employee engagement, and positive working relationships.

4. Reporting & Performance Management

Track recruitment KPIs, hiring targets, and team performance metrics.

Use recruitment data to identify trends, challenges, and opportunities for improvement.

Prepare and maintain recruitment reports and performance updates.

Ensure accurate ATS and CRM records and consistent pipeline management.

Implement strategies to improve recruitment efficiency and overall team output.

5. Stakeholder Management

Work closely with internal stakeholders and hiring managers to understand staffing requirements.

Communicate recruitment progress, challenges, and hiring needs.

Ensure recruitment priorities remain aligned with operational requirements.

Build strong working relationships with internal teams and US-based stakeholders.

Required Experience & Skills

Proven experience leading or supervising a recruitment, talent acquisition, or HR team.

Strong hands-on recruitment experience, including managing multiple vacancies simultaneously.

Solid HR background, with experience in employee relations, performance management, and policy implementation.

Experience managing recruitment targets and using data to drive team performance.

Strong leadership, communication, organisation, and problem-solving skills.

Excellent stakeholder management and the ability to manage competing priorities.

Confident using Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), CRM platforms, and reporting tools.

Ability to motivate, coach, and hold a team accountable for performance.

Preferred Experience

Recruitment experience within ABA therapy, healthcare, behavioural health, or another high-volume staffing environment.

Familiarity with recruiting Behaviour Technicians (BTs), Registered Behaviour Technicians (RBTs), and Board Certified Behaviour Analysts (BCBAs).

Previous experience leading and managing a remote recruitment team.

Experience supporting US-based recruitment operations and working with US stakeholders.

Ideal Candidate Profile

We are looking for someone who is

A natural leader who enjoys developing and supporting people.

Hands-on and comfortable getting involved in recruitment rather than purely overseeing operations.

Target-driven, organised, and results-oriented.

Confident managing people, performance, and operational challenges.

Proactive in identifying inefficiencies and improving processes.

Able to balance empathy and support with accountability.

Comfortable working in a fast-paced, remote environment.
