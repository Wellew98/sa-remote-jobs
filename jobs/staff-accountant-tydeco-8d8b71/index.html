Staff Accountant

TydeCo This position can be remote within Cape Town, with the ability to work from our TydeCo office as required. Alternatively, it can be based remotely in the Gauteng province, with the flexibility to travel to the Cape Town office as required. Full-time Finance & Accounting Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

We are seeking a detail-oriented and highly motivated Staff Accountant who thrives in a fast-paced, client-driven environment. This individual must have a strong ability to manage daily accounting functions, maintain financial accuracy, and support client operations with precision and efficiency.  The Staff Accountant will play a critical role in executing accounting tasks, preparing financial statements, and ensuring seamless financial operations for multiple clients. This role is integral to the Client Accounting & Advisory Services (CAAS) team, working closely with internal teams to streamline processes, ensure compliance, and enhance financial reporting.  Additionally, this individual will leverage cloud-based accounting tools such as Sage Intacct, FloQast, QuickBooks Online, and Xero to automate workflows, optimize efficiencies, and enhance financial close processes. The ability to work with reconciliation automation, workflow approvals, and real-time financial reporting tools will be key to ensuring accuracy and timeliness in financial operations.  If you are a proactive accounting professional who thrives in a collaborative environment and enjoys integrating financial processes with broader business strategies, we invite you to apply.  As a platinum Sage partner, TydeCo and our partner entity TydeCo U specialize in delivering cutting-edge ERP and HRP solutions such as Sage 300 Cloud and Sage Intacct. Our dedication to innovation, excellence, and client success ensures businesses not only adopt our solutions but thrive in competitive markets. If you're ready to lead in a fast-paced, high-impact environment, we invite you to be part of our journey.  This position will report to the Team Manager(s). This position can be remote within Cape Town, with the ability to work from our TydeCo office as required. Alternatively, it can be based remotely in the Gauteng province, with the flexibility to travel to the Cape Town office as required.    Key Responsibilities  Global Support   Operate within a global framework, ensuring adaptability to support teams, meet deadlines and participate in virtual meetings across multiple time zones based in Canada, South Africa, U.K. or the U.S., as needed.  Collaborate with international stakeholders, adjusting to varying schedules and priorities as needed.  Facilitate seamless communication and coordination across CAAS teams to support sales operational efficiency.  Maintain flexibility in scheduling to accommodate time-sensitive tasks and cross-regional collaboration.  Accounting Operations & Financial Reporting  Prepare and maintain accurate financial statements, account reconciliations, and management reports.  Process accounts payable, accounts receivable, and bank reconciliations with a focus on automation and efficiency.  Maintain compliance with GAAP and other regulatory standards while ensuring the integrity of financial records.  Utilize FloQast for financial close management, workflow tracking, and reconciliation processes.  Client Support & Advisory Services  Work closely with clients to support their day-to-day accounting needs and provide timely financial insights.  Assist in onboarding new clients, setting up Sage Intacct, and implementing financial workflows.  Respond promptly to client requests, ensuring exceptional service and relationship management.  Client Engagement & Project Contribution Maintain minimum billable hours as defined by leadership. Provide strategic recommendations to improve clients’ HR and payroll efficiency. Collaborate with sales/account management teams to align deliverables with scope and client expectations. Identify service expansion opportunities and areas of client value. Technology & Process Improvement  Leverage Sage Intacct and QuickBooks Online as primary accounting platforms for financial reporting, automation, and process optimization.  Implement best practices for financial operations, including automation, process standardization, and internal controls to enhance accuracy and efficiency.  Support financial technology integration projects, ensuring seamless adoption of new tools and systems for accounting, payroll, and reporting functions.  Assist in payroll processing and compliance, working with payroll systems to ensure accurate wage calculations, tax filings, and reporting.  Optimize reconciliation and close processes using FloQast, QuickBooks Online, and Sage Intacct, improving efficiency, accuracy, and timeliness.  Internal Collaboration & Process Alignment  Participate in global accounting meetings, ensuring consistency and compliance across financial processes.  Work closely with operations and finance teams to align accounting practices with business needs.  Assist in the preparation of audit schedules, tax filings, and financial compliance documentation.  Provide support to leadership by preparing financial data, reports, and insights for internal decision-making.
