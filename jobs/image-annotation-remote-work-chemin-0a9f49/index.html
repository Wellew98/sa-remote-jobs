About Kaya

Kaya by Chemin Sdn Bhd is a community of AI contributors supporting the development of next-generation AI through high-quality data collection and annotation projects.

About the Role

We're looking for contributors to join a Floor Plan Annotation Project.

You'll help annotate architectural floor plans by identifying and drawing polygons around elements such as walls, doors, windows, rooms, and stairs.

What You'll Do

Annotate floor plans using polygon segmentation to identify and label key architectural elements while following provided annotation guidelines, maintaining a target accuracy of 95%
