Position Type

Full-Time | Remote

Working Hours

About the Role

We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer with strong full-stack development experience to build, improve, and maintain reliable software products.

The ideal candidate should be comfortable working across the frontend and backend, developing APIs, working with databases, and supporting cloud-based applications. You will work closely with engineering and cross-functional teams to build scalable, high-quality solutions and improve existing systems.

Responsibilities

Develop and maintain scalable full-stack applications across frontend and backend.

Build user-facing features using

React, JavaScript, and TypeScript .

Develop and integrate

REST APIs and GraphQL using modern backend technologies.

Work with databases and cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud .

Build and maintain

Docker-based applications and CI/CD pipelines .

Improve application

performance, scalability, security, and reliability .

Write clean, maintainable, and well-tested code while participating in code reviews.

Troubleshoot technical issues and identify effective solutions.

Collaborate with

product, design, QA, and engineering teams to deliver high-quality solutions.

Contribute to system architecture, technical decisions, and process improvements.

Mentor junior developers and support technical knowledge sharing when needed.

Required Experience & Skills

5+ years of professional software engineering experience.

Strong experience with

React, JavaScript, and TypeScript .

Strong backend development experience with

Node.js, Python, Java, .NET, or similar technologies .

Experience developing and integrating

REST APIs and/or GraphQL .

Experience working with relational and/or NoSQL databases.

Experience building and supporting production software applications.

Experience with at least one major cloud platform

AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud .

Experience with

Docker and CI/CD processes.

Strong understanding of software development, debugging, testing, and code quality.

Experience working with Agile/Scrum or similar development processes.

Strong problem-solving and communication skills.

Ability to work independently in a remote environment.

Availability during

U.S. business hours .
