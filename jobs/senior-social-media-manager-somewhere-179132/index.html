Senior Social Media Manager

About the Company

Our client is a premier 10-year-old boutique digital marketing agency delivering high-touch social media management, brand strategy, content creation, and performance marketing for a diverse portfolio of growing brands. Known for exceptional creative execution and seamless client partnerships, the agency combines strategic storytelling with data-driven social campaigns to elevate brand visibility across digital channels.

To ensure uninterrupted service delivery and support agency business development initiatives, the company is seeking an experienced, autonomous Senior Social Media Manager to take complete ownership of social media operations across a portfolio of ~15 active accounts.

Role Overview

We are seeking a senior, self-motivated Senior Social Media Manager to take complete end-to-end ownership of client social accounts, content strategies, client communications, and social scheduling.

In this role, you will act as an indispensable operational lead, managing the day-to-day social media footprint for approximately 15 agency client accounts. You will develop monthly content calendars, write engaging copy, schedule posts across major social platforms, engage with brand communities, and communicate directly with client stakeholders. Additionally, you will utilize basic graphic design, motion/animation (e.g., animating layered PSD files), and video editing capabilities to produce polished, high-performing visual assets.

The ideal candidate is a seasoned social media manager with 5–8+ years (or up to 10+ years) of agency experience, possessing flawless written English, high autonomy, and a track record of managing multi-account portfolios effortlessly.

Key Responsibilities

Multi-Account Social Media Strategy & Content Operations

  • Take complete ownership of content planning, strategy, and daily execution across ~15 assigned agency client accounts.
  • Develop monthly content calendars, creative campaigns, and platform-specific content strategies (Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, TikTok, X).
  • Draft compelling, brand-aligned ad copy, organic post captions, and promotional content tailored to varying target audiences.
  • Schedule and publish approved content across social platforms using agency scheduling tools, ensuring 100% posting accuracy.
  • Conduct active community management—monitoring incoming comments, DMs, and customer inquiries to foster engagement.

Client Relationship Management & Communication

  • Serve as the direct strategic point of contact for assigned client accounts, leading onboarding calls, status updates, and monthly strategy reviews.
  • Translate client goals and feedback into refined creative direction and high-converting social campaigns.
  • Monitor account health, de-escalate concerns proactively, and maintain high client satisfaction and retainer retention.
  • Prepare, format, and present monthly analytics reports, performance insights, and content optimization recommendations.

Visual Asset Creation & Basic Motion Editing

  • Design and format eye-catching social graphics, carousels, and visual promotional assets.
  • Utilize basic motion design skills to animate static assets (e.g., animating Photoshop/PSD files into dynamic video posts).
  • Execute basic video editing for short-form content (Reels, TikToks, Shorts), including trimming, captions, transitions, and audio syncing.
  • Partner with internal agency leads to align social media output with overarching CRM and lead-generation strategies.

Required Qualifications

  • 5–8+ years (or 10+ years) of professional experience managing multi-account social media portfolios, preferably within a digital marketing agency environment.
  • Proven ability to manage ~15 active social media client accounts simultaneously with high autonomy and organization.
  • Flawless written and spoken English communication skills with exceptional copywriting and editing abilities.
  • Proficiency with industry-standard social media management and scheduling platforms (e.g., Buffer, Hootsuite, Later, Sprout Social).
  • Hands-on graphic design skills with experience using Adobe Creative Cloud (Photoshop) or Canva.
  • Basic motion graphics and video editing capabilities (ability to animate layered PSDs and edit short-form video assets).
  • Self-starter mindset with the ability to step in immediately, learn brand guidelines quickly, and work without micromanagement.
  • Reliable high-speed internet connection, secure personal computer, and a dedicated, quiet remote workspace.
  • Ability to connect via a designated Canadian VPN location for operational consistency.
  • Availability to work full-time hours, Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–6:00 PM EST.

Preferred Qualifications

  • 10+ years of senior social media experience with advanced campaign strategy and brand positioning capabilities.
  • Advanced motion design skills (Adobe After Effects, Premiere Pro) for high-end video social content.
  • Practical experience coordinating social media strategies alongside email marketing, CRM lead generation, and paid media funnels.

Technology & Tools

Candidates should be proficient with or capable of quickly mastering:

  • Social Media Management Tools (Sprout Social, Later, Buffer, Hootsuite)
  • Graphic Design & Motion Tools (Adobe Photoshop, Canva, After Effects, Premiere Pro)
  • Social Analytics Platforms (Meta Business Suite, LinkedIn Analytics, Google Analytics)
  • Project Management Software (Asana, ClickUp, Slack)
  • Google Workspace & Canadian VPN Platforms

Role Details

  • Job Title: Senior Social Media Manager
  • Experience: 5–8+ years (10+ years preferred)
  • Employment Type: Full-Time
  • Schedule: Monday–Friday
  • Hours: 9:00 AM–6:00 PM EST
  • Work Arrangement: Fully Remote (Canadian VPN required)
  • Primary Focus: Complete ownership of ~15 social accounts, client communications, content scheduling, copy generation, graphic design, motion/PSD animation, and short-form video editing

What This Role Offers

  • Direct ownership over a core client portfolio within an established, 10-year-old boutique agency.
  • High autonomy, allowing the core leadership team to focus on agency growth while you run social operations.
  • A stable, long-term remote position working consistent U.S./Canadian Eastern Standard Time business hours.
  • Collaborative environment backed by creative brand strategies and dedicated leadership.

Ideal Candidate Profile

The ideal candidate is an articulate, highly autonomous senior social media manager who thrives when given total ownership of a multi-account portfolio.

They do not wait for step-by-step instructions—they review brand guidelines, build creative content calendars, animate PSDs into engaging social posts, and schedule content weeks ahead of schedule. They communicate with clients naturally and professionally over email and call syncs, turn analytics data into clear strategic direction, and take immense pride in keeping client accounts vibrant and growing.

Most importantly, they bring steady reliability, creative flair, and the senior-level confidence needed to step in immediately and keep client operations running seamlessly.

Success Metrics

Success in this role will be measured by

  • Seamless, zero-delay transition and maintenance of social management across all ~15 assigned client accounts.
  • Client satisfaction, trust, and account retention across assigned agency portfolios.
  • Quality, visual polish, and engagement performance of published social content, motion graphics, and video assets.
  • Accuracy and consistency of content scheduling, copywriting, and monthly performance reporting.
  • Proactive, clear communication with client stakeholders and agency leadership.
  • Efficiency in operating autonomously, freeing agency executives to focus on sales, CRMs, and business expansion.
