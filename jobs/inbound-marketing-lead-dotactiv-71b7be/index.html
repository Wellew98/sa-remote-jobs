Own and drive DotActiv’s inbound marketing methodology by combining AI-assisted content creation with internal domain knowledge. This role requires an execution-focused marketer who can manage our graphic designer, interview technical Subject Matter Experts (SMEs), and leverage modern AI tools to maintain a high-volume, high-quality inbound engine.

Who Is DotActiv?

At DotActiv, we pride ourselves on providing the global retail market with high-quality category management software and advisory services that improve the in-store shopping experience for everyone.

Our deeply integrated all-in-one category management software reduces the complexity of the interdependent nature of data-driven clustering, assortment, floor and shelf planning. Meanwhile, our talented people and highly refined processes enable us to deliver stress-free speed to impact with unrivalled convenience and efficiency.

With DotActiv, you get integrated all-in-one software that empowers you with unrivalled support, regular updates and proactive monthly guidance from our customer success team.

Key Responsibilities

Inbound & Content Execution

: Drive the inbound marketing engine by planning, producing, and distributing articles, case studies, emails, and lead magnets aligned with customer journeys.

AI-Powered Workflows

Actively use generative AI platforms (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) to draft, repurpose, and scale written content efficiently without sacrificing tone or technical accuracy.

SME Knowledge Extraction

Interview internal product specialists and industry experts to extract domain knowledge and convert it into high-value marketing assets.

Graphic Designer Management

Direct the workflow, deadlines, and output of the in-house Graphic Designer to ensure visual content supports copy and brand guidelines.

Funnel & Asset Optimisation

Monitor core inbound metrics (traffic, conversion rates, MQLs) using CRM/marketing platforms and iterate on underperforming assets.

Requirements & Qualifications

Experience: 2–4 years of hands-on experience in B2B content marketing or inbound marketing (SaaS/Software experience is a strong advantage).

AI Tool Literacy: Proven, daily proficiency using AI tools to accelerate research, copywriting, and content ideation.

Methodology: Solid understanding of inbound marketing fundamentals, lead generation funnels, and SEO basics.

Leadership & Project Management: Capability to coordinate visual creative work, manage content calendars, and keep production on schedule.

Communication: Exceptional written English skills with a sharp eye for editing and technical accuracy.
