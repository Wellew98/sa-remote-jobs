Company Description

Somewhere, formerly Support Shepherd, is a trusted provider of global talent acquisition solutions for top brands and companies in the United States. We place over 300 individuals with new opportunities every month.

Role Description

This is a full-time remote role for an E-commerce Manager at a United States based E-commerce company. The E-commerce Manager will be responsible for analyzing data, communicating with team members and clients, driving sales initiatives, managing e-commerce platforms, and overseeing marketing strategies to optimize online sales performance.

Qualifications

  • Analytical Skills: Ability to interpret data and make strategic decisions
  • Communication: Strong verbal and written communication skills
  • Sales and E-Commerce: Experience in driving sales and managing e-commerce platforms
  • Marketing: Knowledge of marketing strategies to enhance online sales performance
  • Experience in digital marketing and SEO
  • Strong organizational and project management skills
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a remote team
  • Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, or related field

Compensation

$1,750-$2,500 USD per month

Requirements

  • More than 3 years experience with Shopify or a similar platform
  • Inventory Management experience
  • Pricing strategy and growth initiatives
  • Fast, reliable internet
  • A second monitor and fast computer
  • A quiet and professional work environment
  • Great command of the english language (written and verbal)
  • Work full time on United States time zone

Additional Details

Full-time independent contractor paid 2x monthly. Client looking for long term employment. Role comes with upward mobility and future opportunity for advancement.
