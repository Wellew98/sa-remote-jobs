Graphic Designer and Visual Content Specialist

A fast-growing direct-to-consumer sports nutrition brand is hiring a remote Graphic Designer and Visual Content Specialist. You will own creative work across email, social, paid ads, ecommerce, and 3D product renders.

About the role

This is an all-in-one creative role for someone equally comfortable building a Klaviyo campaign, designing a Meta static ad, or producing a polished 3D product render. Product imagery is central to how the brand competes online. Its existing premium, performance-focused renders set the bar you will meet and raise. You will work within established brand templates while bringing your own ideas to every project and thinking about what makes an asset perform. The team is small and expects a lot of output, so you will use AI tools to work faster and take ownership of your deadlines.

Required Qualifications

  • Expert-level skills in Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, and InDesign, shown across many asset types in your portfolio
  • Design experience for a direct-to-consumer or ecommerce product brand, ideally in supplements, fitness, health, or lifestyle, with a clear sense of what drives sales
  • Proven experience building and loading email campaigns in Klaviyo
  • A strong portfolio of static social posts and paid ads for Meta, Instagram, and YouTube
  • Obsessive attention to alignment, spacing, color accuracy, and brand consistency
  • Already using AI tools in your design work, and genuinely keen to do more

Preferred Qualifications

  • Hands-on product rendering in Blender or similar 3D software, with polished, studio-quality work to show
  • Experience designing for a supplement, nutrition, or athletic performance brand
  • Print design experience, including postcards, direct mail, and print-ready file preparation
  • Basic motion graphics or video editing skills
  • Experience managing work in Trello or a similar project management tool

Responsibilities

  • Load email templates into Klaviyo accurately, with correct formatting and links on every device
  • Design on-brand email campaigns that drive sales for launches, promotions, retention, and subscriber updates, including all supporting graphics
  • Design a steady stream of static social posts for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube, plus YouTube thumbnails that earn clicks
  • Produce static ads for Meta and YouTube, with several versions for testing based on direction from the marketing team
  • Create premium product renders in Blender that match and build on the brand’s existing standard, and organize 3D files so they can be reused
  • Design postcards and printed materials to the same standard as digital work
  • Use AI design and production tools to work faster, keep up with new tools, and share useful shortcuts with the team
  • Manage all tasks in Trello, flag timeline risks early, and deliver on time, every time
  • Keep a clean, organized library of assets, templates, and source files

Working arrangement

This is a full-time, fully remote contractor role with a flexible schedule, open to candidates in Latin America, South Africa, or the Philippines. You need:

  • A capable design computer, with 16 GB RAM recommended and a dedicated graphics card strongly preferred for 3D work
  • Ideally, a color-accurate monitor
  • Internet of at least 25 Mbps download and 10 Mbps upload
  • A UPS or similar backup power
  • Plenty of storage
  • Licensed Adobe Creative Suite, Blender, and access to relevant AI tools

Who thrives in this role

  • Brings real craft to every format, from 3D renders to Meta ads to Klaviyo emails
  • Picks up a brand’s visual identity quickly and never needs the same correction twice
  • Sees AI as a competitive advantage and already uses it to deliver more
  • Manages their own workload without a creative director checking in
  • Cares about kerning, color, pixel precision, and render lighting
  • Wants to grow with a brand that rewards people who grow with it
