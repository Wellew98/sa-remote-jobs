THE ESSENTIALS

💰 Pay : $2,000 USD/month, a flat monthly rate

🌎

Location

: 100% remote

🕘

Schedule

: Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm, US Pacific, Central, or Eastern time️

💬 ️️ Language: Flawless written and spoken English

✍️

Experience

: 2–5 years of professional writing, with a published portfolio

If any of these don't work for you, this role isn't the right fit, and that's okay.

🎯WORDS THAT WORK

Good copy doesn't just sound nice. It gets someone to act.

We're looking for a writer who can take a topic and turn it into:

📖 a blog post people actually finish,

📬 a subject line people actually open,

️🖱️a page that makes someone click instead of leave.

You won't be here to fill a content calendar. Everything you write will have a job to do, and you'll know what that job is before you start typing.

The industry and platforms may change depending on the business you support. The standard doesn't: you write for results, not word count.

WHAT YOU'LL WRITE

📰 Blogs : SEO-friendly articles that inform readers and move them toward a decision.

📧

Emails

: Sequences designed to be opened, read, and acted on.

💻

Website copy

: Clear, engaging pages that keep people reading.

📣

Ads

: Short, sharp copy with a clear conversion goal.

🔍

Research-based content

Complex or unfamiliar topics made simple for anyone.

🤖 You'll use ChatGPT or similar tools to move faster. Your editorial judgment decides what gets published.

YOU'RE A FIT IF YOU HAVE

2–5 years of professional writing experience across blogs, email, web, and/or ads

A portfolio of published work you personally wrote

Flawless English, with grammar, tone, and structure that don't need a cleanup pass

The judgment to know when AI output should be rewritten, challenged, or thrown out

The ability to learn a new topic fast and explain it clearly

⭐ Bonus: SEO knowledge

NON NEGOTIABLES

📶 Reliable internet, plus a backup plan when it fails

⏰ You show up on time, every time

📣 If a deadline is at risk, you say so before it passes

🤝 You follow through without anyone needing to chase you

OUR HIRING PROCESS

📝 Apply → 👀 Profile review → 🎥 Intro video → 📞 Screening interview → 💬 Interviews → 🚀 Selection & onboarding

1.

Apply

: Submit your CV, portfolio, and answers. Your answers matter more than your CV.

2.

Profile review

: Our team reviews every application personally.

3.

Intro video

: If you're a match, we'll ask for a short video to hear how you communicate.

4.

Screening interview

A 30-minute conversation about your experience and the role.

5.

Interviews

Practical writing exercises and a final interview with the client.

6.

Selection & onboarding

Selected candidates complete a background check and employment verification before starting.

⚠️ We read every application. Answers that are generic, copied, or fully AI-generated are declined. We want to hear from you.

WHAT YOU GET

💵 A clear, fixed rate: $2,000/month, no guesswork

🏡 Full remote flexibility with a stable Monday–Friday schedule

🔑 Real ownership of your work, from first draft to published piece

🎯 Writing with purpose: every piece is built to get a response
