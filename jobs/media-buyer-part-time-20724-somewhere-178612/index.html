About the Company

The client is a specialized chiropractic and rehabilitation practice. The clinic focuses on active adults and athletes, moving beyond traditional temporary adjustments to deliver comprehensive, root-cause care.

About the Role

We're looking for an experienced Paid Media Buyer who understands that successful healthcare advertising is about attracting the right patients—not simply producing more clicks or leads.

You'll become an integrated member of the business, working consistent hours alongside the team, participating in weekly KPI meetings, and taking ownership of advertising performance as if you were an in-house marketing leader.

You'll oversee every stage of the paid acquisition process—from campaign strategy and audience research to creative testing, optimization, reporting, and ongoing performance improvements.

The ideal candidate has successfully marketed high-ticket healthcare services, particularly within chiropractic, physiotherapy, rehabilitation, regenerative medicine, functional medicine, or similar premium healthcare businesses.

Key Responsibilities

  • Strategic Media Management: Develop and execute comprehensive paid media strategies that align with business growth objectives, focusing on scaling high-quality lead generation for premium healthcare services.
  • Full-Cycle Ad Ownership: Own the entire advertising lifecycle across Meta, Google Search/PPC, and Local Services Ads (LSA). This includes building campaign structures, conducting keyword research, testing creative variations, and managing daily budgets to improve cost-per-qualified-consultation.
  • Performance Analysis & Optimization: Proactively monitor campaign health using key performance indicators such as ROAS, CPL, and CPA. Go beyond simple reporting to provide data-driven insights and actionable recommendations for A/B testing, landing page improvements, and budget allocation.
  • Channel Optimization: Identify and execute improvements across all platforms, including specifically rebuilding and optimizing Google Ads performance and enhancing LSA profile rankings to maximize booked appointments.
  • Collaborative Growth: Act as an integrated team member by participating in weekly meetings, monitoring lead quality, and continuously refining strategies to reduce wasted spend while scaling winning campaigns.

Qualifications & Skills

  • Proven Experience: Demonstrated success in managing Meta and Google Ads accounts (please provide specific performance metrics such as CPL, lead volume, and ROAS).
  • Industry Background: Candidates with experience marketing businesses in Chiropractic, Physiotherapy or similar healthcare industries is highly preferred.
  • Technical Expertise: Proficient in campaign optimization, independent budget management, and data-driven reporting.
  • Strategic Mindset: A curious, proactive approach that combines technical know-how with strategic thinking to test new ideas and improve long-term performance.
  • Communication & Collaboration: Excellent communicator who works well within a team and provides clear, consistent updates.
  • Professional Attributes: Highly organized, self-motivated, and results-oriented with a strong sense of ownership.
  • Reliability: Ability to maintain consistent availability during U.S. Eastern Time working hours.
