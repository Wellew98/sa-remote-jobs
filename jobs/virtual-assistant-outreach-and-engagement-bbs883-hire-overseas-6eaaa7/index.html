We're looking for a

Travel Consultant to own high-volume outbound member texting for a travel membership company whose members are primarily college students. You will send outbound texts to a large volume of members on a recurring basis, personalizing each message just enough to feel human, fun, and relevant rather than copied and pasted. This role is about speed, accuracy, and quality at scale — not strategy, marketing, or primarily inbound support.

The audience is college-aged. They text casually, they know when something feels scripted, and they respond to people who feel like peers rather than customer service reps. The right person for this role is digitally native, plugged into social media and current culture, and can match the energy and communication style of a college student naturally. If you can text like a friend, bring genuine enthusiasm to the work, and stay sharp and detail-oriented while moving through a high volume of conversations, this role is a strong fit.

Why You'll Want to Join

You will be paid in USD (bi-monthly: every 15th and 30th)

Full-time, 100% remote role

Preferred overlap with EST hours

Paid Time Off and Holiday observance per company policy

Clear path to expanded responsibilities including inbound support and broader member success work as trust is built

What You'll Work On

Send outbound texts to a large volume of members using approved messaging patterns and base templates

Personalize messages so they feel timely, human, and casual rather than copied and pasted

Review recent message history before sending so members do not receive repetitive styles or tones

Notice details such as recent or upcoming travel, or planning stage, and use them to choose the best message variation

Move through a high number of conversations quickly while staying accurate and detail-oriented

Read member conversations regularly to build familiarity and judgment over time

Follow messaging guidance while making smart small adjustments when needed

Escalate unusual, high-stakes, or unclear situations rather than guessing

Match the casual, upbeat tone that resonates with a college-aged audience without losing accuracy

What You Bring

Excellent written English with a strong feel for natural, casual US texting language — messages must sound like a real person texting a friend, not a scripted support rep

Genuinely plugged into current social media, pop culture, and the communication style that resonates with college students

Upbeat, fun, and approachable energy that comes through naturally in written communication

Ability to move through high volumes of conversations efficiently without becoming careless

Comfortable with repetitive communication work at pace without losing quality or tone

Strong memory for prior messages and context across conversations

Reliable, organized, and consistent, with good judgment on when to ask vs. act

Experience in group travel, student travel, campus programs, or similar youth-oriented travel contexts is a strong plus

Nice to Have

Prior experience in customer communication, outreach, texting-based support, or inbox management

Familiarity with travel, membership, or subscription-based businesses

Experience personalizing outreach at scale

Active presence on social media platforms such as TikTok, Instagram, or X

How to Apply

Please include

Your updated resume

A short Loom video (1 to 2 minutes) introducing yourself in your natural, casual communication style, describing your experience with high-volume written communication, and showing us the energy and personality you would bring to texting with a college student audience

Only candidates who submit a Loom video will be moved to the next step.
