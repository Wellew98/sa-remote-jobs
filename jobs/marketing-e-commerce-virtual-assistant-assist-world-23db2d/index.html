Marketing & E-commerce Virtual Assistant

Assist World Philippines, South Africa (Remote) Part-time Marketing & Content Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

About the Company Our client runs a specialty meat shop known for custom processing and high-end specialty products. They've built a loyal following on TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook, and are now expanding into e-commerce and niche shipping (not bulk) through a new Shopify store. Alongside product sales, they're growing an educational arm — teaching their audience how to make their own specialty products at home with plans to explore paid educational content down the line. About the Role This is a strategic marketing and e-commerce role, not a traditional admin-focused VA position. The client's in-house team handles content creation (filming and basic editing), so this role is about turning that content and audience into results: running ads, building out funnels, and growing the customer base. There's strong potential to grow into a full-time role as the relationship develops. Key Responsibilities • Plan, launch, and manage paid ad campaigns on Meta and TikTok to drive lead generation and audience growth • Streamline the customer journey from social media → Linktree → Shopify store, removing friction at every step • Support the build-out of email/SMS marketing — including setting up systems to capture and organize customer data for future campaigns • Help grow the brand's audience across TikTok (primary focus), Instagram, and Facebook • Provide light support on content calendar, scheduling, and posting as needed (creative execution stays in-house) • Support funnel-building for both product sales and the brand's educational/how-to content • Flag and help explore growth opportunities on emerging channels (e.g. YouTube) • As the role grows toward full-time, take on some traditional VA support such as inbox and calendar management • Monitor website and e-commerce performance, flagging and helping resolve issues as they come up • Help create and maintain SOPs and internal processes to keep workflows smooth as the shipping and e-commerce side scales up Added to What We're Looking For: Nice-to-have: ShipStation or other shipping/fulfillment software experience — not required, happy to train the right person What We're Looking For • Proven experience running Meta and/or TikTok ad campaigns focused on lead generation and growth • E-commerce experience, ideally with Shopify • Strong understanding of funnel-building and link-in-bio tools (e.g. Linktree) • Comfortable with Canva and CapCut for light editing/posting support • Familiarity with Trello, Outlook, and Google Drive • A self-starter who's comfortable with flexible, async communication and non-traditional working hours • Bonus: experience setting up email/SMS marketing tools (e.g. Klaviyo) • Nice-to-have: iPhone user (for seamless iMessage/photo/video sharing) Why This Role This is a great fit for someone who wants real ownership over growth strategy rather than just execution — the client is hands-on with content but is looking for a partner who can take the lead on ads, funnels, and turning an already-engaged audience into consistent sales. There's clear room to grow into a full-time position as the partnership proves out.
