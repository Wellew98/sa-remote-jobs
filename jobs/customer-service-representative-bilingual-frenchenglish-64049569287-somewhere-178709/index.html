Customer Service Representative — Bilingual French/English Priority Location: Africa or LATAM preferred Work Hours: Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM Eastern Time Salary Range: 1,000– 2,000 USD/month, depending on experience and language proficiency Type of Contract: Direct Hire Type of Job: Remote, Full-time Start Date: ASAP Languages: Fluent French and English — required About the Company Our client is a Quebec-based direct-to-consumer watch brand serving customers across more than 40 countries. The company operates with a lean, entrepreneurial team and is entering an exciting period of growth with a rebrand, new product launches, renewed international advertising, and expansion into additional markets. With the holiday season accounting for a significant portion of annual demand, the company is looking to strengthen its Customer Service team ahead of increased customer volume. About the Role We are looking for a Bilingual Customer Service Representative who is fluent in both French and English to support customers across international markets. This role will handle approximately 80% written support through email and chat and 20% phone support , helping customers with orders, products, shipping, delivery, and general service inquiries. The ideal candidate is dependable, organized, and customer-focused, with the ability to communicate professionally in both languages and work independently within a lean, fast-moving team. Key Responsibilities Customer Support - Respond to customer inquiries through email, live chat, and phone. - Provide professional support in both French and English. - Assist customers with product, order, shipping, delivery, and service-related questions. - Investigate customer concerns and provide appropriate solutions. - Escalate complex issues when necessary. - Follow up with customers to ensure issues are fully resolved. Inbox & Ticket Management - Manage incoming customer requests through Gorgias . - Prioritize tickets based on urgency and customer impact. - Maintain timely response and resolution times. - Help reduce existing Customer Service backlog. - Keep customer conversations and records organized and properly documented. Customer Experience - Represent the brand professionally in every customer interaction. - Communicate clearly and empathetically with customers across different markets. - Maintain a polished and confident telephone presence. - Adapt communication appropriately between French- and English-speaking customers. - Identify recurring customer concerns and communicate them to the wider team. Team Collaboration - Work closely with the existing Customer Service team. - Coordinate with fulfillment and other internal teams to resolve customer issues. - Communicate recurring problems, customer feedback, and process improvement opportunities. - Support increased customer volume during peak periods, particularly Q4. What We're Looking For Must-Have - Fluent French and English , both written and spoken. - Previous experience in Customer Service, Customer Support, e-commerce support, or a similar customer-facing role. - Strong written communication skills. - Comfortable communicating through email, chat, and phone. - Strong problem-solving and conflict-resolution skills. - Excellent attention to detail and organization. - Able to manage multiple customer conversations simultaneously. - Comfortable working independently in a remote environment. - Reliable and available to work Eastern Time hours. - Able to remain professional and composed during high-volume periods. Nice-to-Have - Experience supporting a DTC or e-commerce brand. - Experience using Gorgias or similar customer service platforms. - Experience supporting customers in Canada, the U.S., UK, or other international markets. - Experience with premium consumer products, fashion, accessories, watches, jewelry, or lifestyle brands. - Experience with order management, fulfillment, shipping, returns, and exchanges. - Experience working in a small or fast-growing company. Ideal Candidate Profile The ideal candidate is a polished and dependable bilingual customer service professional who genuinely cares about delivering an excellent customer experience. They should be comfortable working independently, managing a high volume of inquiries, and taking ownership of customer issues from start to finish. They should also be equally confident communicating with customers in both French and English , whether through written channels or over the phone. What Success Looks Like - Quickly becoming knowledgeable about the company's products, policies, and processes. - Reducing the existing Customer Service backlog. - Maintaining strong response and resolution times. - Providing consistent, high-quality support in both French and English. - Independently handling the majority of routine customer inquiries. - Maintaining service quality as customer volume increases. - Becoming a reliable long-term member of the Customer Service team. Why Join? - Join an international DTC brand during an exciting period of growth. - Work within a lean and entrepreneurial remote team. - Have a direct impact on the customer experience. - Support an international customer base across 40+ countries. - Opportunity to grow alongside the company as its Customer Service function expands.
