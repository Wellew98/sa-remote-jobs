About the Role

We’re looking for an experienced Senior Copywriter & Content Strategist who combines strong writing skills with strategic thinking, SEO, research, and performance marketing.

This is more than a traditional copywriting role. You’ll create and optimize content across websites, landing pages, paid ads, email campaigns, blogs, social media, and other marketing channels , while helping shape messaging and content strategy.

The ideal candidate understands how to turn business goals into compelling content, adapt messaging to different audiences, maintain a consistent brand voice, and write copy designed to drive engagement and conversions.

What You'll Actually Do

Write and edit copy for websites, landing pages, paid ads, emails, blogs, social media, and other marketing materials

Develop content strategies aligned with business and marketing objectives

Create conversion-focused copy for Meta, Google, TikTok, and other advertising channels

Conduct audience, competitor, keyword, and industry research

Develop and maintain consistent brand voice and messaging

Write SEO-focused website and blog content

Create email campaigns, promotional content, and lead-generation copy

Collaborate with designers, marketers, and other team members on campaigns

Edit and proofread content for clarity, accuracy, consistency, and quality

Use performance insights to improve messaging and content

Leverage AI tools for research, ideation, content development, and workflow efficiency

Manage multiple projects and deadlines in a fast-paced remote environment

Expectations

Produce polished, engaging, and conversion-focused copy with minimal supervision

Understand the difference between brand-focused content and performance marketing copy

Adapt tone and messaging across different audiences, industries, and platforms

Maintain strong attention to detail across all written materials

Apply SEO principles naturally while maintaining readability and brand voice

Research unfamiliar industries and quickly understand their audiences

Collaborate effectively with remote creative and marketing teams

Manage multiple content projects while consistently meeting deadlines

Take ownership of assignments from initial research through final delivery

Requirements

5+ years of professional copywriting, content writing, or content marketing experience

Strong portfolio demonstrating experience across multiple content formats

website, landing page, email, social media, and advertising copy

Strong spoken and written English communication skills

Experience with CMS platforms such as WordPress, Webflow, Drupal, Squarespace, or similar

Comfortable using AI tools as part of modern content and research workflows

Ability to work independently and manage multiple deadlines remotely
