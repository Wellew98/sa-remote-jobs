About The Role

We are looking for a compassionate, organised, and proactive Intake Specialist to manage incoming enquiries and guide prospective clients through the initial intake process.

The successful candidate will be responsible for communicating with prospective clients, gathering and recording relevant information, answering questions, coordinating next steps, and ensuring that all required details are accurately captured and followed up on.

This role requires someone with strong empathy and communication skills who can make people feel comfortable while maintaining professionalism and efficiency. The ideal candidate is highly organised, detail-oriented, able to work in a fast-paced environment, and confident handling responsibilities independently.

Previous customer service experience is preferred. Experience in healthcare, client intake, call centres, or administration would be advantageous.

Key Responsibilities

1. Client Communication & Enquiry Management

Handle incoming enquiries from prospective clients via phone, email, or other communication channels.

Communicate with individuals in a friendly, professional, and empathetic manner.

Listen carefully to client needs, questions, and concerns.

Provide clear information about the intake process and explain the next steps.

Respond to enquiries promptly and ensure individuals receive the assistance they need.

Maintain a positive and supportive experience throughout the initial interaction.

2. Intake & Information Gathering

Conduct initial intake conversations to understand client needs and gather relevant information.

Ask appropriate questions to obtain accurate and complete details.

Collect and verify required personal, contact, and service-related information.

Identify missing information and follow up to ensure intake records are complete.

Ensure all information is captured accurately in the relevant systems.

Maintain confidentiality and handle sensitive client information professionally.

3. Documentation & Administrative Support

Maintain accurate and up-to-date client records, intake forms, and documentation.

Review submitted information to identify missing details or inconsistencies.

Organise and maintain digital records and documentation.

Update client information and intake progress in the relevant systems.

Ensure documentation is completed and processed within required timeframes.

Support general administrative tasks related to the intake process.

4. Follow-Ups & Coordination

Follow up with prospective clients regarding outstanding information or documentation.

Keep track of individuals progressing through the intake process.

Coordinate appointments, consultations, or next steps where required.

Communicate updates to the relevant internal team members.

Monitor outstanding enquiries and ensure no prospective client is overlooked.

Escalate complex enquiries or concerns to the appropriate team member.

5. Problem-Solving & Workflow Management

Manage multiple enquiries and intake cases while maintaining accuracy and efficiency.

Identify delays, missing information, or potential issues within the intake process.

Take initiative to resolve routine problems independently.

Prioritise urgent enquiries and manage competing responsibilities effectively.

Maintain a consistent and organised workflow in a fast-paced environment.

Identify opportunities to improve the client intake experience and administrative processes.

Essential Requirements

Strong verbal and written English communication skills.

Excellent interpersonal skills and the ability to communicate with empathy and professionalism.

Highly organised with strong attention to detail.

Ability to manage multiple tasks and enquiries simultaneously.

Strong administrative and information-gathering skills.

Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced environment.

Independent problem-solving skills and the confidence to take initiative.

Reliable follow-up and time-management skills.

Comfortable using computers, email, spreadsheets, and digital systems.

Ability to handle confidential information with discretion.

A professional, patient, and client-focused approach.

Advantageous Experience

Previous customer service or client-facing experience.

Experience in an intake, admissions, or client coordination role.

Healthcare, medical, home care, or social services experience.

Call centre or high-volume telephone experience.

Experience handling client enquiries and gathering personal information.

Familiarity with CRM systems, client management platforms, or electronic record systems.

Experience coordinating appointments and following up on outstanding documentation.

Software & Systems

The successful candidate should be comfortable learning and working with digital systems, including:

Email & Communication Platforms

Gmail, Outlook, Zoom, or Microsoft Teams.

Spreadsheets

Google Sheets or Microsoft Excel.

Client Management Systems

CRM or intake platforms.

Documentation

Digital forms, records, and document management systems.

Experience with specific systems is advantageous, but the ability to learn new platforms quickly is essential.

Ideal Candidate Profile

The ideal candidate is someone who genuinely enjoys helping people and understands the importance of creating a positive first impression.

They should be empathetic and approachable, with the ability to communicate with individuals from different backgrounds and handle sensitive conversations with patience and professionalism.

At the same time, they must be highly organised and comfortable managing the administrative side of the intake process. They should be able to gather information accurately, keep records updated, follow up on outstanding requirements, and ensure that every enquiry is handled appropriately.

We are looking for someone who

Makes people feel heard, comfortable, and supported.

Communicates clearly, confidently, and professionally.

Is naturally organised and pays close attention to detail.

Can remain calm and productive when handling a high volume of enquiries.

Takes ownership of tasks and follows through without constant supervision.

Uses initiative to identify and resolve problems.

Can balance a people-focused approach with accurate administrative work.

Is adaptable, dependable, and quick to learn new systems and processes.

Ultimately, we are looking for someone who can provide an excellent first point of contact for prospective clients while ensuring the intake process runs smoothly, accurately, and efficiently.
