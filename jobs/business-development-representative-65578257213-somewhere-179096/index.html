About the Company

Our client is a fast-growing, dynamic force in the healthcare staffing sector dedicated to bridging critical gaps in medical provider coverage across the United States. Built on a foundation of high energy, continuous development, and accountability, they empower team members to thrive in an autonomous, high-impact remote work environment. Joining their team means becoming part of a relationship-first culture that values hustle, offers clear pathways for career advancement, and helps healthcare organizations solve vital staffing challenges.

About the Role

Our client is seeking a driven, proactive Remote Business Development Representative to serve as the engine behind their sales pipeline. In this role, you will be responsible for initiating high-volume outbound outreach to key decision-makers across US hospitals, health systems, and healthcare facilities. You will play a crucial part in uncovering client needs, qualifying prospects, and scheduling qualified discovery meetings for the sales leadership team. This is an exceptional opportunity for an ambitious professional looking to build a long-term remote sales career within a fast-paced healthcare environment.

Key Responsibilities

- Execute high-volume outbound prospecting campaigns (50–60+ daily dials, emails, and multi-channel touchpoints) to engage decision-makers across targeted US healthcare facilities.

- Qualify prospective clients, uncover workforce staffing challenges, and effectively communicate the value of specialized healthcare staffing solutions.

- Book 8+ high-quality discovery meetings per month for Business Development Consultants and Directors, ensuring seamless handoffs to drive closed business.

- Maintain rigorous CRM hygiene by logging daily touchpoints, updating lead statuses, and ensuring clear visibility into pipeline metrics.

- Collaborate closely with sales leadership to align on targeting strategies, ideal client profiles, and pitch messaging.

- Actively engage in performance coaching, feedback sessions, and skill-building opportunities to optimize conversion rates and accelerate career growth.

Qualifications

Required

- High School Diploma required; Bachelor’s degree preferred.

- Proven resilience, confidence, and comfort conducting high-volume cold calling and outbound sales outreach.

- Fluent English verbal and written communication skills with a polished, professional presence.

- Strong self-motivation, goal orientation, and a high degree of coachability in a fast-paced environment.

- Comfort utilizing modern CRM platforms, managing activity metrics, and maintaining data accuracy independently.

- Full alignment with US working hours as a remote independent contractor.

Preferred

- 1+ years of experience in outbound B2B sales, appointment setting, call center operations, or customer acquisition.

- Exposure to the US healthcare market or medical staffing industry dynamics.

- Experience working remotely within global startups or performance-focused sales environments.
