​​​​About Us

Somewhere.com is a recruiting agency. We source worldwide talent to work remotely for clients based in the United States. We place over 250 individuals a month into remote roles.

Role Description

This is a full-time remote role at a US based digital marketing agency. The specialist will be responsible for developing and implementing paid search, social and retargeting campaigns to drive business growth, analyzing campaign performance, optimizing marketing campaigns, and collaborating with cross-functional teams to achieve marketing goals.

Qualifications

  • Experience in paid media buying across search and social.
  • Google Adwords experience.
  • Keyword research, retargeting, geo-targeting, tracking and reporting.
  • Experience in analyzing campaign performance and optimizing marketing campaigns.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork skills.
  • Ability to work independently and remotely.
  • Experience working with international teams is a plus.
  • Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business, or related field.

Requirements

  • At least 3 years experience in performance marketing.
  • Pay-per-click Google Search.
  • Geo-targeting, budget management, Google Analytics 4, Meta and X.
  • Fast, reliable internet
  • A second monitor and fast computer
  • A quiet and professional work environment
  • Great command of the english language (written and verbal)
  • Work full time on United States time zone

Additional Details

Full-time independent contractor paid 2x monthly. Client looking for long term employment. Role comes with upward mobility and future opportunity for advancement.
