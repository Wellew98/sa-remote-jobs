About the Client

A U.S.-based technology services company specializing in IT support, cybersecurity, and managed technology solutions for small and medium-sized businesses. The company focuses on delivering reliable technical assistance, maintaining secure IT environments, and ensuring a seamless client experience through effective communication, service coordination, and timely resolution of technical requests.

About the Role

We are seeking a Client Experience Support Assistant (CXA) to serve as a primary point of contact for incoming client communications and to keep every client request moving from first call to closeout.

Most of your day is spent on the phone with clients: receiving calls, documenting issues clearly, keeping clients informed, and coordinating next steps with the technical team. You will also run scheduled client outreach projects, and keep our SOPs and knowledge base current.

This is an intake-and-route role: you capture, document and route requests, technicians fix them, and Customer Success handles scope and pricing. This position suits someone who is calm under pressure, highly organized, and comfortable owning deadlines and follow-up in a fast-paced, process-driven environment. Technical knowledge is helpful but not required. Accuracy, clarity and follow-through matter most.

You will not be thrown in. New CXAs follow a structured 90-day certification path with shadowing, daily role-play practice and coaching, and take live calls only as each level is earned.

Role Responsibilities

Client Communication & Call Handling

  • Answer incoming client calls in a professional, friendly and calm manner
  • Ask structured questions to clearly understand and document client issues
  • Set clear expectations with clients on next steps and timelines
  • Make outbound follow-up calls to give updates or request additional information

Ticket Documentation & Ownership

  • Create, update and maintain tickets in our ticketing system according to documented SOPs
  • Capture issues, symptoms and context so technicians can act without back-and-forth
  • Complete every required field when scheduling an appointment, including appointment type, the device being worked on and remote-connection permission
  • Handle client credentials and other confidential information securely, per SOP
  • Keep every assigned ticket current: update due dates and statuses after each contact, and move acknowledged tickets out of New promptly
  • When a ticket is set to Waiting on Customer, note exactly what is needed from the client
  • Coordinate with technicians and fellow CXAs on priorities, updates and clarifications

Follow-Up & Escalation

  • Track open client issues and make sure follow-up happens on time
  • Use every contact method on file (all phone numbers, voicemail and email) before treating a client as unreachable, and flag outdated contact details for updating
  • Identify stalled or urgent issues and escalate to the Assistant Client Experience Associate Manager, or to the Technical Lead for technical issues, per internal guidelines
  • Confirm resolution with clients and close tickets cleanly when work is complete; never resolve a ticket in a way that leaves the client to follow up with us

Client Outreach Projects

  • Run scheduled outreach campaigns (for example, operating-system end-of-support or security update notices) from call list to completion
  • Contact clients by phone; an email may go first as a heads-up but never replaces the call
  • Log every outreach attempt and outcome, and report progress against the project deadline
  • Raise capacity or timeline risks early, before a deadline is missed

Team Meetings & Quarterly Goals

  • Submit weekly pre-work and status updates ahead of team meetings
  • Work with the Assistant Client Experience Associate Manager to establish and support your quarterly goals (Rocks)

Documentation & SOPs

  • Keep client records in our documentation platform, and SOPs and knowledge-base articles in our internal training library, accurate and up to date
  • Draft new or revised SOPs as processes change, with clear scope and version history
  • Route documentation for review and sign-off by the Assistant Client Experience Associate Manager, with Technical Lead review for technical content before it goes live

Process & Team Support

  • Execute daily workflows by following established SOPs
  • Collaborate closely with the Client Experience, Customer Success and Technical teams
  • Maintain consistency, professionalism and attention to detail across all communications

Requirements

  • 1–2 years of experience in customer service, call handling or client support roles
  • Strong verbal and written English communication skills
  • High attention to detail and accurate, consistent data entry, including the ability to document conversations completely
  • Comfortable making a high volume of outbound calls every day
  • Trustworthy handling of confidential client information
  • Comfortable managing multiple active client requests and a project deadline at the same time
  • Ability to follow structured processes and escalation paths
  • Working proficiency with Microsoft 365 (Outlook, Teams, Word, SharePoint) and intermediate Excel (sorting, filters, basic formulas, simple charts)
  • Reliable high-speed internet with a backup plan for power outages, and a professional remote work environment

Nice to Haves

  • Experience in a Managed Service Provider (MSP), IT support or technical services environment
  • Familiarity with ticketing systems, service workflows and response-time targets
  • Exposure to ticketing, documentation or quoting platforms (training provided)
  • Experience building or maintaining process documentation
  • Familiarity with EOS (Rocks, scorecards) or similar goal-tracking frameworks

What Success Looks Like

  • First 90 Days
  • Days 1–30: Work through the CXA training modules, shadow live calls, and become comfortable in our daily working platforms; clear Level 1 certification and begin supervised calls
  • Days 31–60:
  • Take live calls solo, with post-call coaching review (Level 2 certification)
  • Complete tickets from start to finish in collaboration with the technical team and Customer Success Manager
  • Process accurate escalations and keep friction with clients low through proactive updates and follow-ups
  • Work with management to establish your quarterly goals (Rocks)
  • Days 61–90: Own your queue, run an outreach project end to end, maintain assigned SOPs with sign-off, and earn full CXA certification (Level 3)
  • Ongoing
  • Client calls are handled professionally and efficiently
  • Tickets are documented clearly, kept current, and move from intake to resolution without delays
  • Every scheduled appointment has its required fields recorded
  • Due dates and statuses always reflect where each ticket really stands
  • Clients feel informed and supported throughout the resolution process
  • Outreach projects are completed by their deadline, with every contact logged
  • Documentation is accurate, signed off, and reflects how the team works today
