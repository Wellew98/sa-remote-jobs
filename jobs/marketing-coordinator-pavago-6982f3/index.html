About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a Marketing Coordinator to support day-to-day marketing operations, executive coordination, content management, and digital growth initiatives.

This is a hands-on, execution-focused role that blends marketing operations with executive support. You’ll help keep campaigns organized, content moving, leadership aligned, and growth initiatives on track. Rather than creating strategy from scratch, you’ll ensure marketing execution happens consistently, efficiently, and accurately.

Working closely with leadership, you’ll coordinate marketing campaigns, manage content systems, execute LinkedIn-focused initiatives, support lead generation, and leverage automation tools to improve workflows.

If you’re highly organized, proactive, tech-savvy, and enjoy turning plans into action, this role is a great fit.

Responsibilities

Executive Support & Operational Coordination

Support leadership with calendar management, scheduling, inbox organization, and operational coordination.

Schedule recurring meetings aligned with business priorities.

Track priorities, deadlines, and follow-ups to keep projects moving forward.

Maintain organized workflows that support operational efficiency.

Assist with administrative and executive support as needed.

Content Management & Organization

Maintain a centralized library of marketing assets and campaign materials.

Organize, update, and repurpose content across marketing channels.

Ensure consistency in messaging, formatting, and brand presentation.

Coordinate content calendars and publishing schedules.

Keep documentation and marketing resources organized and easily accessible.

Marketing Execution & Digital Strategy

Execute LinkedIn-focused marketing campaigns and outreach initiatives.

Support lead generation and audience engagement activities.

Monitor campaign performance and engagement across digital channels.

Assist with email marketing, content distribution, and promotional activities.

Track campaign metrics and recommend improvements based on performance.

Tools, Systems & Automation

Use GoHighLevel, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, and related tools to support marketing workflows.

Build surveys, forms, and applications using Typeform or Jotform.

Leverage AI tools and automation platforms to streamline marketing operations.

Maintain organized marketing databases, reports, and workflow documentation.

Support process improvements through automation and efficient systems management.

Research & Content Support

Conduct market, audience, and competitor research.

Research industry trends and campaign opportunities.

Support video content planning and marketing asset development.

Contribute ideas to strengthen messaging, positioning, and campaign effectiveness.

What Makes You a Great Fit

You are highly organized and thrive in structured environments.

You enjoy balancing executive support with hands-on marketing execution.

You take ownership and consistently follow through without constant supervision.

You enjoy working with automation tools and modern marketing platforms.

You communicate clearly and collaborate effectively with remote teams.

You are proactive, dependable, and detail-oriented.

Required Experience & Skills

3+ years of experience in marketing coordination, executive support, operations, or similar roles.

Experience supporting digital marketing campaigns and content workflows.

Hands-on experience with

GoHighLevel

LinkedIn Sales Navigator

Typeform or Jotform

Strong Google Workspace proficiency (Docs, Sheets, Drive, Calendar).

Excellent written and verbal English communication skills.

Strong organizational, multitasking, and project coordination abilities.

Ability to work independently in a remote environment.

Preferred Experience

Experience supporting founders, executives, or entrepreneurial businesses.

Familiarity with AI tools for marketing, content creation, or operational workflows.

Experience with LinkedIn marketing and lead generation.

Background in startup or high-growth business environments.

Experience coordinating marketing content and digital campaigns.

What Success Looks Like

Marketing campaigns are executed accurately and on schedule.

Content libraries and marketing assets remain organized and up to date.

LinkedIn and digital marketing initiatives show consistent engagement and growth.

Leadership receives reliable operational and executive support.

Marketing workflows become more efficient through automation and improved processes.

Projects move forward with minimal follow-up or oversight.
