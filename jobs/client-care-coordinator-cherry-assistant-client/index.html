We are hiring an experienced, highly organised Client Care Coordinator to provide comprehensive client support and administrative coordination for our US client, a busy virtual healthcare practice. Acting as the primary point of contact between clients, healthcare practitioners, and the internal team, this role will play an important part in delivering an exceptional client experience while ensuring the smooth day-to-day running of the practice.

The ideal candidate has previous experience in healthcare administration, client coordination, or a similar client-facing role, with direct hands-on experience using OptiMantra EMR. They should have exceptional communication skills, strong organisational abilities, and a naturally service-oriented approach, combining the attention to detail of an experienced healthcare coordinator with the warmth and professionalism of a luxury hospitality professional. The successful candidate will be proactive, dependable, and comfortable managing a high volume of client requests while maintaining accuracy and efficiency. They should take ownership of responsibilities, anticipate client and practitioner needs, and follow tasks through to completion without requiring constant supervision. Previous experience in functional medicine, concierge medicine, hormone optimisation, wellness, or telehealth is highly preferred.

Key Responsibilities

Scheduling & Client Coordination

  • Schedule, confirm, reschedule, and manage client appointments.
  • Monitor upcoming appointments to ensure all required laboratory tests, forms, payments, and other necessary items have been completed.
  • Send clients laboratory requisitions, coordinate testing arrangements, and follow up proactively until laboratory work is completed.
  • Support new-client onboarding and ongoing membership administration workflows.
  • Identify outstanding requirements ahead of appointments and take action to ensure clients are properly prepared.
  • Resolve routine scheduling issues independently and escalate more complex matters when necessary.

Client Communication & Support

  • Serve as the primary communication link between clients, healthcare practitioners, and the internal team.
  • Communicate professionally and warmly with clients via telephone, email, and text.
  • Monitor incoming client communications and ensure questions, requests, and concerns are addressed promptly.
  • Conduct proactive client check-ins and follow-up communications.
  • Assist clients with enquiries relating to appointments, memberships, billing, laboratory testing, and general practice processes.
  • Handle frustrated, confused, or concerned clients calmly and professionally, ensuring they feel heard and supported.
  • Maintain a polished, responsive, and welcoming service standard across every client interaction.
  • Anticipate client needs and provide updates before clients need to follow up themselves.

Clinical & Practitioner Support

  • Route clinical questions and patient-specific medical enquiries to the appropriate licensed practitioner.
  • Follow up on outstanding practitioner responses to ensure clients receive the necessary communication.
  • Assist with prescription and peptide renewal workflows by gathering required information and forwarding requests to the appropriate clinical team member.
  • Help practitioners stay organised by identifying outstanding tasks, requests, and follow-up requirements.
  • Monitor pending client matters and ensure appropriate action is taken.
  • Recognise when an issue requires escalation and bring it to the attention of the relevant team member.
  • Maintain clear professional boundaries and ensure medical decisions and clinical advice are always handled by qualified practitioners.

Documentation & Patient Records

  • Upload, organise, and manage laboratory results and patient documents within OptiMantra EMR.
  • Maintain accurate and up-to-date client records, notes, tags, tasks, and administrative documentation.
  • Manage patient communication records and ensure relevant information is properly documented.
  • Monitor outstanding tasks and follow-up items to ensure nothing is overlooked.
  • Maintain organised digital records while handling confidential patient information responsibly.
  • Ensure OptiMantra remains accurate, organised, and up to date.

Client Experience & Operational Support

  • Take ownership of the overall client experience, ensuring every interaction is warm, professional, and responsive.
  • Proactively identify potential issues before they become larger problems.
  • Follow tasks through to completion rather than simply passing responsibilities to another team member.
  • Identify what clients and practitioners may need next and take appropriate action.
  • Manage a high volume of client requests while balancing speed, accuracy, and attention to detail.
  • Support the smooth operation of the virtual healthcare practice through consistent communication, organisation, and follow-through.
  • Work collaboratively with practitioners and internal team members to ensure an efficient and high-quality service experience.

Required Qualifications

  • Previous experience in client coordination, healthcare administration, medical reception, executive assistance, or a similar client-facing administrative role.
  • Mandatory hands-on experience using OptiMantra EMR. Candidates must have previously worked directly with the platform and be comfortable navigating it independently.
  • Practical experience managing patient records, appointments, laboratory documents, patient communications, tasks, and administrative workflows within OptiMantra.
  • Excellent verbal and written English communication skills.
  • A warm, confident, and professional telephone manner.
  • Proven ability to communicate effectively with clients and healthcare practitioners via telephone, email, and text.
  • Strong organisational and administrative abilities, with exceptional attention to detail.
  • Experience managing a high volume of requests while prioritising competing responsibilities.
  • Ability to take ownership of tasks, work independently, and follow through until matters are fully resolved.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to handle routine client issues independently.
  • Excellent listening skills, emotional intelligence, and the ability to manage sensitive or difficult client interactions professionally.
  • Professional judgement regarding when matters can be handled independently and when they require escalation.
  • Ability to handle confidential patient information with discretion and responsibility.
  • Comfortable working in a fast-paced healthcare environment.
  • Proficiency with Google Workspace, including Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, and Google Sheets.
  • Technologically confident, with the ability to learn additional platforms, systems, and workflows quickly.
  • Fluent in AI platforms, including Claude and ChatGPT.
  • Understanding of the boundaries between administrative support and clinical responsibilities.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Previous experience working within functional medicine, concierge medicine, hormone optimisation, wellness, telehealth, or another high-touch healthcare environment.
  • Previous experience using GoHighLevel CRM, particularly contact management, client communications, task management, follow-ups, workflows, and pipelines.
  • Previous experience in a medical practice, private healthcare facility, or client-focused healthcare environment.
  • Nursing background or previous clinical experience. An RN licence is not required unless the candidate will be performing clinical duties.
  • Familiarity with medical terminology, laboratory testing processes, and healthcare administration.
  • Experience supporting membership-based healthcare services or concierge medical practices.
  • Experience delivering high-touch customer service comparable to luxury hospitality standards.
  • Previous experience working remotely and managing responsibilities independently.

Required Skills & Tools

  • Client care coordination and healthcare administration.
  • Patient scheduling, appointment confirmations, and rescheduling.
  • New-client onboarding and membership administration.
  • Client communication via telephone, email, and text.
  • Professional telephone etiquette and exceptional written communication.
  • High-touch customer service and client relationship management.
  • OptiMantra EMR (mandatory hands-on experience).
  • Patient records, documentation, and laboratory result management.
  • Laboratory requisitions, testing coordination, and follow-ups.
  • Prescription and peptide renewal administration.
  • Clinical enquiry routing and practitioner coordination.
  • Patient follow-ups, task tracking, and outstanding item management.
  • Google Workspace, including Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, and Sheets.
  • GoHighLevel CRM (preferred).
  • AI platforms, including Claude and ChatGPT.
  • Handling confidential patient information and sensitive documentation.
  • Strong organisational, time-management, and prioritisation skills.
  • Exceptional attention to detail and administrative accuracy.
  • Proactive problem-solving and independent decision-making.
  • Emotional intelligence and professional client communication.
  • Ability to manage a high volume of client interactions efficiently.
  • Strong follow-through, accountability, and task ownership.
  • Ability to recognise potential problems and escalate appropriately.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a remote healthcare environment.

Schedule & Pay

  • Full-Time position (EST).
  • Fully remote role for a US based company.
  • Pay ranges from R14,000 p/m - R 24,000 p/m.
  • Annual raises and confirmed holidays.

System Requirements

  • Internet speed of at least 20 Mbps.
  • Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher.
  • 8 GB of RAM or higher.
  • Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer.
  • HD 720p webcam.
  • Headset with a microphone.

Benefits

  • Fully remote work from South Africa.
  • Consistent full-time schedule.
  • Work in a client-focused role in the health space.
