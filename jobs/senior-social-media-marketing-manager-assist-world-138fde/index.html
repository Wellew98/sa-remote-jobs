Overview

We are looking for a sharp, proactive, and genuinely skilled Digital Marketing & Content Specialist to support ongoing marketing execution across email, LinkedIn, website content, and SEO. This is a hands-on execution role — not a strategy-only position. The ideal candidate takes full ownership of their work, communicates transparently, and brings consistent energy and output every single day. AI tools are welcome as a support layer, but strong independent thinking, writing ability, and real platform proficiency are non-negotiable. Key Responsibilities HubSpot & Email Marketing Build and execute email marketing campaigns in HubSpot Upload, tag, and segment contact databases accurately Automate workflows and email sequences Create and send targeted newsletters to segmented audiences LinkedIn & Social Media Manage and post content on LinkedIn (primary platform) Engage with comments and grow audience organically Maintain a consistent, professional brand voice across platforms Content Creation & Copywriting Write high-quality original content for a professional, white-collar audience Produce posts, newsletters, and campaign copy with minimal hand-holding Use AI tools to assist — not replace — original thinking and writing Website & SEO Upload and manage content in WordPress Support on-page SEO and blog publishing Contribute to website improvements and layout updates Reporting & Communication Share work-in-progress proactively — do not wait until everything is "perfect" Provide regular updates on task status and campaign performance Flag blockers early rather than going silent

Must-Haves

Proven, depth-level HubSpot experience (implementation, not just reporting) Hands-on email marketing and campaign execution Strong LinkedIn content and engagement experience Excellent English writing skills — especially for corporate/professional audiences Microsoft 365 proficient (Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint) WordPress experience (content publishing and basic updates) Comfortable using AI tools as an assistant, not a ghostwriter Reliable, high-energy, and proactive communicator Nice to Have SEO experience (sustained, not one-off) Paid ads (LinkedIn, Google) Video editing B2B or professional services industry background

What Success Looks Like

Work is shared openly and in progress — not hidden until it's "ready" Output matches the energy and capability shown in the interview, consistently Campaigns are running, contacts are segmented, content is live Communication is clear, direct, and human — not AI-generated sounding Tasks are completed in realistic, stated timeframes — no surprises
