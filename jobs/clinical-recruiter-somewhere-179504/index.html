PositionClinical Recruiter

Work Hours (Client) : Monday to Friday, 15 to 20 hours per week for the first 3 months, then full-time, during standard US business hours

Pay Range$1,200 to $1,800 / month (varies based on skill set and experience level)

Location of SearchSouth Africa, Philippines, LatAm

Work LocationRemote

Company Overview

A growing digital mental health company providing culturally attuned therapy and coaching to underserved communities across the United States. The company is expanding its network of licensed providers and is hiring a Clinical Recruiter to lead that effort.

Role Overview

The Clinical Recruiter owns the full recruitment cycle for licensed clinicians and associates, sourcing both W2 and 1099 therapists who care about culturally attuned mental health care. The goal is to grow the active provider network substantially.

This role starts at 15 to 20 hours per week for the first 3 months, then moves to full-time. You will work closely with the clinical and operations teams, act as the first point of contact for providers, and receive talking points and mission-focused materials to get started quickly.

Duties and Responsibilities

  • Source providers through LinkedIn, Indeed, therapist-specific organizations and professional groups focused on the communities the company serves.
  • Run initial screening interviews and negotiate pay rates.
  • Schedule clinical vignette interviews for qualified candidates with the clinical leadership team.
  • Coordinate with internal supervisors on California-based associates to confirm alignment.
  • Maintain the provider recruitment board in Jira with real-time updates.
  • Give the clinical and operations teams regular updates so hiring matches current geographic, marketing and clinical gaps.
  • Keep warm leads engaged when immediate onboarding is not available, acting as the face of the company to the provider community.
  • Prioritize recruiting in California first, then New York, followed by Massachusetts and Washington.

Minimum Qualifications

Experience2+ years of clinical recruiting experience

  • Proven track record recruiting in behavioral health or health tech.
  • Working knowledge of clinical credentials, including the differences between an LCSW, an LMFT and a PsyD, and the ability to speak credibly with clinicians.
  • Understanding of BIPOC mental health and the communities the company serves, with the ability to speak authentically to its mission and identify providers who share its values.
  • Comfort discussing and negotiating provider compensation while keeping the candidate experience positive.
  • Availability for 15 to 20 hours per week for the first 3 months, then full-time, during standard business hours.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Clinical recruiting experience in a high-growth startup environment.
