Landscape Construction Estimator — Design/Build (Remote)

Location & Employment: Fully remote. Full-time. U.S. Eastern Time overlap for daily coordination with field and office leadership.

About the Company

We are a rapidly growing, U.S.-based, veteran-owned design-build landscape and outdoor-living company serving residential and commercial clients across Northeast and Central Florida. We design and install custom landscapes, hardscapes, outdoor living spaces, driveways, walkways, lighting, and plantings — from high-end residential backyards to commercial projects. Our work has been featured on national home-improvement television, and we carry a strong regional reputation. We are expanding into a second major market and scaling the operational backbone to support it.

About the Role

We design and build — which means every project starts with a plan and a number. Today, estimating and takeoffs fall on our general manager and operations director, both of whom belong in the field moving between job sites, not behind a laptop pricing work at the end of a long day. As residential design-build volume grows and we expand into a new market, bid volume is outpacing capacity.

This role owns the estimating desk. You'll turn designs and plans into accurate, timely, client-ready estimates and proposals — working from our established cost data, vendor pricing, and production rates — so field leadership stays in the field and no opportunity stalls waiting on a number.

Key Responsibilities

Plan Review & Quantity Takeoffs

  • Review landscape and hardscape designs, site plans, and construction drawings
  • Perform accurate material and labor takeoffs — plant material, sod/turf, pavers and hardscape, lighting, irrigation, drainage, and grading/site work
  • Flag missing information or scope gaps before pricing

Cost Estimating & Pricing

  • Build detailed estimates using the company's unit-cost data, vendor pricing, and labor/production rates
  • Apply markups, margins, and contingencies to company standards
  • Price change orders and estimate revisions as scope evolves

Proposal & Bid Preparation

  • Produce clean, professional, client-ready proposals and scopes of work
  • Assemble commercial bid packages and complete public / HOA / GC bid forms as needed
  • Track bid due dates and ensure on-time submission

Vendor & Cost Data Management

  • Collect and update material and subcontractor pricing; help maintain a living cost database
  • Coordinate with vendors and suppliers on quotes for specialty items
  • Partner with field leadership to keep production rates and unit costs accurate to real job outcomes

Estimate Tracking & Post-Bid Analysis

  • Maintain an organized log of estimates, win/loss, and margins
  • Compare estimated vs. actual costs to sharpen future pricing
  • Report on bid volume, hit rate, and pipeline value

Required Qualifications

  • 3+ years of construction estimating experience — landscape, hardscape, site work, or a related trade (civil, exteriors, general construction)
  • Ability to read and interpret construction drawings and site plans
  • Hands-on experience with takeoff/estimating software (PlanSwift, Bluebeam, STACK, or similar) and strong Excel skills
  • Strong math, sharp attention to detail, and the ability to produce accurate estimates independently
  • Excellent English communication and full-time U.S. Eastern Time overlap

Preferred Qualifications

  • Landscape, design-build, irrigation, hardscape, or outdoor-living estimating experience specifically
  • Experience estimating for the U.S. market, with familiarity with U.S. materials and units of measure
  • Exposure to both residential design-build and commercial/public bid processes
  • Familiarity with landscape/construction platforms (LMN, Aspire, Buildertrend, Jobber, or similar)

Ideal Candidate Profile

You are precise, self-directed, and allergic to sloppy numbers. Hand you a design and a plan set and you'll turn it into a defensible estimate without hand-holding — and you ask sharp questions when scope is unclear instead of guessing. You can juggle multiple bids and deadlines without dropping one, and you understand that an estimate is both a math exercise and a sales document: it has to be accurate, and it has to look professional.

Why Join Us

You'll be the estimating backbone of a growing design-build company at a real inflection point — trusted to own the numbers that win work, with direct line of sight into how your estimates translate into projects on the ground.
