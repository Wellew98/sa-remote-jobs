About The Role

White Stone is looking for an outgoing, confident and highly motivated Lead Generation Specialist to join its team.

The successful candidate will be responsible for identifying potential business opportunities, reaching out to prospective clients and generating new leads through outbound calls and email outreach.

This is a proactive, communication-focused role suited to someone who enjoys speaking to people, building connections and creating opportunities. You will primarily be reaching out to nursing home owners and key decision-makers to introduce the business, generate interest and arrange meetings for the sales team.

You will not be responsible for closing sales. Instead, your main focus will be getting conversations started, identifying potential opportunities and booking qualified meetings.

No previous experience is required. We are looking for someone with the right personality, confidence, communication skills and willingness to learn..

Key Responsibilities

1. Outbound Calling & Lead Generation

Make a high volume of outbound calls to prospective clients.

Cold call nursing home owners and key decision-makers.

Introduce the business and generate interest in its services.

Identify potential business opportunities and suitable leads.

Confidently initiate conversations with new prospects.

Build rapport and create positive first impressions.

2. Email Outreach & Prospecting

Conduct outbound email campaigns to prospective clients.

Identify and contact relevant nursing home owners and decision-makers.

Write professional and engaging outreach emails.

Follow up with prospective clients to encourage engagement.

Maintain consistent communication with potential leads.

3. Appointment Setting & Business Development Support

Identify interested prospects and arrange meetings for the sales team.

Schedule appointments with relevant decision-makers.

Ensure meetings are booked with suitable potential clients.

Pass on relevant information and notes to the sales team.

Follow up on outstanding leads and opportunities.

4. Lead Tracking & Reporting

Maintain accurate records of calls, emails and interactions.

Track lead progress and follow-up activities.

Keep prospect information updated.

Meet daily and weekly outreach targets.

Provide regular updates on lead generation activities and results.

Minimum Requirements

Excellent verbal and written English communication skills.

Confident and comfortable speaking to new people.

Comfortable making a high volume of outbound calls.

Willingness to make cold calls and approach prospective clients.

Strong interpersonal and relationship-building skills.

Good organisational and time-management abilities.

Self-motivated, driven and target-oriented.

Comfortable using email and basic computer systems.

Ability to follow up consistently and work independently.

Previous experience is not required. Candidates from customer service, call centres, sales, hospitality or other customer-facing environments are welcome to apply.

Preferred Personality Traits

We are looking for someone who is

Outgoing

Naturally enjoys speaking to people and starting conversations.

Confident

Comfortable approaching business owners and senior decision-makers.

Self-motivated

Takes initiative and does not need constant supervision.

Driven

Motivated by targets, activity levels and generating results.

Persuasive

Can create interest and engage prospects without being pushy.

Resilient

Comfortable handling rejection and moving on to the next opportunity.

Professional

Represents the business positively through calls and emails.

Proactive

Consistently follows up and looks for new opportunities.

Key Performance Expectations

The successful candidate will be measured on

Daily outbound call activity.

Number of prospective clients contacted.

Email outreach activity.

Number of leads generated.

Number of qualified meetings booked.

Consistency of follow-ups.

Accuracy of lead tracking and reporting.

Important Notes

This is an outbound lead generation role, not an Executive Assistant position.

The role focuses on generating leads and booking meetings, not closing sales.

Candidates must be comfortable with cold calling and handling rejection.

No previous sales or lead generation experience is essential, provided the candidate has the right personality and communication skills.
