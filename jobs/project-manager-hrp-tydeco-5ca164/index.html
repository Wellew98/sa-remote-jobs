We are seeking an organized, client-focused Project Manager to lead the planning, delivery, and close-out of Sage 300 People (HR & Payroll) and related HRP implementation projects. This role bridges project governance and hands-on delivery, coordinating consultants, internal teams, third-party contractors, and clients to deliver projects according to plan.

As a Project Manager (HRP), you will partner with clients, HRP consultants, senior consultants, and internal teams to deliver high-quality implementation projects across multiple geographies, on time, within scope, and within budget. You will be expected to take ownership of project outcomes, contribute to process improvements, and act as a trusted advisor to clients, while continuing to develop your functional and technical understanding of HRP solutions. Software implementation experience is highly preferred; an HRP background is advantageous but not essential.

As a platinum Sage partner, TydeCo and our partner entity TydeCo U. specialize in delivering cutting-edge ERP and HRP solutions such as Sage 300 Cloud and Sage Intacct. We also offer Outsourced Accounting solutions to help clients streamline financial operations and scale with confidence. Our dedication to innovation, excellence, and client success ensures businesses not only adopt our solutions but thrive in competitive markets. If you're ready to lead in a fast paced, high-impact environment, we invite you to be part of our journey.

This position will report to the Team Manager for HRP.  This position can be located in either our TydeCo Cape Town office or remote within Gauteng, with occasional travel to Cape Town as needed.

Key Responsibilities

Global Support & Cross-Regional Collaboration

Operate within a global framework, ensuring adaptability to support teams, meet deadlines and participate in virtual meetings across multiple time zones based in Canada, South Africa, U.K. or the U.S., as needed.

Communicate and coordinate effectively with international stakeholders, adjusting to different time zones, schedules, and priorities.

Provide exceptional client-facing service, ensuring client issues are resolved in a timely and efficient manner in line with SLA requirements.

Provide recommendations for improvements to HRP implementation and project delivery processes tailored to client needs.

HRP Project Planning & Delivery

Develop project plans and objectives with all relevant stakeholders, ensuring technical feasibility for Sage 300 People and related HRP implementations.

Lead end-to-end HRP project delivery, from requirements gathering and system configuration through data migration, user acceptance testing, go-live, and post-launch stabilization.

Identify and manage project dependencies and the critical path, including payroll cut-offs, parallel runs, and statutory compliance requirements (e.g., SARS, HMRC).

Ensure that all projects are delivered on time, within scope, and within budget.

Proactively manage changes in project scope, schedule, and costs through agreed change control processes.

Ensure resource availability and allocation across consultants, internal teams, third-party contractors, and vendors.

Risk, Governance & Reporting

Track and report project progress, milestones, client deliverables, and KPIs using project management tools (e.g., Asana, Intacct).

Perform risk management to identify and minimize project risks, and implement contingency plans where required.

Create and maintain comprehensive project documentation, and contribute to internal process documentation and client-facing SOPs.

Support accurate project time allocation and timesheet oversight, maintaining visibility of billable and non-billable hours in line with leadership targets.

Client Engagement & Stakeholder Management

Develop and maintain strong relationships with clients, internal stakeholders, and third parties/vendors.

Coordinate and lead project team meetings, client status meetings, and weekly catch-ups with the team and Operations Manager.

Collaborate with sales/account management and consulting teams to align deliverables with scope and client expectations.

Collaborate with other departments to ensure that HRP processes are integrated with overall business operations.

Identify service expansion opportunities and areas of client value.

Continuous Learning & Development

Participate in departmental training sessions on HRP, Sage updates, and project management practices.

Build functional knowledge of Sage 300 People and related HRP systems to better guide project teams and clients.

Stay informed about industry trends, project management best practices, and regulatory changes to provide value-added insights.

Identify areas of opportunity to improve performance, recommend changes where necessary, and assist in implementing new processes.

Attend scheduled TydeCo team exercises and meetings in support of business efficiency.

Key Attributes

Client-Focused: Builds trust, manages expectations, and delivers high-quality service tailored to client needs.

Project Leadership: Leads and motivates project teams, sets clear goals and expectations, and keeps delivery on track.

Technical Competence: Quickly understands software implementation lifecycles; knowledge of HRP processes (implementation, configuration, and support) is advantageous.

Problem-Solving: Proactive, analytical, and solutions-oriented in addressing project and client challenges.

Organized & Detail-Oriented: Effectively manages multiple projects, dependencies, and deadlines with accuracy.

Collaborative: Works well with consultants, internal teams, vendors, and cross-functional stakeholders to achieve project goals.

Adaptable: Comfortable with fast-paced, dynamic environments, adjusting priorities as needed.

Professionalism & Communication: High emotional intelligence, strong verbal, written, and presentation skills, and a polished client presence.

Qualifications

At least 3 years of experience in project management; software implementation experience ( HR, Payroll, or similar) highly preferred.

Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Information Systems, Finance, Human Resources, or a related field preferred; equivalent professional experience considered.

Strong knowledge of project management methodologies, processes, and governance.

Solid technical background, with an understanding of, or hands-on experience with, Payroll, and HR systems.

Proven ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment.

Strong leadership, team management, problem-solving, and analytical skills.

Excellent communication, presentation, and interpersonal skills.

Experience working on client projects across multiple geographies is advantageous.

Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel and Word; familiarity with project management tools (e.g., Asana, Intacct).

Preferred

Experience in Sage 300 People Payroll, HR Premiere, or other HRP systems (advantageous, but not essential).

Professional project management certification (e.g., PMP, PRINCE2, or Agile).

Exposure to consulting, SaaS, or outsourcing environments.

Familiarity with regional HR/payroll legislation in South Africa, UK, or North America.

Experience with cloud-based project management or CRM tools.

Work Environment

Work can take place either in an open office setting or remote setting with the expectation to travel onsite based on business and management needs. This is a full-time position that requires the ability to work a flexible and regular fulltime schedule. It requires the ability to sit and work at a computer for extended periods of time and communicate effectively with a diverse audience in person, by phone and by computer.
