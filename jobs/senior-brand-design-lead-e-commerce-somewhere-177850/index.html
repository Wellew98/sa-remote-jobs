“Freelance” Senior Brand Design Lead (E-commerce)

LocationRemote (Global – South Africa & LATAM preferred)

Salary$1,500 -  $2,000 USD/month

Working HoursFull-time (40 hours/week) (Core hours: 8 AM – 5 PM CEST)

TimezoneFlexible with Minimum 3-hour overlap with CEST

To apply, please submit your video introduction and your portfolio showcasing your creative vision and experience.

About the Role

Are you ready to raise the bar on brand aesthetics and set a new visual s tandard? As our Art Director, you will act as the ultimate craftsmanship authority, translating our brand’s soul into a market-dominating look. We are looking for a visionary who can bridge the gap between "good" design and "affordable premium" excellence.

While you function as a core member of the team, you enjoy the autonomy of a remote setup.

Your Mission

  • Establish the "Signature Look": Define and enforce a cohesive visual language across Photo, Video, and UI. Your goal is to measurably increase brand desire, CTR, and conversion rates within your first six months.
  • Visual Asset Transformation: Strategically "upcycle" existing CGI and photography while directing the creation of new assets. You ensure seamless brand continuity while maximizing production efficiency.
  • Creative Excellence & Gatekeeping: Serve as the final visual authority for all touchpoints—from high-impact Meta Ads to our Digital Flagship Store.
  • Mentorship & AI Innovation: Raise the team’s output through technical guidance and "high-taste" mentorship. You will proactively integrate AI-driven workflows (MidJourney, Firefly) to keep the brand at the industry’s cutting edge.

Your Profile

  • Experience: 5–7+ years in premium/lifestyle branding, ideally within an agency or a high-growth in-house environment.
  • Education: You hold a degree in Communication Design, Visual Communication, Brand Design, Fine Arts, Digital Design or a comparable field
  • Market Insight: Prior experience working with German brands or clients . You should understand the cultural nuances, design aesthetics, and consumer behavior of the German market.
  • Technical Mastery: Expert-level skills in typography, grid systems, composition, and lighting. You operate with a "pixel-perfect" mindset.
  • Tool Stack: Mastery of Adobe Creative Cloud (specifically Lightroom for color grading), Figma , and AI tools like MidJourney or Adobe Firefly . Experience with Google Workspace is a plus.
  • Strategic Mindset: You don't just "make things pretty"—you understand how design drives trust and conversion throughout the customer journey.
  • Creative Conviction: You are comfortable challenging mediocrity, saying "no" to protect brand integrity, and providing hands-on guidance to junior designers.
  • Communication: Fluent English is required. You excel at translating abstract brand values into concrete visual guidelines.

Why Join Us?

  • Creative Authority: You aren't just following a handbook; you are writing it. You will define the signature look of a market leader.
  • Impactful Mission: Work for a purpose-driven company dedicated to making the professional world "happy, healthy, and productive."
  • Flexibility & Stability: Enjoy a fully remote, long-term collaboration with a 40-hour weekly commitment and reliable, structured coordination.
  • Professional Growth: Scale with one of Germany’s fastest-growing e-commerce startups and build a portfolio of high-impact, premium brand work.
