Sales Account Manager

Location:  Remote

Employment Type:  Full-Time

Reports To:  Sales Leadership / Client Success Leadership

Company Overview

We are a growth-focused B2B services organization partnering with industrial and manufacturing companies to help them  identify new customers, expand revenue, and diversify their sales pipeline .

Our culture is grounded in  excellence, execution, and measurable results .

Our team is composed of professionals who are  hungry, humble, and people-smart  — competitive, driven, and deeply collaborative.

We value team wins over individual spotlight.

Role Overview

We are seeking a  Sales Account Manager  to drive  revenue growth, pipeline development, and client relationship management  across multiple accounts.

This role blends  outbound prospecting, opportunity management, and account ownership . You will be responsible for generating leads, qualifying opportunities, managing pipelines, and maintaining strong client relationships.

This is an ideal role for someone who thrives in a  fast-paced, high-performance sales environment .
