About the Role

We’re hiring a remote Sales Development Rep  to support a fast-growing U.S. company specializing in cost segregation and energy tax services . This is a client-facing, outbound sales role ideal for motivated professionals with a strong background in prospecting and closing B2B deals.

You’ll be responsible for identifying and reaching out to new prospects, educating them on high-value tax strategies, and helping convert qualified leads into long-term clients.

What You’ll Be Doing

- Prospect and build outbound lead lists through research, outreach, cold calls, and email campaigns

- Engage decision-makers to understand their business needs and identify how our services can help

- Deliver consultative sales presentations on cost segregation and energy tax incentives

- Support proposal creation and deal negotiation alongside our U.S.-based internal team

- Maintain accurate records in the CRM and report on weekly/monthly sales activity

- Collaborate cross-functionally to ensure smooth handoffs to client onboarding teams

- Stay informed on industry trends and competitors to improve outbound strategies

What We’re Looking For

- 3+ years of outbound sales experience , ideally B2B or professional services

- Proven success in building and managing a pipeline of prospects

- Excellent communication, negotiation, and relationship-building skills

- Tech-savvy with experience using CRM tools and Microsoft Office

- Highly motivated, organized, and capable of working independently in a remote setting

- Willingness to learn technical concepts related to U.S. tax strategy (training provided)

- Prior experience selling to U.S. businesses is a plus

Why You’ll Love This Job

- Work directly with a U.S.-based leadership team and tax experts

- Competitive base salary plus commission structure

Fully remote

- Ongoing sales training and professional development

- Be part of a company that’s making a real financial impact for its clients

Ready to join a high-impact team and grow your career in specialized financial sales?
