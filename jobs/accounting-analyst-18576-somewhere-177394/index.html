Accounting Analyst (MSP / Professional Services)

LocationRemote

Employment TypeFull-Time

Reports ToFinance Leadership / CRO

About the Role

We are seeking a highly analytical Accounting Analyst to support the finance function of a growing Managed Services Provider (MSP).

This role sits at the intersection of accounting, financial analysis, and operational data . You will play a critical role in ensuring financial accuracy, reconciling complex revenue streams, and generating insights that drive business decisions.

This is not a transactional accounting role.

You will be expected to analyze, interpret, and improve financial performance — not just report on it.

What You’ll Own

1. Financial Reporting & Analysis

-

Prepare and analyze key financial reports, including

-

Revenue and cost analysis

- Gross margin reporting

-

Department-level performance

- Support monthly and quarterly close processes

- Identify trends, anomalies, and performance drivers across the business

2. PSA ↔ Accounting Reconciliation

- Own reconciliation between PSA systems (e.g., Autotask, ConnectWise, Halo) and QuickBooks Online

-

Validate and ensure accuracy of

- Recurring revenue (MRR) postings

-

Project-based revenue

- Time & materials billing

- Investigate discrepancies and resolve data inconsistencies across systems

3. Revenue & Profitability Analysis

-

Analyze profitability across

-

Managed services contracts

- Projects and implementations

-

Support and service lines

- Evaluate margin performance and identify opportunities for improvement

- Provide actionable insights to leadership on revenue quality and cost efficiency

4. Data Analysis & Financial Modeling

- Build and maintain financial models and reporting dashboards (primarily in Excel)

-

Analyze key operational and financial metrics, including

- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) growth

-

Customer revenue trends and cohort behavior

- Service utilization and capacity

-

Cost of service delivery

- Translate raw data into clear, decision-ready insights

5. Process Improvement & Scalability

- Improve workflows between PSA systems and accounting platforms

- Help design scalable reporting and forecasting frameworks

- Identify inefficiencies and implement process improvements

- Support finance leadership with ad hoc analysis and strategic initiatives

What We’re Looking For

This role requires someone who is both detail-oriented and commercially aware — someone who understands how financial data connects to operational performance.

Required Qualifications

- 4+ years of experience in accounting, financial analysis, or similar roles

- Strong hands-on experience with QuickBooks Online

-

Advanced Excel skills, including

-

Pivot tables

- Financial modeling

-

Data analysis and manipulation

- Experience performing multi-system financial reconciliations

- Solid understanding of professional services or service-based accounting models

Preferred Qualifications

- Experience working within a Managed Services Provider (MSP)

-

Familiarity with PSA tools such as

-

Autotask

- ConnectWise

-

HaloPSA

- Experience working with recurring revenue models (MRR / ARR)

- Accounting degree or progress toward CPA or equivalent certification

Key Success Metrics

- Accuracy and reliability of financial reporting

- PSA-to-accounting reconciliation accuracy and completeness

- Speed and efficiency of monthly close processes

- Quality and clarity of financial insights delivered

- Measurable improvements in financial processes and reporting

What This Role Is Not

- Not a bookkeeping or data-entry role

- Not limited to reporting — analysis and insight generation are core expectations

- Not a siloed accounting function — you will work across finance and operations

Why This Role Matters

In an MSP environment, financial complexity increases quickly due to:

-

Recurring revenue models

- Project-based work

- Multi-system data (PSA + accounting)

This role ensures financial accuracy, operational visibility, and scalable reporting — all of which are critical to sustainable growth.
