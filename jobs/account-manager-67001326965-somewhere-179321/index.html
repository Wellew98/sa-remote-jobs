PositionAccount Manager / Insurance Service Representative

Work Hours (Client) : Monday to Friday, Hours: 8:30am to 5pm EST

Pay Range$ 1,000 - $ 1,700/ month

(varies based on skill set and experience level)

Location of SearchSouth Africa, Latin America or Philippines

Work LocationRemote

Company Overview

Our client is an independent insurance agency that helps businesses and individuals find affordable, reliable coverage. The agency specializes in workers' compensation insurance for businesses and also offers personal lines such as auto insurance, with a focus on protecting clients, their employees, and their families.

Role Overview

We are seeking an experienced, detail-oriented Account Manager / Insurance Service Representative to manage day-to-day client servicing in a fast-paced environment. This is a primary, client-facing role—not a back-office support position. You will serve as the dedicated point of contact for policyholders, stepping directly into an established workflow to ensure accurate, responsive, and professional account maintenance with minimal ramp-up time.

Duties and Responsibilities

  • Client Service & Account Management
  • Serve as the main point of contact for assigned client accounts.
  • Respond promptly and professionally to client service requests by phone and email.
  • Answer questions about coverage, policy details, billing, and changes.
  • Build strong, long-term client relationships that support retention.
  • Certificates & Endorsements
  • Issue certificates of insurance (COIs) accurately and quickly.
  • Process endorsements and policy changes, such as adding or removing drivers, vehicles, employees, locations, or additional insureds.
  • Coordinate with insurance carriers to complete policy changes.
  • Confirm that all changes are reflected correctly in the client's policy records.
  • Quoting & New Business
  • Gather client and business information needed for quotes.
  • Prepare and submit quotes for workers' compensation, commercial, and personal lines policies.
  • Present quote options to clients and answer questions.
  • Enter new policies accurately into the agency management system.
  • Renewals
  • Manage upcoming renewals proactively to prevent lapses in coverage.
  • Review renewal terms and remarket policies when needed.
  • Communicate renewal options to clients and secure renewal decisions on time.
  • Process renewals and update policy records.
  • Policy Entry, Invoicing & Records
  • Enter and maintain accurate policy information in NowCerts and Momentum AMS.
  • Prepare and send client invoices and track payments.
  • Keep client files, documents, and notes organized and current.
  • Follow up on outstanding items so nothing is missed.

Minimum Qualifications

  • 2–5 years preferred in insurance account management, account servicing, or a similar client-facing support role.
  • Hands-on experience handling certificates, endorsements, renewals, and policy servicing.
  • Comfortable working on an AMS platform.
  • Strong attention to detail and follow-through.
  • Professional written and verbal English communication skills.
  • Ability to work independently and manage day-to-day tasks without heavy oversight.
  • Reliable remote workspace setup (High-speed internet & professional headset)

Preferred Qualifications

  • Preferred Sourcing Regions: Philippines, South Africa, Latin America.
  • Experience with NowCerts, Momentum, or comparable AMS systems.
  • Workers' compensation, commercial lines, or personal auto insurance experience.
  • Active insurance license.

What This Role Offers

  • A full Account Manager role with real ownership of client accounts.
  • The chance to make an immediate impact at an independent agency.
  • Work with established agency management tools, including NowCerts and Momentum AMS.
  • A stable full-time remote position within a supportive team

Ideal Candidate Profile

  • The ideal candidate has real, hands-on account servicing experience and can function as a legitimate account manager, not just a back-office assistant.
  • They are comfortable working in a client-facing or semi-client-facing capacity, and are reliable, organized, and proactive with strong English proficiency.
  • They adapt quickly to internal workflows and handle core account servicing functions with minimal disruption.
  • They know the work inside and out, from issuing certificates and processing endorsements to preparing quotes and managing renewals.
  • They are responsive, accurate, and comfortable handling a full book of accounts without needing close supervision.
  • They communicate clearly with clients and carriers and keep every policy record up to date. Most importantly, they are dependable, organized, and committed to making sure clients stay covered and satisfied.

Success Metrics

Success in this role will be measured by

  • Speed and accuracy in responding to client service requests.
  • Turnaround time on certificates and endorsements.
  • On-time renewals with no coverage lapses.
  • Client retention rates.
  • Accuracy of policy entry and invoicing.
  • Number of quotes prepared and converted to policies.
  • Client satisfaction and quality of interactions.
