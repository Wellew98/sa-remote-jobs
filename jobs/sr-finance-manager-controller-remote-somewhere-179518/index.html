Senior Finance Manager / Financial Controller

A growing US residential real estate developer and general contractor that builds new homes from the ground up is hiring a remote Senior Finance Manager or Financial Controller. You will own the whole finance function, from bookkeeping in QuickBooks to cash forecasting and advice for leadership.

About the role

You will be responsible for the company’s financial past, present, and future. The past is clean, accurate books in QuickBooks Online. The present is real-time cash management across accounts payable and receivable, title company funds, and construction draws. The future is projections that guide decisions on acquisitions, sales, and tax planning.

Cash moves in large waves, with big inflows when homes close and steady outflows during construction, so up-to-date visibility and forecasting are essential. This is a demanding role for a true financial partner to the C-suite: someone who raises issues early, brings recommendations without being asked, and starts conversations rather than waiting for them. The title will be Senior Finance Manager or Financial Controller, depending on the seniority of the person hired.

Required Qualifications

  • 8+ years of increasingly senior accounting experience, including running a company’s books, month-end close, and management reporting on your own. Controller-level experience is strongly preferred.
  • Proven ability to take over disorganized books, clean them up, and set up a repeatable accounting and reporting process on your own
  • Strong knowledge of accrual accounting, balance sheet reconciliations, owner equity, loan accounting, cash flow forecasting, and preparing financial statements
  • Advanced QuickBooks Online skills, including setting up accounts, classes, projects, workflows, custom reports, and dashboards
  • Strong written and spoken English, with the confidence to explain results and lead conversations with C-suite leaders
  • Highly proactive, and able to request documents, escalate discrepancies, and make sound recommendations without daily supervision

Preferred Qualifications

  • Construction or real estate development experience with job costing, draws, project profitability, and construction financing
  • Experience supporting US companies and working with US CPAs, with knowledge of US GAAP
  • Experience in a founder-led or small business where finance covers many roles
  • Advanced Excel and Google Sheets skills for cost models, project pro formas, and automation
  • Experience with 13-week cash forecasts and residential development accounting
  • An accounting degree or qualification. A CPA, CA, or equivalent is a plus.

Responsibilities

  • Own cash flow visibility, tracking money coming in from sales and closings and money going out on active construction
  • Build and maintain 13-week and rolling cash forecasts, flag funding gaps early, and bring recommendations to leadership
  • Coordinate with title companies on releasing funds and outstanding balances, and keep the C-suite informed on timing
  • Work with leadership and the CPA on budgets, expected inflows, and planning around upcoming projects
  • Run the full accounts payable and receivable cycle, reconcile balances, track loan payment schedules, and record and allocate incoming funds correctly
  • Coordinate monthly construction draws with project managers. This includes reconciling sworn statements, draw submissions, lien waivers, lender receipts, and payments, and investigating any shortfalls.
  • Own bookkeeping and the monthly, quarterly, and year-end close in QuickBooks Online, keeping records ready for the CPA at all times
  • Track job costs for every active project and produce a monthly profitability report that explains variances and sets out next steps
  • Prepare for tax season with the US CPA and manage financial and insurance audits
  • Lead regular financial review meetings, set clear controls and procedures, and keep improving the company’s finance systems and tools

Working arrangement

This is a full-time, fully remote contractor role, with candidates in South Africa or LATAM preferred. You must overlap with US Central Time business hours, with exact hours confirmed during hiring, and be available to lead regular video meetings. You need reliable high-speed internet, a computer in good working order, and a quiet, professional workspace. Authorized company staff approve and release payments. This role provides the information and coordination those decisions depend on.

Who thrives in this role

  • Takes full ownership of results instead of waiting to be told what to do
  • Brings forecasts, risks, and recommendations to leadership before problems become crises
  • Builds and documents systems that leave the finance function stronger than they found it
  • Senior enough to build a forecast model and still happy to reconcile a receipt
  • Questions assumptions and pushes back when the numbers do not add up
