About Huzzle

At Huzzle, we connect exceptional talent with top opportunities at leading companies across the UK, US, Canada, Europe, and Australia. Our clients include fast-growing startups and established businesses in SaaS, MarTech, FinTech, and EdTech.

Unlike traditional outsourcing, you’ll be placed directly with a client as a core member of their team—giving you real ownership, career progression, and long-term stability.

Role TypeFull-time

EngagementIndependent Contractor

Job Summary

Are you a high-energy hunter who thrives on the phones? This team is at the forefront of the creator economy revolution, connecting innovative brands with world-class creators to drive unprecedented growth. As a Sales Development Representative, you will be the engine of the sales pipeline, engaging marketing leaders at high-growth e-commerce brands and qualifying the opportunities that shape the future of the company. If you are a disciplined, high-volume caller who loves the thrill of the chase and wants to play a pivotal role in a fast-paced creative environment, this is your stage.

Key Responsibilities

Execute 200+ cold calls per day across dedicated, protected call blocks to build a robust sales pipeline

Engage decision-makers (Heads of Growth, Marketing Directors, Founders) to identify pain points and qualify leads based on fit and urgency

Book 10–15 qualified meetings per week, ensuring calendar invites are sent immediately and handoff notes are detailed for the Account Executive team

Maintain a pristine CRM by logging every call outcome, building verified call lists daily via Apollo.io, and managing follow-ups/callbacks with precision

Take ownership of the meeting lifecycle by confirming appointments 24 hours in advance and aggressively re-booking no-shows the same day

Utilize proven talk tracks to navigate gatekeepers, handle objections, and communicate the value of creator-led marketing

1–3+ years of experience as an SDR/BDR with a heavy focus on high-volume cold calling (150-200+ dials per day)

A solid understanding of e-commerce marketing fundamentals, including paid ads, SEO, ROAS, and the Shopify ecosystem

Experience prospecting SMB and mid-market e-commerce brands, ideally generating $10M–$200M in annual revenue

Experience engaging decision-makers, including Marketing Directors, Heads of Growth, E-commerce Managers, Digital Marketing Managers, and founders

Good understanding of digital marketing services, including paid advertising, SEO, email/SMS marketing, conversion rate optimisation, ROAS, and Shopify

Strong cold calling, objection handling, gatekeeper navigation, and appointment-setting skills

Experience using CRMs and sales engagement tools such as HubSpot, Salesforce, Apollo, Aircall, Orum, or Nooks

Ability to qualify prospects, identify business needs, and secure meetings with relevant decision-makers

Strong CRM discipline, including accurate call logging, timely meeting invitations, follow-ups, and AE handover notes

🌎 Fully remote role with flexible working hours

🚀 Work with high-growth international companies

📈 Clear career progression to Account Executive and beyond

🎯 Performance-based bonuses and incentives

🧠 Ongoing training, mentorship, and sales development support
