Content Producer

LocationRemote Employment: Full-time IC · Long-term · Some overlap with U.S. Eastern business hours

About the Company

We are a rapidly growing U.S.-based consultancy that designs and builds modern CRM and go-to-market systems for private capital firms and B2B technology companies. We are one of the highest-rated firms in our category, working with an ambitious, high-performing client base that expects excellence in everything we deliver.

We are an AI-first company — we build automation and AI into how we operate internally and how we serve clients — and our team is fully distributed. We move fast, hold a high bar, and promote quickly.

About the Role

We are building a content engine, and this is one of its first hires.

Our leadership has deep expertise and a strong point of view, but not the time to produce content at the volume our growth demands. This role exists to close that gap: to take raw material — founder brain dumps, call and webinar recordings, half-formed ideas — and turn it into a steady stream of polished, on-brand content that builds our audience and drives pipeline.

This is a hands-on production role. You will edit, create, package, and publish across our channels, working closely with leadership to translate their voice and expertise into content that performs. As the function grows, there is real room to take on more scope and ownership.

Key Responsibilities

Video Production & Editing

  • Edit long-form source material (webinars, live build sessions, interviews) into polished, publish-ready pieces
  • Cut long-form content into short-form clips optimized for LinkedIn, YouTube, and other social channels
  • Add captions, motion graphics, and consistent on-brand styling

Content Creation & Repurposing

  • Turn founder brain dumps, recordings, and existing assets into multiple content formats — clips, posts, graphics, and written snippets
  • Adapt and maintain a consistent brand voice across everything you produce
  • Keep a healthy content pipeline so channels never go quiet

Publishing & Scheduling

  • Schedule and publish content across LinkedIn, YouTube, and our content hub
  • Own and maintain the content calendar

Design & Assets

  • Create supporting graphics, thumbnails, and visual assets
  • Keep all output aligned to brand standards

Performance & Iteration

  • Track engagement and performance, and iterate toward what works
  • Bring ideas for new formats and content plays

AI-Assisted Workflows

  • Use AI tools to speed up editing, repurposing, and drafting without sacrificing quality

Required Qualifications

  • 3+ years in content production and/or video editing, ideally in a B2B or agency environment
  • Strong proficiency with video editing tools (e.g., Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, CapCut, After Effects)
  • Proven experience producing short-form social content, especially for LinkedIn
  • A strong design eye and ability to create clean graphics and thumbnails (Figma, Canva, or Adobe)
  • Strong written English and an instinct for voice, tone, and pacing
  • A portfolio or reel demonstrating range across video and social formats
  • Self-directed with fast, reliable turnaround
  • Meaningful overlap with U.S. Eastern business hours for collaboration

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience creating content for B2B tech, SaaS, or professional services
  • Comfort quickly learning technical subject matter (CRM, AI, go-to-market)
  • Experience building content from executive or founder raw material, or personal-brand / thought-leadership content
  • Familiarity with AI content tools for video, audio, and repurposing
  • Experience with scheduling tools and light content analytics

Ideal Candidate Profile

You are

  • Prolific and fast — you produce high volume without letting quality slip
  • Tasteful — you know what belongs in a piece, not just how to make it
  • A self-starter — hand you a raw brain dump and you run with it
  • Voice-adaptable — you can capture and carry someone else's tone convincingly
  • Organized — you keep a content calendar and multiple formats moving at once
  • Curious — you want to understand B2B tech and AI well enough to make it compelling

Why Join Us

  • Own the production of content for a company that treats content as a core growth engine
  • Work directly with a founder who has a strong, distinctive voice
  • A modern, AI-first environment where creative ideas are valued and tested
  • A genuine growth path as the content function scales around you
