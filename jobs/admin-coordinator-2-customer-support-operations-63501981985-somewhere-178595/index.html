About the Role

We’re looking for a highly organized, proactive Executive Administrative Coordinator to support multi-location franchise operations. This person will own core administrative workflows, help keep communication moving across the business, and ensure important operational tasks are completed accurately and on time.

This is a hands-on, execution-focused role for someone who is comfortable working inside back-end systems, managing email communication, and keeping multiple stakeholders aligned.

Key Responsibilities

  • Manage and triage all incoming email communication across multiple franchise inboxes
  • Draft, review, and send responses to approximately 90% of routine emails
  • Forward escalated or location-specific items to the appropriate internal team member
  • Work inside the franchise management system to support website content and program registration setup
  • Send scheduled program communications and email updates when programs are in season
  • Support HubSpot marketing email execution, including scheduling and sending campaigns using established templates and lists
  • Track onboarding requirements for new workers, including:
  • Background check completion
  • Payroll/onboarding documentation status
  • Follow-up reminders and completion tracking
  • Coordinate with internal leaders to ensure processes are completed accurately
  • Maintain organization across multiple systems and recurring workflows

Qualifications & Skills

Required

  • Strong organizational and follow-through skills
  • Excellent written communication
  • Comfortable working in administrative systems and CRMs
  • Able to learn new software quickly
  • Detail-oriented and dependable
  • Prior experience in operations, executive support, coordination, or administrative roles preferred
  • Ability to manage repetitive workflows with accuracy

Preferred

  • Experience with email inbox management
  • Familiarity with CRM or marketing platforms like HubSpot
  • Background in operations support, admin coordination, or virtual assistance
  • Experience working with multiple stakeholders or franchise-style businesses

What Success Looks Like

  • Email inboxes are managed promptly and accurately
  • Routine communication is handled without constant supervision
  • Onboarding and background check tracking is consistently up to date
  • Scheduled marketing communications go out on time
  • Internal teams are informed when action is needed
  • The role becomes a reliable operational support function across the business

Important: To ensure a smooth application process, please submit your resume or CV in English. We require all candidates to have a strong understanding of the English language and be able to communicate effectively verbally and in writing.
