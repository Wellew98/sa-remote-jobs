About the Company

A high-end automotive customisation business based in Colorado Springs, Colorado is expanding its sales function and is looking for an experienced Sales Manager to join its growing team.

The business specialises in

Paint Protection Film (PPF), window tint, ceramic coating, paint correction, and related automotive customisation services .

With more than

1,100 five-star reviews , higher-tier services averaging approximately $6,000 per ticket , and 500–700 inbound leads per month , the business has established a strong reputation and is continuing to expand into additional locations.

This is an opportunity to become the first key hire in a new dedicated sales function, with the potential to build and lead a team of SDRs as the business grows.

The Opportunity

The successful candidate will initially operate as a hands-on salesperson , managing inbound leads and closing customers across the company's automotive services.

Once individual sales performance has been established, the role will transition into a player-coach leadership position , responsible for training, supporting and managing a team of SDRs.

We are therefore looking for someone who can close today and lead tomorrow .

Phase 1 – Individual Sales

Manage a high volume of inbound leads.

Qualify prospects and understand their automotive requirements.

Quote customers using established tiered pricing.

Sell PPF, window tint, ceramic coating and paint correction services.

Handle objections confidently and professionally.

Follow up consistently with prospective customers.

Convert inbound opportunities into booked appointments and sales.

Build trust through genuine automotive product knowledge.

Provide a warm, consultative customer experience.

Maintain accurate CRM records and ensure no leads are lost.

Phase 2 – Sales Leadership

As the business expands, responsibilities will develop to include:

Training and onboarding new SDRs.

Coaching team members on inbound sales conversations.

Monitoring individual and team performance.

Establishing sales best practices and processes.

Providing call coaching and constructive feedback.

Developing automotive product knowledge across the team.

Managing accountability and sales performance.

Helping build a scalable internal sales function.

Key Requirements

Mandatory Automotive Experience

Candidates

must have hands-on experience within automotive customisation or a closely related automotive environment.

Relevant experience includes

Paint Protection Film (PPF)

Window tint

Ceramic coating

Vehicle wrapping

Automotive detailing

Paint correction

Automotive aftermarket products

Performance and vehicle accessories

Automotive sales

Automotive installation

Automotive installers looking to transition from the tools into sales are encouraged to apply.

General sales candidates without genuine automotive experience will not be considered.

Sales Experience

Approximately 5+ years of sales experience preferred.

Experience in inbound, phone-based or high-ticket sales.

Proven ability to handle objections and close customers.

Strong follow-up and lead-management skills.

Comfortable working with high lead volumes.

Consultative rather than aggressive sales approach.

Demonstrable history of achieving sales targets.

Leadership Potential

Experience mentoring, training or supporting colleagues.

Strong communication and coaching ability.

Potential to manage a small SDR team within approximately 6–12 months.

Comfortable being accountable for individual and team performance.

Able to lead by example while remaining hands-on.

Communication Skills

Excellent spoken and written English.

Naturally conversational and personable.

Able to build rapport quickly over the phone.

Confident communicating with customers independently.

Able to explain automotive products and services clearly.

Professional, engaging and easy to speak with.

Ideal Personal Attributes

The ideal candidate will

Genuinely love cars and the automotive industry.

Be warm, naturally conversational and personable.

Have a strong work ethic.

Be self-motivated and comfortable working remotely.

Demonstrate exceptional attention to detail.

Take ownership without requiring constant supervision.

Be honest about their knowledge and limitations.

Ask questions rather than bluffing.

Have a strong commitment to quality.

Be continuously focused on improvement.

Core Values

The company places strong emphasis on

Showing up every day and being 1% better than yesterday.

Impeccable character.

A relentless standard of excellence.

Technology

GoHighLevel

WhatsApp

The key priority is

automotive knowledge, sales ability, communication and leadership potential , rather than previous experience with specific software.

What Success Looks Like

First 90 Days

The successful candidate will

Effectively manage the inbound lead volume.

Meet agreed sales and closing targets.

Demonstrate strong knowledge of automotive services.

Maintain a clean and accurate CRM.

Ensure no leads or follow-ups are missed.

Deliver a consistently high-quality customer experience.

Demonstrate strong product credibility and sales performance.

Longer Term

The successful candidate will progress into a leadership position by:

Training and developing SDRs.

Establishing effective sales processes.

Maintaining high conversion standards.

Coaching team members.

Taking ownership of team performance.

Supporting the growth of a scalable internal sales function.
