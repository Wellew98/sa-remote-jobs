About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a highly organized and execution-focused

Investment Banking Associate

to support deal operations, CRM management, investor follow-ups, reporting, and pipeline coordination.

This is

not a traditional investment banking role . There is no heavy financial modeling, aggressive client-facing sales pressure, or deal origination requirement .

Instead, you’ll serve as the operational backbone of the deal pipeline, working closely with leadership to ensure every opportunity, investor interaction, follow-up, task, and next step is accurately tracked and executed on time.

The role is ideal for someone with strong experience in HubSpot CRM, deal pipeline management, operations, executive support, or finance-related coordination who thrives on organization and consistent follow-through.

What You’ll Own

Deal Pipeline & HubSpot CRM Management

Own the day-to-day organization and maintenance of the deal pipeline inside

HubSpot CRM

.

You will

Track inbound deal and investor interest.

Maintain accurate deal stages, notes, tasks, and next steps.

Record investor responses and communication history.

Create and manage follow-up tasks and reminders.

Proactively follow up on slow-moving opportunities.

Monitor pipeline movement and identify stalled deals.

Maintain clean, accurate, and up-to-date CRM records.

Flag urgent opportunities and priorities for leadership.

Manage follow-up activity across long deal cycles of 6–12+ months .

Ensure no opportunity disappears because of a missed follow-up or incomplete CRM record.

Deal Operations & Execution Support

Support leadership with day-to-day deal execution and coordination.

Track open tasks, deliverables, deadlines, and follow-up requirements.

Maintain Excel and Google Sheets deal trackers.

Organize deal information into clear, structured summaries.

Prepare pipeline and operational updates for leadership.

Help maintain visibility across active and prospective opportunities.

Track investor responses and required next steps.

Follow up professionally when conversations become inactive.

Coordinate communication between leadership and external stakeholders.

Ensure important conversations receive timely responses.

Maintain accurate communication histories within HubSpot.

Coordinate investor meetings, leadership calls, and deal discussions.

Manage scheduling across multiple stakeholders.

Ensure meeting invitations and details are accurate.

Track post-meeting action items.

Ensure agreed-upon next steps are entered into the CRM and completed.

Administrative & Operational Coordination

Track deadlines, deliverables, and recurring follow-up cadences.

Maintain organized operational workflows.

Support leadership with reporting and execution coordination.

Improve documentation and reporting consistency.

Identify missing information, overdue tasks, or workflow gaps before they become problems.
