About the Business

A well-established US-based construction business specialising in concrete work, excavation and demolition services is seeking an experienced Bookkeeper to take full ownership of its financial operations.

The business requires someone who can work independently, manage the day-to-day bookkeeping function, and ensure all financial records remain accurate and up to date.

This is an opportunity for an experienced professional who is confident making decisions with minimal supervision and enjoys taking ownership of their work.

Role Overview

The Bookkeeper will be responsible for managing the full bookkeeping function of the business, ensuring accounts are accurate, payments are made on time, and financial information is always current.

The successful candidate will work directly with the owner and collaborate with team members when required to gather financial information and reports.

Key Responsibilities

Manage Accounts Receivable (AR)

Manage Accounts Payable (AP)

Process billing and invoicing

Perform bank and account reconciliations

Manage incoming bills and payment schedules

Maintain accurate financial records

Update and manage bookkeeping systems

Pull reports and financial information when required

Ensure nothing falls behind or gets paid late

Identify discrepancies and proactively resolve issues

Tools & Systems

QuickBooks (essential)

Microsoft Excel (advanced level)

Custom Excel spreadsheets that integrate with QuickBooks

Requirements

Essential

Several years of hands-on bookkeeping experience

Strong experience managing AR, AP, billing and reconciliations end-to-end

Advanced QuickBooks proficiency

Strong Microsoft Excel skills

Excellent organisational and time-management skills

Ability to work independently with minimal supervision

Strong attention to detail

Excellent communication skills

Advantageous

Experience working with US-based businesses

Experience working New York/Eastern Time hours

Construction or trades industry experience

Experience working with customised bookkeeping systems and workflows

Ideal Candidate Profile

The ideal candidate is

Self-directed and highly organised

Proactive and dependable

Comfortable making decisions independently

Detail-oriented and analytical

A strong communicator who escalates issues early

Confident managing a complete bookkeeping function without constant oversight
