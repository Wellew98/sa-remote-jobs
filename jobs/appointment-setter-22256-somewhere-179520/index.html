About the Company

Our client is a digital marketing agency that works exclusively with construction companies, especially in excavation, septic and utilities, and heavy landscaping. The agency acts as a full-service, long-term marketing team for its clients, handling branding, websites, SEO, Google and Meta ads, graphic design, and content.

The agency already works successfully with remote team members across several countries. Until now, its sales lead has handled the entire sales process alone, from prospecting to closing. To free up time and fill the calendar with more qualified sales calls, the agency is hiring its first Appointment Setter.

Role Overview

We are seeking an Appointment Setter to support the sales team with prospecting, lead qualification, follow-up and nurturing, and booking qualified appointments.

This individual will work through hot, warm, and cold lead lists, including people who already follow the company's content and social media and a large list of scraped prospects. They will also research and build new prospect lists. Their goal is to qualify each lead carefully and book high-quality sales calls onto the sales lead's calendar, so the sales team can spend its time closing.

The agency is selective about the clients it works with, so careful qualification is essential. The ideal candidate is a hungry, organized appointment setter with 2 to 5 years of experience who wants to grow with the company. As the agency's first person in this role, they will help shape how its appointment setting works.

Key Responsibilities

Prospecting

  • Research and build new prospect lists of construction companies that fit the agency's ideal client profile.
  • Work through existing lead lists, including warm audiences and cold scraped lists.
  • Reach out to prospects by phone, email, and other channels.

Lead Qualification

  • Qualify leads against the agency's criteria before booking a call.
  • Understand each prospect's business, needs, and readiness to invest in marketing.
  • Make sure only qualified leads reach the sales team's calendar.

Follow-Up & Nurturing

  • Follow up consistently with leads who aren't ready to book yet.
  • Nurture warm leads over time until they're ready for a sales conversation.
  • Re-engage inactive or cold leads.

Appointment Setting

  • Book qualified sales appointments directly onto the sales lead's calendar.
  • Send confirmations and reminders to improve show rates.
  • Follow up with no-shows and reschedule them.

CRM & Reporting

  • Track all leads, outreach, and outcomes in GoHighLevel.
  • Keep pipeline stages and contact records accurate and up to date.
  • Report on activity, appointments booked, show rates, and lead quality.
  • Use AI tools such as Claude to work more efficiently.

Required Qualifications

  • 2 to 5 years of experience in appointment setting, SDR, or inside sales roles.
  • Proven experience prospecting, qualifying leads, and booking appointments.
  • Track record of hitting activity and appointment targets.
  • Excellent spoken and written English, with a confident, professional phone presence.
  • Experience working with a CRM to manage leads and pipelines.
  • Strong follow-up habits and organization.
  • Hungry, motivated, and eager to grow with a company.
  • Based in South Africa, Latin America, or the Philippines.
  • Reliable high-speed internet connection and a quiet, dedicated remote workspace with a professional headset.
  • Availability to work full-time, Monday to Friday.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with GoHighLevel.
  • Experience with ClickUp.
  • Experience using AI tools such as Claude in sales work.
  • Experience setting appointments for a marketing agency or B2B services company.
  • Experience selling to construction, trades, or home services businesses.
  • Experience building prospect lists or scraping lead data.

Technology & Tools

Candidates should be comfortable working with or quickly learning:

  • GoHighLevel (CRM)
  • ClickUp
  • Claude and other AI tools
  • Lead research and list-building tools
  • Phone, email, and SMS tools
  • Google Workspace / Microsoft Office

  • The chance to be the agency's first appointment setter and help shape the role.
  • Hot, warm, and cold lead lists to work from.
  • A team that already works successfully with remote talent.
  • Room to grow with a growing agency.
  • A stable full-time remote position.

Ideal Candidate Profile

The ideal candidate is a driven, organized appointment setter who takes pride in booking calls that are worth the sales team's time.

They are comfortable on the phone, persistent with follow-up, and careful about qualifying every lead before it reaches the calendar. They know how to work warm audiences and cold lists, and they can build new prospect lists when needed.

They keep the CRM clean and share clear updates on what's working. Most importantly, they are hungry, coachable, and excited to grow with the company.

Success Metrics

Success in this role will be measured by

  • Number of qualified appointments booked.
  • Show rate for booked appointments.
  • Close rate of appointments set.
  • Lead quality and fit with the agency's ideal client profile.
  • Consistency of follow-up and nurturing.
  • Number of new prospects researched and added.
  • Accuracy of GoHighLevel records.
