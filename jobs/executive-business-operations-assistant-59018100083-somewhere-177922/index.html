Executive Business Operations Assistant

(AI-Enabled)

Remote | Full-Time | CST Overlap Preferred

About the Company

Satori Capital is a values-driven investment firm focused on building enduring businesses and developing exceptional leaders. With a collaborative, high-trust culture and a strong emphasis on innovation, Satori is actively evolving into an AI-first organization , integrating cutting-edge tools to enhance productivity, decision-making, and operational efficiency.

The Opportunity

Satori Capital is seeking a high-caliber Executive Assistant & Business Operations Specialist to support a senior partner and help streamline internal operations.

This is not a traditional EA role .

You will begin by owning high-volume executive support for a demanding stakeholder, then evolve into a process-driven, AI-enabled operator —identifying inefficiencies, implementing automation, and improving workflows across the organization.

This role is ideal for someone with

  • Exceptional intellectual horsepower
  • Strong business judgment
  • A proactive, problem-solving mindset
  • Deep curiosity and fluency with AI tools

Key Responsibilities

Executive Support (Primary Focus Initially)

  • Manage complex calendars, scheduling, and communications for a senior executive
  • Prioritize and triage high-volume requests with minimal direction
  • Prepare materials, coordinate meetings, and ensure follow-through on action items
  • Act as a strategic partner, not just a task executor
  • Navigate ambiguity and clarify incomplete or fast-moving instructions

AI & Workflow Optimization

  • Leverage AI tools (e.g., Claude and similar platforms) to:
  • Automate repetitive tasks
  • Improve communication workflows
  • Increase speed and accuracy of deliverables
  • Identify opportunities to reduce manual work across the organization
  • Build systems that enhance executive productivity without sacrificing quality

Business Operations (Expanded Scope Over Time)

  • Identify bottlenecks and inefficiencies in internal processes
  • Implement systems and workflows to improve operational performance
  • Support cross-functional coordination and execution
  • Contribute to scaling internal processes across portfolio companies

Ideal Candidate Profile

Core Traits

  • High IQ / intellectual horsepower – able to quickly understand complex concepts
  • Grit and persistence – thrives in ambiguity and figures things out independently
  • Proactive mindset – anticipates needs rather than reacting to instructions
  • Strong communication skills – especially with fast-moving or unclear stakeholders
  • High ownership mentality – treats the business as their own

Experience

  • 3–7+ years in:
  • Executive Assistant, Chief of Staff, or Business Operations roles
  • Experience supporting senior executives in high-performance environments
  • Exposure to startups, private equity, consulting, or similar fast-paced settings preferred

Technical Skills

  • Advanced proficiency in:
  • Google Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Docs)
  • Slack
  • Microsoft Office (especially Excel)
  • Smartsheet or similar tools
  • Strong familiarity with AI tools (especially Claude or similar LLMs)
  • Comfort implementing tools, systems, and automations

Working Style & Environment

  • High-trust, high-performance culture
  • Fast-paced environment with evolving priorities
  • Requires comfort working with executives who:
  • Move quickly
  • May not always provide perfectly structured direction
  • Emphasis on autonomy, accountability, and continuous improvement

Compensation & Benefits

  • Flexible and generous PTO policy, including:
  • U.S. holidays
  • Extended time off during year-end
  • Long-term growth opportunity into broader operations or leadership roles

Location & Schedule

  • Remote
  • CST time zone overlap strongly preferred

What Success Looks Like

  • The executive’s workload is streamlined and highly organized
  • Processes become faster, more efficient, and increasingly automated
  • The role evolves from task execution → systems ownership → operational impact
  • You become a trusted operator within the organization

Why This Role is Unique

  • Blend of EA + Ops + AI transformation
  • Opportunity to help shape an AI-first organization
  • High visibility with leadership
  • Clear path to expanded scope and impact
