About Huzzle

At Huzzle, we connect exceptional talent with top opportunities at leading companies across the UK, US, Canada, Europe, and Australia. Our clients include startups, digital agencies, and tech platforms in industries such as SaaS, MarTech, FinTech, and EdTech. Unlike an outsourcing agency, we place you directly with a client where you’re hired in-house as a valued member of their team.

Role TypeFull-time

EngagementIndependent Contractor

Job Summary

This is a high-impact role for a performance-driven specialist looking to join a fast-growing agency at the forefront of the UK renewable energy sector. We are a no-nonsense, results-oriented team dedicated to delivering high-quality lead generation and exceptional ROI for our clients. As a Media Buyer, you will be the engine of our performance delivery, managing the end-to-end lifecycle of paid campaigns primarily on Meta, with a secondary focus on Google. If you thrive on data-driven optimization, funnel architecture, and scaling campaigns that move the needle for B2C and B2B business owners, this is the place to build your career.

Key Responsibilities

Build, launch, and manage high-performing Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and Google Ads campaigns (Search, Display, and PMax).

Continuously monitor and optimize campaigns to achieve strict CPL and ROAS targets.

Design and refine high-converting landing pages targeted at the renewable energy market.

Build and manage sophisticated CRM automations and lead-flow integrations using GoHighLevel.

Collaborate with the Creative Lead and Account Management team to align messaging and execute growth strategies.

Analyze performance data to provide actionable insights and contribute to long-term client strategy.

Communicate results internally and support account managers in maintaining our 95% client retention rate.

Location: Must be based in South Africa.

Schedule: Strict 9–5 working hours in the GMT (UK) timezone.

Experience: Minimum 5 years of experience launching, optimizing, and scaling lead generation ads on Meta.

Technical Proficiency: Proven experience in landing page design and conversion rate optimization (CRO).

Automation: Demonstrated ability to integrate landing pages with CRMs and build lead-nurturing workflows.

Skillset: Strong background in PPC, marketing automation, and data-driven decision-making.

Experience in the renewable energy sector (solar and battery storage).

Proficient in Monday.com for project management and task tracking.
