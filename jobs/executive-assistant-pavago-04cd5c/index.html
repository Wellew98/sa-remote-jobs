About the Role

At Pavago, we’re hiring on behalf of one of our clients for an Executive Assistant to serve as the trusted right hand to a Principal overseeing a diverse portfolio of businesses, investments, and personal commitments.

This is not a traditional Executive Assistant role. It’s a high-trust, high-autonomy position where you’ll operate as the operational hub between the Principal, executives, advisors, attorneys, partners, and key stakeholders.

You’ll protect the Principal’s time, manage critical communications, coordinate priorities, and ensure that both business and personal operations run seamlessly. Success in this role requires exceptional judgment, discretion, ownership, and the ability to anticipate needs before they arise.

If you’re proactive, highly organized, and thrive supporting senior executives in fast-paced environments, this role is built for you.

What You’ll Own

Executive Calendar & Time Management

Own the Principal’s calendar across multiple time zones.

Prioritize meetings, strategic work blocks, and personal commitments.

Protect executive time by filtering low-value meetings and recommending alternatives.

Coordinate meeting logistics and ensure schedules remain optimized.

Prepare agendas, briefing materials, and meeting context in advance.

Inbox & Executive Communications

Manage the Principal’s inbox and daily communications.

Prioritize incoming messages and respond on the Principal’s behalf when appropriate.

Draft professional, executive-level correspondence that reflects the Principal’s communication style.

Track open conversations and ensure no important communication goes unanswered.

Maintain a target of responding to external emails within 24 hours.

Executive Operations & Cross-Functional Coordination

Serve as the primary coordination point between the Principal and:

Leadership teams

Attorneys

Advisors

Investors

Business partners

Translate discussions into actionable tasks and follow-up plans.

Coordinate meetings, agendas, action items, and executive communications.

Ensure decisions are communicated and executed efficiently across teams.

Personal & Lifestyle Management

Coordinate domestic and international travel, including itineraries, accommodations, transportation, and contingency planning.

Manage personal appointments, events, and family logistics.

Coordinate vendors, residences, reservations, and special events.

Ensure personal commitments are handled proactively without requiring Principal involvement.

Task Management & Executive Follow-Through

Track every commitment made by and to the Principal.

Maintain centralized task management systems using tools such as:

Notion

ClickUp

Asana

Monitor deadlines, deliverables, and dependencies.

Provide weekly executive summaries highlighting

Completed tasks

Outstanding priorities

Pending decisions

Upcoming commitments

Research, AI & Process Improvement

Prepare executive briefings for meetings, travel, and strategic initiatives.

Conduct research on companies, investment opportunities, vendors, and business topics.

Leverage AI tools such as Claude, ChatGPT, or equivalent platforms for:

Research

Summarization

Drafting

Continuously improve operational systems, documentation, and executive workflows.

What Happens After You Apply

Right after you apply, you’ll receive an email invitation from

Spark Hire

to record your Intro Video . This short, self-recorded video is the final step that completes your application and can be recorded whenever it’s convenient for you.
