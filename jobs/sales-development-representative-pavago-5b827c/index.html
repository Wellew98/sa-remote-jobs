About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a Sales Development Representative (SDR) to generate qualified sales opportunities through outbound prospecting, cold calling, email outreach, and appointment setting.

This is a hands-on outbound sales role where you’ll identify target accounts, connect with decision-makers, and turn cold prospects into qualified meetings for the sales team.
