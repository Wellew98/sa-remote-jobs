​​​​​About the company: The company is redefining how consumers interact with payments by combining wearable technology with modern financial services.

The company designs NFC-enabled payment wearables, develops embedded financial products, partners with global payment networks, and is preparing for several exciting product launches—including a next-generation payment ring with health tracking capabilities and a new debit banking initiative alongside The Marathon brand.

Working with organizations ranging from manufacturers and banking partners to Mastercard and strategic consumer brands, they operates at the intersection of fintech, hardware, branding, and innovation.

The Founder is now building the operational infrastructure needed to support this next chapter.

The Opportunity

This is far more than a traditional Executive Assistant role.

You'll become the founders operational right hand.

One hour you may be organizing calendars.

The next you'll be drafting investor materials, creating presentations, coordinating project updates, researching potential partners, managing WhatsApp conversations, organizing product launches, or using AI to automate internal workflows.

The ideal person genuinely enjoys variety and loves making executives more productive.

- Manage the founders calendar and inbox

- Prepare presentations, decks and one-pagers

- Assist with project coordination alongside the Project Manager

-

Track follow-ups and action items

- Help organize strategic partnerships

- Build documents, spreadsheets and reports

- Create AI-powered workflows that improve efficiency

- Handle a wide variety of operational and administrative projects

AI & Automation

The founders is specifically looking for someone excited about leveraging modern AI tools to increase productivity.

If you're constantly experimenting with new AI tools, you'll fit right in.

Why Join Us?

Few startups sit at the intersection of fintech, consumer hardware, strategic partnerships, and global product launches.

This role offers the opportunity to work directly with an experienced founder, help shape the company's operations from the ground up, and play a meaningful role in bringing innovative payment technologies to market. You'll gain exposure to banking, payments, consumer technology, product development, and executive decision-making while helping build the operational foundation for the company's next stage of growth.
