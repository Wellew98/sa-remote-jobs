Our client in the UK is looking for a Junior Web Designer/Developer to join their brilliant team of digital marketers! They offer incredible value websites through The 100 Club. The 100 Club has become very popular with businesses looking for cost-effective solutions for website design, development, hosting and updates. Due to their continued success, they are looking to expand their website development team.

They are seeking a Junior Web Designer/Developer with the ability to design website mockups and work with the team to develop the websites from mockups through to going live and thereafter implementing website updates.

This position will include plenty of on-the-job training and mentoring. The successful candidate will be supported by a highly-experienced Creative Director/Web Developer and work alongside a team of digital marketers.

Responsibilities

Designing website mockups for new clients (based on design briefs provided by the client).

Website development for new builds.

Website support and updates.

Liaising with clients about their website design and content.

Collaboration with other team members to deliver effective website development and ongoing client support.

Designing and implementing new features and functionality.

Implementing SEO considerations to get the best from the site.
