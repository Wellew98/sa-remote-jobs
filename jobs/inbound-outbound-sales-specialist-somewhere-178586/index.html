​​​​​​ Inbound & Outbound Sales Specialist (Premium Home Services)

Position TitleInbound & Outbound Sales Specialist

DepartmentSales & Revenue Growth

Employment TypeFull-Time Independent Contractor (40 Hours/Week)

ScheduleMonday–Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (U.S. Pacific Time / PST Aligned)

LocationRemote (Open exclusively to top talent in Latin America / LATAM or South Africa / SA )

Opportunity Overview

Are you a warm, highly articulate, and quick-thinking sales professional who excels at closing fast-moving consumer interest while building high-touch relationships with affluent clients?

We are seeking a seasoned Inbound & Outbound Sales Specialist to drive revenue growth for our premium home services brand.

Important Note on Profile Fit: We are not looking for a traditional B2B SaaS Account Executive, Enterprise SDR, or long-sales-cycle corporate representative. Our environment is fast-paced, phone-driven, high-velocity, and service-oriented. Most opportunities are inbound homeowners, estate managers, realtors, or household decision-makers calling in to book a service today or within the next few days.

We need an articulate, socially intelligent salesperson who can connect with homeowners instantly, explain pricing, handle objections naturally, and confidently close a booking within a 5 to 15-minute conversation —while managing ongoing relationships with high-value recurring accounts.

Position Breakdown & Core Responsibilities

1. Inbound High-Velocity Sales & One-Call Closing (~70% Focus)

  • Fast-Paced Transactional Closing: Field incoming phone calls, web form leads, and live inquiries from homeowners, property managers, and executive assistants.
  • Consultative Quoting in 5–15 Minutes: Rapidly understand project scopes, evaluate pricing options, articulate premium brand value, handle pricing pushback, and close the booking on the spot.
  • Upselling & Order Expansion: Identify opportunities during the sales call to cross-sell specialized service add-ons and convert one-time services into recurring maintenance packages.
  • Pipeline Follow-Up: Execute fast, structured follow-up cadences (phone, SMS, email) for quotes provided but not booked immediately, ensuring zero pipeline leakage.

2. High-Touch Relationship & Outbound Account Growth (~30% Focus)

  • Relationship Management: Build long-term trust and repeat business with higher-value accounts, executive assistants, property managers, luxury realtors, and estate managers—remembering preferences, following up naturally, and acting as a dedicated point of contact.
  • Targeted B2B Outreach: Conduct proactive outbound phone calls and personalized follow-ups to cultivate preferred-vendor relationships with local real estate agents, luxury property management groups, and construction leads.

Ideal Candidate Profile

Required Background & Experience

We value fast-thinking consumer sales grit, warmth, and social intelligence over heavy enterprise resumes. We strongly prefer candidates coming out of:

  • Premium Home Services (HVAC, roofing, restoration, luxury residential services)
  • Luxury Hospitality, Concierge, or High-End Travel
  • High-Ticket B2C Inside Sales
  • Property Management or Real Estate
  • Automotive Sales or Service Advisory
  • Membership, Subscription, or High-Touch Consumer Sales
  • Account Management / Customer Success with a heavy, proven track record of upselling and phone closing.

Core Skills & Attributes

  • Elite Phone Presence & Spoken English: Exceptional fluency with a warm, confident, articulate, and natural North American conversational tone. You must sound polished and trustworthy when speaking to affluent homeowners, executive assistants, and estate managers.
  • One-Call Closing Instincts: Comfortable steering conversations, communicating pricing clearly, overcoming hesitation without sounding overly aggressive or scripted, and asking for the business quickly.
  • Social Intelligence & Adaptability: Able to seamlessly switch between a quick, 5-minute transactional phone quote and a high-touch, relationship-building conversation with an executive assistant or property manager.
  • Technical & Home Office Setup: Quiet, professional home office, noise-canceling headset, high-speed fiber internet, and power backup (inverter/UPS required for South Africa candidates).
