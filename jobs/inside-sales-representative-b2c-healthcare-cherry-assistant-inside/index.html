Inside Sales Representative (B2C / Healthcare)

Cherry Assistant Eligible locations: South Africa Full-time Sales & Business Development Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

We are hiring a Appointment Setter for a marketing agency company in the healthcare/dental industry. This is a Full-Time remote role for a US-based client . The role is focused on qualifying and nurturing leads, conducting proactive outreach, and scheduling high-intent consultations for premium dental treatments while maximizing show rates across multiple practices. The ideal candidate is empathetic yet persuasive, KPI-driven, highly organized, and an excellent English communicator with strong objection-handling skills and healthcare familiarity. Key Responsibilities: - Qualify inbound leads via phone, SMS, and email to assess intent, fit, and readiness to book consultations - Conduct timely outbound follow-ups and reactivation of older leads to drive new bookings - Coordinate calendars and schedule consultations across multiple dental practices - Improve show rates through confirmation calls/texts, reminders, pre-consult education, and swift rescheduling - Manage and update CRM pipelines with accurate notes, statuses, and next steps - Handle objections, nurture prospects, and disqualify when appropriate to maintain quality - Meet or exceed KPIs for set rate, show rate, and scheduled-to-attended conversion - Collaborate with the leadership team to refine scripts, offers, and workflow processes - Maintain patient privacy and confidentiality standards in all communications - Prepare daily/weekly reports on lead flow, bookings, and show-rate performance Required Qualifications: - 2+ years of experience in appointment setting, inside sales, or patient coordination (B2C) - Demonstrated success improving show rates for high-ticket or healthcare services - Excellent verbal and written English; confident phone presence and empathetic communication style - Strong objection handling, discovery, and qualification skills - Proficiency with CRMs and phone/SMS tools; diligent with documentation and follow-through - Ability to work US business hours and manage multiple accounts/practices simultaneously - Process-driven, coachable, and metrics-oriented with high attention to detail Preferred Qualifications: - Experience in dental, medspa, chiropractic, ophthalmology, or similar medical settings - Familiarity with HIPAA-aligned workflows and handling of sensitive information - Prior experience working across multiple client accounts or locations - Hands-on experience with GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Salesforce, Aircall/RingCentral, and SMS platforms Required Skills & Tools: - CRM proficiency (e.g., HubSpot, GoHighLevel, Salesforce) - VoIP dialers and SMS tools (e.g., Aircall, RingCentral, Twilio-powered tools) - Calendar and productivity tools (e.g., Google Calendar, Calendly, Google Workspace/Sheets) Schedule & Pay: - Full-Time position; 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM MT (Mon–Fri), with occasional flexibility as needed - Fully remote role for a US-based client - Pay ranges from R12,600to R16,700 per month (base), plus performance-based commissions tied to attended consultations - Includes structured training, ongoing coaching, and clear growth pathways (e.g., senior setter, team lead) System Requirements: - Internet speed of at least 20 Mbps upload and download - Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher - 8 GB of RAM or higher - Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer - HD 720p webcam - Headset with microphone Benefits: - Competitive pay rates - Consistent hours and predictable workload - Fully remote work with long-term potential - Direct collaboration with the client's leadership team, making a real impact - Supportive and inclusive work environment - Opportunity to grow alongside a fast-growing dental marketing program
