Read the posting in full · 150 words

Our client in the UK is looking for someone to manage a range of costumes through Amazon Marketplace. This is a great opportunity for a hardworking, motivated person, to grow or start a career in e-commerce, to learn all about selling on Amazon.

Key Responsibilities

Implement our SEO strategies and write high converting copy.

Ensure product listings are optimised to maximise conversion (images, videos etc).

Maximise the visibility and sales of our products, using data to influence decisions on factors such as pricing and advertising.

Lead tests and analyse data to help us improve our best practice and strategies.

Deliver Revenue, TACoS and Margin targets.

Required Skills

Can complete complex pieces of work and projects.

Highly Numerate.

Good written communicator.

Good Excel capability.

Good commercial grounding and understanding of the fundamentals of business – marketing mix, % margin etc.

Able to interrogate data, design a test, conclude results and present.
