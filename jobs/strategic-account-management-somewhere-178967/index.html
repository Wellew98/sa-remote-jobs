Strategic Account Manager

  • Experience : 5+ years
  • Employment Type : Full-Time, Fully Remote
  • Sourcing Location : South Africa
  • Schedule : Monday–Friday 9:00 AM–6:00 PM CST
  • Compensation : $2,500 - $3,000/month, Looking for female candidates only
  • Primary Focus : Client relationship management, strategic account planning, campaign oversight, project management, and account growth

About the Company

Our client is a full-service marketing agency specializing in high-impact, direct-response digital marketing strategies, media planning, performance creative, and funnel optimization. By combining strategic vision with data-driven execution, the agency helps businesses scale their brand presence, acquire high-value leads, and maximize customer conversion across diverse digital touchpoints.

As the agency continues to grow, it is expanding its account management team to maintain strong client relationships, ensure seamless project delivery, and drive long-term campaign success across its growing portfolio.

Role Overview

We are seeking a Strategic Account Manager to serve as the primary strategic partner and key operational point of contact for client accounts.

In this role, you will take ownership of client retention, strategy alignment, campaign execution oversight, and cross-functional project management. You will act as the bridge between client stakeholders and internal specialists—including media buyers, copywriters, designers, and SEO teams—ensuring that marketing strategies translate into high-performing deliverables executed on time and on budget. You will leverage AI tools to streamline communication, analyze campaign performance, and generate strategic account plans while keeping all moving parts meticulously organized.

The ideal candidate is a proactive, business-minded client partner with a deep understanding of digital marketing funnels, project management systems, and client satisfaction drivers in an agency setting.

Key Responsibilities

Client Relationship Management & Growth

  • Serve as the main point of contact for key client accounts, building strong, trusted, and long-term relationships.
  • Onboard new clients smoothly, establishing clear expectations, communication cadences, and key performance indicators (KPIs).
  • Lead regular client status meetings, strategy sessions, and performance reviews to communicate campaign progress and business impact.
  • Understand client business goals, industry landscapes, and pain points to proactively recommend growth opportunities and campaign expansions.
  • Address client concerns promptly, manage scope changes gracefully, and navigate difficult conversations with poise and clarity.

Campaign Oversight & Strategic Alignment

  • Translate high-level client objectives into actionable campaign roadmaps, creative briefs, and project scopes for execution teams.
  • Monitor overall account performance and collaborate with paid media, content, SEO, and design teams to ensure performance metrics are hit.
  • Review all client deliverables for strategic alignment, high quality, and brand consistency prior to client presentation.
  • Guide client retention strategies, identifying upsell and cross-sell opportunities to drive account expansion.

AI-Enhanced Strategy & Operations

  • Utilize AI tools (such as ChatGPT and Claude) to draft client communications, synthesize meeting notes, generate account summaries, and organize strategic recommendations.
  • Leverage AI platforms to conduct client industry research, brainstorm campaign concepts, and quickly analyze client performance data.
  • Refine AI-generated outputs to ensure polished, professional, and accurate client communications.
  • Continuously improve internal documentation and client-facing workflows using AI-assisted productivity routines.

Project Management & CRM Integration

  • Manage project timelines, team tasks, scope tracking, and deliverables within ClickUp to ensure zero missed deadlines.
  • Track client lead activity, automated workflows, email campaigns, and CRM health inside Go High Level.
  • Ensure clear communication and alignment between clients and internal creative/media teams.
  • Maintain complete, accurate, and up-to-date account records, contact lists, and strategic notes across agency platforms.

Required Qualifications

  • 5+ years of professional experience as a Strategic Account Manager, Senior Account Executive, Client Success Manager, or similar agency role managing digital marketing clients.
  • Proven track record of managing multi-channel digital marketing campaigns, retaining high-value clients, and growing account revenue.
  • Practical experience using ChatGPT and Claude (or similar AI platforms) to streamline client communication, research, and project workflows.
  • Hands-on experience using ClickUp for project management, task delegation, and timeline tracking.
  • Experience working within Go High Level (CRM) to track leads, monitor funnels, and verify customer communication automation.
  • Deep understanding of direct-response marketing, digital sales funnels, paid media (search/social), and conversion rate optimization principles.
  • Exceptional written and verbal English communication skills with strong presentation and interpersonal abilities.
  • Strong problem-solving, negotiation, and conflict-resolution skills.
  • Excellent organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple client accounts and internal teams simultaneously.
  • Reliable internet connection and a dedicated remote workspace.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Previous experience in a fast-paced digital marketing agency environment managing high-touch client accounts.
  • Familiarity with analytics platforms such as Google Analytics (GA4) to discuss campaign metrics confidently with clients.
  • Track record of driving account expansions, upsells, and high client retention rates.
  • Experience managing cross-functional creative, media buying, and technical teams.

Technology & Tools

Candidates should be proficient with or capable of quickly mastering:

  • ClickUp (Project Management & Task Delegation)
  • Go High Level (CRM, Lead Tracking, and Marketing Automation)
  • ChatGPT & Claude (AI Productivity & Strategy Tools)
  • Google Workspace / Microsoft Office
  • Video Conferencing Platforms (Zoom, Google Meet)
  • Google Analytics (GA4) & Marketing Dashboard Tools

What This Role Offers

  • Direct leadership over key client relationships within a growing digital agency.
  • A highly collaborative environment supported by dedicated creative, paid media, and SEO specialists.
  • Exposure to modern AI tools, efficient project workflows, and performance-driven marketing strategies.
  • Consistent full-time hours within standard Central Time business hours.

Ideal Candidate Profile

The ideal candidate is a proactive, highly articulate account leader who balances client advocacy with agency execution.

They are equally skilled at running strategic client calls and diving into ClickUp and Go High Level to ensure project deliverables stay on track. Rather than acting as a passive liaison, they bring strategic ideas to the table, leverage AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude to speed up research and account documentation, and take true ownership of client success.

Most importantly, they build strong relationships through clear communication, operational reliability, and an unwavering focus on helping clients achieve their business goals.

Success Metrics

Success in this role will be measured by

  • Client satisfaction, retention rates, and account health metrics.
  • Account revenue growth through strategic expansions, upsells, and renewals.
  • On-time and on-budget delivery of client projects and campaign assets within ClickUp.
  • Effective organization and alignment of client workflows within Go High Level.
  • Quality, clarity, and professionalism of client communication and performance presentations.
  • Smart, efficient application of AI tools to enhance productivity and account documentation.
  • Smooth cross-functional collaboration between internal creative/media teams and client stakeholders.
