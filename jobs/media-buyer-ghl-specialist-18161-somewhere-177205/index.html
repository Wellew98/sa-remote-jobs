Media Buyer & GoHighLevel (GHL) Automation Specialist

LocationRemote (South Africa or Latin America preferred)

Working HoursMust maintain strong overlap with U.S. Eastern Time (EST)

Employment TypeFull-Time (Remote)

About the Role

We are seeking a high-performing Media Buyer & GoHighLevel (GHL) Automation Specialist to bridge the gap between top-of-funnel traffic acquisition and bottom-of-funnel conversion systems .

This is not a “button-pusher” role.

You are a full-stack performance marketer who understands how to:

  • Drive efficient, high-quality traffic from Meta and Google Ads
  • Build and optimize funnels inside GoHighLevel (GHL)
  • Design automation systems that convert leads into appointments and revenue

You think in terms of ROI, CPA, MER, and pipeline velocity — not vanity metrics.

What Success Looks Like

  • Campaigns consistently hit CPA / ROAS targets
  • Small daily budgets produce measurable results
  • Funnels convert traffic into qualified leads
  • Workflows move leads from capture through nurture to booked appointments
  • Sales teams have clean pipelines and attribution visibility
  • Accounts remain compliant and healthy

Key Responsibilities

Paid Media Strategy & Execution

Meta Ads (Facebook / Instagram)

  • End-to-end campaign management
  • Creative testing & iteration
  • Budget scaling (ABO / CBO)
  • Retargeting strategy
  • Audience testing & refinement

Google Ads

  • Search (intent-based campaigns)
  • Performance Max
  • YouTube Ads
  • Keyword strategy & structure

Performance Optimization

  • Daily KPI monitoring (CTR, CPC, CPA, ROAS, MER)
  • Budget allocation & pivots
  • Bottleneck identification
  • Scaling & stabilization

Compliance & Account Health

  • Maintain ad account quality scores
  • Ensure policy compliance
  • Prevent account flags or disruptions

Small Budget Performance Mastery (Critical)

Most campaigns operate at

  • $50 – $100 per day
  • Up to approximately $4,500 per month per client

This requires

  • Precision testing
  • Smart budget allocation
  • Efficient creative iteration
  • Strong statistical decision-making

Experience scaling large budgets without small-budget discipline will not be sufficient.

GoHighLevel (GHL) Architecture & Optimization

Funnels & Pages

  • Build landing pages, surveys, and lead forms
  • Optimize for conversions

Automation & Workflows

  • Design lead nurture sequences
  • Configure triggers, conditions, and tagging logic
  • Bridge lead generation to appointment booking

Pipeline Management

  • Build custom pipeline stages
  • Implement lead scoring
  • Establish attribution tracking

Integrations

  • API / Zapier connections
  • CRM & external tool syncing

Snapshots

  • Build and deploy scalable GHL environments

Creative & Copy Collaboration

  • Collaborate on ad hooks and messaging angles
  • A/B test creatives and copy
  • Iterate landing page messaging
  • Coordinate with design and video resources as needed

(Basic Canva or editing skills are helpful but not required.)

Qualifications & Skills

Required Experience

  • 3+ years managing Meta & Google Ads
  • Documented success managing $10k+ monthly ad spend
  • Proven success working with small daily budgets ($50–$100/day)
  • Strong direct-response / lead-generation background

GoHighLevel (GHL) Expertise

You should be confident with

  • Funnel Builder
  • Workflows & Triggers
  • Pipelines
  • Snapshots
  • Tags & Automations
  • CRM Logic

Technical Literacy

  • Meta Pixel
  • Conversions API (CAPI)
  • Google Tag Manager (GTM)
  • Conversion tracking & attribution

Analytical Mindset

  • Identify funnel bottlenecks quickly
  • Interpret performance metrics correctly
  • Make data-driven decisions
  • Optimize toward CPA / ROAS / MER

Performance Orientation

  • ROI-first thinking
  • Lead quality over volume
  • No vanity-metric bias

Nice-to-Have (Not Mandatory)

  • Canva or light creative editing
  • Basic video editing (resizing, overlays)
  • Experience with SMB / local lead generation
  • Agency experience

Ideal Candidate Profile

The ideal candidate is

  • Performance-driven
  • Comfortable with testing and iteration
  • Highly analytical
  • Systems and process-oriented
  • Autonomous and accountable
  • Calm under budget pressure

Compensation & Growth

CompensationFlexible for right candidate

  • Performance-based raises available
  • Signing bonus considered for exceptional candidates
  • Growth opportunities as the client portfolio scales
