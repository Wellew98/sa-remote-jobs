Shopify Implementation Specialist

LocationSouth Africa

Salary Range1300 - 2500 USD

Role Description

The Shopify Implementation Specialist is responsible for setting up, customizing, and launching high-performing e-commerce stores on the Shopify platform. This role involves translating business needs into functional online storefronts, configuring backend systems, integrating third-party apps, and ensuring seamless user experiences. The ideal candidate combines strong technical expertise with an eye for design, UX, and e-commerce strategy, enabling brands to go live on Shopify quickly and successfully.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead the implementation of Shopify stores from setup through launch.
  • Configure and customize themes to meet brand guidelines and user experience standards.
  • Build and manage product catalogs, including product variants, categories, collections, and tagging.
  • Integrate third-party apps, payment gateways, shipping solutions, and marketing tools.
  • Optimize store performance and mobile responsiveness.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams including marketing, design, logistics, and IT to gather assets and content.
  • Ensure compliance with best practices related to SEO, page speed, accessibility, and security.
  • Troubleshoot and resolve technical issues during implementation.
  • Provide training and documentation for internal teams on store management and updates.
  • Stay current with Shopify updates, tools, and ecosystem developments.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Web Development, E-commerce, Computer Science, or related field (or equivalent experience).
  • 2+ years of experience working with Shopify or Shopify Plus.
  • Proficiency in Shopify theme customization (HTML, CSS, Liquid, JavaScript).
  • Strong understanding of e-commerce UX/UI best practices.
  • Familiarity with SEO, conversion rate optimization, and digital marketing fundamentals.
  • Experience with Shopify apps, APIs, and integrations.
  • Detail-oriented with strong organizational and project management skills.
  • Excellent communication skills and ability to work independently and as part of a team.

Preferred Skills

  • Experience with Figma or Adobe XD for UI/UX collaboration.
  • Knowledge of analytics tools (Google Analytics, Hotjar, Shopify Analytics).
  • Familiarity with Klaviyo, Meta Pixel, Google Tag Manager, and other marketing tools.
  • Experience working in an agency or with multiple client accounts is a plus.
  • Understanding of internationalization, multi-currency, and multilingual Shopify setups.

Details

  • Independent contractor agreement paid 2x monthly.
  • Client has a desire for long term commitment.
