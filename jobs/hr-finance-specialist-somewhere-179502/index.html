About the Company

Our client is one of the largest U.S. multi-brand restaurant franchisees, operating over 230 locations across the Wendy’s, Taco Bell, and 7 Brew brands with more than 7,000 employees across 8 states. Committed to operational excellence and employee success, the company provides the training and tools needed for team members to thrive while delivering exceptional service to their guests.

About the Role

As the HR & Finance Specialist within the Finance & Administration (F&A) department, you will report directly to the HR & Finance Supervisor and support day-to-day human resources, benefits, compliance, and accounting operations. Acting as a primary point of contact for store managers and employees, you will manage benefits administration, leave requests, unemployment claims, business licensing, and Accounts Payable invoice reviews. This role is ideal for a highly organized, detail-oriented generalist who exercises sound independent judgment and handles confidential, time-sensitive employee and financial records with complete discretion.

Key Responsibilities

Human Resources & Benefits

- Benefits Administration: Administer employee benefits—managing eligibility, enrollment, setup, status changes, and ongoing maintenance to ensure complete accuracy and compliance.

- FMLA & Leave of Absence (LOA): Process Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) and other leave of absence requests in compliance with federal and state regulations and company policy.

- Unemployment Claims & Verifications: Manage the unemployment claims process with accurate, timely responses and documentation, and respond to external employment and income verification requests.

- Employee Records & Staffing Reports: Maintain confidential, up-to-date employee records (including benefits and payroll data) and prepare recurring staffing reports to track headcount levels and trends.

Finance & Accounting

- Accounts Payable Review: Review and approve Accounts Payable invoices for accuracy, proper general ledger coding, and adherence to company policy.

- Payroll & General Accounting Support: Assist the Finance & Administration team with payroll processing, reconciliations, and general accounting tasks as needed.

Compliance & Administration

- Business Licenses & Permits: Track, obtain, renew, and maintain required business licenses and operational permits for restaurant locations across markets.

- Employee & Manager Helpdesk: Serve as a responsive frontline point of contact for employee and manager HR and finance inquiries via phone and email.

- General Administrative Support: Provide day-to-day administrative support and execute special HR, payroll, or accounting projects to keep back-office operations running smoothly.

Qualifications

Required

- High school diploma or equivalent (or equivalent hands-on professional experience).

- Working familiarity with computerized payroll, HRIS, and bookkeeping/accounting systems.

- Strong proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Microsoft Excel.

- Exceptional attention to detail, organizational rigor, and time-management skills to meet recurring deadlines.

- Clear, professional written and verbal communication skills in English for handling employee and manager phone/email inquiries.

- High integrity, discretion, and proven ability to safeguard confidential employee and financial information.

- Strong self-direction and problem-solving judgment to work independently in a remote environment for a minimum of 45 hours per week.

Preferred

- 2–3 years of hands-on experience in payroll, human resources administration, or accounting/Accounts Payable.

- Associate or Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Business Administration, Human Resources, or a related field.

- Prior experience supporting multi-unit retail, hospitality, or restaurant operations.
