RolePeople Ops Coordinator

Priority Location :South Africa / Latin America / Philippines

Working Hours :  9:00 AM – 5:00 PM EST

Salary RangeMarket related

Type of contractIndependent Contractor

Type of jobRemote

About the Role: The Coordinator is expected to independently handle routine processes, provide approved information, maintain accurate records, drive follow-up, and escalate when a matter requires clinical, legal, financial, managerial, or functional authority.

Priority order for the role

  • First: People operations and frontline clinician or employee support, especially leave, PTO, benefits, payroll questions, records, and lifecycle administration.
  • Second: Clinician-support triage, case tracking, communication, and follow-through across the correct operational owners.
  • Third: Recruiting coordination and candidate support for clinical and nonclinical roles.
  • Always: Process improvement, documentation, accuracy, confidentiality, and no-surprises communication.

Key Responsibilities

1. Frontline people and clinician support

  • Serve as a responsive first point of contact for routine clinician and employee questions involving payroll, benefits, PTO, sick time, leave, people policies, onboarding, offboarding, and related administrative processes.
  • Use approved policies, resources, and subject-matter experts to provide accurate next steps. Do not guess when the answer is uncertain.
  • Log, categorize, acknowledge, route, and track requests so the company can see open items, accountable owners, status, and aging.
  • Follow through until the person receives a complete answer or the underlying action is verified as complete.
  • Communicate with empathy and professionalism without promising outcomes outside your authority.

2. Clinician-support triage and closure

  • Triage clinician questions involving operations, credentialing, revenue cycle, scheduling, technology, people administration, or clinical leadership to the correct owner.
  • Maintain ownership of communication and tracking until the responsible function closes the issue.
  • Identify recurring questions, handoff failures, and administrative pain points, then surface patterns to the Senior Director, Clinician Operations with evidence.
  • Maintain and improve approved FAQs, guides, templates, and self-service resources so repeated questions decline over time.
  • Escalate urgent, sensitive, or high-risk matters promptly under established protocols.

3. Leave and PTO administration

  • Own the administrative handling of leave requests from initial notice through return to work.
  • Administer PTO, sick time, and other leave processes in accordance with approved policies and applicable guidance.
  • Explain process steps, documentation requirements, deadlines, and next actions clearly.
  • Coordinate FMLA and applicable federal, state, and local leave administration using approved resources and external expertise as needed.
  • Track dates, documentation, approvals, balances, deadlines, communications, and return-to-work requirements.
  • Coordinate with payroll, benefits, managers, and leadership to ensure leave administration is accurate and consistent.

4. Benefits and payroll support

  • Support day-to-day administration of health and welfare benefits, including eligibility, enrollment, qualifying life events, terminations, and coverage questions.
  • Work with brokers, carriers, payroll systems, and internal owners to resolve administrative issues.
  • Serve as a resource for paycheck, deduction, PTO-balance, and other payroll-related questions.
  • Research discrepancies, document findings, communicate status, and coordinate through verified resolution.
  • Ensure approved employee changes affecting payroll or benefits are transmitted and processed accurately.

5. People records and lifecycle administration

  • Support onboarding, offboarding, employment or contractor status changes, and other people-administration events.
  • Maintain accurate and current information in the HRIS and related systems so they remain reliable sources of truth.
  • Manage required documents, acknowledgments, deadlines, and records with strong confidentiality and version control.
  • Coordinate administrative handoffs among recruiting, managers, IT, payroll, benefits, operations, and other owners.
  • Handle sensitive employee and clinician information with discretion and appropriate access controls.

6. Recruiting and hiring coordination

  • Support recruiting for clinical and nonclinical roles based on priorities established by recruiting leadership.
  • Conduct candidate outreach and initial screening as assigned.
  • Coordinate interviews, maintain timely candidate communication, and keep recruiting pipelines accurate and moving.
  • Partner with hiring managers on scheduling, documentation, feedback collection, and process follow-through.
  • Coordinate offers and the administrative transition from candidate to new hire or contractor.
  • Identify opportunities to improve recruiting workflows and the candidate experience.

7. Process improvement, documentation, and technology

  • Build and maintain trackers, templates, workflows, checklists, and resources where visibility or consistency is weak.
  • Identify why recurring administrative issues occur and help implement practical fixes.
  • Support implementation and optimization of HR, recruiting, support, and workflow systems.
  • Use automation and AI-enabled tools appropriately to reduce repetitive work, improve accuracy, draft communications, and surface patterns, while preserving confidentiality and required human review.
  • Maintain proactive, no-surprises communication when deadlines, risks, or unresolved dependencies could affect others.

Qualifications

  • Relevant experience: At least two years of success in people operations, HR administration, healthcare or provider operations, recruiting coordination, employee support, customer operations, or another confidential, deadline-driven service role.
  • Experience working in the healthcare or clinical industry is required
  • Functional exposure: Direct experience in at least 2 of the following: leave administration, benefits, payroll support, HRIS or records, recruiting coordination, provider support, or employee lifecycle administration.
  • Exceptional organization: The ability to manage many people, requests, deadlines, documents, and open items without losing visibility.
  • Judgment: You know what you can handle independently, what requires research, and what must be escalated immediately.
  • Follow-through: You close loops, verify completion, and do not assume that sending a message resolves the issue.
  • Resourcefulness and learning speed: You can find reliable answers, learn new systems quickly, and operate effectively without already knowing every policy.
  • Communication: Clear, tactful, and direct communication with clinicians, employees, candidates, managers, and external partners.
  • Accuracy and discretion: A high standard for confidential information, records, payroll, benefits, leave, and employee communications.
  • Technology comfort: The ability to learn new platforms and use automation or AI tools responsibly to improve work.
  • Adaptability: Comfort in a growing organization where processes continue to evolve, without using change as an excuse for disorganization.
