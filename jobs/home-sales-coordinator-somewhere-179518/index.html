Home Sales Coordinator

Location & EmploymentRemote | Full-time | US Eastern Time business hours, Monday through Friday

Who We Are

We are a rapidly growing, family-owned U.S.-based company that acquires and operates manufactured housing communities. Our communities are located in Indiana and Ohio, and we are expanding across the Midwest and Southeast. We are long-term owners and operators, and we run everything in house: acquisitions, asset management, home sales, and accounting.

Our leadership has acquired 30+ communities and 3,000+ lots. Home sales are how our communities grow: every home we sell puts a family into a home they are proud of and fills a lot.

The Role

You will run home sales across our Indiana and Ohio communities, from first inquiry to move-in, working remotely. You keep leads, showings, applications, and closings moving, and you make every buyer feel looked after. You work closely with our on-site managers, Regional Manager, and leadership by phone, text, email, and Zoom.

What You Will Do

  • Leads. Answer every inquiry the same day, track every lead, and follow up until it closes or is clearly dead.
  • Showings. Set showings with our on-site managers and confirm every home is clean, priced, and show-ready before a buyer walks in.
  • Applications and financing. Walk buyers through the application and our lender partners, and keep every file complete.
  • Buy or rent. Use the numbers to help a buyer choose between buying and renting, and help the team decide when to buy, set, and sell a home.
  • Closings. Get every sale to closing on time: paperwork, title, deposits, and move-in.
  • Marketing. Keep listings, photos, and flyers current on our sites and marketplaces.
  • Reporting. Keep the pipeline current and send a short weekly update on leads, showings, applications, and closings.

Who You Are

  • Warm and organized. People trust you, and nothing falls through the cracks.
  • Follows up. Every call and message gets an answer, and every lead gets tracked.
  • Comfortable with numbers. You can explain a monthly payment and the cost of buying versus renting in plain words.
  • Hard worker. You take ownership and see a sale through when it gets complicated.
  • Fast-paced. You thrive in a growing company and adjust quickly when priorities change.
  • Team player. You work well with on-site managers and leadership and share what you learn.

What You Bring

  • Experience in sales, leasing, property management, mortgage, or customer service is a plus. No manufactured housing experience needed; we train on everything.
  • Excellent written and spoken English, and confidence on the phone with US buyers.
  • Comfortable with Excel, AppFolio or similar software, and AI tools, and you check your own work.
  • A reliable computer, internet, phone, and a quiet place to work.
  • Able to work US Eastern Time business hours, Monday through Friday.

What We Offer

  • Monthly pay based on experience, plus bonuses on homes sold.
  • Hands-on training with leadership. We train and promote from within, and you grow as we grow.
  • A great team, a growing portfolio, and more communities and hiring ahead.
