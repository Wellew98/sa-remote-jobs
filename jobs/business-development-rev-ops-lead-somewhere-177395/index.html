About the Role

We are hiring a Business Development & Revenue Operations Lead to fix and scale our sales engine.

This is not a traditional sales role.

We are not looking for someone to “make more calls.”

We are looking for someone to diagnose, rebuild, and execute a high-performing sales process .

Our current challenge is not a lack of effort — it’s a lack of structure, positioning, and consistency. This role will bring clarity, systems, and strategy to how we generate and convert pipeline.

You will combine

- Business Development execution (outreach, follow-ups, reactivation)

- Sales strategy (messaging, qualification, positioning)

- Revenue operations (process design, CRM optimization, funnel tracking)

The Opportunity

- Fix a broken and underperforming sales process

- Build a scalable outbound + inbound system

-

Introduce AI-driven sales workflows

- Work directly with leadership and influence revenue growth

What You’ll Own

1. Sales Process Diagnosis & Rebuild

-

Audit current sales efforts across

-

Cold outreach

- Inbound lead handling

-

Dormant account reactivation

-

Identify breakdowns in

-

Messaging

- Targeting

-

Follow-up structure

- Conversion points

- Design and implement a clear, repeatable sales process

2. Pipeline Generation (Outbound + Reactivation)

- Build and execute outbound strategies targeting Marketing Directors

- Reactivate dormant leads and past opportunities

- Develop structured outreach sequences (email, LinkedIn, etc.)

- Optimize messaging to move beyond “generic pitches” into strategic conversations

3. Inbound Lead Qualification & Conversion

- Manage and qualify inbound leads with a goal of scaling toward 100+ qualified leads/month

-

Lead discovery conversations focused on

-

Business needs

- ROI and execution strategy

-

Agency fit and differentiation

- Improve conversion rates by elevating the quality of sales conversations

4. AI-Powered Sales Enablement

- Use AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT) to:

-

Analyze call transcripts

- Generate and refine sales scripts

-

Build objection-handling frameworks

- Standardize messaging across the team

- Continuously improve scripts based on real performance data

5. CRM & Revenue Operations (HubSpot)

- Own and optimize the sales pipeline in HubSpot

-

Build and maintain

-

Deal stages

- Automation workflows

-

Follow-up sequences

- Reporting dashboards

-

Track key metrics

-

Lead-to-meeting conversion

- Meeting-to-close conversion

-

Pipeline velocity

6. Performance Tracking & Optimization

-

Treat sales as a data-driven funnel

- Identify what’s working and what’s not — and adjust quickly

- Run experiments across messaging, channels, and targeting

- Provide regular reporting and actionable insights to leadership

What We’re Looking For

This role requires a hybrid operator — someone who can think strategically but also execute daily.

You should be comfortable owning ambiguity , building systems from scratch, and improving them over time.

Required Qualifications

- Proven experience in business development, sales, or revenue operations

- Experience owning or improving a sales process end-to-end

- Strong communication skills — especially in consultative / strategic selling

- Ability to engage senior stakeholders (e.g., Marketing Directors)

- Experience using a CRM (preferably HubSpot )

- Strong organizational and pipeline management skills

Strong Advantages

- Background in strategy, operations, or consulting (MBA-style thinking is a plus)

- Experience selling marketing services, agencies, or B2B services

- Experience reactivating dormant leads or rebuilding underperforming pipelines

- Hands-on experience using AI tools in sales workflows

-

Experience working with US-based clients
