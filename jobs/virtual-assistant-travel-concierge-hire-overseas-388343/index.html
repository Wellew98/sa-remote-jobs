Virtual Assistant Travel Concierge

Hire Overseas South Africa (Remote) Admin & Virtual Assistance Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

We're looking for a Travel Concierge who is passionate about crafting seamless travel experiences and delivering high-quality client service. You will be the trusted expert behind every journey, using AI-powered tools to research and recommend hotels, coordinating logistics across platforms, and communicating directly with travelers via WhatsApp to make every trip feel effortless and personal. This role is for someone who loves travel, writes with warmth and clarity, thinks fast when plans change, and takes pride in the kind of attention to detail that makes clients feel like VIPs from the first message through check-in. Why You'll Want to Join You will be paid in USD (bi-monthly: every 15th and 30th) Paid Time Off in accordance with company policy Observance of Holidays per company guidelines 100% remote setup so you can work wherever you're most productive Work in the luxury travel and hospitality space with access to premium brands and destinations Use cutting-edge AI tools to enhance research and elevate the client experience What You'll Work On Client Engagement and Support Chat with travelers via WhatsApp to understand their preferences and deliver personalized recommendations Respond quickly and thoughtfully to incoming inquiries with initial responses landing within 10 to 20 minutes during your shift Handle special requests and ensure smooth communication between clients and hotels or vendors Maintain a calm, professional, and reassuring tone even when plans change at the last minute Research and Booking Research and recommend hotels, restaurants, and attractions using AI tools and reliable online sources Verify every detail including location, timing, currency, and amenities before confirming bookings Coordinate hotel reservations, car rentals, and travel logistics across multiple booking platforms Content and Destination Curation Write and curate destination guides and travel recommendations that blend editorial polish with practical, on-the-ground insight Collaborate with the marketing team to highlight exceptional hotels and destinations for social content Stay current on travel trends, new openings, and hidden gems worth knowing about Problem-Solving and Adaptability Think fast and find creative solutions when plans shift due to flight delays, cancellations, or unexpected changes Communicate clearly and calmly under pressure to maintain client confidence and satisfaction What You Bring Empathetic and client-obsessed: you treat every traveler like a VIP Detail-oriented and reliable: you catch mistakes others miss before they become problems Exceptional written English: you write with clarity, warmth, and professionalism A self-starter: you love learning, exploring, and improving processes without being asked Tech-savvy: comfortable using Slack, WhatsApp, Google Sheets and Docs, and booking platforms Fast but thoughtful: you balance responsiveness with high-quality work Curious and well-informed: you research effectively, verify information, and stay ahead of travel trends Nice to Have Background in hospitality, travel, or concierge services Experience with AI tools such as ChatGPT or travel research assistants Familiarity with airlines, loyalty programs, and travel booking systems International travel experience or strong knowledge of major global destinations Negotiation skills to advocate for clients with vendors and hotels How to Apply Please include: Your updated resume highlighting any customer service or travel-related experience A short Loom video (1 to 2 minutes) introducing yourself and sharing why you are passionate about travel and client service A short writing sample such as an email, WhatsApp message, or short paragraph showing your conversational tone Only candidates who submit a Loom video will be moved to the next step of the hiring process. If you love connecting with people, solving travel challenges, and creating experiences that feel effortless for the traveler, this role gives you the platform and the tools to do that every single day. Application Process Overview Our comprehensive selection process ensures we find the right fit for both you and our clients: Initial Application  - Submit your application and complete our prequalifying questions Video Introduction  - Record an video introduction to showcase your communication skills and work experience Role-Specific Assessment  - Complete a homework assignment tailored to the position (if applicable) Recruitment Interview  - Initial screening with our talent team Executive Interview  - Meet with senior leadership to discuss role alignment Client Interview  - Final interview with the client team you'd be supporting Background & Reference Check  - Professional reference verification Job Offer  - Successful candidates receive a formal offer to join the team Each stage is designed to evaluate your fit for the role while giving you insights into our company culture and expectations. We'll keep you informed throughout the process and provide feedback at each step.
