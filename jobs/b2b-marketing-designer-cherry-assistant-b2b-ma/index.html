We are hiring a Marketing Designer (B2B Marketing) for a boutique marketing consultancy in the Digital Marketing industry. This is a Part-Time remote role for a US based client. The role is focused on producing high-quality, on-brand marketing collateral across out-of-home, print, and digital channels for B2B campaigns.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design clean, modern, and high-resolution print and out-of-home (OOH) marketing collateral (e.g., billboards, bus ads, pole banners, trifolds, flyers, promotional kits).
  • Create high-converting digital ad assets tailored for social media platforms, including Facebook and TikTok.
  • Adapt static graphic assets into basic video ad creative as needed.
  • Translate strategic briefs into polished, production-ready deliverables without needing constant direction.
  • Manage time efficiently to deliver initial drafts and revisions strictly within agreed-upon project deadlines.
  • Accurately track project hours per task using Notion for transparent client reporting.
  • Maintain an organized asset library and version control across campaigns
  • Support web/landing page assets and light web design handoffs

Required Qualifications

  • 3+ years of professional graphic design experience in marketing/advertising (non-entry level)
  • Strong portfolio demonstrating B2B campaign work across OOH/print and digital ads with a modern, clean, professional style
  • Expert proficiency in Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, and InDesign
  • Solid understanding of print production and OOH specifications (sizing, DPI, bleeds, color, preflight)
  • Experience adapting creative across multiple placements (e.g., Meta, TikTok) and aspect ratios
  • Excellent attention to detail, spelling/grammar, and file hygiene; delivers final-draft quality
  • Ability to work independently with minimal guidance and communicate proactively in English
  • Comfortable working asynchronously and managing time to hit deadlines

Preferred Qualifications

  • 5+ years of experience and/or agency background
  • Experience in B2B sectors (education or related industries is a plus)
  • Web/UX familiarity (Figma, Webflow, or WordPress) and light motion/video editing experience

Required Skills & Tools

  • Adobe Creative Cloud (Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign)
  • Notion
  • Slack

Schedule & Pay

  • Part-Time position; 10 hours per week with flexible scheduling; ability to meet US PST review deadlines as needed
  • Fully remote role for a US based client
  • Pay ranges from ZAR 5500 - ZAR 8000 per month, based on skills and experience.
  • Includes long-term growth potential, portfolio-worthy campaigns, and structured onboarding

System Requirements

  • Internet speed of at least 20 Mbps upload and download
  • Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher
  • 8 GB of RAM or higher
  • Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer
  • HD 720p webcam
  • Headset with microphone

Benefits

  • Competitive pay rates
  • Consistent hours and predictable workload
  • Fully remote work with long-term potential
  • Direct collaboration with the Founder, making a real impact
  • Supportive and inclusive work environment
  • Opportunity to grow alongside a boutique marketing consultancy

If you are a proactive, detail-obsessed, autonomous individual and fluent with various design tools and able to match a clean, modern aesthetic, apply today!
