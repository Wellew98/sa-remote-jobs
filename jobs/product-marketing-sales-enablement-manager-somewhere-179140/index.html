About the Company

Our client is an innovative, fast-growing organization dedicated to delivering top-tier commercial solutions through a high-performing, global team. Their company culture prioritizes extreme ownership, high autonomy, and clear execution, enabling remote professionals to make an immediate, direct impact without micromanagement. Partnering with our client offers independent contractors competitive compensation in USD, complete geographical flexibility, and the opportunity to build scalable marketing systems alongside visionary business leaders.

About the Role

As the Growth Marketing Manager, you will serve as the engine driving brand excellence, sales enablement, and demand generation across key commercial channels. In this role, you will bridge the gap between marketing and sales, owning the creation of ICP-segmented sales collateral, conference marketing campaigns, website builds, and customer story pipelines. Your work will directly empower sales representatives with the tools, heat maps, and messaging required to accelerate win rates across core customer segments. This position is ideal for an analytical, creative, and proactive marketing leader who thrives in an asynchronous, remote-first environment.
