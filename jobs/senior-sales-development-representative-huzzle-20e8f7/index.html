About Huzzle

At Huzzle, we connect exceptional talent with top opportunities at leading companies across the UK, US, Canada, Europe, and Australia. Our clients include fast-growing startups and established businesses in SaaS, MarTech, FinTech, and EdTech.

Unlike traditional outsourcing, you’ll be placed directly with a client as a core member of their team—giving you real ownership, career progression, and long-term stability.

Job Summary

We are hiring a highly organized and consultative Senior SDR / Sales Development Assistant to support a rapidly scaling legal and advisory business. This role is ideal for someone experienced in selling high-trust, intangible services and managing a high-volume consultation pipeline while maintaining exceptional client communication.

You will be responsible for qualifying inbound and outbound opportunities, scheduling and confirming consultations, following up on proposals, handling objections, collecting payments, and independently closing select estate planning engagements.

This is a client-facing role requiring strong communication skills, CRM discipline, and the ability to navigate sensitive financial and legal conversations with professionalism and empathy.

Respond to all inbound inquiries within the same business day
