About the Company

Our client is a premier, full-service digital marketing agency that has spent two decades at the forefront of the search and digital landscape. Driven by our philosophy of "making the complex simple," we focus on connecting strategy, media, creative, and technical execution into unified marketing systems rather than fragmented, reactive tactics. We are looking for an experienced Klaviyo expert to join our team and drive end-to-end email and lifecycle marketing systems for our clients.

About the Role

As our Klaviyo & Email Marketing Specialist, you will take full ownership of the lifecycle marketing ecosystem—from high-level strategy and advanced segmentation to creative design and technical execution. You will bridge data and design to build revenue-driving email campaigns, high-converting automated flows, and seamless retention strategies.

Key Responsibilities

  • Strategy & Planning: Develop and execute comprehensive email marketing strategies, retention roadmaps, and content calendars tailored to client goals.
  • Flows & Automations: Map out, design, and optimize automated lifecycle journeys (e.g., Welcome Series, Abandoned Cart, Post-Purchase, Win-Back, VIP) inside Klaviyo.
  • Campaign & Creative Execution: Write compelling copy, design visual email templates, and manage end-to-end email assembly and scheduling.
  • Segmentation & Deliverability: Build dynamic, data-driven audience segments to deliver personalized messaging; monitor domain health, send cadence, and deliverability metrics.
  • Testing & Optimization: Run systematic A/B tests on subject lines, send times, design layouts, and CTA placement to continuously improve open, click-through, and conversion rates.
  • Reporting & Analysis: Analyze campaign and flow performance metrics, delivering actionable insights and clear growth opportunities to team leadership and clients.

Requirements

  • Klaviyo Mastery: Proven experience building, managing, and scaling campaigns and automations within Klaviyo.
  • Hybrid Skillset: Exceptional blend of strategic thinking, copywriting, and visual email design capabilities.
  • Data-Driven Mindset: Hands-on experience with dynamic segmentation, customer journey mapping, and performance analytics.
  • Agency Agility: Ability to balance multiple client accounts, prioritize deliverables, and manage deadlines in a fast-paced agency environment.
  • Technical Savvy: Deep understanding of email deliverability best practices, dynamic tags, and basic HTML/CSS adjustments for email templates.
