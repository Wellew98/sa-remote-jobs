Role Description

This is a full-time remote role for an Email Marketer at a growing US based company. The Email Marketer will be responsible for creating and executing targeted email campaigns to drive customer engagement and sales, analyzing performance metrics, and collaborating with cross-functional teams to align with marketing objectives.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Communications, or a related field preferred.
  • Demonstrated experience in managing email marketing campaigns.
  • Strong analytical abilities to interpret data and make informed, data-driven decisions.
  • Exceptional written communication skills with a keen eye for detail.
  • Proficient in various email marketing platforms and tools.

Preferred Qualifications

  • 2+ years experience in email marketing.
  • Proven track record in optimizing email campaigns for better performance.
  • Familiarity with email compliance regulations

Compensation

$1,500 - $2,500 per month, paid in USD depending on experience.

Responsibilities

  • Design and implement effective email marketing campaigns aimed at boosting engagement, conversions, and sales.
  • Develop innovative email marketing strategies to enhance customer retention and acquisition.
  • Analyze and monitor email campaign performance metrics to drive continuous improvement in future initiatives.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure alignment of email campaigns with overarching marketing objectives.
  • Oversee the email marketing calendar and scheduling to guarantee timely execution of campaigns.

Details

  • Independent contractor agreement paid 2x monthly.
  • Client has a desire for long term commitment.
