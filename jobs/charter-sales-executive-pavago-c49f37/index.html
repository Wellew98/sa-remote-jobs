About the Role

We’re hiring a polished, relationship-driven Charter Sales Executive to convert qualified inbound opportunities into booked private jet charters and long-term client relationships.

You’ll support a leading private aviation company serving high-net-worth individuals, executives, entrepreneurs, and discerning travelers worldwide .

This is a high-touch sales role where responsiveness, trust, discretion, and exceptional service directly impact revenue . You’ll manage the client journey from initial inquiry through booking and post-flight follow-up while working closely with operations to deliver a seamless experience.

If you have experience in

luxury sales, private aviation, high-ticket services, hospitality, travel, real estate, financial services, or other relationship-driven sales environments , this could be an excellent fit.

What a Typical Day Looks Like

You may start by reviewing active charter inquiries and following up with clients considering flight options.

Throughout the day, you’ll speak with prospective and existing clients, understand their travel requirements, recommend appropriate solutions, coordinate with operations, manage bookings, and follow up on active opportunities.

You’ll keep the CRM current, maintain visibility across your pipeline, and ensure clients receive prompt communication throughout their journey.

In short

you turn qualified private aviation inquiries into booked flights while building trusted client relationships that generate repeat business.

What Happens After You Apply

Right after you apply, you’ll receive an email invitation from

Spark Hire

to record your Intro Video. It’s a short, self-recorded video completed on your own time and is the final step needed to complete your application.

Instead of repeating your background across multiple screening calls, you get to introduce yourself once and show the hiring team how you communicate before the first interview. If there’s a strong fit, your first live conversation can focus on your sales experience and the opportunity rather than starting from scratch.

Don’t overthink it. You can record your responses multiple times, and discarded takes are not shared.

Please keep an eye on both your inbox and spam folder for the Spark Hire invitation.
