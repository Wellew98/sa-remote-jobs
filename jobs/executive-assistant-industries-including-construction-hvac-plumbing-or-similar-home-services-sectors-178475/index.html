About the Company: About the Company: Rapidly expanding HVAC enterprise committed to delivering top-tier home solutions.

About the Role: Seeking a highly organized and proactive Executive Assistant to support the owner of multiple businesses in the Plumbing, HVAC, and Mortgage Finance industries. This is a fast-paced role that requires exceptional organizational skills, strong communication, and the ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously.

Key Responsibilities

  • Manage executive calendar, appointments, and scheduling.
  • Coordinate meetings and prepare agendas and meeting notes.
  • Monitor and manage email inboxes, responding or escalating as appropriate.
  • Coordinate with internal teams, contractors, vendors, and clients.
  • Assist with project coordination across multiple businesses.
  • Track tasks, deadlines, and follow up to ensure completion.
  • Prepare reports, presentations, and business documents.
  • Organize digital files and maintain accurate records.
  • Handle travel arrangements and itinerary planning when required.
  • Assist with customer communications and administrative requests.
  • Support hiring, onboarding, and other operational initiatives as needed.
  • Perform research and special projects assigned by the executive.

Qualifications

  • Minimum of 3 years in Executive Support, Personal Assistance, or Operational Coordination supporting a business owner / C-suite.
  • Superior command of English, both in written and oral communication.
  • Proven capacity to organize complex tasks and handle various priorities at once.
  • Meticulous attention to detail and a strong sense of professional autonomy.
  • Thrives within a rapid-scale entrepreneurial setting.
  • Capable of forecasting requirements and resolving issues before they arise.
  • Tech-savvy: Skilled in Google Workspace, MS Office, Slack, and Zoom/Teams; proficient with Project Management (Asana, ClickUp, Monday.com , Trello) and CRM platforms (HubSpot, Jobber, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro).
  • Desirable: Competence in utilizing AI platforms like ChatGPT to enhance output.
