Design Operations Lead (Packaging & Pre-Press)

PositionFull-Time Remote Contractor (37 hours/week)

ScheduleEuropean Business Hours Alignment (CET / Central European Time overlap required)

Location (US Client): Open to talent in South Africa (SA)

About the Business

We are a fast-growing, profitable European e-commerce scale-up specializing in custom-printed takeaway packaging for businesses of all sizes. Operating across 14 European markets, we connect food service brands, cafes, and restaurants with sustainable, high-quality custom packaging.

We have been recognized for exceptional revenue growth three years in a row, with ambitious plans to triple our business over the next three years. Today, our international team of 30+ includes a strong, certified remote culture focused on clarity, performance, and work-life balance.

About the Role

At our company, every customer design must become accurate, production-ready packaging artwork—quickly, consistently, and without mistakes.

As our Design Operations Lead , you will manage our remote team of four designers and own how the design operation performs and develops. Your core mission is to deliver high-quality work within strict service-level agreements (SLAs) while continuously improving the processes, workflows, and AI/automation tools that make the team faster and easier to scale.

This is a hands-on leadership role . You will improve workflows and coach team members, but you will also leverage your deep pre-press and production expertise to review complex artwork, solve print production challenges, and step into production artwork creation whenever needed.

Key Responsibilities

  • Process Improvement & Automation: Identify bottlenecks and manual work, then build smarter workflows, systems, automations, and AI-supported processes to increase team output, speed, and reliability.
  • Team Leadership & Development: Create structure, clarity, and accountability across a remote design team through effective planning, structured feedback, performance tracking, and individual coaching.
  • Artwork Quality Ownership: Maintain a rigorous quality bar, review complex customer artwork, and catch pre-press errors before files reach manufacturing or print production.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Act as the operational bridge between the Design team and international sales, customer support, and production partner teams.
  • Hands-On Production Support: Step into customer design production when required—from reviewing and correcting artwork in Adobe Illustrator to helping the team navigate high-volume surges.

Key Performance Metrics (First 3 Months)

  • Establish a consistent management rhythm and implement measurable workflow improvements.
  • Lead the team to maintain a 99.9% turnaround compliance rate within our standard 2-hour customer design response window.

Qualifications & Requirements

  • Relevant Experience: 4+ years of professional experience in graphic design, pre-press, packaging design, print production, or production artwork.
  • Pre-Press Expertise: Advanced hands-on experience creating, reviewing, correcting, and preparing production-ready artwork for professional print (vector graphics, CMYK, spot colors, dielines, and vectorizing low-quality source files).
  • Software Mastery: Expert proficiency in Adobe Illustrator and professional print preparation tools.
  • Operational Drive: Proven track record of improving workflows, systems, or team processes, ideally incorporating automation or AI tools to eliminate repetitive manual tasks.
  • Leadership Capability: Experience leading, coordinating, or mentoring designers/production workflows. (We welcome senior individual contributors ready to take their first formal management step if they possess strong operational capabilities).
  • Schedule Alignment: Ability to work full-time hours (37 hrs/week) aligned with Central European Time (CET/CEST) operating hours (max 1–2 hours shift variation).

Perks & Working Conditions

  • Flexibility: 100% remote working setup.
  • Work-Life Balance: Standard 37-hour work week with generous paid time off, paid sick days, and paid parental leave. Overtime is rare and always compensated.
  • Impact & Culture: High-growth scale-up environment with certified high employee satisfaction and clear paths for personal development.
