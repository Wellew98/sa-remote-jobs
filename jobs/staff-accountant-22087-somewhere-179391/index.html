About the Company

Our client is a well-established, US-based residential home improvement company dedicated to delivering top-tier renovation solutions and exceptional service. Built on a culture of reliability, craftsmanship, and operational excellence, they provide a collaborative and stable environment where every team member's work directly impacts the company’s bottom line. They value autonomy, integrity, and long-term commitment, offering an independent contractor a secure, rewarding professional home with consistent leadership support.

About the Role

Our client is looking for a sharp, dependable Staff Accountant to take full ownership of day-to-day bookkeeping, accounts payable, and payroll operations. Reporting directly to the COO—an experienced accountant by trade who provides clear guidance, priorities, and mentorship—this individual will handle the hands-on financial operations to maintain clean, accurate books that leadership can rely on for critical business decisions. This is an ideal, long-term opportunity for a dedicated accounting professional seeking stability, trust, and a role where they can stay motivated and thrive for years to come.

Key Responsibilities

- Manage Daily Bookkeeping: Reconcile bank, credit card, and transactional accounts within QuickBooks Online (QBO), maintaining an accurate chart of accounts and profit and loss statement.

- Generate Reliable Financials: Produce clear, up-to-date financial reports and statements to enable leadership to make informed, data-driven operational decisions.

- Execute Accounts Payable & Sub-Contractor Payments: Review, verify, and code incoming vendor and sub-contractor invoices, managing the bill pay workflow, commission disbursements, and payment runs in alignment with cash flow and COO guidance.

- Process Payroll & Variable Compensation: Run end-to-end payroll via Gusto, accurately calculating commissions, bonuses, and rate adjustments while keeping employee records current.

- Handle HR Administrative Workflows: Facilitate seamless, compliant onboarding and offboarding processes for incoming and departing staff members.

- Collaborate with Leadership: Work in close partnership with the COO to resolve transactional discrepancies, optimize financial workflows, and uphold internal financial controls.

Qualifications

Required Qualifications

- Proven experience as a Staff Accountant or similar role managing full-cycle bookkeeping for a US-based contractor, construction, or home services operation.

- Hands-on proficiency in QuickBooks Online (QBO), with proven experience reconciling accounts and maintaining clean P&L records.

- Solid experience in accounts payable (AP) within a construction or home services environment, specifically managing sub-contractor payments, commission payouts, invoice verification, approval processes, and cash-flow-conscious payment runs.

- Demonstrated hands-on experience processing US payroll using Gusto, including commissions and performance bonus calculations.

- Willingness and enthusiasm to perform hands-on, transactional accounting tasks under the direction of an experienced finance leader.

- Strong English communication skills (written and spoken) with exceptional attention to detail.

Preferred Qualifications

- Prior controller-level or senior accounting background paired with a genuine comfort performing staff-level tasks.

- Direct experience handling employee onboarding and offboarding administration.
