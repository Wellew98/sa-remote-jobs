About the Company

Our client is a fast-moving startup operating and supporting two rapidly growing online casino brands across the crypto and U.S. social casino markets.

The business operates within a larger ecosystem of highly established gaming operators. One brand is a well-funded, rapidly scaling crypto casino backed by an international gaming group that operates 17 additional casinos generating more than $50 million per month in gross gaming revenue. The second is a U.S.-facing social casino backed by a major U.S. operator generating more than $10 million per month in gross profit.

The company is building for significant scale. With strong execution, the team believes the business has the potential to exceed $100 million in annual profit within the next year.

This is an entrepreneurial, high-ownership environment where core team members have the opportunity to play a meaningful role in building the company rather than simply joining an established organization.

Role Overview

We are seeking a Chief Product Officer to own the entire product, design, creative, and content experience across two online casino brands.

This is not a traditional executive position where the CPO operates several layers removed from execution. The successful candidate will be a highly hands-on product and creative leader who can personally make things happen while simultaneously building the systems, processes, and AI infrastructure that allow the organization to scale.

The CPO will own how both brands look, feel, communicate, and evolve. This includes product strategy, UI/UX, creative direction, content, social media, and the development of AI-powered workflows capable of generating and managing content at scale.

The ideal candidate has exceptional creative taste, understands Web3 and internet culture, moves extremely quickly, and is completely bought into an AI-first way of working.

Key Responsibilities

Product Ownership & Strategy

- Own the product experience across both casino brands from strategy through execution.

- Define and continuously refine product vision, priorities, roadmap, and user experience.

- Take initiatives from initial concept through implementation without waiting for excessive direction.

- Identify opportunities to improve acquisition, engagement, retention, usability, and overall customer experience.

- Understand user behavior and translate insights into actionable product improvements.

- Maintain awareness of trends across crypto, Web3, online gaming, social casinos, and digital entertainment.

- Make fast, well-reasoned product decisions while remaining willing to change direction when better information becomes available.

- Establish clear deliverables and realistic ETAs and maintain accountability for execution.

Design & Creative Direction

- Own all design and creative direction across both brands.

- Establish a high standard for visual identity, product design, marketing creative, and brand consistency.

- Personally develop and contribute creative concepts rather than functioning exclusively as a manager.

- Own UI/UX decisions across the digital product experience.

- Develop distinctive creative identities appropriate for each brand and audience.

- Create and oversee digital assets, campaigns, social creative, and other visual touchpoints.

- Translate business and product objectives into compelling creative concepts.

- Continuously raise the standard of creative execution across the organization.

Content & Social Media

- Own content strategy and execution across both brands.

- Manage and grow the brands' social media presence.

- Develop content that feels native to crypto, Web3, gaming, and broader internet culture.

- Identify relevant trends, conversations, memes, and cultural moments that can become brand opportunities.

- Build repeatable systems for producing high-quality content quickly.

- Monitor content performance and use results to guide future creative decisions.

- Ensure both brands maintain distinctive voices while supporting broader business objectives.

AI Systems & Automation

- Build AI-powered workflows capable of generating and managing content at scale.

- Treat AI as a fundamental operating layer rather than simply another productivity tool.

- Use AI agents and automation to eliminate repetitive manual work wherever practical.

- Develop workflows for creative ideation, copywriting, imagery, video, research, analysis, and content production.

- Continuously identify processes that can be automated or accelerated using AI.

- Build repeatable systems that allow the team to accomplish more without proportionally increasing headcount.

- Experiment rapidly with emerging AI tools, models, agents, and workflows.

- Accept intelligent experimentation and occasional mistakes as part of building better automated systems.

- Question repetitive manual processes and determine whether they should instead be handled by AI or automation.

Cross-Functional Leadership

- Act as the central owner connecting product, design, content, social, and creative execution.

- Work directly with founders and collaborate closely with development, marketing, operations, and other team members.

- Communicate priorities, decisions, blockers, and progress clearly and frequently.

- Immediately surface issues that could prevent the team from delivering.

- Ask questions until requirements and expectations are fully understood rather than making assumptions.

- Provide direct, actionable feedback and encourage the same level of transparency from others.

- Build systems and processes that make the broader organization faster and more effective.

Required Qualifications

- 2+ years of relevant experience across product, design, creative leadership, Web3, digital products, or a closely related field.

- Demonstrated ability to own digital products or major product initiatives from concept through execution.

- Exceptional creative taste and strong visual judgment.

- Strong understanding of UI/UX and consumer-facing digital product experiences.

- Hands-on creative capabilities with the willingness to personally execute when necessary.

- Genuine understanding of crypto, Web3, gaming, and internet-native culture .

- Strong familiarity with social media and digital content creation.

- Advanced comfort with modern AI tools, LLMs, agents, and automation workflows .

- Strong systems-thinking mentality with a desire to automate repetitive work.

- Ability to operate at exceptional speed without losing sight of priorities.

- Strong written and verbal communication skills.

- Comfortable working with significant autonomy and accountability.

- Ability to thrive in a startup where processes and priorities can change rapidly.

- Willingness to challenge ideas constructively while changing opinions when better information becomes available.

- Availability to work in person in Cape Town, Monday through Friday .

Preferred Qualifications

- Experience within online casinos, gaming, crypto, Web3, fintech, or another internet-native consumer business.

- Experience owning multiple digital products or brands simultaneously.

- Strong UI/UX or product design background.

- Experience creating and managing social media content.

- Experience with AI-generated copy, imagery, video, and other creative media.

- Experience building AI agents or automated content-production systems.

- Experience operating in an early-stage or founder-led startup.

- Strong understanding of online communities, memes, social trends, and emerging digital culture.

- Experience rapidly testing and iterating on consumer-facing products.

Core Working Principles

Speed & Bias for Action

Speed is a major part of the company culture. Team members are expected to operate with a strong "do it now" mentality , respond quickly during working hours, and distinguish between tasks requiring deep work and issues that can be resolved immediately.

Radical Communication

Problems and blockers should be communicated immediately. Team members are expected to ask questions until they fully understand an objective rather than proceeding with incomplete information.

Public Accountability

The team works around clear deliverables, realistic timelines, and visible ownership. Individuals are expected to communicate what they will deliver, when they will deliver it, and raise concerns early if those commitments are at risk.

Build the Machine

The organization takes an AI-first approach to operations. Team members should continually ask whether repetitive work can be performed by AI agents, automation, or better systems rather than manually repeating the same process.

Day-One Mentality

The company values capability, curiosity, adaptability, and execution over pedigree. Candidates should be comfortable challenging established assumptions, learning quickly, and operating without the bureaucracy or thinking patterns associated with large organizations.

Strong opinions are encouraged, but they should be loosely held and open to change when new information emerges.

Tools & Technologies

The exact technology stack will continue to evolve, but candidates should be comfortable working across:

-

AI and LLM platforms

- AI agents and workflow automation tools

-

AI image and video generation platforms

- Product and UI/UX design software

-

Creative design tools

- Social media and content publishing platforms

-

Product management and collaboration tools

- Analytics and performance reporting platforms

The ability to rapidly learn and adopt new tools is more important than familiarity with any single platform.

Reporting

The Chief Product Officer will work directly with company leadership and have significant autonomy over product, design, content, social media, and creative direction across both brands.

This individual will be expected to operate as an owner rather than waiting for leadership to dictate each next step.

Success Metrics

Success in this role will be measured by

- Quality and evolution of the overall product experience.

- Speed from idea to implementation.

- Strength and consistency of design and creative execution.

- Product engagement and customer experience improvements.

- Growth and engagement across social channels.

- Ability to create distinctive identities for both brands.

- Volume and quality of content produced through scalable systems.

- Successful implementation of AI agents and automated workflows.

- Reduction of unnecessary repetitive manual work.

- Ability to consistently deliver against clearly communicated priorities and timelines.

- Proactive identification and communication of blockers.

- Contribution to the overall growth and scale of both brands.

Ideal Candidate Profile

The ideal candidate sits at the intersection of a product leader, creative director, designer, content creator, and AI-native builder .

They have real taste. They understand what makes a digital product or brand feel compelling without needing someone else to prescribe every creative decision. They also understand Web3 and internet culture well enough to build for the audience rather than simply borrowing crypto terminology.

They move extremely quickly. When something can be handled now, they handle it now. When they hit a blocker, they communicate it rather than sitting on it. When they do not understand something, they ask questions until they do.

Most importantly, they think in systems. When they encounter repetitive work, their instinct is to ask how AI agents, automation, or better processes could do it at scale .

This is an ideal opportunity for someone who wants to help build the machine, not simply occupy an executive seat within one.
