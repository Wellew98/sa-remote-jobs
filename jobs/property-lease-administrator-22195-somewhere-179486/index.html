Property & Lease Administrator

Location & Employment Details

  • Location: Remote
  • Schedule: Full-time, 40 hours per week, with working hours that overlap the U.S. Pacific and Mountain time zones.
  • Reports to: The company's finance and operations leads, who will work closely with you to design processes during setup.
  • Target start: December, ahead of bringing property management in-house in Q1.

About the Company

We are a rapidly growing U.S.-based commercial real estate investment firm focused on industrial outdoor storage (IOS). Our properties are large, secured yards with a smaller building on site, leased to the blue-collar businesses that keep cities running: construction and equipment rental companies, landscaping and snow removal fleets, and logistics operators parking trucks and trailers.

We buy these sites one at a time, often off-market from longtime owner-operators, improve them, and hold them in a growing portfolio across multiple states in the western U.S. The leases are triple net and the tenants tend to stay for years. We're adding one to two properties a month and building the team to match.

About the Role

Today our properties are run by outside property management firms. We're bringing property management in-house in Q1, and you'll be the person who runs it day to day.

Our leadership team spends its time sourcing deals, running due diligence, and managing construction after close. That leaves the portfolio itself, the lease calendar, rent and reimbursements, insurance, taxes, vendors, and tenant communication, needing a dedicated owner. You'll help us build those processes from the ground up, take over the properties from the outgoing managers, and keep everything running as we add new sites every month.

This is a first hire for the function. You'll get real training and support on how we want things done, and real ownership of how they get done.

Key Responsibilities

Process Build-Out & Transition

  • Work with leadership to design and document property management processes, templates, and calendars
  • Manage the handoff of each property from third-party managers, including leases, tenant contacts, vendor contracts, and open items
  • Onboard newly acquired properties into the portfolio and set up their records

Lease Administration

  • Abstract leases and maintain a master record of key terms, dates, escalations, renewal options, and tenant obligations
  • Own the lease calendar and flag upcoming expirations, rent increases, and notice deadlines well in advance
  • Maintain organized digital files for every property and tenant

Insurance, Tax & Compliance

  • Collect and track tenant certificates of insurance and chase lapses
  • Track property tax bills and deadlines across jurisdictions
  • Monitor tenant compliance with lease requirements

Tenant & Vendor Coordination

  • Serve as the day-to-day contact for tenants by phone and email
  • Coordinate vendors for fencing, paving, gates, security, and site repairs, including quotes, scheduling, and follow-through
  • Escalate sensitive tenant conversations to leadership with clear context

Reporting

  • Keep leadership updated on portfolio status, open issues, and upcoming deadlines
  • Prepare recurring property and portfolio reports as the function matures

Required Qualifications

  • 2+ years of property management or lease administration experience supporting U.S. properties
  • Strong written and verbal English, comfortable handling phone calls with U.S. tenants and vendors
  • Working knowledge of leases: reading them, tracking key dates, and following up on tenant obligations
  • Proven ability to stay organized across many properties, deadlines, and open items at once
  • Comfortable taking training and then running a process independently
  • Able to work full-time hours that overlap the U.S. Pacific and Mountain time zones
  • Reliable remote setup with high-speed internet and backup power

Preferred Qualifications

  • Commercial or industrial property experience
  • Experience with property management software such as Yardi, AppFolio, or MRI
  • Experience tracking certificates of insurance and property tax across multiple jurisdictions
  • Has helped set up or transition property management processes before
  • Prior work for a U.S. real estate owner, investor, or brokerage

Ideal Candidate Profile

You are highly organized and proactive. You keep a clean calendar across dozens of deadlines and spot the lease expiration or lapsed insurance certificate before anyone has to ask.

You're comfortable building a process with input from leadership and then owning it end to end. You communicate clearly and confidently with tenants and vendors, and you know when a conversation needs to go up the chain. You're steady in a small, fast-growing team where a new property lands every few weeks.

Why Join Us

  • Build the function. You'll shape how property management works here from day one, not inherit someone else's system.
  • Growth with the portfolio. We're adding properties every month, and the role grows as the portfolio does.
  • Direct access to leadership. You'll work hand in hand with the people making the decisions and see the impact of your work right away.
  • A full-time, long-term seat. This is a core role on a small team, not a short-term project.
