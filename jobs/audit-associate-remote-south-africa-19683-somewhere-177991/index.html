Audit Associate (Remote - South Africa | Independent Contractor | US Hours) Overview We’re looking for a detail-oriented and motivated Audit Associate to join our growing team. This role is ideal for someone based in South Africa who is eager to work with a US-based client, gaining international exposure while working remotely. As an Audit Associate, you’ll play a key role in supporting audit engagements, analysing financial data, and ensuring compliance with regulatory standards. You’ll be working closely with a collaborative, high-performing team that values accuracy, accountability, and continuous improvement. What You’ll Be Doing - Assist in the execution of audit engagements from planning through to completion - Analyse financial statements and identify discrepancies or areas of risk - Prepare and maintain audit documentation in line with audit standards - Perform testing of internal controls and substantive procedures - Work closely with senior auditors and managers to meet deadlines and client expectations - Communicate findings clearly and professionally - Stay up to date with accounting and auditing standards (US GAAP exposure is a plus) What We’re Looking For - Completed degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field - Progress toward or completion of SAICA articles is highly advantageous - 1–3 years of audit experience (preferably in a firm environment) - Strong understanding of audit principles and financial reporting - High attention to detail and strong analytical skills - Ability to work independently in a remote setup - Excellent written and verbal communication skills Working Arrangement - Location: Remote (South Africa-based candidates only) - Hours: Must be available to work US business hours (this is non-negotiable) - Type: Independent contractor (not a permanent employment role) - Tools: You’ll need a stable internet connection and a reliable workstation What Makes This Role Different This isn’t just another audit role. You’ll be: - Gaining hands-on experience with a US-based client - Working in a fast-paced, international environment - Building global exposure early in your career - Developing strong remote collaboration skills that are highly valued worldwide
