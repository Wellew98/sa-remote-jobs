ISTA Personnel Solutions South Africa is a dynamic and fast-growing BPO company based in South Africa, specialising in providing top-tier operational support to U.S.-based companies. We are not a recruitment agency. We act as a dedicated extension of our clients' teams, delivering high-quality service with precision, efficiency, and a personal touch.

We are looking for an experienced Full-Cycle Recruiter to support the client's ongoing hiring requirements. You will manage the recruitment process for dietary and housekeeping positions, handling a high volume of applications and candidates while ensuring an efficient and professional hiring experience.

Using Apploi, the client's applicant tracking system, you will review applications, screen and interview candidates, coordinate next steps and maintain consistent communication throughout the recruitment process.

This role is suited to someone who is dynamic, engaging and highly organised, with proven full-cycle recruitment experience and confidence communicating with candidates over the phone and on camera.

Please note

This is a salaried position offering a basic salary only, with no commission or incentive structure.

PLEASE NOTE

Working Hours

Monday to Friday, 4:00pm – 1:00am SAST (subject to US daylight saving changes).

Public Holidays

You will be required to work on both South African and US public holidays. South African public holidays will be compensated in accordance with the BCEA.

Internet Requirements

A fixed fibre line with a minimum speed of 25 Mbps (upload and download) and the ability to support a wired Ethernet connection is mandatory. Applicants without a fixed fibre line cannot be considered.

Power Backup

A reliable power backup solution is required to manage load shedding and power outages. Applicants without a power backup cannot be considered.

Work Environment

Fully remote (you must be located in South Africa).

Key Responsibilities

Manage the full-cycle recruitment process for dietary and housekeeping positions, from application review through to hire.

Review applications and shortlist suitable candidates.

Conduct telephone screenings and video interviews to assess candidate experience and suitability.

Coordinate interview outcomes, next steps and follow-ups with candidates and the recruiting team.

Maintain an active candidate pipeline to support ongoing hiring requirements.

Manage candidate records, notes, and application statuses accurately in Apploi.

Keep candidates informed and engaged throughout the recruitment process.

Tools & Systems

Apploi

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)

Microsoft Office

Microsoft Outlook

Requirements

Proven full-cycle, high-volume recruitment experience.

Strong telephone screening and video interviewing skills.

Clear, professional spoken English and confident communication skills.

Dynamic, engaging and personable personality.

Strong organisational skills and attention to detail.

Ability to manage a high volume of candidates and competing priorities.

Basic computer literacy and confidence navigating different systems.

Working knowledge of Microsoft Office and Outlook.

Ability to work independently and as part of a team.

(Beneficial to have):

Previous recruitment experience within healthcare, dietary, housekeeping or other hourly and service-based environments.

Experience using Apploi or another applicant tracking system (ATS).

If you are not contacted within 14 working days of submitting your application, please consider your application unsuccessful.
