Paired is a global staffing and recruiting agency that pairs remote work with top-tier talent. We help individuals from around the world connect with great companies that are looking for their specific skill set. Our mission is to provide great jobs to talented people, no matter where they are located.

About Our Clients

We are representing four fast-growing brands across the agency and direct-to-consumer (DTC) landscape.

As these businesses continue to scale their marketing and paid media efforts, they are looking for creative talent who can bridge the gap between creative production, AI-powered content, and performance marketing.

Whether you're a video editor who knows how to turn raw footage into high-converting ads, an AI creative producer who can generate compelling UGC-style videos, or a creative strategist who can take an idea from concept to execution, we'd love to hear from you.

Responsibilities

Depending on your expertise and the specific opportunity, your responsibilities may include:

AI-Powered Video Production: Create engaging video ads using AI video generation tools, AI avatars, synthetic UGC, voiceovers, and other AI-assisted creative workflows.

Performance Video Editing: Edit short-form video ads for paid social platforms, including Meta, TikTok, and other relevant channels, with a focus on hooks, pacing, storytelling, and conversion.

Creative Ideation & Strategy: Develop creative concepts, angles, hooks, scripts, storyboards, and ad variations based on audience insights, product positioning, and campaign objectives.

End-to-End Creative Execution: Take creative projects from initial research and ideation through scripting, production, editing, iteration, and final delivery.

UGC-Style Ad Creation: Produce authentic-feeling, creator-style content using AI-generated talent, product demonstrations, testimonials, and other direct-response creative formats.

Performance Optimization: Collaborate with paid media and growth teams to understand ad performance, identify creative opportunities, and develop new iterations based on data and testing insights.

Creative Testing & Iteration: Produce multiple variations of hooks, messaging, visuals, and formats to support structured creative testing and campaign optimization.

AI Workflow Development: Explore new AI tools, techniques, and workflows that improve creative quality, speed, and production efficiency.

We're interested in candidates from one or more of these backgrounds:

1. Performance Video Editors

Strong video editing skills and an understanding of direct-response advertising.

Experience creating short-form video ads for paid social.

A good eye for hooks, pacing, storytelling, retention, and conversion-focused editing.

Familiarity with tools such as Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, CapCut, or similar.

2. AI Creative Producers

Hands-on experience using AI tools to generate or enhance video content.

Familiarity with AI video, image, voice, avatar, and creative production tools.

Ability to create UGC-style ads, product videos, and other advertising assets with AI.

A strong understanding of how to combine AI-generated assets with editing, storytelling, and brand direction.

3. Creative Strategists

Ability to research audiences, competitors, products, and advertising trends.

Experience developing creative briefs, ad concepts, hooks, scripts, and testing roadmaps.

Understanding of direct-response marketing and performance creative principles.

Ability to translate campaign objectives and performance insights into actionable creative ideas.

4. Hybrid Creative Talent

Comfortable working across ideation, scripting, AI production, video editing, and creative optimization.

Able to take ownership of a project from concept to final deliverable.

Interested in experimenting with new AI tools and creative workflows.

Comfortable collaborating with paid media, marketing, and creative teams.

Tools & Skills That Could Be Relevant

Experience with some of the following is a plus

AI video & generation

Runway, Kling, Veo, Sora, HeyGen, Synthesia, or similar tools.

AI image & creative generation

Midjourney, Flux, ChatGPT, or similar tools.

Video editing

Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, CapCut, or equivalent.

Voice & audio

AI voice generation, voiceovers, sound design, and audio editing.

Performance marketing

Meta Ads, TikTok Ads, direct-response creative, creative testing, and ad performance analysis.

To respect everyone’s time, we will not move forward with applications that only include: general video editing portfolios, cinematic showreels, YouTube editing portfolios, documentary work, podcast editing, wedding videos, corporate videos, and motion graphics portfolios without paid advertising experience

We're not looking

for someone who has simply experimented with AI tools. We want people who can use them to create compelling, commercially effective creative assets.

This role is specifically for creators who specialize in performance advertising for DTC brands.

Important

This is a shared talent opportunity across our clients. Applying does not guarantee placement with a specific company, but your profile will be considered for the opportunities that best match your experience and capabilities.

Competitive USD salary based on experience.

Fully remote position — work from anywhere.

Opportunity to work with cutting-edge AI creative technology.

High ownership role with direct impact on company growth.

Collaborate with a fast-moving international team.
