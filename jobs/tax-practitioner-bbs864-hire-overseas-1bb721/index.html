We're looking for a

Tax Practitioner to handle the full tax compliance cycle for a simulated portfolio of small, owner-managed companies, their directors and shareholders, sole proprietors, and family trusts, from registrations and provisional tax through to annual returns, SARS correspondence, and disputes. Your work becomes the training data and workflow designs that a cutting-edge AI tax system learns from. This is a long-term, permanent role working directly with the founder and an AI partner at the frontier of AI development in tax.

This is not a traditional tax role. Yes, you will be running each simulated client's full tax year to a professional standard. But you will also be documenting the steps, decisions, and judgments behind your work, not just the outputs, and helping design and test the AI workflows that follow your methodology. If you have hands-on SARS eFiling experience across at least two full filing seasons, understand the South African tax compliance cycle end to end, and have a genuine interest in breaking tax work into clear, teachable steps for AI to follow, this role is a strong fit.

What You'll Work On

Tax Returns

Prepare individual tax returns (ITR12) including capital gains, collecting IRP5s, IT3 certificates, medical aid and retirement annuity certificates, travel logbooks, and rental schedules, and check auto-assessments before accepting them

Complete company tax returns (ITR14) from the signed AFS and the AFS Accountant's tax computation including small business corporation and assessed loss positions

Estimate taxable income for company provisional tax (IRP6) for the first and second periods and plan top-up payments to avoid underestimation penalties

Estimate taxable income for individual provisional tax (IRP6) for owners and sole proprietors and advise on third top-up payments

Prepare dividends tax returns (DTR01) and payments when clients declare dividends and confirm exemption declarations

Identify donations above the annual exemption and prepare IT144 returns and payments

Prepare IT3(t) submissions and ITR12T trust returns and track distributions and vesting to beneficiaries

SARS Administration

Reconcile EMP201 payments to payroll for interim and annual periods and issue IRP5 and IT3(a) certificates (EMP501)

Handle one-off client requests including tax numbers, notices of registration, copies of assessments, statements, and confirmation letters

Maintain eFiling profiles, practitioner mandates, banking details, and registered particulars; reconcile statements of account; follow up refunds; and obtain Tax Compliance Status PINs

Draft objections and appeals, requests for suspension of payment and penalty remission requests, and track each to outcome

Respond to SARS verifications and audits with complete supporting documents, working with the AFS Accountant on relevant records, and submit requests for correction

Register clients for income tax, provisional tax, PAYE, SDL, UIF, and VAT including mandatory and voluntary VAT registration

Update registered representatives and public officers with SARS when directors change

Register clients as importers or exporters with SARS Customs and keep customs details current

Request instalment payment arrangements for tax debts and track client compliance

Deregister dormant clients from income tax, PAYE, and VAT with SARS and file CIPC deregistration once tax affairs are in order

Portfolio and Deadline Management

Keep a deadline calendar for every client covering filing season dates, provisional tax periods, EMP501 periods, and ITR14 due dates

Plan workload around filing season and the February provisional tax peak

Check that SARS third-party data, prior assessments, and payments match the client's information before submitting

Handle requests to simulated clients and bookkeepers to obtain missing or unclear documents

Flag turnover above the R2.3m VAT registration threshold when a client is not registered

Raise tax risks and planning points to your AI partner including loan accounts, fringe benefits, and missed deductions

Building AI for Tax

Prepare each simulated return and submission to a professional standard so it serves as high-quality training data

Record the steps, decisions, and judgments behind your work in a way that can be taught to an AI model

Design and refine the tax workflows the AI follows, step by step

Test the AI against your own work, identify where it goes wrong, and help fix it

Use AI models day to day to prepare returns, draft SARS correspondence, and check your own work, and apply sound judgment about when their output is correct

What You Bring

BCom in Accounting or Taxation, National Diploma in Accounting, or equivalent

1 to 3 years in a tax practice or an accounting practice with tax work, including at least two full filing seasons and provisional tax cycles

Hands-on SARS eFiling experience including registrations, ITR12, IRP6, ITR14, and EMP501 submissions, and responding to verification letters

Experience drafting objections or SARS audit responses for review by a senior

Working knowledge of the Income Tax Act and the Tax Administration Act including capital gains, provisional tax, and the dispute rules

Able to manage your own portfolio of returns through filing season with limited supervision

Strong Excel skills including lookups, reconciliations, and tax workings

Detail-oriented and deadline-driven with a clear understanding that SARS dates and penalties do not move

Clear, polite written English for client requests, SARS letters, and objections

Genuine interest in AI and in breaking tax work into clear, teachable steps

Comfortable working with AI models and able to check and challenge what they produce

Nice to Have

Experience running objections and appeals independently including applying the dispute rules, building the argument, and anticipating how SARS will respond

Experience handling SARS audits especially VAT and PAYE including judging what to disclose and when to push back

Experience with trusts and IT3(t) submissions including distributions, vesting, and attribution rules

Experience spotting when donations tax and dividends tax apply such as loans to trusts or dividends in specie

Exposure to SARS Customs registrations and processes

A SAIT Tax Technician or Tax Professional designation, or working towards one

Experience with payroll systems such as Sage VIP, Sage Pastel Payroll, or SimplePay

Exposure to practice tax software and Xero, Sage, or Pastel client systems
