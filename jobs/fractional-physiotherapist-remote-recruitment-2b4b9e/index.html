Fractional Physiotherapist Job Overview Our client is a leading online health and wellbeing coaching business dedicated to helping people achieve lasting improvements in their health, fitness and mindset. Rather than focusing on quick fixes, they empower clients to make sustainable lifestyle changes through expert coaching, personalised support and evidence-based guidance. They are seeking an experienced Fractional Physiotherapist to join their multidisciplinary team on a flexible, part-time basis. Working remotely, you will support clients experiencing pain, injuries or movement limitations, helping them continue progressing safely towards their health and fitness goals. This is an excellent opportunity for a qualified physiotherapist who enjoys working collaboratively within a coaching environment and wants the flexibility to fit the role around existing professional commitments. Key Responsibilities Client Assessment & Rehabilitation Conduct remote physiotherapy assessments via video consultations. Assess and manage musculoskeletal pain, injuries and movement limitations. Develop personalised rehabilitation and self-management plans. Monitor client progress and adjust recommendations as required. Provide evidence-based guidance to support safe recovery and ongoing physical activity. Exercise Modification & Injury Prevention Adapt training programmes to accommodate injuries, existing health conditions and physical limitations. Advise clients on mobility, movement quality and injury prevention strategies. Support clients in maintaining consistent progress while reducing the risk of further injury. Promote safe exercise practices and long-term physical wellbeing. Coaching Team Support Collaborate closely with health coaches, dietitians and mindset specialists. Provide practical advice to coaches on exercise modifications and safe programming. Support the wider team with guidance on movement, rehabilitation and load management. Contribute to delivering a consistent, client-centred experience. Clinical Governance Recognise when clients require referral for in-person assessment or specialist medical care. Maintain accurate client records and clinical documentation. Deliver services in accordance with professional standards and best practice guidelines. Maintain confidentiality and uphold ethical practice at all times. Qualifications & Experience To be successful in this role, you should have: Current registration as a Chartered or Registered Physiotherapist. A minimum of 4 years' post-qualification experience . Strong experience in musculoskeletal, sports or rehabilitation physiotherapy. Previous experience providing physiotherapy consultations remotely or via telehealth is advantageous. Excellent assessment, clinical reasoning and rehabilitation skills. Outstanding communication skills with the ability to explain complex concepts clearly and empathetically. Professional proficiency in English, both written and spoken. A proactive, self-motivated approach with excellent organisational skills. A genuine passion for helping clients achieve sustainable health outcomes. Desirable Qualifications Relevant postgraduate qualifications or specialist certifications. Ongoing Continuing Professional Development (CPD). Experience working alongside fitness professionals or health coaches. Experience within health coaching, wellness or preventative healthcare environments. Equipment Requirements Reliable laptop or desktop computer with webcam and microphone. Stable, high-speed internet connection. Private, professional workspace suitable for confidential video consultations. Working Hours Fractional, part-time position. Flexible working hours, scheduled around client demand. Remote role with the flexibility to fit around your existing professional commitments. What We Offer Flexible, part-time working arrangement. Fully remote position. The opportunity to work within a collaborative, multidisciplinary health and wellbeing team. The chance to make a meaningful difference in clients' long-term health and quality of life. A supportive, purpose-driven environment focused on sustainable client outcomes. Opportunity to grow your involvement as client demand increases.
