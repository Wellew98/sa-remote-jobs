Executive Assistant to CEO (Remote)

LocationRemote (LATAM / South Africa / Philippines)

Employment TypeFull-Time (Independent Contractor)

Reports toFounder / CEO

About the Company

We are a fast-growing, tech-enabled company operating in the digital commerce space, focused on building scalable systems, efficient operations, and high-performing teams.

With a lean team of ~10 and ambitious growth plans, we are looking for a high-impact Executive Assistant to CEO who can act as a force multiplier for the Founder — bringing structure, speed, and operational clarity to the business.

This is not a traditional admin role. This is a strategic operator who thrives in ambiguity, builds systems, and proactively drives execution.

The Role

This role exists to buy back the Founder’s time and increase organizational efficiency .

You will operate as the central nervous system of the business , ensuring that priorities are executed, communication flows smoothly, and systems are continuously improved.

You’ll move between executive support, operations, and light project management , owning outcomes — not just tasks.

Key Responsibilities

Executive Support & Communication

  • Manage and optimize the Founder’s calendar, priorities, and daily workflow
  • Coordinate meetings, agendas, and follow-ups to ensure decisions turn into action
  • Act as a gatekeeper and facilitator across internal and external communications
  • Draft, review, and send emails, documents, and internal updates on behalf of the Founder
  • Ensure nothing falls through the cracks across tasks, deadlines, and commitments

Operations & Systems Management

  • Build, document, and improve internal processes and workflows
  • Identify inefficiencies and proactively implement better systems
  • Manage tools such as project management platforms, CRMs, and communication tools
  • Create dashboards, trackers, and reporting systems to improve visibility

Project Management & Execution

  • Own and drive cross-functional projects from start to finish
  • Track progress across initiatives and ensure timely execution
  • Coordinate across team members to align priorities and deliverables
  • Hold stakeholders accountable to deadlines and outcomes

Automation & AI Leverage

  • Identify opportunities to streamline workflows using automation tools
  • Implement AI tools (ChatGPT, Zapier, etc.) to improve efficiency and output
  • Continuously optimize how the business operates using modern tools

Strategic Support

  • Act as a thought partner to the Founder on business priorities
  • Prepare briefs, research, and summaries to support decision-making
  • Help translate high-level ideas into actionable plans
  • Step in wherever needed to solve problems and unblock progress

Ideal Candidate Profile

Experience

  • 3–7+ years as an Executive Assistant, Operations Manager, or Chief of Staff
  • Experience supporting founders, executives, or fast-growing teams
  • Background in startups, agencies, or high-growth environments strongly preferred
  • Experience working with US-based companies is a plus

Skills & Traits

  • Proactive Ownership: You anticipate needs before they arise
  • Systems Thinker: You naturally organize chaos into structure
  • Execution-Oriented: You move fast and get things done without handholding
  • Highly Organized: Strong attention to detail and follow-through
  • Strong Communicator: Clear, concise, and professional in written and verbal communication
  • Tech-Savvy: Comfortable with modern tools, automation, and AI
  • Adaptable: Thrives in fast-paced, evolving environments

Nice to Have

  • Experience with tools like Notion, Slack, Asana, ClickUp, HubSpot, etc.
  • Exposure to automation tools (Zapier, Make, etc.)
  • Multilingual (English required; Spanish/French a plus)

Working Style & Expectations

  • Operates independently with minimal oversight
  • Comfortable working in a remote, async environment
  • Aligns closely with US time zones (partial overlap preferred)
  • Takes full ownership of outcomes, not just tasks

Compensation & Structure

  • Monthly compensation: ~$1,800 – $2,500 USD depending on experience
  • Fully remote, long-term opportunity
  • Independent contractor engagement

Why This Role Matters

This is a high-leverage hire that directly impacts the company’s growth.

Success in this role means

  • The Founder is focused only on high-value work
  • The team operates more efficiently and with clarity
  • Systems scale with the business instead of breaking
