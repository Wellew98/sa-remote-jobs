About the Role

The company is a fast-growing email marketing agency that helps insurance businesses win and keep customers. The founder has scaled a previous company with a largely overseas team and is now building this company the same way.

We're hiring a Marketing Manager / Customer Success lead: a marketer first, who also owns client relationships. You'll run email campaigns for our insurance clients and be their day-to-day point of contact, keeping them informed, happy, and growing with us.

What You Will Do

Marketing

  • Plan, build, and launch email campaigns and sequences for a portfolio of insurance clients.
  • Run day-to-day campaign operations in Instantly and our other email tools: list setup, sequencing, deliverability, and testing.
  • Track performance (opens, replies, leads generated) and turn results into clear next steps.
  • Write and refine email copy and offers that fit each client's audience.

Customer success

  • Be the main point of contact for your client accounts, from onboarding onward.
  • Run regular check-ins and report results in plain, client-friendly language.
  • Spot risks early, handle issues calmly, and keep clients engaged and renewing.
  • Share client feedback with the founder to improve our service and playbooks.

Qualifications & Skills

Required Qualifications

  • 3+ years in marketing, with hands-on email marketing or lead generation experience.
  • Hands-on experience with Instantly (strongly preferred) or similar cold email and outreach tools.
  • Client-facing experience at an agency or in an account management role.
  • Comfortable owning several client accounts at once and staying organized under deadlines.
  • Excellent spoken and written English; confident leading client calls.
  • Data-minded: you read campaign metrics and know what to change next.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Agency background serving US clients.
  • Experience marketing to, or working with, insurance businesses.
  • Familiarity with deliverability best practices, CRMs, and lead-gen data tools.
  • Experience in email sequencing tools, such as Instantly or other similar tools

Compensation & Success

  • Compensation: Competitive monthly salary in USD, matched to experience. Strong candidates will be paid well. Target is $2,000 - $3,500 USD/mo
  • Growth: Join early at a fast-growing agency and help shape how we serve clients as the team scales.
  • Ownership: Work directly with an experienced founder who has built and sold a company with an overseas team, and own real results for your accounts.
