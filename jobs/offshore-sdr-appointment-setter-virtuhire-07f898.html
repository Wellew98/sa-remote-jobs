Offshore SDR / Appointment Setter

VirtuHire to support US operations remotely from South Africa Full-time Open to South Africa

<p><strong>ABOUT THE OPPORTUNITY</strong></p><p>A fast-growing US-based solar lead generation business is expanding its offshore sales and appointment-setting team and is seeking a confident, energetic, and experienced SDR / Appointment Setter to support US operations remotely from South Africa.</p><p>This business operates within a high-volume, fast-paced sales environment where speed-to-lead, professionalism, and customer engagement are critical to success.</p><p><strong>ROLE OVERVIEW</strong></p><p>This is a phone-heavy outbound and inbound appointment-setting role focused on engaging homeowners who have submitted interest in solar online. The successful candidate will qualify prospects, handle basic objections, and book qualified appointments directly into the CRM.</p><p>When inbound lead flow slows down, the role will shift toward outbound follow-up activities, including re-engaging aged homeowner databases and following up with no-show appointments.</p><p>The ideal candidate is warm, articulate, resilient, and highly comfortable speaking with US-based customers throughout the day.</p> <p><strong>KEY RESPONSIBILITIES</strong></p><p>• Contact warm inbound leads who have shown interest in solar products and services<br>• Qualify homeowners against company criteria<br>• Handle basic objections professionally and confidently<br>• Schedule qualified in-person appointments within the CRM<br>• Conduct outbound follow-up calls to aged homeowner lists<br>• Re-engage missed or no-show appointments<br>• Maintain accurate CRM notes and lead statuses<br>• Meet daily and monthly appointment-setting targets<br>• Deliver a high-energy, professional customer experience on every call<br>• Work collaboratively with the US-based sales team</p><p><strong>REQUIREMENTS</strong></p><p>• Previous appointment-setting, telesales, cold-calling, or SDR experience<br>• Prior solar industry experience highly preferred<br>• Experience within adjacent industries such as insurance, mortgage, home Friendly, confident, and conversational phone manner<br>• Emotionally resilient and comfortable handling rejection<br>• Ability to work independently in a remote environment<br>• Comfortable working high call volumes daily<br>• Full-time availability aligned to US Eastern Time<br>• Saturday availability is non-negotiable<br>• Experience using GoHighLevel or similar CRMs advantageous<br>• Stable internet connection and professional remote working setup required</p><p><strong>TOOLS &amp; PLATFORMS</strong></p><p>• GoHighLevel (CRM and dialer)<br>• Excel<br>• Browser-based VoIP / dialer
