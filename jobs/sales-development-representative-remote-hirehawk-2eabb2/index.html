About Us

HireHawk helps companies build high-performing offshore teams by connecting them with rigorously vetted global talent across Latin America, and South Africa. We go beyond traditional staffing by managing recruiting, payroll, compliance, and ongoing workforce support—so clients can scale with confidence. We’re building a globally distributed team that values ownership, high standards, and clear expectations.

About the Role

This is a call-first, high-activity outbound SDR role focused on creating new business opportunities.. You’ll spend your day dialing, handling objections live, following up fast, and consistently generating qualified meetings and pipeline. You’ll speak with HR leaders, hiring managers, recruiters, and business executives to uncover hiring needs and introduce tailored offshore hiring solutions. If you thrive in a target-driven environment and want a role where your activity directly drives results, you’ll do well here.

Responsibilities

Generate qualified pipeline through high-volume outbound prospecting (cold calls, email, and LinkedIn)

Engage prospects live, lead strong discovery conversations, and book meetings that convert to real opportunities

Handle objections confidently and move conversations forward without getting rattled

Build persona-based outreach approaches that improve response and meeting rates over time

Track and summarize objections and market feedback, then recommend improvements to scripts, hooks, and messaging

Maintain clean CRM hygieneaccurate notes, stages, follow-ups, and activity tracking

Follow up with discipline and speed to keep momentum and build credibility with prospects

Partner with recruiting teammates to align client needs with available talent pipelines

Consistently meet or exceed KPIs for calls, meetings booked, qualified opportunities, and pipeline created
