Who are we?

SupportYourApp

is a global Intelligent Support-as-a-Service leader, partnering with tech companies and industry leaders like Mastercard, Calm, and MacPaw in 30+ countries since 2010 to deliver secure customer and technical support. We operate globally, supporting clients in 60 languages across diverse industries. So what if you had a chance to be part of the world’s leading SaaS, software, or hardware solutions?

Join our community as a

Technical Support Consultant today and thrive in a multicultural and multilingual environment while enjoying your home office. Unlock your potential by mastering new skills and achieving challenging goals in cooperation with our People First management approach. Excited? Let’s see what it takes.

What you will do

Assist clients with setting up and troubleshooting secure private access solutions, ensuring proper access to internal resources;

Help clients configure and troubleshoot multifactor authentication (MFA) for VPN and private access solutions;

Analyze network traffic to identify potential bottlenecks and recommend performance improvements without compromising security;

Advise on bandwidth allocation and prioritization for VPN users to ensure reliable and secure connections;

Analyze log files to investigate technical issues and identify their root causes;

Provide technical guidance and troubleshooting support across different operating systems;

Collaborate with clients and technical teams to resolve issues and ensure a smooth user experience.

What you need to succeed in this role

Solid knowledge of networking concepts, including IPsec tunnels, DNS, packet tracing, network ports, and firewall functionality;

Understanding of network infrastructure, including VPNs, routing, split tunneling, and connector-based WireGuard tunnels;

Basic understanding of network protocols such as OpenVPN, IKEv2, and WireGuard;

Ability to read and analyze log files to troubleshoot technical issues;

Confidence in working with Windows, Linux, macOS, Android, and iOS;

Strong analytical thinking and research skills;

Positive, proactive, and responsible attitude;

Personal computer with at least 8 GB of RAM and a stable internet connection (minimum 50 Mbps download and 40 Mbps upload).

Will be a great plus

Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS or Microsoft Azure;

Understanding of Identity Providers (IdPs) and Single Sign-On (SSO) solutions, such as Google Workspace and Microsoft Entra ID (formerly Azure AD);

Customer-facing experience.
