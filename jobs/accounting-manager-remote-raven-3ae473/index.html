Accounting Manager

Remote Raven South Africa (remote) Full-time Finance & Accounting Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

Our client's Bookkeeping Division supports a growing roster of clients who need part-time, high-quality accounting support. Each bookkeeper manages multiple client accounts simultaneously, and the division is led by a Division Manager who oversees operations and quality. We're hiring an Accounting Manager to grow alongside the Division Manager and help take the team to the next level. This is a player-coach role — meaning you are not managing from a distance. You carry your own client workload, stay sharp on the technical side, and step in to support team members when they're stuck, struggling, or need guidance on a tricky accounting situation. The right person for this role is an experienced bookkeeper or accountant ready to take on leadership responsibility without stepping away from the work they love. You lead by example, mentor by doing, and help build a team culture where accuracy, accountability, and client satisfaction are the standard. Responsibilities Team Leadership & Support — Player-Coach This is the defining characteristic of the role. You are in the trenches with your team, not above them. Work alongside the Division Manager to oversee the day-to-day operations of the bookkeeping team Serve as a go-to resource for team members who encounter complex accounting situations, client-specific challenges, or workflow questions Provide hands-on guidance and mentorship to bookkeepers who are struggling — stepping in to help problem-solve, not just redirect Conduct regular check-ins with team members to monitor workload, quality, and morale Identify skill gaps across the team and support development through coaching, training, and knowledge sharing Help onboard new team members and ensure they are set up for success from day one Client Bookkeeping — Own Your Portfolio You still do the work. This is not a purely managerial role. Manage a portfolio of assigned client accounts across multiple industries, delivering accurate and timely bookkeeping services Perform core bookkeeping functions including transaction categorization, bank reconciliations, accounts payable and receivable, and month-end close Prepare and deliver monthly financial reports and statements to assigned clients Maintain clean, organized, and audit-ready client files and records Communicate directly with clients to address questions, gather documentation, and provide financial updates Quality Review & Oversight Review team members' work for accuracy, completeness, and compliance with client expectations and accounting standards Catch and correct errors before they reach the client — your review is the last line of quality control Establish and reinforce consistent standards for how work is documented, formatted, and delivered across the team Flag recurring quality issues to the Division Manager and help develop solutions Operations & Workflow Support the Division Manager in managing client assignments, team capacity, and workflow across the bookkeeping division Help ensure all client deadlines are met and that no accounts fall behind due to bandwidth or complexity Contribute to internal processes, templates, and documentation that help the team work more efficiently Identify tools and workflow improvements and bring recommendations to leadership
