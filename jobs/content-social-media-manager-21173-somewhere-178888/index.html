Content & Social Media Manager

Work HoursMonday to Friday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM UK Time

Pay Range$1,000–$1,600/month

Location of SearchSouth Africa, Philippines, or Latin America

Work LocationRemote

Company Overview

Our client operates within the financial services sector, with a focus on mortgages, personal finance, financial products, and consumer financial education.

The organization is looking to strengthen its digital presence through high-quality, original content that makes complex financial subjects clear, engaging, accurate, and accessible to everyday consumers. Because much of the content relates to the Irish financial services landscape, the successful candidate will need to develop a strong understanding of the Irish mortgage market, lending environment, housing policy, and relevant economic developments.

Role Overview

We are looking for a creative and hands-on Content & Social Media Manager to create, publish, and optimize content across multiple digital channels.

The ideal candidate is an excellent English writer and strong content creator who can independently develop ideas, write copy and scripts, produce and edit videos, manage social media, and analyze content performance. Creative ability and quality of work are more important than experience with specific tools or industries.

Duties and Responsibilities

  • Content Creation: Research topics and produce original articles, social posts, captions, scripts, and other written content.
  • Social Media Management: Plan, publish, and manage content across LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and X/Twitter.
  • Video Production & Editing: Develop concepts, write scripts, record, and edit frequent/daily video content using Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, CapCut, native social editing tools, or similar.
  • Audio & Podcast Production: Record and edit podcast and other audio content.
  • Visual Content: Create graphics, infographics, and supporting visual assets.
  • Content Strategy: Identify relevant trends, topics, and opportunities for engaging content.
  • Analytics: Track content and social media performance and use insights to improve future content.

Qualifications

  • Exceptional written and spoken English is mandatory.
  • Strong original writing, storytelling, and content creation skills.
  • Hands-on video creation and editing experience; specific editing software is not required.
  • Experience managing content across social media platforms.
  • Understanding of social media strategy, analytics, and performance measurement.
  • Ability to independently create high-quality content without relying on generative AI.
  • Audio/podcast editing and visual content creation experience are advantageous.
  • Familiarity with financial services, mortgages, property, fintech, banking, or personal finance is preferred but not required for candidates with strong demonstrated content ability.

Portfolio

Candidates should be able to provide samples of content they personally created , such as written content, social posts, videos, scripts, podcasts, or visual assets. The quality of the work and the candidate's direct contribution will be prioritized over specific tools, job titles, or industry background.
