Operations Associate (HubSpot CRM, Deal Pipeline & Excel Reporting) – Remote | EST Hours Position Type: Full-Time, Remote Working Hours: U.S. Eastern Time (EST) Location: South Africa or Latin America About the Role At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring an Operations Associate to keep a high-value deal pipeline organized, accurate, and moving forward. This is not a traditional finance, investment banking, or sales role . You won’t be responsible for complex financial modeling, sourcing deals, or hitting sales quotas. Instead, your focus will be on execution, organization, CRM management, reporting, and consistent follow-through . You’ll own the operational details behind long deal cycles—making sure every opportunity, follow-up, next step, meeting, and task is properly tracked and completed. If you’re the kind of person who keeps systems clean, follows up without being reminded, and makes sure nothing falls through the cracks, this role is a strong fit.
