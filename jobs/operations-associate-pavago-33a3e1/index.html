About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring an

Operations Associate

to keep a high-value deal pipeline organized, accurate, and moving forward.

This is

not a traditional finance, investment banking, or sales role . You won’t be responsible for complex financial modeling, sourcing deals, or hitting sales quotas.

Instead, your focus will be on execution, organization, CRM management, reporting, and consistent follow-through .

You’ll own the operational details behind long deal cycles—making sure every opportunity, follow-up, next step, meeting, and task is properly tracked and completed.

If you’re the kind of person who keeps systems clean, follows up without being reminded, and makes sure nothing falls through the cracks, this role is a strong fit.

What a Typical Day Looks Like

You’ll start by reviewing HubSpot for new activity, overdue tasks, outstanding follow-ups, and deals requiring attention.

Throughout the day, you’ll send follow-ups, update deal stages and notes, maintain Excel trackers, coordinate meetings, organize deal information, and prepare reports or materials for leadership.

You’ll also review the pipeline for anything that has stalled and proactively make sure the appropriate next step happens.

In short

you keep the deal pipeline clean, organized, and moving so leadership never has to chase updates or wonder what happens next.
