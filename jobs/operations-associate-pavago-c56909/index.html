About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a highly organized

Operations Associate

to keep a high-value deal pipeline accurate, structured, and consistently moving forward.

This is not a traditional finance, investment banking, or sales role. You won’t be responsible for complex financial modeling, sourcing deals, or hitting sales quotas. Instead, your focus will be on execution, HubSpot CRM management, reporting, deal coordination, and consistent follow-through .

You’ll own the operational details behind long, 6–12 month deal cycles , ensuring every opportunity, follow-up, next step, meeting, and task is properly tracked and completed.

If you’re experienced with HubSpot, comfortable with Excel, and naturally make sure nothing falls through the cracks, this role is a strong fit.

What a Typical Day Looks Like

You’ll start by reviewing HubSpot for new activity, overdue tasks, outstanding follow-ups, and deals requiring attention.

Throughout the day, you’ll send professional follow-ups, update deal stages and notes, maintain Excel and Google Sheets trackers, coordinate meetings, organize deal information, and prepare reports or materials for leadership.

You’ll also regularly review the pipeline for opportunities that have stalled and proactively ensure the appropriate next step happens.

In short

you keep the deal pipeline clean, organized, and moving so leadership never has to chase updates or wonder what happens next.
