We are hiring a Business Development Assistant to support a sustainable procurement consultancy working with corporate clients globally on supply chain decarbonisation, alongside an e-learning product for procurement professionals. This role will primarily support outbound sales activity, inbound lead management, customer onboarding, and e-learning platform administration.

The ideal candidate has genuine SDR/BDR outbound sales experience rather than a generalist EA or administrative background. They should be comfortable managing lead follow-up and nurturing sequences, reviewing customer communications for accuracy, and handling administrative processes with a high level of attention to detail.

Key Responsibilities

Outbound Sales Support

  • Manage outbound sequences in Apollo once sequences have been set up, including responding to prospect replies and scheduling meetings.
  • Review AI-drafted outbound emails for accuracy before they are sent.
  • Verify customer names, company information, context, and other relevant details in outbound communications.
  • Support consistent lead nurturing and follow-up cadences.

Inbound Lead Management

  • Manage inbound leads generated through the e-learning platform.
  • Review and organize inbound leads.
  • Transfer qualified leads into the appropriate Apollo sequences.
  • Ensure leads are followed up promptly and accurately.

Customer Onboarding

  • Complete customer supplier onboarding forms and portals as part of the new business closing process.
  • Generate customer invoices using Xero or HubSpot.
  • Ensure onboarding and invoicing information is accurate and completed efficiently.

E-Learning Platform User Management

  • Provide basic account administration for existing customers using the e-learning platform.
  • Assist customers with password resets and other straightforward account-related requests.
  • Maintain accurate customer information and support a positive user experience.

General Administrative Support

  • Assist with ad hoc administrative tasks as required.
  • Support the wider team with operational tasks related to sales, customers, and business administration.

Required Qualifications

  • Previous experience in an SDR, BDR, outbound sales, or business development role.
  • Experience using Apollo or similar sales engagement platforms.
  • Comfortable managing outbound leads, follow-up, and lead nurturing cadences.
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.
  • Ability to review and verify customer information before communications are sent.
  • Strong organizational and time-management skills.
  • Ability to work independently and manage multiple tasks effectively.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience supporting B2B sales processes.
  • Experience with customer onboarding and supplier portals.
  • Familiarity with Xero and/or HubSpot.
  • Experience working with e-learning or SaaS platforms.
  • Experience working with sustainability, procurement, or supply chain-related businesses.

Required Skills & Tools

  • Apollo.
  • Xero.
  • HubSpot.
  • E-learning platform administration.
  • Strong written communication and proofreading skills.
  • Lead management and nurturing.
  • High attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Strong administrative and organizational skills.

Schedule & Pay

  • Part-Time position; Monday - Friday UK time.
  • Pay ranges from R3,500 - R4,000 per month.
  • Annual raises and confirmed holidays.

System Requirements

  • Internet speed of at least 20 Mbps.
  • Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher.
  • 8 GB of RAM or higher.
  • Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer.
  • HD 720p webcam.
  • Headset with a microphone.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to work with a global sustainable procurement consultancy.
  • Exposure to B2B sales, supply chain decarbonisation, and e-learning.
  • Opportunity for increased hours and responsibility as the role develops.
  • Flexible remote working environment.

If you have genuine outbound SDR/BDR experience, are confident managing lead follow-up and customer interactions, and have exceptional attention to detail, we'd love to hear from you!
