LOOKING FOR SOUTH AFRICAN CITIZENS WHO ARE BASED IN SOUTH AFRICA CANDIDATES

PositionOperations Specialist

Working Schedule9a-5p EST or 9a-5p PST

About the Role

We are seeking a diligent, detail-oriented and reliable Operations Specialist to support a fast-paced, dynamic business environment. The ideal candidate is an analytical thinker who thrives in managing both routine operations and project work with minimal oversight. You will play a key internal role in drafting communications, managing data in Excel, and ensuring operational efficiency.

Key Responsibilities

  • Draft and refine written communications for internal and client-facing purposes
  • Manage and analyze data using Excel, including complex spreadsheets and formulas
  • Operate within a software GUI and collaborate via Slack
  • Handle detailed, repetitive tasks with care and consistency
  • Execute urgent, last-minute assignments with quick turnaround when required
  • Proactively take ownership of projects and daily responsibilities
  • Maintain accurate records and documentation
  • Communicate clearly and professionally with team members and stakeholders

Key Requirements

  • Minimum of 1 year of experience
  • Sales and Marketing experience
  • Excellent written English and communication skills
  • Diligent, detailed, and reliable. Able to both propose solutions and ask for help when needed
  • Strong computer literacy; especially MS Windows and Excel
  • Advanced Excel proficiency (formulas, formatting, data handling)
  • Demonstrated ability to work independently and think critically
  • Proven track record of reliability and attention to detail
  • Comfortable working in an environment that demands initiative
  • Flexible to U.S. business hours (Eastern or Pacific Time)

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in healthcare or IT industries
  • Familiarity with tools such as remote desktops, Slack, and Notion

Why Join

This is a unique opportunity to work directly with a business leader who values autonomy, quality, and thoughtful execution. You’ll be given the trust and space to excel while contributing meaningfully to a lean, agile team.
