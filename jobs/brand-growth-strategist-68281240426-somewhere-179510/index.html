About the Company

An Amazon-focused e-commerce agency that partners with established, high-revenue consumer packaged goods (CPG) brands is hiring a full-time Brand Growth Strategist. This is a role for a proven Amazon operator who can own strategy for serious brands, not a junior hire learning the basics.

Role Overview

You will own growth for a portfolio of Amazon brands. That means running advertising across Sponsored Products, planning promotions, and shaping the wider strategy that grows each brand. This is more than an ads manager seat. Plenty of people can launch campaigns and report on ad metrics. This role needs someone who understands how advertising, promotions, and overall account performance fit together, and who makes decisions with the full picture in mind.

The brands in this portfolio are established and sizable, and growing a brand at that level takes far more nuance than selling a simple commodity product. Positioning, category dynamics, pricing, and how every lever affects the others all matter.

The agency has worked with offshore talent before and knows the difference between someone who runs campaigns and someone who truly understands brand strategy. This role is for the second kind of person. The agency is willing to invest in genuinely great talent and expects that level of quality in return.

Success means growth that holds up across the whole account, sound judgment calls made without hand-holding, and recommendations the agency and its clients can trust.

Key Responsibilities

  • Own growth strategy for a portfolio of CPG brands selling on Amazon
  • Plan, manage, and optimize advertising across Sponsored Products
  • Plan and execute promotions that support each brand’s growth goals
  • Tailor strategy to the specific position, category, and goals of each brand
  • Spot what is driving or holding back performance across each account and act on it
  • Report on results in a way that explains what happened, why, and what comes next
  • Work alongside the agency team to turn strategy into results
  • Where the role is client-facing, walk brand partners through performance and plans

Required Qualifications & Skills

  • Proven track record as an Amazon brand manager, growth strategist, or growth marketer, with results you can speak to in detail
  • Hands-on experience running Amazon advertising, including Sponsored Products
  • Experience planning and running Amazon promotions
  • Experience managing established, high-revenue brands where strategy requires nuance beyond a single product
  • Ability to take a holistic account approach, connecting advertising to overall account performance rather than relying on a single ads metric
  • Strong analytical skills and the ability to turn data into clear decisions
  • Ability to work independently with a high standard of quality

Preferred Qualifications & Skills

  • Experience with consumer packaged goods (CPG) brands on Amazon
  • Agency experience managing multiple brands at once
  • Experience working directly with brand clients and presenting strategy to them

Working Arrangement

Full-time, remote. Open to candidates in Latin America, South Africa, and the Philippines. Candidates based in Latin America may work directly with brand clients.

Who Thrives in This Role

  • Thinks like a brand owner, not only a campaign operator
  • Sees the whole account, not just the ads dashboard
  • Has a strong point of view and backs it with evidence
  • Comfortable making judgment calls when the data does not give a clean answer
  • Holds a high bar for their own work and does not need to be chased
  • Communicates clearly and confidently with senior stakeholders
