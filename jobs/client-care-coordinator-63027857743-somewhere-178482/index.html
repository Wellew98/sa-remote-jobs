Client Care Coordinator

LocationRemote (Candidates based in South Africa or the Philippines preferred)

CompensationUSD $600/month base + performance-based incentives

IndustryInvestigations & Asset Recovery

ScheduleU.S. Eastern Time business hours

About the Company

A mission-driven investigations and recovery firm specializing in returning unclaimed assets to

rightful owners across North America. The team works to reconnect families and individuals with

long-lost funds that can support education, housing, or a new beginning.

About the Role

The Client Care Coordinator plays a pivotal role in supporting the return of unclaimed property

to its rightful owners. Acting as the bridge between potential clients and the sales team, this role

is responsible for lead qualification, outreach, and guiding clients through the agreement

process. You’ll be helping people access assets they didn’t know they were entitled to—work

that makes a meaningful difference.

Key Responsibilities

● Qualify leads and initiate outreach via phone, email, text, or mail

● Clearly explain the unclaimed property recovery process, company services, and

associated fees

● Guide clients through completing and submitting required agreements and documents

● Maintain consistent follow-up communication to support lead conversion

● Log all interactions and activity accurately in the company CRM (e.g., Salesforce)

● Ensure compliance with ethical standards, legal guidelines, and internal policies

● Participate in ongoing training and stay informed on industry best practices

Must-Have Qualifications

● Bilingual fluency in English and Spanish (additional languages a plus)

● Minimum 1 year of experience in customer service, sales, or client support

● Excellent verbal communication and active listening skills

● Highly organized with strong attention to detail in documentation and follow-up

● Ability to quickly build trust and rapport with potential clients

● Stable internet, personal laptop, and access to a quiet, professional home workspace

● Willingness to work U.S. Eastern Time business hours

What’s Provided

● Training materials, call scripts, and objection-handling guides

● CRM access and digital tools to track leads and document progress

● Ongoing team support and regular communication

Compensation & Incentive Structure

● Base Pay: USD $500/month (paid via Payoneer in two installments)

● Activity Bonus: $10 USD for each completed and qualifying Claimant Agreement or

UPRA

● Success Bonus: One-time incentive paid after successful claim settlement and

collection (claims may take 6–24 months to close; bonuses paid monthly post-collection)

See internal Milestone & Performance Addendum for onboarding expectations and activity

targets.

Why This Role Matters

This position is central to the company’s mission. You’ll be the trusted guide helping individuals

access funds they may have never known existed—funds that can change lives. Your clarity,

care, and consistency are the driving force behind every recovered dollar.

REQUIREMENTS

We are looking for candidates based in South Africa or the Philippines. Please note that we process only one active application at a time—if you’ve applied for multiple roles, kindly direct any updates to the team handling your first application. To start processing, submit your resume in English. The final offer will be at the client’s discretion and will depend on your interview performance, skills, and experience.
