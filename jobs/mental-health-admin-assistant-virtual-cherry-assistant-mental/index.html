We are hiring a Mental Health Admin Assistant (Virtual) a multi-provider group practice.This is a Part-Time remote role for a Tennessee, USA based company. This role is ideal for someone who is detail-oriented, empathetic, highly organized, and capable of seamlessly managing client tracking, scheduling, and admin workflows for psychological testing services.

Key Responsibilities

  • Schedule and track clients through the insurance-based psychological testing process, coordinating sessions across both psychologists and psychometricians
  • Monitor client progress through the testing pipeline, ensuring prior authorizations (handled by the billing manager) are confirmed before scheduling
  • Collect payments at each testing session and monitor client completion of required online measures and assessments
  • Send timely reminders and follow-up communications to clients as needed to keep testing appointments on schedule
  • Input specific testing CPT codes correctly into the system during scheduling (training provided)
  • Conduct virtual front-desk check-ins via video/screen during required hours while upholding HIPAA standards
  • Support general back-office administration and EMR record-keeping for a 15-provider group practice

Required Qualifications

  • 2+ years of hands-on experience in U.S. healthcare administration, medical office operations, or administrative care coordination
  • 2+ years of consistent use of EMR/EHR systems to record client demographics, schedule appointments, and manage administrative notes
  • 2+ years of experience communicating professionally with patients and providers, displaying clarity, confidence, and empathy in mental health settings
  • Strong organizational and tracking skills with high attention to multi-step client workflows
  • Excellent written and spoken English proficiency supported by professional administrative experience

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience supporting a mental health or behavioral health practice
  • General knowledge of U.S. medical/insurance coding concepts
  • Prior experience coordinating care within a multi-provider group practice

Required Skills & Tools

  • EMR/EHR Systems (Scheduling, Data Entry, Patient Records)
  • Video Conferencing / Virtual Check-in Software
  • Google Workspace / Microsoft Office Suite
  • Secure Practice Management & Messaging Tools

Schedule & Pay

  • Part-time position; Monday–Friday, approximately US Eastern Time (flexibility for occasional Saturday coverage as the business scales)
  • Fully remote role for a United States based client
  • Pay ranges from ZAR 6000 - ZAR 9000 (depending on experience and skill)
  • Includes structured onboarding, SOPs, training, and clear growth potential with milestone-based increases

System Requirements

  • Internet speed of at least 20 Mbps upload and download
  • Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher
  • 8 GB of RAM or higher
  • Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer
  • HD 720p webcam
  • Headset with microphone

Benefits

  • Competitive pay rates
  • Consistent hours and predictable workload
  • Fully remote work with long-term potential
  • Direct collaboration with a founder-led team, making a real impact
  • Supportive and inclusive work environment
  • Opportunity to grow alongside a rapidly scaling cleaning company
