About Assist World

Assist World partners with fast-growing businesses across the United States to build exceptional remote teams. We specialize in sourcing high-caliber talent for healthcare, professional services, finance, and growing SMEs, focusing on long-term placements rather than transactional recruitment. We're looking for a driven  Talent Acquisition Executive  who is passionate about identifying exceptional people and creating outstanding hiring experiences. This is an opportunity to become a key member of a rapidly growing international recruitment team with significant career progression opportunities.

Key Responsibilities

Candidate Sourcing

  • Source high-quality candidates across LinkedIn, job boards, referrals, and other recruitment channels
  • Build and maintain talent pipelines for healthcare, executive support, operations, marketing, sales, and administrative roles
  • Develop proactive sourcing strategies for difficult-to-fill positions
  • Continuously identify new sourcing platforms and recruitment methods

Recruitment & Screening

  • Conduct initial candidate interviews and competency assessments
  • Evaluate candidates for technical skills, communication, cultural fit, and long-term potential
  • Verify employment history, qualifications, and references where required
  • Present shortlisted candidates with detailed interview summaries and recommendations

Recruitment Operations

  • Manage candidate pipelines using the company's ATS and CRM
  • Coordinate interviews between candidates and clients
  • Ensure an exceptional candidate experience throughout the recruitment process
  • Maintain accurate recruitment documentation and hiring records
  • Track recruitment KPIs and hiring performance metrics

Collaboration

  • Work closely with Account Managers and Leadership to understand client requirements
  • Contribute to continuous improvements in recruitment processes
  • Support employer branding initiatives and recruitment marketing campaigns
  • Share market insights and hiring trends with the wider team

Required Qualifications

  • Previous recruitment or talent acquisition experience (agency or internal)
  • Strong sourcing experience using LinkedIn Recruiter and other recruitment platforms
  • Excellent interview and candidate assessment skills
  • Outstanding written and verbal English communication
  • Highly organized with exceptional attention to detail
  • Ability to manage multiple vacancies simultaneously
  • Comfortable working independently in a remote environment

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience recruiting for healthcare, executive support, or professional services
  • Experience recruiting internationally
  • Familiarity with ATS and CRM platforms
  • Experience with behavioural interviewing techniques
  • Strong understanding of remote hiring best practices

What We're Looking For

  • A proactive recruiter who takes ownership of every search
  • Someone who genuinely enjoys connecting exceptional people with life-changing career opportunities
  • A resilient, target-driven individual who thrives in a fast-paced environment
  • A team player with high standards and exceptional professionalism
  • Someone looking to build a long-term career with a growing international business

Growth Opportunity

As Assist World continues to expand globally, this role offers significant opportunities to progress into Senior Talent Acquisition, Team Leadership, Client Success, or Recruitment Operations management.
