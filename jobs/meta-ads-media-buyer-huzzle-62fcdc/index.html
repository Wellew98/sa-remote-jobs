Job Summary

We are seeking an experienced Meta Ads Media Buyer to join a fast-growing UK digital marketing agency specializing in lead generation for local service-based businesses, particularly home improvement companies and healthcare clinics.

The ideal candidate has a proven track record of generating qualified leads, phone calls, and booked appointments through Meta Ads while simultaneously managing a large portfolio of client accounts. You will take ownership of the entire lead generation funnel, from campaign strategy and creative production to tracking, optimization, and CRM integration.

Key Responsibilities

Plan, launch, manage, and optimise Meta Ads campaigns across approximately 18–25 client accounts.

Develop campaign strategies, including audience research, offers, hooks, and advertising angles.

Independently design static and carousel ads and edit short-form video creatives.

Write high-converting ad copy tailored to UK homeowners and healthcare audiences.

Build and manage full-funnel lead generation campaigns, including landing pages, instant forms, and CRM integrations.

Monitor campaign performance daily, optimising for cost per lead, lead quality, and booked appointments.

Implement and troubleshoot Meta Pixel, Conversions API, and Google Tag Manager tracking.

Conduct structured creative testing and implement broad targeting and Advantage+ audience strategies.

Improve lead quality through qualifying questions, landing page optimisation, and CRM feedback.

Resolve rejected ads, account restrictions, and learning phase issues.

Provide weekly account performance updates and monthly reporting.

Collaborate with client success managers to improve campaign performance and client outcomes

3+ years of hands-on experience running Meta Ads specifically for lead generation.

Proven experience generating qualified leads for local service-based businesses. E-commerce-only experience will not be considered.

Demonstrated experience simultaneously managing a large portfolio of client accounts, ideally close to 18–25 accounts across multiple industries.

Proven cost-per-lead results supported by real Ads Manager screenshots.

Strong creative production skills, including independent image design and video editing using Canva, Adobe, CapCut, or similar tools.

Strong technical knowledge of Meta Pixel, Conversions API, and Google Tag Manager, including setup and troubleshooting.

Experience with modern Meta Ads strategies, including creative-first targeting, broad audiences, Advantage+, and structured creative testing.

Strong understanding of lead qualification, lead quality, and converting leads into booked appointments.

Experience building and managing full lead generation funnels, including landing pages, forms, and CRM integrations.

Experience using CRM platforms such as GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Salesforce, or similar.

Excellent analytical, organisational, and time management skills.

Native or bilingual-level written and spoken English proficiency.

Based in South Africa with full-time availability during UK business hours and no conflicting client commitments.

Own laptop, reliable internet connection, and backup power for load shedding.

Nice-to-Haves

Previous experience working in a fast-paced digital marketing agency.

Experience managing UK-based client accounts.

Lead generation experience within home improvement, construction, roofing, healthcare, or chiropractic services.

Hands-on experience with GoHighLevel, including forms, triggers, workflows, and automations.

Knowledge of Meta advertising policies, health claims, and Special Ad Categories.

Experience resolving Meta Business Manager access issues and account restrictions.

Experience with GA4 and Looker Studio reporting.

Familiarity with Asana or similar project management tools.

Proficiency in Adobe creative tools.
