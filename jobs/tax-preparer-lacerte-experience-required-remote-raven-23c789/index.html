Our client is a virtual-first US accounting and advisory firm that works with small and growing business owners, and its tax practice is expanding. They are hiring a remote Tax Preparer (CPA) to prepare individual, business, and trust returns in full compliance with federal and state tax law, working directly inside Lacerte.

This is a hands-on preparer seat with real scope: you own returns from source-document intake through self-review and reviewer handoff, you communicate directly with US business clients, and outside the filing season the work shifts to estimated tax calculations, multi-year projections, and planning support. S-corporation and LLC strategy, estimated tax planning, and multi-state compliance are core to how this firm serves its clients, so the mix of returns is varied and the planning side is real, not an afterthought.

You will report to a US-based reviewer. Success at 90 days looks like this: your returns pass first-round review with minimal diagnostics, your client communication is timely and clear, and your workpaper file is organized enough that any reviewer can pick it up mid-engagement. The rate reflects that this is a CPA-level seat where the firm expects judgment, not just data entry.

Key Responsibilities

Tax Preparation & Filing

Prepare federal and state returns for Form 1040, Form 1120 / 1120S, Form 1065 and Form 1041

Enter financial data accurately in Lacerte from source documents and trial balances

Review prior-year returns and source documents before preparation starts

Identify deductions, credits, and tax planning opportunities while preparing

Ensure compliance with current federal and state tax law

Client Communication

Request missing documentation from clients with clear, itemized requests

Explain tax results clearly to clients in plain language

Respond to IRS and state notices and routine filing questions

Maintain professional client service standards in all correspondence

Quality Control & Review

Self-review returns before submission to the reviewer

Resolve discrepancies and review comments with reviewers promptly

Stay updated on federal and state tax law changes

Documentation & File Management

Maintain organized workpapers and digital files

Track filing deadlines, extensions, and estimated payment due dates

Document tax positions clearly enough to support reviewer sign-off

Follow client confidentiality and data security protocols

Tax Planning Support

Assist with quarterly estimated tax calculations

Support multi-year tax projections during planning engagements

Surface planning opportunities identified during preparation work

Active CPA license

Strong public accounting tax preparation experience

Advanced, hands-on proficiency in Lacerte tax software (required)

Experience preparing multiple return types (1040, 1120 / 1120S, 1065, 1041)

Strong knowledge of federal and state tax law

Proven ability to handle high return volume during US tax season

Strong attention to detail and accuracy under deadline pressure

Excellent written and verbal English communication

Ability to work independently in a fully remote setup

Reliable computer (Intel i5 / AMD Ryzen or higher recommended), stable high-speed internet and a quiet remote work environment

Preferred Qualifications

Multi-state tax preparation experience

Trust and estate (Form 1041) return experience

QuickBooks experience for source-data reconciliation

Experience with professional tax research tools

Prior fully remote accounting or tax work with US clients

Dual monitor setup

Tools & Software

Lacerte (primary tax platform, required)

QuickBooks (source-data reconciliation, preferred)

Professional tax research tools (preferred)

What Makes You a Great Fit

You have prepared real 1120S and 1065 returns in a public accounting firm, not just studied them: you can roll forward a prior-year file, tie the trial balance, find the M-1 adjustments and allocate K-1 items without being walked through it.

You treat Lacerte diagnostics as a to-do list, not background noise, and your returns reach the reviewer clean.

You would rather send a clear, itemized document request on day one than build a return around a guess.

You can explain a balance due or a shrinking refund to a business owner in plain English, with the numbers that caused it.

You hold deadlines during peak season without being chased, and you flag a slipping return before the reviewer has to ask.

You keep workpapers organized enough that another preparer could pick up your file mid-engagement.
