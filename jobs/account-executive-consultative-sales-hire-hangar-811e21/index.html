JOB TITLEACCOUNT EXECUTIVE (CONSULTATIVE SALES)

LocationRemote

Time ZoneUS Time Zones (EST–PST)

Role Overview

We are seeking an experienced Account Executive to join a specialized dental marketing agency. This is a consultative sales role designed to take the majority of sales calls off the founder's plate. You'll work with warm leads, run discovery calls and demos, and close new business — all with a strong emphasis on honest expectation-setting and integrity in selling. This is not a script-reading role; we want someone who genuinely understands a prospect's needs and communicates what our services can and cannot deliver.

Key Responsibilities

  • Conduct discovery calls with warm leads generated by marketing and BDR efforts
  • Deeply understand each prospect's practice, challenges, and goals before presenting solutions
  • Deliver tailored slide deck presentations and proposals that map our services to prospect needs
  • Handle objections with transparency and consultative care
  • Close new business and hand off smoothly to the delivery and onboarding team
  • Maintain accurate pipeline notes and deal stages in our CRM
  • Run structured follow-up sequences via email, messaging, and calls
  • Supplement inbound leads with targeted outreach and light prospecting, especially early on
  • Collaborate closely with the founder and internal team on capacity and priorities

Required Qualifications

  • 3–5+ years of experience in consultative B2B sales, ideally in agency services or SaaS with multi-week/multi-month sales cycles
  • Proven track record running full sales cycles from discovery to close, or strong mid-to-upper funnel AE experience
  • Excellent spoken and written English; must be easy for US-based clients to understand
  • Confident and polished on Zoom and screen-shared presentations
  • Strong integrity in selling — comfortable setting realistic expectations in a marketing context
  • Highly organized with disciplined follow-up habits
  • Prior experience working remotely; candidates without demonstrated remote work experience will not be considered
  • Comfortable reporting to and collaborating closely with the CEO

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience selling marketing or advertising services
  • Background in dental, healthcare, or professional services industries
  • Experience working with US-based clients
  • Familiarity with CRM tools and basic reporting

Tools & Technology

  • CRM platforms (Salesforce, HubSpot, or similar)
  • Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams
  • Slack and Google Workspace
  • Proposal and e-signature tools (PandaDoc, DocuSign, or similar)
  • Scheduling and sales enablement tools (Calendly, Outreach, or similar)

Interview Process – Important This is a highly client-facing role, and the interview process includes a mandatory video submission. Candidates must:

  • Treat the video as a formal business interview
  • Dress in professional business attire (equivalent to a suit-and-tie interview standard)
  • Present themselves exactly as they would for a high-level client or executive meeting

This role is ideal for professionals who enjoy presenting themselves in a refined, professional manner, are confident on camera, and are excited to serve as the visible face of a brand in client interactions.

Please NOTE It is crucial that you complete the application form in full. As part of the application process, you will be required to record a video. If your application is successful, you will receive an email confirming next steps — the video is the first step of the interview process. If you do not record a video, we will not be able to consider you for ANY open roles.
