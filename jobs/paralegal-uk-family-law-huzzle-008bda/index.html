Role TypeFull-time

Job Summary

We are seeking a mid-to-senior level Family Law Paralegal to join a fully remote team, working directly with the business owner. You will take ownership of cases end-to-end, supporting clients through every stage of the process—from documentation and court preparation to client communication.

This role is ideal for a highly independent professional who thrives in a high-responsibility, client-facing environment, and is confident managing legal documentation and sensitive client interactions.

Key Responsibilities

Draft witness statements, position statements, and supporting legal documentation

Prepare court applications across family law matters (Children Act, Family Law Act, Matrimonial Causes Act, TOLATA, etc.)

Review opposing party statements and supporting evidence

Compile, organize, and format court bundles and exhibits to a court-ready standard

Manage cases end-to-end, ensuring all documentation is accurate, compliant, and submitted on time

Conduct client consultations via phone and video calls, providing guidance and reassurance throughout the process

Gather and manage client information for case preparation

Draft professional client correspondence for use in legal proceedings

Manage client onboarding and ensure all required documentation is collected

Maintain and update CRM systems as cases progress

Support legal research, reporting, and administrative tasks as required

Assist with delivery of structured, productised service offerings

Maintain accurate timesheet records and track work progress

3+ years experience as a Paralegal, with a strong preference for family law experience

Proven ability to manage cases independently from start to finish, with minimal supervision

Strong experience drafting legal documents, witness statements, and court applications

Hands-on experience preparing court bundles and organizing supporting evidence

Excellent communication skills with the ability to confidently engage clients via phone and video, including handling sensitive situations

Strong understanding of UK family law

processes and documentation standards

Highly organized with strong administrative skills and the ability to manage multiple cases simultaneously

Exceptional attention to detail, ensuring all documentation is accurate and court-ready

Strong written and verbal English communication skills

Proficient in Microsoft Office and Adobe, with the ability to quickly learn new tools and systems

Experience using CRM systems, legal tools, and AI tools to improve productivity and workflow efficiency

Background in legal research, reporting, and document formatting
