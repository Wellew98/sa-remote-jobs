Operations Coordinator

Remote Recruitment This is a full-time, fully remote position for a South African professional who thrives on structure, follow-through and fixing small problems before they become big ones. Full-time Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

Every smooth-running business has someone behind the scenes who refuses to let things fall through the cracks. This is that role. Job Overview Our UK-based client is hiring an Operations Coordinator to keep the day-to-day engine of their business running cleanly. This is a full-time, fully remote position for a South African professional who thrives on structure, follow-through and fixing small problems before they become big ones. You will coordinate processes, people and information across the business so that nothing stalls and nobody has to chase. About Our Client Our client is a growing UK company with a busy, ambitious team and more moving parts every quarter. They know that growth without operational discipline turns into chaos, which is why this role exists. You will report directly to senior leadership and become the person they trust to make sure what was agreed actually happens. Key Responsibilities - Coordinate day-to-day operational workflows, keeping tasks, owners and deadlines visible and on track - Maintain and improve SOPs so processes survive busy weeks and new team members - Track projects across departments, chasing actions and flagging risks early - Manage operational data and reporting, keeping systems accurate and up to date - Support scheduling, supplier coordination and internal communications - Identify recurring bottlenecks and propose practical fixes, not just workarounds What You Will Be Doing Day-to-Day Your day starts with a clear view of what is due, what is stuck and what needs a nudge. You will update trackers, chase outstanding actions with polite persistence, prepare status summaries for leadership, and keep shared systems clean and reliable. When something unexpected lands, and it will, you triage it calmly: who needs to know, what needs to happen, and what must not be dropped in the meantime. Over time you will spot the patterns behind the firefighting and help build processes that stop the fires starting. If you are a South African professional based in South Africa who takes real pride in being the most organised person in the room, this is a long-term remote role where that skill is properly valued.
