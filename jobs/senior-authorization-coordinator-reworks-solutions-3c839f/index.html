About the Role

We are looking for an experienced, highly organised and analytical Senior Insurance Authorizations Coordinator to join Entiva.

This is a senior-level position responsible for overseeing and managing insurance authorization processes, ensuring accuracy, efficiency and compliance across all authorization-related activities. The successful candidate will bring strong operational experience, excellent attention to detail and previous management or team leadership experience.

You will be responsible for handling complex authorization requirements, reviewing information gathered from multiple sources, monitoring workflows, identifying discrepancies and ensuring that authorization processes are completed accurately and within required timeframes.

Beyond the day-to-day operational responsibilities, this role will involve providing guidance and support to team members, monitoring performance, identifying opportunities for process improvement and helping establish efficient workflows as the company continues to grow.

The ideal candidate is someone who can take ownership, work independently, make informed decisions and confidently manage both operational responsibilities and people.

Key Responsibilities

1. Insurance Authorizations & Operational Oversight

Oversee and manage day-to-day insurance authorization activities and workflows.

Ensure authorization requests are processed accurately, efficiently and within required deadlines.

Review and verify information gathered from multiple sources, ensuring completeness and accuracy.

Handle complex authorization cases, discrepancies and escalations.

Ensure all authorization requirements and procedures are consistently followed.

2. Team Management & Leadership

Provide day-to-day guidance, support and direction to team members.

Monitor team productivity, workload distribution and overall performance.

Assist with onboarding and training new team members.

Conduct quality checks and provide constructive feedback to maintain high standards.

Identify performance gaps and implement corrective measures where necessary.

Help foster an accountable, collaborative and results-driven team environment.

3. Quality Assurance & Process Improvement

Establish and maintain quality standards across authorization processes.

Review records and completed authorizations to identify errors or inconsistencies.

Identify inefficiencies and recommend improvements to existing workflows.

Develop and maintain process documentation, guidelines and internal procedures.

Monitor recurring issues and implement solutions to improve accuracy and turnaround times.

4. Systems & Reporting

Work across multiple operating systems, insurance portals and internal platforms.

Ensure information is accurately captured, updated and maintained.

Monitor authorization volumes, turnaround times and outstanding requests.

Prepare and review operational reports to track team performance and workflow efficiency.

Identify potential system or process issues and escalate where necessary.

Requirements & Qualifications

Previous experience in insurance authorizations, preferably within the US healthcare industry.

Previous management, supervisory or team leadership experience is essential.

Strong understanding of insurance authorization processes and requirements.

Experience managing workflows, monitoring performance and maintaining operational standards.

Excellent analytical, organisational and problem-solving skills.

Exceptional attention to detail and accuracy.

Strong written and verbal communication skills.

Ability to manage competing priorities and work in a fast-paced environment.

Confident working independently, taking ownership and making operational decisions.

Experience working with multiple systems, insurance portals and data management platforms.

Ideal Candidate Profile

We are looking for someone who is more than just an experienced authorization processor. The ideal candidate should have the ability to oversee operations, manage people, maintain quality standards and take initiative in improving processes.

They should be comfortable balancing hands-on authorization responsibilities with leadership duties and have the confidence to step into a position of responsibility as Entiva continues to expand
