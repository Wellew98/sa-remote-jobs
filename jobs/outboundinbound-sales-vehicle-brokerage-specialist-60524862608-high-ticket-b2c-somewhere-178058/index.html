Outbound/Inbound Sales & Vehicle Brokerage Specialist (High-Ticket B2C)

  • Location: Remote, Offshore (LATAM or South Africa)
  • Schedule: Full-time, US Timezone Alignment (CST/EST)
  • Type: Full-time Contractor (40 Hours Per Week)
  • Reporting to: Founder / Sales Lead
  • Hiring Timeline: Immediate (High-Urgency Hire)
  • Salary Benchmarks: (final offer value entirely at client's discretion)
  • LATAM: $1,400 – $1,600 USD/month Base + Commissions
  • South Africa (SA): $1,200 – $1,400 USD/month Base + Commissions
  • Commission Structure: Uncapped and designed for high-velocity closers:
  • $50 USD per closed vehicle for your first 5–10 deals.
  • $100 USD per closed vehicle once you exceed the 10-deal threshold.
  • Target Output: 5–10 closed deals/month initially, scaling to a standard of 20+ closed deals.
  • On-Target Earnings (OTE Commission): An additional $500 – $2,000+ USD per month.

Role OverviewThe Trust & Certainty Closer

We are a high-growth, modern vehicle import and brokerage firm expanding our operations internationally (originally founded in the Bahamas and aggressively entering new territories). We do not operate like a traditional dealership with a physical car lot. Instead, we provide a premium, full-service A-Z concierge solution—sourcing both premium auction vehicles and brand-new cars (ranging from $10,000 economical models to $150,000 luxury vehicles) and coordinating everything from sourcing to door-to-door delivery.

Because our clients purchase these vehicles sight unseen , this is a highly sophisticated, trust-based sales environment. We are seeking a relentless, psychologically astute Outbound Sales & Vehicle Brokerage Specialist to serve as the closing engine of our sales pipeline.

You aren't reading robotic scripts; you are selling certainty, trust, and peace of mind . You are a polished closer who understands human psychology, builds rapid authority over the phone, and guides warm, inbound leads from curiosity to a committed purchase.

Support Structure: To ensure you can focus 100% of your energy on dialing, building relationships, and closing, you will be supported by a dedicated Vehicle Specialist who handles the technical sourcing, logistics, and heavy administrative processing behind the scenes.

Key Responsibilities

I. High-Velocity Lead Conversion & Relationship Selling

  • Warm Inbound Intake: Manage and execute 50–60 high-impact phone calls per day. You will be working with warm inbound inquiries generated from our highly active marketing and advertising engines.
  • The "Sight-Unseen" Pitch: Establish immediate rapport with prospective buyers, guiding them through our concierge process and instilling absolute confidence that their sourced vehicle will arrive exactly as promised.
  • Psychological Closing: Uncover client motivations, identify their specific budget parameters (whether looking for a $10k economical daily driver or a $150k luxury asset), and use persuasive psychology to overcome natural hesitations.
  • Objection Handling: Navigate concerns regarding pricing, shipping timelines, auction terms, and vehicle condition with extreme professionalism and confidence.

II. CRM Pipeline Management (GoHighLevel)

  • Lead Flow Integrity: Own your lead pipeline inside GoHighLevel (GHL) . Meticulously track every call outcome, text thread, and deal stage to ensure zero lead slippage.
  • Nurture Strategy: Leverage automated sequences, stepping in manually the second an automated SMS or email trigger indicates active buying intent to call and finalize the deal.
  • Data Hygiene: Maintain a clean, organized, and audit-ready database, ensuring all buyer preferences and vehicle requests are systematically cataloged.

III. Collaboration & Performance Standards

  • Sales-to-Sourcing Hand-off: Package qualified client briefs and hand them off directly to our back-office Vehicle Specialist to locate the perfect match.
  • High-Accountability Focus: Thrive in a fast-paced, high-integrity startup environment under strict, growth-oriented leadership.

Who You Are

Experience & Technical Skills

  • Proven Sales DNA: 3+ years of experience in outbound sales, closing (AE), high-ticket B2C consulting, or high-volume appointment setting (automotive, real estate, financial services, or similar high-trust sectors are highly preferred).
  • Persuasion & Psychological Mastery: You are a natural student of sales psychology. You know how to build a deep, genuine connection over the phone, ask guiding questions, and create a sense of urgency.
  • GoHighLevel (GHL) Literacy: Experience working with high-end CRMs (GHL, HubSpot, or Salesforce) and a solid understanding of pipeline stages, tagging, and automated follow-up logic.
  • Elite English: Flawless written and verbal English with a clear, neutral, and persuasive phone presence to confidently communicate with international buyers.

Mindset & Work Style

  • The "Hunter-Closer" Mindset: You are highly motivated by commissions. You want your paycheck to reflect your closing velocity, and you are driven to scale from 5 closes to 20+ per month.
  • Extreme Organization: You hate sloppy data. You are disciplined with your callbacks, structured in your pipeline, and treat CRM hygiene as a survival skill.
  • Resolute & Persistent: You don't get rattled by a "no." You view objections as an invitation to consult, staying high-energy from your first call to your last.

Why Join Us?

  • High-Upside commission: Secure a stable USD base salary combined with an uncapped, tiered commission structure that rewards high-volume producers.
  • Operational Leverage: Focus exclusively on the art of selling while our dedicated Vehicle Specialist takes care of the technical logistics and administration.

Foundational Growth: Join an ambitious, expanding global brand at an early stage, with opportunities to scale into sales leadership as we enter new markets.

We are looking for candidates based in South Africa or Latin America or the Philippines. Please note that we process only one active application at a time—if you’ve applied for multiple roles, kindly direct any updates to the team handling your first application. To start processing, submit your resume in English. The final offer will be at the client’s discretion and will depend on your interview performance, skills, and experience.
