SU - Senior Solutions Engineer

Somewhere South Africa (Remote) Full-time Software & IT Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

Role Description This is a full-time remote role for a Senior Solutions Engineer at a growing U.S.-based company. The Senior Solutions Engineer will be responsible for ensuring the successful onboarding, adoption, and ongoing value delivery for the company customers. You will be a trusted technical and product partner to our Delivery, Account Management, and Data Operations teams—supporting everything from preparing enterprise accounts on Link and Sales Hub, to creating and deploying high-impact buyer demand surveys that feed into the company’s proprietary intelligence.   Qualifications - 10+ years in a customer-facing technical role such as Solutions Engineer, Technical Account Manager, Business Analyst, or similar—preferably in SaaS or intelligence/data products. - Proven track record in enterprise onboarding, technical solution delivery, and customer success outcomes. - Strong analytical skills with proficiency in Google Sheets (SQL or BI tools a plus). - Experience designing and deploying surveys (Forsta preferred; SurveyMonkey, Typeform, or a similar acceptable). - Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English, with the ability to convey technical concepts to business audiences. - Ability to manage multiple projects in a fast-paced, dynamic environment while maintaining attention to detail. - Collaborative mindset with a passion for solving complex customer challenges. Responsibilities - Lead the technical onboarding of enterprise customer accounts on Link and Sales Hub, ensuring configurations align with customer-specific needs and workflows. - Guide customers through platform navigation, best practices, and advanced features to accelerate adoption. - Develop tailored demos, proof-of-concept configurations, and data scenarios that illustrate the platform’s value for customer goals. - Partner with Account Management to prepare for strategic customer meetings, QBRs, and renewal discussions. - Create and deploy buyer demand surveys to capture actionable market intelligence and enrich the company’s proprietary datasets. - Leverage Forsta (preferred) or equivalent tools (SurveyMonkey, Typeform) for survey execution, ensuring data accuracy and high response quality. - Extract, analyze, and interpret data from Link to generate insights and recommendations for customers. - Present findings in a clear, business-focused format that drives action. - Troubleshoot and resolve technical issues in collaboration with Delivery, Account Management, and Data Operations teams. - Provide structured feedback to the Product team on recurring issues, feature requests, and user experience improvements. - Develop and maintain comprehensive internal and customer-facing resources, including training guides, knowledge base articles, and onboarding documentation. ​​​​ Compensation $ 2,000 - $ 2,500 per month, paid in USD depending on experience. Details - Independent contractor agreement paid 2x monthly. - The client has a desire for long-term commitment.
