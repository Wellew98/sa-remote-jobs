💼 B2B Software Sales Representative 🌍 Philippines, South Africa, Latin America 🏠 Remote

About Our Client

Our client is a fast-growing, early-stage B2B SaaS company serving field service and operations businesses. Sales has been founder-led to date, and this is the first dedicated sales hire, giving the right person real influence over how the sales function is built. The company sells both software subscriptions and professional services and onboarding packages.

About the Role

We're looking for a B2B Software Sales Representative to be the first dedicated sales hire, owning the entire sales funnel end to end: inbound lead response, outbound prospecting, discovery, demo, proposal, and close. This is not an appointment-setting role.

You'll be the single sales seat reporting directly to the founder, with full ownership of pipeline, CRM hygiene, SOP execution, and closing. If you're a proven closer who can work independently, follow a documented process without being micromanaged, and take genuine ownership of revenue outcomes in an early-stage environment, this role is a strong fit.

Responsibilities

Own the complete sales cycle from research and outreach through qualification, demo, proposal, close, and handoff to onboarding

Respond to inbound leads immediately via call, email, and SMS as defined in the sales SOP, as speed to lead is a hard expectation

Run structured discovery conversations covering pain, current tools, decision process, timeline, and budget signals

Lead product demos with support from the customer success team

Send proposals and quotes, chase signatures, and confirm kickoff details for every won deal

Close both software subscription plans and professional services and onboarding packages

Research target accounts, build call lists, and run a consistent cold and warm outbound motion alongside inbound response

Own CRM management day to day, including pipeline stages, tags, sequences, and logging every touch, keeping next steps, close dates, and notes current

Provide light weekly reporting on activity, pipeline, wins, losses, and blockers

Feed product and marketing teams with call insights, including ICP feedback, common objections, and feature requests

Help refine scripts, talk tracks, and ICP definitions as sales scales from founder-led to team-led

Handle objections and negotiate within approved pricing guidelines, escalating edge cases to leadership

What We're Looking For

Proven experience closing, or strongly contributing to closing, B2B software or high-consideration sales (not appointment setting only)

Excellent spoken English with the confidence and presence to lead live demos and discovery calls independently

Self-directed, high-ownership mindset, comfortable being the only salesperson reporting directly to a founder

Strong CRM discipline, with the ability to follow a documented sales SOP consistently without being reminded

Reliable availability during US business hours with strong outbound phone skills

Nice to Have

Spanish language skills for communicating with Spanish-speaking clients

Experience selling to SMB, field service, or trade service businesses

Prior experience as an early or first sales hire at a startup

Familiarity with CRM tools such as HubSpot or GoHighLevel

Work Schedule

100% remote setup so you can work wherever you're most productive

This position operates on a full-time basis, with dedicated hours to ensure alignment with the team and delivery of quality work

Availability during US business hours
