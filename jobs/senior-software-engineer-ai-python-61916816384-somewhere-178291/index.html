About the Company We are a rapidly growing U.S.-based software company building AI guardrails for modern engineering teams. As organizations adopt AI coding agents, they ship faster — but also introduce security vulnerabilities, business-logic flaws, and standards drift that traditional tooling misses. Our platform solves this by combining LLM-powered review with deterministic analysis to govern, secure, and add observability to AI-generated code across an entire team's tools and agents. In practice, that means we sit between developers, their coding agents, and production: enforcing each team's functional and security standards, surfacing issues in real time, and continuously improving the specifications that keep agents on track. We are early, well-positioned in a fast-moving category, and building the foundational systems that the product will run on for years. About the Role This is a foundational engineering hire on an AI-first team. You will help build the core systems that power our AI review and analysis engine — the LLM and NLP pipelines that interpret code, the multi-agent systems and retrieval layers behind them, and the APIs and services that deliver results to users and agents. The role exists because the heart of our product is hard, specialized AI engineering: reasoning over code with LLMs and agents, building reliable systems on top of inherently probabilistic models, and doing it with the rigor expected of a security product. We work AI-first internally too — every engineer uses frontier AI tooling daily and is expected to improve how we use it. You will work closely with the founder, own significant pieces of the architecture end to end, and make decisions that shape how the platform scales. Titles matter less here than competence and ownership. Working AI-First - Make heavy, daily use of AI in your own engineering workflow — we provide top-tier tooling (e.g., Claude Max 20x, ChatGPT Pro 20x). - Regularly use and contribute to our internal skills, agent integrations, and agentic loops, raising the quality of their outputs across our software development, testing, and documentation use cases. Data Modeling & Storage - Work with vector databases to power retrieval and AI-driven features. - Store and manage data in NoSQL/document databases (e.g., MongoDB or similar). - Make sound decisions about data structure, performance, and scalability. APIs & Services - Design, build, and consume GraphQL and REST APIs. - Contribute to a microservices-oriented architecture within a monorepo environment. Engineering Quality & Practices - Maintain a high bar for code quality through detailed peer review. - Own testing across unit, integration, and end-to-end layers. - Work within CI/CD pipelines and contribute to keeping them healthy. - Document your work clearly using markdown and inline comments so others can build on it.
