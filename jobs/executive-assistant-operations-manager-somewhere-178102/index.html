Executive Assistant / Operations Manager

LocationRemote

TypeFull-time

IndustryCommercial Real Estate

About the Role

We are seeking a highly experienced, self-directed Executive Assistant / Operations Manager to support day-to-day business operations and help remove administrative and marketing workload from leadership. This person will play a key role in keeping deals, communication, reporting, and marketing efforts organized and moving forward.

This is a great fit for someone who is proactive, reliable, detail-oriented, and comfortable working independently with minimal hand-holding.

Key Responsibilities

- Manage and respond to email communications on behalf of leadership

- Handle lead follow-up and client follow-up with strong professionalism and judgment

- Assist with CRM organization and workflow support, especially in monday.com

- Prepare weekly client reporting and internal reporting as needed

- Create and format marketing materials, including flyers and simple social media assets

- Assist with Letter of Intent (LOI) drafting using existing templates

- Help organize deal-related documentation and track next steps

- Support general operations and administrative tasks to keep leadership focused on sales

- Communicate clearly with clients and vendors, potentially handling phone support as needed

- Take ownership of recurring tasks and improve processes where possible

Required Qualifications

- Strong English communication skills, written and verbal

- Prior experience in executive assistance, operations, or a similar support role

- High level of professionalism, accountability, and follow-through

- Strong organizational skills and ability to manage multiple priorities

- Comfortable handling email communication directly

- Ability to work independently and think critically

- Experience with or ability to quickly learn monday.com or similar CRM tools

- Experience creating simple marketing materials in Adobe or similar design tools

Preferred Qualifications

- Experience in commercial real estate or real estate support

- Familiarity with LOIs, client reporting, and CRM workflows

- Experience supporting a high-volume, fast-moving sales professional

- Background in marketing support, flyer creation, or social media content

- Exposure to operations or administrative coordination in a lean team environment

Ideal Candidate Traits

- Reliable and responsive

-

Excellent written English

- Takes initiative without needing constant direction

- Comfortable with ambiguity and evolving responsibilities

- Detail-oriented, especially with follow-up and reporting

- Professional, resourceful, and trustworthy

- Able to quickly learn and adapt to established processes and communication styles

Success in This Role Looks Like

- Leadership has more time to focus on sales and client relationships

-

Inbox and follow-up are consistently managed

- Marketing materials are produced quickly and accurately

- Reporting is completed efficiently and on time

-

Processes run smoothly with minimal oversight

Compensation

Compensation will be based on experience and fit.
