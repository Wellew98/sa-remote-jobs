SU - Chief of Staff

Somewhere South Africa (Remote) Full-time Admin & Virtual Assistance Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

​​​ Role Description This is a full-time remote role for a Chief of Staff supporting a high-performing executive team at a global organization. The Chief of Staff will act as a strategic partner to leadership, ensuring the alignment of priorities, driving operational efficiency, and fostering cross-functional collaboration. This role requires exceptional organizational skills, strategic thinking, and the ability to manage high-impact projects in a fast-paced environment. Qualifications - 5+ years of experience in a Chief of Staff, Executive Assistant, or senior management role. - Proven track record in managing complex projects and cross-functional teams. - Exceptional written and verbal communication skills in English. - Strong analytical skills, with the ability to synthesize data and provide actionable insights. - Proficiency in productivity tools such as Microsoft Office Suite, Google Workspace, and project management platforms like Asana, Trello, or Monday.com. - Demonstrated ability to maintain confidentiality and handle sensitive information with discretion. - Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Management, or a related field (MBA or equivalent is a plus). - Experience working in a remote or global team environment is highly preferred. Responsibilities Strategic Planning & Execution: - Collaborate with executives to develop and implement strategic initiatives. - Drive execution of key priorities, ensuring timely delivery and alignment with company goals. Operational Oversight: - Monitor and streamline workflows to optimize team efficiency and effectiveness. - Identify areas for process improvement and implement solutions. Executive Support: - Act as a trusted advisor to leadership, providing data-driven insights and recommendations. - Prepare executive-level communications, presentations, and reports. Team Collaboration: - Foster collaboration and alignment among cross-functional teams. - Serve as a liaison between executives and department heads to ensure seamless communication. Project Management: - Manage high-priority projects from inception to completion, ensuring quality and impact. - Track progress against goals and report on milestones and deliverables. Stakeholder Management: - Represent the executive team in meetings and communications with internal and external stakeholders. - Build and maintain strong relationships with key partners and clients. Compensation - $2,500 - $4,000 per month, paid in USD depending on experience and expertise.
