Lifecycle Marketing Specialist (Klaviyo & SMS)

Remote | Full-Time | US Time Zone Hours

Company Name Confidential

About the Company

We are a fast-growing lifecycle marketing agency that partners with leading ecommerce brands to drive customer retention, revenue growth, and long-term customer value through email, SMS, loyalty, subscription, and retention marketing programs.

Our team combines strategy, execution, and operational excellence to help brands create exceptional customer experiences throughout the customer lifecycle. We operate in a fast-paced, highly collaborative environment where attention to detail, ownership, and execution quality are critical to success.

We are seeking a highly organized and technically skilled Lifecycle Marketing Specialist to support the execution and delivery of lifecycle marketing programs across multiple ecommerce clients.

About the Role

This role serves as the operational backbone of our lifecycle marketing team.

You will be responsible for building, QA'ing, deploying, and maintaining email and SMS campaigns, automations, customer journeys, and segmentation strategies across a portfolio of ecommerce brands. You will work closely with Lifecycle Strategists, designers, copywriters, and clients to ensure flawless execution and exceptional campaign performance.

This is an ideal opportunity for someone who enjoys the intersection of marketing, technology, project management, and customer retention. The ideal candidate is highly detail-oriented, thrives in a fast-paced agency environment, and has hands-on experience working within Klaviyo, Shopify, and SMS marketing platforms.

What You'll Be Responsible For

Email & SMS Campaign Execution

  • Build, schedule, test, and deploy email and SMS campaigns across multiple client accounts
  • Execute lifecycle marketing initiatives including promotional campaigns, retention programs, customer nurture journeys, and automated flows
  • Configure dynamic content, personalization, segmentation, and audience targeting
  • Create and manage signup forms, popups, lead capture assets, and subscriber acquisition initiatives
  • Support implementation of A/B tests and optimization initiatives
  • Ensure all campaigns are aligned with brand guidelines and campaign objectives

Lifecycle Automation & Flow Management

  • Build, maintain, and optimize automated lifecycle flows including:
  • Welcome Series
  • Abandoned Cart
  • Browse Abandonment
  • Post-Purchase Flows
  • Win-Back Campaigns
  • Customer Retention Programs
  • VIP and Loyalty Journeys
  • SMS Automations
  • Troubleshoot flow logic, segmentation issues, and automation performance
  • Monitor flow health and recommend improvements based on performance data

Quality Assurance & Deployment

  • Own QA processes across all lifecycle marketing initiatives
  • Review campaigns and automations for:
  • Rendering accuracy
  • Personalization and dynamic content functionality
  • Segmentation accuracy
  • Link validation
  • Tracking implementation
  • Mobile responsiveness
  • Deliverability best practices
  • Identify and resolve issues prior to deployment
  • Ensure campaigns are launched accurately and on schedule

Client & Team Support

  • Collaborate closely with Lifecycle Strategists to execute retention marketing initiatives
  • Support client communication regarding approvals, revisions, and deployment schedules
  • Coordinate with designers and copywriters to ensure projects move efficiently through production
  • Assist with gathering feedback and managing revision cycles
  • Participate in internal project planning and workflow coordination

Project Management & Operations

  • Manage project workflows and tasks using Asana or similar project management platforms
  • Track deadlines, priorities, and deliverables across multiple client accounts
  • Maintain organized documentation and execution processes
  • Support process improvement initiatives that increase operational efficiency and scalability
  • Help reduce production bottlenecks and improve delivery consistency

Reporting & Optimization Support

  • Assist with campaign reporting and performance tracking
  • Validate campaign tagging and tracking implementation
  • Support performance analysis through data collection and reporting preparation
  • Document testing results and campaign learnings
  • Identify execution-related opportunities to improve campaign effectiveness

What We're Looking For

Required Experience

  • 2+ years of hands-on experience with Klaviyo
  • Experience building and managing email marketing campaigns and automated lifecycle flows
  • Experience working with Shopify ecommerce brands
  • Experience using project management tools such as Asana, ClickUp, Monday.com, or similar platforms
  • Strong understanding of customer lifecycle marketing principles
  • Experience supporting multiple projects and deadlines simultaneously
  • Excellent written and verbal English communication skills
  • Ability to work during US business hours

Preferred Experience

  • Experience with Attentive or other SMS marketing platforms
  • Agency experience supporting multiple ecommerce clients
  • Experience with email deliverability best practices
  • Familiarity with customer segmentation and personalization strategies
  • Basic HTML/CSS knowledge for email troubleshooting
  • Experience with Canva, Photoshop, or other design tools
  • Experience supporting A/B testing initiatives

Key Traits for Success

  • Extremely detail-oriented
  • Highly organized and process-driven
  • Strong sense of ownership and accountability
  • Excellent problem-solving abilities
  • Comfortable working in a fast-paced agency environment
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities without sacrificing quality
  • Collaborative team player with strong communication skills
  • Passionate about ecommerce, retention marketing, and customer experience

Preferred Backgrounds

Candidates with experience in the following environments are strongly encouraged to apply:

  • Ecommerce marketing agencies
  • Retention marketing agencies
  • Shopify-focused agencies
  • Email and SMS marketing agencies
  • Direct-to-consumer (DTC) brands
  • Customer lifecycle and CRM marketing teams

Success in This Role

Within your first 90 days, you will

  • Successfully manage campaign and flow execution across multiple client accounts
  • Consistently deliver error-free deployments and QA processes
  • Become a trusted operational partner to Lifecycle Strategists
  • Improve project efficiency and workflow organization
  • Contribute to stronger client retention and campaign performance through reliable execution
