Video Editor

Somewhere LATAM, South Africa, Philippines (Remote) Full-time Design & Creative Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

We are seeking a creative and detail-oriented Video Editor to join our team. The ideal candidate will be responsible for editing and producing high-quality video content that aligns with our brand and engages our audience. This role requires technical expertise, creativity, and a strong understanding of storytelling through video. Key Responsibilities: - Edit raw footage into polished video content for various platforms (social media, website, marketing campaigns, presentations, etc.). - Collaborate with content creators, marketing, and design teams to ensure videos meet project goals and brand guidelines. - Incorporate graphics, animations, special effects, sound, and music to enhance video quality. - Manage and organize video assets, project files, and archives. - Ensure all video content is delivered on time and meets quality standards. - Stay up-to-date with the latest video editing trends, tools, and best practices. - Review and revise edits based on feedback from stakeholders. - Optimize videos for different platforms, including web, social media, and broadcast. Qualifications & Skills: - Proven experience as a video editor or similar role. - Proficiency in video editing software (e.g., Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve, After Effects). - Strong understanding of storytelling, pacing, and visual composition. - Knowledge of sound design, color grading, and motion graphics. - Excellent attention to detail and organizational skills. - Ability to manage multiple projects and meet tight deadlines. - Strong communication and collaboration skills. Preferred: - Degree or certification in Film, Media, Multimedia, or a related field. - Experience in [industry-specific experience, e.g., marketing videos, social media content, corporate videos]. - Basic knowledge of 3D animation or visual effects is a plus.
