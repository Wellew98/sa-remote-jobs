About the Company & Opportunity

We are an established and rapidly expanding UK-based enterprise specializing in cutting-edge, eco-friendly insulation and energy-saving solutions for residential and commercial properties. Our high-performance products help property owners significantly lower their heating costs, combat rising utility bills, and reduce their environmental footprint. To support our accelerating market growth across the United Kingdom, we are seeking a highly motivated, results-driven Outbound Sales Agent to join our dynamic offshore team. In this pivotal remote role, you will conduct high-volume outbound calls to engage prospective customers, identify their energy-efficiency needs, and successfully secure qualified appointments for our UK-based field consultants. The ideal candidate brings a proven track record in cold calling or appointment setting, possesses flawless English communication skills, and thrives in a fast-paced, target-driven environment where resilience and rapport-building are rewarded with lucrative conversion bonuses.

Key Responsibilities

Conduct high-volume outbound calls to prospective UK customers using our dedicated cloud-based dialing system.

Introduce the company's premium product portfolio in a professional, engaging, and consultative manner.

Effectively qualify sales opportunities by identifying customer pain points regarding energy efficiency and insulation.

Consistently hit the performance target of securing a minimum of 2 confirmed bookings/appointments per day to be transferred to the UK field sales team.

Maintain meticulous CRM records, ensuring all call notes, customer data, and dispositions are accurately logged.

Actively participate in regular training, continuous coaching sessions, and team alignment meetings.

What We Look For in Our Team

Outstanding interpersonal skills and the ability to build immediate trust over the phone.

A positive, professional, and solution-oriented attitude when handling rejection.

A genuine coachability and desire to receive ongoing performance feedback for professional growth.

High levels of integrity, reliability, punctuality, and a customer-centric approach.
