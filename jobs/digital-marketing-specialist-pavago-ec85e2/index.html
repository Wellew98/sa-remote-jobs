About the Role

At Pavago, we’re hiring on behalf of one of our clients for a Digital Marketing Specialist to execute, optimize, and scale digital marketing campaigns across paid, organic, and social channels.

This is a hands-on, execution-focused role where you’ll take ownership of Google Ads, SEO, social media engagement, and campaign performance. You’ll continuously analyze data, test new ideas, and optimize campaigns to drive traffic, leads, and measurable business growth.

If you’re passionate about digital marketing, enjoy working with performance metrics, and thrive on improving campaign results through data-driven decisions, this role is built for you.

What Happens After You Apply

Right after you apply, you’ll receive an email invitation from

Spark Hire

to record your Intro Video . This short, self-recorded video is the final step that completes your application and can be recorded whenever it’s convenient for you.

Instead of repeating yourself across multiple screening calls, you’ll introduce yourself once, and your video will be shared with the hiring team. This helps hiring managers evaluate your communication style early, making future interviews more meaningful and reducing unnecessary interview rounds.

Don’t overthink it—you can record your video as many times as you’d like before submitting it. Only your final submission will be reviewed.

Please keep an eye on both your inbox and spam folder for your Spark Hire invitation after submitting your application.
