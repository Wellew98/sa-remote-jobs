Job TitleSenior MSP Technical Support Specialist

LocationRemote

Time ZoneUS Time Zones (PST - EST)

Role Overview

We are looking for a Senior MSP Technical Support Specialist who can independently own and resolve tickets across a broad, modern IT stack without relying on constant escalation. This role sits above L1/L2 support — you are the one others escalate to. You'll manage complex Microsoft 365/Azure environments, endpoint security, networking, and backup/DR across multiple client tenants, while working efficiently within a ticket-driven, SLA-focused MSP environment.

What You'll Do

  • Own and work the ticket queue independently — triage, diagnose, and resolve complex issues without requiring constant escalation.
  • Administer Microsoft 365 and Azure/Entra environments, including Conditional Access, - Intune, MFA, SSO, and multi-tenant management via CIPP.
  • Manage endpoint security tools including SentinelOne EDR, Huntress MDR/ITDR, and Windows Defender.
  • Monitor and manage email security through Avanan/Harmony, with transition support to Kaseya Inky.
  • Oversee backup and disaster recovery operations using Datto BCDR, Datto Endpoint Backup, and DropSuite.
  • Configure, troubleshoot, and maintain networking environments across Cisco Meraki, Unifi, Aruba, and CheckPoint.
  • Monitor network health and performance using Auvik.
  • Maintain thorough, accurate documentation in Hudu for systems, processes, and client environments.
  • Support security awareness initiatives via Usecure, including simulated phishing, dark web monitoring, and training campaigns.
  • Manage software licensing through Pax8.
  • Serve as a senior point of escalation for junior support staff, providing guidance and technical mentorship.
  • Maintain clear, professional written and verbal communication across all client and internal interactions.

Required Qualifications

  • Prior MSP experience is non-negotiable — in-house/internal IT experience alone will not be considered.
  • Strong, hands-on administration experience with Microsoft 365 and Azure/Entra.
  • Experience with RMM/PSA platforms such as Syncro, ConnectWise, Autotask, or similar.
  • Hands-on experience with EDR/MDR endpoint security tools.
  • Solid networking competency with Meraki, Unifi, or similar platforms.
  • Demonstrated ability to own a ticket queue independently, with minimal need for escalation.
  • Clear written and spoken English, with availability during US Central business hours.

Nice to Have

  • Direct experience with Syncro specifically.
  • Experience with Datto BCDR.
  • Direct hands-on experience with Huntress or SentinelOne.
  • Microsoft certifications (MS-900, AZ-900, MD-102, SC-900).
