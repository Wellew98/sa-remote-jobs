We are looking for a talented Graphic Illustrator / Designer who can create their own illustrations for an U.S-based Apparel brand. This role is ideal for someone who thrives in a creative environment and is skilled in designing visually compelling content that supports their brands growth.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design and create original illustrations for social media graphics, apparel designs, and other marketing materials.
  • Use Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, or other illustration software to develop and enhance graphics.
  • Collaborate with the team to develop creative concepts that align with our brand identity.
  • Manage multiple design projects while meeting deadlines.
  • Maintain consistency in design across all company platforms and adhere to brand guidelines.
  • Stay updated on design trends to ensure our content remains fresh and engaging.

Required Qualifications

  • 2+ years of experience as a Graphic Designer, Illustrator, or similar role.
  • Proficiency in Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and other illustration software.
  • Strong portfolio showcasing your illustration skills and design versatility (applications without a portfolio will not be considered).
  • Ability to create original illustrations tailored for social media and apparel graphics.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Knowledge of apparel graphic design
  • Experience with social media content creation and digital marketing is a plus.

Key Competencies

  • Strong attention to detail and a keen sense of aesthetics.
  • Ability to work independently and take creative direction.
  • Excellent time management skills and the ability to handle multiple projects.
  • Good communication skills and a collaborative mindset.

Required Skills & Tools

  • Phone / VoIP calling systems
  • Zoom (or similar video conferencing tools)
  • CRM platforms for logging, tracking, and updating live conversations
  • Email and calendar tools (e.g. Gmail, Outlook, Calendly) for managing scheduling, follow-ups, and coordination where required
  • Fast, accurate typing skills for real-time note-taking during or immediately after live conversations

Schedule & Pay

  • Part-time position; US business hours (9am–1pm EST)
  • Fully remote working environment, working U.S hours (EST)
  • Salary: 38000 PHP - 43000 PHP / R 7500 - R 8500

System Requirements

  • A fibre internet connection  with a minimum speed of 25 Mbps.
  • Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher.
  • 8 GB of RAM or higher.
  • Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer.
  • HD 720p webcam.
  • Headset with a microphone.

Benefits

  • Fully remote, stable full-time role
  • US-based operational exposure
  • No commute and full location flexibility
  • Consistent client interaction and structured workflows
  • Long-term engagement and career development potential
  • Supportive and structured working environment

If you're a creative illustrator with a passion for original design and apparel graphics, we want to hear from you. Please  include a link to your portfolio directly on your resume  - applications without a portfolio will not be considered.
