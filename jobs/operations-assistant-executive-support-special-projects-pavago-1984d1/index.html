About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring an Operations Assistant to support a fast-growing, entrepreneurial healthcare services company by driving operational excellence, executive support, and cross-functional project execution.

This is not a traditional administrative role. You’ll become a trusted extension of the leadership team—owning projects, solving problems, improving processes, and ensuring critical initiatives move forward with minimal supervision.

You’ll work across operations, executive support, reporting, vendor management, marketing coordination, people operations, and special projects. Every day is different, requiring adaptability, resourcefulness, and a strong ownership mindset.

If you enjoy bringing structure to fast-moving environments, learning new skills, and becoming the person everyone relies on to keep the business running smoothly, this role is built for you.

What Happens After You Apply

Right after you apply, you’ll receive an email invitation from Spark Hire to record your Intro Video . This short, self-recorded video is the final step that completes your application and can be recorded whenever it’s convenient for you.

Instead of repeating yourself across multiple screening calls, you’ll introduce yourself once, and your video will be shared with the hiring team. This helps hiring managers evaluate your communication style early, making future interviews more meaningful and reducing unnecessary interview rounds.

Don’t overthink it—you can record your video as many times as you’d like before submitting it. Only your final submission will be reviewed.

Please keep an eye on both your inbox and spam folder for your Spark Hire invitation after submitting your application.
