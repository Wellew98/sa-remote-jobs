We are looking for a Special Operations & Client Retention Specialist to bridge the gap between Onboarding and Account Management—taking ownership of complex, high-friction customer situations where there is a risk of disengagement, cancellation, refund, or loss of trust.

Sitting directly between our Onboarding Specialists and Account Managers, this role functions as our frontline "Special Operations" seat. You will manage high-touch client communications, lead live office hours and drop-in sessions, and step in to stabilize at-risk accounts before they churn.

This is a high-accountability, client-facing role requiring strong judgment, confident phone and video communication, SaaS familiarity, commercial awareness, and the ability to remain calm and effective in difficult situations. You will investigate the full customer journey, identify the true cause of an issue, troubleshoot blockers directly, and coordinate across internal teams while maintaining full ownership of the relationship through resolution.

Growth Path: Our client operations processes are evolving rapidly. Over the next 3 months, this role is designed to transition into a full, standard Account Manager position. We are specifically looking for candidates with the drive,commercial acumen, and growth potential to step into full Account Management responsibilities as the team scales.

THE COMPANY

A multi-8-figure ARR business with 200+ team members, bootstrapped and profitable. The company launches and scales information and software products on a marketing and sales engine refined across a dozen product launches and $100M+ in ad spend.

This seat sits within our client operations team and plays an important role in strengthening customer relationships, resolving complex issues, improving retention, and protecting long-term customer value.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

Bridge Onboarding & Account Management: Serve as a "Special Operations" resource between Onboarding and Account Management to ensure seamless client handoffs, proactive check-ins, and ongoing account stability.

Lead Live Client Sessions: Host and facilitate recurring client office hours and drop-in sessions to answer questions, unblock progress, and drive product adoption.

Own At-Risk Retention: Take ownership of high-friction, at-risk customer cases involving dissatisfaction, disengagement, cancellation, refund concerns, or loss of confidence.

Conduct Rapid, Multi-Channel Outreach: Contact assigned customers quickly by telephone and use SMS and email when appropriate.

Diagnose Root Causes: Review the customer’s complete journey before making contact to understand history and context; separate the immediate complaint from underlying practical, technical, delivery, expectation, or mindset blockers.

De-Escalate & Influence: Speak directly with customers by phone to build trust quickly when emotions are high, lead calm resolution conversations, and reframe unhelpful conclusions without becoming defensive or applying high-pressure tactics.

Drive Cross-Functional Resolution: Troubleshoot appropriate issues directly and coordinate with Onboarding, Tech, Delivery, Sales, and Finance while maintaining end-to-end ownership of the customer relationship.

Commercial Judgment & Documentation: Use approved support options or concessions when justified, escalate complex refund requests through proper channels, keep accurate records of all commitments and outcomes, and surface recurring churn drivers.

Prepare for Account Management: Actively build the skills, product mastery, and portfolio management habits required to transition into a full Account Manager role within 3 months.

Previous experience in consultative sales, SaaS customer success/support, Account Management, Customer Retention, Escalations, or Dispute Resolution.

Demonstrated potential and ambition to grow into a full Account Manager role within 3 months.

Comfortable facilitating live group environments (such as office hours and drop-in sessions) as well as 1-on-1 high-stakes calls.

Strong conversational influence, conflict-resolution skills, and call control, with the ability to confidently lead frustrated or skeptical customers toward a constructive next step.

Able to listen carefully while remaining confident enough to challenge an unhelpful conclusion without relying on pressure, manipulation, or unsupported promises.

Strong investigative and problem-solving skills to identify root causes and troubleshoot technical/practical blockers across teams.

Good commercial judgment and an understanding of customer value, SaaS retention, and financial impact.

Strong organization, attention to detail, and written documentation skills.

Reliable, resilient, coachable, and willing to review calls and feedback to continuously improve.

Full-time opportunity (Independent Contractor Agreement)

Compensation$60,000 – $70,000 USD annually ($5,000 – $5,833 USD monthly base, depending on experience)

Additional monthly performance incentive based on results

Fully remote

Clear 3-Month Growth Track: Direct trajectory to transition into a standard Account Manager role as our client operations model evolves.

Application & Assessment Process

After reviewing applications, selected candidates who fit the role will be invited to complete a 15-minute Loom video recording assessment, which must be submitted within a strict 48-hour deadline to move forward.
