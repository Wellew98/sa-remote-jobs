We are looking for a Client Relationship & Retention Specialist to take ownership of complex customer situations where there is a risk of disengagement, cancellation, refund, or loss of trust.

This is a high-accountability, client-facing role requiring strong judgment, confident phone communication, commercial awareness, and the ability to remain calm and effective in difficult situations.

You will investigate the full customer journey, identify the true cause of an issue, and work directly with customers to help them regain confidence and move forward. This means looking beyond the immediate complaint to understand what is actually driving the customer’s concern, whether that is a practical, technical, delivery, expectation, or mindset blocker.

You will be expected to troubleshoot appropriate issues directly, coordinate across internal teams when additional action is required, and maintain ownership of the customer relationship through resolution.

This is not passive customer support or an aggressive refund-prevention role. It is a high-trust, consultative role for someone who can combine strong call control and influence with genuine curiosity, empathy, and problem-solving.

Success requires the ability to build trust quickly, lead challenging conversations confidently, diagnose problems accurately, and help customers move toward a constructive next step without pressure, manipulation, or unsupported promises.

THE COMPANY

A multi-8-figure ARR business with 200+ team members, bootstrapped and profitable. The company launches and scales information and software products on a marketing and sales engine refined across a dozen product launches and $100M+ in ad spend.

This seat sits within our client operations team and plays an important role in strengthening customer relationships, resolving complex issues, improving retention, and protecting long-term customer value.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

Take ownership of at-risk customer cases involving dissatisfaction, disengagement, cancellation, refund concerns, or loss of confidence

Contact assigned customers quickly by telephone and use SMS and email when appropriate

Review the customer’s complete journey before making contact to understand the history, context, and potential issues

Speak directly with customers by phone and build trust quickly, even when emotions are high

Diagnose practical, technical, delivery, expectation, or mindset blockers and identify the true root cause of the issue

Troubleshoot appropriate issues directly and coordinate additional support when required

Lead calm, confident resolution and de-escalation conversations

Separate the immediate complaint from the underlying issue and determine the most appropriate path forward

Help customers reframe unhelpful conclusions without dismissing legitimate concerns

Reconnect customers with their original goals and help establish a clear recovery plan and next action

Correct misunderstandings clearly and respectfully without becoming defensive or argumentative

Use strong conversational influence and judgment to help restore customer confidence without applying pressure or making unsupported promises

Make sound judgment calls while balancing customer experience, commercial considerations, and the facts of each case

Coordinate with Onboarding, Tech, Delivery, Sales, Finance, and other teams when additional action is required

Maintain ownership of the customer relationship while internal work is being completed rather than simply passing the customer between departments

Use approved support options or concessions when appropriate and justified

Escalate continued refund requests or complex cases through the appropriate process

Keep customers informed while outstanding issues are being resolved

Maintain clear and accurate records of conversations, decisions, commitments, actions, and outcomes

Identify recurring causes of disengagement, dissatisfaction, and refunds and recommend practical improvements to prevent them in the future

Previous experience in consultative or high-ticket sales/closing, Customer Retention, Customer Success, Account Management, Escalations, Complaints, Dispute Resolution, Service Recovery, or a similarly high-touch client-facing role

Strong conversational influence and call control, with the ability to confidently lead a customer toward a constructive next step

Strong ability to build rapport with frustrated, disappointed, uncertain, or skeptical customers

Comfortable leading difficult and emotionally charged conversations

Able to listen carefully while remaining confident enough to challenge an unhelpful conclusion when appropriate

Able to recognize legitimate customer or company-side issues rather than treating every concern as a mindset problem

Persuasive without relying on pressure, manipulation, or unsupported promises

Strong investigative and problem-solving skills with the ability to identify root causes rather than simply addressing surface-level issues

Comfortable troubleshooting practical issues and coordinating with other teams when additional expertise is required

Good commercial judgment and an understanding of customer value, retention, and financial impact

Strong organisation, attention to detail, and written documentation skills

Comfortable taking personal ownership of a customer issue and driving it toward resolution across multiple teams

Able to manage multiple complex customer cases and priorities at the same time

High level of integrity and sound judgment when determining the appropriate next step

Reliable, resilient, coachable, and willing to review calls and feedback to continuously improve
