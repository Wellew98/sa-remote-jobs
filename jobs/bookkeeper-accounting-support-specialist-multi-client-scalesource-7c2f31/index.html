Bookkeeper & Accounting Support Specialist – (Multi-Client)

Location

Remote (Work from Home)

Schedule

Monday – Friday | 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM (PST, CST, EST)

Compensation

$1,250 USD/month for the first 90 days / $1,500 USD/month after that.

About the Role

This is not a slow, back-office accounting role where things can wait until next week. This is clean books, tight deadlines, and real ownership over financial accuracy.

If numbers being off bothers you and you take pride in reconciling everything down to the cent, you’ll fit right in. If you need constant follow-up or let deadlines slip, this role will feel heavy fast.

This role owns the accuracy, consistency, and timeliness of financial operations across one or multiple businesses. You’ll handle day-to-day bookkeeping, reconciliations, and month-end close processes to ensure clean, reliable books that business owners and finance teams can trust.

By keeping records organized and up to date, you directly support better decision-making and eliminate the chaos caused by late or inaccurate financials.

What You'll Actually Do

Transaction coding, reconciliations, AP/AR support, invoicing, and collections follow-up.

Month-end prep and close checklists shipped on a tight, consistent cadence

Clean books and timely documentation kept ready for the owner, CPA, or finance lead

Recurring finance reports, cash updates, and close summaries delivered on schedule

Expense review, vendor follow-up, invoice workflows, and recordkeeping owned end to end

Expectations

Maintain clean, accurate books at all times with zero unexplained discrepancies

Complete monthly reconciliations and close processes on time (day 10 or faster)

Handle high transaction volume ($100k+ / 150+ transactions monthly) with consistency and precision

Keep all financial records, documentation, and reports organized and audit-ready

Deliver timely financial reports, cash updates, and summaries to stakeholders

Manage AP/AR, invoicing, and collections with follow-through and attention to detail

Communicate clearly with owners, CPAs, and internal teams regarding financial status

Take full ownership of accuracy, deadlines, and financial integrity

Requirements

3+ years of full-cycle bookkeeping or accounting support experience

2+ years of experience with QuickBooks Online and/or Xero

Experience reconciling 3+ accounts monthly for at least 12 months

Experience managing $100k+ in monthly transactions or 150+ transactions per month

Ability to close books by day 10 or faster for 6 consecutive months, or manage 10+ client books simultaneously
