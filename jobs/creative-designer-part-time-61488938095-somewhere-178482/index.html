Role Overview

We are seeking a highly skilled, mid-to-senior level Creative (2+ years of experience) to join our team as a lead for client projects, with a specific focus on Motion and 3D visual storytelling. This is a high-autonomy role for a self-starter who can take a brief, establish clear visual direction, and execute high-fidelity assets efficiently.

In this role, you will be responsible for conceptualizing, designing, and delivering high-impact visual assets—ranging from static brand collateral to dynamic 3D animations and motion graphics—to lead our client engagements. You will work closely with our leadership and internal team to ensure all outputs are visually cohesive, brand-aligned, and ready for high-stakes deployment. This role is scoped for 20 hours per week with a potential path to full-time conversion based on performance and business needs. Key Responsibilities

  • Creative Leadership: Take ownership of client projects from brief to final delivery. Set visual direction for campaigns and assets, ensuring they elevate our clients' brand authority.
  • Motion & 3D Execution: Create compelling CGI elements, 3D product visualizations, and motion graphics that bring brand narratives to life.
  • Asset Creation: Design polished, professional marketing collateral, including pitch decks, presentations, digital assets, and high-quality motion content.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Collaborate with the internal team to ensure creative outputs match campaign calendars, brand tone, and technical specifications.
  • Workflow Management: Maintain an organized system for raw files, 3D assets, and project renders, ensuring efficient hand-offs and consistent brand alignment across all projects.
  • Communication: Participate in scheduled calls during 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM ET, take direct feedback, and iterate quickly to meet project deadlines.

Required Qualifications & Competencies

  • Design Suite Mastery: Elite proficiency in Adobe Creative Cloud (After Effects, Premiere Pro, InDesign, Illustrator, Photoshop).
  • 3D & Motion Expertise: Demonstrated experience in 3D modeling, texturing, rendering, and motion design. Proficiency in tools such as Cinema 4D, Blender, Maya, or Unreal Engine.
  • Experience: 2+ years of professional creative/design experience, with a strong portfolio showcasing successful motion and/or CGI projects.
  • Self-Starter Autonomy: Proven ability to thrive in a high-autonomy environment. You are comfortable working from a blank page and setting a visual direction.
  • Communication: Excellent verbal and written English communication skills; comfortable collaborating with remote/offshore teams.
  • Reliability: Highly organized, detail-oriented, and able to manage variable workloads (5–20 hrs/wk range depending on project needs).
  • Availability: Must be available for team and client calls during 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM ET.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience working with B2B brands or agencies.
  • Strong portfolio demonstrating a mix of static design, motion graphics, and 3D craft.
  • Proficiency in presentation design (PowerPoint/Google Slides).
  • Experience with character animation or advanced physics simulation (e.g., fluid/cloth) is a plus.
