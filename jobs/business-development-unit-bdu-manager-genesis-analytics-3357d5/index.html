About Genesis Analytics

Genesis Analytics is an impact firm founded and headquartered in Africa, with offices in Johannesburg, Nairobi, Abidjan and London. We have worked in more than 115 countries, helping governments, development partners, foundations and companies make better decisions. Our work spans seven practices, from financial inclusion and jobs to health, climate and education.

About the role

The Business Development Unit (BDU) is Genesis's central engine for winning new work. It identifies opportunities, supports practices through the full bid cycle and submits competitive proposals to multilateral, bilateral and philanthropic clients such as the World Bank, UN agencies, FCDO, the European Union, the Global Fund and the Gates Foundation.

We are looking for a BDU Manager to lead the unit's day-to-day operations. You will manage and develop the BDU team, allocate opportunities, assure the quality of what we submit and make sure every bid is delivered on time and in full compliance. You will also act as a trusted partner to our technical practices, bringing market intelligence and performance insight that help them decide where to compete and how to win.

About 80% of your role focuses on competitive procurement: tenders, expressions of interest, proposals, frameworks and long-term agreements. The remaining 20% sits where tendering meets client relationships, such as tracking donor forecasts, understanding client priorities and positioning Genesis before opportunities reach the market.

The BDU works at pace. Each team member typically carries several live proposals at once, often with deadlines falling on the same day, alongside around ten large framework and key-account bids a year.

The unit is also an early adopter of AI in business development, using its own proposal tools to speed up research, drafting and compliance, so that the team can focus on strategy, judgement and quality.

Key responsibilities

Team leadership and resourcing

Line-manage the BDU team, including objectives, regular 1:1s, performance reviews and development plans.

Allocate opportunities and requests for collaboration from the Genesis technical practices’ to the most suitable team member, balancing workload and deadlines across the unit.

Coach and mentor team members on bid management, proposal quality and client communication.

Act as the first point of escalation for problems on live bids.

Client and internal engagement

Serve as the firm's first point of contact for new business, managing the business development mailbox, client approaches and direct invitations.

Ensure every client enquiry is acknowledged promptly and introduced to the right Genesis technical practice team.

Run the internal support request process so that Genesis technical practices receive fast, reliable support from the BDU.

Represent the BDU in internal and external settings.

Processes, tools and performance

Own the BDU standard operating procedures, ensure they are followed and refine them as the market changes.

Maintain the company knowledge base: corporate capability statements, CVs, past performance, templates and boilerplate.

Champion the use of AI and automation in business development, including training the team and maintaining data quality.

Track submissions, outcomes and win rates, run lessons-learned reviews and share insight with Genesis technical practice and senior management.

Bid and proposal management

Oversee the full bid cycle, from capture planning and kick-off to submission, clarifications and debrief.

Ensure compliance and lead final quality assurance before submission.

Create an enabling environment to ensure portal registrations and compliance documents are up to date, and supervise the team to execute submissions accordingly.

Coordinate selected large and strategic bids, frameworks and long-term agreements.

Work with the commercial and compliance teams on pricing, budgets, contractual terms and risk.

Ensure smooth coordination with consortium partners, subcontractors and individual experts, including teaming agreements and expert recruitment.

Pipeline development and market intelligence

Own the opportunity scanning system: the team leads day-to-day scanning, supported by AI tools, and you ensure it is complete, accurate and kept up to date in the pipeline tracker.

Track donor forecasts and procurement plans to anticipate opportunities before they are published.

Lead deep dives into priority clients, markets and themes, and share clear recommendations with Genesis technical practices.

Work with the Genesis technical practices to understand their markets and identify emerging areas where Genesis should build a presence.

Gather competitor and pricing intelligence to inform bid/no-bid decisions.

What we are looking for

Essential

At least 8 years' experience in business development, bid or proposal management in a consulting firm serving development partners, governments or foundations.

At least 3 years managing and developing a bid or business development team.

In-depth knowledge of the procurement rules and processes of major development partners, such as the World Bank, UN agencies, FCDO, the EU and the African Development Bank.

Strong understanding of proposal compliance, pricing and contractual requirements.

Experience with capture planning and pre-positioning for large opportunities.

Experience building or refining business development processes, tools and dashboards.

Desirable

A degree in economics, international development, public policy, business or a related field, or equivalent experience.

A proven track record of winning competitive tenders, including leading proposals of over USD 1 million and multi-lot frameworks.

Strong understanding of how successful bids look like with the ability to review and strengthen technical proposals.

Strong interpersonal and influencing skills, with confidence working with partners, senior experts and clients across cultures.

Excellent organisational skills, with the ability to manage multiple competing deadlines without compromising quality.

Comfort with AI tools, digital pipeline systems and Google Workspace, and an appetite to improve how a team works with them.

Familiarity with consulting work in Africa and with Genesis's sectors, such as financial inclusion, jobs, health, climate or evaluation.

Working knowledge of French or Portuguese.

A recognised bid management qualification, such as APMP Foundation or Practitioner.

Eligibility to work in South Africa or Kenya.
