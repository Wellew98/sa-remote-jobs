Role Overview

The Bookkeeper is responsible for maintaining accurate financial records and supporting day-to-day accounting operations for growing businesses. This role focuses on full-cycle bookkeeping, including transaction recording, reconciliations, accounts payable and receivable, and financial reporting.

You will support clean, reliable financial systems that enable leadership to make informed decisions and remain compliant. This role is ideal for detail-oriented professionals who value accuracy, consistency, and ownership over financial data.

Your Impact

Your work ensures financial clarity, compliance, and operational stability. By maintaining accurate books and timely reports, you help businesses understand performance, manage cash flow, and avoid costly errors. Your precision directly supports confident decision-making and long-term growth.

Core Responsibilities

General Bookkeeping & Accounting — 30%

Record and categorize daily financial transactions

Maintain general ledger and chart of accounts

Prepare journal entries and maintain documentation

Ensure accuracy, consistency, and confidentiality of financial records

Bank & Account Reconciliations — 20%

Reconcile bank, credit card, and merchant accounts

Identify and resolve discrepancies promptly

Maintain reconciliation schedules and supporting files

Accounts Payable — 15%

Process vendor bills and manage payment schedules

Maintain vendor records and AP aging

Ensure invoices are accurate and properly approved

Accounts Receivable — 15%

Issue customer invoices and track collections

Apply payments accurately

Monitor AR aging and follow up on overdue balances

Financial Reporting & Month-End Close — 10%

Support month-end close activities

Prepare basic financial statements (P&L, Balance Sheet)

Generate reports for internal review

Payroll & Tax Support — 10% (Client-Dependent)

Support payroll processing and recordkeeping

Organize documentation for tax preparation and compliance

Assist external accountants or CPAs as needed
