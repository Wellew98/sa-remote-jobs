Account Manager

Hire Overseas Sangoma, South Africa (Remote) Admin & Virtual Assistance Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

We're looking for an Account Manager to own client relationships and internal team coordination at a fast-moving B2B GTM content studio. You will lead weekly client syncs, manage the internal team's weekly deliverables, and serve as the primary point of contact between clients and the production side of the business. This is a client-facing, coordination-heavy role. You need to communicate with confidence, keep multiple accounts and internal workstreams organized simultaneously, and make sure nothing slips between client expectations and team execution. If you are a strong communicator, highly organized, and comfortable running client meetings and managing a team's weekly output without losing quality or momentum, this role is a strong fit. account management role with direct client access and real impact on retention and delivery quality What You'll Work On Client Relationship Management Serve as the primary point of contact for a
