Social Content Designer – Instagram, LinkedIn & Brand Creative | Remote

Position Type

Fractional, Remote

Function

Creative / Social Media Design

About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a

Social Content Designer

with strong visual taste who can transform an existing library of photography, brand assets, and creative direction into polished, ready-to-publish social content.

This is a

fractional, execution-focused creative role , not a social media strategy or management position. We provide the direction and assets; you turn them into high-quality Instagram and LinkedIn posts, graphics, and carousels while helping maintain a consistent backlog of finished content ready to publish.

What You’ll Own

Social Content Design

Design polished content for

Instagram and LinkedIn

Create single-image posts, graphics, and multi-slide carousels

Transform existing photography and brand assets into engaging social creative

Translate provided creative direction into finished designs

Maintain consistent visual quality across social content

Adapt creative for different social formats and placements

Brand & Visual Consistency

Work within established brand guidelines and creative direction

Maintain consistency across typography, layout, imagery, and visual hierarchy

Use strong design judgment to elevate existing assets

Ensure every piece of content feels cohesive with the broader brand

Create premium, intentional designs rather than generic or template-driven content

Content Production & Backlog

Maintain a consistent pipeline of completed social creative

Build and maintain a

backlog of finished content ready to publish

Manage multiple creative requests efficiently

Incorporate feedback accurately and quickly

Keep design files and creative assets organized

Deliver finished assets in appropriate formats for publishing

Requirements

Professional experience in graphic design, social media design, content design, or a similar creative role

Strong visual taste and attention to detail

Portfolio demonstrating polished social or digital design work

Experience designing for

Instagram and/or LinkedIn

Strong understanding of typography, layout, composition, and visual hierarchy

Ability to work effectively from existing brand assets and creative direction

Comfortable producing content consistently and efficiently

Strong organizational and file-management skills

Ability to receive feedback and translate it into polished creative

Comfortable working in a

fractional, remote capacity

Preferred Skills

Strong experience with Figma

Experience with

Adobe Illustrator

Experience with

Adobe Photoshop

Strong carousel design experience

Experience designing for both Instagram and LinkedIn

Experience working within established brand systems

Ability to work efficiently with large photography and creative asset libraries

Tools & Technology

Figma | Adobe Illustrator | Adobe Photoshop | Instagram | LinkedIn | Creative Asset Libraries | Social Media Design Tools

What Makes You a Strong Fit

You’ll likely thrive in this role if you

Have a naturally strong eye for design

Can make existing assets feel polished and intentional

Understand what makes social content visually engaging

Are particularly strong at designing clean, compelling carousels

Can follow established creative direction without requiring detailed instructions for every asset

Care about typography, spacing, hierarchy, and consistency

Work efficiently without sacrificing visual quality

Are execution-oriented rather than looking to own social strategy

Can provide reliable ongoing creative support on a fractional basis

What a Typical Project Looks Like

You’ll receive photography, brand assets, and creative direction for upcoming content. From there, you’ll turn those materials into polished Instagram or LinkedIn posts and carousels, incorporate feedback, prepare final assets, and organize completed creative for publishing.

In short

we provide the direction and assets; you turn them into premium social content and keep the content pipeline full.

Key Metrics for Success

Consistent delivery of high-quality social creative

Healthy backlog of finished assets ready to publish

Visual and brand consistency

Quality of Instagram and LinkedIn carousels

Turnaround time on creative requests

Accuracy to provided creative direction

Efficiency incorporating feedback and revisions

Organization of design files and creative assets

Reliability of fractional creative support
