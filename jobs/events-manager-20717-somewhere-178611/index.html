Events Manager About the Company Our client is a growing, mission-driven organization committed to creating impactful experiences that engage communities, strengthen relationships, and deliver memorable events. They are seeking a highly organized and proactive Events Manager to oversee the planning, coordination, and execution of live events, conferences, community initiatives, and special productions. Role Overview The Events Manager will be responsible for managing the complete lifecycle of events—from initial planning and logistics through on-site execution and post-event follow-up. This individual will work closely with leadership and internal teams to ensure every event reflects the organization's mission, brand, and commitment to excellence. The ideal candidate is an exceptional project manager who thrives in fast-paced environments, enjoys coordinating multiple moving pieces, and has a passion for creating outstanding attendee experiences. Key Responsibilities Event Planning & Execution - Lead the planning, coordination, and execution of live events, conferences, community activations, and special productions - Develop detailed event timelines, project plans, and execution checklists - Coordinate logistics, vendors, venues, equipment, and event materials - Ensure all event elements are completed accurately and on schedule - Prepare for upcoming events by confirming production details and resolving potential issues proactively - Support on-site event execution and troubleshoot operational challenges as needed Project & Operations Management - Manage multiple event projects simultaneously while maintaining attention to detail - Maintain event documentation, project trackers, and planning materials - Audit event assets and production elements to ensure readiness prior to each event - Coordinate with cross-functional teams to ensure all deliverables are completed on time - Track project status and communicate updates to leadership regularly Marketing & Event Promotion - Collaborate with the marketing team to promote upcoming events - Support the execution of social media content related to events and organizational initiatives - Ensure event communications align with brand standards and messaging - Help maximize event visibility and audience engagement across digital platforms Administrative Support - Monitor and respond to event-related communications - Maintain organized event files, schedules, and documentation - Coordinate meeting agendas and planning sessions - Assist leadership with event reporting and operational updates - Handle confidential information with professionalism and discretion Required Qualifications - 2–5 years of experience in Event Management, Project Management, Marketing, Hospitality, or a related field - Experience planning and coordinating events from concept through execution - Strong organizational and project management skills - Excellent written and verbal English communication skills - Ability to manage multiple deadlines and priorities simultaneously - Strong problem-solving and decision-making abilities - High attention to detail and commitment to delivering exceptional experiences - Professional, proactive, and adaptable work style - Comfortable working independently while collaborating with multiple stakeholders Preferred Qualifications - Experience coordinating conferences, corporate events, nonprofit events, or community programs - Familiarity with project management software and event planning tools - Experience working with vendors, venues, and production teams - Knowledge of social media promotion for events - Experience with event budgeting and logistics coordination - Familiarity with livestream or hybrid event production - Experience using AI productivity tools to improve planning and organization Technology & Tools Experience with the following is preferred: - Microsoft Office Suite - Google Workspace - Project management platforms (ClickUp, Monday.com, Asana, Trello, or similar) - Event management software - Canva - Social media management platforms - AI tools such as ChatGPT or Claude Role Details - Fully remote position - Full-time - Monday–Friday - 9:00 AM–5:00 PM EST - 2–5 years of experience required - Occasional flexibility may be required to support live events and special projects outside standard business hours What This Role Offers - Opportunity to lead and execute meaningful events with lasting impact - High level of ownership over event strategy and execution - Collaborative work environment with direct exposure to executive leadership -
