About the Opportunity

We are currently recruiting for a Sales Development Representative & Lead Coordinator to join a growing premium home-services business operating in Arizona, USA.

The business specialises in high-end residential and commercial services and works with homeowners across affluent areas of the Phoenix Valley.

This is a permanent opportunity for someone who enjoys customer interaction, lead qualification and appointment setting and is confident communicating with U.S. customers.

The successful candidate will play an important role in managing inbound enquiries and ensuring qualified opportunities are efficiently progressed through the sales pipeline.

About the Role

This is a lead qualification and appointment-setting position , rather than a traditional closing sales role.

You will be responsible for responding quickly to new enquiries, understanding the customer's requirements, qualifying opportunities and booking suitable appointments for the sales team.

You will also manage follow-ups, assist with Instagram enquiries and ensure the CRM remains accurate and up to date.

Key Responsibilities

Respond to new inbound enquiries within 1–3 minutes wherever possible.

Engage professionally and naturally with U.S. homeowners.

Qualify leads based on

Property location

Project requirements

Budget

Timing

Decision-maker availability

Set appropriate expectations around typical project budgets without providing final pricing.

Book qualified onsite appointments or route suitable enquiries for remote quoting.

Follow up with leads who have not responded.

Follow up on open quotes and opportunities.

Manage conversations through

Instagram DMs and convert suitable enquiries into phone conversations.

Maintain accurate customer records and notes within the CRM.

Keep pipeline stages, tasks and follow-ups up to date.

Escalate pricing questions, discounts, design decisions, unusual projects and other matters requiring management input.

Provide a short daily summary of activity, opportunities and follow-ups.

Maintain a high standard of customer service throughout every interaction.

Ability to work independently with limited supervision.

What We're Looking For

We are looking for someone who can create a warm and positive customer experience while maintaining strong qualification standards.

You should be able to have a genuine conversation with a customer, understand their needs and determine whether the opportunity is suitable before booking an appointment.

The ideal candidate will

Sound natural rather than scripted.

Build rapport quickly.

Focus on

quality appointments rather than appointment volume .

Be highly organised with CRM and follow-up tasks.

Take ownership of their work.

Be comfortable working independently.

Long-Term Opportunity

This is a permanent, open-ended position and is not a seasonal role.

As the business continues to grow, the successful candidate will have the opportunity to broaden their responsibilities into additional areas of the business, including lead qualification, customer reactivation, booking estimates and CRM/pipeline management across other service divisions.

Application Process

If you are interested in the opportunity and meet the requirements, please submit your application.

Shortlisted candidates will be required to complete a one-way introductory video , as strong spoken English, communication style and customer-facing presence are important aspects of the position.

Candidates may also be asked to participate in a live inbound lead scenario as part of the interview process.
