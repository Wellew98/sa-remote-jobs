About the Company

Our client is a California-based digital marketing agency specializing in lead generation for outdoor living contractors, including patio cover, deck, and screen enclosure businesses. The agency helps contractors generate opportunities through digital marketing and improve the way they manage and convert incoming leads.

The Opportunity

We’re looking for a proactive, marketing-savvy Client Success Manager to independently own client relationships, retention, communication, and account success.

You’ll manage approximately 30 accounts initially, growing toward 37–40, and coordinate with a fulfillment team of approximately four to five people. Reporting directly to agency leadership, you’ll make sure clients receive consistent support, actionable guidance, and reliable delivery.

This is a client success role that calls for strong marketing literacy. Dedicated media buyers handle campaign execution, while you own the client relationship and the outcomes around it.

What You’ll Do

Own client onboarding, pre-launch meetings, and recurring weekly, biweekly, or monthly check-ins based on each account’s needs.

Build trusted relationships with U.S.-based contractor business owners and serve as their primary point of contact.

Monitor account health, identify churn risks early, resolve escalations, and proactively communicate next steps.

Review Meta Ads and lead-generation performance, explain campaign reports, and translate findings into actionable recommendations.

Coach clients on lead response, follow-up, CRM usage, and conversion opportunities, and distinguish campaign issues from downstream sales and lead-management problems.

Coordinate with media buyers and fulfillment specialists to make sure campaigns, client requests, and deliverables are completed; assist with minor campaign adjustments when needed.

Maintain accurate account notes, monitor Slack communications, track tasks in ClickUp, and follow through on outstanding commitments.

Provide leadership with regular updates on client satisfaction, performance, risks, and operational priorities.

Identify appropriate upsell opportunities and proactively generate client reviews, testimonials, and referrals.

How Success Is Measured

Client retention of approximately 95%.

At least five new client reviews per month.

Approximately two to three video testimonials or interviews per month.

Approximately two successful upsells per month.

Consistent, proactive client communication, timely issue resolution, and reliable follow-through.

Clear visibility over account performance, client requests, and team deliverables without routine founder oversight.

Your First 90 Days

Days 1–30: Learn the agency’s services, client portfolio, campaign reporting, systems, and team workflows, and begin taking ownership of client communication and assigned accounts.

Days 31–60: Independently manage an increasing share of the portfolio, establish recurring account reviews, and identify retention and lead-conversion opportunities.

Days 61–90: Own the assigned portfolio and coordinate delivery with minimal supervision, proactively addressing client risks and tracking agreed KPIs.

These milestones describe the intended ramp-up approach.

What We’re Looking For

Approximately 3–5 years of relevant experience in Client Success, Account Management, or a closely related client-facing marketing role.

Strong understanding of Meta Ads, lead generation, campaign reporting, and conversion metrics.

Demonstrated experience managing multiple client accounts, handling challenging conversations, and supporting client retention.

Excellent spoken and written English, with the confidence and interpersonal skills to advise U.S.-based business owners.

Working knowledge of CRM systems and lead-management workflows, plus the ability to learn GoHighLevel quickly if you’re not already familiar with it.

Experience coordinating internal teams, tracking deliverables, and taking ownership of follow-through.

Strong organizational skills, reliability, attention to detail, initiative, and the ability to work independently during required hours.

Based in Canada or South Africa.

Availability to work 9 AM–6 PM, with a one-hour break.

Nice to Have

Previous experience in a digital marketing or lead-generation agency.

Experience supporting outdoor living, home services, construction, or contractor businesses.

Experience managing high-volume client portfolios and coaching business owners on lead conversion.

Previous team leadership or operational coordination experience.

Familiarity with GoHighLevel, Slack, ClickUp, Zapier, and CRM integrations or automations.

Demonstrated success with retention, upselling, testimonials, reviews, and client recovery.
