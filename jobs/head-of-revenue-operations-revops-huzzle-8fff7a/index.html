Job Summary

This is a senior-level

Head of Revenue Operations (RevOps) role combining internal ownership and client-facing delivery. You will design, build, and optimize revenue systems while also leading RevOps engagements for external clients.

This role is ideal for someone who can architect CRM systems from scratch, build integration workflows, and translate complex revenue data into strategic decisions .

You’ll start as an individual contributor and quickly evolve into building and leading a RevOps team.

Key Responsibilities

CRM Architecture & Data Management

Own and optimize the HubSpot CRMpipeline design, lifecycle stages, data models, and automation workflows

Define and enforce data standards across CRM and GTM tools (HubSpot, Airtable, outbound platforms)

Continuously audit and improve data quality, attribution logic, and record consistency

Build and maintain integrations across the revenue stack using tools like n8n, Zapier, or APIs

Revenue Analytics & Reporting

Develop real-time reporting systems for pipeline health, conversion rates, and revenue performance

Define and own key GTM metrics (pipeline coverage, CAC, LTV, retention, forecast accuracy)

Deliver actionable insights and strategic recommendations to leadership

Build executive-ready dashboards and client-facing reporting frameworks

RevOps-as-a-Service Delivery

Lead CRM audits and RevOps assessments for external clients

Design and implement CRM systems (HubSpot, Salesforce) for client organizations

Act as a trusted advisor on revenue operations, reporting, and system architecture

Build scalable playbooks and frameworks for RevOps service delivery

Systems Integration & Automation

Design automation workflows across CRM, outbound tools, and data platforms

Integrate billing systems (e.g., Stripe) with CRM for revenue tracking

Leverage AI tools (e.g., Claude, Gemini) for reporting, documentation, and workflow optimization

Ensure scalable, reliable system architecture across all tools

Core Requirements (Non-Negotiable)

Advanced

HubSpot CRM architecture experience (designing systems from scratch)

Strong experience with

workflow automation and integrations (n8n, Zapier, Make, APIs)

Deep understanding of

revenue analytics (pipeline velocity, CAC, retention, forecasting)

Working knowledge of

Salesforce (SFDC) and CRM migration strategies

Proven ability to operate in both

internal RevOps and client-facing environments
