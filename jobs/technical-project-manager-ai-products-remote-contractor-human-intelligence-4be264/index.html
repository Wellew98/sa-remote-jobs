Deliver AI-first projects end-to-end — senior TPM/BA wanted

TitleTechnical Project Manager (AI Products) — Remote (Contractor)

Location: Remote - Preference Philippines, Vietnam, Indonesia, Latin America (Mexico, Colombia, Brazil), Eastern Europe (Poland, Ukraine, Romania, Bulgaria, Belarus), Africa (Kenya, South Africa, Nigeria, Ethiopia, Ghana, Egypt) if English is strong.)

Base feeUSD $1,000 - $1,500 / MONTH + Performance Bonus (paid via bank direct deposit)

Employment typeContractor

Who We Are

We build AI-enabled products across health, wellbeing, education and enterprise. We ship end-to-end projects quickly, measure outcomes, and require strong delivery leadership combined with rigorous business analysis.

What you’ll experience

Real ownership — You’ll shape features from discovery to delivery, not just gather requirements.

Deep impact — You won’t just support the roadmap; you’ll help design it.

High-growth environment — We’re scaling quickly, and this role grows with us.

End-to-end exposure — Business analysis, product strategy, delivery leadership, stakeholder alignment, user insight — all in one role.

A mission that matters — We are building technology that helps organisations scale intentionally, transparently, and human-first. We care about clarity, empowerment, and creating tools that let teams work smarter together.

Who You Are

You are a Technical Project Manager who is equally strong in business analysis. You can convert high-level goals into actionable requirements, keep delivery on track, and guide teams through structured governance. You manage daily execution, communicate clearly, and produce documentation that reduces ambiguity and accelerates engineering throughput. Most importantly, you are technically fluent enough to work closely with developers, challenge assumptions, understand architecture discussions, and independently structure delivery plans — without needing constant engineering translation.

Responsibilities

Manage and deliver AI-first projects end‑to‑end (scope → launch → iterate) while acting as a hands‑on business analyst to translate clinical/market goals into shippable requirements.

Delivery leadership: Scope, schedule, budget; RAID; standups/scrum; stakeholder updates; vendor orchestration.

BA excellence: Elicit & document requirements (epics/user stories/acceptance criteria), process mapping, gap analysis.

Quality & readiness: UAT plans, test coordination, cut‑over/runbooks, KPI/OKR tracking, retrospective loops.

Ops & governance: Define RACI, change control, documentation canon, and risk & privacy checklists per project.

Automation: Stand up dashboards, templates, and AI/automation to reduce PM overhead and improve signal.

Project Management: Exceptional time efficiency, task prioritisation and delegration practices utilising tools including Basecamp, Mattermost/Slack, and instant messaging.

Must-have qualifications

5–8+ yrs TPM/PM; BA toolkit; experience delivering cloud-based or AI-enabled products/features alongside engineering teams; excellent writing; tool fluency -- we use ClickUp for project management.

Must have 3+ years proof of fast paced project management, with multiple stakeholders to manage and projects to deliver

Has over 90%+ delivery rate within projected timelines

KPIs

On-time/within‑scope delivery; stakeholder NPS; escaped‑defect rate; time‑to‑decision; documentation completeness.

Nice-to-have

  • Experience in health/clinical product delivery or regulated data projects.
  • Familiarity with automated KPI dashboards and AI tooling for PM workflows.

Compensation & performance bonuses

Base feeUSD $1,000 - $1,500 / MONTH

Performance incentivesAnnual bonus for KPI over-performance

Payment & contractor terms

Independent Contractor status only. Payment in USD via bank direct deposit. Invoicing monthly.

How to apply

You will complete a self-initiated 16mins Ai-video Interview as well as personality profile survey result. Also submit a one-page delivery plan and a sample spec with acceptance criteria you authored. Shortlist candidates will be invited for a rapid delivery case discussion.

Closing note

We hire TPMs who can both lead delivery and produce crystal-clear requirements that reduce rework and accelerate launches.
