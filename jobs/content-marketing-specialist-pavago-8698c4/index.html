About the Role

We are looking for a creative and execution-focused Content Marketing Specialist to manage video and written content across the company's digital channels.

You will work directly with the founder to turn ideas, conversations, and business insights into engaging YouTube videos, short-form social media content, written posts, and newsletters. Your work will help establish a consistent online presence, communicate the founder's expertise, and build meaningful connections with the target audience.

This role is ideal for someone who enjoys storytelling, understands how to create content for different platforms, and can independently manage the process from content development to publishing.

What You'll Own

Video Content Creation

Work with the founder to develop video ideas and turn interviews, discussions, and insights into engaging content.

Create and edit long-form videos for YouTube and short-form videos for social media platforms.

Identify compelling moments from longer videos and repurpose them into shorter clips.

Develop video titles, descriptions, captions, and other supporting content.

Maintain a consistent video production and publishing schedule.

Written Content & Social Media

Create engaging written content for platforms such as LinkedIn and X.

Turn founder conversations, business insights, and video content into relevant social media posts.

Write in a natural and engaging style that reflects the founder's voice and perspective.

Adapt content to suit the audience and format of each platform.

Plan and maintain a consistent social media content calendar.

Newsletter Creation & Management

Write, edit, and publish newsletters for the company's target audiences.

Transform business insights, founder discussions, and existing content into valuable newsletter material.

Plan newsletter topics and maintain a consistent publishing schedule.

Monitor subscriber engagement and identify opportunities to improve content quality and relevance.

Content Planning & Performance

Organize content ideas, production schedules, and publishing calendars.

Ensure content is published consistently across the relevant platforms.

Monitor content performance, including views, reach, engagement, and subscriber growth.

Identify successful topics and formats to guide future content creation.

Share regular updates on content production, publishing progress, and performance.

Requirements

Experience in content marketing, video content production, social media content creation, or a similar role.

Strong writing and storytelling skills, with the ability to create engaging content for different audiences.

Experience creating and editing video content for YouTube and social media.

Understanding of long-form and short-form video formats and how to adapt content for different platforms.

Experience writing social media posts for platforms such as LinkedIn and X.

Experience writing or managing newsletters.

Ability to turn raw ideas, interviews, and discussions into structured and engaging content.

Familiarity with content planning, scheduling, and publishing tools.

Comfortable using AI tools to support content research, writing, editing, and production.

Strong organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple content projects independently.

Excellent communication skills and the ability to work directly with a founder.

Nice to Have

Experience managing a founder's personal brand or executive social media presence.

Familiarity with AI, automation, SaaS, or technology-focused businesses.

Experience managing a YouTube channel and optimizing video content for discovery.

Familiarity with professional video editing tools.

Experience growing newsletters or online audiences through consistent content.

Knowledge of AI-assisted video editing and content repurposing workflows.

What Makes You a Strong Fit

Creative Storyteller

You know how to turn ideas and information into content that captures attention and communicates value.

Strong Content Execution

You can manage the entire content process, from planning and creation to editing and publishing.

Platform-Aware

You understand how content should be adapted for YouTube, LinkedIn, X, and newsletters.

Independent & Organized

You can manage deadlines, content calendars, and multiple deliverables without constant supervision.

Ownership Mentality

You take responsibility for the quality and consistency of the content you produce.

AI-Driven

You use AI tools to improve your workflow and increase content output while maintaining quality.

Audience-Focused

You understand how to create content that resonates with the intended audience.

Communicative

You proactively share progress, ideas, and potential blockers.
