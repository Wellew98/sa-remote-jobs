Video Editor (In-House)

RolePerformance Video Editor

FunctionPost-production editing

Responsibility: Edit and deliver short-form, ad content, and wider video content for social channels and landing pages.

Reports toHead of Marketing

LocationRemote

Employment3 days per week (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday)

About Us

Founded in 2019, Soar With Us is one of the UK's fastest-growing e-commerce advertising agencies — a team of 30+ A-Players who've generated over £250m in client revenue over the last 12 months, working with brands like TRIP, Cowshed, Heights, IM8, 47 Skin, and Nutrition Geeeks.

We're investing heavily in video, and we're hiring our second in-house Video Editor to increase output across our own marketing funnel — not client work. You'll own editing for our organic social, website-converting VSLs, and Meta ad creative, working closely with our video content executive.

What You'll Do

Edit 8–12 short-form clips a week (30–60 sec) for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and LinkedIn — subtitled, clean audio, minimal extra polish

Cut longer-form, sales-driven VSLs for our website, with motion graphics where needed

Produce 8–10 Meta ad variants a month — hooks, CTAs and motion graphics testing off core assets

Pull 5–10 minute YouTube highlight clips from longer recordings

Occasionally support podcast production (trailers, full episodes)

What You'll Need

Attitude

Take full ownership of both your successes and failures.

Entrepreneurial mindset with the ability to see the bigger picture.

Proactive, solution-oriented, and adaptable.

Exceptional attention to detail and strong problem-solving skills.

Confident in building long-term relationships.

Ability to self-manage and prioritize effectively in a fast-paced environment.

Experience & Skills

3+ years' video editing experience, ideally in e-commerce

Expert in Premiere Pro and After Effects

A sharp eye for short-form pacing, hooks, and what actually drives action — not just what looks good

Ability to take a long, raw recording and independently find what's worth cutting

Comfortable working from timestamps with minimal hand-holding

Strong written and spoken English, reliable with deadlines, proactive communicator

*Please submit a valid portfolio or showreel that demonstrates your expertise*

Incredible Client Base

: Work with some of the fastest-growing and most established DTC e-commerce brands globally.

Skill Development

Broaden your skill set with diverse projects, ongoing learning opportunities, and regular mentoring from senior management.

Start-Up Sensibilities

Be part of a rapidly growing agency where you’ll have a direct impact on shaping how we do things.
