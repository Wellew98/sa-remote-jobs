About the Role

We are seeking a detail-oriented Construction Accountant to support the day-to-day accounting operations of a growing construction and real estate business. The ideal candidate has experience with construction accounting, is proficient in Sage Accounting , and is comfortable working in a remote, fast-paced environment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Maintain accurate financial records and general ledger transactions.
  • Process accounts payable and accounts receivable.
  • Perform bank and account reconciliations.
  • Assist with month-end and year-end closing activities.
  • Track project costs, job costing, and construction-related expenses.
  • Prepare financial reports and support budgeting activities.
  • Ensure accounting records are accurate and compliant with company policies.
  • Collaborate with internal teams to resolve accounting discrepancies.
  • Maintain organized financial documentation and records.

Required Qualifications

  • 1–3 years of experience in construction accounting or a similar role.
  • Hands-on experience using Sage Accounting (required).
  • Strong understanding of accounting principles and financial reporting.
  • Excellent attention to detail and organizational skills.
  • Strong communication skills in English.
  • Ability to manage priorities and work independently in a fully remote environment.
