Cross Border Talents is actively looking for a DevOps Team Lead to join our client. Our client is fast-growing non-profit social enterprise with different office locations in Europe and Africa. Our client builds digital platforms that supports young women. Our client has a fast-paced, informal, and friendly work environment.

CANDIDATES from EUROPE this is for you

As a DevOps Team Lead you will be responsible for managing and leading the team as well as being able to follow management tasks.

Responsibilities

Coordinating the DevOps team's tasks with the different teams projects.

Making sure the infrastructure that is develop is secure (information security & personal data).

Leading and developing your team members as well as yourself.

Documenting your work in order to make sure that it can be understandable for future use.

Working in an agile way.

Building, configuring and maintaining the framework needed by the client's software. (Automating the creation, maintenance, and monitoring of the framework)

Setting up, testing, and maintaining the frameworks of tools that the client use in order to support their software development and launch.

Being responsible for optimizing the flow of work from build to release.

Minimum 5 years of experience working as a DevOps.

Experience managing a team of DevOps Engineers is a plus.

Previous experience with Kubernetes container orchestration (using K8s within AWS ecosystem).

You have previous experience working with Cloud tools (using AWS, RDS, EC2, SQS).

Experince with Terraform, Grafana, Prometheus, Kibana, and Github Actions is a plus.

You are currently based in Portugal/South Africa.

You are fluent in English.

Personal development budget

Unlimited holidays

Growth opportunities

Being part of a product to help shape while our client grow, you will be able to leave your mark on things are done
