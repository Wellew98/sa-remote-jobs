Job DescriptionRevOps & Data Enrichment Specialist (AI-Enabled)

LocationRemote (LATAM or South Africa)

TypeFull-Time

Role Overview

We are looking for a hybrid RevOps & Data Enrichment Specialist to join our team. This role sits at the intersection of revenue operations, analytics, and modern AI-assisted execution ("vibe coding").

You will be responsible for scaling our data enrichment workflows, optimizing go-to-market (GTM) processes, and building lightweight automated tools to keep our GTM pipeline clean, actionable, and scalable. You should have a sharp commercial mindset, strong analytical fundamentals, and the ability to leverage AI-first developer tools to solve operational bottlenecks fast.

Key Responsibilities

1. Data Enrichment & Pipeline Hygiene

  • Design, build, and maintain automated data enrichment workflows across target account and lead lists.
  • Source, normalize, and validate firmographic, technographic, and workforce intelligence data using APIs, scraping tools, and enrichment vendors (e.g., Clay, Apollo, Clearbit).
  • Audit and optimize our CRM/database hygiene to ensure high match rates and high data fidelity for revenue teams.

2. Revenue Operations & Analytics

  • Map GTM processes to identify bottlenecks in lead routing, scoring, and data flow between marketing, sales, and customer success.
  • Build dashboards and reporting models tracking core pipeline metrics, enrichment hit rates, sequence conversion, and unit economics.
  • Partner with commercial leadership to translate raw pipeline data into actionable revenue and workforce strategy insights.

3. Rapid Tooling & Automation ("Vibe Coding")

  • Leverage modern AI tools (e.g., Cursor, Claude, v0, Bolt, Replit) to quickly build custom internal scripts, micro-apps, and webhooks for data transformation.
  • Connect disparate systems via REST APIs, webhooks, and automation tools (Zapier, Make, n8n).
  • Turn complex data operations into repeatable, automated micro-services without waiting on central engineering resources.

Required Qualifications & Experience

  • Experience: 3–6 years in Revenue Operations, Sales Operations, Growth Marketing, or Commercial Analytics within a B2B SaaS, management consulting, or financial services environment.
  • RevOps Tech Stack: Hands-on experience with CRMs (HubSpot, Salesforce), automation platforms (Zapier, Make, n8n), and enrichment tools (Clay, Apollo, PhantomBuster, etc.).
  • Analytics & Data: Strong proficiency in SQL, Excel/Google Sheets, and business intelligence platforms (Power BI, Tableau, Looker).
  • AI-Assisted Prototyping: Demonstrated ability to use AI tools/LLMs to write functional Python, JavaScript, or custom API integrations rapidly.
  • Business Acumen: Ability to view data through a revenue lens — connecting data quality directly to sales efficiency and customer acquisition cost (CAC).
  • Communication: Fluency in English (written and spoken) with a strong capability to distill complex technical workflows into clear business recommendations.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Prior background at a top-tier management consultancy or fintech/SaaS scale-up.
  • Experience with web scraping frameworks or custom API wrapper development.
  • Familiarity with workforce data, firmographic intelligence, or account-based marketing (ABM) operations.
