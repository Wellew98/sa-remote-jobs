Head of Branding & Marketing - Gaming Tech

LocationSouth Africa (Remote / Hybrid)

Compensation$4,500–$5,000/month USD (or 80,000ZAR/month)

IndustryGaming / Technology / Entertainment

ScheduleFull-time, aligned to UK business hours

____________________________________________________________________________

About the Company

Combining a passion for gaming, technology, and motorsport culture with a focus on high-quality content and user experience, they are global leaders in the space,

Operating with a small, highly talented, and collaborative team, the company values creativity, innovation, and craftsmanship. Their products and digital experiences reach a global audience, and they are known for delivering engaging, interactive, and community-driven solutions for racing game enthusiasts.

Candidates joining the team will have the opportunity to shape the brand’s voice, creative direction, and impact across multiple platforms, while contributing to a culture that prizes quality, creativity, and long-term growth.

About the Role

We’re seeking a Head of Branding & Marketing to lead all brand and marketing activities for a gaming academy focused on tools and apps for racing video game fans.

This is a senior, hands-on creative leadership role responsible for shaping how the world sees and experiences the brand across every channel, product, and piece of content.

You’ll define and refine brand identity, oversee all communications, lead a small marketing and creative team, and ensure every launch, video, and message meets high audience expectations.

You’ll collaborate closely with a performance marketing specialist and manage a small but talented team including a graphic designer, writer, and social media manager.

____________________________________________________________________________

Key Responsibilities

  • Brand Leadership: Own the visual and verbal identity across all platforms.
  • Creative Direction: Lead creation of video, visual, and written content that communicates the story with clarity and impact.
  • Team Management: Guide and mentor the marketing and creative team, ensuring quality and consistency across output.
  • Product Launches: Plan and execute cohesive brand-led campaigns for new products, features, and collaborations.
  • Messaging and Positioning: Craft compelling narratives and ensure alignment across web, social, email, and app.
  • Social Media and Communications: Oversee community-facing messaging, tone of voice, and storytelling.
  • Collaboration: Work closely with the performance marketing lead to align paid and organic initiatives.
  • Brand Health: Monitor and maintain brand integrity across all assets and touchpoints.
  • Content Calendar: Build and manage a structured campaign and content roadmap tied to product goals.

____________________________________________________________________________

Requirements

  • 10+ years of experience in brand marketing, creative strategy, or communications, ideally within gaming, technology, or entertainment.
  • Proven ability to lead creative and marketing teams with high attention to detail and strong creative standards.
  • Fluent in English, with exceptional communication and storytelling skills.
  • Passionate about video games and technology, ideally with an appreciation for motorsport and esports culture.
  • Based in South Africa with strong organizational and leadership skills in a remote setting.
  • Strong understanding of building emotional, long-term connections with an audience through content and brand voice.
  • Experience managing agencies, freelancers, or internal creative teams.
  • Comfortable balancing brand craft with strategic goals and commercial awareness.

Nice to Have

  • Experience overseeing video production from concept to release.
  • Familiarity with community marketing or creator-driven content ecosystems.
  • Background in app or digital product branding.
  • Understanding of social media storytelling and audience development.

What You’ll Get

  • Creative freedom to shape one of sim racing’s most recognizable global brands.
  • Direct collaboration with a founder-led team focused on quality and craft.
  • A small, high-performing team and the ability to make a visible impact.
  • Competitive compensation, flexible work setup, and long-term growth potential.
