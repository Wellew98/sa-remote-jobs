Executive Administrator

Remote Recruitment Our UK-based client is hiring a full-time, remote Executive Administrator based in South Africa Full-time Admin & Virtual Assistance Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

Some people manage tasks. You manage the machinery that makes a whole business run on time. Job Overview Our UK-based client is hiring a full-time, remote Executive Administrator based in South Africa - a senior-level administrator who combines rock-solid organisation with the confidence to deal directly with leadership, clients and suppliers. This is a long-term role on UK business hours. About Our Client The client is an established UK company where the administrative backbone carries real weight: contracts, reporting, scheduling, compliance and communication all flow through it. They want an experienced professional who can own that backbone, not just support it. Key Responsibilities - Run core administrative operations: documents, records, reporting and correspondence - Manage diaries, meetings and travel for senior team members - Prepare board packs, reports and client-ready documents - Own key registers - contracts, renewals, compliance dates - and act before deadlines - Coordinate between departments so decisions turn into completed actions - Handle sensitive information with discretion and sound judgement - Refine administrative processes so the business scales without friction What You Will Be Doing Day-to-Day Your mornings start with the operational picture: today's meetings, this week's deadlines, and the registers that need attention. You will prepare documents to a client-ready standard, keep leadership diaries realistic, and chase the actions that keep projects honest. Afternoons bring the deeper work: reports, process improvements, renewals and the quiet housekeeping that prevents next month's emergencies. You will operate with autonomy, backed by a team that communicates openly and values initiative. This suits a senior South African professional who has outgrown junior admin roles and wants genuine ownership. You are organised to your bones, unflustered by executives, and known for delivering exactly what you promised, when you promised it. If you are based in South Africa with excellent English and a track record of running serious administration, this is a stable remote career with a UK employer that treats administrators as professionals. Apply today.
