Customer-Facing Video Content Creator & YouTube SEO Specialist

LocationRemote | LATAM | South Africa

Working HoursFlexible (8:00 AM–5:00 PM / 9:00 AM–5:00 PM / 9:00 AM–6:00 PM)

Salary Range$1,300 - $2,000/month

Role Overview

We are looking for a creative, hands-on Video Content Creator who can work directly with clients while transforming long-form video and audio content into engaging short-form clips for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok, and Facebook. The ideal candidate combines editing skills, storytelling, and YouTube SEO knowledge to maximize audience engagement. This role is client-facing and requires coaching clients to capture content effectively.

Responsibilities

  • Edit and repurpose long-form content into short-form videos (shorts/reels) with a raw, authentic feel.
  • Apply YouTube SEO strategies: titles, descriptions, tags, and thumbnails to maximize visibility and engagement.
  • Conduct client-facing sessions to coach clients on recording content and refining messaging.
  • Use tools like CapCut, Opus Clips, or similar editing software to streamline workflow.
  • Add hooks, call-to-actions, captions, and graphics that elevate the content.
  • Source and integrate supporting visuals (images, graphics, approved assets) as needed.
  • Collaborate with the team to maintain brand voice, target audience alignment, and campaign objectives.
  • Stay current with trends in short-form video editing, content marketing, and YouTube SEO.

Requirements

  • 1–3 years of experience in video editing or content creation, preferably with social media or marketing content.
  • Proficiency in CapCut, Opus Clips, or similar video editing software.
  • Strong storytelling abilities: hooks, pacing, delivery, and calls-to-action.
  • Excellent design sensibility and ability to match visuals with messaging.
  • Strong English communication skills for captions, coaching, and client interaction.
  • Ability to maintain a professional yet authentic, engaging style in client-facing scenarios.
  • Organizational skills to manage content libraries and video assets.
  • Self-starter with attention to detail and creative problem-solving.
  • Curiosity or experience with YouTube SEO and audience engagement strategies.
  • Portfolio or examples of previous work demonstrating editing, social media content, or client-facing interactions.

Preferred Skills

  • Knowledge of motion graphics or light animation for text/graphics overlays.
  • Familiarity with social media trends and platform-specific best practices.
  • Understanding of marketing principles and audience engagement strategies.

Assessment Task

Candidates will be asked to create a 3-minute short-form video from a provided coaching snippet to demonstrate editing skills, storytelling, and YouTube SEO understanding.
