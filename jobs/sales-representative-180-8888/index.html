Sales Representative

Work HoursMonday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST/MST

Pay Range$1,000 - $1,500/month + Commission

Location of SearchSouth Africa, LatAm

Work LocationRemote

Company Overview

A growing real estate media company providing listing photography, video and related marketing services to real estate professionals. The company has grown steadily through referrals and is now building its first dedicated sales function to support its next stage of growth. It is hiring a Sales Representative to help build and refine that function.

Role Overview

As a Sales Representative, you will own the full sales cycle, from first outreach to a confirmed booking. You will prospect real estate agents, respond to inbound leads from advertising campaigns, and turn interest into scheduled shoots.

You will also stay close to clients after the work is delivered, helping build repeat business and referrals, and you will have a real voice in shaping the scripts and outreach approach as the sales function takes shape.

Responsibilities

  • Identify and contact real estate agents by phone, email and messaging to introduce the company's services.
  • Present the offer clearly and confidently, and qualify prospects by understanding their listing and branding needs.
  • Build and manage a steady pipeline of new opportunities.
  • Respond quickly to inbound leads from ad campaigns and move them toward booking.
  • Close deals and book shoots, collecting the details needed for each appointment.
  • Schedule appointments directly for clients or guide them through self-scheduling.
  • Follow up after each shoot to confirm the experience went well and to encourage repeat business and referrals.
  • Coordinate with the operations and customer service teams on client needs.
  • Track all leads, conversations and deals in GoHighLevel, keeping pipeline stages and client records accurate.
  • Report on activity and sales results.
  • Help develop and refine scripts, offers and outreach approaches, and share feedback on what is working.
  • Use AI tools to work more efficiently.

Qualifications

  • Language requirement: Excellent English communication skills
  • Experience: 2 to 5 years of sales experience, ideally including outbound prospecting.
  • Experience running a full sales cycle, from outreach to close.
  • Comfortable with high-volume outreach and handling objections, with a confident, clear phone presence.
  • Strong drive and motivation, with a desire to grow with the company.
  • Organized, with strong follow-up habits.
  • Open to using AI tools in daily work.
  • Comfortable with phone, email, and messaging tools, an online booking system, and Google Workspace or Microsoft Office.
  • Reliable high-speed internet connection and a quiet, dedicated remote workspace with a professional headset.
  • Available to work full-time, Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM U.S. Eastern Time or U.S. Mountain Time.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with GoHighLevel.
  • Experience selling to real estate agents or within the real estate industry.
  • Experience selling creative, media or marketing services.
  • Experience as a BDR or SDR.
  • Spanish language skills.
