AI Video Editor & Content Producer - Job Description

Job Title

AI Video Producer & Editor (Short-Form Social)

Location / Employment Details

  • Location: Fully remote.
  • Schedule: Full-time.
  • Time zone: Flexible. The company operates on U.S. Eastern Time, and the role needs enough overlap for creative check-ins and feedback, not a full shared workday.
  • Reports to: Founder
  • Start date: As soon as possible

About the Company

We are a rapidly growing U.S.-based AI-native video content studio. We make short-form video for social, built with the newest generative video tools and designed to stop the scroll.

The studio was founded this year by a content industry veteran who spent years building content inside other companies before going out on their own. Demand has grown faster than one person can produce, and we're now building the team.

Our work sits where traditional content roles blur. Writing, generating, and editing happen at the same time, in the same workflow. We've built an internal system that turns a creative conversation into ready-to-use AI prompts, so the people on our team spend their time on story, taste, and craft.

About the Role

The founder is currently the bottleneck. Client demand is there, but every video still runs through one person, and the studio can't grow until someone else can take a concept from conversation to finished cut.

This is not a traditional editing seat. In AI video, the lines between creator, writer, shooter, and editor collapse, and this person will work across all of them. You'll hear the idea, shape the story, generate the shots, and edit it into something people actually watch.

You don't need to be an AI prompting expert on day one. Our system handles much of the prompt writing. What we can't teach is storytelling instinct and taste, and that is what this hire is really about.

Key Responsibilities

Story & Concept Development

  • Turn a short creative conversation or brief with the founder into a clear concept, hook, and story arc for each video
  • Write or refine scripts, scene breakdowns, and shot lists for short-form social
  • Bring your own ideas, angles, and trend reads to the table rather than waiting for direction

AI Video Generation

  • Generate scenes, characters, and shots with AI video and image tools (Higgsfield, Seedance, and similar), working from the studio's prompt system
  • Iterate on prompts and outputs until each shot matches the creative intent, keeping characters and visual style consistent across a piece
  • Track new model and tool releases and recommend what the studio should adopt

Editing & Post-Production

  • Edit AI-generated and live footage into finished, platform-ready cuts: pacing, transitions, sound design, music, captions, and on-screen text
  • Deliver multiple aspect ratios and hook variations for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts
  • Keep project files and the asset library organized so work can be revisited and reused

Performance & Studio Workflow

  • Review how content performs and apply what you learn to hooks, length, and pacing
  • Manage several projects at once and hit agreed turnaround times
  • Document repeatable workflows and templates as the studio adds people

Required Qualifications

  • A reel or portfolio of short-form social video you edited, showing strong hooks and clear storytelling
  • 2+ years editing video for social media, organic content, or marketing
  • Proficiency in at least one professional editing tool (Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, CapCut, or Final Cut Pro)
  • Proven ability to take a loose brief and return a strong first version without step-by-step direction
  • Strong written and spoken English, comfortable talking through creative ideas on a call
  • Genuine interest in AI video tools and the ability to pick up new ones quickly
  • Reliable internet and a computer capable of handling video editing and rendering

Preferred Qualifications

  • Hands-on work with AI video and image tools such as Higgsfield, Seedance, Kling, Runway, Veo, or Midjourney
  • Content in your portfolio that went viral or drove strong view counts
  • Experience as a creator running your own social channels
  • Scriptwriting or copywriting for short-form social
  • Motion graphics or compositing skills (After Effects or similar)
  • Prior work with U.S. brands, creators, or a content agency or studio

Ideal Candidate Profile

You are

  • A storyteller first and an editor second. You think in hooks, beats, and payoffs.
  • Someone with real taste, who knows within seconds whether a video works and can explain why
  • A self-starter who hears an idea once and comes back with something better than expected
  • Curious about AI video and excited to experiment, not intimidated by tools that change every month
  • Comfortable in an early-stage, founder-led environment where the process is still being built
  • Fast without being sloppy, and happy to iterate on feedback

Why Join Us

  • Ground floor: You'll be one of the first hires at a studio with real client demand behind it.
  • The frontier of video: You'll work daily with the newest generative tools, and your portfolio will show it.
  • Direct access to the founder: You'll learn from someone with years of experience building content at established companies.
  • Room to grow: As the studio adds people, the right person can grow into a lead creative role.

Hiring Process

  • Recruiter screen and portfolio review
  • Conversation with the founder
  • Short creative brief: take a concept and turn it into a short piece using AI tools and your editing workflow
  • Offer

Please include a link to your reel or portfolio when you apply.
