About the Company

Our client is an early-stage, founder-led AI-native Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) technology company. The platform helps compliance and risk teams at regulated financial services organizations—including fintechs, lenders, community banks, credit unions, and insurers—automate manual workflows across risk management, vendor oversight, and regulatory compliance. Joining this fast-growing team offers the opportunity to work directly alongside company leadership and make a direct impact on the company's trajectory.

Role Overview

We are seeking a sharp, reliable, and exceptionally organized Executive Assistant to support both company leadership and a growing client base.

This is a high-impact, hybrid position combining executive administrative support with client-facing coordination. You will keep leadership organized and operations running smoothly while serving as a first point of contact for client inquiries.

The ideal candidate is proactive, detail-oriented, and polished, with the ability to communicate professionally with compliance and risk professionals at U.S. financial institutions. You should be comfortable taking full ownership of your responsibilities, working independently without constant direction, and ensuring no critical detail falls through the cracks.

Key Responsibilities

Executive & Administrative Support

  • Provide day-to-day administrative support to the founding and leadership team.
  • Manage executive calendars, coordinate complex meetings across multiple time zones, and organize commitments.
  • Attend internal and client meetings to take detailed notes, document action items, and track follow-ups through completion.
  • Assist with executive email triage, general communication organization, digital file maintenance, and recurring operational workflows.
  • Conduct research, data entry, light bookkeeping-related administrative support (e.g., invoice tracking), and special projects as assigned.
  • Proactively anticipate administrative needs to keep leadership focused on core business priorities.

Client Support & Onboarding Coordination

  • Serve as the primary first point of contact for client inquiries via email and designated support channels.
  • Triage incoming requests, resolve routine questions independently, and escalate technical issues with full context to the appropriate team member.
  • Support new client onboarding by managing account setup, document collection, data entry, and deliverable tracking.
  • Assist in formatting and preparing questionnaires, reports, templates, and client-facing materials.
  • Maintain high responsiveness and clear communication to keep clients informed throughout support and onboarding workflows.

CRM & Data Management

  • Maintain accurate, up-to-date client records and interaction logs within the CRM.
  • Track open client items, deliverables, and outstanding action items.
  • Ensure data hygiene across administrative and client support systems.

Operational Support

  • Assist in documenting internal processes and refining administrative workflows as the company scales.
  • Proactively identify missing information, process bottlenecks, or outstanding tasks before they escalate.
  • Take ownership of evolving operational projects to support company growth.

Qualifications & Skills

Required

  • Experience: 2+ years of professional experience in executive assistance, administrative support, client success, or customer support.
  • Communication: Native or near-native written and spoken English with a polished, professional tone suitable for U.S.-based financial services clients.
  • Organization: Exceptional organizational skills, high attention to detail, and a proven ability to manage multiple open workstreams simultaneously.
  • Autonomy: Strong self-starter capability with sound judgment on when to resolve issues independently versus when to escalate.
  • Technical Proficiency: Mastery of Google Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Docs, Sheets).
  • Work Environment: Dedicated, quiet remote workspace with reliable high-speed internet.
  • Availability: Full-time availability aligned with U.S. business hours.

Preferred

  • Direct experience supporting founders, C-suite executives, or senior leadership in a fast-paced environment.
  • Background in a SaaS, tech startup, fintech, banking, lending, insurance, or GRC company.
  • Previous experience managing client onboarding or support channels using CRM and help desk software.
  • Familiarity with AI productivity tools and modern work-automation applications.

Core Software & Tools

Candidates should be proficient in or comfortable quickly adopting:

  • Collaboration & Productivity: Google Workspace, Video Conferencing, Shared Inbox Tools
  • Operations & CRM: CRM platforms, Help Desk software, Calendar/Scheduling tools
  • Documentation & AI: Document management systems, AI productivity utilities, Spreadsheet software

Key Performance Indicators (Success Metrics)

Performance and success in this role are evaluated on

  • Accuracy and proactive organization of executive calendars and task pipelines.
  • Client satisfaction, measured by communication responsiveness, quality, and resolution efficiency.
  • Completeness and data integrity of CRM records and client onboarding documentation.
  • Effective meeting documentation and consistent follow-through on action items.
  • Measurable reduction in overall administrative burden on company leadership.
