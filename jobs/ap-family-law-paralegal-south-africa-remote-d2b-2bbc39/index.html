We are seeking an experienced and knowledgeable Family Law Paralegal with genuine practical experience in England and Wales family law, particularly in Legal Aid-funded matters and non-molestation order applications.

The successful candidate will provide specialist legal and administrative support to UK-based solicitors, assisting with the preparation, progression and management of family law cases, with a particular focus on domestic abuse proceedings, injunction applications and protective orders.

This is a specialist role requiring someone who understands the practical and procedural requirements of progressing a non-molestation order application from initial instructions through to court proceedings and final outcome.

The successful candidate must be capable of working independently on complex case preparation while remaining under the appropriate supervision of a UK-qualified solicitor.

Key Responsibilities

A. Family Law Casework

Assist with the preparation and management of family law matters within the jurisdiction of England and Wales.

Support solicitors with Legal Aid-funded family cases, ensuring relevant procedures and documentation are followed.

Review client instructions, identify relevant legal issues and assist with case progression.

Prepare correspondence, applications, statements and other family court documentation.

Maintain accurate case records and ensure deadlines and court directions are monitored.

B. Non-Molestation Orders and Injunction Proceedings

Prepare non-molestation order applications from initial instructions through to completion of the relevant court process.

Draft and prepare Form FL401 applications and supporting witness statements.

Assist with preparing supporting evidence, exhibits and relevant documentation.

Understand the requirements for without-notice and on-notice applications.

Prepare and organise court bundles in accordance with applicable court requirements.

Assist with the preparation of documentation for hearings, including return hearings and contested proceedings.

Understand the procedural stages involved in injunction proceedings and the requirements for progressing cases effectively.

Assist with monitoring court orders, deadlines, service requirements and subsequent court directions.

C. Legal Aid-Funded Family Matters

Demonstrate a strong understanding of Legal Aid-funded family law work in England and Wales.

Assist with the preparation of Legal Aid applications and supporting documentation.

Understand the evidence and eligibility requirements relevant to domestic abuse-related Legal Aid matters.

Support the preparation and administration of Legal Aid-funded case files.

Assist with maintaining appropriate case records and documentation in line with Legal Aid Agency requirements.

Liaise with UK solicitors regarding case funding, supporting evidence and procedural requirements.

D. Court Preparation and Advocacy Support

Prepare court applications, statements, hearing documents and indexed court bundles.

Understand the procedural requirements of family court hearings and injunction proceedings.

Prepare clear case summaries, chronologies and relevant hearing notes.

Brief UK-based solicitors and counsel effectively, ensuring they have the necessary case information and supporting documentation.

Understand the purpose and structure of advocacy submissions and the key issues requiring consideration at hearings.

Assist with remote hearing preparation, including electronic bundles, document organisation and coordination with clients and legal representatives.

E. Case Management and Remote Working

Manage assigned case preparation tasks independently and efficiently.

Work closely with UK-based supervising solicitors and legal teams.

Maintain confidentiality and comply with applicable data protection requirements.

Ensure work is accurate, properly documented and completed within agreed deadlines.

Escalate complex legal issues, urgent matters and matters requiring solicitor input appropriately.

Essential Requirements

Candidates must demonstrate the following

Genuine practical experience in England and Wales Family Law. This is an essential requirement and is non-negotiable.

Strong understanding of Legal Aid-funded family matters and the associated procedures.

Practical, hands-on experience preparing non-molestation order applications from start to finish.

Experience preparing FL401 applications, witness statements, supporting evidence, exhibits and court documentation.

Experience preparing court bundles and organising documentation for family court hearings.

Strong understanding of injunction proceedings and the procedural stages involved.

Sufficient knowledge of advocacy and court procedures to prepare and brief UK-based solicitors or counsel effectively.

Ability to work independently on specialist family law case preparation while remaining under appropriate UK solicitor supervision.

Excellent written English, legal drafting and attention to detail.

Ability to manage sensitive and confidential information, particularly in domestic abuse and safeguarding-related cases.

Desirable Requirements

The following experience would be advantageous

Previous advocacy experience in family law proceedings.

Experience supporting solicitors or counsel during contested hearings.

Experience assisting with remote and electronic court hearings.

Experience dealing with urgent and without-notice injunction applications.

Experience managing Legal Aid-funded domestic abuse cases.

Familiarity with HMCTS procedures, Family Court processes and electronic filing systems.

Experience working remotely with UK-based solicitors or law firms.

Benefits

Competitive salary based on experience and skill set

100% remote role — work from home anywhere in the Philippines

Paid local holidays aligned with the UK business calendar

Opportunities for training and professional growth

Work directly with a supportive UK team — no agency middleman

Long-term engagement with a stable and growing business
