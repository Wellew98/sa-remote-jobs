STA Personnel Solutions is a dynamic and fast-growing BPO company. We are not a recruitment agency. We partner with international US clients and integrate our staff members directly within our clients' operations. We focus on long-term placements where you contribute directly to the client's success.

About the Role

We are seeking a highly organised and proactive Executive Assistant & Project Coordinator to support the Director of Business Intelligence in a fast-moving, complex, multi-entity environment.

This is not a traditional EA role.

You will operate as a critical extension of a senior BI leader — managing executive workload, coordinating cross-functional delivery, and bringing structure to a high-volume, multi-system environment.

The role exists to free up senior technical capacity and ensure operational execution across projects, systems, and stakeholders.

PLEASE NOTE

Working Hours

Monday – Friday, 4:00 PM – 1:00 AM South African time – subject to daylight saving time.

Public Holidays

This role requires working on both South African and U.S. public holidays (compensation for SA public holidays in accordance with the BCEA).

Internet Requirements

A fixed fibre line with a minimum speed of 25 Mbps (upload & download) and wired Ethernet capability is mandatory. Applicants without a fixed fibre line cannot be considered.

Power Backup

Reliable backup required to manage load shedding or outages. Applicants without a power backup cannot be considered.

Work Environment

Fully remote.

Key Responsibilities

Executive Support

Manage a high-volume inbox, prioritising and summarising key actions.

Own a fast-moving calendar with frequent changes and competing priorities.

Proactively protect the Director's time and manage scheduling conflicts.

Coordinate correspondence, meetings and follow-ups.

Project Coordination (ClickUp)

Attend project kick-offs, take structured notes, and convert discussions into ClickUp task boards.

Track project progress across multiple teams and subsidiaries.

Follow up on deliverables, flag risks, and ensure accountability.

Manage project timelines, schedules and outstanding actions.

Meeting & Documentation Support

Capture clear, structured meeting notes and action items.

Produce summaries for team visibility.

Support creation of scope-of-work documentation before project initiation.

Maintain organised records and project documentation.

Operational & Financial Support

Support recurring operational and financial administration tasks, including report extraction across multiple entities in QuickBooks.

Assist with CRM and data-related administrative tasks, including HubSpot updates and reporting.

Support financial reporting, reconciliations and general administrative processes where required.

Continuously identify and absorb repetitive workload from the BI team.

Systems & Tools

Learn and take ownership of new tools as required.

Work across multiple platforms and troubleshoot independently where possible.

Maintain accurate information across systems and ensure tasks and reporting are completed timeously.

The Tools You'll Use

ClickUp | Microsoft Outlook | Teams | Excel | OneNote | QuickBooks | HubSpot

Requirements

Experience working in technology or similarly fast-paced technical environments is a strong advantage.

Prior exposure to project coordination or project management type work (e.g. tracking deliverables, managing workflows, cross-team coordination).

Strong Excel skills (advanced formulas required – pivot tables advantageous).

Experience with financial administration, reporting, reconciliations or accounting-related processes is advantageous.

Previous experience supporting senior management or executives, including calendar and inbox management.

Extremely organised with strong attention to detail.

Comfortable in a fast-paced, high-volume environment.

Strong communication skills and disciplined follow-through.

Proactive and resourceful — able to work independently once briefed.

Experience with ClickUp or similar tools is advantageous.

Familiarity with QuickBooks and/or CRM systems advantageous.

High level of confidentiality and professionalism.

Comfortable supporting technical teams in an operational capacity.

Culture & Working Style

Fast-moving environment with shifting priorities.

Direct, clear communication style with emphasis on asking questions before acting.

Collaborative and supportive team culture.

Regular video-based meetings and strong cross-team engagement.

If you are not contacted within 14 working days, please consider your application unsuccessful.
