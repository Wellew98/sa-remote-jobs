About Huzzle

At Huzzle, we connect exceptional talent with top opportunities at leading companies across the UK, US, Canada, Europe, and Australia. Our clients include fast-growing startups, digital agencies, SaaS companies, and tech-enabled businesses across industries such as MarTech, FinTech, EdTech, and eCommerce. Unlike traditional outsourcing agencies, we place you directly with innovative companies where you become a valued member of the team.

About the Company

Our client is a private equity investment firm focused on connecting high-net-worth individuals (HNWIs) with private equity investment opportunities. The company prioritises relationship-driven sales, consultative discovery, investor qualification, and long-term client relationships.

Job Summary

We are hiring experienced Business Development Managers and 360 Investment Brokers with a proven track record of closing high-ticket investment deals.

This is an opportunity for commercially driven investment sales professionals who excel at engaging affluent investors, conducting in-depth discovery conversations, building trust, and converting qualified prospects into long-term clients.

Non-Negotiable Requirement

Candidates must have direct experience soliciting and closing investments of £25,000–£50,000 per transaction or higher, ideally with experience securing investments of £100,000+.

Experience limited to trading platform deposits, low-ticket brokerage sales, or investment transactions of £5,000–£10,000 will not qualify for this position.

Key Responsibilities

Prospect and engage high-net-worth individuals through outbound telephone sales.

Conduct structured discovery and consultative investment conversations.

Understand prospective investors' financial circumstances, motivations, investment objectives, available capital, and risk tolerance.

Qualify prospective investors according to company eligibility requirements.

Introduce relevant private equity investment opportunities using approved materials.

Confidently lead high-value investment conversations involving £25,000–£100,000+ in capital.

Clearly communicate investment risks, limitations, and potential illiquidity.

Handle objections with professionalism, empathy, and commercial awareness.

Guide qualified investors through closing and onboarding.

Maintain accurate CRM records, pipeline forecasts, and follow-up schedules.

Adhere to applicable regulatory requirements and internal compliance procedures.

Build investor confidence without aggressive sales tactics, misleading claims, or promises of returns.

Proven experience closing high-ticket investment transactions of £25,000–£50,000 or more.

Demonstrable experience directly asking investors to commit significant amounts of capital.

Previous experience in investment brokerage, private equity sales, capital raising, wealth-related sales, or similar high-value investment environments.

Strong track record of outbound prospecting, telephone sales, and full-cycle closing.

Experience engaging high-net-worth individuals and affluent investors.

Exceptional English communication, active listening, and consultative selling skills.

Ability to uncover client motivations, financial objectives, concerns, and unmet needs through structured discovery.

Confidence discussing substantial financial commitments without relying on high-pressure closing techniques.

Strong objection-handling, negotiation, and relationship-building skills.

Proficiency with CRM systems and sales pipeline management.

Comfortable working in a high-volume, performance-driven outbound sales environment.

Highly disciplined, coachable, commercially ambitious, and accountable.

Sound judgement and the ability to recognise when an investment opportunity should not be progressed.

Preferred Qualifications

Experience closing individual investment transactions of £100,000 or more.

Previous experience selling private equity or alternative investment products.

Experience building and managing a personal book of investors.

Familiarity with UK investment markets and investor qualification procedures.
