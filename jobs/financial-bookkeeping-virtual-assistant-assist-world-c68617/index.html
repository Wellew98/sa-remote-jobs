POSITION OVERVIEW We are hiring a Financial & Bookkeeping Virtual Assistant to support a growing franchise organization that operates multiple business units in the hospitality and entertainment space. This role will focus heavily on financial operations, bookkeeping, payroll support, and financial reporting across several operating units. The ideal candidate will have strong experience with QuickBooks, payroll systems, and financial reporting , and be comfortable managing daily financial data and operational reporting. The role will work closely with the company's leadership team and Director of Finance to ensure accurate financial tracking and reporting across multiple locations.
