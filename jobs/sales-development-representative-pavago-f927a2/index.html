About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a motivated and results-driven Sales Development Representative (SDR) to generate qualified sales opportunities through outbound prospecting and appointment setting.

Our client is a U.S.-based engineering and tax consulting firm that helps commercial property owners, real estate investors, and rental property owners reduce federal tax liabilities through cost segregation studies.

This is

not a closing role .

You’ll be responsible for initiating conversations, qualifying prospects, overcoming objections, gathering key information, and scheduling meetings for senior consultants. Success in this role comes from building trust quickly, maintaining high outbound activity, and consistently generating qualified appointments.

If you’re confident on the phone, resilient in the face of rejection, and motivated by performance, this role is a strong fit.

What You’ll Own

Outbound Prospecting & Lead Generation

Conduct outbound calls to commercial property owners and real estate investors.

Introduce the company’s tax consulting services using a structured sales script.

Research prospects and personalize conversations where appropriate.

Generate interest and identify qualified opportunities.

Maintain consistent outbound activity to build a healthy sales pipeline.

Lead Qualification

Ask discovery questions to determine prospect fit.

Gather accurate qualification information.

Educate prospects on cost segregation studies at a high level.

Identify decision-makers and buying intent.

Ensure prospects meet qualification criteria before handoff.

Appointment Setting

Schedule qualified meetings for senior consultants.

Coordinate appointment times with prospects.

Confirm meeting details and follow up when necessary.

Ensure all required information is collected before handoff.

CRM & Sales Administration

Maintain accurate records in the CRM.

Log calls, notes, follow-ups, and appointment details.

Keep pipeline information organized and up to date.

Ensure data accuracy throughout the sales process.

Customer Experience

Represent the company professionally during every interaction.

Build rapport with business owners and investors.

Handle objections confidently and professionally.

Create a positive first impression that reflects the company’s reputation.

What Success Looks Like

What Happens After You Apply

After submitting your application, you’ll receive an email invitation from Spark Hire to record a short 3–5 minute Intro Video . This is the first step in our hiring process and can be completed whenever it’s convenient for you.

Instead of multiple initial screening calls, you’ll have one opportunity to introduce yourself, discuss your sales experience, and explain how you’ve handled cold calling, objection handling, lead qualification, and appointment setting. You’ll also have the chance to highlight your communication style, experience working with U.S.-based clients, and ability to build rapport with business owners and decision-makers. Your video will be reviewed by the hiring team before moving to the client interview.

You can record your video as many times as you’d like before submitting it—only your final version will be reviewed.

Please keep an eye on both your inbox and spam folder for your Spark Hire invitation after applying.
