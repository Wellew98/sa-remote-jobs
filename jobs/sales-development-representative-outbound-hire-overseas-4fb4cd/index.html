We're looking for a

Sales Development Representative to own pure outbound cold calling for a fast-moving B2B data and sales intelligence platform. You will work from pre-built prospect lists, make high-volume outbound calls, and convert cold contacts into booked meetings for the sales team.

This is a cold calling role, not a warm inbound role. There are no hand-raisers, no inbound leads to work, and no email sequences to hide behind. You will be dialing cold prospects all day, handling objections, and booking meetings through sheer volume and skill. If you are comfortable on the phone, resilient under rejection, and motivated by a clear and measurable output, this role is a strong fit.

Why You'll Want to Join

You will be paid in USD (bi-monthly: every 15th and 30th)

in accordance with company policy

Observance of

Holidays per company guidelines

100% remote setup so you can work wherever you're most productive

US business hours

Pre-built prospect lists and call software provided so you can focus entirely on calling

Clear, measurable performance metrics with a direct line between your activity and your results

What You'll Work On

High-Volume Outbound Cold Calling

Make high-volume outbound cold calls to B2B prospects from pre-built lists every day

Open cold conversations professionally and pivot quickly to qualify interest and book a meeting

Handle objections confidently and keep conversations moving toward a booked outcome

Maintain a consistent, high call volume throughout the full working day without losing energy or quality

Meeting Booking and Pipeline Management

Book qualified meetings for the sales team as the primary output metric for this role

Log all call activity, outcomes, and follow-up notes accurately in the CRM after every call

Keep prospect records clean and current so the sales team has full context going into every meeting

Process and Performance

Follow the calling script and objection handling frameworks provided while adapting naturally to each conversation

Meet daily and weekly call volume and meeting booking targets consistently

Flag patterns in objections, prospect quality, or list performance to help improve the outbound process over time

What You Bring

Prior outbound cold calling experience with a track record of booking meetings from cold conversations

Confident, natural, and resilient phone presence with the ability to stay sharp across a high volume of calls

Fluent, clear, and professional spoken English — this is a phone-only role and your voice is everything

Comfortable with rejection and able to reset quickly between calls without losing momentum

Organized with clean CRM habits and accurate call logging

Reliable high-speed internet connection and a quiet, professional calling environment

Available to work full-time during US business hours

Nice to Have

Experience cold calling for a B2B SaaS, data, or technology company

Familiarity with sales engagement or power dialer platforms

Background in a high-volume outbound sales environment with daily call targets

How to Apply

Please include

Your

updated resume

A short Loom video (1 to 2 minutes) introducing yourself, describing your cold calling experience, and giving an example of how you handle a prospect who immediately says they are not interested

Shortlisted candidates will be invited to complete a 2-day paid trial before a full offer is extended.

Only candidates who submit a Loom video will be moved to the next step of the hiring process.

If you are comfortable on the phone, thrive on high activity, and want a role where your results are directly tied to how many dials you make and how well you handle objections, this role gives you the volume and the clear metrics to do your best work.
