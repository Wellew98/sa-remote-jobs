About ALX Africa

ALX Africa, a non-profit organisation under the ALX Foundation, is dedicated to unlocking the potential of Africa's digital future. We've embarked on an independent journey to provide world-class tech, creative and entrepreneurship skills training and career acceleration programmes. Our mission is to bridge the digital divide, upskill and re-skill talent, and create a generation of innovative leaders. By 2030, we aim to empower 2 million Africans to secure sustainable careers.

With hubs in 8 cities across Africa and counting, we provide safe access to quality learning and a dedicated network of expert instructors. Our innovative programmes equip learners with the practical skills and knowledge needed to succeed in today's rapidly evolving tech, creative and entrepreneurship industry. Through a combination of rigorous coursework, industry partnerships, and hands-on projects, we prepare our learners for in-demand roles.

Providing young professionals with access to the most in-demand tech, creative and entrepreneurship skills that will power the future.

Empowering the next generation of technology innovators, entrepreneurs, and creators through challenging, real-world coursework.

Building a lifelong, impactful community of professionals and entrepreneurs that support them at all stages of their career journey.

Role Summary

ALX South Africa is looking for an Entrepreneurship Development Manager who will own the strategic growth, revenue generation, and ecosystem positioning of ALX Ventures in South Africa, including end-to-end leadership of the Founder Academy and Freelancer Academy.

Based at the Johannesburg hub, the manager will act as ALX Ventures’ primary relationship-builder in South Africa — driving revenue and strategic partnerships, building the B2B ecosystem through corporate and investor events, growing the B2C community through founder and freelancer experiences, deepening alumni engagement by uncovering and sharing their stories, and owning the Founder and Freelancer Academies end-to-end across the full programme lifecycle, from recruitment and activation through to engagement, graduation, and alumni integration.

Success in this role will be measured by

Revenue generated through partnerships, and programme sales

Growth and quality of the B2B ecosystem network, including corporate, investor, and partner relationships

Community growth and engagement across B2C founder and freelancer events

Founder and Freelancer Academy conversion, activation, retention, and graduation rates

Depth, reach, and impact of alumni engagement and story-led brand building

By combining revenue generation, strategic partnerships, ecosystem and community building, and end-to-end academy ownership, the manager will grow ALX Ventures’ brand, footprint, and impact across the South African entrepreneurship landscape. As a Functional Specialist and non-people manager, the role holder will translate this mandate into a robust operational plan and execute it with a high degree of independence — proactively managing upwards, flagging issues early, and proposing solutions — while ALX Ventures leadership sets the broader strategy.

Specific Responsibilities

Revenue Generation & Business Development

Own revenue generation for ALX Ventures South Africa, including programme fees, and partner-funded contracts

Identify and convert new revenue opportunities through corporate partnerships, sponsorships, and grant-funded programmes

Track revenue performance against targets, and report progress, risks, and opportunities to leadership

Build and manage a pipeline of paying clients, partners, and programme participants to sustain and grow revenue

Strategic Partnerships & Ecosystem Building (B2B Events)

Build and manage a network of corporate, investor, and ecosystem partners to support ALX Ventures’ strategic goals in South Africa

Design and deliver B2B ecosystem events — investor showcases, corporate partner sessions, and ecosystem convenings — that position ALX Ventures as a key player in the South African entrepreneurship landscape

Negotiate and manage partnership agreements that create tangible value for learners, alumni, and the business

Community Building (B2C Events)

Design and execute a community-building strategy and calendar of B2C events — masterclasses, meetups, and demo days — for founders and freelancers

Build a vibrant, engaged community of current learners and alumni across Johannesburg and virtually for those outside the city

Collaborate with marketing and cross-functional teams to grow community reach, engagement, and brand affinity

Founder & Freelancer Academy Management (End-to-End)

Own the Founder and Freelancer Academies end-to-end, from recruitment and conversion through activation, engagement, and graduation

Manage recruitment, activation, retention, and graduation targets, adapting strategy to the different needs of FA and FLA learners

Continuously improve programme delivery to ensure a high-quality learner experience and strong outcomes

Manage relationships with facilitators, mentors, and coaches who support the academies

Monitor learner data throughout the programme lifecycle to proactively identify and address risks to conversion, activation, or graduation

ALX Ventures Brand Building in South Africa

Grow the ALX Ventures brand and reputation across the South African entrepreneurship ecosystem

Represent ALX Ventures at speaking engagements, media opportunities, and public platforms to build brand visibility and credibility

Partner with the marketing team to develop content, campaigns, and messaging that reflect ALX Ventures’ impact, voice, and positioning

Data Monitoring & Reporting

Monitor and analyse programme and revenue metrics — recruitment, activation, retention, graduation, and revenue — to identify areas for improvement

Evaluate the success of programmes, partnerships, and events, and recommend strategies to improve impact and return on investment

Maintain accurate, up-to-date records of programme performance, partnerships, and community engagement for reporting and decision-making

Prepare regular performance updates and reports for leadership and key stakeholders

Use data and dashboards to track progress against targets, and escalate risks or blockers proactively

Alumni Engagement & Stakeholder Storytelling

Act as the primary point of contact for programme alumni, building trusted, ongoing relationships as key ecosystem stakeholders

Proactively uncover, document, and share alumni success stories to fuel marketing, brand, and fundraising efforts

Design and implement alumni engagement strategies, including events, mentorship opportunities, and peer networks

Encourage alumni participation in community events, masterclasses, and mentorship for current learners

Support alumni in accessing opportunities such as partnerships, clients, funding, and collaborations, and track engagement to grow long-term ecosystem value

Skill Requirements - Essential

6+ years of experience within the entrepreneurship ecosystem, including startup support, incubation, acceleration, or venture-building environments

Experience building and managing strategic partnerships with corporates, investors, or ecosystem stakeholders, including designing and delivering B2B events

Experience managing end-to-end programmes or cohorts, from recruitment through to graduation, ideally within an accelerator, incubator, or education environment

Experience building and growing communities, including designing and delivering in-person and virtual events

Strong stakeholder management experience, including engaging alumni, partners, and senior stakeholders to build relationships and surface opportunities

Skill Requirements - Preferable

Proven experience generating revenue through programme sales, or partnership-driven business development

Experience coaching or supporting founders and freelancers, ideally within the African entrepreneurship ecosystem

Familiarity with the South African funding landscape, including corporate ESD/B-BBEE, DFI, and grant-funded programme models

Experience using CRM systems, learner management systems (LMS), or data dashboards to track programme and revenue performance

Prior experience building or scaling a brand within a fast-paced, mission-driven organisation
