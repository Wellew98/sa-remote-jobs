We are seeking a highly skilled Technical Interior FF&E Specialist with advanced Revit expertise to support the technical delivery of luxury hospitality interior projects.

This role focuses on developing, coordinating, and delivering detailed interior documentation from Stage 2 through Stage 5, ensuring design intent is accurately translated into coordinated, buildable, and procurement-ready information.

You will work across high-end hospitality and residential projects, contributing to the production of technically robust interior packages within a BIM-led environment.

Responsibilities

Produce and coordinate detailed Revit-based interior documentation across all project stages (2–5)

Develop technical drawing packages including GA plans, layouts, RCPs, elevations, sections, and bespoke joinery details

Prepare and manage FF&E and OS&E schedules, specifications, and asset registers

Ensure documentation is suitable for tender, procurement, and construction use

Coordinate closely with architectural, structural, MEP, and interior design teams within shared BIM environments

Support technical resolution of materials, finishes, furniture systems, and bespoke interior elements

Support FF&E/OS&E procurement documentation, sample tracking, and product approvals

Ensure all solutions meet durability, performance, maintenance, and luxury hospitality standards

Maintain consistency, accuracy, and quality across all deliverables

Bachelor's or Master's Degree in interior Architecture or similar

8-15 years of experience in interior architectural design

Professional English proficiency, both written and verbal.

Proven proficiency in

Revit (Architectural)

Experience in hospitality or residential projects (villas, hotels, resorts).

Solid understanding of materials, structural elements, detailing, and documentation workflows.

Excellent communication and coordination skills.
