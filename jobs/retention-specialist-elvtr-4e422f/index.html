At ELVTR, we're transforming online education by connecting ambitious professionals with industry leaders from companies like Nike, Netflix, Google, Amazon, and more.

Since launching in 2020, we've grown into a global learning community with over 300 expert instructors, delivering more than 200 live online courses across 12 industries. To date, we've helped 22,000+ graduates worldwide gain the skills, confidence, and connections needed to advance their careers.

Now we're looking for a Retention Specialist to help our learners find the right solution when they consider leaving, while protecting revenue and keeping customer satisfaction high. If you're a natural communicator, enjoy turning difficult conversations into positive outcomes, and like hitting clear targets, we'd love to meet you.

This is a full-time, remote role with a working schedule of 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM EST.

Why This Role Is Different

At ELVTR, customer relationships don't end after enrollment—they're just beginning. As a Retention Specialist, you'll handle refund requests and overdue payments day to day, offering the right alternatives, resolving billing questions, and keeping learners on track to finish what they started. Reporting to our Retention Lead and working alongside Sales, Customer Success, and Billing, you'll have a direct, measurable impact on revenue and on how our learners feel about ELVTR.

You'll Love This Role If You

Enjoy turning challenging customer conversations into positive outcomes

Thrive in a fast-paced, KPI-driven environment with clear daily targets

Are a persuasive communicator who can find win-win solutions

Stay calm, empathetic, and professional under pressure

Are highly organized and keep accurate records of every interaction

Like learning from feedback and sharpening your skills over time

Enjoy working as part of a team and helping colleagues succeed

Believe outstanding customer experiences drive long-term business success

What You'll Do

Handle incoming refund and cancellation requests by phone, email, and chat, aiming to retain revenue while delivering a positive customer experience

Understand each learner's reasons for leaving and offer suitable alternatives, including account credits, payment plans, partial refunds, course transfers, and deferrals

Follow up on overdue installment payments, reminding customers, resolving billing questions, and agreeing on realistic repayment arrangements

Work with the Billing team to resolve failed payments and help prevent chargebacks

Log every interaction, outcome, and agreement accurately in the CRM

Follow retention scripts, workflows, and policies, and suggest improvements based on what you hear from customers

Flag recurring refund reasons and customer pain points to your Lead so they can be shared with Sales, Product, and Operations

Escalate complex or sensitive cases to the Retention Lead with clear context and a recommended solution

Meet individual targets for retention rate, retained revenue, and installment recovery

Coordinate with Sales, Customer Success, and Finance to make sure each customer's issue is fully resolved

1–2+ years of experience in Customer Retention, Sales Retention, Customer Success, Collections, or a related customer-facing role

Excellent English communication skills, both written and spoken

Experience supporting customers in the US market

Strong negotiation and objection-handling skills, with a track record of saving customers or recovering payments

Comfort handling refund requests, billing questions, and payment follow-ups

Ability to navigate difficult conversations with empathy while protecting company interests

A results-oriented mindset and comfort working toward daily and monthly targets

Strong organizational skills and attention to detail, especially in CRM record-keeping

Openness to coaching and feedback, with a drive to keep improving

Nice to Have

Experience in the online education or EdTech industry

Experience in high-ticket B2C sales or retention environments

Familiarity with CRM platforms such as HubSpot, Salesforce, or similar systems

Experience working with subscription or installment payment models

Basic knowledge of billing operations, chargebacks, and payment recovery

Familiarity with AI-powered customer support or sales tools, including conversation intelligence platforms

Interest in growing into a senior or lead retention role

Competitive Compensation: Competitive salary, performance-based bonuses, and a clear path to senior and lead roles as ELVTR continues to grow globally

Learn and Grow: Free access to ELVTR courses, ongoing training and coaching, and career growth within one of the world's fastest-growing online education companies

Remote-First Culture: Work from wherever you're most productive, in a flexible and supportive environment with a collaborative international team

Make an Impact: Every learner you help stay on track gets closer to building new skills, advancing their career, and unlocking opportunities they never thought possible.

Grow Your Career: We're committed to developing our people. As ELVTR continues to expand globally, you'll have opportunities to grow professionally, take on new challenges, and shape the future of online education.

Join a Winning Team: Work alongside passionate educators, creators, marketers, and innovators who are committed to delivering world-class learning experiences and making education more accessible around the world.
