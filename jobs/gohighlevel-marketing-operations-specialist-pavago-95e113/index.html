GoHighLevel Marketing Operations Specialist – CRM, Email & Social Media

Full-Time | Remote | U.S. Business Hours (PST Preferred)

About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a GoHighLevel Marketing Operations Specialist to manage day-to-day marketing execution, CRM workflows, email campaigns, social media, and operational coordination.

This is a hands-on execution role — not a strategy-only position. You’ll work directly inside GoHighLevel (GHL) to manage automations, pipelines, nurture sequences, and lead follow-ups while also supporting content, social media, and broader business operations.

If you’re highly organized, comfortable with marketing technology, and know how to keep CRM + marketing + operational workflows running smoothly, this role is a strong fit.

What a Typical Day Looks Like

You may start by reviewing GoHighLevel pipelines and automations to make sure leads and follow-ups are moving correctly. From there, you could build an email campaign, update CRM records, schedule social content, and respond to community messages.

Throughout the day, you may edit short-form content, support participant onboarding, conduct prospect research, organize operational tasks, and coordinate with leadership on priorities.

you keep GoHighLevel, marketing campaigns, content, CRM workflows, and operational execution organized and moving every day.
