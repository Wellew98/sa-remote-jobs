We're looking for a

GTM Engineer to build the automated systems that power outbound and pipeline generation for a fast-moving GTM content studio working with B2B companies. You will design and build outbound automation, data enrichment pipelines, and workflow integrations connecting tools like Clay, N8N, CRMs, and AI models to make go-to-market execution faster, smarter, and more scalable.

This is a builder's role. You are not running campaigns manually, you are building the systems that run them. If you can architect a workflow that pulls data, enriches it, personalizes outreach using AI, and pushes it into a CRM without anyone touching it by hand, this role is a strong fit.

Why You'll Want to Join

Build real GTM infrastructure for venture-backed B2B companies rather than running manual outbound

High-ownership, high-autonomy role with direct impact on pipeline efficiency and scale

What You'll Work On

Outbound Automation and Workflow Building

Design and build automated outbound workflows using tools such as N8N to reduce manual effort across the GTM process

Connect data sources, enrichment tools, CRMs, and outreach platforms into seamless end to end systems

Build and maintain reusable automation templates that can scale across multiple campaigns or accounts

Data Enrichment and Personalization

Build data enrichment pipelines using tools such as Clay to gather and structure prospect and account data at scale

Use AI tools such as Claude to generate personalized outreach content and research summaries within automated workflows

Continuously test and refine enrichment and personalization logic based on response and conversion data

CRM and Systems Integration

Integrate outbound and enrichment workflows directly with CRM platforms to ensure clean, accurate, and timely data flow

Troubleshoot and maintain automations, fixing breakages and improving reliability over time

Document workflows clearly so systems can be understood, maintained, and extended by others

Continuous Improvement

Identify new tools, models, or techniques that could improve speed, personalization, or scale of GTM systems

Monitor automation performance and proactively flag issues, bottlenecks, or opportunities for optimization

What You Bring

Demonstrated experience building outbound automation or workflow systems using tools such as N8N, Zapier, Make, or similar platforms

Experience with data enrichment tools such as Clay or similar platforms

Strong working knowledge of AI tools such as Claude or ChatGPT and how to use them inside automated workflows rather than just as a chat interface

Experience integrating systems with CRM platforms and ensuring clean data flow across tools

Comfortable working independently to design, build, and troubleshoot systems with minimal oversight

Strong problem-solving skills with the ability to debug broken automations and improve them iteratively

Clear written communication skills for documenting workflows and systems

Nice to Have

Experience building GTM systems for B2B SaaS, agency, or venture-backed startup environments

Familiarity with sales engagement or outbound platforms such as Apollo, Instantly, or Smartlead

Basic scripting ability to extend automation capabilities beyond no-code tools

Experience working directly with sales or marketing teams to translate GTM needs into technical systems

How to Apply

Please include

Your

updated resume

A short Loom video (1 to 2 minutes) introducing yourself, walking through an outbound automation or workflow you built, and describing the tools you used, the problem it solved, and the outcome

Only candidates who submit a Loom video will be moved to the next step of the hiring process.

If you think in systems, love connecting tools together to eliminate manual work, and want to build the infrastructure behind modern outbound, this role gives you the ownership and the technical scope to do your best work.
