Job DescriptionAccount Manager

DepartmentSales

Reports toHead of Business Operations and/or CEO

Role Overview

The Account Manager is responsible for the overall success, growth, and retention of assigned client accounts. This role acts as a trusted client advisor, ensuring strong client relationships, account expansion, sustainable revenue growth, and partners closely with the recruitment team to ensure consistent, high-quality recruitment delivery aligned with company goals and operational expectations.

Key Responsibilities

Inbound Sales

  • Convert inbound leads into closed won and allocate to Recruitment & Client Success Manager based on capacity and specialty
  • Grow pipeline using the existing sales framework to maintain a high level of capacity for your respective teams
  • Follow up on old and current leads
  • Review projects on hold and cancelled deals regularly to keep prospects and clients active

Client Advisory & Relationship Management

  • Serve as the primary point of contact and strategic advisor for assigned clients
  • Build and maintain long-term, trust-based client relationships
  • Understand the client's business objectives and hiring needs to provide proactive guidance
  • Manage client expectations, risks, and escalations to ensure high satisfaction and retention
  • Collect and act on client feedback to continuously improve service delivery

Revenue Ownership

  • Own revenue targets across managed accounts and ensure consistent performance
  • Work with the Recruitment & Client Success Manager to analyze lost deals, discounts, and performance trends to improve outcomes
  • Protect profitability by adhering to pricing strategies and approval frameworks

Business Development (Cross-sell & Upsell)

  • Identify opportunities to expand account value through cross-sell and upsell of services
  • Introduce additional recruitment solutions or roles aligned with client needs
  • Collaborate with internal teams to pitch and close new opportunities
  • Track incremental revenue from account growth initiatives

Team Coordination & Performance

  • Ensure recruitment teams are aligned with client expectations, priorities, and timelines
  • Foster accountability, collaboration, and engagement across recruitment teams

Success Measures

  • High client satisfaction and retention (CSAT, repeat business)
  • Consistent achievement of revenue and profitability goals
  • Measurable growth through cross-sell and upsell activities

Core Competencies

  • Strong client advisory and stakeholder management skills
  • Commercial and revenue-driven mindset
  • Ability to lead, influence, and hold teams accountable
  • Analytical, proactive, and solution-oriented approach
  • Excellent communication and relationship-building skills
