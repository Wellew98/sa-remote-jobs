Role Description

This is a full-time remote role as a Financial Analyst at a United States based Real Estate / Private Equity firm. The Financial Analyst will underwrite new acquisitions, do research on necessary variables, run forward-looking projections and past reporting.

You will work directly with the executive team at a growing US based company.

Qualifications

  • 5+ years experience in the Finance field
  • A University Degree in the finance field
  • Experience in financial reporting (profit and loss, balance sheet, forcasts, etc)
  • Experience in the real estate or private equity industry
  • Strong communication skills
  • Excellent organizational skills
  • Proven track record of handling confidential information
  • Ability to work independently and remotely
  • Proficiency in project management tools

Requirements

  • Typing speed 50+ words per minute
  • Ability to work on US time zone
  • A clean and professional remote work environment
  • Fast internet, a second monitor and a fast computer

Salary

R20000-R40000 per month, paid in USD depending on experience.

Details

  • Independent contractor agreement paid 2x monthly.
  • Client has a desire for long term commitment.
