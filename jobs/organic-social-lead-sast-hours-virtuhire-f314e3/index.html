Organic Social Lead - SAST hours

A client based in South Africa looking for an Organic Social Lead to own and scale their organic social media strategy across multiple platforms. This person will be responsible for driving brand awareness, audience engagement, and ultimately revenue through high-performing organic content. You'll combine creativity with commercial thinking, using data, AI tools, creators, and repeatable content systems to build a best-in-class organic social presence. Responsibilities Develop and lead the company's organic social media strategy aligned with business objectives. Manage organic social channels across Meta, TikTok, YouTube, and Pinterest . Translate commercial goals into engaging social content strategies and campaigns. Build and manage a network of content creators, freelancers, and AI tools to produce high-quality content efficiently. Create scalable processes for identifying, testing, and scaling successful content formats. Monitor content performance and use analytics to continuously optimise strategy. Ensure content drives brand awareness, audience engagement, and commercial outcomes. Oversee or contribute to video and image editing to maintain high content quality. Manage multiple projects, priorities, and content calendars effectively.
