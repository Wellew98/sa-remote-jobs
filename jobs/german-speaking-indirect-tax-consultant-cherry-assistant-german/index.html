We are hiring a German-Speaking Indirect Tax Consultant to support a global client portfolio with VAT compliance, advisory services, and international tax obligations. This is a fully remote role focused on VAT consultancy, client relationship management, compliance support, and cross-border tax guidance.

The ideal candidate is highly organized, analytical, and client-focused, with experience in VAT, tax, accounting, finance, or legal environments. This role requires strong communication skills, attention to detail, and the ability to manage multiple client engagements while delivering exceptional service and technical expertise.

Key Responsibilities

VAT Advisory & Compliance Support

  • Provide high-quality VAT consultancy and business advice to a portfolio of international clients.
  • Calculate, prepare, and file VAT returns accurately and within required deadlines.
  • Review client cases, identify compliance risks, and recommend practical solutions.
  • Advise clients on VAT liabilities, reporting requirements, and payment obligations.
  • Support clients with VAT registrations, filings, and ongoing compliance requirements.
  • Ensure all client work is completed in accordance with applicable VAT legislation.
  • Stay up to date with changes in VAT regulations and international tax requirements.
  • Identify recurring compliance issues and escalate findings where appropriate.

Client Relationship Management

  • Build and maintain strong relationships with clients through exceptional customer service.
  • Communicate complex VAT matters clearly and professionally via email, phone, and virtual meetings.
  • Provide proactive guidance on tax deadlines, outstanding returns, and compliance obligations.
  • Act as a trusted advisor to clients by offering practical and commercially focused solutions.
  • Manage client expectations while ensuring timely and accurate delivery of services.
  • Support clients throughout audits, investigations, and compliance reviews.

Tax Authority & Internal Team Collaboration

  • Liaise with tax authorities regarding client accounts, obligations, and compliance matters.
  • Support VAT audits and investigations in collaboration with tax offices and regulatory bodies.
  • Work closely with operations, compliance, tax, and client service teams to resolve issues efficiently.
  • Maintain accurate records of client communications, advice provided, and compliance activities.
  • Contribute ideas and recommendations to improve internal procedures and client service delivery.
  • Collaborate with managers and directors on process improvements and operational efficiencies.

Required Qualifications

  • 5+ years of experience in VAT, tax, accounting, finance, or legal environments.
  • Strong understanding of VAT principles and compliance requirements.
  • Fluency in German.
  • Excellent verbal and written English communication skills.
  • Experience managing client relationships and providing professional advice.
  • Strong organizational and time-management abilities.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively within a team environment.
  • Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple deadlines and priorities in a fast-paced environment.
  • High level of accuracy and attention to detail.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with international VAT compliance and advisory services.
  • Knowledge of international e-commerce taxation and cross-border transactions.
  • Experience communicating with tax authorities and regulatory bodies.
  • Additional language skills, particularly German or Spanish.
  • Experience supporting VAT audits, investigations, or tax reviews.

Required Skills & Tools

  • Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, Outlook).
  • Tax compliance and VAT reporting systems.
  • Client relationship management tools.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.
  • Research, analytical, and problem-solving abilities.
  • Document management and record-keeping systems.
  • Ability to interpret and apply tax legislation.

Schedule & Pay

  • Full-Time position; Monday - Friday UK time.
  • Fully remote role for a UK-based company.
  • Pay ranges from R15,000 - R34,000 per month depending on skills and experience .
  • Annual raises and confirmed holidays.

System Requirements

  • Internet speed of at least 20 Mbps.
  • Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher.
  • 8 GB of RAM or higher.
  • Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer.
  • HD 720p webcam.
  • Headset with a microphone.

Benefits

  • Competitive pay rates
  • Remote work flexibility - from home or any location of your choice
  • Elimination of commute time
  • Consistent work with the same clients, fostering long-term relationships
  • Stable work hours and consistent pay
  • Supportive and inclusive environment that values diversity and growth

If you are a client-focused tax professional with experience in VAT compliance and advisory services, apply to join the team!
