About Monaco Solicitors

Monaco Solicitors is an award-winning UK employment law firm specialising exclusively in employee representation. We’re proud of our innovative legal tech, our fully remote working culture, and our mission to help individuals resolve workplace disputes without going to court.

The Role

We’re looking for passionate South Africa-based lawyers to join our team and support our specialist employment lawyers. You’ll be working on a variety of employment law matters, especially cases that fall under our Access to Justice Scheme, which helps clients who might not qualify for traditional No Win No Fee representation. This is your opportunity to gain UK legal experience, use the latest in legal technology and AI, and contribute to meaningful change – all while working remotely.

Key Responsibilities

Handling new client enquiries and assisting with eligibility assessments

Helping to draft legal documents such as ‘without prejudice’ letters, schedules of loss, and employment tribunal claim forms, using AI and legal tech

Communicating with clients and/or respondents under supervision

Using AI and internal legal tech to manage workflows

Efficiently participating in regular remote team meetings and legal training

Undertaking some legal admin work

Supporting UK solicitors with legal research, correspondence and negotiations

What We’re Looking For

Previous experience as a lawyer, dealing with contentious work

Legal drafting & legal research experience

Negotiating skills

Passionate about access to justice and employee rights

Experience in project work and an understanding of project processes

Self-employed status and ability to work full-time remotely, with excellent self organisation

Demonstrable tech-savviness, including use of AI tools, to improve efficiency

Excellent written English, spoken English and professional communication skills

Positive, diligent and reliable team player

A reliable, proactive and detail-oriented work ethic

Case handling strategy & case work experience are preferred, but not necessary

Pay and Structure

Full-time self-employed position

Pay will be based on local market rates, depending on experience

100% remote working, with remote onboarding and training provided

Supportive and inclusive team culture, with regular virtual collaboration

Strong performers may have future growth opportunities within the team
