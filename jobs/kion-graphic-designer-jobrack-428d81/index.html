Design digital ads for Kion’s marketing team! Apply now.

$1,500–$1,800/month | Part-time (20–25 hours/week) | Fully Remote (3–5 hours of CST/MST overlap per week)

Hey there! I’m Andrew, and I want to tell you a little bit about

Kion

.

We’re all about helping people unlock their best selves through high-quality supplements designed to fuel performance, recovery, and overall well-being.

Our flagship product, Kion Aminos, is loved by athletes, fitness enthusiasts, and everyday people who want to stay energized, sculpt a toned body, and age powerfully.

At Kion, we believe in creating a positive, collaborative, and growth-driven environment where every team member can contribute and make an impact.

If you enjoy working with creative people and want to be part of something meaningful, you’ll feel right at home here.

We’re looking for a Graphic Designer who can combine creativity with precision and thrive in a collaborative environment.

You’ll play a key role in supporting our marketing and eCommerce teams by creating engaging digital ads and high-performing email designs that stay true to Kion’s brand aesthetic.

More specifically, you will

📱 Work directly with Kion’s Creative Director and the marketing and eCommerce teams to design digital ads across social platforms 📧 Translate creative briefs into high-performing email designs using clear visual hierarchy, mobile-first design, accessibility best practices, and conversion-focused layouts 🎨 Follow established brand standards while finding creative ways to bring visuals to life 🖼️ Translate complex scientific information into compelling, easy-to-understand visuals 🤝 Collaborate with the marketing and eCommerce teams to ensure campaigns align with brand goals

You’re organized, detail-oriented, and thorough. You take feedback gracefully and use it to improve and refine your work thoughtfully.

The ideal candidate thrives in a creative environment, enjoys collaborating with others, and is motivated to deliver high-quality designs every time.

Working Hours

This is a part-time role (20–25 hours per week) requiring 3–5 hours of overlap with CST or MST each week.

To be considered for the position, you need

✅ 4+ years of professional design experience (agency or studio experience)

✅ Proficiency in Figma, Adobe Illustrator, and Photoshop ✅ Experience designing marketing emails in Klaviyo or another email service provider (ESP) ✅ Strong understanding of mobile-first email design, visual hierarchy, accessibility, and conversion-focused layouts ✅ Ability to transform complex scientific information into engaging visual communication

Brownie points

⭐ Experience with Shopify page-building tools; Shogun experience is a plus ⭐ Animation ⭐ Web/digital design ⭐ Basic video editing

What’s in it for you?

💰 Salary: $1,500–$1,800/month

🕒 Part-time: Fully remote role (20–25 hours/week)

🌎 Flexible working with 3–5 hours of CST/MST overlap per week

*Please note that this is a gross salary and that you are responsible for any tax-related procedures in your country of residence. We kindly ask you to apply only if you are happy to work remotely as an independent contractor.
