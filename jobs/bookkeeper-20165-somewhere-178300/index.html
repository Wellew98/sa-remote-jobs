Role Summary

We are seeking a highly meticulous, process-driven  Bookkeeper based in South Africa to take full operational ownership of the day-to-day general ledger maintenance across our multiple US-based entities. This position is built for a senior numbers operator who goes beyond mechanical data entry to actively audit transactions, run complex reconciliations, and drive our month-end close workflows to a clean, on-time completion.

Reporting directly to the Finance Operations Lead, you will bridge day-to-day transactional accounting with financial statement preparation. The ideal candidate is deeply autonomous, possesses a rigorous understanding of US bookkeeping workflows (including accrual accounting adjustments), and maintains flawless communication protocols inside a fast-moving, remote environment.

Key Responsibilities

- General Ledger & Chart of Accounts: Maintain clean, audit-ready bookkeeping records across multiple distinct entities inside QuickBooks Online or Xero, ensuring precise transactional categorization.

- Month-End Close Acceleration: Own core components of the month-end close pipeline—including preparing journal entries, calculating monthly accruals, adjustments, and handling inter-company transactions.

- Advanced Reconciliations: Perform comprehensive monthly reconciliations across high-volume business bank accounts, corporate credit cards, and complex merchant gateway processors (e.g., Stripe, PayPal).

- Payroll & Benefits Allocation: Reconcile US payroll runs (including salary, hourly tracking, and commissions) to the general ledger, ensuring proper cross-departmental cost allocations.

- Liaison & Reporting: Package preliminary balance sheets and P&L statements for management review, ensuring complete data synchronization across auxiliary operations platforms.

Required Skills & Qualifications

- Experience:  3 - 5+ years of progressive, full-charge bookkeeping or accounting experience, ideally supporting US or UK-based corporate clients remotely.

- Educational Foundation: Degree or formal diploma in Accounting, Finance, or a related field. Professional registration with a recognized body (SAIPA or equivalent) is highly preferred.

- Software Mastery: Elite proficiency in QuickBooks Online or Xero, alongside advanced Excel capabilities (pivot tables, complex multi-sheet linking, formulas).

- US Market Fluency: Solid operational understanding of US corporate workflows, merchant clearing cycles, and accrual vs. cash mechanics.

- Infrastructure Autonomy: Must have a dedicated, quiet home office setup with a high-speed fiber internet connection and a verified inverter/UPS backup system to ensure continuous connectivity during regional grid outages (loadshedding).

REQUIREMENTS

We are looking for candidates based in Latin America, South Africa, or the Philippines. Please note that we process only one active application at a time—if you’ve applied for multiple roles, kindly direct any updates to the team handling your first application. To start processing, submit your resume in English. The final offer will be at the client’s discretion and will depend on your interview performance, skills, and experience.
