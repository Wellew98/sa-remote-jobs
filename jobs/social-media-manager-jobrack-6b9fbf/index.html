Turn creators into a steady stream of content for a mission-driven oral care brand. Remote, organized, people focused.

$3,500-4,000/month | Full-time | US overlap

Click here to watch the video from Peter of Dr. Jen Oral Care.

Dr. Jen Oral Care is a family founded oral care brand created by dentist Dr. Jen after her daughter needed gentler products during recovery from a serious illness. We develop distinct toothpaste formulations and sell online and through dental practices. Our story matters to our customers because they understand what it means to need a product that works differently.

We've built an established customer base and several formulations, and real people talking about our products is one of the best ways to show why they matter. We have access to a creator program with hundreds of thousands of potential influencers, and an ads team ready to turn good creator videos into ads. What we're missing is someone in house to run it all day to day. That's where you come in.

We're looking for a Social Media Manager to run our influencer and creator program and look after our social media content on TikTok, Meta and YouTube. You'll find and contact creators, get product samples into their hands, make sure their videos come back, and send each one to our ads team or post it on our own channels. You'll also manage our affiliate program and build lasting partnerships with the creators who do well for us. You'll work closely with our Media Buyer and ads team, so the content you bring in turns into ads that perform.

🔍 Find and vet creators through our creator program, which gives us access to hundreds of thousands of potential influencers

✉️ Contact potential influencers and invite them to receive product samples

📦 Keep track of every creator, from first message to sample sent to video received, and follow up when videos are late

🎬 Make sure videos are submitted, then route them to the ads team or post them on our channels

📱 Manage our social media content across TikTok, Meta and YouTube

🤝 Run our affiliate program and turn the best creators into long-term partners

📞 Learn directly about customers and the dental market during onboarding, so the creators and content you choose reflect real customer needs

This is a full-time, remote role. During the first 2-4 weeks of training, you'll work the US team's regular hours and spend time learning from customer interactions. After that, some independent work can be done before the US workday, with meaningful daily overlap.

To be successful in this role, you need

✅ Experience running influencer or creator programs, from first outreach to finished video

✅ Partnerships or affiliate program experience

✅ Hands-on experience managing social media content on TikTok, Meta and YouTube

✅ Clear, friendly and persuasive written English: outreach messages that get replies

✅ Strong organization: you can keep hundreds of creator conversations, samples and deadlines on track

✅ An eye for which creator videos will work as ads and which belong on our own channels

✅ Care with product claims: you keep creators within what we can say about our products

Brownie points for

⭐ Experience with creator or influencer platforms, such as sampling programs or creator marketplaces

⭐ Experience working alongside a media buyer or ads team

⭐ Experience with Meta and TikTok creatives

⭐ Consumer health or ecommerce experience

The ideal candidate is organized, persuasive and curious about customers. You enjoy building relationships with creators, you follow up without being asked, and you know a good video when you see one. Rather than chasing follower counts, you look for creators whose audience would care about what less tooth sensitivity could mean, like enjoying hot coffee or ice cream with their child. You take initiative, you want to learn the product inside out, and you're keen to get involved with the wider business while staying careful about product claims.

What's in it for you?

💰 $3,500-4,000 USD per month*

💼 Full-time

🌍 Fully remote

⏰ Meaningful daily overlap with US team hours required; initial 2-4 weeks on US regular hours for training

*Salary is negotiable depending on experience and fit

*Please note that this is a gross salary and that you are responsible for any tax-related procedures in your country of residence. We kindly ask you to apply only if you are happy to work remotely as an independent contractor.
