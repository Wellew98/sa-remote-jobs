Overview

We are hiring an Operations & Scheduling Coordinator for an owner-operated company in the residential home cleaning industry. This is a part-time remote role to start (with conversion to full-time after a 30-day probation period) for a Oregon, US-based client. The role is focused on morning dispatch, schedule management, lead intake/sales, and cleaner coordination to keep daily operations running smoothly.

The ideal candidate is calm under pressure, highly organized, proactive, and an excellent communicator who thrives in fast-paced, service-based environments.

Key Responsibilities

  • Own morning dispatch: handle cleaner call-outs/no-shows, secure same-day coverage, and rapidly reschedule jobs to minimize service disruptions
  • Manage daily schedules in the booking platform (create, adjust, and optimize routes; send confirmations and reminders)
  • Handle inbound leads via phone and Google Local Services Ads (LSA) messages; qualify, quote, and book jobs
  • Coordinate with cleaners/contractors: confirm assignments, track progress, handle time changes, and communicate updates
  • Provide responsive client support: address inquiries, resolve issues, and deliver status updates with empathy and professionalism
  • Maintain CRM pipelines and follow-ups in GoHighLevel; support review requests and light retention initiatives
  • Assist with recruiting and onboarding cleaners as needed (e.g., posting/screening on Indeed, coordinating documentation)
  • Support property management work orders and move-out scheduling, including detailed notes and requirements
  • Perform light admin tasks: data entry, reporting, SOP upkeep (e.g., Loom documentation), and post-service follow-ups

Required Qualifications

  • 1+ year in a scheduling/dispatch, operations, or customer support role within residential cleaning or similar field service
  • Strong written and spoken English; confident phone demeanor and chat-based sales capability
  • Proven experience managing dynamic schedules and solving same-day coverage challenges
  • Familiarity with booking/CRM tools and VoIP systems; able to learn new tools quickly
  • Availability to work consistent morning hours aligned with Pacific Time (PT)

Preferred Qualifications

  • Prior experience supporting a US-based residential cleaning business
  • Hands-on work with BookingKoala, GoHighLevel (GHL), and OpenPhone
  • Experience sourcing and coordinating contractors via Indeed or similar platforms

Required Skills & Tools

  • BookingKoala (or similar home services booking platform)
  • GoHighLevel (CRM and automations)
  • OpenPhone (or similar VoIP and texting platform)

Schedule & Pay

  • Part-Time position to start; 20 hours/week in the Pacific Time Zone (Monday–Friday, 8:00am – 12:00pm PT) during a 30-day probation period, transitioning to Full-Time (40 hours/week, Monday–Friday, 8:00am – 4:00pm PT) upon successful completion
  • Fully remote role for a Oregon, US-based client
  • Pay ranges from ZAR 8,600 to ZAR 10,000 per month , depending on experience and skill
  • Annual raises, bonuses, and confirmed holiday PTO

System Requirements

  • Internet speed of at least 20 Mbps upload and download
  • Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher
  • 8 GB of RAM or higher
  • Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer
  • HD 720p webcam
  • Headset with microphone

Benefits

  • Competitive pay rates
  • Consistent hours and predictable workload
  • Fully remote work with long-term potential
  • Direct collaboration with the founder/operations team, making a real impact
  • Supportive and inclusive work environment
  • Opportunity to grow alongside a scaling residential home cleaning company

If you are a calm, decisive problem solver with strong organizational skills and an eagerness to keep operations running smoothly in a fast-paced, service-based environment, we encourage you to apply and grow your career with our team!
