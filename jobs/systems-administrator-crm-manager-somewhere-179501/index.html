Systems Administrator and CRM Manager

Budget2,000 - 3,500 USD/month

CountrySouth Africa and Philippines

A US-based mortgage lender is looking for a full-time, senior Systems Administrator and CRM Manager to own its CRM and point-of-sale platform, working closely with its leadership team, brokers, and account executives.

About the role

You will be the go-to person for the company’s core systems: keeping the CRM and loan point-of-sale platform running smoothly, resolving support tickets, and building the automations that keep brokers and clients engaged. You will be fully trained on the company’s platform, so your strength lies in learning systems quickly, solving problems, and bringing ideas to the table. In your first 90 days, success looks like taking ownership of the support ticket queue, delivering the first round of CRM automation projects, and keeping users updated without needing to be asked.

Required Qualifications

  • 4+ years of experience as a systems administrator, CRM administrator, or CRM implementation specialist
  • Hands-on experience building and fixing CRM workflows, triggers, and automations, including email and text campaigns
  • Strong troubleshooting skills and the ability to learn new software platforms quickly
  • Fluent written and spoken English, comfortable communicating with brokers and account executives
  • Customer service mindset, with a proactive approach to updates and follow-ups
  • Reliable availability from 8:30am to 5:30pm Pacific Time

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in mortgage, lending, or financial services
  • Experience administering a loan origination or point-of-sale system
  • Experience with CRM platforms such as Salesforce, HubSpot, or similar
  • Comfortable using AI tools to work faster and improve processes
  • Basic reporting or data skills, for example building dashboards or user reports

Responsibilities

  • Serve as the primary administrator for the company’s CRM and point-of-sale platform
  • Manage, prioritize, and resolve support tickets submitted by internal users
  • Build and maintain CRM automations, including email and text campaigns and workflow triggers
  • Implement new CRM projects and features in partnership with leadership
  • Manage user accounts, permissions, and system settings
  • Troubleshoot issues and coordinate with software vendors when needed
  • Keep brokers and account executives informed with clear, proactive updates
  • Document processes and create simple guides for internal users
  • Recommend improvements to systems and workflows based on user feedback

Working arrangement

Full-time and remote, working 8:30am to 5:30pm Pacific Time. Open to candidates in South Africa and the Philippines, with South Africa preferred.

Who thrives in this role

  • Proactive and solution-oriented, brings ideas rather than waiting for instructions
  • Patient and service-minded when helping non-technical users
  • Detail-oriented and organized, keeps the ticket queue under control
  • Enjoys automating processes and making systems work better
  • Takes ownership and follows through to the finish
