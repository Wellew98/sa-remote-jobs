Sales Setter / Sales Executive

Remote Recruitment South Africa (remote) Full-time Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

Job Overview We are seeking a motivated and resilient Sales Setter / Sales Executive to join a fast-paced, performance-driven sales team. The successful candidate will be responsible for re-engaging dormant leads, speaking with business owners and entrepreneurs, and inviting qualified prospects to attend paid business events. This is a high-volume outbound calling role suited to someone who is confident on the phone, commercially minded, target-driven, and comfortable working in a highly accountable sales environment. You will receive regular coaching and AI-generated feedback on your calls, allowing you to review your performance, improve your approach, and develop your sales skills continuously. Key Responsibilities Contact approximately 3,000 dormant leads per month through outbound calling. Re-engage previous prospects and generate renewed interest. Speak with entrepreneurs and business owners about relevant paid events. Qualify prospects and identify suitable sales opportunities. Invite and convert prospects to attend paid events. Work towards daily call, conversion, and sales targets. Follow structured sales scripts and processes. Handle objections confidently and professionally. Build rapport quickly with prospective customers. Review AI-generated feedback following sales calls. Apply coaching and performance feedback to improve results. Maintain accurate records of calls, conversations, and outcomes. Provide regular updates on activity, conversions, and performance. Candidate Requirements Previous experience in sales, telesales, appointment setting, business development, or a commercial environment is advantageous but not essential. A strong understanding of business and the reasons customers make purchasing decisions. Excellent verbal communication and telephone skills. Confident speaking with business owners and decision-makers. Comfortable making a high volume of outbound calls. Strong work ethic and a positive, proactive attitude. Resilient and able to remain motivated when handling rejection. Target-driven and comfortable working towards measurable KPIs. Coachable and willing to receive regular feedback. Data-driven and interested in reviewing personal performance. Able to work independently in a remote environment. Reliable, disciplined, and highly accountable. Performance Expectations The role will include clear daily and monthly targets, which may include: Approximately 150 outbound calls per day. Approximately 3,000 dormant leads contacted per month. Daily ticket sales and conversion targets. Consistent improvements in call quality and conversion performance. Active participation in coaching and performance reviews.
