Remote · Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm (Pacific, Central, or Eastern) · $1,250/month for the first 90 days, then $1,500/month

A campaign doesn't stop needing you once it goes live. That's actually when the job starts!

Anyone can build a campaign. The real work is what happens after: watching what's landing, catching what's not, and using that to shape the next round of copy, creative, and sends, while it's still running, not three weeks later in a debrief.

The platforms and mix of work shift depending on which business you're supporting. What doesn't shift: you own the campaign after launch, not just before it.

What the day-to-day looks like

Plan, launch, and adjust paid campaigns on Meta, Google, and LinkedIn , shifting budgets and targeting as results roll in, not waiting for a weekly check-in to react

Work with writers and designers to get ad copy and creative ready, then check it against what's actually converting once it's live

Build and send email/SMS campaigns in Klaviyo or Mailchimp, testing subject lines and timing to see what actually moves opens and clicks

Use ChatGPT or similar tools to speed up ad variations. It's a shortcut, not a replacement for your judgment on what's worth running

Pull performance numbers on a regular schedule and turn them into a short, clear list of what to change next, not a 30-slide report nobody reads

Stay in sync with design, content, and any PMs on the account so nothing launches late and nobody gets blindsided

What we're actually looking for

2–5 years running digital marketing campaigns end to end, where you owned the results, good or bad

Real, hands-on time in Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads, and LinkedIn Campaign Manager

Comfortable enough in Photoshop and Premiere to handle basic creative edits and asset prep yourself

Actual experience running email or SMS campaigns in Klaviyo or Mailchimp, not just watching someone else do it

Written English that's genuinely fluent. You'll be writing copy real customers see, so "pretty good" isn't quite enough

Nice to have, not required: SEO or Google Analytics/SEMrush, basic HTML/CSS for email templates, or some video editing chops

A few things that matter more than they sound like they should

Internet that won't cut out mid-launch or during a client call

You show up when you say you will. Every time, not most of the time

If something's underperforming or looks off, you say so early instead of hoping it fixes itself

You follow through on what you said you'd do, without anyone needing to remind you

How the process actually works

Six steps, whether or not there's a client waiting on the other end:

We read your CV — actually read it, not scan for keywords. Clearly AI-written with nothing specific in it? That tends to show.

A short intro video. Two minutes of you talking tells us more than a page of text — we're looking at how you come across on camera, your English, and your setup.

A recruiter call, about 30 minutes. Bring a real example you can walk us through, not a general answer.

A couple of short take-home exercises. Nothing that eats your whole evening.

A reference check with someone who actually managed or worked closely with you.

If a client's ready: one interview with them. They make the final call, but you won't repeat steps you've already done here.

Sometimes you'll finish this process and there won't be a client lined up yet. That's not a runaround, we'd rather get to know good people properly now, so we're ready the moment a client shows up, instead of starting from zero. If that's your situation, we'll tell you plainly where things stand, including when the honest answer is "nothing yet." Take another job while you wait? Completely fine, just let us know, and we'll pick things back up when the timing works.

If you make it to an interview, you'll get real, specific feedback either way. If your CV doesn't move forward, you'll still hear back, just via a quicker, more automated note. With the volume a posting like this gets, that's the only honest way to get back to everyone.
