Head of Content (Execution-Focused | Social + Growth)

LocationRemote

TypeFull-Time

ScheduleUS-facing (EST overlap required)

About the Company

We are a fast-growing technology company building at the intersection of AI, automation, and digital workflows.

Our team moves quickly, values ownership, and prioritizes people who take initiative and execute without needing constant direction. We are now looking to bring on a Head of Content to own and scale our content and social presence.

The Role

This is a hands-on, execution-first content role — not a high-level strategist sitting above the work.

You will be responsible for publishing, managing, and growing our content presence across social platforms , while helping shape the voice and consistency of the brand.

This role is ideal for someone who

  • Loves being in the weeds creating and posting content
  • Is highly reliable and consistent with output
  • Takes ownership and follows through without constant oversight
  • Is excited to be part of a fast-moving team

Key Responsibilities

Content Execution & Publishing

  • Own day-to-day content posting across platforms (X, LinkedIn, Instagram, etc.)
  • Schedule, publish, and manage content calendars
  • Ensure consistent output and brand voice across all channels
  • Repurpose existing content into multiple formats (short-form, threads, posts)

Social Media Management

  • Manage and grow social accounts with a focus on engagement and consistency
  • Monitor comments, messages, and interactions
  • Stay on top of trends and adapt content accordingly

Content Operations

  • Organize and manage content pipelines and assets
  • Coordinate with internal team members for content ideas and inputs
  • Ensure deadlines are met and nothing falls through the cracks

Creative & Growth Contribution

  • Suggest content ideas, formats, and improvements
  • Identify what’s working and double down on high-performing content
  • Bring energy, creativity, and initiative to the role

What We’re Looking For

Core Traits (Non-Negotiable)

  • Highly reliable and consistent (this is critical)
  • Strong sense of ownership and accountability
  • Proactive communicator
  • Detail-oriented with strong follow-through
  • Team player with a positive, “figure it out” mindset

Experience

  • 2–5+ years in content creation, social media management, or similar roles
  • Experience managing and posting content across multiple platforms
  • Familiarity with content scheduling tools and workflows

Skills

  • Strong written English (must be clear, natural, and engaging)
  • Basic understanding of what performs well on social media
  • Ability to repurpose and adapt content across formats
  • Organized and comfortable working in fast-paced environments

Nice to Have

  • Experience working with startups or tech companies
  • Basic design or video editing skills
  • Exposure to AI tools for content creation

What Success Looks Like

  • Consistent, high-quality content being published daily/weekly
  • Strong improvement in engagement and social presence over time
  • Reliable execution without missed deadlines
  • Becoming a trusted operator within the team

Why This Role

  • High ownership and autonomy
  • Fast-moving, growth-oriented environment
  • Opportunity to directly impact brand and growth
  • Work closely with leadership and a highly technical team
