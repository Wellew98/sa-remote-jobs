CMC Specialist (Quality) – Job Description

Job Title

CMC & Quality Operations Specialist

Location / Employment Details

  • Type: Full-time
  • Location: Remote
  • Hours: Overlap with U.S. business hours (time zone to be confirmed)
  • Reports to: Director of Quality
  • Works closely with: Director of Science and Technology, Quality and Regulatory teams, and external CDMO partners

About the Company

We are a rapidly growing U.S.-based biopharmaceutical company advancing our product candidates through clinical development. We partner with contract development and manufacturing organizations (CDMOs) to produce our drug product, and we run a lean, highly collaborative team where Quality, Regulatory, and Science and Technology work side by side. Every batch, stability result, and regulatory document we produce moves our programs closer to patients.

About the Role

Our CMC work runs across several internal leaders and multiple external manufacturing partners, and keeping it all on schedule is a full-time job. We need one person who owns the tracking, documentation, and follow-through so that our directors can focus on technical and regulatory decisions.

As our CMC & Quality Operations Specialist, you will be the connective tissue of the CMC program. You will run the team's meeting cadence, keep CDMO deliverables on track, manage stability data from pull to shelf-life assessment, and make sure our regulatory submissions have the CMC content they need, on time and inspection-ready.

Key Responsibilities

CMC Program Coordination

  • Partner with the Director of Quality, the Director of Science and Technology, and the Regulatory team to track CMC projects, timelines, and deliverables
  • Run CMC team meetings: set agendas, capture minutes, track action items, and drive follow-through to closure
  • Maintain a single, current view of CMC status, risks, and upcoming milestones for leadership

CDMO and Vendor Management

  • Serve as a day-to-day point of contact with CDMO partners to track deliverables and keep milestones on schedule
  • Follow up on open items, escalate delays early, and coordinate document exchange between our team and vendors
  • Support batch record review and help resolve deviations or discrepancies with CDMOs

Stability Program

  • Track stability study schedules, pulls, and testing across sites
  • Review and log stability results, flagging trends or out-of-specification data
  • Support shelf-life assessments and expiry dating with the Quality and Science and Technology teams

Regulatory and Quality Documentation

  • Support CMC sections of regulatory submissions, including drafting, compiling, and reviewing documents
  • Maintain CMC documentation to GMP and GDP standards so it is accurate, controlled, and inspection-ready
  • Help coordinate clinical supply documentation, including cold-chain and distribution records

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Chemistry, Pharmaceutical Sciences, or a related scientific field
  • 3+ years of experience in CMC or GMP Quality Assurance within pharma or biotech
  • Hands-on experience with batch record review and stability data review
  • Working knowledge of GMP and GDP requirements
  • Strong project coordination skills, with a track record of running meetings and driving action items to closure across multiple stakeholders
  • Clear, professional written and verbal communication, including with external vendors

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience contributing to CMC sections of regulatory submissions (e.g., IND or Module 3)
  • Direct experience working with CDMOs on deliverables and timelines
  • Knowledge of cold-chain logistics and clinical supply requirements
  • Experience supporting shelf-life or expiry assessments
  • Background in a small or clinical-stage biotech environment

Ideal Candidate Profile

You are

  • Highly organized, and the person teams rely on to know where every deliverable stands
  • Proactive about chasing open items, with vendors and internal leaders alike
  • Detail-oriented with data and documents, and you catch the discrepancy before it becomes a deviation
  • Comfortable owning CMC tracking end to end with minimal supervision
  • Sound in your independent judgment, knowing when to resolve an issue yourself and when to escalate
  • Energized by a fast-paced, lean environment where priorities can shift

Why Join Us

  • Real ownership: you will run the CMC operating rhythm, not just support it
  • Direct exposure: you will work daily with our Directors of Quality and Science and Technology and our Regulatory team
  • Visible impact: your work feeds directly into the regulatory submissions and clinical supply that move our programs forward
  • Room to grow: broad exposure across Quality, Regulatory, and Technical Operations in a growing company
