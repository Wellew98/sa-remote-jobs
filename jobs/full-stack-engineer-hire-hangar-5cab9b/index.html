LocationRemote

Time ZoneUS Time Zone (PST - EST)

Role Overview

We are seeking an AI-Assisted Full Stack Engineer to support the development of SaaS products in different industries. This role is ideal for an engineer who combines strong product thinking with modern AI-assisted development workflows using tools such as Claude.

Key Responsibilities

  • Build and maintain full stack applications using Node.js and JavaScript
  • Use AI-assisted coding tools such as Claude to generate and improve code
  • Collaborate with Product and Engineering teams to deliver SaaS features
  • Translate logistics and trucking workflows into technical solutions
  • Maintain clean, scalable, and well-documented code
  • Work effectively in a remote, distributed environment

Required Qualifications

  • Experience building SaaS products in a Full Stack Engineering role
  • Strong proficiency in Node.js and JavaScript across front and back end
  • Logistics or trucking software industry background is mandatory
  • Experience using AI coding tools such as Claude for code generation
  • Strong product mindset and understanding of customer workflows
  • Ability to work with 4–5 hours overlap with US PST
  • Prior remote work experience required

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with modern front-end frameworks and APIs
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills

Tools & Technology

  • Languages: Node.js, JavaScript
  • AI Tools: Claude or similar
  • Collaboration: Slack, Zoom, Google Workspace

Please Note It is crucial that you complete the application form in full. As part of the application process, you will be required to complete a short technical assessment. If your application is successful, you will receive an email confirming next steps. Applications that are not completed in full will not be considered for any open roles.
