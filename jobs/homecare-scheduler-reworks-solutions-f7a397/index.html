About The Role

We are looking for a highly organised, proactive, and detail-oriented Caregiver Scheduling Coordinator to join a growing US-based home healthcare provider.

The successful candidate will be responsible for coordinating daily caregiver schedules to ensure clients receive consistent and reliable in-home care. This includes scheduling and confirming visits, matching caregivers to clients based on their care requirements and availability, and arranging replacement coverage when caregivers call out or cancel.

This is a fast-paced, coordination-heavy role that requires someone who can think quickly, manage multiple moving schedules, and respond effectively to unexpected changes. The candidate must be comfortable handling same-day call-outs, communicating schedule adjustments promptly, and escalating unresolved staffing gaps before they impact client care.

Previous experience in home care, healthcare scheduling, caregiver staffing, or workforce coordination would be highly advantageous.

Key Responsibilities

Caregiver Scheduling & Coordination

Coordinate daily caregiver schedules to ensure all client visits are appropriately staffed.

Schedule, confirm, and monitor caregiver visits.

Match caregivers with clients based on care needs, availability, eligibility, and suitability.

Manage same-day caregiver call-outs, cancellations, and scheduling changes.

Arrange replacement caregivers to ensure continuity of care.

Monitor uncovered shifts and proactively work to secure replacement coverage.

Escalate unresolved scheduling gaps before a scheduled visit is missed.

Ensure caregivers are assigned appropriate hours based on client requirements and approved service authorizations.

Client & Caregiver Communication

Notify clients and caregivers of scheduling changes, cancellations, and replacement arrangements.

Maintain clear and timely communication with caregivers regarding their assigned shifts.

Handle scheduling-related enquiries professionally and efficiently.

Communicate urgent staffing issues to the relevant team members.

Ensure clients are kept informed of any changes that may affect their scheduled care.

Administrative & CRM Management

Maintain accurate and up-to-date schedules within the company's CRM system.

Update client and caregiver records when scheduling changes occur.

Track scheduled, confirmed, cancelled, and uncovered visits.

Monitor approved service hours and assist with ensuring scheduled care aligns with authorised hours.

Maintain accurate records of scheduling adjustments and communication.

Ensure all relevant information is captured correctly within the system.

Onboarding & Recruitment Support

Assist with onboarding new clients and caregivers.

Help coordinate caregiver availability for newly onboarded clients.

Support caregiver recruitment and staffing activities when required.

Assist with identifying suitable caregivers for current and upcoming client requirements.

Minimum Requirements

Previous experience coordinating shifts, appointments, or staffing in a fast-paced environment.

Excellent verbal and written English communication skills.

Strong organisational and time-management abilities.

Exceptional attention to detail and accuracy.

Ability to manage multiple schedules and competing priorities.

Confident handling urgent changes and same-day scheduling challenges.

Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to work independently.

Comfortable working with CRM systems and digital scheduling platforms.

Ability to communicate changes promptly and escalate issues appropriately.

Preferred Experience

Previous experience in home care, healthcare scheduling, or caregiver staffing.

Experience coordinating nurses, caregivers, or other healthcare professionals.

Familiarity with service authorizations and tracking approved care hours.

Experience managing shift coverage, cancellations, and replacement staffing.

Previous exposure to healthcare CRM or scheduling systems.

Ideal Candidate Profile

The ideal candidate will be

Highly organised, detail-oriented, and methodical.

A strong communicator who is comfortable dealing with clients and caregivers.

Quick-thinking and able to manage unexpected scheduling changes.

Proactive in identifying potential scheduling gaps.

Calm and solution-oriented under pressure.

Accurate when managing multiple urgent requests simultaneously.

Comfortable taking ownership and working independently.

Reliable and committed to ensuring clients receive consistent care.

Key Performance Expectations

Ensure scheduled client visits are appropriately covered.

Respond promptly to same-day caregiver call-outs.

Minimise missed visits and unresolved staffing gaps.

Ensure scheduling changes are communicated timeously.

Maintain accurate CRM records and scheduling information.

Monitor approved service hours to avoid scheduling outside authorised limits.

Escalate unresolved issues before they affect client care
