About the Company

A fast-growing, remote-first accounting firm supporting small businesses across the U.S. The company operates with efficient systems, structured workflows, and a collaborative team environment focused on delivering reliable support to clients.

With continued growth, the business is expanding its internal support team to improve operational efficiency and maintain strong organizational processes.

Why This Role Exists

As the company scales, additional operational and administrative support is needed to help maintain smooth day-to-day workflows.

This role exists to support back-office operations, maintain organized records, and assist the team with administrative coordination while following established SOPs and internal systems.

Role Overview

We are looking for a proactive and detail-oriented Administrative Assistant to support day-to-day operations across the business.

This is an entry-level role with strong growth potential, ideal for someone who is organized, dependable, and eager to learn. You will work within structured processes and receive training from the internal team while helping ensure smooth administrative and operational execution.

Key Responsibilities

1. Administrative & Operational Support (Core Focus)

  • Perform billing checks and basic financial reviews
  • Generate, organize, and maintain simple reports
  • Save, organize, and manage client documentation
  • Maintain accurate records and filing systems
  • Assist with day-to-day administrative and operational tasks

2. Process & Workflow Coordination

  • Follow SOPs and internal workflows consistently
  • Ensure documents and records remain organized and accessible
  • Support smooth operational execution across the team

3. Team Support & Communication

  • Assist team members with ad hoc administrative tasks and projects
  • Communicate clearly regarding task updates or missing information
  • Support overall coordination and operational efficiency

Requirements

Must-Have

  • 1+ year of administrative or similar experience preferred
  • Strong attention to detail and organizational skills
  • Ability to follow instructions and SOPs consistently
  • Good written and verbal English communication skills
  • Comfortable using tools such as Google Workspace and spreadsheets
  • Reliable, proactive, and eager to learn

Nice to Have

  • Experience working in an accounting or bookkeeping environment
  • Familiarity with QuickBooks or financial documentation
  • Experience supporting remote teams
