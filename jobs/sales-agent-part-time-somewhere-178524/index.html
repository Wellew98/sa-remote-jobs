About the Role

We are seeking driven, persuasive Part-Time Remote Sales Agents to join a fast-paced automotive warranty sales team. In this role, you will handle warm transferred leads and sell extended vehicle protection plans to car owners across the US.

We operate on a collaborative sales model: Lead Openers generate and qualify initial prospect interest, then transfer live leads directly to you—the Sales Agent —to present options, handle objections, and close the deal.

Key Responsibilities

- Close Sales: Receive warm live transfers from lead-gen team members and execute high-converting pitches (targeting an 80% one-call close rate).

- Consultative Selling: Educate vehicle owners on extended auto coverage options, walk them through plan benefits, and resolve hesitations confidently.

- System Navigation: Efficiently toggle between dual CRM systems, internal messaging tools (Slack), and phone platforms during active calls.

- Pipeline & Compliance: Accurately record customer info, process initial plan down payments, and adhere to compliance standards.

- Quick Onboarding: Complete an intensive 3-to-4-day training program covering call scripts, objection handling, and platform management.

Requirements & Qualifications

- Sales Experience: Proven track record in phone sales, call center environments, inside sales, or high-volume one-call close roles.

- Exceptional Communication: Fluent, clear verbal and written English to connect easily with American customers over the phone.

- Tech Savvy: Comfortable running dual CRMs and chat apps concurrently without losing momentum on sales calls.

- Self-Motivated: Strong work ethic and discipline to maintain high activity levels in a remote environment.

-

Home Office Setup

- A quiet, distraction-free workspace.

- High-speed, stable internet connection.

- Noise-canceling USB headset.

Ideal Candidate Profile

- Resilient Closer: Thrives under fast-paced pitch conditions and stays focused when navigating customer objections.

- Rapid Learner: Able to memorize structured scripts, pricing terms, and platform workflows within a short training window.

- Goal-Oriented: Highly driven by performance and closing targets.
