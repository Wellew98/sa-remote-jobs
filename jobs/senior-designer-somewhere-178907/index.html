About the Company

Our client is a specialized, full-service marketing agency dedicated to supporting complex organizations—both public and private—at pivotal stages of growth.

A core pillar of the agency’s business is corporate and investor communications for publicly listed companies. In this space, design is not merely decorative; it serves as a critical strategic tool to translate complex data, financial messaging, and business objectives into clear, highly credible visual narratives for sophisticated investors and stakeholders.

About the Role

We are seeking a Senior Designer who brings the strategic clarity, visual rigor, and judgment of a Creative Lead.

This is not an execution-only design role where layouts are pre-packaged or "spoon-fed." You will be expected to take loose, high-level briefs or raw business objectives, determine the underlying communication goals, and independently architect the visual strategy, hierarchy, and final execution.

As a cornerstone member of the agency's creative team, you will elevate the overall quality of creative output, act as a trusted creative authority, and mentor junior design staff to help scale the agency’s capabilities.

Key Responsibilities

Strategic Design & Creative Direction

  • Interpret High-Level Briefs: Translate vague or incomplete briefs into strategically aligned, visually sophisticated design solutions without requiring micro-managed direction.
  • Establish Visual Hierarchy: Structure complex, information-heavy content into clean, digestible layouts that guide the reader’s focus effortlessly.
  • Strategic Communication: Ensure all design decisions directly reinforce the client's corporate messaging, audience needs, and overarching business objectives.
  • Creative Quality Control: Proactively challenge weak design concepts and introduce stronger visual solutions across all agency collateral.

Corporate & Investor Communications

  • Investor-Focused Design: Produce high-end corporate communications (e.g., pitch decks, annual reports, investor presentations, corporate one-pagers) tailored to shareholders and financial analysts.
  • Clarity & Credibility: Balance visual innovation with the restraint, professionalism, and structure expected by publicly traded companies.
  • Data Visualization: Transform dense numerical or operational information into clean, intuitive graphics and charts.

Branding & Identity Systems

  • Brand Development: Create comprehensive visual identity systems, logos, typography stacks, and color palettes for emerging and established brands.
  • Guideline Expansion: Apply and expand existing brand guidelines seamlessly across new mediums and asset types.

Web & Digital Design

  • UI/UX Fundamentals: Conceptualize and design responsive web layouts, digital assets, and interactive experiences in Figma.
  • Digital Brand Alignment: Ensure digital applications maintain absolute visual parity with print and corporate identity standards.

Print & Editorial Layout

  • Editorial Mastery: Design print collateral, corporate whitepapers, brochures, and multi-page reports with meticulous attention to grid systems and typography.
  • Pre-Press Precision: Prepare flawless, production-ready files for commercial print environments.

Leadership & Mentorship

  • Junior Designer Mentorship: Provide ongoing art direction, design guidance, and constructive feedback to junior design team members (including an existing full-time junior designer).
  • Process Elevation: Assist in refining internal design workflows, asset management, and quality assurance standards.

Required Qualifications

  • 5+ Years of Experience: Proven track record in graphic design, visual hierarchy, and brand execution within a fast-paced agency or corporate environment.
  • Core Software Mastery: High proficiency with Figma and the Adobe Creative Cloud suite (specifically Illustrator and Photoshop).
  • Multi-Disciplinary Design Portfolio: Demonstrated expertise spanning identity/logo design, heavy print/editorial layouts, and modern web/digital UI design.
  • Independent Problem Solving: Ability to self-direct from brief to execution, solving structural and visual challenges independently.
  • English Fluency: Exceptional verbal and written English skills (near-native or native level) for seamless daily collaboration.
  • Remote Work Experience: Self-motivated and disciplined enough to thrive in a fully remote environment during Eastern Standard Time (EST) business hours.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Financial & Investor Comms Background: Prior experience designing for publicly listed companies, investor relations (IR), corporate communications, or capital markets (e.g., mining, energy, tech, or finance sectors).
  • Mentorship Experience: Previous experience managing, art directing, or formalizing feedback for junior or intermediate designers.
  • Client-Facing Aptitude: Though client interaction is not a primary duty, the ability to articulate design rationale clearly to non-design stakeholders is highly valued.

What Success Looks Like

  • Within 30 Days: You seamlessly take ownership of incoming loose briefs, eliminating the need for senior leadership to pre-architect layouts or dictate design directions.
  • Within 90 Days: You elevate the visual consistency and standard of all investor materials while actively guiding and mentoring the junior designer.
  • Within 6 Months: You become the agency’s go-to creative lead for complex corporate brand packages and web projects, serving as a pillar for long-term agency growth.
