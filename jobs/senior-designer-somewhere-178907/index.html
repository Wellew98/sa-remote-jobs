Senior Designer

About the Company

Our client is a full-service marketing agency built to support complex organizations at pivotal stages of growth. The agency works with both public and private companies, delivering integrated marketing solutions across corporate and digital platforms.

A significant portion of the work involves corporate communications for publicly listed companies, where design must communicate information clearly and effectively to investors and other sophisticated stakeholders. Projects span branding, logos, print collateral, websites, digital assets, and broader corporate communications.

About the Role

We are seeking an experienced Senior Designer to serve as a cornerstone member of the agency's creative team.

This is not an execution-only design role . The ideal candidate must be able to receive a loose or high-level brief, understand the underlying communication objective, and independently determine the appropriate visual strategy, hierarchy, and layout.

The Senior Designer will help elevate the overall quality and strategic thinking behind the agency's creative work while also mentoring a junior designer and providing oversight across creative output.

The right candidate combines excellent hands-on design ability with the judgment and strategic thinking typically associated with a creative lead.

Key Responsibilities

Strategic Design & Creative Direction

- Translate loose, high-level briefs into strategically relevant and polished visual solutions.

- Understand the communication objective behind each project before determining the visual approach.

- Develop design concepts without requiring highly detailed or prescriptive creative direction.

- Establish clear visual hierarchy to ensure complex information is easily understood.

- Think critically about how design can strengthen corporate messaging and investor communications.

- Recommend layouts, concepts, and visual approaches based on the intended audience and business objective.

- Maintain a balance between creativity, clarity, professionalism, and strategic communication.

- Challenge weak creative approaches and proactively recommend stronger solutions.

- Help establish and maintain a consistently high creative standard across the agency.

Corporate & Investor Communications

- Create sophisticated corporate communications for public and private companies.

- Design materials intended for investor and stakeholder audiences.

- Translate complex corporate information into clear and visually compelling layouts.

- Understand that investor-focused design often prioritizes clarity, credibility, hierarchy, and communication over purely conceptual creativity.

- Develop polished materials that reflect the standards expected of established and publicly listed organizations.

- Maintain consistency across corporate branding and communications.

Branding & Identity

- Develop logos, brand identities, and supporting visual systems.

- Translate company positioning and messaging into effective visual identities.

- Establish typography, color, layout, and other brand standards.

- Expand existing brand guidelines into new applications.

- Maintain brand consistency across print and digital touchpoints.

- Provide thoughtful recommendations when existing brand systems need refinement.

Digital & Web Design

- Create website layouts and digital experiences that balance brand, usability, and business objectives.

- Develop page concepts and layouts in Figma.

- Apply strong UX/UI fundamentals and visual hierarchy to web projects.

- Design responsive digital assets and supporting graphics.

- Collaborate with other team members to ensure creative concepts translate effectively into final digital experiences.

Print & Marketing Collateral

- Design professional print and corporate marketing materials.

- Create presentations, reports, brochures, one-pagers, and other collateral.

- Develop layouts capable of communicating information-heavy content without becoming visually overwhelming.

- Prepare polished production-ready creative files.

- Maintain consistency across print and digital applications.

Creative Leadership & Mentorship

- Mentor and support a junior designer.

- Review creative work and provide clear, constructive feedback.

- Help junior team members strengthen their design judgment, hierarchy, layout, and execution.

- Provide creative guidance without unnecessarily limiting individual creativity.

- Oversee creative output to ensure work consistently meets agency standards.

- Help establish efficient design processes, standards, and best practices.

- Serve as a reliable senior creative resource for the broader team.

Required Qualifications

- 5+ years of professional graphic design experience.

- Demonstrated senior-level experience developing creative concepts and independently solving design problems.

- Strong portfolio demonstrating high-quality work across multiple design disciplines.

- Advanced proficiency with Adobe Creative Cloud , particularly Illustrator and Photoshop.

- Strong proficiency with Figma .

- Demonstrated experience with logo and brand identity design.

- Strong print and editorial layout capabilities.

- Strong web and digital design experience.

- Excellent understanding of typography, composition, spacing, visual hierarchy, and information design.

- Ability to receive a loose brief and independently determine the appropriate creative direction.

- Strong attention to detail and commitment to polished execution.

- Ability to manage multiple projects and priorities simultaneously.

- Comfortable reviewing and providing feedback on another designer's work.

- Fluent professional English communication skills.

- Ability to work independently in a fully remote environment.

Preferred Qualifications

- Previous experience within a marketing, branding, or creative agency.

- Experience designing for publicly listed companies or corporate organizations.

- Background in investor relations, financial communications, corporate communications, or similar work.

- Experience designing information-heavy materials for sophisticated audiences.

- Previous mentorship or creative leadership experience.

- Experience working directly with clients and presenting creative rationale.

- Ability to provide both high-level creative direction and hands-on design execution.

- Experience establishing or improving creative processes within a growing team.

What Success Looks Like

Success in this role means becoming the creative team member who can be handed a business objective or loose brief and figure out what the design actually needs to be , rather than waiting for someone else to dictate the layout.

The Senior Designer should elevate the quality of the agency's creative output, introduce stronger strategic thinking into corporate communications, and help develop the junior designer into a more capable and independent creative professional.

Over time, this individual should become a trusted creative authority who can oversee quality while remaining hands-on enough to personally deliver exceptional branding, print, web, and corporate design work.

Ideal Candidate Profile

The ideal candidate sits somewhere between a highly experienced Senior Designer and a hands-on Creative Director .

They are not someone who needs to be "spoon-fed" a layout before beginning a project. They can take incomplete information, ask the right questions, understand the audience and objective, and determine how the information should be visually communicated.

They understand that designing for investors and corporate audiences requires more than making something attractive. It requires clear hierarchy, strategic thinking, credibility, restraint, and the ability to make complicated information easy to understand .

They are also comfortable serving as a creative leader: mentoring a junior designer, reviewing work, raising standards, and taking ownership of the overall quality of creative output.
