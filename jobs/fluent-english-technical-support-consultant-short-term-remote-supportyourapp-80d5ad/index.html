SupportYourApp is a global Intelligent Support-as-a-Service leader, partnering with tech companies and industry leaders like MasterCard, Calm and MacPaw in 30+ countries since 2010 to deliver secure customer and technical support. We operate globally, supporting clients in 60 languages across diverse industries. So what if you had a chance to be a part of the world’s leading SaaS, software, or hardware solutions?

Join our community as a

Technical Support Consultant on a short-term basis, with the potential to continue into a long-term collaboration. Unlock your potential by mastering new skills and achieving challenging goals in cooperation with our People First management approach.

Excited? Let’s see what it takes 💛

What you will do

Provide technical support via email and chat in a professional and efficient manner;

Troubleshoot product issues and guide customers through solutions step-by-step;

Identify whether an issue is related to the bulb, app, connectivity, setup, or user configuration;

Ask the right questions to understand and diagnose the problem;

Follow troubleshooting procedures while also using good judgment when the situation isn't covered by a standard process;

Document customer interactions and troubleshooting steps clearly;

Escalate complex or unresolved technical issues when necessary;

Provide a positive, patient, and professional customer experience;

Manage multiple conversations efficiently while maintaining accuracy and quality.

What you need to succeed in this role

Excellent English communication skills (at least C1 for both spoken and written);

At least 1 year of experience in technical customer support role;

Previous video support experience would be plus;

Tech-savvy mindset;

Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills, with the ability to investigate issues and find effective solutions;

Critical thinking and strong common sense, with a practical and analytical approach to resolving customer issues;

Personal computer (at least 8Gb of RAM) with a stable internet connection (minimum 50 Mbps–download and 40 Mbps–upload).
