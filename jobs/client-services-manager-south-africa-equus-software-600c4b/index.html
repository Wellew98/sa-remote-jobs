About Global Expansion

Global Expansion is an Employer of Record. We let companies hire and manage people internationally without setting up their own legal entities. We are a premium, high-touch, relationship-driven service business, not a software platform, and that is the whole basis of the difference between us and most of our competitors.

Client & Employee Services is the front line of that promise. Everyone in this family is accountable for two things at once: the transaction being right, and the relationship being intact. A job in this family that delivers only the transaction has missed the point.

About the Role

As a Client Services Manager at GX, you'll be the face of our white-glove service for EOR clients. You'll be their main point of contact and will work with our Employee Onboarding, Ongoing Service, Offboarding and Experience teams to deliver a seamless experience from first presentation to long-term partnership. Your work drives client satisfaction, retention and growth.

What You’ll Do

1.  Build and own client relationships:

Lead the onboarding of new clients through presentations and our established processes, through to full completion.

Act as the primary contact for your clients, building long-lasting relationships and understanding their business objectives so you can recommend tailored EOR solutions.

Run regular check-ins and Quarterly Business Reviews to discuss performance, gather feedback and agree actions.

2.  Coordinate service delivery:

Oversee the Employee Onboarding, Ongoing Service and Offboarding teams so clients get consistent, high-quality service.

Implement and monitor SLAs to keep response and resolution times on target.

Work with the Onboarding team to bring new employees on smoothly while meeting local regulations and client requirements.

Oversee offboarding with professionalism and legal compliance, and take on management responsibility when a client requests a termination.

3.  Communicate and report:

Bridge communication between clients and internal teams, keeping everything clear and transparent.

Prepare and present service performance reports covering employee satisfaction, onboarding and offboarding timelines, and issue resolution rates.

Keep clients up to date on new services, compliance updates and changes that could affect their business.

4.  Resolve problems:

Identify potential issues early and work with the right teams to resolve them quickly.

Resolve client concerns within SLA, and use interaction data and feedback to identify trends and recommend process improvements.

5.  Grow your accounts:

Identify opportunities to expand services within your accounts and partner with Sales to convert them.

Develop client expansion strategies and track metrics such as additional services adopted and employee growth.

6.  Advise strategically:

Work with clients to understand their future hiring plans and advise on scaling through EOR.

Align internal service delivery with client business goals.

Stay on top of industry trends and regulatory changes, and offer proactive advice.

What You’ll Bring

Essential

5+ years of experience in a client-facing role, with a strong background in managing client relationships.

A confident, credible presence and strong communication skills. You instill confidence in clients and colleagues alike.

A business mindset, with the ability to understand client goals and connect them to commercial outcomes.

A collaborative approach to working across teams.

Willingness to travel. Travel is part of the role, ranging from once a year to more than once a quarter depending on client needs, so please apply only if you are comfortable with this.

Desirable

EOR, global mobility, international payroll, PEO or professional services background.

Exposure to multi-country employment, in-country partner or vendor models.

Experience preparing and presenting business reviews to client stakeholders.

Experience using Monday.com.

Why Join Us

You'll join a team that takes pride in delivering high-touch service and doing right by clients and their people. You'll own your client relationships and have real influence over how we serve and grow them.
