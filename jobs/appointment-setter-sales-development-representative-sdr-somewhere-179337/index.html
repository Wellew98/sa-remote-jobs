Appointment Setter / Sales Development Representative (SDR)

LocationRemote, South Africa

Employment TypeFull-Time, Independent Contractor, 40 hours/week

ScheduleMonday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM U.S. Eastern Time

Compensation$1,500 to $1,800 USD/month plus performance bonuses

Reports ToOperations Manager / Growth Manager

About the Company

Our client is a high-growth digital marketing agency that works exclusively with private practice physical therapy clinics across North America. For four years they have helped clinic owners win their local markets through SEO, paid acquisition, web optimization and clear ROI reporting.

Growth so far has come from referrals and founder-led sales. With a dedicated closer now in place, they are building their first outbound sales engine, and this role is its first seat.

Role Overview

The Appointment Setter / SDR owns top-of-funnel prospecting and fills the Growth Manager’s calendar with qualified discovery calls.

You will reach out to clinic owners, directors and practice managers by phone, email and LinkedIn, getting past the front desk and starting real business conversations about how to fill empty schedules.

Key Responsibilities

Outbound Prospecting & Appointment Setting (about 65%)

  • Make daily cold calls and send personalized email and LinkedIn outreach to private practice owners.
  • Work professionally with front-desk staff to reach decision makers.
  • Explain how specialized marketing and patient acquisition systems fill clinic schedules.
  • Qualify prospects and book confirmed discovery calls for the Growth Manager.
  • Follow up quickly on warm leads from conferences, campaigns and inbound enquiries.

List Building & Lead Research (about 20%)

  • Research clinics that fit the ideal customer profile, using Google Maps, industry directories and internal tools.
  • Verify decision-maker contact details and log clean records in the CRM.
  • Use AI tools to research clinics and personalize outreach.

Pipeline Hygiene (about 15%)

  • Confirm booked calls on the day by SMS and email to reduce no-shows.
  • Keep accurate CRM notes on every interaction, outcome and follow-up.
  • Support sales admin, meeting prep and CRM clean-ups as needed.

Hard Skills & Qualifications

  • Clear, confident spoken and written English that U.S. business owners understand easily on the phone.
  • Comfortable making high volumes of cold calls and starting conversations with business owners.
  • Organized, with accurate record keeping in a CRM.
  • Comfortable learning new tools and using AI thoughtfully.
  • Full availability 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM U.S. Eastern Time, Monday to Friday.
  • A proven ability to work independently in a remote setting.

Strong plus

  • Experience selling digital marketing services (SEO, PPC, web design or reputation management).
  • Experience prospecting into medical, dental, allied health or physical therapy practices.
  • Previous appointment-setting or SDR experience. Strong communicators who are new to sales are welcome.

Preferred Tools

  • GoHighLevel (CRM)
  • Slack, ClickUp and Zoom
  • Google Workspace
  • AI research and writing tools

Remote Setup

  • A quiet, dedicated workspace for calling.
  • A modern computer (Core i5 or Ryzen 5, 16GB RAM preferred).
  • Stable fiber internet, with power and connectivity backup (UPS or inverter).
  • A professional USB noise-cancelling headset.

Soft Skills

  • Picks up the phone without hesitation.
  • Resilient and positive after rejection.
  • Curious, listens well and asks good questions.
  • Coachable, and applies feedback quickly.
  • Competitive about hitting weekly targets.
  • Reliable, and follows through on every commitment.

About the Ideal Candidate

You enjoy the phone. A gatekeeper doesn’t put you off, and a “no” doesn’t ruin your day. You’re curious about how a clinic runs, you can hold your own with a business owner, and you get a buzz every time you put a qualified call on the closer’s calendar.

Why This Role Matters

This is the first seat in the client’s outbound sales engine. Every booked call you generate feeds the closer’s pipeline, and your results will shape how fast the agency grows beyond referrals.
