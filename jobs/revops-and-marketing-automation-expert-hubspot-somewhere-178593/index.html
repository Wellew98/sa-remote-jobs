PositionRevOps and  Marketing Automation Expert (HubSpot Expert)

LocationRemote

Working hoursAligned with EU business hours - Central European Time (CET / UTC +2 )

Contract typeFull time, permanent position

As we look to increase our investments into growth, we are looking for a Marketing Automation Expert that can build the backbone of data for our GTM organization . This person will ensure that our demand gen systems are working flawlessly to provide next-level customer experiences across every touchpoint. Not only will this person be in charge of our marketing automation and CRM systems , they will also design data and automation flows across the organization . This role requires excellent communication skills and will work closely with Product, Engineering, Sales and Leadership.

Core Technical Skills

- Deep HubSpot expertise . Not only using it as a marketer, but owning data model and system design

- Basic SQL or data querying. Able to pull their own reports without waiting on a data team

- Webhook/API literacy. Doesn't need to code, but understands how systems talk to each other

- UTM/attribution modeling. Knows how to track where demand actually comes from

- Reporting expert. Able to visualize impact and help leadership make informed decisions

-

Data Driven

Systems & Analytical Thinking

- Ability to map and document complex workflows before building them

- Comfortable with data hygiene and lead routing logic

- Understands funnel stages deeply — MQL, SQL, pipeline, etc.

- Can debug a broken automation without panicking

Demand Gen Knowledge

- Paid media fundamentals (enough to brief an agency or manage a budget)

- Email nurture strategy — segmentation, sequencing, personalization logic

- Webinar/content syndication ops experience is a bonus

You are

- ​​​ Genuinely hands-on in HubSpot recently (built workflows, segments, properties yourself, not just directed a team).

-

Fluent in HubSpot specificsSegments vs Lists, workflow vocab etc

- Knowlegable on Commercial modeling: multi-deal per company, MRR aggregation, renewal pipelines, associations.

- Comfortable wiring HubSpot into billing / product systems (Chargebee, AWS Marketplace, etc).

- Able to evolve a mature instance without breaking things.

Nice to Have

- Experience marketing developer tools or PLG products

- Familiarity with product usage data (e.g. Amplitude, Database) feeding into marketing automation

- Some exposure to community-led or open source GTM motions
