We are hiring a Medspa Sales Specialist for a boutique private practice in the aesthetics, functional medicine, and sexual health industry. This is a Full-Time remote role for a Virginia, USA-based client. The role is focused on converting inbound leads and inquiries into booked appointments through a high-touch, concierge-style sales experience. The ideal candidate is proactive, persuasive without being pushy, highly responsive, commercially minded, tech-savvy, discreet, empathetic, confident discussing intimate health topics, and motivated by measurable conversion results.

Key Responsibilities

  • Serve as the primary contact for new leads via calls, texts, emails, and approved channels
  • Respond quickly to inquiries about services, pricing, availability, and next steps
  • Build rapport, understand patient needs, and recommend the appropriate next step
  • Educate leads on services and guide them toward consultations or appointments
  • Follow up consistently with leads who have not yet booked
  • Convert qualified leads into booked consultations while delivering a premium experience
  • Manage leads and updates within the Symplast (EMR/CRM) pipeline
  • Track lead interactions, follow-ups, objections, outcomes, and bookings
  • Re-engage older or inactive leads to generate new opportunities
  • Identify booking objections and share insights with the founder/clinical team
  • Maintain fast response times and ensure no qualified lead is missed
  • Coordinate with the Patient Concierge & Appointment Coordinator on successfully booked leads
  • Escalate clinical questions to the appropriate clinical team member
  • Maintain strict HIPAA compliance, confidentiality, NDA, and security protocols
  • Communicate discreetly and professionally around sensitive health topics
  • Track conversion metrics and identify opportunities to improve lead-to-booking performance
  • Contribute to sales scripts, SOPs, objection handling, and CRM processes
  • Support extended Friday coverage and potential future after-hours/weekend sales coverage

Required Qualifications

  • 2+ years of experience in healthcare sales, medical aesthetics sales, med spa sales, patient acquisition, lead conversion, inside sales, or a related patient-facing sales environment
  • Proven experience converting inbound leads into consultations, appointments, or sales
  • Experience working with a CRM pipeline and documenting lead activity and outcomes
  • Strong verbal and written English communication skills with a confident, professional phone presence
  • Strong ability to build trust and rapport quickly with prospective patients
  • Comfort and professionalism discussing sensitive sexual health topics using accurate clinical terminology
  • Exceptional follow-through, responsiveness, organization, and task ownership in a fully remote setting
  • Comfortable working toward measurable KPIs such as response time, booked appointments, conversion rate, and follow-up completion

Preferred Qualifications

  • Direct experience with Symplast (EMR/CRM) strongly preferred
  • Experience selling aesthetics, functional medicine, sexual health, wellness, dermatology, urology, plastic surgery, or related services
  • Experience with high-ticket or elective healthcare services
  • Background in high-touch concierge sales, luxury hospitality, or premium client services
  • Experience supporting high-profile or VIP clientele
  • Demonstrated success improving lead-to-booking conversion rates
  • Experience working remotely with a U.S.-based practice

Required Skills & Tools

  • Symplast EMR/CRM (strongly preferred) or comparable EMR/CRM platforms
  • CRM pipeline and lead-management systems
  • VoIP/call management systems and secure messaging tools
  • Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 for documentation and scheduling
  • Strong sales follow-up, objection handling, and appointment-setting skills

Schedule & Pay

  • Full-Time position; Monday–Thursday 10:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. ET; Friday 10:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. ET
  • Fully remote role for a Virginia, USA-based client
  • Pay ranges from 15 000 ZAR - 18 000 ZAR ( $935 - $1100)
  • Includes structured onboarding, HIPAA certification support if needed, and strong long-term growth potential

System Requirements

  • Internet speed of at least 20 Mbps upload and download
  • Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher
  • 8 GB of RAM or higher
  • Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer
  • HD 720p webcam
  • Headset with microphone

Benefits

  • Competitive pay rates
  • Consistent hours and predictable workload
  • Fully remote work with long-term potential
  • Direct collaboration with the founder and clinical team, making a real impact
  • Supportive and inclusive work environment
  • Opportunity to grow alongside a high-touch, concierge-style practice
  • Opportunity to grow into a senior patient acquisition or sales leadership role

If you’re a driven sales professional who knows how to build trust, overcome objections, and consistently turn leads into booked appointments, we’d love to hear from you.
