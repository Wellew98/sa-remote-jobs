About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a skilled HubSpot Implementation Specialist to help businesses maximize the value of the HubSpot platform through successful implementations, system optimization, and client enablement.

In this role, you’ll lead HubSpot implementations across Sales Hub, Marketing Hub, and Service Hub , building scalable CRM solutions, automating business processes, migrating data, and delivering exceptional client experiences. You’ll work closely with Project Managers, Solutions Architects, Developers, and clients to ensure every implementation aligns with business goals and best practices.

If you’re passionate about HubSpot, enjoy solving complex technical challenges, and thrive in a client-facing consulting environment, this role is a strong fit.

What Happens After You Apply

After submitting your application, you’ll receive an email invitation from Spark Hire to record a short 3–5 minute Intro Video . This is the first step in our hiring process and can be completed whenever it’s convenient for you.

Instead of an initial screening call, you’ll have one opportunity to introduce yourself, discuss your HubSpot implementation experience, and explain how you’ve configured CRM systems, built automations, managed data migrations, and supported clients throughout implementation projects. You’ll also have the chance to highlight your consulting approach, troubleshooting expertise, and experience working across HubSpot’s Sales, Marketing, and Service Hubs. Your video will be reviewed by the hiring team before moving forward to the client interview.

You can record your video as many times as you’d like before submitting it—only your final version will be reviewed.

Please keep an eye on both your inbox and spam folder for your Spark Hire invitation after applying.
