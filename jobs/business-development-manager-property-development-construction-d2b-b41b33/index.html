ROLE OVERVIEW

Our Client is an ambitious, fast-growing UK property development and construction firm specializing in the delivery of modern, purpose-built care homes. Partnering with leading care home operators, institutional investors, family offices, and Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs), the company is executing a major expansion strategy to scale its nationwide development pipeline. As Business Development Manager, you will play a pivotal role in driving this growth by identifying high-potential acquisition sites across the UK and initiating high-level commercial conversations with landowners, developers, and planning consultants. Working directly alongside the Company Director, your ability to uncover off-market opportunities and build lasting industry relationships will directly shape the firm's trajectory.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Identify Acquisition Opportunities

Utilize construction intelligence platforms (such as Barbour ABI) and UK planning portals to track potential care home development sites across pre-application, outline, and approved planning stages.

Execute Outbound Outreach

Conduct approximately 200 targeted outbound telephone calls per week to engage architects, planning consultants, developers, landowners, and commercial estate agents across the UK.

Qualify Commercial Viability

Initiate consultative, commercially focused conversations to assess site availability, gather critical project data, and qualify genuine acquisition targets for the Company Director.

Cultivate Long-Term Relationships

Nurture a high-value network of property and construction stakeholders, maintaining consistent, long-cycle follow-ups as site statuses and market conditions evolve.

Coordinate Executive Introductions

Navigate gatekeepers, overcome objections, and arrange qualified virtual meetings and introductions between key decision-makers and the Company Director.

Manage Pipeline & Reporting

Maintain meticulous records of all outreach, stakeholder interactions, and opportunity statuses within the CRM, leveraging AI-assisted research and automation tools to maximize efficiency.

Meet Performance Benchmarks

Deliver against core commercial KPIs, including maintaining weekly call volumes and generating an average of one qualified, viable site acquisition opportunity per quarter.

QUALIFICATIONS

Proven B2B Sales Experience

Demonstrable track record in outbound, telephone-intensive business development or commercially focused relationship management.

Industry Background

Prior experience within property development, construction, commercial real estate, land acquisition, or a closely aligned sector.

Exceptional Communication Skills

Fluent, articulate spoken and written English with a confident, persuasive telephone manner capable of engaging senior executives and technical professionals.

Commercial Acumen

Strong negotiation, questioning, and objection-handling skills, with the judgment to distinguish a basic lead from a viable commercial opportunity.

Long-Cycle Sales Management

Proven ability to manage extended sales cycles, maintain organized pipelines, and consistently hit commercial targets.

Remote Discipline

Self-motivated, accountable, and highly organized, with the ability to work independently from South Africa aligned to UK business hours (9:00 AM–5:00 PM Monday–Thursday; 9:00 AM–3:00 PM Friday).

DESIRED (NOT REQUIRED):

Construction Intelligence Tools

Hands-on experience using Barbour ABI or comparable UK construction and planning intelligence platforms.

UK Market Familiarity

Previous experience working with UK businesses, navigating UK local authority planning portals, or engaging UK architects and planning consultants.

Sector Knowledge

Familiarity with the UK care home, healthcare property, or institutional real estate investment sectors (REITs, family offices).

Tech-Enabled Prospecting

Experience utilizing CRM systems, AI research tools, and outreach automation to streamline prospecting workflows.
