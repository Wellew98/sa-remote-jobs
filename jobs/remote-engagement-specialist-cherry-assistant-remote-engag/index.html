We are hiring a Candidate Engagement Specialist  for a fast-growing U.S.-based company operating in the workforce engagement space. This is a full-time remote role designed for someone who is highly articulate, confident on camera, and experienced in structured, high-quality candidate and client-facing conversations. This role is ideal for individuals from backgrounds such as customer success, sales, talent acquisition, support, training, hospitality, communications, or other service-driven environments  where professionalism, clarity, and presence are key. You will lead structured live conversations and ensure every interaction reflects a consistent, high-quality candidate experience.

Key Responsibilities

  • Conduct structured live conversations with individuals via video and/or phone
  • Guide interactions using predefined frameworks while maintaining natural flow and rapport
  • Ensure every conversation is handled with professionalism, clarity, and consistency
  • Ask structured questions to understand background, experience, and alignment
  • Capture detailed and accurate notes in internal systems (ATS or CRM)
  • Represent the company as a professional, client-facing brand representative
  • Maintain composure and engagement across back-to-back scheduled interactions
  • Ensure a smooth and positive experience for every participant

Required Qualifications

  • 2-4+ years in client-facing or people-facing roles  (talent acquisition, customer success, sales, online teaching, hospitality, communications, or similar)
  • Strong verbal communication skills with a clear, professional tone
  • Strong interpersonal intelligence and ability to build rapport quickly
  • Comfortable speaking on camera and engaging in structured conversations
  • Strong active listening and ability to follow conversational frameworks

·High attention to detail and accuracy in documentation

  • Ability to work in a structured, time-sensitive environment
  • Based in South Africa and available for US business hours (Mon–Fri)

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in talent acquisition, training, onboarding, or customer experience roles
  • Exposure to CRM or structured workflow tools

Required Skills & Tools

  • Phone / VoIP calling systems
  • Zoom (or similar video conferencing tools)
  • CRM platforms for logging, tracking, and updating live conversations
  • Email and calendar tools (e.g. Gmail, Outlook, Calendly) for managing scheduling, follow-ups, and coordination where required
  • Fast, accurate typing skills for real-time note-taking during or immediately after live conversations

Schedule & Pay

  • Full-time position; US business hours (8am–5pm EST/CST/PST depending on assignment)
  • Fully remote role for a U.S.-based company
  • Pay ranges from R15,000 – R18,000 per month

Includes

  • Weekly performance incentives (up to +50% additional earnings)
  • Stable income and consistent hours
  • Annual review and progression opportunities

System Requirements

  • 20 Mbps+ internet speed
  • 2.4 GHz processor or higher
  • 8 GB RAM or higher
  • Windows 10+ or macOS 10.10+
  • HD webcam (720p minimum)
  • Noise-cancelling headset

Benefits

  • Fully remote, stable full-time role
  • US-based operational exposure
  • No commute and full location flexibility
  • Consistent client interaction and structured workflows
  • Long-term engagement and career development potential
  • Supportive and structured working environment

If you are someone who thrives in structured conversations, enjoys engaging with people professionally, and takes pride in delivering clear, high-quality communication experiences, we’d love to hear from you.
