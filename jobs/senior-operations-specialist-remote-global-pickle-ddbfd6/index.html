About Pickle

Pickle is a talent partner for fast-growing businesses. We help ambitious founders and leadership teams hire exceptional people across operations, product, technology, customer success, commercial, finance, and executive leadership.

We specialise in identifying high-quality permanent and contractor talent that can make an immediate impact, supporting businesses through key growth stages with a thoughtful, consultative approach to hiring. Whether it's a critical leadership appointment or building out an entire function, we work as an extension of our clients' teams to find people who combine the right skills, mindset, and cultural fit.

We believe great hiring goes beyond matching CVs to job descriptions. It's about understanding how businesses operate, what success looks like, and connecting them with people who will help them grow for the long term.

About Our Client

We’re building the AI that the world’s charities run on, so machines do the work and humans do the caring.

We believe history will remember the machine age not for the work machines took from us, but for the work they gave back: the oldest and most human work of all, caring for each other.

Through our products, donors have already given over $200 million to hundreds of charities, including Save the Children, Oxfam and Amnesty International.

Our client is a UAE-based software company working with donors and charity partners across the world. We’re entering an exciting new phase of growth, moving beyond a single product towards a broader portfolio of products and opportunities.

We’re ambitious about what we can build, how quickly we can build it and the impact we can have.

The Opportunity

We’re looking for an exceptional Operations Specialist to work directly with our founders and own turning business strategy into execution.

As our Client's space evolves from a single-product business into a multi-product organisation, we need someone who can bring structure, focus and operational discipline to our next stage of growth, without slowing down the pace that got us here.

This is not a traditional Operations Specialist position focused primarily on coordination, presentations and executive support. It’s a hands-on leadership role for someone who can operate at both strategic and operational levels, own complex business challenges, and drive results across the organisation.

You’ll act as a key operational partner to the founders, translating their vision and priorities into actionable plans, building the systems and teams needed to deliver them, and ensuring that important initiatives don’t get lost between strategy and execution.

You’ll have significant autonomy and responsibility across the business, working closely with and driving accountability across Sales, Product, Engineering, Operations and Customer Success.

We need someone who can see the bigger picture, identify what matters most and then get into the detail to make it happen.

What You’ll Own

Business Strategy & Execution

Translate the founders’ vision and strategic priorities into clear business plans, objectives and measurable outcomes.

Drive execution of the company’s most important initiatives, ensuring priorities remain focused and progress is visible.

Identify operational, commercial and organisational bottlenecks and take ownership of resolving them.

Challenge existing approaches, identify opportunities for improvement and drive decisions that move the business forward.

Ensure resources, people and investment are aligned with the company’s highest-priority objectives.

Maintain a clear view of business performance, risks and opportunities, providing the founders with the information needed to make effective decisions.

Operational Leadership

Take ownership of the day-to-day operational effectiveness of the business.

Establish and continuously improve the operating structures, processes and systems required to support a growing, multi-product organisation.

Drive cross-functional collaboration and accountability across all business functions.

Ensure teams have clear objectives, ownership and measurable performance expectations.

Improve operational efficiency, remove unnecessary complexity and build scalable ways of working.

Establish effective reporting, planning and communication rhythms that keep the organisation aligned and moving.

People & Organisational Development

Build, develop and retain a high-performing team capable of delivering the company’s ambitions.

Work with functional leaders to establish clear roles, responsibilities and accountability.

Identify capability gaps and take ownership of addressing them through recruitment, development or organisational changes.

Create an environment where high standards, ownership, direct communication and continuous improvement are expected.

Challenge and support leaders to improve performance and develop their teams.

Commercial & Business Performance

Develop a strong understanding of the company’s commercial model, revenue drivers, costs and growth opportunities.

Work with Sales and Customer Success to improve commercial execution, customer outcomes and retention.

Establish meaningful business metrics and performance reporting to support evidence-based decisions.

Identify opportunities to improve profitability, efficiency and scalability.

Support the founders in evaluating new product opportunities and business initiatives, ensuring ideas translate into viable execution plans.

AI, Automation & Innovation

Champion the practical application of AI across the organisation.

Actively use and deploy AI tools, agents and automation to improve productivity, decision-making and operational performance.

Identify opportunities to automate repetitive work and eliminate unnecessary manual processes.

Build and implement AI-enabled workflows that can be adopted across teams.

Help shape how we use emerging AI capabilities to create competitive advantage.

What We’re Looking For

We’re looking for someone who combines exceptional operational capability with the personality, drive and resilience to thrive in a demanding startup environment.

Experience & Expertise

Significant experience in senior operational leadership, such as Chief of Staff, COO, General Manager, Head of Operations or an equivalent role.

A proven track record of helping scale a technology, SaaS or product-led business.

Experience leading multiple functions and driving delivery across complex, cross-functional teams.

Demonstrable experience building operational processes, systems and structures in fast-growing organisations.

Strong commercial understanding, including business performance, revenue drivers, costs and resource allocation.

Experience recruiting, developing and managing high-performing teams.

Exceptional project and programme management capabilities, with the ability to manage multiple strategic priorities simultaneously.

Strong analytical skills and confidence using data to identify problems, evaluate options and measure results.

Practical experience using AI tools and automation to improve business operations.

The Person

This is where we need someone exceptional.

This is a demanding, fast-moving startup environment. We need someone who thrives in that environment rather than simply tolerates it.

You’ll likely recognise yourself in the following

Extreme ownership. You take responsibility for outcomes, not just tasks. You don’t wait for someone else to solve a problem or tell you what to do.

High agency. You are resourceful, proactive and comfortable operating without a playbook. You find ways forward when others see obstacles.

Exceptional drive. You have an extraordinary work ethic and are motivated by ambitious goals. You bring energy, urgency and momentum to everything you take on.

Intellectual curiosity. You ask intelligent questions, challenge assumptions and actively seek evidence that might prove you wrong. You are interested in understanding the real problem before jumping to solutions.

Strong judgement. You know when to move quickly, when to slow down and when a decision needs more thought. You can distinguish between what matters and what is simply noise.

Resilience and adaptability. You remain effective under pressure, navigate uncertainty and adapt quickly when priorities change. Setbacks don’t derail you.

Low ego, high standards. You’re confident enough to challenge senior people but humble enough to do the groundwork yourself. No task is beneath you if it needs doing.

Exceptional communication. You communicate clearly, directly and thoughtfully. You’re comfortable having difficult conversations, giving and receiving candid feedback and bringing people along with you.

A builder’s mentality. You enjoy creating something from nothing. You can build the first version yourself, prove it works and then bring the right people in to scale it.

How We Work

Our culture is built around a few fundamental principles

Initiative over permission: We expect people to take ownership and make decisions without unnecessary bureaucracy.

Speed over perfection: We favour rapid iteration, informed decisions and continuous improvement over endless discussion.

Candour over comfort: We value honesty, direct feedback and constructive challenge. Getting to the truth matters more than being agreeable.

Writing over unnecessary meetings: Clear thinking and good written communication are essential. We value thoughtful documentation and concise decision-making.

AI throughout everything: We actively look for ways to use AI to improve how we work, build and deliver.

Hands-on leadership: We operate at every level. Leaders stay close to the work, understand the detail and never lose touch with what’s happening on the ground.

What Success Looks Like

In your first 6–12 months, you’ll have:

Established a clear operating rhythm across the business, with visibility of priorities, performance and accountability.

Built stronger operational structures to support the transition from a single product to a multi-product organisation.

Improved execution speed and delivery across key business functions.

Established clear ownership and performance expectations across teams and functional leaders.

Identified and resolved significant operational bottlenecks.

Improved business performance visibility and the quality of decision-making.

Introduced meaningful AI-enabled workflows and automation that improve productivity and efficiency.

Built a stronger operational foundation that allows the founders to focus on product vision, strategic opportunities and long-term growth.

The Reality of Working Here

Our client's space is not a conventional corporate environment. We move quickly, operate with limited resources and regularly tackle problems without established playbooks or obvious answers.

This environment isn’t for everyone, and we don’t pretend it is.

You’ll need to be comfortable with ambiguity, changing priorities and a level of personal ownership that goes beyond a traditional job description. There will be times when you’ll need to roll up your sleeves, solve problems yourself and create structure where none exists.

We expect exceptional standards, a strong work ethic and an unwavering commitment to making things happen.

If you need extensive direction, established processes and clearly defined boundaries to do your best work, this probably isn’t the right environment for you.

If you thrive on building, solving, leading and making things happen, you’ll find an extraordinary opportunity here.

We’re not looking for someone to maintain the status quo. We’re looking for someone who can help us build what’s next.
