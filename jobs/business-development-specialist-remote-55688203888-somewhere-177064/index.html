Business Development Representative Full-Time | Remote (Latin America Preferred) About the Company We are a fast-growing, modern temporary housing company focused on helping families secure comfortable, short-term housing during times of disruption. Whether due to home damage, relocation, or unforeseen circumstances, we simplify what can often be a stressful process by combining technology, urgency, and a high-touch, service-driven approach. Our team operates in a dynamic, fast-paced environment with a strong focus on impact, growth, and delivering meaningful solutions to those who need it most. The Opportunity We are seeking a proactive, resourceful, and relationship-oriented Business Development Representative to support our continued growth. This role sits at the intersection of housing operations, landlord partnerships, and business development. The successful candidate will be responsible for sourcing housing options, building relationships with landlords and property managers, negotiating lease terms, and ensuring families have access to suitable temporary housing solutions. While business development and sales experience are valuable, the highest priority is experience working within housing, real estate, relocation, property management, or temporary accommodation environments. Key Responsibilities - Source and identify temporary housing options that meet client requirements - Conduct housing searches across multiple platforms, networks, referrals, and direct outreach channels - Build and maintain strong relationships with landlords, property owners, property managers, and housing providers - Negotiate rental rates, lease terms, and housing agreements - Evaluate housing options based on suitability, location, pricing, availability, and client needs - Proactively contact landlords and housing partners via phone, email, text, and other communication channels - Convert outreach efforts into active housing partnerships and inventory opportunities - Gather and verify critical housing details including availability, pricing, policies, and lease requirements - Maintain accurate records and activity tracking within CRM systems - Follow up consistently with partners and stakeholders to secure housing solutions quickly - Take ownership of housing sourcing targets and partnership development goals Required Experience - Experience in housing, relocation services, property management, temporary accommodation, real estate, leasing, or a related field - Demonstrated experience conducting housing searches and securing accommodation solutions - Experience negotiating with landlords, property owners, or housing providers - Strong communication and relationship-building skills - Comfortable managing multiple housing requests and priorities in a fast-paced environment - Highly organized with strong attention to detail - Self-motivated, resourceful, and solutions-oriented Preferred Experience - Business development, account management, partnerships, or sales experience - Experience with high-volume outreach and prospecting - Background working with temporary housing providers or relocation companies - Experience using CRM systems and maintaining detailed records - Experience working remotely within a distributed team environment What Success Looks Like The ideal candidate demonstrates: - Strong urgency and follow-through - Persistence when sourcing difficult-to-find housing options - Confidence engaging with landlords and negotiating agreements - Excellent communication skills with both internal and external stakeholders - A proactive, ownership-driven approach to problem solving - The ability to quickly identify solutions during time-sensitive situations Preferred Candidate Profile Based on the success of previous team members, candidates from Latin America are strongly preferred. We are seeking individuals who combine housing or real estate experience with a strong sense of urgency, accountability, and customer service. Candidates with backgrounds in housing coordination, relocation services, real estate operations, leasing, landlord relations, or property management will be particularly well suited to this opportunity. Work Environment - Fully remote team - Fast-paced, growth-focused culture - Strong emphasis on ownership, accountability, and communication - Direct collaboration with leadership - Opportunity to make a meaningful impact on families during critical life situations Why Join Us? - Be part of a mission-driven company helping families when they need support most - Work in a high-growth entrepreneurial environment - Play a key role in expanding housing partnerships and supply - Career growth and development opportunities - Join a collaborative and supportive remote team
