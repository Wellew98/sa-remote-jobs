Paid Media Manager (Meta & Google Ads)

LocationRemote (South Africa or Latin America)

ScheduleFixed hours, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM U.S. Eastern Time (EST/EDT), Monday – Friday

Compensation$1,800 – $2,500 USD / month (commensurate with regional experience and track record)

Reports ToChief Executive Officer

About the Company

Our client is an established, high-growth direct-to-consumer (DTC) e-commerce brand recognized for its distinctive design aesthetic, passionate customer community, and strong digital presence.

The brand is transitioning its paid acquisition in-house to drive higher agility, sharper creative testing, and tighter performance control. They are seeking a data-driven, hands-on Paid Media Manager to take end-to-end operational ownership of their multi-channel advertising accounts across Meta and Google Ads.

This is an execution-heavy, high-visibility role. While executive leadership provides macro-level growth targets and commercial priorities, the Paid Media Manager owns the daily levers: campaign builds, bid strategies, audience segmentation, budget pacing, creative performance tracking, and rapid experimentation.

This is not a passive coordinator or task-taker position. The ideal candidate brings a well-formed point of view, challenges assumptions with clean data, and actively surfaces optimization opportunities rather than waiting for top-down instructions.

Role Overview

The Paid Media Manager will lead daily campaign operations, media buying, testing roadmaps, and attribution analysis across the brand’s digital advertising portfolio.

Approximately 65–70% of the role focuses on direct account management, campaign builds, bidding adjustments, feed maintenance, and creative testing. The remaining 30–35% centers on cross-functional alignment with creative leads, performance reporting, data attribution, and collaborative growth strategy with the CEO.

The ideal candidate has managed mid-to-large monthly ad spend for DTC brands, understands modern attribution modeling, and thrives in a fast-paced environment where creative fatigue is met with disciplined, structured iteration.

Key Responsibilities

Day-to-Day Campaign Operations & Account Hygiene (~40%)

- Own daily execution and optimization across Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads (Search, Performance Max, Shopping, and YouTube).

- Actively manage daily and monthly budget pacing, bid strategies, and audience segmentation to maintain efficient blended ROAS and target Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC).

- Ensure rigorous technical account hygiene: verify Meta Pixel and Conversions API (CAPI) events, Google Tag Manager tracking, UTM taxonomy, catalog feeds, and dynamic product ad configurations.

- Monitor live campaigns daily to immediately resolve platform disapprovals, budget anomalies, delivery stalls, or performance dips.

Structured Testing & Creative Strategy (~25%)

- Design, execute, and analyze structured creative and audience testing sprints with defined hypotheses and measurable success criteria.

- Collaborate closely with internal creative and marketing leads to review asset performance, identify creative fatigue early, and brief high-priority asset variations (hooks, angles, visual pacing).

- Proactively suggest account restructures, novel bidding strategies, and channel expansion opportunities backed by quantitative performance data.

- Stay ahead of major ad platform updates, algorithm shifts, and emerging targeting methodologies.

Attribution, Reporting & Analysis (~20%)

- Deliver clear weekly and monthly performance reports covering spend efficiency, blended ROAS, first-time customer CAC, MER, and channel contribution trends.

- Tie campaign performance directly to commercial e-commerce context (promotional calendars, inventory velocity, and product-page conversion rates).

- Conduct root-cause analysis on underperforming campaigns and present data-backed corrective action plans.

- Leverage modern attribution platforms to evaluate multi-touch user journeys and optimize cross-channel budget allocation.

Cross-Functional Collaboration & Marketing Operations (~15%)

- Maintain close, daily operational communication with the CEO on target spend, growth milestones, and commercial priorities.

- Coordinate with marketing operations to ensure paid campaigns align seamlessly with on-site promotional offers, email/SMS drops, and inventory levels.

- Support ongoing workflow documentation, naming conventions, and standard operating procedures (SOPs) for the internal media buying desk.

- Integrate approved AI tools into reporting workflows, ad copy generation, and data synthesis to increase analysis velocity.

Hard Skills & Qualifications

- 3–5+ years of hands-on experience managing performance-driven Meta and Google advertising accounts for DTC / consumer e-commerce brands.

- Demonstrated Budget Ownership: Proven track record of managing substantial monthly ad spend with direct responsibility for ROAS and revenue efficiency.

- Technical Tracking & Attribution Fluency: Deep understanding of GA4, Google Tag Manager, Conversions API (CAPI), UTM parameters, and modern DTC attribution platforms (e.g., Triple Whale, Northbeam, or comparable tools).

- Creative Testing Acumen: Strong familiarity with direct-response creative frameworks, hook rates, hold rates, and rapid iteration workflows.

- Flawless English Communication: High-caliber written and spoken English, comfortable engaging in real-time technical debates and strategic discussions with executive leadership.

- Time Zone Alignment: Must be able to work fixed, non-negotiable hours of 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM Eastern Time (EST/EDT) , Monday through Friday, with a reliable high-speed internet connection and power backup.

Soft Skills & Cultural Fit

- High Ownership & Initiative: Operates proactively; solves problems before they become executive escalations.

- Analytical & Critical Thinker: Looks beyond top-line platform metrics to understand blended e-commerce economics.

- Confident & Respectful Communicator: Comfortable challenging executive direction when supported by objective data.

- Meticulous Eye for Detail: Zero tolerance for broken tracking, sloppy naming conventions, or unmonitored budget pacing.

- Agile & Adaptable: Thrives in an evolving e-commerce environment with changing creative and promotional priorities.

Why This Role Matters

By transitioning paid acquisition in-house, our client is establishing a dedicated, high-agility performance marketing engine.

As the Paid Media Manager, you will not just run ads; you will directly influence how marketing dollars are deployed, build a robust creative testing pipeline, and partner with executive leadership to scale a beloved consumer brand efficiently.
