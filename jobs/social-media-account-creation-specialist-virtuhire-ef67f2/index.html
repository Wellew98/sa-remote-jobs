About the Role

We are looking for a hands-on Social Media Account Creation Specialist to join the marketing team of a fast-growing, UK-based consumer brand.

This role focuses on

setting up, growing and maintaining healthy social media accounts , particularly across TikTok and Instagram. You will also help build and coordinate a network of creators, UGC talent and account operators across relevant markets.

The ideal candidate understands how to launch social accounts from scratch, build genuine reach and engagement, and maintain strong account health while working within platform guidelines.

Key Responsibilities

Set up new TikTok, Instagram and other social media accounts , including profiles, bios, branding and business settings.

Warm up and grow new accounts organically while following platform guidelines.

Monitor account performance and health, including reach, views, restrictions and suspensions.

Identify and resolve potential account-health issues quickly.

Source, vet and onboard creators, influencers, UGC talent and account operators .

Coordinate creator activity and posting across different accounts and markets.

Manage posting schedules and content distribution across a growing account network.

Maintain accurate and secure records of accounts, ownership and access.

Track which account setup methods, posting frequencies and content formats perform best.

Stay up to date with social media platform policies, community guidelines and algorithm changes.

Requirements

2+ years' hands-on experience creating and growing social media accounts for a brand, agency or creator network.

Proven experience growing new

TikTok and/or Instagram accounts from the ground up.

Able to provide examples of accounts you have grown and the results achieved.

Strong understanding of platform community guidelines, account health signals and potential causes of restrictions.

Experience working with or managing

creators, influencers or UGC talent .

Highly organised and comfortable managing multiple accounts simultaneously.

Excellent written and spoken English.

Comfortable working remotely during

UK business hours .

Nice to Have

Experience with UK or US audiences , social media scheduling tools, consumer brands, e-commerce or entertainment businesses would be advantageous. Basic content creation or video-editing skills would also be beneficial.

What Success Looks Like

Success will be measured through the number of new accounts successfully launched, account health, genuine reach and views achieved over the first 30–90 days, and the successful onboarding and management of creators and account operators.

This opportunity would suit a

resourceful, proactive and commercially minded social media specialist who understands that sustainable growth comes from strong content, genuine audience engagement and responsible platform management—not shortcuts or workarounds. The wider team operates in a fast-moving, creative environment where cultural awareness, speed and adaptability are important.
