We are hiring a Deal Operations Coordinator for a boutique investment firm in the financial services/private investments industry. This is a Part-Time remote role for a US based client. The role is focused on end-to-end deal intake coordination, CRM and pipeline management, and outbound email research and execution. The ideal candidate is process-driven, detail-obsessed, tech-savvy, an excellent written communicator, and comfortable managing multi-step workflows with minimal supervision.

(Note: This is strictly an operations and workflow coordination role, not a financial analysis or investment evaluation position.)

Key Responsibilities

  • Inbound Deal Triage & Pipeline Management: Review inbound business opportunities (typically 3–4 per day) against specific size and target industry criteria (education, workforce development, healthcare-adjacent services, software, and business services), rapidly disqualifying non-fits and advancing qualified leads through the pipeline.
  • NDA & Document Coordination: Request, review, and manage Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) and confidentiality documents with third-party brokers, bankers, and intermediaries; check 2–3 standard terms against templates, propose simple redlines in Microsoft Word, coordinate signatures, and organize executed records in the file repository.
  • CRM Administration (HubSpot): Maintain accurate and complete deal records, contact details, communications, and activity logs in HubSpot, generating pipeline summaries and ensuring nothing falls through the cracks.
  • Outbound Email Campaign Execution: Manage a multi-step outbound outreach workflow, including using AI/LLM tools to research and qualify prospects from purchased lists, logging qualified leads into HubSpot, and executing email sequences through outreach platforms.
  • Contact List & Data Manipulation: Clean, deduplicate, validate, and enhance large prospect lists (up to 2,000+ records) in Microsoft Excel prior to outreach.
  • External Stakeholder Communication: Liaise professionally via email with business owners, intermediaries, bankers, and brokers to request deal materials, track responses, and follow up on outstanding action items.
  • SOP Adherence & Executive Operations Support: Follow detailed Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and checklists across 10–15 step processes, prepare meeting briefs, track deadlines, and suggest process refinements over time.
  • Confidentiality & Discretion: Handle sensitive deal materials, NDAs, and proprietary contact lists with strict integrity.

Required Qualifications

  • 4+ years of proven experience in project operations, project administration, executive operations (supporting founders/CEOs), sales operations/CRM coordination, or contracts/legal administration
  • Demonstrated ability to manage multiple workflows simultaneously, track deadlines, and independently execute repetitive, multi-step processes with exceptional attention to detail
  • Exceptional written and spoken English with polished, professional email etiquette for external correspondence
  • Intermediate-to-advanced Microsoft Excel skills (filtering, deduplicating, data manipulation) and solid proficiency in Microsoft Word (document formatting and redlining)
  • Comfort and proficiency using AI/LLM tools to conduct prospect research and summarize information
  • High degree of professionalism, reliability, and strict discretion when handling confidential agreements
  • Genuine commitment to a long-term, part-time (20 hours/week) arrangement without expectation of transitioning into a full-time role

Preferred Qualifications

  • Prior experience with HubSpot CRM and remote work supporting US-based executives or business owners
  • Background in contract administration, paralegal/legal coordination, real estate transaction coordination, or complex pipeline management
  • Familiarity with email outreach and list validation tools (e.g., Instantly, ZeroBounce)
  • Basic familiarity with M&A/acquisition terminology and documents (e.g., NDAs, CIMs) is helpful, though direct investment or finance experience is not required

Required Skills & Tools

  • Microsoft Excel (Intermediate+ for large list manipulation and data enhancement)
  • Microsoft Word (Document review and redlining)
  • HubSpot CRM (or easily adaptable to straightforward CRM workflows)
  • AI / LLM Research Tools (e.g., ChatGPT)
  • Outbound Email & Data Validation Software (training provided via detailed SOPs)

Schedule & Pay

  • Part-Time position; 20 hours/week - 9:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m. Eastern Time
  • Fully remote role for a United States based client
  • Pay ranges from ZAR 16 000 – ZAR 21 000 per month, depending on skills and experience
  • Includes comprehensive SOPs, hands-on training during ramp-up, long-term part-time stability, and schedule flexibility after onboarding

System Requirements

  • Internet speed of at least 20 Mbps upload and download
  • Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher
  • 8 GB of RAM or higher
  • Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer
  • HD 720p webcam
  • Headset with microphone

Benefits

  • Competitive pay rates
  • Consistent hours and predictable workload
  • Fully remote work with long-term potential
  • Direct collaboration with the Founder, making a real impact
  • Supportive and inclusive work environment
  • Opportunity to grow alongside a boutique investment firm
