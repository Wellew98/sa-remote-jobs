We're HiringFinancial Controller (Remote)

Are you an experienced

Financial Controller who enjoys combining hands-on finance with strategic business support?

We're looking for someone who can take ownership of budgeting, bookkeeping, financial reporting, and cost management while partnering with leadership to drive smarter financial decisions.

Location

Remote (South Africa)

Working Hours

9:00 AM – 5:00 PM Tennessee Time (Central Time) with some flexibility

Salary

R 54,100–59,000

What You'll Be Doing

Own budgeting, forecasting, and financial oversight across the business.

Manage day-to-day bookkeeping in QuickBooks.

Oversee accounts payable and receivable, reconciliations, and general ledger accuracy.

Review transactions across QuickBooks, Rippling Spend, and American Express.

Prepare management reports, budget vs. actuals, and financial insights.

Identify cost-saving opportunities and improve financial processes.

Partner with department leaders to keep spending aligned with budgets.

Strengthen financial controls and resolve discrepancies proactively.

What We're Looking For

5–10 years of experience in Financial Control, Finance Management, or Accounting.

Strong hands-on experience with

QuickBooks (or a similar accounting platform).

Proven experience with budgeting, bookkeeping, reconciliations, and financial reporting.

Excellent financial analysis skills with the ability to turn data into actionable insights.

Strong attention to detail and confidence working independently.

Highly Preferred

Experience in a residential healthcare environment , such as a rehab facility, nursing home, hospital, or assisted living facility.

Experience working with a

US-based company .

Exposure to

Rippling Spend , American Express , or similar expense management platforms.

A CA qualification is not required—practical, hands-on experience is valued just as highly.

Who You Are

We're looking for someone who is

Professional and approachable.

A strong communicator who can explain financial information clearly.

Proactive and solutions-focused.

Comfortable collaborating with both finance and operational teams.

Organised, accountable, and driven to improve processes.
