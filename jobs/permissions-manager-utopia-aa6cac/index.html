About the role

We are looking for a hands-on Global Permitting & Approvals Manager to lead permitting and regulatory approvals across our international development portfolio.

You will research local requirements, translate them into clear technical tasks for project teams, coordinate AORs and local consultants, and drive approval processes through to completion.

This role requires independent problem-solving, strong execution skills and the ability to establish effective processes in a fast-moving international environment.

Projects span Spain, South Africa, Brazil, Costa Rica, the Dominican Republic, the UAE and other locations, each with different regulatory requirements.

Responsibilities

Own permitting and regulatory approvals across multiple countries and projects.

Research local regulations and establish approval strategies and schedules.

Translate statutory requirements into technical documentation tasks for internal teams.

Participate in selecting and managing local AORs and permitting consultants.

Coordinate application submissions, authority comments and final approvals.

Identify critical risks, resolve blockers and protect construction milestones.

Implement standardized permitting processes and portfolio-wide reporting.

Hands-on construction permitting experience in at least two countries and three project locations.

Proven record of personally securing development or construction approvals.

Experience of permitting in at least 2 of selected locations: Spain, South Africa, Brazil, Costa Rica, the Dominican Republic, the UAE

Strong understanding of regulatory requirements and technical submission documentation.

Experience managing local AORs, consultants and government authorities.

Ability to research new jurisdictions and manage multiple projects simultaneously.

Strong ownership, analytical thinking and process-building capabilities.

Fluent English

Nice to Have

Spanish and/or Portuguese.

Hands-on permitting experience in Spain, Brazil, Latin America, the UAE, or South Africa.

Background in luxury residential or hospitality development.

Fully remote with business trips. You'll collaborate from anywhere within GMT-3 to GMT+3.

All the tools you need. A laptop and access to our project and documentation tools from day one.

Properties across three continents. You'll take projects through approval in Europe, Africa, the Middle East, and Latin America, so you won't be limited to one market, one regulator, or one type of permit.

A team that spans 30+ countries. Developers, architects, engineers, and legal specialists from every corner of the world, held to the same standards and the same bar for quality.
