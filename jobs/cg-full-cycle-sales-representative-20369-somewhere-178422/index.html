About the Company

Our client is a fast-growing open-source software company building a modern alternative to traditional enterprise survey platforms. Their product supports both cloud and self-hosted deployments and is trusted by product, engineering, and data teams around the world that value privacy, flexibility, and transparency.

The company operates as a fully remote, English-speaking team aligned primarily with the Germany/EU timezone. They are currently a lean team of eight and continuing to grow.

About the Role

The Full-Cycle Sales Representative will take ownership of mid-market revenue by managing the entire sales process from discovery to close. This role is responsible for converting inbound leads generated through product adoption and marketing while also building a structured outbound pipeline. The ideal candidate is a proactive and self-directed salesperson who can balance inbound opportunity management with outbound prospecting to drive consistent revenue growth.

Key Responsibilities

Revenue Ownership (Mid-Market Focus)

- Manage the full sales cycle from discovery to closing deals.

- Own mid-market opportunities end-to-end, including discovery calls, product demos, negotiation, and closing.

- Handle objection management and guide prospects through the buying process.

- Close cloud and on-premise licensing agreements with qualified buyers.

Inbound Lead Management

- Respond to and manage approximately 5–10 inbound leads per day.

- Qualify and nurture prospects through the sales funnel.

- Maintain organized pipeline management and strong CRM hygiene.

- Provide clear reporting on pipeline health and conversion metrics.

Outbound Pipeline Development

- Build and execute outbound prospecting strategies targeting ideal-fit accounts.

- Develop messaging, outreach scripts, and sales sequences.

- Prospect through LinkedIn, email, and phone outreach.

- Identify and engage companies seeking alternatives to traditional enterprise survey platforms.

- Continuously test and optimize outbound strategies to improve results.

Qualifications

- 3–6+ years of B2B SaaS sales experience.

- Proven experience managing the full sales cycle and closing mid-market deals.

- Experience selling technical products to product, engineering, or data teams.

- Strong ability to build outbound sales processes and prospect pipelines.

- Highly organized and capable of working independently with minimal supervision.

- Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.

- Based in South Africa with availability aligned to European time zones.

Nice to Have

- Experience selling developer tools, open-source products, or API-driven platforms.

- Experience competing with larger enterprise software vendors.

- Familiarity with both short and long sales cycles.

What Success Looks Like

- Consistently closing mid-market revenue within the first 6–12 months.

- Building a predictable and scalable outbound pipeline.

- Becoming a key contributor to overall company revenue growth.
