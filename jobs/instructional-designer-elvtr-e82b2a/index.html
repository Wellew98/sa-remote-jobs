Join One of the Fastest-Growing Online Education Companies in the World At ELVTR, we're transforming online education by connecting ambitious professionals with industry leaders from companies like Nike, Netflix, Google, Amazon, and more. Since launching in 2020, we've grown into a global learning community with over 300 expert instructors, delivering more than 200 live online courses across 12 industries. To date, we've helped 22,000+ graduates worldwide gain the skills, confidence, and connections needed to advance their careers. Now we're looking for a highly organized and creative Instructional Designer to help us bring exceptional online learning experiences to life. If you're passionate about education, thrive in a collaborative environment, and enjoy turning ideas into impactful courses, we'd love to meet you. This is a full-time, remote role for candidates based in South Africa. Why This Role Is Different At ELVTR, you'll work at the intersection of education, innovation, and collaboration. You'll partner with industry-leading instructors and cross-functional teams to develop engaging online courses that help professionals around the world advance their careers. From the earliest stages of course planning through production and launch, you'll play a key role in ensuring every learning experience meets our high standards for quality, relevance, and learner engagement. You'll Love This Role If You: Enjoy managing projects from concept to completion Thrive in a fast-paced, collaborative environment Are highly organized and detail-oriented Love working with experts and helping bring their knowledge to life Enjoy solving problems and finding creative solutions Stay curious about learning, education, and emerging technologies Believe education has the power to transform careers and lives What You'll Do Coordinate the end-to-end production of live online courses, from planning through launch Conduct market research and help define course objectives, learning outcomes, and target audiences Manage production timelines, priorities, and deliverables to ensure projects stay on schedule Partner closely with instructors, guiding them through course preparation and rehearsals Collaborate with Marketing, Creative, Sales, and Student Success teams to deliver high-quality learning experiences Review course materials, marketing content, and sales collateral for clarity, accuracy, and educational value Identify and resolve production challenges while maintaining project momentum Stay informed about industry trends, instructional design best practices, and emerging learning technologies Continuously share ideas and feedback to improve our courses and production processes
