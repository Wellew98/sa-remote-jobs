Role: Executive Assistant to CEO Priority Location: South Africa and then global (recommended based on talent pool and remote adaptability) Working Hours: Monday–Friday, 9 AM–6 PM EST (flexible to align with CEO’s schedule) Salary Range: $1,500–$2,000 per month (final offer depends on candidate experience, interview results, and skills) Type of Contract: Independent Contractor Type of Job: Remote About the Company: We are a growing public insurance adjusting firm specializing in residential and commercial property damage claims . We advocate for policyholders to ensure fair and full settlements after losses caused by fire, water, wind, and other perils. The company values professionalism, efficiency, and attention to detail while fostering a collaborative and supportive work environment. About the Role: We are seeking a proactive and detail-oriented Executive Assistant to support our lead Public Adjuster. The ideal candidate will help streamline operations, manage communications, and keep the CEO organized in a fast-paced environment. This role provides an opportunity to directly impact company efficiency, client satisfaction, and operational excellence. The successful candidate will thrive in a remote, high-responsibility role with autonomy and scope to influence workflow improvements. Key Responsibilities: - Serve as a secondary point of contact for clients via email, ensuring timely and professional communication. - Coordinate and schedule inspections with vendors, including industrial hygienists, engineers, and contractors. - Manage CEO’s calendar, appointments, and claim-related deadlines. - Track and maintain company and individual state public adjuster licenses and renewals. - Review and organize property insurance policies, estimates, and correspondence. - Assist with claim documentation, uploading reports, and maintaining organized digital files. - Support general administrative and operational needs of the company. Qualifications: Required: - Proven experience as an executive assistant or in a similar administrative role (3+ years preferred). - Excellent written and verbal communication skills. - Strong organizational skills and attention to detail. - Proficiency with Microsoft Office, Google Workspace, and CRM platforms (e.g., Pipedrive). - Ability to handle confidential information with discretion. - Self-motivated and able to manage multiple priorities with minimal supervision. Preferred: - Experience in the insurance, claims, or property restoration industry. - Familiarity with project management tools or workflows. - Prior experience supporting a CEO or senior executive in a startup or fast-paced environment.
