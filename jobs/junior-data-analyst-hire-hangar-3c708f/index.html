Job TitleJunior Data Analyst

LocationRemote

Working HoursUS Time Zone (PST - EST)

Are you eager to get started in the world of data analytics? We’re offering an exciting opportunity for a Junior Data Analyst to join our team and grow alongside experienced professionals in a fast-paced, data-driven organization.

Whether you’re a recent graduate, a career switcher, or a self-taught learner, if you’re comfortable with numbers, curious about patterns, and ready to roll up your sleeves, this role is for you.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist in collecting, cleaning, and organizing data for analysis
  • Build simple reports and visualizations to support business teams
  • Help monitor key metrics and investigate anomalies
  • Document data sources, definitions, and processes
  • Support senior analysts on ongoing analytics projects

Qualifications

  • Degree in a STEM field or equivalent experience (bootcamp, internship, self-taught)
  • Basic knowledge of Excel and an interest in SQL and BI tools
  • Strong desire to learn and grow within data analytics
  • Great attention to detail and time management
  • Effective communicator and team player

Please NOTE

It is crucial that you complete the application form in full. As part of the application process, you will be required to record a video. If your application is successful, you will receive an email confirming next steps—the video is the first step of the interview process. If you do not record a video, we will not be able to consider you for ANY open roles
