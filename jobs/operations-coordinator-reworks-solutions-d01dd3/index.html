About The Role

We looking for a proactive, organised, and personable Operations Coordinator to support its growing operations. This position combines relationship management, referral follow-ups, recruitment coordination, and onboarding administration.

The successful candidate will play an important role in building and maintaining relationships with support coordinators, following up on new and existing referrals, and helping recruit and onboard Direct Support Professionals (DSPs).

This is a great opportunity for someone with strong administrative skills, excellent communication abilities, and a natural talent for staying organised and following through on tasks. While previous experience in home care or the New Jersey Division of Developmental Disabilities (DDD) environment would be advantageous, the client is also open to training someone with solid administrative foundations who is intelligent, proactive, and quick to learn.

1. Support Coordinator Outreach & Referral Management

Make outbound calls to support coordinators to introduce services, build relationships, and generate new client referrals.

Maintain regular communication and follow up with existing support coordination contacts.

Follow up on open referrals and help move cases through the intake and service-start process.

Send emails, share relevant information, and coordinate appointments or meetings.

Maintain accurate records of outreach activities, conversations, and referral progress.

Build and maintain positive working relationships with support coordination agencies.

2. Recruitment & Applicant Coordination

Monitor and follow up with applicants through Indeed and other recruitment channels.

Contact prospective DSP candidates to assess their interest and availability.

Schedule interviews and coordinate communication between applicants and the hiring team.

Follow up with candidates who have incomplete applications or outstanding requirements.

Maintain consistent communication with applicants throughout the recruitment process.

Help ensure suitable candidates progress through the recruitment pipeline efficiently.

3. Onboarding & Compliance Administration

Follow up on outstanding applicant paperwork and onboarding documentation.

Track background checks and fingerprinting results.

Monitor required training completion and follow up on outstanding requirements.

Organise applicant files and ensure onboarding packages are complete and accurately maintained.

Keep spreadsheets and applicant records updated.

Communicate outstanding requirements to the relevant team members and candidates.

4. General Administrative & Operational Support

Assist with emails, scheduling, document management, and general administrative tasks.

Maintain accurate spreadsheets and trackers using Google Sheets and other Google Workspace tools.

Use AI tools such as Claude to assist with drafting emails, organising information, and improving administrative efficiency.

Ensure daily follow-ups and outstanding tasks are completed without requiring constant supervision.

Support additional operational responsibilities as the business grows.

Essential Requirements

Previous experience in administration, recruitment coordination, customer service, or a similar role.

Strong verbal and written English communication skills.

Confident and comfortable making outbound calls and communicating with different people.

Basic computer literacy, including email management and spreadsheet navigation.

Good working knowledge of Google Workspace, particularly Google Sheets, Google Docs, and Gmail.

Strong organisational and time-management skills.

Excellent follow-up skills and the ability to keep multiple tasks moving.

High attention to detail, particularly when updating spreadsheets and maintaining applicant records.

A proactive approach with the ability to work independently.

Quick learner who is comfortable adapting to new systems and processes.

Friendly, personable, and professional telephone manner.

Advantageous Experience

Previous experience within the home care, healthcare, or disability support industry.

Exposure to New Jersey DDD services or similar developmental disability support programmes.

Existing relationships or connections with support coordinators or support coordination agencies.

Experience working with Direct Support Professionals (DSPs), caregivers, or home care recruitment.

Previous experience using Indeed for applicant sourcing and follow-ups.

Familiarity with background checks, fingerprinting, healthcare onboarding, or compliance documentation.

Experience using AI tools such as Claude to support administrative workflows.

Software & Systems

Google Workspace (Gmail, Google Sheets, Google Docs).

Indeed (applicant communication and follow-ups).

Claude (AI-assisted administrative tasks and email drafting).

General CRM, recruitment, and administrative platforms.

The client is willing to provide training on its specific systems, processes, and workflows. Candidates must already have basic computer, email, spreadsheet, and telephone skills.

Ideal Candidate Profile

We are looking for someone who is friendly, confident, organised, and naturally proactive. This person should enjoy speaking to people, building professional relationships, and ensuring that nothing falls through the cracks.

They should be comfortable making regular calls, following up persistently but professionally, managing spreadsheets, and keeping recruitment and onboarding processes moving.

Someone with previous home care or DDD experience would be particularly valuable, especially if they already understand the support coordinator network and how referrals are generated. However, a strong administrator with excellent communication skills and the ability to learn quickly will also be considered.
