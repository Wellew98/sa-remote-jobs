New Patient Sales Representative (Sports Rehab and Chiropractic)

Location: Remote (South Africa preferred, Latin America also considered). Remote team members work Pacific Time hours.

TypeFull-time, independent contractor

ScheduleFull-time, 40 hours a week, Monday through Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Pacific

Salary$2,500 - $3,500 USD/month

Reports toSales Team

Help people get their lives back, starting with the first conversation

Some people can talk with a stranger, connect with them immediately and figure out what matters to them within a few minutes, and help them decide what to do next. If that sounds like you, and you want to use that skill somewhere it changes people's lives, keep reading.

Most of the people who reach out to us have been dealing with pain or injury for a long time. They have tried physical therapy, chiropractic, massage, rest, or pushing through it on their own, and many of them are frustrated and skeptical by the time they call.

You will be the first real conversation they have with us. Your job is to listen well, understand what they are dealing with, decide honestly whether we are the right fit, answer their questions and concerns, and help the people we can help book and attend an in-person Discovery Visit with one of our clinicians.

We want someone who is comfortable on the phone, organized, coachable, and motivated by helping people make a decision they feel good about.

This is a sales position. It comes with structure, a call framework, clear numbers, accountability, and regular coaching on your communication, objection handling, and conversion so you keep getting better.

About the company

We work with active adults, including lifters, runners, CrossFit athletes, and people who want to stay capable as they get older. Every visit is one-on-one and an hour long, and our clinicians focus on finding the root cause of the problem and building a plan to address it.

We have grown every year since opening, we are building toward several locations, and we are hiring people who want to grow with us and have a growth mindset.

Professional development is part of the job here. We are building a team of clinicians, operators, and future leaders, and we invest in the people who want to get there with us. If you want to sharpen your skills, do work that matters, and join a company with momentum and a clear direction, we would love to have a conversation with you.

The role's mission

Turn booked intro calls into qualified, booked, and attended Discovery Visits, so more of the right people get help and our clinicians spend their time with patients who are ready to solve their problem. As the first conversation most people have with us, how well you connect and guide that conversation directly affects how many people we are able to help.

What you will do

Core responsibilities

  • Run intro calls with prospective patients to learn their goals, what they cannot do right now, what they have already tried, and whether we are a good fit
  • Book qualified patients into in-person Discovery Visits
  • Call to confirm upcoming Discovery Visits and keep no-shows low
  • Reach out to anyone who cancels or misses an intro call or Discovery Visit, find out what is holding them back, and help them reschedule when it makes sense
  • Use our call framework, follow-up process, and qualification standards while keeping every conversation natural and personal

Secondary part of the job, which will be delegated upon the next hire after you

  • Make outbound calls for our masterclasses, workshops, and past-patient reactivation campaigns
  • Contact new leads quickly and book them into intro calls, whether they come from ads, referrals, social media, our website, or the phone
  • Answer the clinic phone during your hours and guide callers to the right next step
  • Follow up with people who showed interest but have not booked yet
  • Keep our CRM, tracking sheets, and patient records accurate and current so every lead is documented and moves forward

How we measure success

You will own your numbers and report them every week. The main ones are:

  • Number of intro calls
  • Number of Discovery Visits showed
  • Conversion from call to Discovery Visit

You will do well here if you

  • Have a growth mindset and want to unlock your potential
  • Are motivated by hitting your numbers and problem solving
  • Enjoy talking to people and earn their trust quickly
  • Prefer to lead a conversation instead of waiting for the other person to steer it
  • Have good sales instincts and like helping people make decisions
  • Stay calm and curious when someone pushes back
  • Care about details and follow-through
  • Learn quickly and put coaching to use right away
  • Want feedback, accountability, and a clear path to grow
  • Want a long-term career with room to move up
  • Get excited about helping a small business grow
  • Understand the goal of your role well enough to work backward from it and decide what needs to happen each day
  • Work independently and hold yourself to high standards

What we are looking for

  • Clear, confident communication on the phone
  • Strong organization across many conversations and follow-ups at once
  • Comfort learning new software and digital tools
  • Attention to detail
  • Ability to manage your own day, whether you are in the clinic or working remotely

Strongly preferred: experience in consultative sales, inside sales, membership sales, or another customer-facing role where you built rapport, followed up on leads, handled objections, and helped people commit. Gym membership sales, healthcare or wellness sales, patient intake with a conversion goal, and phone-based inside sales are all relevant. Experience with a CRM platform and Google Sheets is a plus.

What we value

  • Take ownership of your decisions, bring solutions, and act without waiting to be chased.
  • Be honest and transparent, and tell people what they need to hear to reach their health and fitness goals.
  • Expect things to change, in the world and in your role, and be willing to evolve with them.
  • Keep getting better, personally and professionally.

Why this role is different

You will get ongoing sales training and coaching here, so your communication, objection handling, and conversion skills keep improving. You will also see how marketing, sales, and patient care work together inside a growing healthcare company. As we open more locations, this role can lead to bigger opportunities in sales, marketing, operations, and leadership for the person who earns them.
