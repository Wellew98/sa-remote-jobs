About the Company: Transforming how businesses manage their cash flow. By automating the end-to-end Accounts Receivable (AR) lifecycle—from billing and invoice delivery to collection follow-ups and cash application—our platform eliminates the friction, delays, and manual errors of traditional collections.

Help finance teams get paid faster, maintain healthier working capital, and build stronger, friction-free relationships with their customers.

About the Role:  Seeking a high-performing, self-motivated Account Executive to join a remote sales team. This is a highly outbound-driven role focused on building pipeline, booking demos, and closing new business.

The ideal candidate is a proven sales closer professional who is comfortable working independently, thrives in a fast-paced environment, and can generate opportunities through consistent outbound activity. This position requires someone who can hit the ground running and contribute quickly.

Key Responsibilities

  • Outbound Prospecting: Make high-volume outbound calls daily to generate new opportunities, build targeted prospect lists, and own pipeline creation from cold outreach to qualified opportunity.
  • Demo & Closing: Conduct product demos for both outbound and inbound leads, close new business across all plan tiers, and drive high conversion rates from demo to close.
  • Lead Qualification: Identify high-quality prospects through structured discovery and focus efforts on qualified, revenue-generating opportunities.
  • CRM & Pipeline Management: Track all outreach, demos, and deals accurately in the CRM. Maintain a clean, up-to-date pipeline with consistent follow-up.

Qualifications

  • 5+ years of full-cycle B2B software sales experience.
  • 3+ years of cold calling experience.
  • Proven success selling to executives and consistently meeting sales targets.
  • Strong executive presence with excellent listening and communication skills.
  • Skilled in sales methodologies such as MEDDIC, Challenger, or Win by Design.
  • Demonstrated ability to build a pipeline from scratch and achieve consistent daily/monthly KPIs.
  • Fluent in English (slight accent acceptable).
  • Prior remote work experience is required.

Tools & Systems

  • Hubstaff: All remote team members are required to use Hubstaff for time tracking. This is non-negotiable.
  • CRM: Proficiency is required; HubSpot experience is a strong plus.

Payment & Compliance

  • This role is an independent contractor engagement.
  • Payment is made directly to the candidate; no third-party arrangements.
  • Candidates must be prepared to manage their own tax obligations.
