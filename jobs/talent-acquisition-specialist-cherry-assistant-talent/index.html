Talent Acquisition Specialist

Cherry Assistant Based in South Africa with availability to work full-time U.S. business hours (Monday–Friday). Full-time Admin & Virtual Assistance Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

We are hiring a Talent Acquisition Specialist for a fast-paced, high-volume recruiting client in the recruitment and talent acquisition industry. This is a full-time remote role for a U.S.-based company. This role is ideal for someone who is highly organized, proactive, and thrives in a performance-driven environment while supporting multiple recruiting teams and ensuring hiring goals are consistently achieved. Key Responsibilities: - Provide recruiting support to assigned markets when primary recruiters or administrators are unavailable or require additional assistance. - Manage candidate pipelines and maintain consistent follow-up with applicants throughout the hiring process. - Schedule, coordinate, and confirm interviews while ensuring an exceptional candidate experience. - Conduct high-volume outbound recruiting efforts through phone calls, text messages, and email communication. - Support interview retention by confirming candidate attendance and addressing questions or concerns before scheduled interviews. - Monitor recruiting metrics and proactively identify opportunities to improve market performance. - Maintain accurate and detailed records within the client's applicant tracking system (ATS) and recruiting databases. - Collaborate with recruiters, administrators, and market leadership to ensure hiring targets are met. - Manage calendars by scheduling interviews, sending Zoom links, responding to candidate inquiries, and confirming appointments in a timely manner. - Maintain professional communication with candidates and internal team members across multiple communication platforms. Required Qualifications: - Minimum 2+ years of experience in recruitment, talent acquisition, customer service, sales, or a related high-volume environment. - 1+ years of experience working remotely. - Fluent English with excellent verbal and written communication skills. - Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities and markets simultaneously. - Comfortable working in a fast-paced, performance-driven environment. - Based in South Africa with availability to work full-time U.S. business hours (Monday–Friday). Preferred Qualifications: - Experience using Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), CRM platforms, and recruiting software. - Comfortable using scheduling tools, Zoom, Slack, WhatsApp, Discord, and other collaboration platforms. - Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to work independently while maintaining attention to detail. - Experience supporting multiple recruiters or hiring teams simultaneously. Required Skills & Tools: - Phone / VoIP calling systems - Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) - CRM platforms for candidate tracking and pipeline management - Zoom (or similar video conferencing software) - Email and calendar management tools (Gmail, Outlook, Calendly) - Slack, WhatsApp, Discord, or similar communication platforms - Fast and accurate typing skills with the ability to update recruiting systems in real time Performance Expectations: - Maintain a minimum 50% Call List Completion Rate for assigned markets. - Maintain a minimum 50% Interview Retention Rate . - Complete a minimum of 200 outbound phone calls per day . - Maintain timely, professional communication with candidates and internal teams. - Accurately document all recruiting activity and candidate interactions. Schedule & Pay: - Full-time position; U.S. business hours (8:00 AM – 5:00 PM EST/CST/PST depending on assignment) - Fully remote role supporting a U.S.-based company - Pay ranges from R12,000 – R15,000 per month Includes: - Weekly performance incentives (up to +50% additional earnings) - Stable income and consistent hours - Annual performance reviews and career progression opportunities System Requirements: - Fibre internet connection with a minimum speed of 25 Mbps - Computer with a 2.4 GHz processor or higher - 8 GB RAM or higher - Windows 10 or newer, or Mac OS X 10.10 or newer - HD 720p webcam - Headset with a microphone Note: Technical specification verification is required before we can proceed with your application. Benefits: - Fully remote, stable full-time role - U.S.-based operational exposure - No commute and complete location flexibility - Performance-driven environment with measurable growth opportunities - Long-term career development potential - Supportive and collaborative recruiting team - Consistent candidate interaction and structured workflows If you're an organized, results-driven professional who enjoys building candidate relationships, managing recruiting pipelines, and supporting high-performing hiring teams, we'd love to hear from you. Apply today and become part of a recruitment team operating at scale.
