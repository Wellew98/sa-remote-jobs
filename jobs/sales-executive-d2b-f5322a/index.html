ROLE OVERVIEW

As a Sales Executive, you will be responsible for driving new customer acquisition for the company.

This is a front-end sales role where you will take ownership of the entire sales process, from identifying potential clients and making the first approach through to arranging sales meetings, presenting our services, handling objections, closing deals and managing the initial onboarding process.

You'll be targeting contractors and construction-related businesses across the UK, identifying those who could benefit from having a dedicated outsourced business development team working on their behalf.

You'll need to be comfortable picking up the telephone, sending targeted emails, networking through LinkedIn and proactively approaching business owners and directors.

Your objective is straightforward

identify businesses that need help winning more work, demonstrate how our services can help them and convert those opportunities into paying clients.

This isn't a passive sales role. We want someone who is hungry for new business, enjoys speaking to decision-makers, follows up consistently and takes pride in closing deals.

You'll be supported by the wider company team, but you'll be responsible for managing your own sales pipeline and driving your own results.

RESPONSIBILITIES

1. Prospecting & Identifying New Clients

●       Research and identify potential new clients within the UK construction industry.

●       Build targeted prospect lists of contractors, subcontractors, specialist trades and construction-related businesses.

●       Identify business owners, directors and senior decision-makers.

●       Use LinkedIn, company websites, construction directories and other research tools to identify suitable prospects.

●       Understand each prospect's business, services, market position and potential need for outsourced business development.

●       Proactively build and maintain a pipeline of potential clients.

2. Outbound Sales & Lead Generation

●       Make consistent outbound telephone calls to prospective clients.

●       Send targeted cold emails introducing the company and explaining the benefits of our service.

●       Use LinkedIn to connect with business owners, directors and other relevant decision-makers.

●       Build relationships with prospective clients through consistent communication and follow-up.

●       Generate interest in the company's outsourced business development service.

●       Identify businesses experiencing challenges with winning new work, developing relationships or maintaining a consistent sales pipeline.

●       Convert initial interest into qualified sales conversations.

3. Booking & Conducting Sales Meetings

●       Arrange telephone and online meetings with prospective clients.

●       Understand their current business development arrangements, commercial objectives and growth ambitions.

●       Explain how the company operates and how our team would work as an extension of their business.

●       Present the benefits of having dedicated outsourced business development support.

●       Explain our approach to prospecting, relationship building, introductions and generating new work opportunities.

●       Demonstrate the value of our services through relevant examples and case studies.

●       Handle questions, concerns and objections confidently.

4. Closing New Business

●       Follow up on proposals and sales conversations.

●       Maintain consistent communication with prospective clients throughout the sales process.

●       Handle commercial discussions and explain pricing and contractual arrangements.

●       Overcome objections and help prospects understand the value of investing in business development.

●       Secure signed agreements and confirm new client commitments.

●       Follow opportunities through to successful payment and onboarding.

●       Maintain ownership of your sales pipeline until the client has successfully joined the company.

5. Client Onboarding & Handover

●       Coordinate the initial onboarding process for new clients.

●       Collect relevant company information, service details, capabilities and target market requirements.

●       Ensure agreements and initial payments are completed.

●       Arrange introductions and handovers to the Business Development Account Management team.

●       Ensure the client has a clear understanding of what happens next and how their service will operate.

●       Maintain a professional relationship with newly signed clients throughout the transition.

6. Sales Reporting & Performance

●       Maintain accurate records of prospecting, calls, emails, LinkedIn activity and sales conversations.

●       Manage your pipeline using our internal CRM and tracking systems.

●       Monitor conversion rates from initial contact through to signed agreements.

●       Provide regular sales activity and performance updates.

●       Work towards agreed targets for prospecting, appointments, proposals and new client acquisitions.

●       Take responsibility for developing your own pipeline and achieving your sales objectives.

QUALIFICATIONS

We're looking for someone who is naturally confident, commercially minded and motivated by bringing new business into a growing company.

You should enjoy speaking to people, be comfortable approaching businesses you have never spoken to before and have the persistence to follow opportunities through to a successful sale.

You don't necessarily need construction industry experience, as training will be provided. However, previous experience in B2B sales, telesales or business development would be beneficial.

Essential Skills & Attributes

●       Confident in making outbound cold calls.

●       Excellent verbal and written communication skills.

●       Comfortable speaking with business owners, directors and senior decision-makers.

●       Confident presenting and selling a professional business service.

●       Strong objection-handling and negotiation skills.

●       Persistent and proactive when following up sales opportunities.

●       Organised and able to manage multiple prospects and sales conversations.

●       Target-driven with a genuine desire to achieve and exceed sales targets.

●       Comfortable using LinkedIn, email, telephone and CRM systems.

●       Able to work independently and take ownership of sales performance.

●       Professional, personable and confident representing the company.

Desirable Experience

●       Previous B2B sales experience.

●       Experience in telesales, outbound sales or appointment setting.

●       Experience selling professional services or business-to-business solutions.

●       Experience dealing with company directors and business owners.

●       Knowledge of the UK construction industry.

Commission

In addition to your basic salary, you'll receive commission for every new client you successfully bring into the business. We will pay a commission of £100.00 per sale and we will also provide a commission ladder where you will receive further bonuses dependent on the number of sales made.
