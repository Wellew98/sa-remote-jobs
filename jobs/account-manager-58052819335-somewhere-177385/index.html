Account Manager (B2B Client Retention)

Location:  Remote

Employment Type:  Full-Time Contractor

Working Hours:  EST or PST (flexible alignment with North American clients)

Compensation:  Competitive base salary + variable compensation

About the Role

We are seeking proactive, relationship-driven Account Managers to support and grow our portfolio of B2B travel partners across North America.

This is not a new business / hunter role — your primary focus will be retention, relationship management, and revenue expansion within existing accounts. You will serve as the main point of contact for agency partners, ensuring a high-quality experience while identifying opportunities to drive repeat bookings.

While this is a retention-focused role, a strong  passion for sales is essential . Without that drive, it will be difficult to succeed as an Account Manager in our environment, as the role requires actively growing accounts and increasing revenue.

This role is ideal for someone who thrives in a fast-paced, client-facing environment and understands the dynamics of a B2B sales cycle.

Key Responsibilities

Client Relationship Management

  • Manage and nurture relationships with existing B2B travel partners (agencies, resellers, etc.)
  • Serve as the primary point of contact for day-to-day communication
  • Build strong rapport through consistent, proactive engagement

Revenue Retention & Expansion

  • Drive repeat bookings through outbound calls and email outreach
  • Identify upsell and cross-sell opportunities within existing accounts
  • Monitor account performance and take action to improve engagement

Communication & Outreach

  • Conduct regular check-ins with partners via phone and email
  • Respond promptly to inquiries, requests, and issues
  • Maintain a high-touch, service-oriented communication style

Pipeline & Activity Management

  • Track all partner interactions and opportunities in CRM systems
  • Maintain organized records of account activity and performance
  • Meet activity and revenue targets consistently

What We’re Looking For

Experience

  • 2+ years in Account Management, Customer Success, or B2B Sales
  • Experience working with North American clients (US/Canada) strongly preferred
  • Background in travel, hospitality, or tourism is a plus

Skills

  • Strong understanding of the B2B sales cycle and retention strategies
  • Excellent verbal and written English communication
  • Confident in outbound outreach (calls + email)
  • Highly organized with strong follow-up discipline

Traits

  • Relationship-first mindset (farmer vs. hunter mentality)
  • Proactive and self-motivated in a remote environment
  • Results-driven with a focus on revenue retention
  • Comfortable managing multiple accounts simultaneously
  • Passion for sales and growth — someone who enjoys driving revenue, not just maintaining relationships

Success Metrics

  • Client retention rate
  • Repeat booking volume
  • Revenue growth within assigned accounts
  • Outreach activity (calls/emails)
  • Client satisfaction / responsiveness

Why Join

  • Fully remote, globally distributed team
  • Established North American client base
  • Clear performance-driven upside through variable compensation
  • Opportunity to grow within a scaling travel organization
