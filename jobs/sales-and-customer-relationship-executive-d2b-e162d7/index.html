ABOUT THE COMPANY

The company is a prestigious UK specialist joinery and restoration business serving discerning homeowners, alongside commercial clients such as builders and developers. Renowned for uncompromising quality, heritage restoration, and sustainability—with a client portfolio that even includes the British Royal Family—the company delivers a white-glove, top-tier service from initial enquiry through to project completion.

ROLE OVERVIEW

We are seeking a consultative, high-ownership

Sales and Customer Relationship Executive

to take control of warm inbound customer enquiries and a substantial pipeline of outstanding quotations. While the field sales team focuses on large-scale commercial and residential contracts, your primary mission will be driving the consistent conversion of domestic projects—particularly window refurbishment, restoration, and bespoke joinery (typically valued at a few thousand pounds per project). These smaller, high-margin jobs are vital to maintaining steady cash flow and keeping specialist joiners booked between major builds.

Working exclusively with warm leads (enquiry joiner site visit/measure team prepares quote you follow up and close), you will build genuine trust with affluent homeowners, guide them through considered purchasing decisions, and confidently close deals over the phone and email without sounding scripted or pushy.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Inbound Qualification & Triage

Respond promptly to warm incoming enquiries, uncover the customer’s project scope, qualify the opportunity, and determine the appropriate next step.

Appointment Coordination

Schedule and manage site visits for specialist joiners to measure, assess, and scope domestic projects.

Consultative Quote Follow-Up & Closing

Proactively follow up on prepared quotations by phone and email. Walk homeowners through proposals clearly, articulate the value of bespoke craftsmanship over cheaper alternatives, handle objections, and confidently ask for the sale.

Pipeline & Backlog Revival

Systematically re-engage older enquiries and stalled quotations to determine project status, overcome hesitation, and secure a clear next action or confirmed booking.

Equal-Opportunity Ownership

Give smaller domestic jobs (e.g., single-window restorations around £2,500) the exact same urgency, care, and attention as larger projects, ensuring no revenue is left on the table.

CRM & Pipeline Hygiene

Maintain immaculate CRM records, contact histories, pipeline stages, and follow-up reminders so every live opportunity has a scheduled next step—eliminating guesswork and "it will land next week" updates.

Internal Collaboration & Handover

Partner closely with leadership (Gary and Rob) and technical colleagues to escalate complex pricing/technical queries, share customer feedback, and ensure seamless handovers once deposits/bookings are secured.

Proven track record in telephone sales, warm lead conversion, or consultative B2C sales —specifically demonstrating the ability to chase quotes, overcome hesitation, and close deals rather than just booking appointments.

Exceptional spoken and written English

with a warm, articulate, and polished communication style suited to affluent, discerning UK homeowners.

Strong consultative selling skills

the ability to build rapid, natural rapport, listen actively, ask insightful questions, and ask for a decision calmly and confidently.

High levels of personal accountability, self-management, and pipeline discipline, with proven proficiency in using

CRM platforms

and adapting to new job management systems.

A r

eliable, quiet remote home-office setup with high-speed internet and full availability to work UK business hours.

Preferred

Hands-On Trade Background

Practical experience working "on the tools" (e.g., as a joiner, carpenter, decorator, or tradesperson) who has transitioned into a telephone-based sales or customer-facing role.

Industry-Specific Sales Experience

Background in home improvements, heritage restoration, bespoke windows/doors, kitchens, decorating, or comparable high-ticket, quoted domestic services.

Luxury & High-Net-Worth Client Exposure

Experience representing high-end, prestigious brands and building authentic rapport with wealthy consumers who value quality over the lowest price.

Proven experience reviving dormant pipelines and guiding homeowners through multi-stage, considered purchases.

What You’ll Bring

Ownership Over Excuses

You don’t blame lead quality or let quotes gather dust; you take personal responsibility for working every warm opportunity through to a definitive yes or no.

Value-First Storytelling

An authentic appreciation for traditional craftsmanship and sustainable restoration, enabling you to justify premium pricing with confidence.

Consultative Patience Paired with Commercial Drive

You never use high-pressure tactics, yet you are unafraid to address objections head-on and guide a hesitant homeowner across the finish line.

Adaptability

Comfort navigating evolving internal systems while learning the nuances of UK joinery and restoration terminology alongside a supportive team.
