ISTA Personnel Solutions

South Africa is a dynamic and fast-growing BPO company based in South Africa, specialising in providing top-tier operational support to U.S.-based companies. We are not a recruitment agency. We act as a dedicated extension of our clients’ teams, delivering high-quality service with precision, efficiency, and a personal touch.

We’re looking for a

Recruiter to join our client’s team, a U.S.-based ABA therapy provider in the healthcare space. They need a high-volume RBT Recruiter to build and support their clinical teams.

** This is a salaried position with a basic salary only. There is no commission or incentive structure.**

You’ll

source, screen and hire Behavior Technicians (BTs) and Registered Behavior Technicians (RBTs) across multiple locations and states. You’ll work mostly in the applicant tracking system (ATS) to move candidates through the full recruitment cycle , from application to hire. This includes candidate sourcing, interview scheduling, video interviewing and onboarding handover , and building a steady talent pipeline .

Success is measured on both

hiring and retention , so every hire needs to be a strong, lasting fit for the children and families they’ll support.

Who will thrive here

You’re organised, detail-oriented, and highly driven. You are comfortable managing a large volume of candidates and competing priorities, and you assess people quickly on both phone and video. You give every candidate a positive, responsive experience from application to onboarding.

PLEASE NOTE

Working Hours

Monday – Friday, 4:00pm – 12:00am SAST (subject to daylight saving time).

Public Holidays

You will work on all South African public holidays. Compensated in line with the BCEA.

Internet Requirements

A fixed fibre line with a minimum speed of 25 Mbps (upload & download) and the ability to support a wired Ethernet connection is mandatory. Applicants without a fixed fibre line cannot be considered.

Power Backup

A reliable power backup solution is required to manage load shedding and power outages . Applicants without a power backup cannot be considered.

Work Environment

Fully remote

(you must be located in South Africa)

What you’ll do

Run

full-cycle, high-volume recruitment for BT and RBT roles, from sourcing and interviewing through to offer and onboarding handover.

Manage the whole candidate flow in the

ATS

, keeping records, notes and statuses accurate and up to date.

Source candidates

through Indeed, LinkedIn and social media. Build a steady pipeline, including students, recent graduates and career changers (for example in psychology and education).

Conduct

video interviews to assess skills, availability and fit, and make hire or no-hire decisions using the interview rubric.

Match new hires to open cases using

HubSpot

, working with the clinical and operations teams on skills, location, preferences and availability.

Set offer rates within the approved

salary guidelines and pay matrix and send offer letters through the ATS.

Work with the training and onboarding teams so new hires, including BTs working towards

RBT certification

, settle in smoothly.

Give candidates a

positive, responsive experience from application to onboarding.

Keep up to date with

RBT certification requirements and best practice in ABA recruiting .

Send the Recruiting Manager a

weekly metrics report by Friday end of day, with a pipeline update.

Targets

(subject to change with business needs)

At least

6 virtual interviews a day , with a target of 8.

At least

10 qualified BTs/RBTs signed each week , including at least 7 RBTs, matched to open cases.

90% of signed offers converting to a start date.

90% retention at 30 days and 85% at 90 days .

What we’re looking for

Grade 12 (matric) certificate.

Proven success in full-cycle, high-volume recruitment.

Hands-on experience managing high candidate volumes in an ATS, and the ability to pick up new tools quickly.

Experience sourcing on Indeed, LinkedIn and social media.

Strong phone and video interviewing skills, with the ability to assess a candidate’s skills quickly.

Highly organised and detail-oriented, able to manage a large volume of candidates and competing priorities in a deadline-driven environment.

Proficiency in Microsoft Office or Google Workspace, video interviewing platforms and recruitment reporting.

Fluent, professional English.

Beneficial

Experience with HubSpot, Indeed Employer or Workable.

Experience recruiting in ABA, healthcare or clinical settings.

Knowledge of RBT certification requirements.

If you are not contacted within 14 working days, please consider your application unsuccessful.
