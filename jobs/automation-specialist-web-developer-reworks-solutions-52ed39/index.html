About The Role

We are looking for an experienced Automation Specialist to join our client's team and play a key role in transforming and improving the organisation's operational processes through technology, automation, and intelligent system integration.

This is a hands-on technical role with significant strategic impact. The successful candidate will take ownership of the organisation's Monday.com CRM environment, designing, building, and maintaining advanced workflows, automations, dashboards, and integrations using Make.com.

The role will also involve integrating multiple platforms, exploring AI-driven solutions, supporting web development and maintenance, and identifying opportunities to eliminate manual and repetitive processes.

The ideal candidate is a proactive self-starter with at least 4 years of advanced Monday.com experience, strong Make.com integration capabilities, and the ability to translate business requirements into practical, scalable technical solutions.

Key Responsibilities

1. Monday.com CRM Management

Design, build, configure, and optimise Monday.com boards, workflows, dashboards, and reporting structures.

Manage and maintain the organisation's Monday.com CRM environment.

Work with operational teams and executive leadership to understand business requirements and translate them into effective CRM configurations.

Improve data quality, consistency, reporting, and visibility across the organisation.

Take ownership of the DOE and ELC Monday.com boards.

Facilitate Monday.com setup and configuration for state expansion projects.

Train and support employees in using Monday.com effectively.

2. Automation & Workflow Development

Design, build, and maintain sophisticated automation scenarios using Make.com.

Identify manual, repetitive, and inefficient processes and develop automation solutions.

Troubleshoot, maintain, and optimise existing automations to ensure reliability.

Integrate multiple platforms and systems to streamline information and data flows.

Implement appropriate testing, error handling, and monitoring practices.

Maintain clear and detailed documentation of automation workflows and system configurations.

3. API & Platform Integrations

Integrate Monday.com with various third-party platforms and business tools.

Develop and maintain integrations with platforms including

Illuminate

Inflow

Telego

Gmail and Google Workspace

Slack

CRMs and databases

Support additional integrations such as JXT and LeadTrap.

Manage email and SMS-related integrations and workflows.

4. AI Integration & Utilisation

Explore and implement opportunities to incorporate AI tools into existing business processes.

Integrate Claude and other relevant AI platforms into Monday.com workflows.

Identify opportunities to use AI to improve efficiency, automate tasks, and enhance operational processes.

5. Website Development & Maintenance

Update, maintain, and support the organisation's website: LighthouseCH.com.

Assist with website integrations and related technical requirements.

Work with external development partners and oversee ongoing website maintenance requirements.

Take responsibility for ongoing maintenance once Bitbean has completed its development work.

6. Projects & Continuous Improvement

Lead and facilitate Monday.com state expansion setup projects.

Take ownership of the DOE and ELC Monday.com boards.

Collaborate with internal stakeholders to understand evolving business needs.

Proactively recommend system improvements and scalable automation solutions.

Stay up to date with Monday.com, Make.com, API integration, and AI developments.

Minimum Requirements

Minimum of 4+ years of hands-on Monday.com experience , including advanced board configuration, automations, dashboards, and workflow management.

Demonstrated experience building and managing integrations using Make.com.

Experience integrating multiple platforms, such as Gmail, Google Workspace, Slack, CRMs, and databases.

Strong understanding of APIs, data flows, system logic, and process design.

Experience troubleshooting complex automations and resolving integration issues.

Ability to manage multiple technical projects and priorities independently.

Strong documentation, organisational, and analytical skills.

Excellent problem-solving and critical-thinking abilities.

Preferred Personality Traits

Proactive self-starter who takes ownership and accountability.

Comfortable working with ambiguity and evolving business requirements.

Innovative and solution-oriented, with a passion for improving processes through technology.

Clear, empathetic, and confident communicator.

Collaborative team player who can also work independently.

Highly organised and detail-oriented.

Adaptable and eager to learn new technologies.

Motivated by using technology to support a meaningful organisational mission.
