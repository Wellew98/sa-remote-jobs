About The Role

We are looking for an experienced Automation Specialist to join our client's team and play a key role in transforming and improving the organisation's operational processes through technology, automation, and intelligent system integration.

This is a hands-on technical role with significant strategic impact. The successful candidate will take ownership of the organisation's Monday.com CRM environment, designing, building, and maintaining advanced workflows, automations, dashboards, and integrations using Make.com.

The role will also involve integrating multiple platforms, exploring AI-driven solutions, supporting web development and maintenance, and identifying opportunities to eliminate manual and repetitive processes.

The ideal candidate is a proactive self-starter with at least 4 years of advanced Monday.com experience, strong Make.com integration capabilities, and the ability to translate business requirements into practical, scalable technical solutions.

Key Responsibilities

What you'll do

Automate operational workflows

Find manual, repetitive steps in how our teams work and replace them with automations in Make.

Build, test and maintain Make scenarios, including error handling and monitoring so they keep running.

Troubleshoot and improve existing automations when something breaks or slows down.

Build and run Monday.com

Set up and improve Monday.com boards, workflows, dashboards and reports.

Keep data clean and consistent so leadership can see what's happening across the business.

Help set up Monday.com for each new state we expand into, and look after key operational boards.

Show colleagues how to use Monday.com well, and make boards easy for them to use.

Connect our systems

Link Monday.com with tools like Gmail, Google Workspace, Slack and our other business platforms.

Support email and SMS workflows and other third-party integrations.

Improve continuously

Look for ways to use AI tools, such as Claude, to save time and improve processes.

Write clear documentation of every workflow and configuration you build.

Support small updates to our website alongside our external developers.

Work with operations staff and leadership to turn their needs into practical system changes.

What you need

1+ year of hands-on experience building workflow automations or business systems.

Familiarity with an automation platform

such as Make, Zapier, n8n or similar.

A solid understanding of operations

: how work, requests and data move through a business, and where they get stuck.

A logic and sequencing mindset. No coding is needed, but you should enjoy working out triggers, conditions and steps.

Clear written and spoken English, and the ability to document your work.

Nice to have

Monday.com experience: boards, automations, dashboards or CRM setup.

An eye for UX/UI, so the boards and forms you build are simple for colleagues to use.

Some understanding of APIs, webhooks and data flows.

Experience using AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT or similar) in business workflows.

Exposure to any of the other tools listed below, or to website content management.

Tools you'll work with

Core

Monday.com, Make

Workplace

Gmail, Google Workspace, Slack

Business platforms

Illuminate, Inflow, Telego, JXT, LeadTrap

Other

Claude and other AI tools, email and SMS platforms, website content management tools
