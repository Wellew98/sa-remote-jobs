Company Description

Vantage is a globally recognized multi-asset brokerage firm headquartered in Sydney, operating under the oversight of reputable regulatory authorities. With a workforce of over 1,000 employees spread across more than 30 offices worldwide, we bring over 15 years of industry experience to our clients. We offer trading opportunities in CFDs for Forex, Commodities, Indices, Shares, and Cryptocurrencies. Our commitment is to provide a robust trading environment, complemented by a fast and user-friendly trading platform, enabling our clients to capitalize on trading opportunities efficiently.

To learn more about our company, please visit our website

Job Description

Key Responsibilities

  • Seeking for new potential Introducing Broker (IB) or client
  • Researching, planning, and implementing new target market initiatives (South Africa)
  • Need to be active to call customers regarding product problems, service questions and general client concerns
  • Follow up and maintain customer relationships
  • Provide feedback on the efficiency of the customer service process
  • Handle and resolve customer complaints
  • Achieve the best in sales target and KPI

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in any fields or higher.
  • Professionals with early to mid-level experience
  • Working experience in stock, securities, banking, insurance, wealth management is preferred
  • Knowledge of Stock, Securities, Forex market, Meta trader 4 will be an advantage
  • Good communication skills. Good service mind and good customer handing skill.
  • Able to work under pressure
  • Sales-oriented and excellent interpersonal skill
  • Good attitude and team player
