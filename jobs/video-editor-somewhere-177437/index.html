Video Editor - Motion Graphics & L&D Production

Full-Time | Remote

Salary$2,000 – $2,500 per month

About the Role

We are hiring a talented Video Editor with strong motion graphics skills to support the production of high-quality Learning & Development (L&D) content. This role focuses on creating short-form educational video courses designed to teach practical, real-world skills.

This is not a basic editing role — you will be responsible for visually translating learning concepts into engaging, easy-to-understand content through thoughtful motion design and structured storytelling.

Key Responsibilities

  • Edit and produce short-form educational videos using provided scripts, storyboards, presenter footage (AI or recorded), and assets
  • Design and animate motion graphics that enhance comprehension and engagement
  • Build clear, step-by-step tutorial sequences demonstrating tools and software
  • Ensure all deliverables align with brand guidelines and visual standards
  • Source and integrate stock images and supporting visuals effectively
  • Maintain consistency in layout, spacing, symmetry, and safe zones
  • Track production time per video to support planning and delivery timelines
  • Collaborate asynchronously with the production team and communicate progress proactively

Required Skills & Experience

  • Strong motion graphics and animation skills (ability to bring concepts to life visually)
  • Proficiency in Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects , or equivalent tools
  • Strong design sensibility (layout, spacing, hierarchy, and visual clarity)
  • Experience editing educational, tutorial, or instructional video content
  • Ability to work efficiently while maintaining high-quality output
  • Excellent English proficiency (written and comprehension)
  • Self-directed, reliable, and comfortable working in a remote environment

Nice to Have

  • Experience with Learning & Development (L&D) or e-learning content
  • Familiarity with stock image/video platforms
  • Experience working with structured production workflows

Working Style & Expectations

  • Operate with a high level of ownership and accountability
  • Translate written scripts and learning objectives into effective visual storytelling
  • Deliver polished, accurate work with strong attention to detail
  • Communicate clearly and proactively in a remote, async environment

Schedule & Commitment

  • Full-time, Monday–Friday
  • Must have reliable overlap with 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM Eastern Time
  • Overtime available as needed
  • Occasional company travel (fully covered)
  • Long-term opportunity with potential for growth

Growth Opportunity

This role offers the opportunity to grow into broader production responsibilities as the L&D function scales. Ideal for candidates who want to be part of building and shaping a high-performing content production team.

Application Requirements

  • Portfolio showcasing motion graphics, tutorial, or educational video work
  • Brief explanation of your editing process from brief to final delivery
  • Short note on what quality means in your work
