We are currently recruiting a proactive, high-trust Executive Virtual Assistant on behalf of an Executive Director of Professional Learning at a leading public school district.

Our client oversees district-wide educator development and currently operates without internal administrative support. They are looking for a dedicated remote partner to manage critical operational, clerical, and documentation workflows. By taking full ownership of administrative mechanics, you will directly enable executive leadership to focus on high-impact strategic initiatives.

Responsibilities

- Data Entry & Catalog Maintenance:  Keep the district professional learning course catalog up-to-date and accurately log inbound conference and training submissions.

- Document Design & Formatting:  Polish and standardize executive materials, training catalogs, meeting agendas, and presentations for leadership and district staff.

- Pipeline & Deadline Tracking:  Manage the lifecycle of conference approval requests, proactively track down missing documentation, and enforce key timelines.

- Digital Asset Organization:  Maintain a structured, intuitive shared drive system for approved resources,executive templates, and active action items.

- Routine Communication Support:  Draft concise email correspondence, issue scheduled reminders to educators and administrators, and ensure timely follow-ups.

What We Are Looking For

- Tool Proficiency:  Strong, practical command of Google Workspace (Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides) and Microsoft 365, with solid capability in Microsoft Excel (formulas, filtering, structured data organization).

- Uncompromising Discretion:  Proven experience managing confidential personnel, professional, and budgetary details with absolute integrity.

- Independent Initiative:  A self-starter mindset capable of managing daily tasks seamlessly without constant supervision.

- Punctuality & Precision:  Strict respect for deadlines, quick turnaround times, and high attention to detail in written materials and spreadsheets.

- Professional Communication:  Polished, courteous written communication suitable for public education executives and administrative teams.

Why Join Assist World?

NO TRACKER. NO PROBLEM

Important Hiring Note / Eligibility:  We are a remote-first team, but due to international payment regulations, we can only hire candidates who can receive payments via  Wise (SA, UK, US & PH) . Before applying, please ensure Wise operates and supports local bank transfers in your country of residence/bank location.

Regrettably, we cannot proceed with applications from regions where Wise services are unavailable.
