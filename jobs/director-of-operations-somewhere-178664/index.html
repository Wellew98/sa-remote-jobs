Director of Operations

Somewhere Remote (Latin America or South Africa preferred) Full-time Admin & Virtual Assistance Open to South Africa
Apply on Employer Site →

Role Overview Our fast-paced, results-oriented, and strategic PR agency is seeking a high-caliber Director of Operations to serve as the operational backbone of our organization. This is an executive-level leadership role designed for a "systems-first" thinker who can manage the agency’s infrastructure with minimal oversight. As the Director of Operations, you will bridge the gap between vision and execution. You won't just manage tasks; you will own the agency’s operational cadence, refine our systems, and lead team accountability. We are looking for a proactive partner who reduces the CEO's operational load by anticipating needs two steps ahead and solving problems before they escalate. Position Details - Employment Type: Full-Time Independent Contractor - Compensation: Market Related - Hours: 40 hours/week (Fixed Schedule: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM EST) - Location: Remote (Latin America or South Africa preferred) - Reporting To: Founder & CEO Core Responsibilities - Executive Support & Gatekeeping - Calendar & Time Protection: Manage the CEO's calendar, prioritizing requests, resolving conflicts, and actively protecting strategic working time. - Inbound Triage: Triage email and meeting requests to flag urgent items requiring direct CEO attention while autonomously handling, delegating, or deferring the rest. - Event & Logistics Coordination: Coordinate all logistics for external commitments (e.g., speaking engagements, media interviews) to ensure the CEO arrives fully briefed and prepared. - Team & Self-Accountability & Workflow - Operational Cadence: Own daily Asana management, tracking deadlines, and ensuring team members stay on schedule. - Proactive Accountability: Lead weekly team check-ins, identify bottlenecks, and resolve capacity risks. Demonstrate rigorous self-accountability by proactively flagging your own at-risk deadlines before they are missed, including end-of-day status updates whenever a deliverable is running behind schedule. - Deliverable Standards: Ensure all final work products (reports, documents, decks) are delivered complete, proofed, and ready to use with zero rework required. Clearly mark in-progress work as such—never presenting drafts as final. - SOP Development: Audit existing processes and build/refine Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for onboarding, reporting, and internal communications. - New Business & Research Support - Prospect Intelligence: Conduct deep-dive research into target brands (funding, decision-makers, current PR agency relationships) to build a prospect library. - Meeting Prep: Generate one-page pre-call briefs and polish pitch decks/capability presentations using existing templates. - Pipeline Management: Manage post-meeting follow-ups and lead the transition from signed contract to client onboarding. - Vendor & Financial Management - Vendor Relations: Research, vet, and negotiate terms with US-based vendors and contractors; maintain organized contract records. - Budget & Financial Oversight: Process timecards, invoices, and expense reports weekly, flagging discrepancies. Drive proactive budget management by tracking spend against target budgets and actively identifying opportunities to streamline operations or reduce agency costs. - HR & Hiring Support - Talent Acquisition: Write job descriptions, screen resumes, and conduct first-round candidate screenings to streamline hiring for the CEO. - Onboarding: Manage documentation and system access for all new contractors. Candidate Profile - Hard Skills & Qualifications - Experience: 5+ years in Operations, Executive Assistance, or Account/Project Management within a PR, communications, or marketing agency strongly preferred. Direct familiarity with media/press cycle deadlines and high-caliber client-facing deliverable standards is a major differentiator. - Track Record: Demonstrated history of meeting tight deadlines consistently without requiring reminders, hand-holding, or follow-up. - AI-Augmented, Not AI-Dependent: Expert-level proficiency in Asana (or similar PM tools) and AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT) for research and drafting. Must treat all AI output strictly as a first draft—independently verifying all facts, dates, tool references, and figures before presenting work. - US Context: Prior experience supporting US-based clients or businesses remotely is strongly preferred. - Communication: Exceptional written and verbal English; ability to synthesize complex research into actionable summaries. - Logistics: Reliable high-speed internet and a dedicated, professional home workspace. - Soft Skills (The "Must-Haves") - Executive-Level Thinking: The ability to operate independently and make confident decisions within your scope. - Proactive Ownership: You bring solutions to problems, not just observations. - Direct Communication: High emotional intelligence paired with the ability to hold team members accountable professionally. - Intellectual Curiosity: A natural drive to research US industry events, media landscapes, and competitive benchmarks without being prompted. What to Expect - High Autonomy: We provide the tools and context; we expect you to run the engine. - Structured Success: Structured onboarding with formal review check-ins at 30, 60, and 90 days featuring clear go/no-go criteria based on deliverable quality and communication cadence. - Consistent Rhythm: Daily async updates via Asana and weekly video syncs with the CEO. Why This Role? This is a rare opportunity for a top-tier operator in Latin America or South Africa to take a seat at the leadership table of a fast-paced, strategic US agency. You will have a direct impact on the scale and efficiency of a high-performing firm representing some of the most exciting brands in the beauty industry.
