About the Role

At Pavago, one of our clients is hiring a Real Estate Transaction Coordinator / Real Estate Specialist to manage real estate transactions, listings, documentation, and compliance from accepted offer through closing .

This is a hands-on, detail-intensive role for someone who understands real estate workflows and can keep multiple transactions moving simultaneously without missing critical deadlines.

You’ll work closely with agents, brokers, buyers, sellers, lenders, title companies, inspectors, and other stakeholders to ensure every transaction remains organized, compliant, and on schedule .

If you have hands-on experience with

MLS platforms, DocuSign, transaction management systems, and real estate documentation , this role is a strong fit.

What a Typical Day Looks Like

You may start by reviewing your active transaction pipeline and checking upcoming contingency and closing deadlines.

From there, you might update an MLS listing, request a missing disclosure, coordinate an inspection, send documents through DocuSign, follow up with a lender or title company, update an agent on closing progress, or prepare a new listing for syndication.

Throughout the day, you’ll keep transaction files organized, communicate with stakeholders, and make sure every deal continues moving toward closing.

In short

you keep real estate transactions organized, compliant, and on track from contract to close.
