ISTA Personnel Solutions

South Africa is a dynamic and fast-growing BPO company based in South Africa, specialising in providing top-tier operational support to U.S.-based companies. We are not a recruitment agency, we act as a dedicated extension of our clients’ teams, delivering high-quality service with precision, efficiency, and a personal touch.

We’re looking for a

Recruitment & Onboarding Coordinator to join our clients team who is a U.S.-based ABA therapy provider . They are looking for a detail-driven individual who will need to ensure that newly hired clinicians are fully credentialed, compliant and set up in the company’s systems before their first day. You’ll work with the recruitment, clinical, compliance and scheduling teams to keep onboarding moving without delays.

Who will thrive here

You’re organised, detail-oriented, and proactive. You follow up until the job is done, and you’re comfortable chasing outstanding documents by phone and email. You have a professional, patient phone manner, and you stay on top of multiple tasks in a deadline-driven environment.

PLEASE NOTE

Working Hours

Monday – Friday, 4:00pm – 1:00am SAST (subject to daylight saving time).

Public Holidays

You will work on all South African public holidays. Compensated in line with the BCEA.

Internet Requirements

A fixed fibre line with a minimum speed of 25 Mbps (upload & download) and the ability to support a wired Ethernet connection is mandatory. Applicants without a fixed fibre line cannot be considered.

Power Backup

A reliable power backup solution is required to manage load shedding and power outages . Applicants without a power backup cannot be considered.

Work Environment

Fully remote

(you must be located in South Africa)

Responsibilities

Manage the

full onboarding process for new hires, from offer acceptance through to being fully cleared and ready for scheduling .

Track and follow up on

background checks and confirm the results are clear before moving a hire forward.

Collect and verify

employment documents , including work eligibility (I-9) documents, professional certifications, and liability insurance .

Verify

RBT and BCBA certification numbers and expiry dates with the certification board.

Create and maintain

employee profiles across HR, payroll, clinical, and time-tracking systems.

Prepare and send

contracts and offer documents and confirm they are signed.

Follow up by email and phone

on outstanding items until onboarding is complete.

Track every new hire’s progress in an onboarding tracker , updating statuses promptly.

Act on

case coordination emails , updating employee status and calendars once a start of care date is confirmed.

Review

timesheet discrepancies each payroll period and follow up with employees.

Support

offboarding by closing accounts and arranging equipment returns.

Review

inbound resumes , screen against role requirements, and schedule interviews .

Act as the

link between recruitment, compliance, clinical, and scheduling teams to meet hiring timelines and prevent onboarding delays.

Requirements

Experience in an administrative, HR, recruitment or compliance-style roles.

Strong verbal and written

English communication skills , with a professional and confident phone manner.

Excellent

attention to detail and accuracy when entering data across multiple system s.

Strong

organisation , and follow-up skills, whilst manage multiple tasks in a deadline-driven environment.

Comfortable working

across multiple systems and learning new ones quickly.

Good

judgement when applying set rules and processes .

Professional and discreet when handling

confidential employee information .

Proficiency in

MS Office and Outlook .

Beneficial

Experience with Paycom, CentralReach, Monday.com or similar HR, payroll and workflow systems.

Knowledge of US employment verification (I-9) and healthcare compliance.

A background in healthcare or ABA therapy.

If you are not contacted within 14 working days, please consider your application unsuccessful.
