Frequently Asked Questions

Are these jobs really available to South Africans?

Yes. Every listing on our board is verified to accept applicants based in South Africa. We check location requirements before adding any job. Some positions may require specific work authorization, and we note this where applicable.

Do I need an account to apply?

No. Each job listing links directly to the employer's application page. We don't sit between you and the company — we just help you find the right opportunities.

How often are new jobs added?

Daily. We scan multiple sources every morning and add fresh listings. Expired positions are removed within 24 hours.

What kind of salary can I expect?

Entry-level remote roles typically range from R8,000 to R15,000 per month. Mid-level positions R15,000 to R40,000. Senior and specialized tech roles can reach R40,000 to R100,000+. International companies often pay above local market rates.

Can I get a remote job with no experience?

Yes. Look for entry-level data entry, customer service, and virtual assistant roles. Many companies provide full training. What matters most is reliability, good internet, and willingness to learn.

What equipment do I need?

Most roles require a reliable laptop (not just a phone), stable internet of at least 10 Mbps, and a quiet workspace. Some companies provide equipment; others expect you to have your own.

How do I get my company's jobs listed?

Email us at hello@saremotejobs.co.za. Employer listings start at R349 per post, with discounts for bulk. We review every submission to ensure it's a legitimate opportunity.

Is remote work legal in South Africa?

Yes. Remote work is fully legal and increasingly common. Many SA companies adopted hybrid or fully remote policies after 2020, and the trend continues to grow.
