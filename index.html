Frequently Asked Questions

Are these jobs really available to South Africans?

Yes. Every listing is verified to accept applicants based in South Africa. We check location requirements before adding any job.

Do I need an account to apply?

No. Each job listing links directly to the employer's application page.

How often are new jobs added?

We update listings regularly. AI training and annotation jobs are cross-referenced from RemoWork, and SA company jobs are updated from career pages.

What kind of salary can I expect?

Entry-level remote roles: R8,000-R15,000/mo. Mid-level: R15,000-R40,000. Senior tech: R40,000-R100,000+. AI training roles pay $9-$80/hr depending on expertise.

Can I get a remote job with no experience?

Yes. Data entry, customer service, and virtual assistant roles often provide full training. AI annotation jobs also offer on-the-job training.

What equipment do I need?

A reliable laptop, stable internet (10+ Mbps), and a quiet workspace. Some employers provide equipment.

How do I get my company's jobs listed?

Email hello@saremotejobs.co.za. Employer listings from R349 per post.

Is remote work legal in South Africa?

Yes. Remote work is fully legal and increasingly common in SA.
