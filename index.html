Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about finding remote work as a South African.

Are these jobs really available to South Africans?

Yes. Every listing is verified to accept applicants based in South Africa. We check location requirements before adding any job.

Do I need to create an account to apply?

No. Each listing links directly to the employer's application page. You apply the same way you normally would — we just help you find the right opportunities.

How often are new jobs added?

Daily. We scan multiple sources every morning and add fresh listings. Expired positions are removed within 24 hours.

What salary can I expect for remote jobs in SA?

Entry-level: R8,000–R15,000/mo. Mid-level: R15,000–R40,000/mo. Senior/tech: R40,000–R100,000+/mo. International companies often pay more.

Can I get a remote job with no experience?

Yes — look for entry-level data entry, customer service, and virtual assistant roles. Many companies provide full training.

How do I get my company's jobs listed here?

Contact us at hello@saremotejobs.co.za. Employer listings start at R349 per post.

What equipment do I need for remote work?

Most roles require a reliable laptop, stable internet (10+ Mbps), and a quiet workspace. Some companies provide equipment.

Are these jobs permanent or contract?

We list both. Each card shows the employment type — full-time, part-time, contract, or freelance.
