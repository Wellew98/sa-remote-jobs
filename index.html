Frequently Asked Questions

Are these jobs really available to South Africans?

Yes. We verify every listing to ensure it accepts applicants based in South Africa. Some positions may require specific work authorization — we note this where applicable.

Do I need to create an account to apply?

No. Every job listing links directly to the employer's application page. You apply the same way you would on any other job board.

How often are jobs updated?

We add new listings daily and remove expired ones. The job count at the top of the page reflects our current active listings.

What types of remote jobs are listed?

Full-time, part-time, contract, and freelance positions across IT, marketing, customer service, data entry, design, finance, and more.

Are these international or local companies?

Both. We list SA companies hiring remotely as well as international companies that accept SA-based remote workers.

How can I get my company's jobs listed?

Visit our Contact page and send us your job listing details. Employer posting starts at R349 per listing.

What salary ranges can I expect for remote jobs in SA?

Entry-level remote positions typically range from R8,000-R15,000/month. Mid-level R15,000-R35,000. Senior and tech roles R35,000-R80,000+. International companies often pay USD-equivalent rates, which can be significantly higher.

Is remote work legal / recognized in South Africa?

Yes. Remote work is fully legal in South Africa. Many companies have adopted hybrid or fully remote policies since 2020.
