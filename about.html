About SA Remote Jobs

We built SA Remote Jobs because finding legitimate remote work as a South African is frustrating. Most global job boards are flooded with positions restricted to the US, UK, or EU. Even when a job says "remote," it often means "remote within the United States."

We manually curate listings from companies that actively hire in South Africa, and verify that each position accepts SA-based applicants.

What Makes Us Different

  • SA-first curation — every job on our board accepts South African applicants
  • No account needed — browse and apply without sign-up
  • Daily updates — fresh listings every morning, expired ones removed
  • Salary transparency — we show salary ranges whenever available
  • Direct apply — you apply on the employer's site, no middlemen

For Employers

Want to feature your remote positions? SA professionals are skilled, English-fluent, and in convenient time zones for European and African markets. Contact us about listing your jobs.
