About SA Remote Jobs

We built SA Remote Jobs because finding legitimate remote work as a South African is genuinely frustrating.

Here's the problem: most global job boards — the big ones everyone uses — are flooded with positions that say "remote" but actually mean "remote within the United States" or "remote within the EU." South African professionals waste hours scrolling past irrelevant listings, applying to jobs that will never consider them, and getting discouraged in the process.

Meanwhile, South Africa has incredible talent. Developers who've built systems for major banks. Designers who've worked on global campaigns. Customer service professionals fluent in multiple languages. Finance graduates from top universities. And yet finding remote work that actually hires South Africans remains unnecessarily difficult.

What We Do Differently

We manually curate job listings from companies that actively hire in South Africa. Every position on our board is checked to confirm it accepts SA-based applicants. We don't rely on automated scrapers that can't tell the difference between "Remote (Worldwide)" and "Remote (US Only)" — a person verifies each listing.

We also remove expired listings within 24 hours. Most job boards leave up positions that closed weeks ago, creating a frustrating experience where you apply to jobs that no longer exist. We keep our board clean.

How We Make Money

SA Remote Jobs is free for job seekers — no accounts, no subscriptions, no paywalls. Employers pay to feature their positions (from R349 per listing), which keeps the lights on without putting barriers between you and your next job.

Who's Behind This

SA Remote Jobs is run by a small team based in South Africa. We've been on both sides of the job search — sending out dozens of applications and hearing nothing back, and later hiring for our own teams. We built the job board we wish had existed when we were looking.

If you have questions, suggestions, or want your company's jobs listed, email us at hello@saremotejobs.co.za.
