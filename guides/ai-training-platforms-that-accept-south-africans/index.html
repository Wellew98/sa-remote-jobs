Which AI Training Platforms Actually Accept South Africans

2026-08-12

The problem with AI training job lists

Most “AI training jobs for Africans” articles are written by people who have never applied. They list platforms that quietly reject South African applicants at signup, quote pay rates that do not exist, and recommend routes that dead-end at a “country not supported” screen.

This list is different. Every platform below is one we hold live job data for on this site, or one whose own careers page names South Africa. We do not list a platform unless it demonstrably takes South Africans.

The platforms we can verify today

Micro1 — expert contract roles, global

Micro1 posts expert contract roles (engineering, science, finance, law) with published hourly rates. Listings on their board are global remote and South Africans can apply. Observed rates in our current data: $50–$200 per hour depending on speciality, with most expert roles in the $60–$130 range.

Apply through their opportunities board at micro1.ai.

Mercor — global AI evaluation work

Mercor runs AI evaluation and expert consulting tasks, paid per hour or per task. Their current listings include generalist work from $50–$60/hr, expert consulting at $2,000 per task, and specialist roles (law, finance, CUDA engineering) at up to $150/hr. South Africans are accepted on the platform.

Apply through the Mercor work platform at work.mercor.com.

Vetto — AI evaluation and safety work

Vetto runs freelance AI evaluation and safety projects, including role-play actors and clinical specialists for safety testing. Contract, task-based pay. Global remote, SA applicants accepted.

AfterQuery — AI training and annotation

AfterQuery sources general AI data annotation, content evaluation and coding data work. Their roles are remote and open to South Africans.

Braintrust, Turing, Ethos, SME Careers, RemoExperts

All five operate global AI/tech talent platforms with open roles in our current data. All accept South African applicants. Braintrust and Turing specialise in software and AI/ML roles; Ethos, SME Careers and RemoExperts focus on AI training and evaluation.

Lionbridge

Lionbridge, one of the longest-running language and AI data companies, holds a standing global AI data training application open to South Africans. Pay varies by project and is not published up front.

Platforms whose own pages name South Africa

Two companies go further and name South Africa explicitly on their careers pages:

  • RAY AI — places AI-literate executive assistants with founders and operators in the US and Europe. Their careers page lists South Africa on three of four hiring tracks, with 19 open SA-eligible roles at the time of our last check. Their clients pay €2,520–€3,000 per month per assistant; what the assistant is paid is not published.
  • Somewhere (formerly Support Shepherd) — a US staffing firm that runs a dedicated South Africa hiring page. Their own site states placements of South Africans at US companies with salaries around $18,000 USD per year. They also post on PNet, so expect competition.

What to watch out for

  • “Remote” does not mean South Africa. Many global boards advertise “remote” roles that are US-only or EU-only. Always check the eligible-countries section before applying.
  • Unpublished pay. Some platforms never publish rates. That is normal for project-based work, but never pay a fee to apply or to “unlock” a listing. Legitimate platforms do not charge applicants.
  • This list changes. Platform availability for South Africans shifts. We re-check listings regularly and update this post when a platform changes policy.
