What Remote Work Can South Africans Actually Get in 2026

2026-08-12

The honest picture

South Africans are not short of remote job boards. We are short of boards that only show jobs South Africans can actually get. Most aggregators dump thousands of listings that quietly exclude us at the application stage.

This breakdown is drawn from the 189 live listings on this site on 12 August 2026 — every one filtered for SA or global-remote eligibility, with apply links checked before publishing.

Where the jobs actually are

The remote work genuinely open to South Africans today clusters in eight areas:

  • AI & Data Training — the biggest single cluster. Platforms like Micro1, Mercor, Vetto and AfterQuery pay $15–$200/hr for annotation, evaluation and expert consulting. No degree required for entry-level annotation; expert tracks pay the top rates.
  • Software & IT — developers, QA and data engineers hired remotely by foreign companies through VirtuHire and Braintrust, often on UK or US hours.
  • Sales & Business Development — SDR and closer roles for foreign companies, typically base plus commission, US/UK hours.
  • Admin & Virtual Assistance — executive assistants and operations support for founders abroad, via VirtuHire, RAY AI and Somewhere.
  • Marketing & Content — PPC, SEO, content and social roles for UK and US clients.
  • Finance & Accounting — bookkeepers, accountants and FP&A analysts for foreign SMEs, $18K–$24K/yr observed.
  • Customer Support — support and client-success roles, remote from SA.
  • Design & Creative — design, video editing and artwork for international brands.

Who is actually hiring South Africans

Our board's largest verified sources:

  • VirtuHire — a South African Employer of Record placing SA talent with UK/US clients. Over 100 open roles across all eight categories. This is currently the largest single verified supply of foreign-currency remote work for South Africans.
  • Micro1 and Mercor — the two largest global AI training platforms open to SA applicants.
  • ALX Africa — pan-African roles in tech, L&D and operations.
  • INFUSE — B2B demand generation roles open to SA.

What the pay looks like

Observed rates in our data: entry AI annotation from $15/hr, generalist AI work $50–$70/hr, expert consulting $80–$200/hr, and salaried roles from $18,000–$24,000/yr. Converted at R18/$ that is R21,600–R28,800 a month for salaried roles and R260–R2,800 an hour for contract work. Rates are indicative and move with the exchange rate.

What to do next

  1. Pick one category and one company from the lists above — do not spray applications everywhere.
  2. Read the job description in full before applying; confirm the country list mentions South Africa or global remote.
  3. For AI training platforms, apply through the platform signup, not through a middleman.
  4. Never pay anyone to apply for a job. All listings on this site are free to apply to.
