HireSava

VerifiedAgencyOpen to beginners hiresava.com

A South-Africa-focused job board rather than an agency: employers post roles and hire directly, with no recruiter in between. You register as a worker, build a profile and apply to listings yourself. Roles skew towards virtual assistance, admin and sales support for overseas businesses.

Can a South African join?

"Hire Top Remote Talent and Virtual Assistants from South Africa" and "No agencies. No middlemen. Just direct access to top talent in South Africa." The entire platform is built around South African workers.

Checked against:

  • https://www.hiresava.com/jobs
  • https://www.hiresava.com/why-hiresava
  • https://www.hiresava.com/

What it pays

Not published. HireSava sets no rates itself - pay is whatever the individual employer offers on each listing, so check each posting. Their marketing to employers emphasises hiring "for a fraction of the cost" of local staff, which is worth knowing when you negotiate.

How hard is it to get in?

No experience needed to get started.
