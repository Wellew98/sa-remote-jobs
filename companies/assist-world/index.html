Assist World

AgencySome experience needed assistworld.com

US virtual-assistant agency placing vetted VAs with American businesses across accounting, legal, healthcare, real estate, e-commerce, logistics and other industries. In their own words you are "employed by Assist World and contracted to the client" - you work on one client account, but Assist World is the employer. South Africa is one of their main hiring markets alongside the Philippines.

Can a South African join?

Bottom line: Yes — South Africans can join Assist World.

Their public Lever board lists "South Africa" as an eligible location on 96 of 105 current postings - 51 of those name South Africa as the primary location, the rest list it alongside the Philippines, Colombia or Argentina.

Checked against these sources on 24 August 2026

  • https://jobs.lever.co/assist-world
  • https://assistworld.com

What it pays

Bottom line: Not published. Pay is agreed per role, so ask what it is before you accept.

Not published. Assist World's own site quotes costs to clients, never contractor pay, and none of the 94 South African postings we currently list state a figure. Expect pay to be agreed per role at offer stage - ask before accepting.

How the money reaches you

Bottom line: Assist World employs you and pays you, not the client - but it does not say how, in what currency, or how often. Ask before you accept.

Assist World pays you, not the client. Their own posting sets the structure out: "All HR, payroll, and employment matters are managed by Assist World... The candidate will be employed by Assist World and contracted to the client." The rail itself is not published - no Deel, Wise, Payoneer or bank transfer is named on their site or in any of the 98 postings we hold, and not one of them states a currency either. Ask how, in what currency, and on what schedule you would be paid before you accept.
