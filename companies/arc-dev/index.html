Can a South African join?

Bottom line: Yes — South Africans can join Arc.dev.

Arc's own South Africa page lists real, named South African developers available for hire — Emmanuel M., Adekunle U., Kudakwashe T. and others — rather than a written eligibility policy. It is the clearest evidence found that South African developers are actively placed through Arc, though no formal "who can join" statement was located.

Checked against these sources on 24 August 2026

  • https://arc.dev/en-za/hire-developers
  • https://arc.dev/salaries/software-engineers-in-south-africa

What it pays

Bottom line: Freelancers set their own rate, and Arc says these run from $15 to over $110 an hour. South-African developers on Arc report averaging about $68,956 a year.

For freelance placements, their own FAQ: "you pay an hourly rate set by the freelancer based on their skills, experience, and location. Rates range from $15 to $110+." That range is global, not South-Africa-specific. Their separate salary page reports South-Africa-based remote developers on Arc self-reporting an average of $68,956/year.

How the money reaches you

Bottom line: Arc does not publish how it pays freelancers out. Ask before you take a contract.

Not published. Arc's terms confirm only the shape of it: "Freelancers are paid on a 'time' basis, meaning they are compensated for the time spent working on Freelance Jobs and approved expenses"; "All Freelancers are required to complete a tax form to receive payouts"; and "Arc is not responsible for any additional fees or charges that the Clients or Freelancers incur from their banks or financial institutions". No payout rail is named on any page we could reach. Arc's blog does run a general comparison of freelance payment methods - that is market commentary, not a list of what Arc itself pays through, and must not be read as one. Ask before you take a contract.
