AmazingTalker

PlatformSome experience needed amazingtalker.com

Language-tutoring marketplace with an AI-matching system pairing students to tutors. Requires teaching experience or a language-teaching certificate, plus a live 25-minute demo class scored out of 5 stars - a real bar, not just a sign-up form.

Can a South African join?

Bottom line: Yes — South Africans can join AmazingTalker.

AmazingTalker runs an Afrikaans-teaching track (Afrikaans is essentially a South African/Namibian language), and its own tutors listing page names eight currently active tutors who each state "from South Africa" in their own profile text, several with the SA flag - e.g. Danie van Schalkwyk: "I am a native Afrikaans speaker from Johannesburg South Africa." Same shape as Welo Data's Afrikaans/Zulu Talent Hub evidence already in this database.

Checked against these sources on 28 August 2026

  • https://en.amazingtalker.com/tutors/afrikaans

What it pays

Tutor-set, no upper limit. Own page: "most Afrikaans teachers charge US$15-28 per 50 minute private lesson, and US$6-10 per 25 minute trial lesson."

How the money reaches you

PayPal, Payoneer or Bank Transfer; paid monthly on a 2nd-15th cycle.
