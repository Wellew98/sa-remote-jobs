Upwork

PlatformSome experience needed upwork.com

General freelance marketplace spanning almost every skill category. You build a profile, buy "Connects" credits, and bid on client-posted jobs - some jobs invite qualified freelancers automatically instead.

Can a South African join?

Upwork's own eligibility article names a short, specific list of places the platform is unavailable - Iran, North Korea, Cuba, the Crimea, Luhansk and Donetsk regions of Ukraine, and (since May 2022) Russia and Belarus. South Africa is not on it. Upwork separately publishes a South-Africa-specific VAT guide for freelancers, confirming it treats South Africa as a real operating market rather than an omission.

What it pays

Freelancer-set rates - no platform-wide figure. Upwork deducts its own service fee from earnings before payout; the exact rate depends on your plan and lifetime billings with each client, not independently confirmed this pass.

How the money reaches you

Upwork's own support articles describe "Direct to Local Bank" transfers in your local currency, alongside PayPal and Payoneer (outside the US) - these titles come from Upwork's help center, but the pages themselves blocked a direct fetch, so this is not independently confirmed as available in South Africa specifically.
