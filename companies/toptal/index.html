Toptal

PlatformCompetitive toptal.com

Selective freelance network for developers, designers and finance/project experts - markets itself as the "top 3%". A multi-stage vetting process (language screen, technical tests, live interviews) gates entry; once in, you are matched to client engagements rather than applying to postings.

Can a South African join?

Toptal's own site publicly lists multiple real, named, currently-listed developers based in South Africa - e.g. toptal.com/resume/dembe-tshifaro: "Johannesburg, Gauteng, South Africa" - alongside others in Johannesburg and Cape Town. Same evidentiary shape as this site's existing Arc.dev entry.

What it pays

Own FAQ: "All members of the Toptal network set their own hourly rate"; "Toptal does not take a cut from that." No platform-wide figure - it is individually negotiated.

How the money reaches you

USD, "fully automated" payment (own FAQ) - the specific rail (bank/wire) is not named on their public pages.

How hard is it to get in?

Selective intake - a strong track record helps.
