RemoExperts

PlatformSome experience needed remoexperts.com

AI-training platform ("REX", legal entity Remoexperts Inc.) where experts do LLM evaluation, annotation and similar project work. A bachelor's degree is required for all contributors, there is a one-shot entry exam with no retakes, and VPN use is prohibited. Pay is monthly.

Can a South African join?

Every open posting on rex.zone carries an "Available in" field, and on each one it reads "All Countries" (checked 27 August 2026). Their FAQ puts it directly: "Can I sign up if I am not in the US? Yes. REX welcomes talents globally unless restricted by the project." The homepage invites experts "from any country, any time zone", and the Terms of Use state that "This Agreement applies to all users worldwide, regardless of nationality, place of residence, or payment method" (section 2). The only geographic exclusion anywhere in their Terms is standard US-sanctions language, which does not affect South Africa. Note that RemoExperts publishes no supported-country list and never names South Africa specifically - the permission above is global rather than country-by-country.

What it pays

Their own postings state "$30 - $65 USD per hour" (Generalist AI Trainer) and "USD $80 - 120/Day, dependent on actual skills and experience" (Repo-based Code Annotator). The FAQ adds only that rates depend on project complexity, skill level, region and accuracy performance.

How the money reaches you

Per their FAQ: "REX currently supports: Stripe, Payoneer, Wise", paid typically monthly.

How hard is it to get in?

Bachelor's degree required for every contributor; one entry exam with no retakes; persona identity verification; VPN use prohibited.
