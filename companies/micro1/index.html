Micro1

Platform micro1.ai

Micro1 is a US AI data-research company — styled on its own site as “the data research company to accelerate AI in the real world” — that hires remote contractors worldwide as “experts” for its AI training products (Realm, Cortex, Robotics). Roles range from AI Trainer to specialist reviewer positions across engineering, medicine, law and other domains, applied for through their own jobs portal rather than a South-Africa-specific programme.

Evidence They Hire South Africans

  • https://www.micro1.ai/experts
  • https://library.micro1.ai/working/faqs

How You Get Paid

USD, paid bi-monthly (twice a month), through Deel as their payroll and contracting platform — per Micro1's own FAQ.
