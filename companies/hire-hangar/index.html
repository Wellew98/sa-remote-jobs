VerifiedAgencySome experience needed hirehangar.com

US (Sacramento) staffing intermediary. Static careers copy says "US-based companies only" but that describes their clients, not candidates - their live Ashby board is heavily South African in practice.

Can a South African join?

Their public Ashby board (2026-08-27): of 105 total open roles, 47 set the location to South Africa - 27 "South Africa - Cape Town", 20 "South Africa - Johannesburg" - the single largest country on the board, ahead of Mexico and Argentina at 6 each. Roles span Data Analyst, Account Executive, SDR, UI/UX Designer, Recruiter and Customer Success.
