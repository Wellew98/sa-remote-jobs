VirtuHire

VerifiedAgencySome experience needed virtuhire.com

South African recruitment agency (founders Nicholas and Brandon) placing local professionals into fully remote roles with international clients. Their own site: "bridge the gap between businesses and exceptional remote talent from South Africa." They report 272+ clients served, 783+ hires made and a 94% retention rate. Does not publish whether it acts as an Employer of Record for these placements.

Can a South African join?

VirtuHire is built around South African candidates specifically, not a global board that happens to include them. Their live Workable API (2026-08-27) reports 124 open roles; every one of the first 10 we read set "South Africa" as the location, country code ZA.

Checked against

  • https://virtuhire.com/about
  • https://apply.workable.com/virtuhire/

What it pays

Not published platform-wide - pay is set per role and not shown on the board listings we read. VirtuHire's own site markets to employers on cost savings rather than to candidates on rate; ask before accepting a role.

How the money reaches you

Not published company-wide. Pay is set by the individual client, in the client's currency — one observed VirtuHire listing (Tech Support Specialist) pays GBP 40–50/hour, confirming placements bill in foreign currency rather than a single VirtuHire rate.
