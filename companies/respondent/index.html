Respondent

PlatformSome experience needed respondent.io

US research-recruitment platform that pays people to take part in paid research studies, interviews and surveys for other companies' UX and market-research teams. You are not hired — you apply to individual studies as they are published and are paid per session you are accepted into.

Can a South African join?

Bottom line: Yes — South Africans can join Respondent.

South Africa is named directly among Respondent's primary target markets: "Australia, Brazil, Canada, Colombia, France, Germany, Italy, India, Indonesia, Ireland, Kenya, Mexico, Netherlands, New Zealand, Philippines, Singapore, South Africa, Spain, the United Kingdom, and the United States."

Checked against

What it pays

Bottom line: Most sessions pay $50-250 depending on length and seniority, and senior business roles often pay $400 or more.

Their own participant page states it plainly: "Most sessions pay $50–$250 depending on length and seniority. Senior B2B roles regularly pay $400+." Paid per study, not hourly, and only once a researcher approves the session.

How the money reaches you

Tremendous, once your session is verified — not PayPal, despite what most third-party reviews of the platform claim.
