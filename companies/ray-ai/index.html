RAY AI

VerifiedAgencySome experience needed getray.ai

Berlin-based agency placing full-time AI-literate executive assistants with founders, VCs and operators in the US and Europe, recruiting from Africa, South America and Asia.

Can a South African join?

Bottom line: Yes — South Africans can join RAY AI.

Their own careers page lists South Africa explicitly across their open tracks. Structurally confirmed too: every posting we hold is scraped straight from their Teamtailor board's own JSON-LD `jobLocation.address.addressCountry` field filtered to ZA - not their unreliable `applicantLocationRequirements` field, which defaults to "Argentina" on every posting regardless of the real location (see CLAUDE.md).
