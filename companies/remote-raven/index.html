Remote Raven

Agency hireremoteraven.com

Remote Raven is a US remote-staffing agency placing virtual assistants and specialists — it advertises 100+ role types across admin, support, tax/accounting and marketing — with individual US client companies, and bundles managed IT, cybersecurity and communications services around the placements. Remote Raven's own marketing site does not name South Africa; the evidence instead sits in individual job postings, each of which sets an explicit country requirement in its structured listing data.
